# stages/01 — Infrared / Archaic

## 1. Purpose

The world bible for the first stage — survival, sensori-motor, raw
embodiment. Mysterium's tutorial and prologue live here. Mechanically
*minimal*; emotionally *primal*. The player's first 30–60 minutes of
play.

## 2. Stage definition

Infrared is the **archaic** structure of consciousness — the
animal-instinctual heritage. Survival is total concern; the world is
sensed, not interpreted. Self and environment are *not yet
differentiated* — the player's body and the world are one continuous
felt-pressure.

In real human development this corresponds roughly to the first 1–2
years; in Mysterium's compressed arc, it is the prologue / tutorial. The
defining capacities are **sensorimotor intelligence**, **basic
fight-or-flight**, and the rudimentary **five-sense awareness**.

Energetic correspondence (optional layer): the **Red-Ray** energy
centre — root, foundation, the "basic strengthening ray for each
density." Blockages here destabilise everything above. In Mysterium this
means the prologue must *land* — players must feel grounded by the end
of it, or they will struggle in every later stage.

The Infrared world is a **hostile but grounded** place. Caves, hearths,
raw earth, primal forests. It is not a *bad* place; it is a *real*
place. The player's relationship with it is "I survive here, therefore
I am."

## 3. The eight lines AT Infrared

| Line | What it looks like at Infrared |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | Sensorimotor intelligence — object permanence emerging. The player can track a moving target with the eyes; that is the cognitive capacity. No symbols, no language, no plans. |
| Emotional | Raw, primary affect — fear, rage, primary pleasure. Emotions ARE the player at this stage; there is no observer separate from them. |
| Moral | Pre-moral. Survival-instinctual choices only. "Eat or be eaten" — no moral content. |
| Intrapersonal | None — pre-introspective. Pure reaction. |
| Spiritual | Survival as ultimate concern. No spiritual line in any developed sense — the sacred IS the warmth of fire, the safety of shelter. |
| Somatic | Pure reflex. The body responds before the mind notices. This is the *only* line at high expression at Infrared — somatic intelligence is the ground of the stage. |
| Willpower | Reflex only; no chosen goal. Will is biological — hunger, thirst, sleep. |
| Interpersonal | Pre-social — co-presence only. Mother-infant bond is the prototypical interpersonal experience here, with no theory-of-mind. |

The line that *carries the player* through Infrared: **Somatic**.
Everything else is dormant or barely active.

## 4. AQAL quadrant snapshots

| Quadrant | What's happening at Infrared |
|---|---|
| **UL** Individual Interior | Felt-pressure, fused with environment. No "I" yet. |
| **UR** Individual Exterior | The body — hunger, fatigue, pain, pleasure as biological signals. |
| **LL** Collective Interior | Survival-band fellowship; non-verbal co-regulation; warmth of presence. |
| **LR** Collective Exterior | Foraging, shelter-building, fire-tending — the primal cooperative tasks. |

## 5. World aesthetic

### 5.1 Visual language

- **Palette anchor:** crimson / oxblood (Red-Ray); secondary charcoal,
  bone, deep umber.
- **Motifs:** caves, hearths, fire, raw earth, animal silhouettes,
  scattered bones, primitive ochre paintings.
- **Lighting:** firelight, low contrast, lots of shadow. Cold blues
  for the dangerous outside; warm reds for the sheltered interior.
- **Architecture:** none, or proto — overhangs, cave mouths, simple
  stacked stones, hide tents. Nothing beyond the immediate.
- **Camera / framing:** close, low, body-anchored. The world feels
  *near* — there are no panoramic establishing shots until the
  player exits Infrared.

### 5.2 Sonic language

- **Mode / scale:** drone-based, microtonal, modal-Lydian fragments.
- **Instrumentation:** throat-singing, deep frame drums, breath, fire
  crackles, animal calls, rain.
- **Cadence:** slow, breath-paced. No melodic hooks. The soundtrack
  IS the environment.
- **Silence:** used heavily. The fire in the cave with no music.

### 5.3 Narrative tone

- **Voice:** wordless. Grunts, breath, gestures. Any text is haiku —
  three lines maximum.
- **Theme:** *something is alive in you that wants to keep living*.
- **Conflict:** survival against indifferent environment. Predators,
  cold, hunger.
- **Ally:** a single primary ally — an animal-companion or a maternal
  figure — whose presence is felt before it is named.

## 6. Bestiary at Infrared

### 6.1 Side-character concepts (one per line)

| Line tested | Concept |
|---|---|
| Cognitive | **Sense-Seeker** — a predator that tracks the player by scent. Mechanic: a sustained-attention task — keep eyes on the moving cue without losing it. |
| Emotional | **Maw of Hunger** — fear-driven, no affect signal. The player must engage motor combat without the comfort of reading the enemy's state. |
| Moral | (None at Infrared — pre-moral.) Replace with a survival choice: eat now / save for later. |
| Intrapersonal | **Mirror-Pool** — passive; rewards the player who *looks*. The first introspective-spark moment. |
| Spiritual | **Bone-Hunger** — a survival shrine that takes ritual offerings. Establishes the proto-sacred. |
| Somatic | **Pounce-Beast** — fast reflex challenge. The signature Infrared enemy. |
| Willpower | **Inertia** — passive resistance; the player must *act* despite the world's drag. |
| Interpersonal | **Stranger** — non-interactive presence; the player must learn that not every figure is hostile or to-be-engaged. |

### 6.2 Mini-boss concepts (dual-line)

- **Somatic × Cognitive:** *The Stalker Pack* — three coordinated
  beasts; track one while dodging the others.
- **Somatic × Willpower:** *The Long Cold* — endurance encounter
  through environmental hazard; survival, not damage, is the win
  condition.

### 6.3 Main-boss concept (synthesis exam)

**The Old One** — the apex predator of the Infrared world. A great
beast, possibly mythic, possibly real. The fight has four phases that
together touch all four quadrants:

- **UR phase:** raw motor combat — dodges, strikes, sustained attention.
- **UL phase:** the moment of pause — the boss looks at the player; a
  non-mechanical choice (run / fight) that the game treats as the
  player's *first interior moment*.
- **LL phase:** the animal-companion / maternal-figure ally arrives;
  coordination matters more than personal skill.
- **LR phase:** the fire and terrain are weapons — dragging the boss
  toward hazards; the *world* becomes part of the fight.

Defeating The Old One is not killing it — at the player's choice, it
can be *spared* (a future ally) or *slain* (resources). Either choice
counts; this introduces moral content as a *seed* before the moral
line begins to develop.

### 6.4 Shadow concept

**The Hunger That Will Not End** — the player who cannot leave
Infrared, who fixates on survival even when safe. Manifests in late
play as a returning shadow — when the player has been over-cautious
or over-scarcity-minded for several sessions. The shadow encounter
is a feast that the player must *accept* — break the survival
fixation, eat well, rest, trust the warmth.

## 7. Stage advancement criteria

To pass Infrared and unlock Magenta, the player must:

1. Survive 5 hostile encounters (the floor)
2. Demonstrate basic dodge / posture proficiency (somatic line ≥
   Magenta-altitude in our two-line-pull rule)
3. Have at least one *interior moment* — completing the Mirror-Pool
   side-content
4. Defeat or spare The Old One
5. Have *spared* an enemy at least once (sows moral content)
6. Spend ≥ 30 minutes in the world (Infrared is short but not
   disposable — players who rush it have a worse Magenta)

## 8. Open questions

- **Onboarding length.** 30–60 minutes feels right for tutorial pacing;
  some players will resent any prologue. A "skip Infrared" option
  would *break* the foundational layer the rest of the game depends
  on. Default: no skip; the prologue is genuinely playable, not just
  a tutorial.
- **The maternal figure.** A single primary ally at Infrared is
  evocative but stereotypically gendered. Default: ally is rendered
  ambiguously, voiced in any chosen language by a non-binary actor.
- **Wordless onboarding.** Players are accustomed to text-heavy
  tutorials; Infrared's wordlessness is a UX risk. Mitigated by
  diegetic prompts (the fire crackles where the player should look)
  and a *post-stage* text-based recap.

## 9. Principles served

Principles **1, 4, 5** — the foundation must be solid for everything
above to develop, the player earns it through felt experience, and
the UX is the felt experience.
