# Cognitive / Magenta — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** The strategic-planning axis probes cognitive intelligence through MULTI-STEP SEQUENCING — can the player plan ahead? At Magenta, planning is MINIMAL: the player can hold ONE step in mind and execute it. True multi-step planning (n>1) is Red+. But PROTO-PLANNING exists: "I do THIS, then THAT happens."
>
> **Why this axis for Cognitive/Magenta:** Even at n=1, the seed of planning exists: "If I put the fire-rune here, the door opens." This is single-step causal reasoning — the foundation of all later planning. This modality tests whether the player can hold a single cause-effect link and ACT on it.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Spell-Planner"
- **Core mechanic:** The player plans single-step symbolic actions — "place this rune HERE to make THAT happen." Proto-planning: one cause → one effect, held in mind before execution. The seed of Tower-of-London at its most primitive.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Single Step → The Cause-Effect → The Delayed Action → The Two-Path → The First Plan

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "If you place the fire-rune on the altar, the door opens. Can you hold that in mind and DO it?" The contact boundary is: "Can you hold a cause-effect link and act on it?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* Can the player hold cause→effect? The Magical Thinker believes ANY action causes ANY effect (magical causation). The Concrete-Bound acts without planning (just does things). The Premature Reasoner tries to plan 3 steps when 1 is the ceiling. The Wonder-Refuser won't plan (planning commits to the symbolic world).
- *Emergent:* Can they hold the link stably? Can they delay action until the right moment?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE single-step planning — hold the cause-effect link, wait for the right moment, execute. Not magical causation, not random action, not over-planning.

**Successful integration:** The player holds a single cause-effect link in mind, waits for the appropriate moment, and executes the planned action accurately.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Planning where the planned action requires patience (delay before execution).
- Healthy: Waits for the right moment; executes when ready
- Pathological: Cannot wait; executes immediately regardless of timing
- **Detection:** Whether the player can delay planned action

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Self-generated plans (no prompt about what to do).
- Healthy: Identifies cause-effect link independently and acts on it
- Pathological: Cannot plan without being told the cause-effect link
- **Detection:** Self-generated planning quality

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Planning that requires coordination (ally must act first, then player acts).
- Healthy: Holds plan while waiting for ally's action
- Pathological: Cannot hold plan while attending to ally; loses the link
- **Detection:** Plan-holding during coordination

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Receiving a plan from ally (ally tells player what to do).
- Healthy: Integrates ally's plan and executes accurately
- Pathological: Cannot follow others' plans; must discover independently
- **Detection:** Execution quality on received plans

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar plans (well-known cause-effect links).
- Healthy: Executes familiar plans reliably
- Pathological: Disengages from familiar plans; "I already know this"
- **Detection:** Execution quality on below-level familiar plans

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel cause-effect links (new symbols, new outcomes).
- Healthy: Explores with curiosity; tests the link
- Pathological: Attempts multi-step plans before single-step is stable
- **Detection:** Whether novel links produce appropriate single-step exploration or premature multi-step attempts

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining planning capacity over time.
- Healthy: Single-step planning remains reliable across sessions
- Pathological: Planning capacity degrades; cause-effect links forgotten
- **Detection:** Planning consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether planning appears spontaneously in other contexts.
- Healthy: Cause-effect reasoning appears in non-planning tasks
- Pathological: Planning exists only during explicit planning tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of cause-effect reasoning to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Magical Thinker"
**How it surfaces:** Believes ANY action causes ANY desired effect. "If I wave the rune, everything I want happens." Magical causation — no accurate cause-effect link, just wish-fulfilment disguised as planning.
- **Detection signals:** Claims plans will work without accurate cause-effect link; surprised when magical plans fail; "But I did the ritual!"; no learning from plan-failure; attributes failure to insufficient magic rather than wrong plan

### Dark-Allergy: "The Concrete-Bound"
**How it surfaces:** Acts WITHOUT planning. Just does things and sees what happens. No cause-effect holding. "I'll just try stuff." Random action without symbolic mediation.
- **Detection signals:** No delay between stimulus and action; no evidence of cause-effect holding; trial-and-error without learning; "I'll just see what happens"; no plan-before-action pattern

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Reasoner"
**How it surfaces:** Attempts MULTI-STEP plans when single-step is the ceiling. "First I'll place the fire-rune, then the water-rune, then..." — planning beyond capacity. Plans collapse because n=1 can't hold multi-step sequences.
- **Detection signals:** Multi-step plan attempts; plan-collapse mid-execution; verbalised complex plans that fail; attempts Tower-of-London-style reasoning at Magenta; frustration at single-step tasks ("too easy")

### Golden-Allergy: "The Wonder-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Refuses to plan because planning commits to the symbolic world. "What if the plan is wrong?" is really "What if planning makes the symbols real?" Avoids the cause-effect commitment.
- **Detection signals:** Plan-avoidance; excessive hesitation before planned action; "What if it doesn't work?"; can execute plans when forced but avoids voluntary planning; approach-withdrawal at plan-execution

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Make cause-effect links EXPLICIT and TESTABLE: "Fire-rune → door opens. Try it. Did it work?"
- Introduce plan-FAILURE as learning: "That rune didn't open the door. Which one does?"
- Frame: "Magic isn't wishing. It's knowing which rune does what. Test it."
- Build: accurate cause-effect reasoning (not magical wishing)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Introduce MINIMAL planning: "Before you act — what will happen? Just guess."
- Make planning produce BETTER outcomes than random action
- Frame: "Think first, then act. One thought. What will happen?"
- Reward ANY plan-before-action; celebrate first accurate prediction

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- BLOCK multi-step attempts; lock to single-step until mastery
- Frame: "One step. Master this one. The next step comes when this one is solid."
- Reward DEPTH of single-step mastery (speed, accuracy, transfer)
- Make single-step planning feel COMPLETE (not "baby stuff")

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make plans REVERSIBLE: "Try it. If it doesn't work, try something else."
- Remove consequence-anxiety: "Nothing bad happens if the plan fails."
- Frame: "It's just a guess. See if you're right. No penalty for wrong."
- Reward planning-attempts; accuracy secondary to engagement

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Spell-Planner: a cause-effect space where single symbolic actions produce outcomes. The player must hold the cause-effect link in mind and execute at the right moment. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage ritual-ground — altars that respond to runes, doors that open to symbols, a world where one action produces one result.

### Interaction
- **The Single Step (1-5):** "Place fire-rune on altar → door opens." Immediate cause-effect. Execute now.
- **The Cause-Effect (5-15):** Must HOLD the link before executing. Delay between learning and action.
- **The Delayed Action (15-30):** Must wait for the RIGHT MOMENT to execute (timing + planning).
- **The Two-Path (30-50):** Two possible cause-effect links; choose the correct one for desired outcome.
- **The First Plan (50+):** Full single-step planning mastery — hold, wait, choose, execute accurately.

### Feedback
- Accurate planned action → "You knew what would happen. You planned. It worked."
- Magical wishing → "You hoped it would work. But did you KNOW? Which rune opens which door?"
- Random action → "You just tried. Next time — think first. What will happen?"
- Over-planning → "One step. Just one. What's the ONE thing to do right now?"
- Avoidance → "It's safe to try. What do you think will happen? Just guess."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Cause-effect transparency: obvious → learned → inferred
- Delay between learning and execution: 0s → 2s → 5s → 10s
- Number of possible actions: 1 → 2 → 3
- Timing precision: generous → moderate → precise

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Single Step | 1-5 | Immediate cause-effect; no delay |
| The Cause-Effect | 5-15 | Delay between learning and execution |
| The Delayed Action | 15-30 | Timing matters; wait for right moment |
| The Two-Path | 30-50 | Choose between cause-effect links |
| The First Plan | 50+ | Full single-step planning mastery |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Immediate cause-effect:** Place rune → outcome (no delay)
- **Delayed cause-effect:** Learn link → wait → execute
- **Timed cause-effect:** Execute at specific moment
- **Choice cause-effect:** Multiple links; choose correct one
- **Transfer items:** Cause-effect links from other contexts

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ immediate, 20+ delayed, 15+ timed, 15+ choice, 10+ transfer

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Magical causation → dark-addiction; random action → dark-allergy
- Multi-step attempts → golden-addiction; plan-avoidance → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Cause-effect accuracy:** Does the player correctly predict outcomes?
- **Plan-holding duration:** How long can the cause-effect link be held before execution?
- **Timing accuracy:** Can the player execute at the right moment?
- **Choice accuracy:** Can the player select the correct cause-effect link?
- **Plan-before-action rate:** Does the player plan before acting (vs. random trial)?

### LLM rubric
- **Low-Medium:** Core scoring algorithmic (accuracy, timing). LLM generates scenarios and contextual framing. Some evaluation of plan-quality on choice items.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Drag-and-drop (place rune on target); tap-to-execute (timed action); tap-to-select (choose between options)

### Timing requirements
- Execution timing at ±100ms precision; delay measurement continuous; plan-holding duration tracked

### NPC/AI requirements
- Environmental cause-effect system (altars, doors, creatures that respond to runes)
- Timing system (moments when action is effective)
- Optional: ally who demonstrates cause-effect links (communion probing)

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Scenario generation, cause-effect narration. Core scoring algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Cause-effect accuracy history; plan-holding durations; timing accuracy; choice accuracy; plan-before-action rates; magical-causation indicators; random-action indicators; multi-step attempt indicators; avoidance indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
