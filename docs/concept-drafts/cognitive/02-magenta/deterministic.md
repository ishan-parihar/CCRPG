# Cognitive / Magenta — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis provides OBJECTIVE measurement of cognitive capacity — no LLM interpretation, no subjective scoring. Pure psychometric signal. At Magenta, this means: can the player hold one symbol in mind, recognise its return, and inhibit response to non-targets?
>
> **Why this axis for Cognitive/Magenta:** This is the GROUND TRUTH for the module. All other modalities cross-validate against deterministic. If the player can't pass n=1 here, their performance elsewhere is either procedural (body-memory) or performed (golden-addiction).

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Glimmer-Catcher"
- **Core mechanic:** Rune-symbols flash; the player must recognise when a symbol RETURNS (n=1 echo detection), match symbols to their referents, and inhibit response to distractors. Pure symbolic working-memory measurement wrapped in magical aesthetics.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Rune → The Echo → The Substitution → The Pattern → The Living Symbol

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "A rune flashes in the dark. Remember it. When it returns, catch it. When something else appears, let it pass." The contact boundary is: "Can you hold a symbol in mind?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* Does the player engage symbolically at all? Do they confuse symbols with reality (dark-addiction)? Refuse to engage (dark-allergy)?
- *Emergent:* Can they hold the symbol cleanly? Do they rush to hold two (golden-addiction)? Fear the symbol's return (golden-allergy)?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE symbol-holding — not magical attribution, not avoidance, not premature advancement. The rune is a tool. Hold it. Use it. Let it go.

**Successful integration:** The player holds one symbol stably, recognises its return accurately, inhibits on non-targets, and uses symbols as representations (not magical forces).

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Independent symbol-holding under increasing distractor load.
- Healthy: Holds symbol independently; manages distractors through own effort
- Pathological: Treats symbol-holding as magical control ("I COMMAND the rune to stay")
- **Detection:** Whether success is attributed to skill or magical power

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Self-initiated symbol engagement (choosing which rune to track).
- Healthy: Chooses and commits to tracking a symbol
- Pathological: Cannot choose without external direction; waits to be told which rune
- **Detection:** Self-initiation rate on free-choice trials

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Shared symbol-tracking (two players tracking same rune stream).
- Healthy: Coordinates with partner; shares symbolic space
- Pathological: Loses own tracking when attending to partner's performance
- **Detection:** Whether own accuracy degrades in shared context

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Receiving symbolic information from partner (partner signals which rune to track).
- Healthy: Integrates partner's signal into own tracking
- Pathological: Cannot receive symbolic input from others; must discover alone
- **Detection:** Accuracy on partner-signalled vs. self-discovered targets

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar runes (well-practiced symbols).
- Healthy: Maintains engagement and accuracy on familiar symbols
- Pathological: Disengages from familiar symbols; "I already know this one"
- **Detection:** Accuracy maintenance on below-level familiar trials

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel runes (never-seen symbols introduced).
- Healthy: Engages with curiosity; attempts to hold novel symbol
- Pathological: Attempts to hold multiple novel symbols simultaneously (premature n=2)
- **Detection:** Whether novel symbols produce appropriate single-hold or premature multi-hold

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Returning to basic symbol-holding after advancement.
- Healthy: Returns to n=1 with ease; maintains foundation
- Pathological: Treats return to basics as regression; "I'm past this"
- **Detection:** Engagement quality on maintenance trials

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether symbol-holding appears spontaneously outside structured tasks.
- Healthy: Symbol-holding capacity transfers to other game contexts
- Pathological: Symbol-holding exists only during explicit n-back tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer evidence from ecological observation

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Magical Thinker"
**How it surfaces:** The player treats the rune as a FORCE rather than a REPRESENTATION. High engagement but accuracy doesn't improve because they're relating to symbols magically rather than cognitively. They "believe in" the rune rather than "holding" it.
- **Detection signals:** High engagement + plateaued accuracy; ritual behaviours around symbol-presentation; distress when symbols "don't work"; attribution of power to symbols themselves

### Dark-Allergy: "The Concrete-Bound"
**How it surfaces:** The player shows minimal engagement with the symbolic task. Responds to physical properties (colour, position) but not symbolic content. Treats runes as meaningless shapes.
- **Detection signals:** Near-chance accuracy on symbol-matching; high accuracy on physical-property tasks; no improvement over sessions; low engagement metrics

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Reasoner"
**How it surfaces:** The player attempts n=2 before n=1 is stable. Tries to track multiple symbols, create logical rules about symbol sequences, or "figure out the pattern" rather than simply holding one symbol.
- **Detection signals:** Premature difficulty-seeking; unstable n=1 + attempts at n=2; verbalised "rules" about sequences; accuracy drops when trying to be "advanced"

### Golden-Allergy: "The Wonder-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** The player CAN hold the symbol (capacity exists) but AVOIDS doing so. Approach-withdrawal: engages briefly then disengages. The symbol's return produces anxiety rather than recognition.
- **Detection signals:** Adequate capacity on forced trials + avoidance on free-choice; declining engagement over session; approach-withdrawal pattern; anxiety markers at symbol-presentation

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Strip magical framing; present symbols as TOOLS ("This rune opens the door. It's a key.")
- Reward ACCURACY over engagement intensity
- Frame: "The rune doesn't have power. YOU have the power to remember it. That's the real magic."
- Gradually build: symbol-as-representation (healthy Magenta relationship)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Bridge from physical to symbolic: "This shape means 'open.' Tap it when you see it."
- Make symbolic engagement produce TANGIBLE results (door opens, path clears)
- Frame: "It's just a shape that means something. Like a key looks like a key."
- Reward ANY symbolic recognition; celebrate first accurate match

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- BLOCK premature advancement; lock n=2 until n=1 is 90%+ stable across 3 sessions
- Frame: "Master this one first. The next rune will come when this one is truly yours."
- Reward DEPTH of n=1 mastery (speed, consistency, transfer) over breadth
- Make n=1 mastery feel COMPLETE and satisfying (not "baby stuff")

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Present symbols in SAFE, low-stakes contexts (no failure consequence)
- Make symbols BEAUTIFUL and INVITING (aesthetic reward for engagement)
- Frame: "Just look. What do you see? No wrong answer. Just notice."
- Reward approach behaviour; never punish withdrawal; build trust gradually

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Glimmer-Catcher: rune-symbols appear in a magical space (painted cave, totem circle, dream-ground). The player catches returning runes and lets non-targets pass. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage wonder — glowing symbols in darkness, each one alive and meaningful.

### Interaction
- **The First Rune (1-5):** Single symbol presented; player taps when it appears among blanks. Pure recognition.
- **The Echo (5-15):** Symbol appears, disappears, reappears among distractors. n=1 echo detection.
- **The Substitution (15-30):** Symbol appears; player must USE it (tap the thing it represents, not the symbol itself). Symbol-as-tool.
- **The Pattern (30-50):** Symbol returns across longer sequences; player must recognise return despite intervening items.
- **The Living Symbol (50+):** Full n=1 mastery — hold, recognise, substitute, inhibit, all integrated.

### Feedback
- Accurate catch → The rune glows; the world responds. "You remembered. The symbol lives in you."
- False alarm → The rune fades. "That wasn't yours. Let it pass."
- Miss → The rune dims. "It returned, but you didn't see it. Watch again."
- Magical-thinking detected → "The rune is a tool, not a force. Hold it gently."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Distractor similarity: very different → similar → near-identical
- Inter-stimulus interval: long (3s) → medium (2s) → short (1.2s)
- Sequence length: 3 items → 5 → 8 → 12
- Symbol complexity: simple geometric → complex rune → abstract glyph

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Rune | 1-5 | Single symbol recognition; no distractors |
| The Echo | 5-15 | n=1 with distractors; echo detection |
| The Substitution | 15-30 | Symbol-as-tool; use not just recognise |
| The Pattern | 30-50 | Longer sequences; sustained n=1 |
| The Living Symbol | 50+ | Full integrated n=1 mastery |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Target symbols:** Runes/glyphs the player must track (varied complexity)
- **Distractor symbols:** Non-target runes (varied similarity to target)
- **Substitution targets:** Objects/actions the symbol represents
- **Sequence configurations:** Varied lengths and distractor densities

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ target symbols, 50+ distractors, 20+ substitution mappings, 25+ sequence configurations

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Magical attribution → dark-addiction; zero engagement → dark-allergy
- Premature n=2 attempts → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Hit rate:** % of target-returns correctly identified
- **False alarm rate:** % of non-targets incorrectly responded to
- **d-prime (sensitivity):** Signal detection metric (hit rate vs. false alarm rate)
- **Response latency:** Speed of correct responses (faster = more automatic)
- **Consistency:** Stability of d-prime across sessions

### LLM rubric
- **Low:** No LLM needed for core scoring. All metrics are algorithmic. LLM generates contextual framing and feedback text only.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (respond to target); withhold (inhibit on non-target); drag (substitution tasks)

### Timing requirements
- Stimulus presentation: 60Hz minimum; response window: 100-3000ms; latency measurement at ±16ms precision

### NPC/AI requirements
- None. Pure player-vs-task. Environmental aesthetics only.

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Feedback text generation; contextual framing. Core psychometrics entirely algorithmic.

### State persistence
- d-prime history; hit/false-alarm rates per session; response latency distributions; consistency metrics; shadow signals (magical-attribution, avoidance, premature-advancement, approach-withdrawal); drive/shadow indicators; fatigue state; checkpoint position; item difficulty calibrations
