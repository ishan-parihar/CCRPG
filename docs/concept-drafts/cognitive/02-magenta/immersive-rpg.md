# Cognitive / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes cognitive intelligence through SPONTANEOUS SYMBOLIC BEHAVIOUR in an open magical world — not structured n-back tasks but free symbolic engagement in an environment saturated with symbols. How does the player's cognitive life LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Cognitive/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe symbolic capacity through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world IS symbolic — everything has meaning, everything is alive. The player navigates this world, and their SPONTANEOUS symbolic engagement reveals genuine cognitive capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Dream-Ground"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world saturated with symbols — runes on walls, glowing glyphs, animated totems, responsive sacred spaces. The player's cognitive intelligence is assessed through the QUALITY of their spontaneous symbolic engagement: do they notice symbols? Hold them? Use them? Share them?
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Symbol-Noticing → Symbol-Using → Symbol-Remembering → Symbol-Creating → The Living Dream

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world is full of symbols. Nobody asks "can you hold a rune?" — the player simply NAVIGATES, and the world reveals whether they engage symbolically or not. Runes glow on cave walls. Totems pulse. Glyphs respond to proximity. The question is: does the player SEE and ENGAGE?

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL cognitive style surfaces. Do they notice symbols? Engage them magically? Ignore them? Rush past them? Their ecological behaviour reveals genuine symbolic capacity.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage more of the symbolic world? Hold symbols longer? Use them more accurately? Create new ones?

**Integration path:** The world rewards symbolic intelligence NATURALLY. Players who notice runes find hidden paths. Players who hold symbols unlock doors. Players who use symbols gain power. The reward is WONDER and ACCESS (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates symbolic intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — noticing, holding, using, and creating symbols without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world where symbols are ALIVE and RESPONSIVE. Painted caves with glowing runes. Totem circles that pulse. Dream-grounds where glyphs float. Sacred groves where the trees have faces. The world IS symbolic — the player navigates it, and their cognitive life reveals itself.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a world saturated with symbolic content
- **Spontaneous symbol-engagement:** Noticing, approaching, touching, using runes
- **Natural symbol-holding:** Remembering where symbols are; recognising returns
- **Ecological symbol-use:** Using symbols to navigate, unlock, access
- **Shared symbolic activity:** Engaging companion in symbolic exploration

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: symbolic intelligence → hidden paths, unlocked doors, wonder
- Natural consequences: symbolic blindness → missed content, stuck paths, flat world
- No explicit "cognitive score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Symbol density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Symbol complexity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated exploration
- Companion symbolic activity scales with demonstrated sharing

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol-Noticing | 1-5 | Basic: does the player notice symbols? |
| Symbol-Using | 5-15 | Can they use symbols to navigate/unlock? |
| Symbol-Remembering | 15-30 | Do they remember symbol locations/meanings? |
| Symbol-Creating | 30-50 | Can they create new symbolic meaning? |
| The Living Dream | 50+ | Full ecological symbolic intelligence |

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Environmental runes:** Symbols on walls, floors, objects (varied visibility)
- **Responsive totems:** Sacred objects that respond to symbolic engagement
- **Symbol-gated paths:** Routes requiring symbolic knowledge to access
- **Dream-glyphs:** Floating symbols with varied functions
- **Shared sacred spaces:** Areas requiring coordinated symbolic engagement

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ environmental runes, 20+ responsive totems, 15+ gated paths, 15+ dream-glyphs, 10+ shared spaces

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Indiscriminate ritual → dark-addiction; zero symbolic engagement → dark-allergy
- Systematic/analytical approach → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (interact with symbols); trace (activate runes); hold (engage totems); navigate (explore)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, symbol-interaction rates, exploration quality)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of cognitive patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Responsive symbolic environment (runes that glow when approached, totems that pulse)
- Companion with own symbolic behaviour (models engagement)
- Symbol-gating system (paths that require symbolic knowledge)
- Dream-glyph system (floating symbols with varied functions)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, symbol-response dynamics
- Detects spontaneous symbolic engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates cognitive intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive symbolic content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; symbol engagement history; exploration patterns; symbol-memory records; use-accuracy rates; noticing rates; approach-withdrawal patterns; ritual-behaviour indicators; systematic-behaviour indicators; drive/shadow signals; companion relationship; fatigue state; checkpoint position
