# Cognitive / Infrared — Module Specification

> **Line:** Cognitive  **Stage:** Infrared / Archaic  **Vibration:** The first flicker of awareness tracking movement in a field of undifferentiated sensation.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Sensorimotor intelligence at its most primitive. The capacity being measured is the ability to track a moving stimulus, detect object permanence (something continues to exist when occluded), respond to novel stimuli (dishabituation), and form basic stimulus-response associations. No symbols, no language, no planning. Pure perceptual engagement with the immediate sensory field.

**Core tasks:** Visual tracking, object permanence detection, stimulus-response latency, habituation curves, simple A-B-A-B pattern detection.

**Healthy expression:** Relaxed perceptual alertness — the organism tracks what matters, rests when nothing demands attention, and notices novelty without fixation.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: Survival-Fixation

Cannot leave sensorimotor scanning mode. Hypervigilant tracking without rest. The world is reduced to threat-detection. Every stimulus demands tracking. The organism cannot disengage — perceptual system locked in survival overdrive.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: Dissociation

Refuses embodied perception. Numbs out. Cannot track because sensory input itself is aversive. The organism dissociates from its own perceptual field — "checked out," unreachable by stimuli. Tracking accuracy near zero not from incapacity but from withdrawal.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: Premature Symbolism

Attempts symbolic/magical interpretation without genuine sensorimotor grounding. "Sees meaning" in random stimuli. Pattern-matches where no pattern exists. Tracking accuracy is poor because attention leaps to interpretation before perception completes.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: Perceptual Fortress

Sensorimotor capacity is intact but refuses symbolic emergence. Terrified of meaning beyond the literal. Tracks perfectly but resists any pattern that implies something beyond raw sensation. "It's just a thing" — locks the door against Magenta.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Curiosity toward novel stimuli; readiness for pattern | Premature leap to meaning (GA); frantic upward reach |
| Agape | Return to embodied rest after tracking; grounding | Collapse into numbness (DAll); refusal to engage |
| Agency | Sovereign attention — tracks what it chooses | Hypervigilant compulsion (DA); cannot choose not to track |
| Communion | Perceptual openness to the field as given | Merges with stimulus-flood; no boundary (DA variant) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

DA: Teach the organism it can STOP tracking and survive. Agency = "I choose where attention goes." Agape = "I can return to rest." The game rewards voluntary disengagement.

DAll: Teach the organism that embodied perception is safe. Agency = "I can track one thing at a time." Agape = "Sensation is home, not threat." The game rewards re-engagement with gentle stimuli.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

GA: Teach that accurate perception must precede interpretation. Eros = "Reach toward meaning" but Communion = "with the actual stimulus, not your projection." The game rewards perceptual accuracy before pattern-recognition.

GAll: Teach that pattern is not threat. Eros = "Something beyond raw sensation is calling." Communion = "Join with the emerging pattern." The game rewards noticing regularity without forcing meaning.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Module is integrated when: (a) tracking accuracy is stable across sessions, (b) voluntary disengagement is smooth, (c) habituation/dishabituation curves are healthy, (d) simple patterns are detected without over- or under-reading.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Frozen Scanner (Cognitive DA × Emotional DA)

Hypervigilant tracking fused with raw undifferentiated fear. The organism is paralysed in alertness — scanning everything, processing nothing. Cannot act because every stimulus is threat. Requires simultaneous work on Cognitive/Infrared DA and Emotional/Infrared DA.

### 6.2 The Checked-Out Body (Cognitive DAll × Somatic DAll)

Perceptual dissociation fused with body numbness. Complete disconnection from both sensory input and somatic awareness. The organism is neither perceiving nor feeling. Requires re-entry through the gentlest possible stimuli in both modules.

### 6.3 The Meaning-Seeker (Cognitive GA × Spiritual GA)

Premature symbolism fused with premature sacred attribution. Sees cosmic meaning in every flicker. Cannot simply perceive because everything is already "a sign." Requires grounding in raw perception before any interpretive frame.

### 6.4 The Sensory Fortress (Cognitive GAll × Intrapersonal GAll)

Refuses symbolic emergence fused with refuses self-emergence. Locked in pure sensation with no interiority. Perception works but nothing is allowed to mean anything or point inward. Requires gentle invitation toward pattern without forcing.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Shadows surface in order of accessibility: DAll first (most visible as non-engagement), DA second (visible as inability to disengage), GAll third (visible only when patterns are offered), GA last (visible only when interpretation is possible).

### 7.2 Per-Modality Surfacing Table

| Modality | Primary Shadow Surfaced | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DA (compulsive tracking) | DAll (non-tracking) |
| Language-Reflective | DAll (sound-withdrawal) | GA (over-reading rhythm) |
| Scenario-Choice | DA (approach everything) | DAll (avoid everything) |
| Embodied-Somatic | DAll (body disconnect) | DA (body hypervigilance) |
| Strategic-Planning | GA (premature sequencing) | GAll (refuses sequence) |
| Social-Cooperative | DAll (co-presence withdrawal) | GAll (refuses attunement) |
| Immersive-RPG | GAll (refuses emergence) | GA (over-interprets field) |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

A shadow is confirmed only when it appears across ≥2 modalities. Single-modality signals are flagged but not scored as shadow-active. Compound shadows require confirmation in both constituent modules independently.
