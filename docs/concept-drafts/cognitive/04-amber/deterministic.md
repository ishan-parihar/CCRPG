# Cognitive / Amber — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis probes cognitive intelligence through OBJECTIVE MEASUREMENT — tasks with correct/incorrect answers, precise timing, and algorithmic scoring. At Amber, this is: rule-following accuracy, n=2 stability, Corsi-3 reproduction, classification accuracy, and WCST shift-detection.
>
> **Why this axis for Cognitive/Amber:** Concrete operational thinking IS measurable. Can the player follow a rule? Hold two items? Reproduce a sequence? Classify correctly? Detect a shift? These are binary, objective, and the backbone of Amber cognitive assessment.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Ledger of Laws"
- **Core mechanic:** The player encounters a rule-governed system and must demonstrate mastery — follow the rule, hold the pattern, reproduce the sequence, classify the items, detect the shift. The system is a living codex that tests whether the player's mind can serve the order.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The First Law → The Stable Code → The Living Classification → The Shifting Rule → The Master Ledger

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Here is the rule. Can you follow it? Not once — CONSISTENTLY. Can you hold the pattern? Not briefly — STABLY. Can you see when the rule changes? Not eventually — QUICKLY." The contact boundary is: "Can your mind serve a system reliably?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player relate to rules? The Rigid Systematiser follows perfectly but CANNOT SHIFT. The Rule-Breaker ignores rules or responds randomly. The Premature Theorist attempts tasks beyond capacity. The System-Refuser performs well but avoids shift-detection tasks.
- *Emergent:* Can they follow rules flexibly — consistently but adaptably?

**Integration path:** Rewards FLEXIBLE RULE-MASTERY — consistent rule-following WITH shift-detection. Not rigid, not chaotic, not bypassing, not avoiding.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates concrete operational mastery — stable, flexible, systematic.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Independent rule-following without external cues.
- Healthy: Follows rules independently; applies system without prompting
- Pathological: Cannot follow rules without authority present; OR: enforces rules rigidly on the system itself
- **Detection:** Whether rule-following is self-generated and proportionate

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Rule-following under ambiguity (unclear which rule applies).
- Healthy: Selects appropriate rule under ambiguity; makes sovereign choice
- Pathological: Freezes when rule is ambiguous; cannot choose without certainty
- **Detection:** Rule-application quality under ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Rule-following when system provides conflicting feedback.
- Healthy: Maintains own rule-application while processing conflicting signals
- Pathological: Abandons own rule when system contradicts; OR: ignores all feedback
- **Detection:** Whether own rule-application survives contradiction

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared rule-following (coordinating with system's rhythm).
- Healthy: Synchronises with system's pace while maintaining accuracy
- Pathological: Cannot coordinate; either too fast or too slow for the system
- **Detection:** Quality of coordinated rule-following

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Rule-following consistency over time (same rules, many trials).
- Healthy: Maintains accuracy across extended sequences without degradation
- Pathological: Accuracy degrades with repetition (boredom/fatigue); OR: perseverates without noticing errors
- **Detection:** Accuracy stability over extended trials

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Engaging with increasing complexity (longer sequences, more rules).
- Healthy: Engages increasing complexity with appropriate effort
- Pathological: Cannot tolerate complexity increase; OR: seeks complexity compulsively
- **Detection:** Response to difficulty escalation

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining rule-mastery across sessions.
- Healthy: Returns to tasks with maintained capacity
- Pathological: Capacity degrades between sessions; cannot maintain
- **Detection:** Cross-session stability

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether rule-following capacity transfers to novel contexts.
- Healthy: Applies learned rules to new item-sets without retraining
- Pathological: Cannot transfer; each new context requires full relearning
- **Detection:** Transfer quality to novel items

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Systematiser"
**How it surfaces:** PERSEVERATES. When the rule shifts (WCST), continues applying the old rule. When classification criteria change, continues sorting by old criterion. Cannot let go of the established pattern.
- **Detection signals:** >3 perseverative errors on WCST; continues old classification after shift; accuracy drops ONLY at shift-points; high accuracy on stable rules, zero on shifted rules; "but that WAS the rule"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Rule-Breaker"
**How it surfaces:** IGNORES rules. Responses are random or impulsive. Cannot complete multi-step sequences. Abandons tasks mid-trial. Treats the system as meaningless.
- **Detection signals:** Below-chance accuracy; random response patterns; incomplete sequences; early abandonment; no learning curve; "this is pointless"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Theorist"
**How it surfaces:** ATTEMPTS BEYOND CAPACITY. Tries n=3 when n=2 is unstable. Attempts Corsi-4 when Corsi-3 fails. Seeks the harder task before mastering the current one.
- **Detection signals:** Requests harder difficulty; fails at current level but attempts next; n=2 accuracy <80% but attempts n=3; Corsi-3 <85% but attempts Corsi-4; "this is too easy" (while failing)

### Golden-Allergy: "The System-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** AVOIDS SHIFT-DETECTION. Excellent on stable rules but disengages when shifts are introduced. Doesn't WANT to notice the rule changed. Treats rule-change as threat.
- **Detection signals:** High accuracy on stable trials, sudden drop at shift introduction; avoidance of WCST-type tasks; anxiety signals at rule-change; "why did it change?"; strong Corsi-3 but avoids novel sequences

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Introduce GRADUAL shifts (not sudden WCST shifts but slow transitions)
- Reward noticing change: "The rule is shifting. Can you see it? Good. Now follow the NEW rule."
- Frame: "The old rule served you. The new rule serves you now. Both are tools. Neither is truth."
- Build: flexible rule-following (can shift without distress)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Start with SIMPLE, REWARDING rules (one-step, immediate payoff)
- Make rule-following feel like POWER not submission: "You followed the code. The system opened for you."
- Frame: "Rules are keys. Follow the rule, open the door. Your choice. Your power."
- Build: rule-following as skill (chosen, not imposed)

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- LOCK higher difficulty until current level is mastered (>90% accuracy required)
- Reward mastery at current level: "Perfect. You OWN this level. Now you've earned the next."
- Frame: "Mastery first. The next level requires THIS level to be solid. Build the foundation."
- Build: concrete mastery before advancement (patience with the current)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Introduce shifts as GENTLE and SAFE: "The rule is changing. That's okay. Watch. See? New rule. Same you."
- Reward noticing without requiring immediate adaptation
- Frame: "Rules change. You don't. You're still here. The new rule is just another tool."
- Build: comfort with change (rule-change isn't self-change)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Ledger of Laws: a codex-interface where rules are presented as illuminated pages. The player interacts with the codex — following rules, holding patterns, reproducing sequences, classifying items, detecting shifts. The aesthetic: Amber-stage monastery library — parchment, ink, candlelight, systematic order.

### Interaction
- **The First Law (1-5):** Single rule, follow consistently. "Red goes left. Blue goes right." Binary classification.
- **The Stable Code (5-15):** n=2 holding. "Remember the last TWO. Match the pattern." Sustained accuracy.
- **The Living Classification (15-30):** Multi-criterion sorting. "Sort by colour. Now by shape. Now by function." Flexible classification.
- **The Shifting Rule (30-50):** WCST-lite. The rule changes WITHOUT announcement. Detect and adapt.
- **The Master Ledger (50+):** Full integration: stable holding + flexible classification + shift-detection + sequence reproduction.

### Feedback
- Correct rule-following → "The code holds. You serve the order well. The ledger glows."
- Perseveration → "The rule has changed. Look again. The old law is honoured but the new law is HERE."
- Rule-breaking → "The code awaits. One rule. Simple. Follow it. The door opens for those who serve."
- Premature attempt → "Not yet. Master THIS page first. The next page requires this one to be perfect."
- Shift-avoidance → "The rule changed. That's natural. Rules live. They grow. Watch. See? Safe."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Rule complexity: binary → ternary → multi-criterion
- Holding load: n=1 review → n=2 stable → n=2 under interference
- Sequence length: Corsi-2 → Corsi-3 → Corsi-3 under load
- Shift frequency: rare → occasional → moderate (never high — that's Orange)
- Classification depth: single-criterion → dual-criterion → hierarchical

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Law | 1-5 | Single rule, binary classification |
| The Stable Code | 5-15 | n=2 holding, sustained accuracy |
| The Living Classification | 15-30 | Multi-criterion flexible sorting |
| The Shifting Rule | 30-50 | WCST-lite shift-detection |
| The Master Ledger | 50+ | Full concrete operational integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Rule-following items:** Binary/ternary classification rules (colour, shape, size, function)
- **Holding items:** n-back sequences at n=2 (visual, spatial, auditory)
- **Sequence items:** Corsi-style spatial sequences (length 2-3)
- **Classification items:** Multi-criterion sorting sets (20+ items per set)
- **Shift items:** WCST-style rule-shift trials (old rule → new rule)

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ rule-following, 30+ holding, 20+ sequence, 20+ classification sets, 15+ shift trials

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Perseverates at shift → dark-addiction; ignores rules → dark-allergy
- Attempts beyond capacity → golden-addiction; avoids shifts → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Rule-following accuracy:** % correct on stable rule trials
- **Holding stability:** n=2 accuracy across extended sequences (d' sensitivity)
- **Sequence reproduction:** Corsi-3 accuracy (% correct sequences)
- **Classification flexibility:** Trials-to-criterion on new classification rules
- **Shift-detection speed:** Trials-to-shift after rule change (perseverative errors)

### LLM rubric
- **None/Minimal:** Fully algorithmic. All scoring is objective (correct/incorrect, latency, perseverative error count). No LLM needed for core assessment.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (classify/select); swipe (sort); sequence-tap (Corsi reproduction); hold (sustained attention)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked (ms precision); inter-trial intervals standardised; session duration monitored for fatigue

### NPC/AI requirements
- Adaptive difficulty system (IRT-based item selection)
- Perseveration detection algorithm (consecutive same-rule errors after shift)
- Fatigue detection (accuracy degradation over time)
- Shadow-signal accumulator (cross-session pattern detection)

### LLM requirements
- **None:** Fully deterministic scoring. No language generation needed.

### State persistence
- Full trial history; accuracy per capacity; perseverative error counts; shift-detection latency; d' scores; classification flexibility metrics; Corsi accuracy; cross-session stability; drive/shadow signal accumulation; fatigue indicators; checkpoint position
