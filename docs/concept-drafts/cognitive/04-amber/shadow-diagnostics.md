# Cognitive / Amber — Shadow Diagnostics

> **Line:** Cognitive (executive function, perspective-taking, reasoning)
> **Stage:** Amber / Mythic-Membership (concrete operational, rule-following, systematic classification)
> **Shadow landscape:** The pathologies of the rule-following mind — rigidity, rebellion, premature abstraction, and refusal to grow.

---

## 1. The Four Shadow Archetypes

### 1.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Systematiser"

**Core pattern:** Clings to rules as ABSOLUTE TRUTH. Cannot tolerate ambiguity, exception, or novelty. The rule IS reality. Perseverates on known classifications even when evidence contradicts them. The system must not change.

**Drive pathology:**
| Drive | How it manifests |
|---|---|
| Agency | Enforces rules on others rigidly; "I know the correct way" |
| Communion | Demands others follow the same rules; conformity as love |
| Eros | Refuses new rules; clings to first-learned system; perseverates |
| Agape | Cannot return to simpler rules; "the system is complete" |

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Perseverates on WCST (continues applying old rule after shift)
- Classifies rigidly (cannot reclassify when criteria change)
- Applies rules even when context makes them inappropriate
- Becomes distressed when rules are ambiguous or contradictory
- Treats exceptions as errors rather than information
- "That's not how it works" — the rule cannot be wrong

**Atman Project defenses:** Rigidification, systematisation, denial of novelty. The self is the system; if the system changes, the self dissolves.

**What it protects against:** The chaos of Red (impulsive, rule-less). Rigidity is the defense against the terror of having no structure. "If I let go of the rule, I'll fall back into chaos."

**Healing direction:** Agape + Agency — return to the rule with COMPASSION (it served you; it's not absolute) + sovereign examination (YOU can evaluate the rule; the rule doesn't own you).

---

### 1.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Rule-Breaker"

**Core pattern:** Refuses systematic thought entirely. Stays in egocentric Red reasoning — impulsive, rule-dismissive, "rules are for sheep." Cannot or will not follow a multi-step procedure. Treats all structure as oppression.

**Drive pathology:**
| Drive | How it manifests |
|---|---|
| Agency | Uses rule-breaking as identity; "I'm too smart for rules" |
| Communion | Cannot coordinate with others through shared rules |
| Eros | Refuses to learn new systems; "boring" |
| Agape | Cannot maintain any rule-based practice over time |

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Ignores rules even when following them would succeed
- Cannot complete multi-step procedures (abandons mid-sequence)
- Treats classification tasks as pointless
- Performs poorly on Corsi (won't attend to sequence)
- "Why should I?" — the rule has no authority
- Impulsive responses override systematic thought

**Atman Project defenses:** Rebellion, dismissal, counter-dependence. The self is the rebel; if it follows rules, it loses its Red-stage identity.

**What it protects against:** The loss of Red autonomy. Rule-following feels like submission. "If I follow the rule, I lose myself."

**Healing direction:** Agape + Agency — return to the CAPACITY for rules with compassion (rules aren't slavery; they're tools) + sovereign choice (YOU choose to follow; it's not submission).

---

### 1.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Theorist"

**Core pattern:** Rushes toward hypothetical-deductive reasoning (Orange) without mastering concrete operations. Performs formal logic without genuine rule-mastery. Talks about "systems thinking" and "meta-rules" while unable to follow a 3-step procedure reliably. Bypasses Amber toward Orange.

**Drive pathology:**
| Drive | How it manifests |
|---|---|
| Agency | Claims independent reasoning while actually confused |
| Communion | Dismisses "rule-followers" as beneath them |
| Eros | Compulsive reaching toward abstraction; cannot rest in concrete |
| Agape | Cannot embody abstract insights in concrete practice |

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Talks about rules abstractly but fails concrete rule-following tasks
- n=2 unstable but attempts n=3 tasks
- Corsi-3 unreliable but attempts Corsi-4
- Uses abstract language to describe simple concrete situations
- "I understand the PRINCIPLE" — but cannot execute the procedure
- Performs well on verbal reasoning, poorly on concrete operations

**Atman Project defenses:** Intellectualisation, abstraction, spiritual bypass (cognitive version). The self identifies with the HIGHER capacity to avoid mastering the current one.

**What it protects against:** The "boring" work of concrete mastery. Amber feels pedestrian. "I'm already beyond this."

**Healing direction:** Eros + Communion — reach toward Orange GENUINELY (not performatively) + join with others who have mastered concrete operations (learn from the tradition before transcending it).

---

### 1.4 Golden-Allergy: "The System-Refuser"

**Core pattern:** Fears the expansion into formal operations. Refuses abstraction. Senses the call toward hypothetical reasoning but retreats. "I don't need to think about thinking." Comfortable with concrete rules but terrified of the meta-level.

**Drive pathology:**
| Drive | How it manifests |
|---|---|
| Agency | Cannot assert independent reasoning beyond the given system |
| Communion | Clings to group's concrete rules; fears individual thought |
| Eros | Refuses the call to abstract; "this is enough" |
| Agape | Over-embodies concrete rules; makes them sacred/unchangeable |

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Excellent at rule-following but freezes when asked "why this rule?"
- Strong Corsi-3 and n=2 but avoids any task requiring meta-cognition
- Becomes anxious when rules are questioned (not just changed — QUESTIONED)
- "The rule is the rule" — refuses to examine the rule's basis
- Treats concrete operations as the ceiling; "I don't need more"
- Avoids WCST shift-detection (doesn't WANT to notice the rule changed)

**Atman Project defenses:** Concretisation, literalism, anti-intellectualism. The self is safe within the known system; abstraction threatens dissolution.

**What it protects against:** The vertigo of formal operations. "If I can think about my thinking, what IS my thinking? Who am I without the rules?" The terror of Orange's relativism.

**Healing direction:** Eros + Communion — reach toward abstraction with aspiration (it's not dangerous; it's the next gift) + join with others who have made the transition (see that they survived).

## 2. Compound Shadows (Cross-Module)

### 2.1 The Dogmatic Enforcer
**Modules:** Cognitive/Amber dark-addiction + Moral/Amber dark-addiction
**Pattern:** Rigid rules + rigid morality = fundamentalist thinking. The code is absolute. Questioning is heresy. Cannot distinguish "the rule works" from "the rule is sacred."
**Surfacing:** Present a situation where the cognitive rule and the moral rule CONFLICT. The Dogmatic Enforcer cannot tolerate the contradiction — they either deny it or become distressed.
**Healing:** Separate cognitive rules from moral rules. "The classification system is useful. The moral code is important. They're not the same thing. One can change without the other collapsing."

### 2.2 The Clever Rebel
**Modules:** Cognitive/Amber dark-allergy + Willpower/Amber dark-allergy
**Pattern:** Refuses rules AND refuses discipline. Stuck in Red impulsivity with cognitive justification. "I'm too smart for your systems AND too free for your discipline."
**Surfacing:** Present a task that requires BOTH rule-following AND sustained effort. The Clever Rebel fails at both — cannot follow the rule AND cannot sustain the attempt.
**Healing:** Show that rule-following IS a form of intelligence (not submission) and discipline IS a form of freedom (not slavery).

### 2.3 The Abstract Escapist
**Modules:** Cognitive/Amber golden-addiction + Intrapersonal/Amber golden-addiction
**Pattern:** Performs abstract reasoning while avoiding concrete self-examination. Theory without practice. "I understand the system" but cannot apply it to their own life.
**Surfacing:** Present a task requiring concrete self-application of an abstract principle. The Abstract Escapist can state the principle but cannot demonstrate it concretely.
**Healing:** Ground abstraction in concrete practice. "You understand the principle. Now DO it. Here. Now. Concretely."

### 2.4 The Frozen Conformist
**Modules:** Cognitive/Amber dark-addiction + Interpersonal/Amber dark-addiction
**Pattern:** Rigid rules + rigid group-belonging = cannot think independently. The group's rules are absolute. Individual reasoning is impossible because the group IS the mind.
**Surfacing:** Present a task requiring independent classification that contradicts group consensus. The Frozen Conformist cannot classify independently — they default to group answer.
**Healing:** Demonstrate that independent thought doesn't mean leaving the group. "You can think for yourself AND belong. The group doesn't need you to be mindless."

## 3. Shadow Surfacing Mechanics

### 3.1 How each game modality surfaces shadows

| Modality | Dark-Addiction signal | Dark-Allergy signal | Golden-Addiction signal | Golden-Allergy signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | Perseverates on WCST; cannot shift | Ignores rules; random responses | Attempts n=3 with unstable n=2 | Avoids WCST shift-detection |
| Language-reflective | Rigid rule-language; "always/never" | Cannot articulate any rule | Abstract meta-language without concrete backing | Refuses to discuss rule-basis |
| Scenario-choice | Applies rule rigidly regardless of context | Ignores rule in scenario; impulsive choice | Applies hypothetical reasoning to concrete dilemma | Refuses to consider alternatives to given rule |
| Embodied-somatic | Rigid body-form; cannot adapt movement | Refuses disciplined movement; chaotic | Attempts complex sequences beyond capacity | Freezes when movement pattern must change |
| Strategic-planning | Over-plans rigidly; cannot adapt | Cannot plan; impulsive execution | Plans at Orange complexity with Amber capacity | Refuses to plan beyond immediate step |
| Social-cooperative | Enforces rules on group rigidly | Cannot coordinate through shared rules | Claims meta-understanding while group fails | Defers all reasoning to group |
| Immersive-rpg | Follows world-rules obsessively | Ignores world-rules; chaotic navigation | Attempts Orange-level world-reasoning | Stays in known areas; refuses exploration |

### 3.2 Surfacing sequence (progressive revelation)

The shadow surfaces through the capacity sequence:

1. **Rule-following** — Does the player follow rules? (Dark-allergy surfaces here: refusal)
2. **Stable-holding** — Can they hold the rule stable? (Dark-addiction surfaces here: perseveration)
3. **Classification** — Can they classify flexibly? (Dark-addiction deepens: rigid classification)
4. **Rule-change-detection** — Can they detect shifts? (Golden-allergy surfaces here: avoidance of change)
5. **Sequence-reproduction** — Can they reproduce complex sequences? (Golden-addiction surfaces here: attempting beyond capacity)

### 3.3 Shadow intensity scoring

| Level | Description | Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No shadow signal | Continue normal progression |
| 1 | Mild signal (1-2 indicators) | Note; continue; observe across sessions |
| 2 | Moderate signal (3-4 indicators) | Adjust difficulty; present healing opportunities |
| 3 | Strong signal (5+ indicators) | Activate healing mechanics; present shadow-specific catalyst |
| 4 | Dominant pattern (consistent across modalities) | Full shadow-mode engagement; cross-module healing |

## 4. Healing Mechanics

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**For Dark-Addiction (Rigid Systematiser):**
1. Present rules that WORK but are NOT absolute (context-dependent rules)
2. Reward flexible application (same rule, different contexts = different outcomes)
3. Introduce "the rule served you" framing — gratitude without rigidity
4. Build: "I can follow rules AND know they're tools, not truths"

**For Dark-Allergy (Rule-Breaker):**
1. Present rules as CHOSEN (not imposed) — "you can follow this because YOU decide to"
2. Reward rule-following that leads to desired outcomes (instrumental value)
3. Introduce "rules are power" framing — following rules is a SKILL, not submission
4. Build: "I can follow rules without losing myself"

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**For Golden-Addiction (Premature Theorist):**
1. Present concrete tasks that REQUIRE mastery (cannot be bypassed with abstraction)
2. Reward concrete execution (not abstract understanding)
3. Introduce "master the foundation" framing — Orange requires Amber mastery
4. Build: "I can rest in concrete operations; they're not beneath me"

**For Golden-Allergy (System-Refuser):**
1. Present gentle meta-cognitive tasks (notice the rule, don't change it)
2. Reward noticing without requiring action (observation is safe)
3. Introduce "thinking about thinking is natural" framing — it's not dangerous
4. Build: "I can examine rules without losing them"

## 5. Integration Criteria

### 5.1 What "healed" looks like at Cognitive/Amber

The player demonstrates:
1. **Flexible rule-following:** Follows rules consistently BUT can adapt when context requires
2. **Stable without rigid:** n=2 consolidated AND can tolerate ambiguity
3. **Classification with flexibility:** Sorts accurately AND can re-sort when criteria change
4. **Rule-awareness without terror:** Can notice rules changing without distress
5. **Concrete mastery without ceiling:** Solid concrete operations AND openness to abstraction

### 5.2 Drive-health at integration

| Drive | Healthy expression post-integration |
|---|---|
| Agency | Applies rules independently; can evaluate rules; sovereign within the system |
| Communion | Coordinates through shared rules; learns from tradition; contributes to group knowledge |
| Eros | Reaches toward deeper understanding with genuine curiosity; not compulsive |
| Agape | Maintains rule-mastery with care; returns to basics without shame; embodies the system |

### 5.3 Transition readiness indicators

- n=2 perfect (>95% accuracy across 5+ sessions)
- WCST shift-detection reliable (>80% within 3 trials of shift)
- Corsi-3 stable (>90% accuracy)
- Classification flexible (can re-sort within 2 trials of new criterion)
- No dominant shadow pattern (all quadrants < level 2)
- At least 2 other lines at Amber
