# Cognitive / Amber — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes cognitive intelligence through SPONTANEOUS RULE-ENGAGEMENT in an open world — not structured cognitive tasks but free systematic behaviour. How does the player relate to rules, classifications, and sequences when nobody's asking "follow the rule"?
>
> **Why this axis for Cognitive/Amber:** The other 6 modalities probe cognitive capacity through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Amber, the world is RULE-GOVERNED — codes on walls, classification systems in libraries, sequences in liturgies, hierarchies in institutions. The player's spontaneous engagement with systematic content reveals genuine cognitive intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Ordered World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Amber-stage world saturated with rule-governed content. Codes inscribed on walls. Libraries with classification systems. Liturgies with sequences. Institutions with hierarchies. The player's spontaneous engagement with systematic content — how they navigate a rule-governed world without being prompted — reveals genuine cognitive intelligence.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Rule-Noticing → Rule-Following → Rule-Applying → Rule-Adapting → The Living Systematiser

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world contains systematic content. Nobody says "follow the rule" — but codes are inscribed, libraries are organised, liturgies proceed, institutions function. The player's NATURAL systematic engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL cognitive style surfaces. Do they read every code? Ignore all systems? Exploit rules for advantage? Follow rigidly without adapting? Their ecological cognitive behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage systematic content more healthily? Follow rules more flexibly? Navigate the ordered world with both discipline and adaptability?

**Integration path:** The world rewards cognitive intelligence NATURALLY. Players who follow rules access rule-gated content. Players who classify correctly unlock library-gated knowledge. Players who sequence properly complete liturgies. The reward is SYSTEMATIC BELONGING (being at home in an ordered world).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates cognitive intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — following, classifying, sequencing, and adapting within rule-systems without being prompted.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Opportunities to engage systematic content spontaneously.
- Healthy: Engages rules naturally; reads codes; follows systems
- Pathological: Over-engages ALL rules (rigid obsession); OR: ignores all systems (chaos)
- **Detection:** Quality of spontaneous systematic engagement

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Rule-gated content requiring cognitive assertion.
- Healthy: Applies rules to access gated content with confidence
- Pathological: Cannot assert rule-knowledge; avoids rule-gates
- **Detection:** Spontaneous rule-application rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** NPC interactions requiring shared rule-following.
- Healthy: Coordinates with NPCs through shared rules without dominating
- Pathological: Enforces rules on NPCs; OR: cannot coordinate through rules
- **Detection:** Quality of spontaneous rule-based social interaction

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Collaborative systematic spaces (shared libraries, group liturgies).
- Healthy: Engages shared systematic spaces naturally
- Pathological: Avoids shared systems; prefers solo rule-following
- **Detection:** Engagement rate in shared vs. solo systematic content

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar systematic content (same codes, same libraries).
- Healthy: Maintains systematic engagement in familiar territory
- Pathological: Systematic engagement fades with familiarity; rules become invisible
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in familiar vs. novel systematic content

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel systematic content (new codes, deeper classifications).
- Healthy: Engages novel systems with appropriate curiosity
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by novel systems; OR: treats them as routine
- **Detection:** Whether novel systematic content evokes appropriate response

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining systematic engagement over time.
- Healthy: Consistent systematic engagement across sessions
- Pathological: Systematic engagement degrades; world becomes unordered
- **Detection:** Systematic engagement consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether structured cognitive skills appear in free-navigation.
- Healthy: Rule-following, classification, sequencing appear naturally
- Pathological: Cognitive skills exist only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of structured cognitive skills to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Systematiser"
**How it surfaces:** OVER-ENGAGES all systematic content. Cannot pass a code without reading it. Follows every rule obsessively. The world is ONLY rules — no spontaneous exploration. Systematic engagement is compulsive.
- **Detection signals:** Reads every inscription; follows every rule encountered; cannot navigate freely; world is only systems; compulsive rule-checking; "what's the rule here?"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Rule-Breaker"
**How it surfaces:** IGNORES all systematic content. Walks past codes. Doesn't engage libraries. Cannot access rule-gated content. The ordered world is invisible — just terrain.
- **Detection signals:** Zero systematic engagement; walks past codes; ignores libraries; cannot access rule-gates; world has no order; purely impulsive navigation

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Theorist"
**How it surfaces:** Engages systematic content ONLY at meta-level. Talks about the system rather than following it. Seeks the "deeper structure" rather than mastering the concrete rules.
- **Detection signals:** Meta-engagement only; discusses systems abstractly; doesn't follow concrete rules; seeks "the principle behind the code"; cannot complete rule-gated tasks despite "understanding"

### Golden-Allergy: "The System-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** APPROACHES systematic content then RETREATS from complexity. Follows simple rules but avoids complex systems. Libraries are too much. Multi-step liturgies are abandoned.
- **Detection signals:** Engages simple rules; avoids complex systems; brief library visits; abandons multi-step sequences; "too complicated"; stays in simple-rule areas

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces RULE-FREE zones (areas where spontaneous exploration is rewarded)
- Codes that respond to PROPORTIONATE engagement (not obsessive checking)
- Natural consequence: proportionate engagement = deeper access; obsessive = flat response

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World makes systematic content NECESSARY for navigation (rule-gated paths)
- Codes that INVITE engagement (clear, simple, immediately rewarding)
- Natural consequence: rule-engagement = access; ignoring = stuck

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces content that requires CONCRETE rule-following (not meta-understanding)
- Systems that respond to execution, not theory
- Natural consequence: concrete mastery = access; abstract talk = nothing

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces GRADUAL complexity (simple rules that build toward systems)
- Complex content that INVITES without DEMANDING immediate mastery
- Natural consequence: gradual engagement = growing access; no punishment for partial engagement

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Amber-stage world saturated with systematic content. Codes inscribed on cathedral walls. Libraries with classification systems. Liturgies proceeding in sequence. Institutions with hierarchies and rules. Every zone has rule-governed content — opportunities to follow, classify, sequence, and adapt.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a rule-governed world
- **Spontaneous rule-engagement:** Noticing and following codes
- **Rule-gated content:** Paths/items requiring rule-knowledge
- **Library encounters:** Classification systems to engage
- **Liturgical participation:** Sequences to join and complete

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: rule-engagement → gated access, library knowledge, liturgical completion
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, stuck paths, incomplete world
- No explicit "cognitive score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Systematic content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Rule-gate complexity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Library depth scales with demonstrated classification skill
- Liturgical length scales with demonstrated sequencing
- Novel systems appear as capacity grows

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Rule-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice systematic content? |
| Rule-Following | 5-15 | Do they follow rules they encounter? |
| Rule-Applying | 15-30 | Can they apply rules to access gated content? |
| Rule-Adapting | 30-50 | Can they adapt when systems change? |
| The Living Systematiser | 50+ | Full ecological cognitive intelligence |

---

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Codes:** Rules inscribed in the world (to notice and follow)
- **Libraries:** Classification systems (to engage and use)
- **Liturgies:** Sequences (to join and complete)
- **Rule-gates:** Paths requiring rule-knowledge
- **Institutions:** Hierarchies with systematic rules

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ codes, 15+ libraries, 15+ liturgies, 10+ rule-gates, 10+ institutions

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Over-engages all rules → dark-addiction; ignores all systems → dark-allergy
- Meta-only engagement → golden-addiction; avoids complexity → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Systematic engagement rate:** How often does the player engage rule-governed content spontaneously?
- **Rule-following quality:** When they engage, do they follow accurately?
- **Classification engagement:** Do they use library systems?
- **Sequencing engagement:** Do they complete liturgies?
- **Transfer evidence:** Do structured cognitive skills appear in free-play?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium-High:** World generation, ecological cognitive assessment, NPC rule-interactions. Evaluates spontaneous systematic behaviour without explicit tasks.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage codes/rules); drag (classify in libraries); sequence-tap (liturgical participation)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, rule-following quality)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of systematic patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Rule-governed environment (systematic content throughout)
- Library system (classification challenges embedded in world)
- Liturgical system (sequences embedded in world events)
- Rule-gating system (paths requiring cognitive capacity)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium-High:** World generation, ecological assessment, NPC interactions within rule-systems
- Detects spontaneous systematic engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates cognitive intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive systematic content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; systematic engagement history; navigation patterns; rule-following records; classification quality; sequencing quality; adaptation metrics; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
