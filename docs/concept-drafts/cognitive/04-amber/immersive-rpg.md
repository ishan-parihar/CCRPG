# Cognitive / Amber — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes cognitive intelligence through SPONTANEOUS RULE-ENGAGEMENT in an open world — not structured cognitive tasks but free systematic behaviour. How does the player relate to rules, classifications, and sequences when nobody's asking "follow the rule"?
>
> **Why this axis for Cognitive/Amber:** The other 6 modalities probe cognitive capacity through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Amber, the world is RULE-GOVERNED — codes on walls, classification systems in libraries, sequences in liturgies, hierarchies in institutions. The player's spontaneous engagement with systematic content reveals genuine cognitive intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Ordered World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Amber-stage world saturated with rule-governed content. Codes inscribed on walls. Libraries with classification systems. Liturgies with sequences. Institutions with hierarchies. The player's spontaneous engagement with systematic content — how they navigate a rule-governed world without being prompted — reveals genuine cognitive intelligence.
- **Duration:** 5–15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Rule-Noticing → Rule-Following → Rule-Applying → Rule-Adapting → The Living Systematiser

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** A rule-governed open world where systematic content exists passively — codes inscribed on cathedral walls, libraries with classification systems, liturgies proceeding in sequence, institutions with hierarchies. Nobody says "follow the rule." The world simply IS ordered.

**How it differs from the other 6:** Other modalities present explicit cognitive challenges (puzzles, tasks, structured tests). This one embeds cognitive content ecologically — the player's NATURAL systematic engagement is the data. No prompts, no instructions, no "solve this."

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Whether cognitive intelligence is genuine (spontaneous) or performative (only under instruction). Reveals the player's ecological relationship to order, rules, classification, and sequence — their cognitive style in the wild.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates cognitive intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — following, classifying, sequencing, and adapting within rule-systems without being prompted. The reward is systematic belonging (being at home in an ordered world).

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Amber-stage world saturated with systematic content. Codes inscribed on cathedral walls. Libraries with classification systems. Liturgies proceeding in sequence. Institutions with hierarchies and rules. Every zone has rule-governed content — opportunities to follow, classify, sequence, and adapt.

### Interaction Phases
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a rule-governed world
- **Spontaneous rule-engagement:** Noticing and following codes
- **Rule-gated content:** Paths/items requiring rule-knowledge
- **Library encounters:** Classification systems to engage
- **Liturgical participation:** Sequences to join and complete

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: rule-engagement → gated access, library knowledge, liturgical completion
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, stuck paths, incomplete world
- No explicit "cognitive score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Systematic content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Rule-gate complexity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Library depth scales with demonstrated classification skill
- Liturgical length scales with demonstrated sequencing
- Novel systems appear as capacity grows

### Progression Table
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Rule-Noticing | 1–5 | Does the player notice systematic content? |
| Rule-Following | 5–15 | Do they follow rules they encounter? |
| Rule-Applying | 15–30 | Can they apply rules to access gated content? |
| Rule-Adapting | 30–50 | Can they adapt when systems change? |
| The Living Systematiser | 50+ | Full ecological cognitive intelligence |

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Inputs:** Navigation (exploration); tap (engage codes/rules); drag (classify in libraries); sequence-tap (liturgical participation)
- **Timing:** Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, rule-following quality). No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of systematic patterns.
- **NPC/AI:** Rule-governed environment with systematic content throughout; library system (classification challenges embedded in world); liturgical system (sequences in world events); rule-gating system (paths requiring cognitive capacity).
- **LLM level:** Medium-High — world generation, ecological assessment, NPC interactions within rule-systems. Detects spontaneous systematic engagement from behaviour patterns. Evaluates cognitive intelligence quality without explicit tasks. Generates responsive systematic content based on demonstrated capacity.
- **State persistence:** Full world state; systematic engagement history; navigation patterns; rule-following records; classification quality; sequencing quality; adaptation metrics; fatigue state; checkpoint position.
