# Cognitive / Amber — Module Specification

> **Line:** Cognitive (executive function, perspective-taking, reasoning)
> **Stage:** Amber / Mythic-Membership (concrete operational, rule-following, systematic classification)
> **Vibration:** The rule that holds — a system of symbols that RELATE to each other. Hold two things stable. Classify. Follow the code. The mind serves the order.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Cognitive intelligence at Amber is **concrete operational capacity** — the ability to follow rules, classify systematically, hold stable representations (n=2 consolidated), detect rule-changes, and reproduce spatial sequences. The birth of SYSTEMATIC THOUGHT: the first time the player can follow a multi-step rule, classify objects by category, and detect when a rule has changed.

### Core capacity dimensions

| Dimension | Definition | Assessment vehicle | Ceiling at this stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rule-following** | Identify and follow an explicit rule consistently | WCST-lite (low shift cadence); rule-gated encounters | Multi-criterion classification |
| **Stable holding** | Hold 2 items in working memory RELIABLY (n=2 consolidated) | n-back at n=2 with high accuracy threshold | n=2 perfect (>95%) |
| **Sequence reproduction** | Reproduce a spatial sequence of length 3 | Corsi block-tapping / sigil-tracing | Corsi-3 stable (>90%) |
| **Classification** | Sort items by a given rule (colour, shape, function) | Category-sorting tasks; hierarchical classification | Dual-criterion flexible sorting |
| **Rule-change detection** | Detect when a rule has CHANGED (early WCST) | Shift-detection after perseveration window | Within 3 trials of shift |

### What this is NOT

- **NOT Red:** Red is n=2 EMERGING, egocentric, impulsive. Amber is n=2 STABLE, rule-following, systematic. The player can now hold two things AND apply a rule to them consistently.
- **NOT Orange:** Orange is n=3, formal operational, hypothetical-deductive. Amber cannot yet reason about HYPOTHETICALS — only about concrete, present, rule-governed situations.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Systematiser"

**Core pattern:** Clings to rules as ABSOLUTE TRUTH. Cannot tolerate ambiguity, exception, or novelty. Perseverates on known classifications even when evidence contradicts them.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Enforces rules on others rigidly; "I know the correct way"
- Communion: Demands conformity; shared rules become mandatory
- Eros: Refuses new rules; clings to first-learned system; perseverates
- Agape: Cannot return to simpler rules; "the system is complete"

**Behavioural signatures:** >3 perseverative errors on WCST; continues old classification after shift; applies rules even when context makes them inappropriate; distress at ambiguity; treats exceptions as errors.

**Protects against:** The chaos of Red (impulsive, rule-less). Rigidity defends against the terror of having no structure.

**Atman Project defense:** Rigidification, systematisation, denial of novelty.

---

### Dark-Allergy: "The Rule-Breaker"

**Core pattern:** Refuses systematic thought entirely. Stays in egocentric Red reasoning — impulsive, rule-dismissive. Cannot or will not follow a multi-step procedure.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Uses rule-breaking as identity; "I'm too smart for rules"
- Communion: Cannot coordinate through shared rules
- Eros: Refuses to learn new systems; "boring"
- Agape: Cannot maintain any rule-based practice over time

**Behavioural signatures:** Ignores rules even when following them would succeed; cannot complete multi-step procedures; treats classification as pointless; below-chance accuracy; impulsive responses override systematic thought.

**Protects against:** The loss of Red autonomy. Rule-following feels like submission.

**Atman Project defense:** Rebellion, dismissal, counter-dependence.

---

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Theorist"

**Core pattern:** Rushes toward hypothetical-deductive reasoning (Orange) without mastering concrete operations. Performs formal logic without genuine rule-mastery. Talks about "systems thinking" while unable to follow a 3-step procedure reliably.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Claims independent reasoning while actually confused
- Communion: Dismisses "rule-followers" as beneath them
- Eros: Compulsive reaching toward abstraction; cannot rest in concrete
- Agape: Cannot embody abstract insights in concrete practice

**Behavioural signatures:** n=2 unstable but attempts n=3; Corsi-3 unreliable but attempts Corsi-4; uses abstract language for simple concrete situations; verbal sophistication exceeds operational capacity.

**Protects against:** The "boring" work of concrete mastery. Amber feels pedestrian.

**Atman Project defense:** Intellectualisation, abstraction, cognitive bypass.

---

### Golden-Allergy: "The System-Refuser"

**Core pattern:** Fears expansion into formal operations. Refuses abstraction. Comfortable with concrete rules but terrified of the meta-level. "I don't need to think about thinking."

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Cannot assert independent reasoning beyond the given system
- Communion: Clings to group's concrete rules; fears individual thought
- Eros: Refuses the call to abstract; "this is enough"
- Agape: Over-embodies concrete rules; makes them sacred/unchangeable

**Behavioural signatures:** Excellent rule-following but freezes when asked "why this rule?"; strong Corsi-3 and n=2 but avoids meta-cognition; anxious when rules are QUESTIONED (not just changed); avoids WCST shift-detection.

**Protects against:** The vertigo of formal operations — "If I can think about my thinking, who am I without the rules?"

**Atman Project defense:** Concretisation, literalism, anti-intellectualism.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Dark-domain healthy | Dark-domain pathological | Golden-domain healthy | Golden-domain pathological |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Applies rules independently; classifies without needing confirmation | Enforces rules rigidly on others; OR: cannot apply without authority | Asserts into novel reasoning with confidence | Cannot reason without group validation; waits for authority |
| **Communion** | Coordinates through shared rules; learns from tradition | Loses own reasoning in group consensus; OR: rejects all shared knowledge | Joins others at new cognitive level; learns collaboratively | Cannot learn from tradition; OR: fuses with group-think |
| **Eros** | Rests in current mastery with satisfaction; curious without compulsion | Refuses new rules; perseverates; OR: compulsive growth without integration | Reaches toward abstraction genuinely; engages next stage | Rushes past mastery (bypass); OR: refuses growth entirely (Jonah) |
| **Agape** | Returns to basics without shame; maintains mastery with care | Cannot return ("too advanced"); OR: regresses into simplicity as avoidance | Embodies new capacity in concrete practice | Cannot embody; stays abstract; OR: smothers emergent with premature inclusion |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → dark shadows)

**For Dark-Addiction (Rigid Systematiser):**
- Present context-dependent rules (same rule, different outcomes by context)
- Reward flexible application; introduce "the rule served you; it's not absolute" framing
- Build: "I can follow rules AND know they're tools, not truths"

**For Dark-Allergy (Rule-Breaker):**
- Present rules as CHOSEN (not imposed); make rule-following feel like power
- Reward rule-following that leads to desired outcomes (instrumental value)
- Build: "I can follow rules without losing myself"

### Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → golden shadows)

**For Golden-Addiction (Premature Theorist):**
- Lock higher difficulty until current level mastered (>90% required)
- Reward concrete execution (not abstract understanding)
- Build: "I can rest in concrete operations; they're not beneath me"

**For Golden-Allergy (System-Refuser):**
- Introduce gentle meta-cognitive tasks (notice the rule, don't change it)
- Reward noticing without requiring action; "thinking about thinking is natural"
- Build: "I can examine rules without losing them"

### Integration criteria

The player demonstrates: flexible rule-following (consistent BUT adaptable); n=2 stable without rigidity; classification with re-sort capacity; rule-awareness without terror; concrete mastery with openness to abstraction.

## 5. Scoring Parameters

### Theta model
- **Half-life:** 16 days
- **Max decay:** 18% of peak theta
- **Composite weights:** rule-following 0.25, stable-holding 0.25, sequence-reproduction 0.20, classification 0.15, rule-change-detection 0.15

### Stage-transition thresholds
- **Amber → Orange:** Theta ≥ 0.7 on stable-holding (n=2 perfect) AND rule-change-detection ≥ 0.6; confirmed across ≥ 4 sessions; ≥ 2 other lines at Amber
- **Regression toward Red:** stable-holding < 0.4 OR rule-following < 0.5 across 3+ sessions

### Drive-health weight: 0.30
### Shadow-drag coefficient: 0.20
### Cross-line bonus cap: +0.15 (from Willpower + Somatic)

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### Progressive revelation order
1. **Rule-following** → Dark-allergy surfaces first (refusal is immediately visible)
2. **Stable-holding** → Dark-addiction surfaces (perseveration becomes visible)
3. **Classification** → Dark-addiction deepens (rigid classification)
4. **Rule-change-detection** → Golden-allergy surfaces (avoidance of change)
5. **Sequence-reproduction** → Golden-addiction surfaces (attempting beyond capacity)

### Per-modality surfacing

| Modality | Dark-Addiction signal | Dark-Allergy signal | Golden-Addiction signal | Golden-Allergy signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | Perseverates on WCST | Ignores rules; random | Attempts n=3 with unstable n=2 | Avoids shift-detection |
| Language-reflective | Rigid absolute language | Cannot articulate any rule | Abstract meta-language without backing | Refuses to discuss rule-basis |
| Scenario-choice | Applies rule rigidly regardless of context | Ignores rule; impulsive choice | Hypothetical reasoning on concrete dilemma | Refuses alternatives to given rule |
| Embodied-somatic | Rigid body-form; cannot adapt | Refuses disciplined movement | Attempts complex sequences beyond capacity | Freezes when pattern must change |
| Strategic-planning | Over-plans rigidly; cannot adapt | Cannot plan; impulsive execution | Plans at Orange complexity | Refuses to plan beyond immediate step |
| Social-cooperative | Enforces rules on group | Cannot coordinate through shared rules | Claims meta-understanding while group fails | Defers all reasoning to group |
| Immersive-rpg | Follows world-rules obsessively | Ignores world-rules; chaotic | Attempts Orange-level reasoning | Stays in known areas; refuses exploration |

### Cross-validation rules
- Deterministic high + Language-reflective low = implicit capacity without metacognition (normal early)
- Language-reflective high + Deterministic low = verbal performance without capacity (golden-addiction)
- Deterministic high + Immersive-rpg low = compartmentalised capacity (no ecological transfer)
- Social-cooperative high + Deterministic low = group-dependent capacity (communion-addiction)
