# Cognitive / Red — Shadow Diagnostics

> **Purpose:** Define the complete shadow landscape for Cognitive/Red. This is the ANCHOR document — all 7 game concepts reference this to know what shadows they must surface and what resolution looks like.
>
> **The vibration:** Egocentric strategic cognition. The mind as weapon. Short-horizon concrete planning in service of the power-self.

---

## 1. The Four Shadow Archetypes at This Vibration

### 1.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Compulsive Strategist"

**Core pattern:** The player CLINGS to cognitive control. Thinking becomes a defence against vulnerability. Every situation must be "figured out" before engagement. The mind never rests.

**How it manifests in gameplay:**
- Excessive planning time on simple tasks (over-analysis)
- Refusal to act without complete information
- Using cognitive tasks as avoidance of emotional/somatic/relational challenges
- Treating uncertainty as threat rather than invitation
- Compulsive pattern-seeking even in random stimuli
- Cannot "just do" — must always "think first"

**Drive dynamics when this shadow is active:**
| Drive | Expression |
|---|---|
| Agency | Hyper-sovereign — refuses all input, must figure it out ALONE |
| Communion | Collapsed — cannot share cognitive process, hoards strategy |
| Eros | Compulsive — driven to "solve" everything, cannot rest in not-knowing |
| Agape | Absent — cannot return to simplicity, always complexifying |

**Developmental origin:** The child who learned that thinking = safety. If I can predict, I won't be hurt. Cognition as armour against a chaotic Red-stage environment where power was unpredictable.

**What resolution looks like:**
- Can engage tasks without pre-planning when appropriate
- Tolerates not-knowing without anxiety
- Can act on intuition when the situation calls for it
- Cognitive effort is proportional to actual demands
- Can rest the mind — silence is not threatening

---

### 1.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Anti-Intellectual Warrior"

**Core pattern:** The player REJECTS cognitive engagement. Thinking is dismissed as weakness, cowardice, or irrelevance. Pure action without reflection. The mind is an enemy.

**How it manifests in gameplay:**
- Skipping/abandoning cognitive tasks
- Impulsive responses on tasks that reward planning
- Dismissing strategy as "overthinking"
- Preferring brute-force approaches even when they fail repeatedly
- Anger or frustration when cognitive challenges are presented
- "Just let me fight" — avoidance of all planning mechanics

**Drive dynamics when this shadow is active:**
| Drive | Expression |
|---|---|
| Agency | Impulsive — acts without thought, confuses impulse with sovereignty |
| Communion | Absent — cannot join in collaborative thinking, dismisses it |
| Eros | Blocked — refuses the upward pull toward cognitive development |
| Agape | Distorted — "returns" to pre-cognitive action as if it were wisdom |

**Developmental origin:** The child who learned that thinking = paralysis. In a Red-stage environment where action was rewarded and hesitation punished, cognition became associated with weakness. "Thinkers get beaten; doers survive."

**What resolution looks like:**
- Engages cognitive tasks without avoidance or contempt
- Can sustain attention on a planning problem
- Acknowledges that thinking serves power (not opposes it)
- Chooses to plan when planning would help
- Integrates thought and action — strategy enhances rather than replaces doing

---

### 1.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Abstractionist"

**Core pattern:** The player BYPASSES concrete-operational mastery to perform formal-operational thinking they haven't earned. They reach for complexity without foundation. Intellectual performance without genuine capacity.

**How it manifests in gameplay:**
- Attempting n=3 before n=2 is stable
- Using abstract language without concrete grounding
- Skipping practice/mastery phases to reach "harder" content
- Performing understanding they don't have
- Dismissing current-level challenges as "too easy" while failing them
- Intellectual posturing — complexity as identity rather than capacity

**Drive dynamics when this shadow is active:**
| Drive | Expression |
|---|---|
| Agency | Performative — asserts capacity they don't have, cannot admit limits |
| Communion | Distorted — joins "smart" groups to perform belonging at higher level |
| Eros | Compulsive upward — reaches without grounding, bypasses integration |
| Agape | Absent — refuses to embody current capacity, always "above" it |

**Developmental origin:** The child who was rewarded for appearing smart rather than being competent. In a Red-stage environment where cognitive performance = status, the appearance of intelligence became more important than its reality. "Seem smart" > "be capable."

**What resolution looks like:**
- Engages current-level challenges fully before reaching for harder ones
- Demonstrates concrete mastery before attempting abstraction
- Can articulate reasoning in concrete terms (not just abstract)
- Growth is genuine — performance matches aspiration
- Comfortable being a beginner at the next level (doesn't need to perform mastery)

---

### 1.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Growth-Refuser"

**Core pattern:** The player REFUSES cognitive development. They actively avoid the growth edge. Comfort becomes a fortress against the discomfort of not-yet-knowing. "I'm fine where I am" as defensive posture.

**How it manifests in gameplay:**
- Staying at comfortable difficulty indefinitely
- Actively avoiding harder challenges when presented
- Anxiety or withdrawal when complexity increases
- "I don't want to think harder" — explicit refusal
- Choosing repetitive familiar tasks over novel ones
- Treating the growth edge as threatening rather than inviting

**Drive dynamics when this shadow is active:**
| Drive | Expression |
|---|---|
| Agency | Contracted — cannot assert toward the unknown, only toward the familiar |
| Communion | Distorted — joins others in comfortable stagnation, mutual reinforcement of "enough" |
| Eros | Blocked — the upward pull is experienced as threat, not invitation |
| Agape | Hyper-active — "returning" to comfort disguised as wisdom, but actually fear |

**Developmental origin:** The child who was overwhelmed by cognitive demands. In a Red-stage environment where failure was punished and complexity was forced, the growth edge became associated with pain. "Growing hurts; staying is safe."

**What resolution looks like:**
- Willingly engages growth-edge challenges
- Tolerates the discomfort of not-yet-knowing
- Can sit with cognitive difficulty without fleeing
- Approaches new complexity with curiosity rather than dread
- Self-selects harder challenges occasionally (intrinsic motivation to grow)

## 3. Shadow Detection Signals

### 3.1 Behavioural signals (observable in gameplay)

| Signal | What it indicates | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Planning latency on easy tasks** | Dark-addiction (over-thinking) | > 2× expected RT on tasks below current level |
| **Task avoidance rate** | Dark-allergy (refusal) | > 30% skip/abandon rate on cognitive tasks |
| **Difficulty self-selection gap** | Golden-addiction (bypass) | Attempts > 2 levels above stable performance |
| **Growth-edge avoidance** | Golden-allergy (refusal) | > 5 consecutive sessions without attempting harder material |
| **Help rejection rate** | Agency dark pathology | Rejects > 80% of offered hints/scaffolding |
| **Strategy abandonment in social context** | Communion dark pathology | Changes approach > 60% of the time when others present |
| **Checkpoint rushing** | Eros dark pathology | Advances before meeting stability criteria |
| **Below-level dismissal** | Agape dark pathology | Performance drops > 40% on "easy" tasks (phoning it in) |

### 3.2 Pattern signals (emerge over multiple sessions)

| Pattern | Sessions needed | What it indicates |
|---|---|---|
| **Consistent over-planning** | 3+ sessions | Dark-addiction stabilising |
| **Consistent avoidance** | 3+ sessions | Dark-allergy stabilising |
| **Repeated premature advancement** | 3+ sessions | Golden-addiction stabilising |
| **Plateau without attempt** | 5+ sessions | Golden-allergy stabilising |
| **Cognitive-only engagement** | 5+ sessions | Cross-module: using cognition to avoid other lines |
| **Action-only engagement** | 5+ sessions | Cross-module: using action to avoid cognition |

### 3.3 Compound shadow detection

| Compound pattern | Signals | Cross-module involvement |
|---|---|---|
| "Thinks instead of feeling" | High cognitive engagement + emotional task avoidance | Cognitive/Red dark-addiction + Emotional/Red dark-allergy |
| "Brute force" | Cognitive avoidance + somatic over-engagement | Cognitive/Red dark-allergy + Somatic/Red dark-addiction |
| "Premature philosopher" | Abstract performance + concrete failure | Cognitive/Red golden-addiction + Intrapersonal/Red golden-addiction |
| "Comfortable warrior" | Growth refusal across cognitive + volitional | Cognitive/Red golden-allergy + Willpower/Red golden-allergy |
| "Lonely genius" | High cognitive + interpersonal avoidance | Cognitive/Red dark-addiction (agency pole) + Interpersonal/Red dark-allergy |

## 4. The Heal/Evolve Vectors at This Module

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (bottom-up): Agape + Agency integrate dark shadows

**For Dark-Addiction (Compulsive Strategist):**
- **Agape:** Return to simplicity. Present tasks that are genuinely simple and reward simple engagement. Let the player experience that NOT thinking is sometimes the right move.
- **Agency:** Sovereign choice to NOT plan. The game presents situations where acting without strategy is the powerful choice. Reframe impulsivity as sometimes-wisdom.

**For Dark-Allergy (Anti-Intellectual Warrior):**
- **Agape:** Return to the body-mind connection. Present cognitive tasks embedded in physical action (not separate from it). Thinking AS part of fighting, not instead of it.
- **Agency:** Sovereign choice TO think. The game presents situations where planning is clearly the power move. Reframe strategy as a weapon, not a weakness.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (top-down): Eros + Communion dissolve golden shadows

**For Golden-Addiction (Premature Abstractionist):**
- **Eros:** Genuine aspiration toward EARNED complexity. Present the beauty of genuine mastery (not performed mastery). Make real n=2 excellence more attractive than fake n=3.
- **Communion:** Join with others who are genuinely at the growth edge. Collaborative challenges where performance is transparent — you can't fake it in partnership.

**For Golden-Allergy (Growth-Refuser):**
- **Eros:** Gentle invitation toward complexity. Present harder material as OPTIONAL, genuinely attractive, and non-threatening. Make the growth edge feel like adventure, not demand.
- **Communion:** Join with others who are growing. Social context where growth is normalised and celebrated. "We're all learning together" dissolves the isolation of fear.

## 5. Resolution Criteria (When Is This Shadow "Healed"?)

### 5.1 Per-quadrant resolution thresholds

| Quadrant | Resolved when... | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark-Addiction** | Planning latency normalises; can act without pre-analysis; cognitive effort proportional to demands | Latency ratio < 1.3× on easy tasks for 5+ sessions |
| **Dark-Allergy** | Avoidance rate drops; engages cognitive tasks willingly; integrates thinking with action | Skip rate < 10% for 5+ sessions |
| **Golden-Addiction** | Self-selected difficulty matches stable performance; concrete mastery demonstrated before advancement | Difficulty gap < 1 level for 5+ sessions |
| **Golden-Allergy** | Self-selects harder challenges; tolerates growth-edge discomfort; approaches novelty with curiosity | Attempts harder material in 3+ of last 5 sessions |

### 5.2 Module-level shadow resolution

The module's shadow is considered RESOLVED when:
1. All 4 quadrant severities are below 0.2 for 10+ consecutive sessions
2. No compound shadow patterns are active involving this module
3. Drive-health scores are all above 0.7
4. The player demonstrates capacity transfer (uses cognitive planning in free-play contexts)

### 5.3 What "maintenance mode" looks like after resolution

Once resolved, the module enters maintenance mode:
- Periodic catalyst presentation (every 3-5 sessions) to confirm health
- Theta-decay monitoring (if engagement drops, shadow can re-emerge)
- Compound pattern monitoring (other modules' shadows can re-activate this one)
- The game presents Cognitive/Red challenges in service of OTHER modules (e.g., cognitive planning as part of a Moral/Red dilemma)

## 6. The Shadow Surfacing Mechanics (How Games Must Present These)

Each of the 7 game concepts must create conditions where shadows CAN surface. This section specifies what those conditions are:

### 6.1 Conditions that surface Dark-Addiction

- Present a task that is genuinely simple but allow unlimited planning time
- Observe: does the player over-plan? Do they take 30 seconds on a 3-second task?
- Present a "just do it" challenge with no planning phase
- Observe: can they engage without strategy? Or do they freeze?

### 6.2 Conditions that surface Dark-Allergy

- Present a cognitive challenge after a series of action/somatic tasks
- Observe: do they engage or avoid? Do they express frustration?
- Present a situation where planning is clearly the optimal strategy
- Observe: do they plan or brute-force?

### 6.3 Conditions that surface Golden-Addiction

- Present current-level mastery challenges alongside harder optional ones
- Observe: do they master current before reaching? Or skip to hard?
- Present a challenge that requires concrete demonstration (not just abstract claim)
- Observe: can they DO it, or only TALK about doing it?

### 6.4 Conditions that surface Golden-Allergy

- Present optional harder challenges with no penalty for attempting
- Observe: do they attempt? Or stay in comfort?
- Gradually increase difficulty and observe the response
- Observe: at what point do they withdraw? What's the emotional quality of withdrawal?
