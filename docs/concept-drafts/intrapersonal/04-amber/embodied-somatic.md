# Intrapersonal / Amber — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as the site of role-identity — how the body holds the role, posture-as-identity, embodied self-concept within the code.
> **Why this axis for this module:** Role-identity that is genuinely integrated lives in the body. The guardian's posture is different from the healer's. The body BECOMES the role. This modality reveals whether role-identity is embodied or merely conceptual.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body of the Role"
- **Core mechanic:** The player enacts role-identity through physical posture, movement quality, and body-awareness. The game measures whether the body holds the role naturally or whether role-identity is disconnected from physical self-concept.
- **Duration:** 4-8 minutes per session
- **Internal progression:** Body Scan → Role Posture → The Body's Identity → Identity Under Load → The Embodied Self

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The SOMATIC dimension of role-identity — whether the self-as-role is genuinely embodied. A player who identifies as "the guardian" cognitively but whose body doesn't hold guardian-posture has a mind-body identity split.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike deterministic (cognitive identity-consistency), this modality measures whether role-identity is GROUNDED IN THE BODY. Unlike scenario-choice (decision quality), this modality measures the physical texture of being-the-role.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** The role-prisoner's body is rigid in role-posture (cannot relax out of it). The role-refuser's body refuses role-posture. The premature individualist's body performs "authentic movement" without role-grounding. The identity-clinger's body holds known role-posture but freezes at novel identity demands.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates embodied role-identity — the body holds the role naturally, transitions between role-demands fluidly, and can rest without losing the role.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Body of the Role: a somatic-interface game where the player enacts role-identity through physical tasks. The interface presents role-posture challenges and measures body-identity alignment. Aesthetic: Amber-stage embodiment — the knight's stance, the healer's hands, the body as vessel of duty.

### Interaction
- **Body Scan (1-5):** "How does your body hold your role right now? What posture does your role require?" Baseline body-identity awareness.
- **Role Posture (5-15):** "Take the guardian's stance. Hold it." Sustained role-posture enactment. Tests whether the body can become the role.
- **The Body's Identity (15-30):** "Your role changes. From guardian to healer. How does your body change?" Tests role-transition fluidity.
- **Identity Under Load (30-50):** "Hold your role-posture while distracted. The body must remember who you are." Tests embodied identity-stability.
- **The Embodied Self (50+):** Full integration: role-posture + transition + stability + rest simultaneously.

### Feedback
- Natural role-posture → "The body knows the role. The guardian stands as a guardian stands. The identity lives in the flesh."
- Rigid role-posture → "The body holds the role. But it cannot rest. Can you be the guardian AND breathe?"
- Refused role-posture → "The body did not take the role. The posture was available. Was this a choice or an absence?"
- Performed posture → "The body moved as if taking the role. But was the identity there? The body cannot lie."
- Novel role-posture → "A new role is offered. Your body knows the old one. Can it learn the new?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Posture complexity: simple stance → sustained → under load
- Role-transition speed: slow → moderate → rapid
- Distraction intensity: mild → moderate → strong
- Novel role frequency: rare → occasional → frequent

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Body Scan | 1-5 | Baseline body-identity awareness |
| Role Posture | 5-15 | Sustained role-posture enactment |
| The Body's Identity | 15-30 | Role-transition fluidity |

| The Embodied Self | 50+ | Full somatic role-identity integration |

- **Input types:** Hold (posture maintenance); tap (transition); accelerometer (posture detection if available); slider (identity-intensity rating)

- **NPC/AI:** Adaptive posture complexity; transition-speed calibration; distraction-intensity scaling; shadow-signal accumulator
- **LLM:** None — fully somatic scoring
- **State persistence:** Full posture history; transition quality; stability patterns; cross-session somatic tracking; drive/shadow signal accumulation; checkpoint position
