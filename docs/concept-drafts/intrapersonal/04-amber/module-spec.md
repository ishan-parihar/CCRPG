# Intrapersonal / Amber — Module Specification

> **Line:** Intrapersonal  **Stage:** Amber / Mythic-Membership  **Vibration:** The self crystallises as its role within the code — identity given, not chosen.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Role-identity: the capacity to locate the self within a larger order and derive stable identity from one's assigned or earned place. The player experiences selfhood as inseparable from duty, title, and group-membership. Unlike Red's "I am my will" or Magenta's "I am the chosen one," Amber intrapersonal says "I am the guardian. I am the guild member. I am my duty." The self is not yet reflective (Orange) but is stable, code-bound, and justified by belonging.

---

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction (DA): The Role-Prisoner
Cannot exist outside the role. Identity IS the role absolutely — remove the title and the self collapses. No personal identity beneath the assignment. Terrified of role-loss because it equals self-death.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy (DAll): The Role-Refuser
Refuses role-identity entirely. Remains stuck in Red ego-identity ("I am my will, I bow to no code"). Cannot accept that the self has a legitimate place in a larger order. Experiences role-assignment as annihilation.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction (GA): The Premature Individualist
Performs Orange-level reflective self-examination without genuine Amber role-identity underneath. Claims "I am more than my role" before ever genuinely BEING the role. Spiritual bypassing of the conformist stage.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy (GAll): The Identity-Clinger
Role-identity is present and healthy but refuses the call to self-examination (Orange). Terrified of asking "who am I beyond my role?" Clings to the code as permanent identity rather than developmental stage.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadow Integration)
DA: Agape returns the player to the self BENEATH the role; Agency acts from that self. "I serve the code AND I exist without it."
DAll: Agape accepts the gift of belonging; Agency chooses the role rather than being consumed by it. "I can choose a place without losing my will."

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadow Dissolution)
GA: Eros reaches toward genuine role-embodiment first; Communion joins the code authentically before transcending it. "I must BE the role before I can be more."
GAll: Eros reaches toward the self-beyond-role; Communion shares the vulnerability of identity-questioning. "Who I am beyond my role is safe to explore."

### 4.3 Integration Criteria
- Player can name their role AND name themselves apart from it
- Role-loss triggers grief but not identity-collapse
- Role-acceptance feels chosen, not imposed
- Self-examination begins without role-abandonment

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Frozen Conformist (Intrapersonal DA × Interpersonal DA)
Rigid role-identity fused with rigid group-belonging. Cannot think independently — the self is the role is the group. Intervention: separate role-identity from group-identity through solo role-exercises.

### 6.2 The Hollow Soldier (Intrapersonal DA × Willpower DA)
Role-identity fused with duty-will. Self-punishment through role-obligation — "I must endure because I am the guardian." Intervention: introduce role-rest and duty-boundaries.

### 6.3 The Spiritual Bypasser (Intrapersonal GA × Spiritual GA)
Premature individualism fused with premature transcendence. No grounded identity at all — floats above without ever landing. Intervention: ground in concrete role-tasks before any self-inquiry.

### 6.4 The Emotional Stranger (Intrapersonal DAll × Emotional DAll)
Refuses role-identity AND refuses role-affect. Disconnected from entire interior — neither knows who they are nor what they feel. Intervention: pair identity-exercises with affect-labelling.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation
Shadows surface in order: DAll first (most visible — refusal is overt), DA second (fusion is subtler), GAll third (clinging emerges under growth-pressure), GA last (bypassing requires sustained observation).

### 7.2 Per-Modality Surfacing Table

| Modality | Primary Shadow Surfaced | Secondary | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DA / DAll | GAll | Role-consistency under pressure reveals fusion or refusal |
| Language-Reflective | GA / GAll | DA | How player narrates identity reveals bypassing or clinging |
| Scenario-Choice | DAll / GAll | GA | Dilemmas force role vs self confrontation |
| Embodied-Somatic | DA | DAll | Body reveals role-fusion or role-rejection somatically |
| Strategic-Planning | GAll / DA | GA | Planning reveals rigidity or premature transcendence |
| Social-Cooperative | DA / DAll | GAll | Group context amplifies fusion or refusal |
| Immersive-RPG | All four | — | Free-play reveals dominant shadow ecology |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation
No shadow is diagnosed from a single modality. Minimum 3 modalities must converge before shadow-state is confirmed. Compound shadows require convergence with the paired line's module data.
