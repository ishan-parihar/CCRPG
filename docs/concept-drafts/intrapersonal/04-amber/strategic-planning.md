# Intrapersonal / Amber — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** Planning identity-maintenance — how the player structures role-consistent behaviour across time, manages competing role-demands, and sustains identity coherence.
> **Why this axis for this module:** Role-identity at Amber requires planning — knowing how to maintain role-consistency across contexts, manage competing role-demands, and sustain identity coherence over time. This modality reveals whether the player can organise their identity-life within the code.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Role's Calendar"
- **Core mechanic:** The player plans and manages role-consistent behaviour across a multi-step scenario. Each step requires role-appropriate action; the player must sequence, sustain, and transition between role-demands while maintaining identity coherence.
- **Duration:** 5-10 minutes per session
- **Internal progression:** The Role Brief → The Identity Map → The Execution → The Role Conflict → The Calendar Complete

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The PLANNING dimension of role-identity — how the player structures identity-maintenance across time. Unlike deterministic (raw identity-consistency), this modality measures whether the player can ORGANISE their role-life effectively.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike scenario-choice (single-decision quality), this modality measures multi-step identity planning. Unlike embodied-somatic (physical identity), this modality measures cognitive structuring of role-demands.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Golden-addiction (elaborate identity-planning without underlying role-identity), dark-allergy (cannot plan role-maintenance; impulsive), and the quality of the player's relationship to identity-structure.

**Successful integration:** The player plans role-maintenance effectively — sequencing role-demands clearly, transitioning appropriately, adapting when conflicts arise — without using planning to bypass genuine role-identity.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Role's Calendar: a planning interface where the player receives a role-demand brief and must plan role-consistent behaviour across multiple contexts. Aesthetic: Amber-stage order — duty-rosters, role-calendars, the systematic structure of identity-maintenance.

### Interaction
- **The Role Brief (1-5):** Simple 3-context scenario. "As guardian: morning watch, midday training, evening ceremony." Tests basic role-consistency planning.
- **The Identity Map (5-15):** Player plans role-consistent behaviour across contexts. "What does the guardian do in each context? How do you maintain the role?"
- **The Execution (15-30):** Execute the planned role-behaviour. Tests whether the identity-plan holds under execution pressure.
- **The Role Conflict (30-50):** Mid-plan role-conflict. "A competing role-demand arises. Your plan must adapt. But the identity remains."
- **The Calendar Complete (50+):** Full integration: planning + execution + adaptation + sustained role-identity simultaneously.

### Feedback
- Effective identity-planning → "The role is mapped across contexts. Each demand is clear. The identity holds."
- Over-planning → "The identity-plan is elaborate. But the first context is simple. Can you be the guardian without the full plan?"
- No planning → "The contexts begin without identity-preparation. The role has demands. Without structure, how will you hold them?"
- Plan-as-bypass → "You planned the optimal identity-expression. But the role gave you a duty, not an optimisation problem."
- Adaptive identity → "The conflict arose. The plan changed. The role held. This is mastery."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Context count: 3 → 5 → 8
- Role complexity: single role → dual role → role-hierarchy
- Conflict frequency: none → occasional → frequent
- Transition speed: slow → moderate → rapid

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Role Brief | 1-5 | Simple 3-context role-consistency |
| The Identity Map | 5-15 | Planning within the role |
| The Execution | 15-30 | Plan-execution under pressure |
| The Role Conflict | 30-50 | Adaptive identity-maintenance |
| The Calendar Complete | 50+ | Full planning-identity integration |

## 4. Item Pool

- **Role briefs:** Multi-context role-demand sequences — 25+
- **Identity-map tasks:** Role-consistency planning challenges — 20+
- **Execution tasks:** Role-plan execution under varying pressure — 20+
- **Role-conflict scenarios:** Mid-plan identity conflicts — 15+
- **Transition tasks:** Role-transition planning — 10+
