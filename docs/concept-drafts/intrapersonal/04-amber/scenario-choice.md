# Intrapersonal / Amber — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Identity dilemmas where role conflicts with personal desire or competing roles — reveals all 4 shadows through choice patterns under identity pressure.
> **Why this axis for this module:** Role-identity is revealed most clearly when the role's demands conflict with personal desire. The role-prisoner always chooses the role. The role-refuser always chooses personal desire. The premature individualist chooses "authentic self-expression." The identity-clinger chooses the role but refuses to examine why.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Mirror's Choice"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces branching scenarios where their role-identity conflicts with personal desires, competing roles, or the call to self-examination. Choices reveal the structure of their role-identity.
- **Duration:** 4-10 minutes per session
- **Internal progression:** The Simple Role → The Competing Identity → The Costly Role → The Self Questioned → The Mirror's Integration

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The DECISION dimension of role-identity — how the player chooses when role and personal desire conflict. Unlike deterministic (raw identity-consistency), this modality measures the QUALITY of identity-choice under pressure.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike language-reflective (verbal articulation), this modality measures actual choice behaviour. A player who articulates role-identity beautifully but chooses personal desire here has a language-behaviour gap.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** All 4 shadow quadrants through choice patterns. The role-prisoner always chooses the role regardless of cost. The role-refuser always chooses personal desire. The premature individualist chooses "authentic self-expression." The identity-clinger chooses the role but refuses to examine it.

**Successful integration:** The player makes contextually appropriate identity choices — inhabiting the role when appropriate, acknowledging personal desire when appropriate, examining the role when appropriate.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Mirror's Choice: a narrative interface presenting identity conflicts within an Amber-stage world. Each scenario has 3-4 choices representing different identity responses. Aesthetic: Amber-stage world — mirrors, role-ceremonies, the weight of identity-as-duty.

### Interaction
- **The Simple Role (1-5):** Clear role-demand, clear personal desire. "Your role requires you to stand guard. You want to explore. What do you do?"
- **The Competing Identity (5-15):** Two roles conflict. "You are both a guild member and a family member. Both roles demand you now."
- **The Costly Role (15-30):** Maintaining the role costs significantly. "Being the guardian means giving up the adventure. What do you do?"
- **The Self Questioned (30-50):** The role itself is challenged. "Your elder asks: who are you beneath the role? Do you examine it?"
- **The Mirror's Integration (50+):** Full integration: competing roles + cost + examination + sovereign choice simultaneously.

### Feedback
- Role chosen with awareness → "You are the guardian. And you know it is a choice. The mirror reflects both the role and the self."
- Role chosen compulsively → "You are the guardian. But was this a choice? The mirror asks: can you be the role AND choose it?"
- Personal desire chosen → "You chose yourself over the role. Notice: was this sovereign or was this avoidance of the role?"
- Premature individualism → "You chose 'authentic self-expression.' But do you know the role you're transcending? The mirror asks for the role first."
- Self-examination → "You looked beneath the role. This is not betrayal — this is depth. The mirror shows more when you look."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Conflict intensity: mild → moderate → severe → identity-level
- Role complexity: single → competing → multi-layered
- Examination depth: gentle → direct → fundamental
- Choice consequences: immediate → delayed → cross-session

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Simple Role | 1-5 | Single role-demand vs. personal desire |
| The Competing Identity | 5-15 | Two roles in conflict |
| The Costly Role | 15-30 | Role-maintenance under significant cost |
| The Self Questioned | 30-50 | Role-identity examined |
| The Mirror's Integration | 50+ | Full role-identity integration under maximum pressure |

---

## 4. Item Pool

- **Simple role scenarios:** Single role-demand vs. personal desire — 25+
- **Competing identity scenarios:** Two roles in conflict — 20+
- **Costly role scenarios:** Role-maintenance under sacrifice — 20+
- **Self-examination scenarios:** Role-identity challenged — 15+
- **Integration scenarios:** Multi-dimensional identity conflicts — 10+

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input types:** Tap (choice selection); hold (deliberation); swipe (scenario navigation)
- **Timing:** Choice latency tracked; deliberation time measured; avoidance patterns detected
- **NPC/AI:** Adaptive scenario selection based on shadow-signal accumulation; consequence-delivery; cross-session narrative continuity
- **LLM:** Consequence narration; scenario adaptation; choice-quality assessment
- **State persistence:** Full choice history; shadow-pattern tracking; drive-health signal accumulation; cross-session narrative coherence; identity-conflict history; checkpoint position
