# Intrapersonal / Amber — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological role-identity — whether role-identity appears spontaneously in free-play, without structured prompts or explicit identity tasks.
> **Why this axis for this module:** The deepest test of role-identity is whether it appears SPONTANEOUSLY in unstructured contexts. A player who maintains role-identity in structured tasks but drops it in free-play has compartmentalised capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Self in the World"
- **Core mechanic:** The player inhabits a role within an Amber-stage world and navigates a free-play environment where identity-relevant situations arise naturally. No explicit identity tasks — the player's role-identity is revealed through spontaneous behaviour, role-consistent choices, and how they handle identity challenges that arise naturally.
- **Duration:** 8-20 minutes per session (open-ended; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Taking the Role → The World's Identity Demands → The Self Tested → The Role Examined → The Self in the World

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The ECOLOGICAL dimension of role-identity — whether the self-as-role appears spontaneously in unstructured contexts. Unlike all other modalities (which present explicit identity challenges), this modality observes the player's natural identity-behaviour.

**How this differs from other modalities:** No explicit identity challenges. The player simply inhabits the world. Their role-identity (or lack of it) emerges naturally through spontaneous choices and behaviour.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Whether role-identity has genuinely transferred to lived behaviour. The role-prisoner will find identity-demands everywhere. The role-refuser will ignore role-cues spontaneously. The premature individualist will perform "authentic self-expression." The identity-clinger will stay in known identity-territory.

**Successful integration:** The player inhabits their role naturally — maintaining identity when it arises, resting when appropriate, adapting when the world demands it — without either compulsive role-performance or spontaneous identity-abandonment.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Self in the World: an open-world RPG environment set in an Amber-stage world. The player has a role with associated identity-demands. The world presents situations that naturally invoke role-identity — without labelling them as "identity challenges." Aesthetic: Amber-stage world — guild structures, role-ceremonies, the lived texture of being-someone-in-a-group.

### Interaction
- **Taking the Role (1-5):** Player selects a role. "As a guardian, you are: vigilant, loyal, duty-bound." No explicit tasks — just the role.
- **The World's Identity Demands (5-15):** The world presents situations that invoke role-identity. Someone asks "who are you?" A situation requires role-appropriate response. The player responds spontaneously.
- **The Self Tested (15-30):** The world presents situations where role-identity is tested. A temptation to act out-of-role. A challenge to the identity. The player's spontaneous response is observed.
- **The Role Examined (30-50):** The world presents situations where the role itself is questioned. "Is this who you really are?" The player's spontaneous response to identity-examination is observed.
- **The Self in the World (50+):** Full integration: spontaneous role-identity + identity-testing + examination — all in free-play.

### Feedback
- Role-consistent spontaneous behaviour → "The guardian acts as a guardian. The world sees you. The identity is alive."
- Role-inconsistent spontaneous behaviour → "The guardian acted outside the role. The world noticed. What does this mean?"
- Compulsive role-performance → "The guardian finds identity-demands everywhere. Even in rest. Can you be the guardian AND rest?"
- Identity-examination → "You questioned the role. This is not betrayal — this is depth. The self grows stronger when examined."
- Novel identity-demand → "A new situation requires a new aspect of the role. Can the identity grow?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- World identity complexity: simple demands → competing → multi-layered
- Identity-test frequency: rare → occasional → frequent
- Examination depth: gentle → direct → fundamental
- Role complexity: single → multiple → hierarchy

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Taking the Role | 1-5 | Role selection and identity awareness |
| The World's Identity Demands | 5-15 | Spontaneous role-identity response |
| The Self Tested | 15-30 | Spontaneous identity-test response |
| The Role Examined | 30-50 | Spontaneous identity-examination response |
| The Self in the World | 50+ | Full ecological role-identity integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool

- **Identity-demand scenarios:** Situations invoking role-identity spontaneously — 30+
- **Identity-test scenarios:** Situations testing role-identity spontaneously — 25+
- **Examination scenarios:** Situations questioning role-identity — 20+
- **Rest scenarios:** Situations where identity-rest is appropriate — 15+
- **Novel-demand scenarios:** Situations presenting new identity-demands — 15+

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input types:** Tap (action selection); hold (deliberation); swipe (world navigation); free movement
- **Timing:** Response latency to identity-invoking situations tracked; identity-test response time measured; examination engagement duration recorded
- **NPC/AI:** World-event generation based on role and shadow-signal accumulation; identity-invocation calibration; identity-test generation; ecological identity-behaviour analysis
- **LLM:** World narration; NPC dialogue; consequence delivery; identity-invocation naturalness; examination depth
- **State persistence:** Full world-interaction history; spontaneous identity patterns; identity-test responses; examination engagement; role-consistency tracking; cross-session world-state continuity; drive/shadow signal accumulation; checkpoint position
