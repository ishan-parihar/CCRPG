# Intrapersonal / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Intrapersonal  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The self turns its gaze upon itself and, for the first time, authors what it finds.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The Orange intrapersonal capacity is **reflective self-authorship** — the ability to examine one's own identity from an external vantage point, generate hypothetical selves ("what if I were…?"), predict one's own behaviour accurately, and provide honest self-feedback unclouded by role-identity or social desirability. This is the first stage where the self can genuinely *think about itself* as an object of inquiry rather than simply *being* itself. The developmental leap from Amber is the shift from "I am my role" to "I author my identity." The self becomes a project — knowable, shapeable, accountable to evidence.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Self-Optimizer

Compulsive self-improvement. Identity fused with achievement metrics. Cannot rest in being — every moment must optimise. "I am my accomplishments." Treats the self as a project to be perfected rather than a being to be known. Confuses self-knowledge with self-improvement. The reflective capacity collapses into an endless feedback loop of measurement without presence.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Unexamined

Refuses self-reflection entirely. Remains in Amber role-identity: "I am what I am." Cannot or will not examine motivations, avoids introspection, experiences self-inquiry as threatening. The Orange capacity is available but unused — the player retreats to the safety of an unquestioned self-concept rather than risk what examination might reveal.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Process-Self

Performs Green multiperspectival self-concept without genuine Orange self-authorship. Claims "I contain multitudes" before ever genuinely authoring a coherent self. Bypasses the hard work of self-examination by leaping to a pseudo-pluralistic identity that has no centre. The appearance of depth without the substrate of honest self-knowledge.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Fixed Self

Self-authorship is present and genuine — but refuses multiplicity. Terrified of paradox. "I know who I am and I will not change." The Orange capacity is achieved but calcified. Growth toward Green is blocked by the fear that examining multiple perspectives on the self would dissolve the hard-won coherent identity.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

For DA: Agency reclaims the right to *be* without optimising; Agape returns to present-moment self-acceptance. For DAll: Agency authors the first genuine self-examination; Agape makes it safe by grounding in embodied presence rather than abstract threat.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

For GA: Eros pulls toward genuine depth (not performed); Communion reveals that authentic relating requires a real self first. For GAll: Eros invites curiosity about what lies beyond the known self; Communion shows that others' perspectives on you are data, not dissolution.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) accurate self-prediction across domains, (b) capacity to examine self without compulsion or avoidance, (c) willingness to revise self-concept based on evidence, (d) stable identity that can hold uncertainty without rigidity.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Performance Addict (Intrapersonal DA × Somatic DA)

Self-as-metrics fuses with body-as-metrics. Identity exists only through measurable performance. Intervention: somatic games that reward presence over performance; intrapersonal games that validate non-achievement self-knowledge.

### 6.2 The Brilliant Stranger (Intrapersonal DAll × Emotional DAll)

Unexamined self combines with emotional illiteracy — disconnected from the entire interior. Intervention: emotional games that make interior states visible; intrapersonal games that begin with the body (safe entry) before cognitive self-reflection.

### 6.3 The Identity Performer (Intrapersonal GA × Interpersonal GA)

Premature process-self meets premature authentic relating — performed depth without genuine self-knowledge. Intervention: interpersonal games requiring verifiable self-disclosure; intrapersonal games demanding concrete self-prediction (not narrative).

### 6.4 The Self-Made Fortress (Intrapersonal GAll × Willpower GAll)

Fixed self meets achievement fortress — coherent identity defended with willpower against all revision. Cannot grow without perceiving existential threat. Intervention: willpower games rewarding flexibility; intrapersonal games framing identity-revision as strength.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: Establish baseline self-prediction accuracy (no shadow surfacing). Sessions 4–8: Introduce discrepancies between predicted and actual self. Sessions 9–14: Surface the dominant shadow through repeated pattern exposure. Sessions 15+: Direct catalyst targeting the identified shadow quadrant.

### 7.2 Per-Modality Shadow Surfacing

| Modality | Primary shadow surfaced | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DA / DAll | Prediction accuracy reveals over-monitoring or avoidance |
| Language-Reflective | GA / GAll | Verbal self-concept reveals performed depth or rigidity |
| Scenario-Choice | All four | Dilemmas force shadow-consistent choices |
| Embodied-Somatic | DA / DAll | Body-prediction reveals metrics-fusion or disconnection |
| Strategic-Planning | DA / GAll | Planning style reveals compulsion or calcification |
| Social-Cooperative | GA / DAll | Group self-presentation reveals performance or absence |
| Immersive-RPG | All four | Free-play reveals ecological shadow expression |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

No shadow is confirmed until surfaced in ≥3 modalities. Contradictory signals trigger extended observation rather than premature classification. The system holds uncertainty about the player's shadow profile until convergent evidence accumulates.
