# Intrapersonal / Orange — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Body as site of self-knowledge — interoceptive self-awareness, embodied self-concept, physical self-prediction.  **Why this axis for this module:** The reflective self often lives only in thought; this modality tests whether self-authorship extends into the body — whether you know yourself as a physical being, not just a thinking one.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Body Cartography  
**Core loop:** Player predicts their own physical responses (heart rate change, fatigue onset, reaction time, tension patterns), then performs embodied tasks and confronts the accuracy of their somatic self-model. The game measures interoceptive self-knowledge — how well you know your body's patterns, limits, and signals.  
**Feel:** Grounded, physical, present. A practice of inhabiting rather than observing. The body is not an instrument to optimise but a dimension of self to know.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Somatic discrepancy — the gap between how you think your body responds and how it actually does.  
**DA surfacing:** The Self-Optimizer treats the body as a machine to be perfected. Predictions are aspirational rather than accurate. They predict what they *want* their body to do, not what it actually does. Somatic self-knowledge is contaminated by self-improvement goals.  
**DAll surfacing:** The Unexamined has no somatic self-model. Predictions are random or defaulted. They cannot report internal states because they have never attended to them. The body is background noise.  
**GA surfacing:** The Premature Process-Self narrates elaborate body-stories ("my body holds trauma here…") without accurate interoceptive data. Performed somatic wisdom without actual body-knowledge.  
**GAll surfacing:** The Fixed Self has a rigid somatic self-model that refuses updating. "I'm not a physical person" or "I know my body perfectly" — either way, new somatic data is rejected.  
**Drive probes:** Agency = willingness to inhabit the body sovereignly (not as performance). Communion = body-in-relation (how does your body respond to others?). Eros = curiosity about unknown body-patterns. Agape = acceptance of the body as it is (not as it should be).

## 3. Game Design

**Prediction phase:** Player predicts somatic responses before embodied tasks: "How tired will I feel after X?" "Where will I feel tension?" "How fast will my heart rate return to baseline?" "How long can I sustain Y?"  
**Embodied tasks:** Rhythm-matching, breath-holding, sustained attention with body-scan interrupts, progressive muscle engagement, balance challenges. Tasks are vehicles for interoceptive measurement, not fitness tests.  
**Measurement:** Device sensors (accelerometer, heart rate if available) + self-report at timed intervals. Discrepancy between predicted and reported/measured states is the primary signal.  
**Progression:** Early sessions use gross somatic predictions (fatigue, energy). Mid-game introduces subtle interoception (tension location, emotional-body mapping). Late-game targets the specific somatic blind spots revealed by the player's shadow pattern.  
**Shadow-mode:** For DA: tasks where relaxation is the goal and effort is penalised. For DAll: tasks requiring any interoceptive report at all. For GA: tasks requiring specific, verifiable body-predictions (not narratives). For GAll: novel somatic experiences that challenge the fixed body-model.

## 4. Item Pool

| Item category | Examples | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Gross predictions | "Rate your energy 1–10 before and after" | Basic interoceptive access |
| Subtle predictions | "Where will you feel this emotion in your body?" | Interoceptive specificity |
| Temporal predictions | "How long until you feel fatigued?" | Somatic self-model accuracy |
| Relational body | "How does your body respond when observed?" | Social-somatic awareness |
| Novel somatic | Unfamiliar movement patterns, new breath rhythms | Body-model flexibility |
| Recovery predictions | "How long to return to baseline?" | Autonomic self-knowledge |

Items scale from gross → subtle → relational → novel.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Sensor integration:** Accelerometer (required), heart rate monitor (optional, enhances accuracy). Graceful degradation to self-report-only mode on devices without sensors.  
**Scoring:** Primary metric = somatic calibration (prediction-outcome discrepancy across body dimensions). Secondary = interoceptive specificity (granularity of body-reports). Tertiary = body-model flexibility (willingness to update predictions after disconfirmation).  
**Session length:** 8–12 minutes. Embodied tasks must not exceed fatigue thresholds — the game measures self-knowledge, not endurance.  
**Accessibility:** All tasks adaptable to seated/limited-mobility players. No task requires specific physical capacity — only self-knowledge about whatever capacity exists.  
**Privacy:** Biometric data stored locally only. No cloud transmission of physiological signals.
