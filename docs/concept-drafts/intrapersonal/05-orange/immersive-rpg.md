# Intrapersonal / Orange — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological self-authorship — whether reflective self-concept appears spontaneously in free-play without prompting.  **Why this axis for this module:** The truest test of self-authorship is whether it emerges naturally when no one is asking for it — whether the player examines themselves without being told to.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Inner Kingdom  
**Core loop:** An open-world RPG where the player's character is a literal externalisation of their self-concept. The world responds to who the player *is* (as measured by other modalities), not just what they *do*. Self-reflection is never prompted — but the environment creates conditions where it emerges naturally. The diagnostic signal is whether and how the player engages in spontaneous self-examination.  
**Feel:** Immersive, mysterious, personal. The world is a mirror that never announces itself as one. Discovery is self-discovery — but only if the player looks.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Environmental mirroring — the game world reflects the player's self-concept back to them through narrative, NPC behaviour, and world-state, creating conditions for spontaneous self-reflection.  
**DA surfacing:** The Self-Optimizer min-maxes the character, treats every world-interaction as optimisation opportunity, cannot explore without purpose. The world offers rest, beauty, purposelessness — and the DA player cannot receive it.  
**DAll surfacing:** The Unexamined plays the RPG as pure action — never pauses, never reflects, never engages with the mirror-elements. The world offers self-knowledge and they walk past it. Their character remains generic because no self-concept was invested.  
**GA surfacing:** The Premature Process-Self creates an elaborate character backstory that doesn't match their actual gameplay behaviour. They narrate depth but play shallowly. The world reflects the gap between stated and enacted identity.  
**GAll surfacing:** The Fixed Self creates a character and never revises it. World events that challenge the character's identity are resisted or ignored. The character cannot grow because the player's self-concept cannot grow.  
**Drive probes:** Agency = character choices reflecting genuine self-authorship. Communion = character relationships reflecting authentic self-in-relation. Eros = exploring unknown aspects of the world/self. Agape = accepting world-feedback that challenges the character/self.

## 3. Game Design

**Character-as-self:** Character creation draws from the player's actual profile data. Stats reflect real capacities. The character is not a fantasy — it's a mirror. Players can diverge from the mirror, and that divergence is diagnostic.  
**World mirroring:** NPCs respond to the player based on their actual self-concept data (from other modalities). If the player claims to be brave but consistently avoids challenge, NPCs notice. If the player claims humility but optimises obsessively, the world reflects it.  
**Spontaneous reflection opportunities:** Journals, mirrors, NPCs who ask "Who are you?", quiet spaces designed for pause. None are mandatory. The diagnostic signal is engagement vs. avoidance.  
**Narrative arcs:** Story threads that parallel the player's actual developmental trajectory. The character faces the same shadows the player faces — externalised as narrative challenges.  
**Progression:** The world evolves as the player's self-knowledge deepens. Early world is simple, reflecting surface self-concept. As other modalities reveal deeper patterns, the world complexifies to match.

## 4. Item Pool

| Item category | Examples | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Mirror encounters | NPCs who reflect player's behaviour back to them | Spontaneous self-recognition |
| Identity challenges | Quests requiring self-knowledge to complete | Applied self-authorship |
| Rest spaces | Beautiful, purposeless areas with no objectives | DA tolerance for being |
| Reflection prompts | Optional journals, meditation spots, dream sequences | Voluntary self-examination |
| Character revision | Opportunities to change character based on new self-knowledge | GAll flexibility |
| Relational mirrors | NPCs whose relationships reflect player's actual relational patterns | Ecological self-awareness |

Items are embedded in world design — never presented as "tests."

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Profile integration:** Deep integration with all other modalities. The world-state is a function of the player's cross-module profile. Changes in self-knowledge across modalities propagate to world-state.  
**Behavioural telemetry:** Track spontaneous self-reflective behaviours (journal use, mirror engagement, pause frequency, character-revision frequency) without prompting. These are the primary diagnostic signals.  
**Scoring:** Primary metric = spontaneous self-reflection frequency and depth. Secondary = character-self alignment (does the character reflect genuine self-knowledge?). Tertiary = world-feedback integration (does the player update behaviour based on environmental mirrors?).  
**Session length:** 20–45 minutes. Immersive play requires longer sessions for ecological validity. Infinite checkpoint model — player leaves whenever.  
**Content generation:** World events and NPC dialogue adapt to player's current shadow profile and developmental edge. LLM-assisted narrative generation within fixed mechanical constraints.
