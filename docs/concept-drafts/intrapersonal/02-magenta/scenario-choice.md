# Intrapersonal / Magenta — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes intrapersonal intelligence through SELF-CONSISTENT DECISION — can the player make choices that reflect who they are? At Magenta, this is: "The brave one would go left. The careful one would go right. Which one are YOU? Choose as yourself."
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Magenta:** Before reflective decision-making (Red+), choosing-as-self is IDENTITY-DRIVEN — "I choose this because I am THIS kind of person." This modality tests whether the player can make choices consistent with their magical self-concept.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Self-Path"
- **Core mechanic:** Branching paths where each branch corresponds to a different self-image. The player chooses based on WHO THEY ARE, not what's optimal. "The dreamer goes here. The brave one goes there. The quiet one stays. Which are YOU?" Self-consistent choice reveals intrapersonal capacity.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Choice → The Self-Fork → The Changing Choice → The Hidden Self-Choice → The Living Chooser

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Two paths. One is for the brave one. One is for the careful one. Which one are you? Choose as YOURSELF — not what's best, but what's YOU." The contact boundary is: "Can you choose as a self?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player choose? The Fantasy-Prisoner always picks the GRANDEST path ("I'm the hero, obviously"). The Self-Stranger cannot choose ("I don't know which one I am"). The Premature Ego picks the MOST POWERFUL path ("the strong one"). The Identity-Refuser approaches a choice then retreats ("maybe the brave one… no, I don't know").
- *Emergent:* Can they choose consistently with their actual self-image — neither inflated nor absent?

**Integration path:** Rewards SELF-CONSISTENT CHOICE — choosing paths that match demonstrated self-image. Not the grandest, not random, not the most powerful, not avoidant.

**Successful integration:** The player makes choices that reflect who they actually are — the decisional foundation of self-knowledge.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing independently (no guidance, no hints about "right" answer).
- Healthy: Chooses from self-knowledge; "I pick this because I'm this kind of person"
- Pathological: Always picks grandest (inflation); OR: cannot choose (absence)
- **Detection:** Whether choices are self-generated and self-consistent

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing under ambiguity (paths aren't clearly labelled).
- Healthy: Makes tentative self-consistent choice under ambiguity
- Pathological: Cannot choose without clear identity-labels; freezes
- **Detection:** Choice quality under identity-ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing when companion chooses differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own choice while acknowledging companion's
- Pathological: Copies companion's choice; loses own self-direction
- **Detection:** Whether own choice survives social influence

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing together (paths that require shared identity).
- Healthy: Contributes own self-knowledge to shared decision
- Pathological: Cannot contribute self to shared choice; disappears
- **Detection:** Quality of self-contribution to shared decisions

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing consistently over time (same self, same choices).
- Healthy: Choices remain consistent with self-image across sessions
- Pathological: Choices are random; no self-consistency
- **Detection:** Choice consistency over time

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing as the GROWING self (new paths for the emerging self).
- Healthy: Can choose new paths that reflect growth without losing core identity
- Pathological: Cannot choose new paths; OR: abandons old identity entirely
- **Detection:** Growth-choice quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining self-consistent choice over time.
- Healthy: Choice patterns remain coherent across sessions
- Pathological: Choice coherence degrades; self-consistency dissolves
- **Detection:** Choice coherence across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether self-consistent choice appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Makes identity-consistent choices naturally in free-play
- Pathological: Self-consistent choice exists only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous self-consistent choice

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Fantasy-Prisoner"
**How it surfaces:** Always picks the GRANDEST, most HEROIC path. "I'm the hero. Obviously I go on the adventure." Inflated self-choice. Cannot choose the ordinary, quiet, or humble path because the fantasy demands grandeur.
- **Detection signals:** Always picks grandest path; "the hero"; inflated choices; cannot choose ordinary; defends grand choices; "obviously I'm the brave one"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Self-Stranger"
**How it surfaces:** CANNOT CHOOSE. "I don't know which one I am." Random selections. No self-basis for choice. The fork is meaningless because there is no self to choose from.
- **Detection signals:** Cannot choose; "I don't know"; random; long latency; no self-basis; "any of them"; choices have no pattern

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Ego"
**How it surfaces:** Always picks the MOST POWERFUL, EFFECTIVE path. Not the grandest (fantasy) but the most CAPABLE. "The one that wins." Function-based choice without self-knowledge.
- **Detection signals:** Picks most effective path; "the one that wins"; optimises not self-expresses; function-based; no identity in choices; self = optimal strategy

### Golden-Allergy: "The Identity-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** APPROACHES a choice then RETREATS. "Maybe the brave one… no, I don't know." Begins to choose-as-self then pulls back. Self-choice approach-withdrawal.
- **Detection signals:** Approach-withdrawal; "maybe… no"; begins choice then cancels; multiple near-choices without commitment; choosing-as-self feels dangerous

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present paths where ORDINARY is rewarding: "The quiet path. The one who watches. That's also a real choice."
- Reward non-grand self-consistent choices
- Frame: "Not the hero path. YOUR path. Maybe you're the quiet one today. That's real."
- Build: accurate self-choice (actual not inflated)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present BINARY choices with clear self-relevance: "Do you like this or that? Just preference. That's you choosing."
- Make self-choice EASY; start with preferences not identity
- Frame: "You DO have preferences. This or that? Whichever you pick — that's you choosing. That's a self."
- Reward ANY self-consistent choice; celebrate first "I choose this because I'm me"

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Present paths where FEELING matters more than effectiveness: "Not which is best. Which FEELS like you?"
- Block optimisation; reward felt-choice
- Frame: "Not the winning path. The YOU path. Which one feels like home? That's your choice."
- Build: felt self-choice (identity-driven not optimisation-driven)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make self-choice BRIEF and REVERSIBLE: "Just pick. You can change later. Which one right now?"
- Reduce commitment; choice isn't permanent identity-lock
- Frame: "Just for now. Pick one. You're not stuck. Which one feels like you RIGHT NOW?"
- Build: self-choice safety (choosing-as-self isn't permanent)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Self-Path: branching paths in a magical landscape. Each branch is labelled with a self-quality (brave/careful, loud/quiet, dreamer/doer). The player chooses based on who they ARE. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage crossroads — glowing paths diverging, each with a spirit-quality marker.

### Interaction
- **The First Choice (1-5):** Binary fork with clear self-labels. "Brave or careful? Which are you?"
- **The Self-Fork (5-15):** Multiple paths with subtler self-labels. Find YOUR path.
- **The Changing Choice (15-30):** Same fork, different day. "Who are you TODAY?"
- **The Hidden Self-Choice (30-50):** Paths without labels. Choose from felt-sense of self.
- **The Living Chooser (50+):** Full self-consistent choice: accurate + flexible + felt + spontaneous.

### Feedback
- Self-consistent choice → "Yes! That's your path. You know who you are. The path knows you."
- Inflated → "Not the hero path. YOUR path. Maybe quieter. Maybe smaller. But truly yours."
- Cannot choose → "Just pick one. Either one. Whichever you pick — that's you choosing. That's real."
- Optimising → "Not the best path. YOUR path. Which one feels like home? Not winning — belonging."
- Avoidant → "Quick! Left or right? … Yes! You chose. That's you. See? You know."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Label clarity: obvious self-labels → subtle → unlabelled
- Number of paths: 2 → 3 → 5
- Self-aspect: familiar (brave/careful) → novel (dreamer/watcher) → ambiguous
- Consistency demand: single choice → series of consistent choices
- Social dimension: solo → with companion → group

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Choice | 1-5 | Binary self-labelled fork |
| The Self-Fork | 5-15 | Multiple paths with subtle labels |
| The Changing Choice | 15-30 | Same fork, different contexts |
| The Hidden Self-Choice | 30-50 | Unlabelled paths; choose from felt-self |
| The Living Chooser | 50+ | Full self-consistent choice integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Binary forks:** Two clearly labelled self-paths
- **Multiple forks:** 3-5 paths with varying self-labels
- **Contextual forks:** Same paths, different situations
- **Unlabelled forks:** Paths requiring felt-sense choice
- **Growth forks:** Paths representing current vs. emerging self

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ binary, 20+ multiple, 15+ contextual, 15+ unlabelled, 10+ growth

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Always picks grandest → dark-addiction; cannot choose → dark-allergy
- Always optimises → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Self-consistency:** Do choices match demonstrated self-image?
- **Flexibility:** Can they choose differently in different contexts while maintaining coherence?
- **Felt-basis:** Are choices felt (not optimised or random)?
- **Independence:** Are choices self-generated (not copied)?
- **Growth-integration:** Can they choose new paths without losing core identity?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Choice-pattern analysis partially algorithmic (consistency metrics). LLM for felt-basis assessment, self-consistency evaluation against demonstrated behaviour, and growth-integration quality.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (choose path); swipe (explore options); hold (confirm choice)

### Timing requirements
- Choice latency tracked; hesitation patterns diagnostic; no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Path generation system (self-labelled branching)
- Self-consistency tracking (choices vs. demonstrated self-image)
- Companion choice system (social influence testing)
- Difficulty adaptation (label clarity, number, context)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Self-consistency evaluation; felt-basis assessment; growth-integration quality. Core choice-pattern tracking algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Choice history; self-consistency scores; flexibility metrics; felt-basis quality; independence scores; growth-integration quality; inflation indicators; absence indicators; optimisation indicators; avoidance indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
