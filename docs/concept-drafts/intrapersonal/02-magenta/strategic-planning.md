# Intrapersonal / Magenta — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** The strategic-planning axis probes intrapersonal intelligence through SELF-PREDICTION — can the player anticipate their own future states? At Magenta, this is: "What will YOU do next? What will YOU feel? Who will you BE tomorrow?" Proto-self-prediction through magical narrative.
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Magenta:** Before rational self-prediction (Orange+), self-forecasting is MAGICAL — "I will be the brave one tomorrow because the spirits say so." This modality tests whether the player can predict their own future self-states — the temporal dimension of self-knowledge.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Future-Self"
- **Core mechanic:** The player predicts their own future states — what they will do, feel, choose, be. Then observes whether the prediction matches. Not planning ACTIONS (that's willpower) but predicting SELF-STATES. "Who will you be when…? What will you feel when…?"
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Prediction → The Self-Forecast → The Many Futures → The Changing Prediction → The Living Predictor

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "What will you do next? Not what SHOULD you do — what WILL you do? Who will you be in this situation? Predict yourself." The contact boundary is: "Can you predict your own future states?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player predict themselves? The Fantasy-Prisoner predicts GRANDLY ("I will be the hero! I will be amazing!"). The Self-Stranger cannot predict ("I don't know what I'll do"). The Premature Ego predicts OPTIMALLY ("I'll do the best thing"). The Identity-Refuser begins predicting then stops ("I'll probably… no, I don't know").
- *Emergent:* Can they predict themselves accurately — neither inflated nor blank nor optimised nor avoidant?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE SELF-PREDICTION — predicting own future states that match actual behaviour. Not grandiose, not blank, not optimal, not avoidant.

**Successful integration:** The player can predict their own future states — the temporal foundation of self-knowledge.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Self-prediction independently (no hints about what they "should" predict).
- Healthy: Predicts own states with confidence; "I think I'll feel…"
- Pathological: Predicts grandly (inflation); OR: cannot predict (absence)
- **Detection:** Whether self-predictions are self-generated and realistic

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Self-prediction under ambiguity (novel situations).
- Healthy: Offers tentative self-predictions under uncertainty
- Pathological: Cannot predict self in novel situations; freezes
- **Detection:** Self-prediction quality under novelty

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Self-prediction when companion predicts differently about them.
- Healthy: Maintains own self-prediction while hearing companion's view
- Pathological: Adopts companion's prediction about them; loses own self-forecasting
- **Detection:** Whether own self-prediction survives social input

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared self-prediction (predicting selves to each other).
- Healthy: Shares own self-prediction AND receives companion's
- Pathological: Cannot share self-predictions; self-forecasting is private
- **Detection:** Quality of mutual self-prediction sharing

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Self-prediction consistency (predicting same self across time).
- Healthy: Self-predictions remain consistent with demonstrated self
- Pathological: Self-predictions are random; no temporal self-coherence
- **Detection:** Self-prediction consistency

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Predicting the GROWING self (who will I become?).
- Healthy: Can predict growth without losing current self-knowledge
- Pathological: Cannot predict growth; OR: only predicts wished-for self
- **Detection:** Growth-prediction quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining self-prediction accuracy over time.
- Healthy: Self-prediction accuracy remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Self-prediction accuracy degrades; loses self-forecasting ability
- **Detection:** Prediction accuracy across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether self-prediction appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Anticipates own states naturally in non-prediction tasks
- Pathological: Self-prediction exists only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous self-prediction

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Fantasy-Prisoner"
**How it surfaces:** Predicts GRANDLY. "I will be amazing! I will be the bravest! I will feel incredible!" Inflated self-prediction. Cannot predict ordinary, scared, or uncertain future states.
- **Detection signals:** Grand predictions; "amazing"; "the best"; cannot predict ordinary states; inflated future-self; "I will be incredible"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Self-Stranger"
**How it surfaces:** CANNOT PREDICT. "I don't know what I'll do." "I have no idea what I'll feel." Zero self-forecasting. The future-self is as unknown as the present-self.
- **Detection signals:** "I don't know"; cannot predict; blank; no self-forecasting; "no idea"; random guesses; future-self is stranger

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Ego"
**How it surfaces:** Predicts OPTIMALLY. "I'll do the best thing. I'll make the right choice." Not what they WILL do but what they SHOULD do. Optimal-self prediction, not actual-self prediction.
- **Detection signals:** Optimal predictions; "the best thing"; "the right choice"; should-language; no actual-self in predictions; self-prediction = ideal behaviour

### Golden-Allergy: "The Identity-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** BEGINS predicting then STOPS. "I'll probably feel… no, I don't know." Approach-withdrawal in self-prediction. The future-self almost appears then retreats.
- **Detection signals:** Incomplete predictions; "probably… no"; approach-withdrawal; begins then cancels; "never mind"; self-prediction feels dangerous

---

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present situations where ORDINARY predictions are accurate: "You'll probably feel a little tired. A little hungry. Just ordinary."
- Reward accurate ordinary self-predictions
- Frame: "Not amazing. Just real. What will you ACTUALLY feel? Probably something ordinary. That's okay."
- Build: accurate self-prediction (realistic not inflated)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Start with IMMEDIATE predictions: "In 5 seconds, will you feel the same or different? Just that."
- Make self-prediction EASY; very short time-horizon
- Frame: "Right now you feel X. In a moment, will you still feel X? Just yes or no. That's predicting yourself."
- Reward ANY accurate self-prediction; celebrate first correct forecast

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require ACTUAL predictions not IDEAL: "Not what you SHOULD do. What will you ACTUALLY do? Be honest."
- Block should-language; reward honest self-prediction
- Frame: "The real you. Not the perfect you. What will the REAL you do? Maybe something imperfect. That's okay."
- Build: honest self-prediction (actual not ideal)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make self-prediction BRIEF and LOW-STAKES: "Just guess. What will you feel? Quick guess. No commitment."
- Reduce threat of self-prediction; make it playful
- Frame: "Just a guess. Not a promise. What do you THINK you'll feel? Quick. Done."
- Build: self-prediction safety (predicting self isn't binding)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Future-Self: a temporal self-exploration space. The player predicts their own future states, then observes whether predictions match. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage seeing-pool — looking forward in time to see the future-self. Predictions glow; matches confirm.

### Interaction
- **The First Prediction (1-5):** "What will you feel in the next moment?" Immediate self-prediction.
- **The Self-Forecast (5-15):** "What will you do when X happens?" Situational self-prediction.
- **The Many Futures (15-30):** Multiple situations → multiple self-predictions. Build prediction-map.
- **The Changing Prediction (30-50):** Predict self in NOVEL situations (no prior data).
- **The Living Predictor (50+):** Full self-prediction: accurate + honest + flexible + spontaneous.

### Feedback
- Accurate prediction → "You knew! You predicted yourself correctly. You know who you are."
- Grand prediction → "Simpler. Not amazing. What will you ACTUALLY feel? Probably something ordinary."
- Cannot predict → "Just this moment. Same or different? … Yes! You predicted yourself. That's self-knowledge."
- Optimal prediction → "Not the best thing. The REAL thing. What will the real you do? Honestly."
- Avoidant → "Quick guess! What will you feel? … See? You DO know yourself. Trust that."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Time horizon: immediate (seconds) → short (minutes) → medium (session-length)
- Situation familiarity: familiar → novel → ambiguous
- Prediction specificity: binary (same/different) → categorical → specific
- Accuracy demand: loose match → close match → precise
- Social dimension: private → shared → mutual prediction

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Prediction | 1-5 | Immediate self-prediction (seconds) |
| The Self-Forecast | 5-15 | Situational self-prediction |
| The Many Futures | 15-30 | Multiple situation predictions |
| The Changing Prediction | 30-50 | Novel situation self-prediction |
| The Living Predictor | 50+ | Full self-prediction integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Immediate prompts:** "What will you feel in 5 seconds?"
- **Situational prompts:** "What will you do when X happens?"
- **Emotional prompts:** "What will you feel when you see X?"
- **Novel prompts:** "In this new situation, what will you do?"
- **Growth prompts:** "Who will you be after this experience?"

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ immediate, 20+ situational, 15+ emotional, 15+ novel, 10+ growth

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Grand predictions → dark-addiction; cannot predict → dark-allergy
- Optimal predictions → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Prediction accuracy:** Do predictions match actual outcomes?
- **Honesty:** Are predictions realistic (not inflated or idealised)?
- **Specificity:** Are predictions specific (not vague)?
- **Consistency:** Do predictions reflect stable self-knowledge?
- **Flexibility:** Can they predict differently in different contexts?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Prediction-outcome matching partially algorithmic. LLM for honesty assessment, specificity evaluation, inflation detection, and growth-prediction quality.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (predicted state); text/voice (specific predictions); slider (intensity predictions)

### Timing requirements
- Prediction-to-outcome delay tracked; accuracy scoring after outcome observed

### NPC/AI requirements
- Situation generation system (contexts requiring self-prediction)
- Outcome tracking system (what actually happened vs. prediction)
- Accuracy scoring system (prediction vs. actual)
- Difficulty adaptation (time horizon, familiarity, specificity)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Honesty assessment; inflation detection; specificity evaluation. Core prediction-matching algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Prediction history; accuracy rates; honesty scores; specificity quality; consistency metrics; flexibility scores; inflation indicators; absence indicators; optimisation indicators; avoidance indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
