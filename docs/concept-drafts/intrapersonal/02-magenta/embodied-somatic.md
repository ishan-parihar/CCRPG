# Intrapersonal / Magenta — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The embodied-somatic axis probes intrapersonal intelligence through FEELING ONESELF IN THE BODY — can the player locate "I" somatically? At Magenta, this is: "Where in your body do you feel YOU? Where does 'I am' live? What does being-you feel like from inside?"
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Magenta:** Before cognitive self-reflection (Red+), self-knowledge is SOMATIC — "I feel myself here, in my chest, in my belly." The body as the seat of identity. This modality tests whether the player can feel themselves as an embodied self.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-Self"
- **Core mechanic:** The player locates WHERE in their body they feel "I am." Not thinking about self — FEELING self somatically. "Where does 'you' live in your body? Point to where you feel most YOU." The body-map of identity.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; body-fatigue monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The First Feeling → The Body-Home → The Many Places → The Changing Body-Self → The Living Body-I

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Where in your body do you feel YOU? Not your hand, not your foot — where does 'I am' live? Your chest? Your head? Your belly? Point to where you feel most yourself." The contact boundary is: "Can you feel yourself in your body?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player locate self somatically? The Fantasy-Prisoner locates self EVERYWHERE ("I am all of it! My whole body is me!") — inflated somatic self. The Self-Stranger locates self NOWHERE ("I don't feel me anywhere"). The Premature Ego locates self in HEAD/HANDS only (functional body). The Identity-Refuser begins to locate then loses the feeling.
- *Emergent:* Can they locate self somatically with appropriate specificity — neither everywhere nor nowhere?

**Integration path:** Rewards SPECIFIC SOMATIC SELF-LOCATION — feeling "I am" in a particular body-area with genuine felt-sense. Not everywhere, not nowhere, not only functional areas, not flickering.

**Successful integration:** The player can feel themselves as an embodied self — the somatic foundation of identity.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Body-Self: a somatic self-exploration space. A body-outline is presented. The player locates where "I am" lives. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage body-altar — a glowing body-outline that lights up where the player indicates self-presence. Warm colours for "I am here."

### Interaction
- **The First Feeling (1-5):** "Where do you feel most YOU?" Tap one body-area.
- **The Body-Home (5-15):** Identify quality: warm/cool, heavy/light, big/small at self-location.
- **The Many Places (15-30):** "Where ELSE is you?" Multiple self-locations; build body-map of identity.
- **The Changing Body-Self (30-50):** Self-location in different contexts (happy/sad/scared).
- **The Living Body-I (50+):** Full somatic self-integration: locate + qualify + expand + sustain.

### Feedback
- Specific location → "There! That's where you live. Your body knows you. You're HOME."
- Everywhere → "Smaller. Not all of it. Your CENTER. Where is the middle of you? Just there."
- Nowhere → "Breathe. Feel your chest move. That's you. Right there. You're in your breath."
- Head-only → "Lower. Your heart. Your belly. Not thinking — FEELING. Where do you feel warm?"
- Flickering → "You found it! For a moment. That's real. Can you stay? Just one more breath?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Location precision: large area → specific spot
- Quality discrimination: binary (warm/cool) → multiple qualities
- Expansion: single location → multiple locations → full body-map
- Context variation: neutral → emotional → challenging
- Duration: brief → sustained somatic self-contact

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Feeling | 1-5 | Basic body-self location (tap area) |
| The Body-Home | 5-15 | Quality of body-self (warm/cool/heavy/light) |
| The Many Places | 15-30 | Multiple self-locations; body-map |
| The Changing Body-Self | 30-50 | Self-location across contexts |
| The Living Body-I | 50+ | Full somatic self-integration |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Neutral contexts:** Basic "where are you?" prompts
- **Emotional contexts:** "Where are you when happy/sad/scared?"
- **Activity contexts:** "Where are you when playing/resting/exploring?"
- **Social contexts:** "Where are you when with others/alone?"
- **Growth contexts:** "Where is the NEW you? The growing you?"

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ neutral, 20+ emotional, 15+ activity, 15+ social, 10+ growth

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Everywhere → dark-addiction; nowhere → dark-allergy
- Head/hands only → golden-addiction; flickering → golden-allergy

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-on-body-map (where self is felt); select quality (warm/cool/heavy/light); hold (sustain feeling)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked; no time pressure; body-fatigue monitored

### NPC/AI requirements
- Body-map visualization (interactive body-outline)
- Self-location tracking system (consistency, specificity)
- Context generation (emotional, activity, social situations)
- Difficulty adaptation (precision, quality, expansion)

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Quality description evaluation; inflation detection; context-sensitivity. Core tap-location algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Body-self locations; quality reports; specificity scores; constancy quality; expansion capacity; inflation indicators; absence indicators; functional-only indicators; flickering indicators; drive/shadow signals; body-fatigue state; checkpoint position
