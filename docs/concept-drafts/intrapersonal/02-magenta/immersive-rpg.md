# Intrapersonal / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes intrapersonal intelligence through SPONTANEOUS SELF-EXPRESSION in an open world — not structured self-tasks but free self-behaviour. How does the player's self-concept LIVE when nobody's asking "who are you?"
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe self-knowledge through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world contains mirrors, identity-challenges, companions, and self-relevant content. The player's spontaneous self-behaviour reveals genuine intrapersonal capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Self-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world where the player's self-concept is tested ECOLOGICALLY. Mirror-pools reflect them. Companions challenge their identity. Phantom-selves appear. The player's spontaneous self-behaviour — how they navigate identity-relevant content without being prompted — reveals genuine intrapersonal capacity.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Self-Noticing → Self-Maintaining → Self-Sharing → Self-Growing → The Living Self

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world contains identity-relevant content. Nobody says "who are you?" — but mirror-pools are there, phantom-selves appear, companions ask questions, identity-forks present themselves. The player's NATURAL self-engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL self-style surfaces. Do they inflate at every mirror? Avoid all self-relevant content? Dominate every companion? Merge with everyone? Their ecological self-behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage self-relevant content more healthily? Maintain self more flexibly?

**Integration path:** The world rewards intrapersonal intelligence NATURALLY. Players who maintain self-identity access self-gated content. Players who share self gain companions. Players who grow find new territories. The reward is SELF-BELONGING (knowing who you are in the world).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates intrapersonal intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — recognising, naming, maintaining, and growing self without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world with self-relevant content woven throughout. Mirror-pools, phantom-selves, identity-forks, companion circles, self-gated paths. Every zone has identity-content — opportunities to recognise, name, maintain, and grow self.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through an identity-textured world
- **Spontaneous self-engagement:** Noticing and responding to self-content
- **Identity-gated content:** Paths/items requiring self-knowledge
- **Phantom-self encounters:** Meeting reflections of own self-image
- **Companion interactions:** Relating while maintaining self

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: self-engagement → self-gated access, hidden content, companions
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, stuck paths, flat world
- No explicit "self-score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Self-content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Identity-gate sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated self-knowledge
- Phantom-self complexity scales with demonstrated self-recognition

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Self-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice self-content? |
| Self-Maintaining | 5-15 | Do they maintain self during interaction? |
| Self-Sharing | 15-30 | Can they share self with companions? |
| Self-Growing | 30-50 | Do they engage growth-content? |
| The Living Self | 50+ | Full ecological intrapersonal intelligence |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Mirror-pools:** Self-recognition opportunities
- **Phantom-selves:** Reflections to identify/confront
- **Identity-forks:** Paths requiring self-consistent choice
- **Companion circles:** Relational self-maintenance contexts
- **Growth-gates:** Content requiring self-expansion

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ mirror-pools, 15+ phantom-selves, 15+ identity-forks, 15+ companion circles, 10+ growth-gates

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Inflates at mirrors → dark-addiction; avoids all self-content → dark-allergy
- Functional self-engagement → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage self-content); hold (sustain self-contact); gesture (interact with companions)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, self-maintenance quality)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of self-patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Identity-textured environment (self-content throughout)
- Phantom-self system (reflections of player's self-image)
- Identity-gating system (paths requiring self-knowledge)
- Companion system (various relational styles)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, phantom-self dynamics
- Detects spontaneous self-engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates intrapersonal intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive self-content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; self-engagement history; navigation patterns; self-maintenance records; relational self quality; growth engagement; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
