# Intrapersonal / Magenta — Language-Reflective Game Concept

> **Axis:** The language-reflective axis probes intrapersonal intelligence through SELF-NAMING — can the player put words to who they are? At Magenta, this is: "I am the one who…" Magical self-narrative. Not reflection (Red+) but NAMING — giving the self a story.
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Magenta:** Before reflective self-analysis (Red+), self-knowledge through language is NARRATIVE — "I am the brave one, I am the dreamer, I am the one who found the sacred thing." This modality tests whether the player can NAME themselves in magical narrative.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Name-Self"
- **Core mechanic:** The player names who they are. Not analysis — NAMING. "I am the one who…" Magical self-narrative construction. The game presents contexts and the player completes self-statements. The quality of self-naming reveals intrapersonal capacity.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Name → The Many Names → The Story-Self → The Changing Name → The Living Name

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Who are you? Not what you DO — who you ARE. Tell me. 'I am the one who…' Complete it. Name yourself." The contact boundary is: "Can you name your own self?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player name themselves? The Fantasy-Prisoner names a GRANDIOSE self ("I am the greatest, the chosen"). The Self-Stranger cannot name at all ("I don't know… I'm nobody"). The Premature Ego names by FUNCTION ("I am the one who wins, who is strong"). The Identity-Refuser begins naming then stops ("I am… no, never mind").
- *Emergent:* Can they name themselves accurately — neither inflated nor absent nor functional nor avoidant?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE SELF-NAMING — putting words to the actual self with appropriate richness. Not grandiose, not blank, not purely functional, not avoidant.

**Successful integration:** The player can name who they are in magical narrative — the verbal foundation of self-knowledge.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Self-naming independently (no prompts, no options).
- Healthy: Generates own self-names with confidence; "I am the one who…"
- Pathological: Only grandiose names (inflation); OR: cannot generate (absence)
- **Detection:** Whether self-names are self-generated and accurate

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Self-naming under ambiguity (who are you when you don't know?).
- Healthy: Offers tentative self-names under uncertainty; "I think I am…"
- Pathological: Cannot name self without clear context; freezes
- **Detection:** Self-naming quality under ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Self-naming when companion names differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own self-name while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's name; loses own self-naming
- **Detection:** Whether own self-name survives social comparison

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared self-naming (naming selves to each other).
- Healthy: Shares own name AND receives companion's; mutual naming
- Pathological: Cannot share self-name; self-naming is private/shameful
- **Detection:** Quality of mutual self-naming

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Self-naming over time (same self, different moments).
- Healthy: Self-names remain consistent but flexible
- Pathological: Self-names change completely every time; no narrative constancy
- **Detection:** Self-naming constancy

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Naming the GROWING self (who are you becoming?).
- Healthy: Can name emerging self without losing current self
- Pathological: Cannot name growth; OR: only names wished-for self
- **Detection:** Growth-naming quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining self-naming over time.
- Healthy: Self-naming quality remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Self-naming degrades; cannot name self after absence
- **Detection:** Naming consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether self-naming appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Names self naturally in non-naming tasks
- Pathological: Self-naming exists only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous self-naming

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Fantasy-Prisoner"
**How it surfaces:** Names a GRANDIOSE self. "I am the greatest. I am the chosen one. I am the most special." Inflated self-narrative. The name is a prison of magnificence.
- **Detection signals:** Grandiose names; "the greatest"; "the chosen"; superlative self-descriptions; cannot name ordinary self; defends grand name

### Dark-Allergy: "The Self-Stranger"
**How it surfaces:** CANNOT NAME. "I don't know who I am." "I'm nobody." Blank self-narrative. No words for self. The naming space is empty.
- **Detection signals:** "I don't know"; "nobody"; blank; long silence; cannot complete "I am…"; no self-narrative available

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Ego"
**How it surfaces:** Names by FUNCTION. "I am the strong one. I am the one who wins. I am the one who can." Will-based naming. No imaginative, playful, or feeling-based self-names.
- **Detection signals:** Function-names; "the strong one"; "the one who can"; capability-based; no feeling/imagination in names; self = function

### Golden-Allergy: "The Identity-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** BEGINS naming then STOPS. "I am the… no. Never mind." Approach-withdrawal in self-naming. The name almost comes then retreats.
- **Detection signals:** Incomplete names; "I am… no"; approach-withdrawal; begins then cancels; "never mind"; self-naming feels dangerous

---

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Offer ORDINARY self-names: "Not the greatest. Just: 'I am the one who is here.' That's enough."
- Reward simple, accurate self-naming
- Frame: "The real name. Not the biggest name. The true one. 'I am here. I am me.' That's your name."
- Build: accurate self-naming (true not inflated)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Offer STARTER names: "Try: 'I am the one who is looking.' Just that. You're looking right now. That's you."
- Make self-naming EASY; provide scaffolding
- Frame: "You ARE someone. Try: 'I am…' and just say what you're doing right now. That's a name."
- Reward ANY self-naming; celebrate first "I am…"

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Offer FEELING/IMAGINATION names: "Not what you CAN do. What you FEEL. What you DREAM. 'I am the one who dreams of…'"
- Block function-names; reward feeling-names
- Frame: "Not strong. Not capable. What do you FEEL like? 'I am the one who feels…' Try that."
- Build: imaginative self-naming (felt not functional)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make naming BRIEF and SAFE: "Just one word. Who are you? One word. Then done."
- Reduce commitment; naming isn't permanent
- Frame: "Just for now. One word. You can change it later. Who are you right now? Just one word."
- Build: self-naming safety (naming isn't a trap)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Name-Self: a verbal self-exploration space. The player is invited to name who they are. Contexts are presented; self-statements are completed. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage naming-ceremony — a sacred circle where names are spoken and become real. Words glow when spoken.

### Interaction
- **The First Name (1-5):** Complete "I am the one who…" with one word/phrase.
- **The Many Names (5-15):** Multiple contexts → multiple self-names. "Who are you HERE? And HERE?"
- **The Story-Self (15-30):** Build a brief self-narrative. "Tell me about you in three names."
- **The Changing Name (30-50):** Name the self that's CHANGING. "Who are you becoming?"
- **The Living Name (50+):** Full self-naming: accurate + flexible + felt + shared.

### Feedback
- Genuine naming → "Yes! That's your name. You know who you are. The name is true."
- Grandiose → "Simpler. Not the greatest. Just you. The one who is here. That's enough."
- Blank → "Try: 'I am the one who is looking.' You're looking right now. That's you. Say it."
- Functional → "Not what you can do. What you feel. What you dream. That's also your name."
- Avoidant → "Just one word. Quick. Who? … Yes! That's you. See? Not so hard."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Naming complexity: one word → phrase → sentence → narrative
- Context specificity: clear context → ambiguous → no context
- Self-aspect: familiar → novel → changing
- Social dimension: private → shared → mutual
- Temporal: present self → past self → future self

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Name | 1-5 | Single self-name completion |
| The Many Names | 5-15 | Multiple context-specific self-names |
| The Story-Self | 15-30 | Brief self-narrative construction |
| The Changing Name | 30-50 | Naming the growing/changing self |
| The Living Name | 50+ | Full self-naming integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Context prompts:** Situations requiring self-naming ("Who are you when…?")
- **Completion stems:** "I am the one who…" with varying difficulty
- **Narrative prompts:** "Tell me about you…" requiring multi-sentence self-story
- **Growth prompts:** "Who are you becoming?" requiring future-self naming
- **Shared prompts:** "Tell your companion who you are" requiring social self-naming

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ context prompts, 25+ completion stems, 15+ narrative prompts, 10+ growth prompts, 10+ shared prompts

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Grandiose names → dark-addiction; blank/cannot name → dark-allergy
- Function-only names → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Naming presence:** Can they name themselves at all?
- **Accuracy:** Does the name match demonstrated behaviour/feeling?
- **Richness:** Multiple dimensions (feeling, imagination, action) in naming?
- **Flexibility:** Can they name differently in different contexts?
- **Constancy:** Do names maintain coherent identity across contexts?

### LLM rubric
- **Very High:** Self-naming is inherently linguistic. LLM evaluates naming quality, accuracy, richness, grandiosity detection, absence detection, function-only detection, and approach-withdrawal patterns.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Text/voice input (self-naming); tap-to-select (from options); hold (confirm name)

### Timing requirements
- Naming latency tracked; hesitation patterns diagnostic; no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Context generation system (situations requiring self-naming)
- Name evaluation system (accuracy, richness, shadow detection)
- Companion naming system (mutual self-naming)
- Difficulty adaptation (complexity, context, social dimension)

### LLM requirements
- **Very High:** Core mechanic is linguistic self-expression. LLM evaluates all naming quality, detects shadow patterns, generates contexts, and assesses accuracy against demonstrated behaviour.

### State persistence
- Self-names generated; naming quality scores; richness metrics; flexibility scores; constancy quality; grandiosity indicators; absence indicators; function-only indicators; avoidance indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
