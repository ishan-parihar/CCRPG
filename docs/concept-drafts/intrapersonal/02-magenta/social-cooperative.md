# Intrapersonal / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes intrapersonal intelligence through BEING A SELF WITH OTHERS — can the player maintain self-identity in relationship? At Magenta, this is: "Who are you when you're with someone else? Are you still YOU? Or do you become them?"
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Magenta:** Before differentiated social self (Red+), self-in-relationship is the PARTICIPATION MYSTIQUE challenge — "Am I still me when I'm with you?" This modality tests whether the player can maintain magical self-concept while in relationship.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Self-With"
- **Core mechanic:** The player interacts with a companion. During interaction, their self-image is tested — do they maintain it? Lose it? Inflate it? Refuse it? "You are with someone now. Are you still you? Who are you when you're together?" Self-maintenance in relationship.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Together → The Self-Beside → The Different-Together → The Changing-With → The Living Self-With

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Here is your companion. You are together now. Who are you? Still you? Or have you changed? Are you still the same person you were alone? Show me." The contact boundary is: "Can you be a self WITH another?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player's self-concept respond to relationship? The Fantasy-Prisoner inflates further ("I'm the LEADER, the special one"). The Self-Stranger dissolves into companion ("I'm whoever they are"). The Premature Ego dominates ("I'm the strong one, they follow"). The Identity-Refuser merges ("We're the same, there's no difference").
- *Emergent:* Can they maintain self-identity while genuinely relating — neither inflating nor dissolving?

**Integration path:** Rewards SELF-MAINTENANCE IN RELATIONSHIP — being "I" while being "with." Not inflating, not dissolving, not dominating, not merging.

**Successful integration:** The player maintains self-concept while in relationship — the relational foundation of identity.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Self-With: a relational self-exploration space. Player and companion interact. During interaction, self-identity is monitored. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage companion-circle — two figures in a sacred space, each with their own glow (self-image), observing whether glows remain distinct or merge.

### Interaction
- **The First Together (1-5):** Companion appears. "Who are you now? Still you?" Confirm self-identity.
- **The Self-Beside (5-15):** Interact with companion while maintaining self. "Do things together. Stay you."
- **The Different-Together (15-30):** Companion is DIFFERENT. "They're not like you. Can you be you AND be with them?"
- **The Changing-With (30-50):** Companion challenges self-image. "They say you're different. Are you?"
- **The Living Self-With (50+):** Full relational self: maintain + share + receive + grow + stay.

### Feedback
- Self-maintained → "You're still you! Even together. Your glow is yours. Beautiful."
- Inflating → "Smaller. Not the leader. Just you. Equal. They're real too. Both of you."
- Dissolving → "You're still here! Your name. Your glow. Still yours. Even with them."
- Dominating → "Not over them. Beside them. Equal. Can you be WITH, not above?"
- Merging → "Different. You're different from them. And that's okay. Different AND together."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Companion similarity: very different (easy to distinguish) → similar → near-identical
- Social pressure: none → mild → strong (companion challenges self-image)
- Duration: brief interaction → sustained relationship
- Complexity: one companion → multiple companions
- Challenge: passive companion → active companion → challenging companion

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Together | 1-5 | Basic self-maintenance with companion |
| The Self-Beside | 5-15 | Self-maintenance during interaction |
| The Different-Together | 15-30 | Self-maintenance with different other |
| The Changing-With | 30-50 | Self-maintenance under social challenge |
| The Living Self-With | 50+ | Full relational self-integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Similar companions:** Easy to maintain self (clearly different)
- **Mirror companions:** Reflect player back (test dissolution)
- **Challenging companions:** Question player's identity
- **Group contexts:** Multiple companions (test self among many)
- **Growth companions:** Companions who model next-stage self

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ similar, 15+ mirror, 15+ challenging, 10+ group, 10+ growth

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Inflates with companion → dark-addiction; dissolves → dark-allergy
- Dominates → golden-addiction; deliberately merges → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (confirm self-identity); gesture (interact with companion); hold (maintain self during challenge)

### Timing requirements
- Self-identity checks during interaction; no time pressure; relationship-fatigue monitored

### NPC/AI requirements
- Companion system (various companion types with different relational styles)
- Self-identity tracking (before/during/after interaction comparison)
- Challenge system (companions that test self-maintenance)
- Difficulty adaptation (similarity, pressure, duration)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic self-consistency tracking. LLM for companion behaviour generation and nuanced shadow detection.

### State persistence
- Self-maintenance scores; flexibility metrics; mutuality quality; differentiation capacity; consistency across companions; inflation indicators; dissolution indicators; domination indicators; merger indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
