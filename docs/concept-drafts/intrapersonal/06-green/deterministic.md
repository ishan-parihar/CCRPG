# Intrapersonal / Green — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Objective multiplicity measurement — paradox tolerance, self-concept flexibility, identity-switching accuracy.  **Why this axis for this module:** Multiplicity must be measurable without language, body, or social confounds; pure cognitive-identity flexibility isolated.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** Prism Self  
**Core loop:** Player navigates identity-sorting tasks where multiple self-descriptors must be held simultaneously, switched between contextually, and maintained under cognitive load. Reaction-time and accuracy metrics reveal rigidity (DAll), fragmentation (DA), bypass (GA), or refusal-to-commit (GAll) without any verbal or social component.  
**Session length:** 4–8 minutes per checkpoint.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each sorting block is self-contained.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Identity-flexibility demands that escalate from binary self-concept switching to simultaneous multi-aspect holding.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DAll surfaces as slower switching and error on multiplicity trials. DA surfaces as random responding (no coherent centre). GA surfaces as premature "meta" responses that skip the holding task. GAll surfaces as equal-weighting all aspects (no contextual prioritisation).  
**Drive probes:** Agency measured via centre-maintenance under load. Communion measured via relational-context sensitivity. Eros measured via willingness to attempt higher-complexity trials. Agape measured via return-to-basics accuracy after stretch trials.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Identity Card Sort:** Self-aspect cards (e.g., "creative," "analytical," "nurturing") appear. Player must sort into "me-now" vs "me-also" vs "not-me" under time pressure. Healthy Green holds many in "me-also" without collapsing into "me-now" or rejecting into "not-me."  
**Mechanic 2 — Context Switch:** Scenarios flash (work/family/alone/crisis) and player must rapidly re-sort which aspects are foregrounded. Speed and accuracy of switching without losing the background aspects.  
**Mechanic 3 — Paradox Hold:** Two contradictory self-descriptors appear simultaneously. Player must hold both as valid (button-hold task) for increasing durations. Release = collapse. Premature meta-response = bypass detection.  
**Progression:** Complexity increases from 3 aspects → 5 → 8. Context-switch speed increases. Paradox-hold duration extends. Adaptive staircase per player.

## 4. Item Pool

- 40 self-aspect descriptors (balanced across cognitive, emotional, relational, somatic, spiritual domains)
- 12 context scenarios (work, family, solitude, crisis, play, intimacy, conflict, creativity, rest, service, learning, celebration)
- 8 paradox pairs (e.g., "strong/vulnerable," "leader/follower," "certain/uncertain," "independent/belonging")
- Difficulty parameters: number of aspects (3–8), switch speed (3s–0.8s), hold duration (2s–12s)

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Tap/click for sorting; sustained press for paradox-hold; swipe for context-switch.  
**Metrics captured:** Sort accuracy, switch latency, hold duration, error patterns (fragmentation vs rigidity vs bypass vs fortress), drive-balance ratios.  
**Adaptive engine:** IRT-based difficulty staircase. Theta estimated per drive axis. Shadow classification via error-pattern clustering (k=4 shadow types).  
**State persistence:** Per-trial data stored. Session theta saved at each checkpoint. Cross-session trajectory tracked for decay/growth curves.  
**Accessibility:** Colour-blind safe card designs. Adjustable time pressure for motor impairment. Audio cues for hold-task feedback.
