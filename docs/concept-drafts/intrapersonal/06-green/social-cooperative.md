# Intrapersonal / Green — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Plural self in relation — how multiplicity functions in group, maintaining coherence while showing different aspects.  **Why this axis for this module:** The social field is where multiplicity is most tested; showing different selves to different people is either authentic contextual expression or fragmented performance — the group reveals which.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** Facets in Circle  
**Core loop:** Player participates in simulated group interactions where different social contexts call for different self-aspects. Player must navigate showing authentic multiplicity (not a fixed mask, not chaotic shapeshifting) while maintaining relational coherence. Group feedback reveals whether multiplicity reads as authentic or shadow-driven.  
**Session length:** 6–10 minutes per social scenario.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each group scenario is self-contained.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Social situations that demand different self-aspects in rapid succession or simultaneously, creating the developmental demand to be authentically multiple in the eyes of others.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as inconsistent self-presentation that confuses the group (no one knows "who you are"). DAll surfaces as rigid social mask (same presentation regardless of context, others feel they never see the real you). GA surfaces as performed equanimity ("I'm just witnessing this interaction") that creates distance. GAll surfaces as showing ALL aspects to everyone equally (over-sharing, no social discernment).  
**Drive probes:** Agency = maintaining centre while adapting presentation. Communion = genuine responsiveness to group needs. Eros = risking new self-aspects in social space. Agape = returning to familiar aspects when group needs grounding.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Context Shifting:** Player moves between 3 social sub-groups in a gathering (e.g., colleagues, close friends, new acquaintances). Each context calls for different self-aspects. Player selects which aspects to foreground in each.  
**Mechanic 2 — Coherence Check:** NPCs from different sub-groups interact, comparing notes on the player. If presentations are too different → fragmentation flag. If too same → rigidity flag. Sweet spot = recognisably the same person expressed differently.  
**Mechanic 3 — Vulnerability Bid:** Moments arise where showing a less-familiar self-aspect to a group would deepen connection but risks rejection. Player chooses whether to bid. Quality of bid (timing, context-appropriateness) scored.  
**Progression:** 2 sub-groups → 3 → 4. Overlap between groups increases. Stakes of vulnerability bids increase. NPCs become more perceptive (harder to perform without authenticity).

## 4. Item Pool

- 6 social contexts (professional, intimate, casual, mentoring, conflict, celebration)
- 8 self-aspects available for foregrounding (maps to strategic-planning aspects for cross-modality coherence)
- 20 NPC archetypes with distinct relational needs and perceptiveness levels
- 12 vulnerability-bid opportunities (graded by risk and potential reward)
- Coherence rubric: 5-point scale from "fragmented stranger" to "authentic multiplicity"

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Selection-based (choose which aspect to foreground), dialogue choices for vulnerability bids, tap to move between sub-groups.  
**Metrics captured:** Aspect-selection patterns per context, coherence scores from NPC feedback, vulnerability-bid frequency and quality, shadow classification from social presentation patterns.  
**Adaptive engine:** NPC perceptiveness scales with player level. Context complexity increases. Vulnerability-bid opportunities calibrated to player's edge (not too safe, not overwhelming).  
**Social simulation:** NPCs maintain memory across the scenario — inconsistency is noticed and flagged naturally through dialogue, not as explicit game feedback.  
**Accessibility:** All interactions via selection (no time pressure). NPC expressions conveyed through text and icon (not solely facial animation). Simplified group-count for younger players.
