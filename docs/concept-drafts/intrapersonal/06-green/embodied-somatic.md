# Intrapersonal / Green — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Body as site of plural self — multiple body-states, somatic paradox, embodied multiplicity.  **Why this axis for this module:** Multiplicity that lives only in thought is incomplete; genuine Green intrapersonal capacity requires the body to hold multiple states without rigidifying or fragmenting.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** Body of Many  
**Core loop:** Player navigates somatic tasks requiring simultaneous or rapid-switching embodiment of different self-states. The body becomes the testing ground for whether multiplicity is genuinely integrated or merely conceptual. Rhythm, posture-shifting, and breath-pattern tasks reveal somatic shadow patterns.  
**Session length:** 4–7 minutes per checkpoint.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each somatic sequence is self-contained.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Somatic demands that require the body to express multiplicity — holding two body-states, switching between embodied identities, maintaining somatic coherence across contradictory physical expressions.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as somatic chaos (body cannot settle into any state, random movement). DAll surfaces as somatic rigidity (body locked in one posture/rhythm, refuses to shift). GA surfaces as disembodied observation (player watches body rather than inhabiting it). GAll surfaces as equal-energy all-states (no somatic prioritisation, exhausting diffusion).  
**Drive probes:** Agency = somatic centre maintenance during shifts. Communion = body responding to relational rhythm cues. Eros = reaching toward unfamiliar body-states. Agape = returning to ground after expansion.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — State Embodiment:** Player must match a target body-state (via rhythm tapping, breath pattern, or posture gesture) that corresponds to a self-aspect. Accuracy and speed of embodiment measured.  
**Mechanic 2 — Somatic Switching:** Two or more body-states alternate rapidly. Player must switch cleanly without residue from the previous state bleeding through (measured via rhythm accuracy post-switch).  
**Mechanic 3 — Paradox Posture:** Two contradictory somatic states must be held simultaneously (e.g., grounded + expansive, tense + flowing). Player finds the both/and in the body. Duration and stability measured.  
**Progression:** Single-state mastery → dual-state switching → triple-state cycling → paradox-hold. Tempo increases. States become more subtle and contradictory.

## 4. Item Pool

- 8 primary body-states mapped to self-aspects (grounded/achiever, expansive/seeker, contracted/protector, flowing/nurturer, sharp/analyst, soft/empath, still/witness, dynamic/creator)
- 12 switching sequences (graded by speed and number of states)
- 6 paradox-posture pairs (grounded+expansive, tense+flowing, still+dynamic, sharp+soft, contracted+open, heavy+light)
- Rhythm patterns: 4/4 baseline with syncopation for switching, polyrhythm for paradox-hold

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Touchscreen rhythm taps, accelerometer for posture/gesture (mobile), or keyboard rhythm input (desktop). Optional: device motion for posture detection.  
**Metrics captured:** Rhythm accuracy per state, switch latency, residue bleed (errors in first 2 beats post-switch), paradox-hold duration and stability, somatic shadow classification.  
**Adaptive engine:** Tempo and complexity adjusted via staircase. State-combinations selected to probe suspected shadow (rigid players get more switching; fragmented players get more centre-finding).  
**Embodiment validation:** Minimum engagement threshold — player must demonstrate genuine somatic participation (not just cognitive pattern-matching of rhythms).  
**Accessibility:** Multiple input modalities (tap, voice rhythm, breath via microphone). Reduced tempo mode. Visual-only mode for hearing-impaired players.
