# Intrapersonal × Infrared — Language-Reflective

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Proto-Self Vocalisations.** The avatar produces simple sounds — grunts, hums, cries. The player must distinguish self-directed sounds (comfort-hum, self-soothing murmur) from other-directed sounds (alarm-cry, call-out). This is pre-verbal self-expression: the seed of 'I am making a sound for myself' vs. 'I am making a sound at the world.'

### 1.1 Sound Design

- **Self-directed:** low-frequency hums, rhythmic self-soothing patterns, closed-mouth vocalisations
- **Other-directed:** sharp cries, open-mouth calls, directional vocalisations aimed outward
- No words. No symbols. Only tonal quality, directionality, and rhythm encode self/other orientation
- Player input: tap-and-hold for self-directed, flick-outward for other-directed

### 1.2 Difficulty Staircase

- Level 1: maximally distinct (deep hum vs. sharp cry)
- Level 2: same volume, different directionality
- Level 3: ambiguous vocalisations (whimper — self-soothing or distress-call?)
- Level 4: sequences where the same sound shifts from self-directed to other-directed mid-stream

## 2. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Pattern | Behavioural Signal |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| DA (Boundary-Fixation) | All sounds classified as other-directed — cannot recognise self-soothing | Self-directed accuracy < 30% |
| DAll (Boundary-Dissolution) | All sounds classified as self-directed — no outward orientation | Other-directed accuracy < 30% |
| GA (Premature Self-Image) | Player adds elaboration to simple sounds — over-interprets proto-self | Attempts complex input patterns on simple stimuli |
| GAll (Boundary Fortress) | Accurate classification but avoids producing self-directed sounds in active trials | Self-directed production latency > 3x other-directed |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 3.1 Catalyst

Ambiguous vocalisations at the boundary between self-soothing and world-calling. The game adapts to deliver sounds at the player's discrimination edge.

### 3.2 Experience

Correct self-directed classification: avatar curls inward slightly (self-containment). Correct other-directed classification: avatar orients outward (world-facing). The felt-sense of sound-as-boundary-marker.

### 3.3 Integration

The avatar develops a 'voice-boundary' — a subtle aural field that distinguishes its own sound-space from environmental sound. Shadow-mode: the player's bias-pattern manifests as a sound-entity (a drone that won't stop calling outward, or a hum that never reaches the world).

## 4. Progression

- **Diagnosis (trials 1–15):** Baseline classification accuracy and directional bias
- **Healing (trials 16–45):** If DA-biased, increase self-directed sound exposure with confirmation feedback; if DAll-biased, increase other-directed with boundary-sharpening cues
- **Evolution (trials 46+):** Introduce proto-naming — sounds that begin to carry self-referential quality, seeding Magenta's magical self-concept

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** classification accuracy (self-directed vs. other-directed)
- **Bias metric:** directional preference ratio — skew toward self = DAll, skew toward other = DA
- **Production metric:** latency and willingness to generate self-directed sounds in active trials
- **Drive-health:** balanced accuracy with willing self-directed production = healthy proto-voice
- **Shadow-drag:** bias magnitude + production avoidance, weighted 0.18
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.10 when emotional-line affect-recognition is also healthy (sound carries feeling)
