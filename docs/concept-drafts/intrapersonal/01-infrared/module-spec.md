# Intrapersonal × Infrared — Module Spec

## 1. Module Identity

- **Line:** Intrapersonal (self-knowledge, self-relation)
- **Stage:** Infrared / Archaic (Red-Ray, sensorimotor, survival)
- **Core Capacity:** Self-Other Differentiation — can the organism distinguish 'me' from 'not-me'?
- **Developmental Essence:** Pre-introspective. No 'I' yet. Pure body-boundary awareness. The seed of all later self-knowledge is knowing where the body ends and the world begins.
- **Below:** Nothing (first stage)
- **Above:** Magenta intrapersonal — magical self-concept, fantasy-image identity

## 2. Shadow Taxonomy

| Quadrant | Name | Pattern |
|----------|------|---------|
| Dark-Addiction | Boundary-Fixation | Hypervigilant self-other boundary; cannot relax; defensive self-enclosure |
| Dark-Allergy | Boundary-Dissolution | Cannot distinguish self from environment; merged with world; no sense of where 'I' end |
| Golden-Addiction | Premature Self-Image | Attempts fantasy-identity without genuine body-boundary; 'I am special' without body-ground |
| Golden-Allergy | Boundary Fortress | Self-other distinction present but refuses self-emergence; terrified of becoming a 'someone' |

## 3. Compound Shadows

1. **The Armoured Seed** (Intrapersonal DA × Somatic DA): boundary-fixation + hyperarousal = rigid body-boundary under constant threat
2. **The Dissolved Body** (Intrapersonal DAll × Emotional DAll): boundary-dissolution + affect-numbness = no self, no feeling
3. **The Premature Someone** (Intrapersonal GA × Cognitive GA): premature self-image + premature symbolism = 'identity' without body-ground
4. **The Boundary Fortress** (Intrapersonal GAll × Interpersonal GAll): refuses self-emergence + refuses co-presence = isolated non-self

## 4. Drive Model

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|-------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Eros (vertical-up) | Reaching toward self-emergence from undifferentiated ground | Golden-Addiction: premature self-image bypass |
| Agape (vertical-down) | Relaxing back into body-ground, accepting pre-self state | Dark-Addiction: fixated boundary-rigidity |
| Agency (horizontal-out) | Asserting body-boundary, claiming space as 'mine' | Golden-Allergy: boundary present but refuses emergence |
| Communion (horizontal-in) | Permeable boundary, allowing world-contact without dissolution | Dark-Allergy: boundary-dissolution, merger |

## 5. Dual Vectors

- **Heal/Evolve (bottom-up):** Agape + Agency integrate dark shadows — relax fixation while asserting genuine boundary
- **Evolve/Heal (top-down):** Eros + Communion dissolve golden shadows — reach toward emergence while staying permeable
