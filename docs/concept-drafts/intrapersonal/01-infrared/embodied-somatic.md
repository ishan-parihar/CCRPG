# Intrapersonal × Infrared — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Body-Boundary Mapping.** The player traces, discovers, and maintains awareness of where the avatar's body ends. This is the most literal modality for Intrapersonal/Infrared — the body-boundary IS the proto-self. Skin-awareness, proprioceptive edges, the felt-sense of body-limit.

### 1.1 Mechanic Design

- The avatar's body-outline is initially invisible/fuzzy. The player activates body-regions by directing attention (touch/hover) to them
- Each region 'lights up' when attended to — proprioceptive confirmation
- The player must trace the complete body-boundary without gaps
- Environmental intrusions (fog, tendrils, pressure) attempt to blur the boundary — player must re-assert it

### 1.2 Difficulty Staircase

- Level 1: large body-regions, clear edges, no environmental interference
- Level 2: smaller regions, subtle edges (between fingers, behind ears)
- Level 3: environmental fog obscures sections — player must find boundary from proprioceptive memory
- Level 4: dynamic boundary — avatar is moving, boundary shifts with posture, player must track in real-time

## 2. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Pattern | Behavioural Signal |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| DA (Boundary-Fixation) | Player traces boundary obsessively, re-traces completed sections, cannot move on | Re-trace rate > 40%, time-on-completed > time-on-new |
| DAll (Boundary-Dissolution) | Player cannot complete boundary — leaves gaps, traces into environment | Gap rate > 30%, traces extend beyond body-edge |
| GA (Premature Self-Image) | Player traces a 'desired' boundary larger than actual body — inflated body-image | Traced area > 130% of actual avatar boundary |
| GAll (Boundary Fortress) | Player traces accurately but refuses to 'activate' — traces without lighting up | Trace-without-activation rate > 50% |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 3.1 Catalyst

Environmental boundary-challenges: fog that dissolves edges, pressure that compresses them, warmth that expands them. Each challenges the player's body-boundary model at its weakest point.

### 3.2 Experience

Successful boundary-assertion: the traced region glows with warmth, the avatar 'feels' that part of itself. Failed assertion: the region remains dim or merges with environment. The felt-sense of 'I end here.'

### 3.3 Integration

Complete boundary-mapping produces a fully-lit avatar — the first whole-body self-awareness. Shadow-mode: the player's boundary-pattern manifests as a body-distortion (rigid shell for DA, dissolving edges for DAll, inflated aura for GA, dim-but-present outline for GAll).

## 4. Progression

- **Diagnosis (phase 1):** How completely and accurately can the player map their body-boundary? Where are the gaps and distortions?
- **Healing (phase 2):** Guided attention to neglected or distorted boundary-regions. If DA, gentle boundary-softening. If DAll, boundary-sharpening with proprioceptive anchors.
- **Evolution (phase 3):** The mapped boundary begins to pulse — proto-aliveness. The body is not just bounded but INHABITED. Seeds Magenta's 'I am alive in here.'

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** boundary-completion percentage (how much of the body-edge is accurately mapped)
- **Accuracy metric:** boundary-precision (deviation from actual avatar edge)
- **Pattern metric:** trace-behaviour analysis (obsessive re-tracing = DA, gaps = DAll, inflation = GA, dim-tracing = GAll)
- **Drive-health:** complete, accurate, activated boundary = healthy body-self
- **Shadow-drag:** incompleteness + distortion + behavioural anomaly severity, weighted 0.18
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.10 when somatic-line body-awareness scores are also healthy (proprioception grounds boundary)
