# Intrapersonal × Infrared — Scenario-Choice

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Mine vs. Not-Mine Decisions.** The avatar encounters objects and sensations. The player must decide — based purely on body-sensation cues — whether something belongs to the self-boundary or lies outside it. No reasoning, no morality. Only: does this feel like 'mine' or 'not-mine'?

### 1.1 Scenario Design

- Objects drift toward the avatar. Each carries a body-sensation cue (warmth = self-resonant, cold = other-resonant, neutral = ambiguous)
- Player choice: pull inward (claim as 'mine') or push outward (reject as 'not-mine')
- Correct answers are determined by whether the object originated from the avatar's own body-field or from the environment
- No narrative framing. Pure sensation-based ownership decisions.

### 1.2 Difficulty Staircase

- Level 1: objects are clearly self-originated (shed skin, exhaled breath-cloud) or clearly external (falling rock, drifting debris)
- Level 2: objects at body-boundary (sweat droplet — still mine? or now environment?)
- Level 3: objects that were self but became other (cut hair, shed scale)
- Level 4: objects that were other but feel self (absorbed warmth, ingested food becoming body)

## 2. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Pattern | Behavioural Signal |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| DA (Boundary-Fixation) | Claims everything near body as 'mine' — hoarding boundary | Over-claiming rate > 65% |
| DAll (Boundary-Dissolution) | Rejects self-originated objects as 'not-mine' — cannot own | Over-rejection of self-objects > 65% |
| GA (Premature Self-Image) | Claims objects based on desirability rather than body-sensation | Accuracy uncorrelated with sensation-cue, correlated with object-appeal |
| GAll (Boundary Fortress) | Accurate but refuses to claim even clearly self-originated objects | Self-object claim latency > 2.5x other-object rejection latency |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 3.1 Catalyst

Ambiguous-ownership objects that sit exactly at the body-boundary. The game adapts to present objects at the player's ownership-discrimination threshold.

### 3.2 Experience

Correct 'mine' claim: object absorbs into avatar's body-outline, boundary brightens. Correct 'not-mine' rejection: object drifts away, boundary clarifies by contrast. Errors: boundary flickers with confusion.

### 3.3 Integration

The avatar's body-field becomes more defined — a clear zone of 'mine' surrounded by clear 'not-mine.' Shadow-mode: the player's ownership-bias manifests as a creature that hoards everything (DA) or dissolves into the environment (DAll).

## 4. Progression

- **Diagnosis (scenarios 1–12):** Establish ownership-bias direction and magnitude
- **Healing (scenarios 13–36):** Target the bias — if DA, present clearly-not-mine objects with gentle release cues; if DAll, present clearly-mine objects with ownership-confirmation
- **Evolution (scenarios 37+):** Introduce objects that require CHOOSING to own — proto-agency, seeding the emergence of 'I want this to be mine'

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** ownership-discrimination accuracy (correctly claiming self-objects, correctly rejecting other-objects)
- **Bias metric:** claim-ratio — over-claiming = DA, over-rejecting = DAll
- **Choice-quality metric:** correlation between sensation-cue and decision (low correlation = GA bypass)
- **Drive-health:** accurate ownership with sensation-grounded decisions = healthy boundary-choice
- **Shadow-drag:** bias magnitude + sensation-decision decorrelation, weighted 0.18
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.10 when somatic-line proprioception is also healthy (body-ground supports ownership)
