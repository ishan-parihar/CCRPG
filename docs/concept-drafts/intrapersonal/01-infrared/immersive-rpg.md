# Intrapersonal × Infrared — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Ecological Self-Other.** An open micro-environment where the avatar exists freely. No explicit instructions. The game observes whether body-boundary awareness emerges SPONTANEOUSLY during free-play. Does the player naturally distinguish self from environment, or do they need prompting? This is the ecological validity check for all other modalities.

### 1.1 World Design

- A small, sensory-rich environment: textures, temperatures, moving objects, ambient creatures
- The avatar can move, touch, interact with anything — no goals, no prompts
- Environmental elements occasionally contact the avatar — the game silently tracks whether the player reacts differently to self-contact vs. other-contact
- Hidden boundary-probes embedded in natural interactions (a vine brushes the avatar — does the player treat it as self or other?)

### 1.2 Observation Metrics

- Spontaneous self-touch frequency (does the player explore their own avatar's body?)
- Self-other response differentiation (different reactions to self-generated vs. external stimuli)
- Boundary-assertion behaviours (does the player naturally avoid boundary-violations?)
- Exploration pattern (does the player move AS a bounded entity or as a diffuse presence?)

## 2. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Pattern | Behavioural Signal |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| DA (Boundary-Fixation) | Player avoids all environmental contact — stays in centre, touches nothing | Interaction rate < 20th percentile, movement radius minimal |
| DAll (Boundary-Dissolution) | Player moves through objects without reacting — no boundary-awareness | Self-other response differentiation near zero |
| GA (Premature Self-Image) | Player performs elaborate self-display without exploring body-boundary | Display behaviours > exploration behaviours |
| GAll (Boundary Fortress) | Player explores environment extensively but never touches own avatar | Self-touch frequency = 0, other-touch frequency high |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 3.1 Catalyst

The environment subtly adapts to the player's shadow-pattern. If DA, gentle invitations to interact appear. If DAll, boundary-highlighting events occur (a glow where avatar meets world). If GAll, self-touch opportunities are made more salient.

### 3.2 Experience

Natural, unforced. The player simply exists in the world. Boundary-awareness emerges (or doesn't) organically. No feedback loops — the game observes without judging. The felt-sense is simply 'being here, in this body, in this world.'

### 3.3 Integration

Over sessions, the avatar's relationship to the environment becomes more differentiated — clearer boundary, more confident interaction, neither avoidant nor merged. The ecological self emerges as the ground for all structured modalities.

## 4. Progression

- **Diagnosis (sessions 1–3):** Pure observation. Establish baseline ecological boundary-behaviour without any intervention.
- **Healing (sessions 4–8):** Subtle environmental adaptations target the observed shadow-pattern. No explicit instruction — only environmental affordances shift.
- **Evolution (sessions 9+):** The environment introduces proto-narrative elements — objects that 'belong' to the avatar, spaces that are 'home.' Seeds Magenta's 'my world' concept.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** ecological boundary-differentiation index (composite of self-other response difference, self-touch frequency, boundary-assertion rate)
- **Naturalness metric:** how spontaneously boundary-awareness emerges without prompting
- **Consistency metric:** boundary-behaviour stability across sessions
- **Drive-health:** spontaneous, consistent, differentiated boundary-behaviour in free-play = healthy ecological self
- **Shadow-drag:** absence of differentiation + avoidance patterns + display-without-ground, weighted 0.18
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.10 when all other intrapersonal modality scores are healthy (ecological validity confirms structured findings)
