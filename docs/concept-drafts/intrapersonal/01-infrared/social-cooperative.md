# Intrapersonal × Infrared — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Core Mechanic

**Boundary-in-Relation.** The player's avatar exists near another entity. The challenge: maintain body-boundary awareness WHILE in proximity to another. Co-presence without merger, distinction without isolation. The proto-social intrapersonal capacity: 'I am still me when you are near.'

### 1.1 Mechanic Design

- A companion entity moves near the avatar. Their body-fields overlap at edges.
- The player must continuously track their OWN boundary (not the other's)
- Proximity challenges: the closer the other entity, the harder it is to maintain self-boundary
- Player input: periodic boundary-confirmation taps on their own avatar (not the other)

### 1.2 Difficulty Staircase

- Level 1: other entity is distant — boundary-maintenance is easy
- Level 2: other entity is close — boundary-fields overlap slightly
- Level 3: other entity mirrors the avatar's movements — boundary-confusion increases
- Level 4: multiple entities surround the avatar — maintaining self among many

## 2. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Pattern | Behavioural Signal |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| DA (Boundary-Fixation) | Player pushes other entity away or refuses proximity — cannot co-exist | Proximity-avoidance actions > 50% of inputs |
| DAll (Boundary-Dissolution) | Player begins tapping the OTHER entity as self — boundaries merge | Other-entity taps > 30% of self-confirmation taps |
| GA (Premature Self-Image) | Player claims the other entity's boundary as extension of self | Boundary-traces encompass both entities |
| GAll (Boundary Fortress) | Player maintains perfect boundary but moves to maximum distance — isolation | Average distance > 80th percentile, zero proximity tolerance |

## 3. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 3.1 Catalyst

The other entity moves closer. The game adapts proximity to the player's boundary-maintenance threshold — always at the edge of where self-tracking becomes difficult.

### 3.2 Experience

Maintained self-boundary during proximity: warm glow on own avatar, clear distinction visible. Lost boundary: avatar's outline blurs into the other. Isolation: avatar's outline hardens but dims (present but disconnected).

### 3.3 Integration

Successful co-presence builds 'relational-boundary' — the avatar can be near others without losing self or rejecting them. Shadow-mode: the player's relational pattern manifests as a companion that clings (triggering DA), dissolves into them (triggering DAll), or flees (triggering GAll).

## 4. Progression

- **Diagnosis (phase 1):** Baseline proximity-tolerance and boundary-maintenance accuracy at various distances
- **Healing (phase 2):** If DA, gentle proximity-increase with boundary-confirmation support. If DAll, boundary-sharpening exercises during proximity. If GAll, reward for maintained proximity.
- **Evolution (phase 3):** Introduce cooperative boundary-tasks — both entities must maintain their own boundaries while completing a shared goal. Seeds Magenta's relational self.

## 5. Scoring

- **Primary metric:** boundary-maintenance accuracy during proximity (self-confirmation tap accuracy)
- **Proximity metric:** minimum tolerated distance while maintaining boundary-integrity
- **Relational metric:** co-presence duration (time spent near other with intact boundary)
- **Drive-health:** close proximity + accurate self-boundary + no avoidance = healthy relational-self
- **Shadow-drag:** avoidance-rate + merger-rate + isolation-distance, weighted 0.18
- **Cross-line bonus:** +0.10 when interpersonal-line co-presence scores are also healthy (social capacity supports relational boundary)
