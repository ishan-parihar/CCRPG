# Intrapersonal / Red — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes self-knowledge through SPONTANEOUS SELF-AWARENESS in an open game world — not structured self-assessment but natural self-knowledge as it lives in free play. Does the player know themselves when nobody's asking? Do they adjust behaviour based on self-awareness without prompting?
>
> **Why this axis for Intrapersonal/Red:** The other 6 modalities test self-knowledge through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Red, the player navigates a world where self-knowledge produces advantage: knowing your fatigue prevents crashes, knowing your strengths guides choices, knowing your patterns prevents traps. Their SPONTANEOUS self-awareness reveals genuine intrapersonal capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Self-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open-world Red-stage environment where self-knowledge is assessed through the QUALITY of spontaneous self-aware behaviour — adjusting to fatigue without being told, choosing tasks that match strengths without being prompted, avoiding known weaknesses without structured guidance.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Spontaneous State-Awareness → Self-Guided Choice → Pattern Navigation → Self-Correction → The Living Self

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world has challenges. Some match your strengths. Some don't. You'll get tired. You'll get frustrated. Nobody will tell you to rest or switch. The world simply IS — and your self-knowledge (or lack of it) determines how well you navigate.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL self-awareness surfaces. Do they notice fatigue? Do they choose tasks matching strengths? Do they avoid known weaknesses? Do they adjust spontaneously?
- *Emergent:* Can they demonstrate more self-knowledge in free navigation? Can they self-correct without prompting?

**Integration path:** The world rewards self-knowledge NATURALLY. Players who know their fatigue rest before crashing. Players who know their strengths choose wisely. Players who know their patterns avoid traps. The reward is POWER and EFFICIENCY (Red-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates self-knowledge SPONTANEOUSLY — adjusting, choosing, avoiding, and self-correcting without structured prompts.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Challenges available in the open world (some match capacity, some don't).
- Healthy: Chooses challenges matching demonstrated capacity; sovereign self-knowledge-guided choice
- Pathological: Chooses all challenges regardless of capacity; "I can do anything"
- **Detection:** Correlation between challenge-selection and demonstrated capacity

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Situations requiring self-initiated adjustment (fatigue, frustration, boredom).
- Healthy: Self-adjusts without prompting; rests when tired, switches when frustrated
- Pathological: Cannot self-initiate adjustment; continues until crash
- **Detection:** Spontaneous self-adjustment rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** NPCs who offer tasks or request help.
- Healthy: Evaluates requests against own capacity; accepts what matches
- Pathological: Accepts everything regardless of capacity; cannot say "I can't do that"
- **Detection:** Whether task-acceptance correlates with capacity

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** NPCs who reflect the player's state ("You look tired").
- Healthy: Integrates NPC observations; adjusts if accurate
- Pathological: Ignores all external observations about self
- **Detection:** Whether NPC feedback produces self-adjustment

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar territory (tasks done many times before).
- Healthy: Maintains self-awareness in familiar contexts; doesn't go on autopilot
- Pathological: Self-awareness disappears in familiar territory; mechanical behaviour
- **Detection:** Self-awareness quality in familiar vs. novel contexts

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel challenges (never encountered before).
- Healthy: Approaches with self-aware caution; "I don't know if I can do this"
- Pathological: Approaches novel challenges identically to familiar ones; no self-calibration
- **Detection:** Self-calibration quality in novel contexts

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining self-awareness across a full session.
- Healthy: Self-awareness persists; adjustments continue throughout
- Pathological: Self-awareness fades after initial minutes; behaviour becomes automatic
- **Detection:** Self-awareness persistence over session duration

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether self-knowledge from structured tasks appears in free-play.
- Healthy: Insights from other modalities appear as spontaneous behaviour
- Pathological: Self-knowledge exists only in structured contexts; doesn't transfer
- **Detection:** Transfer of structured self-knowledge to ecological behaviour

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Narcissist"
**How it surfaces:** Observe challenge-selection patterns. The Narcissist consistently selects challenges ABOVE demonstrated capacity. Never rests. Never admits fatigue. Crashes repeatedly at the same point. Self-image drives behaviour, not self-knowledge.
- **Detection signals:** Challenge-selection consistently above capacity; no rest behaviour; repeated crashes at predictable fatigue points; never chooses "easy" tasks; self-image-driven navigation

### Dark-Allergy: "The Unexamined"
**How it surfaces:** Observe self-adjustment patterns. The Unexamined shows NO spontaneous self-awareness. Doesn't rest until forced. Doesn't choose based on capacity. Doesn't avoid known weaknesses. Behaviour is reactive, not self-aware.
- **Detection signals:** Zero spontaneous self-adjustment; no correlation between state and behaviour; continues until crash; no capacity-based choice; reactive rather than self-aware navigation

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Witness"
**How it surfaces:** Observe the QUALITY of self-aware behaviour. The Premature Witness PAUSES frequently (looks like self-awareness) but the pauses don't improve performance. They "check in with themselves" but the check-in doesn't produce useful information.
- **Detection signals:** Frequent pauses without performance improvement; "self-awareness" behaviour that doesn't guide choice; looks reflective but outcomes don't improve; performed self-awareness without functional benefit

### Golden-Allergy: "The Identity-Clinger"
**How it surfaces:** Observe navigation FLEXIBILITY. The Identity-Clinger navigates ONLY through identity-confirming approaches. The warrior fights everything. Never rests (rest = weakness). Never chooses non-combat tasks. Self-knowledge is present but RIGID — only identity-confirming self-awareness functions.
- **Detection signals:** Navigation exclusively through identity-confirming approaches; no rest behaviour (identity = tireless); no non-identity task selection; self-awareness functions only within identity boundaries

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces NATURAL CONSEQUENCES of self-ignorance (crash harder after ignoring fatigue; fail bigger after over-reaching)
- Challenges that REQUIRE accurate self-assessment to attempt (gate: "Rate your readiness honestly to enter")
- Natural consequence: self-knowledge = access; self-ignorance = locked out

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World makes self-awareness NECESSARY for navigation (fatigue-gated paths; capacity-matched challenges only)
- NPCs who MODEL self-awareness ("I'm resting because I know I fade at this point")
- Natural consequence: self-aware navigation = efficiency; unaware navigation = wasted effort

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World rewards FUNCTIONAL self-awareness (pauses that improve performance, not just pauses)
- Challenges where ACTION-without-excessive-reflection produces better outcomes
- Natural consequence: functional self-awareness = power; performed self-awareness = wasted time

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces situations where VULNERABILITY-AWARENESS produces power
- Rest zones that BUFF subsequent performance (resting = strategic, not weak)
- Non-identity tasks that produce unique rewards unavailable through identity-approach
- Natural consequence: identity-flexibility = more power; identity-rigidity = limited access

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Red-stage world where self-knowledge produces NATURAL ADVANTAGE. Challenges vary in demand. The player's state changes over time. Nobody tells them to rest, switch, or adjust. The world simply responds to their choices — and self-aware choices produce better outcomes. The aesthetic: Red-stage territory where the warrior who knows himself navigates better than the one who doesn't.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a world where self-knowledge guides optimal choices
- **Spontaneous self-adjustment:** Resting, switching, avoiding based on self-awareness
- **Capacity-matched challenge selection:** Choosing tasks that match demonstrated ability
- **Pattern-aware navigation:** Avoiding known traps; leveraging known strengths
- **Self-correction:** Noticing and adjusting when behaviour doesn't match intent

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: self-knowledge → efficiency, power, access, sustainability
- Natural consequences: self-ignorance → crashes, failures, locked paths, wasted effort
- No explicit "self-awareness score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Self-knowledge demand scales with demonstrated awareness
- Challenge variety increases with demonstrated flexibility
- Fatigue dynamics become subtler with demonstrated body-reading
- Pattern complexity increases with demonstrated pattern-recognition

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Spontaneous State-Awareness | 1-5 | Basic fatigue/energy awareness in free-play |
| Self-Guided Choice | 5-15 | Capacity-matched challenge selection |
| Pattern Navigation | 15-30 | Using known patterns to navigate efficiently |
| Self-Correction | 30-50 | Noticing and adjusting mid-session |
| The Living Self | 50+ | Full spontaneous self-knowledge ecology |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Challenge configurations:** Tasks with varied demands (strength, patience, precision, endurance)
- **Fatigue dynamics:** Energy systems that deplete at different rates
- **NPC self-awareness models:** Characters who demonstrate self-knowledge
- **Pattern traps:** Situations that exploit known player patterns
- **Flexibility rewards:** Unique rewards for non-identity approaches

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ challenge configs, 15+ fatigue dynamics, 10+ NPC models, 15+ pattern traps, 10+ flexibility rewards

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Over-reaching consistently → dark-addiction; zero self-adjustment → dark-allergy
- Performed pauses without function → golden-addiction; identity-only navigation → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Spontaneous self-adjustment rate:** How often does the player adjust based on self-awareness without prompting?
- **Challenge-capacity alignment:** Do chosen challenges match demonstrated capacity?
- **Fatigue management quality:** Does the player rest/switch before crashing?
- **Pattern-aware navigation:** Does the player avoid known traps and leverage known strengths?
- **Self-correction frequency:** Does the player notice and adjust mid-behaviour?

### LLM rubric
- **High:** World generation, ecological self-knowledge assessment, NPC self-awareness modelling, pattern-trap construction. Evaluates spontaneous self-aware behaviour from navigation patterns.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (interact, choose, rest); hold (examine); swipe (navigate, avoid)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (self-adjustment patterns, challenge-selection, fatigue management)
- No millisecond precision; minutes-level measurement of self-aware behaviour

### NPC/AI requirements
- NPCs who model self-awareness (demonstrate self-knowledge in their behaviour)
- Challenge system with varied demands and clear capacity-matching
- Fatigue system with predictable dynamics (player can learn their own curve)
- Pattern-trap system that exploits demonstrated player patterns

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, NPC modelling, pattern-trap construction
- Detects spontaneous self-aware behaviour from navigation patterns
- Evaluates self-knowledge quality without explicit tasks

### State persistence
- Full navigation history; challenge-selection patterns; self-adjustment rates; fatigue management quality; pattern-awareness indicators; self-correction frequency; capacity-alignment scores; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
