# Somatic / Amber — Scenario-Choice

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes somatic intelligence through BODY-DECISIONS — dilemmas where the body must choose between competing forms, conflicting disciplines, or situations where somatic wisdom is needed.
>
> **Why this axis:** The disciplined body faces DILEMMAS — when two forms conflict, when the code demands what the body resists, when fatigue meets discipline. This modality tests somatic WISDOM: can the player make good decisions ABOUT their body within a rule-governed system?

---

## §1 Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body's Dilemma"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces SOMATIC DILEMMAS — situations where body-discipline conflicts with body-wisdom, where two forms compete, where the code demands and the body resists. Not just following form but CHOOSING wisely about form.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Clear Form → The Competing Forms → The Body's Limit → The Changing Code → The Living Wisdom

---

## §2 Catalyst Delivery

**What it uniquely presents:** Somatic DILEMMAS requiring wisdom — not just capacity but JUDGEMENT about the body within a rule-system.

**How it differs from other 6:** Only modality that tests somatic DECISION-MAKING. Not "can your body do it?" but "SHOULD your body do it? Which form? When? How much?" Tests the wisdom dimension of embodied discipline.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Dark-addiction as always-follows-code (ignores body-signals). Dark-allergy as always-follows-comfort (ignores discipline). Golden-addiction as always-chooses-harder. Golden-allergy as always-chooses-familiar. Choice PATTERNS reveal shadow structure.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates somatic wisdom — decisions that honour both the code AND the body.

---

## §3 Game Design

### Setup
A decision-space where somatic dilemmas are presented. Each dilemma has a context (body-state, form-demand, social situation) and options. Aesthetic: Amber-stage council-chamber — the body's wisdom honoured as a form of knowing.

### Interaction Phases
- **The Clear Form (1-5):** Simple dilemmas with clear wise choice. "Hold or release? (Body is fresh, form is short. Hold.)"
- **The Competing Forms (5-15):** Two valid forms compete. "Form A or Form B? Both valid. Which serves your body better now?"
- **The Body's Limit (15-30):** Fatigue-discipline dilemmas. "The code says continue. Your body signals fatigue. What's wise?"
- **The Changing Code (30-50):** The code changes mid-practice. "The form just changed. Adapt immediately or consolidate first?"
- **The Living Wisdom (50+):** Complex multi-factor dilemmas requiring integrated somatic wisdom.

### Feedback
- Wise choice → "Good. Your body and the code are honoured. That's somatic wisdom."
- Rigid choice → "You followed the code. But your body needed rest. Wisdom includes listening."
- Impulsive choice → "You followed comfort. But your body was ready for more. Wisdom includes persistence."
- Overreach choice → "You chose harder. But your body wasn't ready. Wisdom includes patience."
- Avoidant choice → "You chose familiar. But your body was ready to grow. Wisdom includes courage."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Dilemma complexity: clear → ambiguous → multi-factor
- Body-state: fresh → moderate fatigue → significant fatigue
- Stakes: low (practice) → moderate (performance) → high (injury-risk)
- Time pressure: unlimited → moderate → competing demands
- Social context: solo → dyad → group

### Progression Table
| Phase | Checkpoints | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Clear Form | 1-5 | Simple dilemmas, clear wise choice |
| The Competing Forms | 5-15 | Two valid options, context-dependent |
| The Body's Limit | 15-30 | Fatigue-discipline balance |
| The Changing Code | 30-50 | Adaptation dilemmas |
| The Living Wisdom | 50+ | Complex integrated somatic decisions |

## §5 Technical Requirements

- **Inputs:** Selection (choose between options); text/voice (explain reasoning — optional); tap (indicate body-state)
- **Timing:** Decision latency tracked; no time pressure but deliberation time noted
- **NPC/AI:** Dilemma generation system (context-appropriate somatic decisions); body-state simulation (fatigue levels, readiness); choice-pattern detection (always-rigid, always-impulsive, always-harder, always-familiar); wisdom assessment (does choice honour both code and body?)
- **LLM level:** Medium — generates contextual dilemmas; evaluates reasoning quality; detects shadow patterns in choice-making; adapts dilemma difficulty to demonstrated wisdom level
- **State persistence:** Choice history; wisdom scores; pattern indicators (rigidity, impulsivity, overreach, avoidance); fatigue-reading accuracy; context-sensitivity metrics; drive/shadow signals; checkpoint position
