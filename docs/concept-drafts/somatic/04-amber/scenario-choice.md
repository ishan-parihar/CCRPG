# Somatic / Amber — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes somatic intelligence through BODY-DECISIONS — dilemmas where the body must choose between competing forms, conflicting disciplines, or situations where somatic wisdom is needed. At Amber, this is: "The code says hold. Your body says release. What do you choose?"
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Amber:** The disciplined body faces DILEMMAS — when two forms conflict, when the code demands what the body resists, when fatigue meets discipline. This modality tests somatic WISDOM: can the player make good decisions ABOUT their body within a rule-governed system?

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body's Dilemma"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces SOMATIC DILEMMAS — situations where body-discipline conflicts with body-wisdom, where two forms compete, where the code demands and the body resists. Not just following form but CHOOSING wisely about form.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Clear Form → The Competing Forms → The Body's Limit → The Changing Code → The Living Wisdom

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "The code says hold. Your body says release. The form demands left. Your fatigue says rest. Two forms compete — which do you follow? The discipline requires — but at what cost?" The contact boundary is: "Can you make wise decisions about your body within a rule-system?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player decide about body-discipline? The Rigid Driller ALWAYS follows the code (ignores body-signals). The Undisciplined Body ALWAYS follows impulse (ignores the code). The Premature Virtuoso chooses the harder option regardless. The Form-Refuser chooses the familiar option regardless.
- *Emergent:* Can they make WISE body-decisions — honouring both discipline and body-wisdom?

**Integration path:** Rewards SOMATIC WISDOM — decisions that honour both the code AND the body. Not rigid obedience, not impulsive rebellion, not overreach, not avoidance.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates somatic wisdom — the capacity to make good decisions about body-discipline.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing to maintain form when body signals fatigue.
- Healthy: Recognises fatigue AND maintains form appropriately (wise persistence)
- Pathological: Overrides all body-signals (rigid); OR: abandons at first discomfort (impulsive)
- **Detection:** Quality of fatigue-discipline balance

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing to attempt a challenging form under uncertainty.
- Healthy: Asserts into challenge with body-awareness; knows limits
- Pathological: Cannot choose challenge; OR: chooses recklessly without body-awareness
- **Detection:** Challenge-selection wisdom

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing between own body-rhythm and group rhythm.
- Healthy: Adapts to group while maintaining body-integrity
- Pathological: Sacrifices body-integrity for group; OR: refuses group entirely
- **Detection:** Group-adaptation wisdom

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing to support another's body-discipline (spotting, pacing).
- Healthy: Supports others' form while maintaining own
- Pathological: Cannot support (self-focused); OR: loses own form supporting others
- **Detection:** Mutual body-support quality

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing between rest and continued practice.
- Healthy: Rests when body needs it; practices when body is ready
- Pathological: Never rests (compulsive); OR: always rests (avoidant)
- **Detection:** Rest-practice balance wisdom

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing to advance to harder form vs. consolidate current.
- Healthy: Advances when ready; consolidates when needed
- Pathological: Always advances (overreach); OR: never advances (stagnation)
- **Detection:** Advancement-timing wisdom

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing to return to practice after absence.
- Healthy: Returns with appropriate re-entry (not full intensity immediately)
- Pathological: Returns at full intensity (injury risk); OR: cannot return (avoidance)
- **Detection:** Re-entry wisdom

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing how to apply body-discipline in daily life.
- Healthy: Applies discipline proportionately to context
- Pathological: Applies rigidly everywhere; OR: cannot apply outside practice
- **Detection:** Transfer wisdom

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Driller"
**How it surfaces:** ALWAYS chooses the code over the body. "The form says hold. I hold." Ignores fatigue, pain, body-signals. Discipline without wisdom. The code is absolute.
- **Detection signals:** Always chooses discipline over body-signals; never rests; overrides fatigue; "the code says hold"; no body-listening in decisions; rigid rule-following regardless of context

### Dark-Allergy: "The Undisciplined Body"
**How it surfaces:** ALWAYS chooses comfort over discipline. "My body says stop. I stop." Cannot persist through appropriate discomfort. Impulse over form.
- **Detection signals:** Always chooses rest/comfort; abandons at first discomfort; "I don't feel like it"; cannot persist; no discipline in decisions; impulsive body-following

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Virtuoso"
**How it surfaces:** ALWAYS chooses the harder option. "I'll do the advanced form." Regardless of readiness. Overreach disguised as ambition.
- **Detection signals:** Always chooses harder; never consolidates; "I can handle it" (while failing); reckless advancement; no readiness-assessment; ambition without wisdom

### Golden-Allergy: "The Form-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** ALWAYS chooses the familiar option. "I'll stay with what I know." Regardless of readiness for growth. Safety disguised as mastery.
- **Detection signals:** Always chooses familiar; never advances; "this works fine"; avoids new challenges; no growth in decisions; comfort over development

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present dilemmas where BODY-LISTENING is the wise choice: "Your body signals fatigue. The code says hold. But: the WISE practitioner rests. Rest IS discipline."
- Reward body-listening: "You chose rest. That's wisdom. The form will be stronger tomorrow."
- Frame: "Discipline includes rest. The code serves the body. Not the other way around."
- Build: wise discipline (code + body-listening)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present dilemmas where APPROPRIATE PERSISTENCE is the wise choice: "Your body says stop. But: this is not pain. This is effort. Effort is growth."
- Reward appropriate persistence: "You held through the discomfort. That's discipline. Your body grew."
- Frame: "Not all discomfort is danger. Some is growth. Can you tell the difference?"
- Build: wise persistence (body-listening + discipline)

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Present dilemmas where CONSOLIDATION is the wise choice: "You could try the harder form. But: your current form is 80%. Master THIS first."
- Reward consolidation: "You chose to master the current form. That's wisdom. Foundation first."
- Frame: "Advancement without mastery is collapse waiting to happen. Solid foundation first."
- Build: wise advancement (readiness-based growth)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Present dilemmas where GROWTH is the wise choice: "You could stay with the familiar. But: your body is ready. The new form calls."
- Reward growth-choice: "You chose the new form. That's courage. Your body is ready."
- Frame: "You've mastered this. Staying is stagnation now. The new form is the wise choice."
- Build: wise growth (readiness-based advancement)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Body's Dilemma: a decision-space where somatic dilemmas are presented. Each dilemma has a context (body-state, form-demand, social situation) and options. The player chooses. The aesthetic: Amber-stage council-chamber — the body's wisdom honoured as a form of knowing.

### Interaction
- **The Clear Form (1-5):** Simple dilemmas with clear wise choice. "Hold or release? (Body is fresh, form is short. Hold.)"
- **The Competing Forms (5-15):** Two valid forms compete. "Form A or Form B? Both valid. Which serves your body better now?"
- **The Body's Limit (15-30):** Fatigue-discipline dilemmas. "The code says continue. Your body signals fatigue. What's wise?"
- **The Changing Code (30-50):** The code changes mid-practice. "The form just changed. Adapt immediately or consolidate first?"
- **The Living Wisdom (50+):** Complex multi-factor dilemmas requiring integrated somatic wisdom.

### Feedback
- Wise choice → "Good. Your body and the code are honoured. That's somatic wisdom."
- Rigid choice → "You followed the code. But your body needed rest. Wisdom includes listening."
- Impulsive choice → "You followed comfort. But your body was ready for more. Wisdom includes persistence."
- Overreach choice → "You chose harder. But your body wasn't ready. Wisdom includes patience."
- Avoidant choice → "You chose familiar. But your body was ready to grow. Wisdom includes courage."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Dilemma complexity: clear → ambiguous → multi-factor
- Body-state: fresh → moderate fatigue → significant fatigue
- Stakes: low (practice) → moderate (performance) → high (injury-risk)
- Time pressure: unlimited → moderate → competing demands
- Social context: solo → dyad → group

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Clear Form | 1-5 | Simple dilemmas, clear wise choice |
| The Competing Forms | 5-15 | Two valid options, context-dependent |
| The Body's Limit | 15-30 | Fatigue-discipline balance |
| The Changing Code | 30-50 | Adaptation dilemmas |
| The Living Wisdom | 50+ | Complex integrated somatic decisions |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Fatigue dilemmas:** Continue vs. rest (body-signal reading)
- **Form-conflict dilemmas:** Two forms compete (context-dependent choice)
- **Advancement dilemmas:** Grow vs. consolidate (readiness assessment)
- **Social dilemmas:** Own rhythm vs. group rhythm (adaptation)
- **Adaptation dilemmas:** Code changes; respond how? (flexibility)

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ fatigue, 15+ form-conflict, 15+ advancement, 10+ social, 10+ adaptation

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Always follows code → dark-addiction; always follows comfort → dark-allergy
- Always chooses harder → golden-addiction; always chooses familiar → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Wisdom quality:** % decisions that honour both discipline and body-signals
- **Context-sensitivity:** Do choices adapt to body-state and situation?
- **Balance:** Distribution across choice-types (not always same direction)
- **Fatigue-reading:** Accuracy of body-signal interpretation
- **Growth-timing:** Appropriateness of advancement decisions

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Dilemma generation and context-creation require LLM. Choice evaluation partially algorithmic (pattern detection) but nuanced wisdom-assessment requires LLM evaluation of reasoning.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Selection (choose between options); text/voice (explain reasoning — optional); tap (indicate body-state)

### Timing requirements
- Decision latency tracked; no time pressure but deliberation time noted

### NPC/AI requirements
- Dilemma generation system (context-appropriate somatic decisions)
- Body-state simulation (fatigue levels, readiness indicators)
- Choice-pattern detection (always-rigid, always-impulsive, always-harder, always-familiar)
- Wisdom assessment (does choice honour both code and body?)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Generates contextual dilemmas; evaluates reasoning quality; detects shadow patterns in choice-making; adapts dilemma difficulty to demonstrated wisdom level.

### State persistence
- Choice history; wisdom scores; pattern indicators (rigidity, impulsivity, overreach, avoidance); fatigue-reading accuracy; context-sensitivity metrics; drive/shadow signals; checkpoint position
