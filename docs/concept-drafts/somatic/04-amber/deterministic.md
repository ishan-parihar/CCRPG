# Somatic / Amber — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis provides OBJECTIVE, LLM-free measurement of somatic capacity at Amber. Pure psychophysics: timing, accuracy, duration, synchrony. The body's capacity measured without interpretation.
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Amber:** The disciplined body is measurable — posture duration, form accuracy, breath-movement coupling, synchrony precision. This modality provides the ground-truth calibration for all other Somatic/Amber games.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Stance Trials"
- **Core mechanic:** Objective measurement of disciplined body capacity — sustained holds, form reproduction, breath-gated timing, group synchrony, coordination under load. Pure psychophysics. No narrative, no interpretation — just the body's measurable capacity.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The First Hold → The Reproduced Form → The Breath-Gate → The Shared Beat → The Loaded Stance

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Hold. Reproduce. Breathe. Synchronise. Coordinate." The body is asked to demonstrate disciplined capacity. The contact boundary is: "What can your body actually DO in terms of sustained, coordinated, disciplined movement?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The body's relationship to discipline surfaces. The Rigid Driller holds perfectly but cannot release. The Undisciplined Body breaks immediately. The Premature Virtuoso attempts beyond capacity. The Form-Refuser holds familiar stances but freezes at novel ones.
- *Emergent:* Can the body demonstrate flexible discipline — sustained, accurate, adaptive?

**Integration path:** Rewards FLEXIBLE DISCIPLINED CAPACITY — sustained holds that release cleanly, accurate forms that adapt, breath-coupling that maintains under load.

**Successful integration:** The body demonstrates Amber-level somatic mastery — disciplined, coordinated, breath-coupled, synchronised.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Sustaining posture independently (no external rhythm/cue).
- Healthy: Holds stance with sovereign discipline; body serves self-chosen form
- Pathological: Cannot hold without external cue; OR: holds rigidly past fatigue
- **Detection:** Whether sustained posture is self-regulated

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Asserting into novel stances (unfamiliar forms).
- Healthy: Attempts novel forms with appropriate effort and body-confidence
- Pathological: Cannot attempt novel forms; body freezes at unfamiliar
- **Detection:** Novel-form attempt rate and quality

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining own form when external rhythm differs.
- Healthy: Maintains own timing while hearing different beat
- Pathological: Loses own rhythm to external; OR: rigidly ignores external
- **Detection:** Whether own form survives competing rhythm

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Synchronising with external beat (group rhythm).
- Healthy: Matches external rhythm precisely while maintaining form quality
- Pathological: Cannot synchronise; either independent or fused (loses form)
- **Detection:** Synchrony precision (ms deviation from beat)

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Sustained accuracy over extended trials.
- Healthy: Maintains form quality across many repetitions
- Pathological: Quality degrades with repetition; OR: compulsive repetition without rest
- **Detection:** Accuracy trajectory over time

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Growing difficulty (longer holds, more elements, faster beats).
- Healthy: Engages increasing difficulty with appropriate effort
- Pathological: Cannot grow; OR: attempts beyond capacity
- **Detection:** Response to difficulty escalation

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Capacity maintenance across sessions.
- Healthy: Returns with maintained capacity; body remembers
- Pathological: Capacity degrades between sessions; must relearn
- **Detection:** Cross-session capacity stability

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether disciplined capacity transfers to novel contexts.
- Healthy: Applies learned discipline to new movement challenges
- Pathological: Discipline exists only in familiar contexts
- **Detection:** Transfer quality to novel forms

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Driller"
**How it surfaces:** Holds stances PAST the required duration. Cannot release. Repeats forms without being asked. Body locked in drill-mode.
- **Detection signals:** Hold duration exceeds requirement (won't release); immediate restart without pause; same form repeated when variety requested; fatigue-override (continues past signals)

### Dark-Allergy: "The Undisciplined Body"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot sustain ANY hold. Breaks form within 2-3 seconds. Cannot reproduce even 2-element sequences. Body refuses discipline.
- **Detection signals:** Hold breaks <3s; form reproduction fails at 2-element; random timing (no rhythm); high variability; abandons trials

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Virtuoso"
**How it surfaces:** Attempts longer holds, more complex forms, faster beats than capacity allows. Fails at current level but requests harder.
- **Detection signals:** Attempts 6+ element forms when 4-element <85%; requests faster BPM when current sync >40ms; "too easy" claims while failing; overreach pattern

### Golden-Allergy: "The Form-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** High accuracy on familiar forms; sharp drop on novel forms. Body tenses/freezes when new stance is demonstrated.
- **Detection signals:** >90% on familiar, <50% on novel; body-tension at novel demonstration; returns to familiar forms unprompted; avoids novel trials

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Introduce RELEASE CUES: "Hold... and release. Good. The form includes the rest."
- Reward clean release (not just clean hold)
- Frame: "Discipline includes knowing when to stop. Release IS part of the form."
- Build: flexible discipline (hold AND release)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Start with MICRO-HOLDS: "Two seconds. Just two. Hold... done. Good."
- Make brief discipline feel POWERFUL
- Frame: "Two seconds of perfect form. That's discipline. Your body did it."
- Build: sustained capacity from micro to macro

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- LOCK difficulty until current level mastered (>90% required for advancement)
- Reward mastery: "Perfect 4-element form. You OWN this level."
- Frame: "Master this first. The longer form needs this foundation solid."
- Build: mastery satisfaction before advancement

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Introduce SLIGHT novelty (familiar form + one new element)
- Reward attempt (not just accuracy): "You tried the new stance. Good. Your body is learning."
- Frame: "Almost the same form. One new thing. Your body noticed. That's growth."
- Build: comfort with novel movement

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Stance Trials: a clean measurement space. No narrative — just the body and the form. Stances demonstrated; player reproduces. Beats played; player synchronises. Breath cadence set; player couples. The aesthetic: Amber-stage training ground — clean stone floor, form-master demonstrating, player's body-outline responding.

### Interaction
- **The First Hold (1-5):** Sustained posture. Hold for 4s → 6s → 8s. With 0 → 1 → 2 distractors.
- **The Reproduced Form (5-15):** Form reproduction. 2-element → 3-element → 4-element sequences.
- **The Breath-Gate (15-30):** Breath-coupled movement. 3-breath → 4-breath → 6-breath cadences.
- **The Shared Beat (30-50):** Group synchrony. 80 BPM → 100 BPM → 120 BPM. Precision measured.
- **The Loaded Stance (50+):** Coordination under load. Posture + classification. Form + counting. Breath + sequencing.

### Feedback
- Clean hold → "The stance holds. Disciplined. Good."
- Cannot release → "Release now. The form includes the rest. Let go."
- Breaks early → "Shorter. Just two seconds. Perfect. Build from there."
- Overreaches → "Not yet. Master this form first. Four elements. Perfect. Then five."
- Freezes at novel → "New stance. Just try. Your body will learn. Attempt is enough."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Hold duration: 2s → 4s → 6s → 8s (with distractors: 0 → 1 → 2)
- Form length: 2-element → 3-element → 4-element
- Breath cadence: 3-breath → 4-breath → 6-breath
- Synchrony BPM: 80 → 100 → 120 (precision: ±50ms → ±40ms → ±30ms)
- Dual-task load: none → simple → moderate

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Hold | 1-5 | Sustained posture (duration + distractors) |
| The Reproduced Form | 5-15 | Form reproduction (sequence length) |
| The Breath-Gate | 15-30 | Breath-coupled movement (cadence length) |
| The Shared Beat | 30-50 | Group synchrony (BPM + precision) |
| The Loaded Stance | 50+ | Coordination under dual-task load |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Hold trials:** Sustained posture at varying durations and distractor loads
- **Form trials:** Sequence reproduction at varying lengths
- **Breath trials:** Breath-coupled movement at varying cadences
- **Synchrony trials:** Beat-matching at varying BPMs and precision demands
- **Dual-task trials:** Form/posture maintained under cognitive load

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ hold, 20+ form, 15+ breath, 15+ synchrony, 10+ dual-task

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Won't release / compulsive repetition → dark-addiction
- Breaks immediately / cannot sustain → dark-allergy
- Attempts beyond capacity → golden-addiction
- Freezes at novel forms → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Hold duration:** Maximum sustained posture (seconds) under load
- **Form accuracy:** % correct sequence reproduction at ceiling length
- **Breath coupling:** % trials where movement matches breath cadence
- **Synchrony precision:** Mean absolute deviation from beat (ms)
- **Dual-task maintenance:** % form-accuracy maintained under cognitive load

### LLM rubric
- **None:** Fully deterministic. All measures are objective (duration, accuracy, timing, deviation). No interpretation needed.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Hold (sustained touch/press); sequence-tap (form reproduction); rhythm-tap (synchrony); breath-paced-tap (breath-gating)

### Timing requirements
- Millisecond precision for synchrony measurement
- Sub-second precision for hold duration
- Breath-rate calibration (player's resting rate measured at session start)

### NPC/AI requirements
- Form demonstration system (shows sequences to reproduce)
- Beat generation system (metronome at varying BPMs)
- Breath calibration system (measures player's natural rate)
- Distractor generation (interference during holds)
- Fatigue detection (accuracy degradation, response-time increase)
- Adaptive difficulty (all parameters scale independently)

### LLM requirements
- **None:** Fully deterministic. Pure psychophysics.

### State persistence
- Hold duration history; form accuracy history; breath-coupling metrics; synchrony precision history; dual-task scores; fatigue indicators; shadow signals (rigidity, chaos, overreach, freeze); drive/shadow state; checkpoint position; breath-rate calibration
