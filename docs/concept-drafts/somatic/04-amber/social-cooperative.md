# Somatic / Amber — Social-Cooperative

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes somatic intelligence through SHARED BODY-DISCIPLINE — can the player move WITH others? Synchronise? Coordinate forms? The GROUP BODY: the congregation moving together, the martial arts class in unison.
>
> **Why this axis:** Amber is the stage of GROUP BELONGING. Somatic capacity at Amber includes moving WITH the group — shared rhythm, coordinated forms, synchronised breath. This modality tests whether the player's body can be disciplined TOGETHER with others.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Shared Form"
- **Core mechanic:** The player and companions must MOVE TOGETHER — synchronised forms, shared rhythm, coordinated sequences. Not solo discipline but GROUP discipline. The form is: hold together, move together, breathe together. One body, many limbs.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Unison → The Shared Beat → The Coordinated Form → The Changing Together → The Living Congregation

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What it uniquely presents:** The demand to be disciplined TOGETHER — individual form maintained within group synchrony.

**How it differs from other 6:** Only modality that tests SOCIAL somatic capacity. Not solo discipline but GROUP discipline. The body must maintain its own form while synchronising with others — a fundamentally different challenge than solo mastery.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Dark-addiction as accurate-but-isolated (too locked in own pattern to synchronise). Dark-allergy as chaotic-even-in-group (cannot benefit from group rhythm). Golden-addiction as domination (tries to lead/direct beyond role). Golden-allergy as frozen-at-novel-group-forms.

**Successful integration:** The player's body can be disciplined together with others — individual form maintained within genuine group synchrony.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
A group movement space. Player and companions move together — synchronised forms, shared rhythm, coordinated sequences. Aesthetic: Amber-stage monastery courtyard — practitioners moving in unison, the group-body as sacred form.

### Interaction Phases
- **The First Unison (1-5):** Simple beat-matching with companion. "Tap together. Same beat. One rhythm."
- **The Shared Beat (5-15):** Sustained group rhythm. "Hold the beat together. 120 BPM. Stay with the group."
- **The Coordinated Form (15-30):** Shared form-execution. "The form together. Same sequence. Same timing."
- **The Changing Together (30-50):** Group adapts to new form. "New form. Learn it together. Synchronise the new."
- **The Living Congregation (50+):** Full group-body integration: complex coordinated forms with role-flexibility.

### Feedback
- Good synchrony → "Together. One body, many limbs. The group-form holds. Beautiful."
- Isolated → "You're accurate but alone. The group is HERE. Match their timing. Join."
- Chaotic → "Simpler. Just the beat. One tap with the group. That's enough. Join the rhythm."
- Dominating → "Not leading. Joining. Equal. Match THEIR pace. Participate."
- Frozen at novel → "New form for everyone. You're not alone. The group learns together. Join them."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Group size: dyad → triad → small group
- Synchrony precision: ±50ms → ±40ms → ±30ms
- Form complexity: beat-matching → simple forms → complex sequences
- Role flexibility: fixed roles → occasional swap → frequent rotation
- Novelty: familiar group-forms → slight variations → novel coordinated movement

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Inputs:** Rhythm-tap (synchrony); sequence-tap (coordinated forms); hold (sustained group posture)
- **Timing:** Millisecond precision for synchrony measurement; group-timing comparison (player vs. companions vs. target beat)
- **NPC/AI:** Companion system (NPCs with own movement patterns/timing); group-rhythm system (shared beat generation/tracking); synchrony measurement (player-to-group deviation); domination detection; role-rotation system
- **LLM level:** Low — companion behaviour generation; nuanced group-dynamics assessment. Core synchrony metrics algorithmic.
- **State persistence:** Synchrony history; form-in-group accuracy; adaptation metrics; role-flexibility scores; group-body emergence indicators; isolation/domination/chaos/rigidity indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
