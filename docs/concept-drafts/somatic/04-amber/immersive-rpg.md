# Somatic / Amber — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes somatic intelligence through SPONTANEOUS DISCIPLINED MOVEMENT in an open world — not structured body-tasks but free embodied behaviour. How does the player's body relate to discipline when nobody's saying "hold the stance"?
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Amber:** The other 6 modalities probe somatic capacity through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Amber, the world is EMBODIED — monasteries with movement rituals, dojos with open practice, processions through streets, rhythmic environments. The player's spontaneous engagement with disciplined movement reveals genuine somatic intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Embodied World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Amber-stage world saturated with embodied-discipline content. Dojos with open practice. Processions to join. Rhythmic environments. Breath-gated passages. The player's spontaneous engagement with disciplined movement — how they inhabit a body-honouring world without being prompted — reveals genuine somatic intelligence.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Body-Noticing → Body-Joining → Body-Sustaining → Body-Adapting → The Living Body

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world contains embodied-discipline content. Nobody says "hold the stance" — but dojos are open, processions pass, rhythms pulse, breath-gates guard passages. The player's NATURAL embodied engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL somatic style surfaces. Do they join every procession? Ignore all movement opportunities? Push through every breath-gate regardless of readiness? Avoid all novel body-challenges? Their ecological somatic behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage embodied content more healthily? Move with more discipline? Join with more flexibility? Rest with more wisdom?

**Integration path:** The world rewards somatic intelligence NATURALLY. Players who hold stances access posture-gated content. Players who synchronise join processions that reveal hidden paths. Players who breathe well pass breath-gates. The reward is EMBODIED BELONGING (being at home in a body-honouring world).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates somatic intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — holding, synchronising, breathing, and adapting within embodied environments without being prompted.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Opportunities to engage disciplined movement spontaneously.
- Healthy: Engages body-discipline naturally; enters dojos; holds stances; joins rhythms
- Pathological: Over-engages ALL body-content (compulsive); OR: ignores all (avoidant)
- **Detection:** Quality of spontaneous somatic engagement

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Body-gated content requiring somatic assertion.
- Healthy: Attempts breath-gates and posture-challenges with confidence
- Pathological: Cannot assert body into challenges; avoids body-gates
- **Detection:** Spontaneous body-challenge attempt rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Group movement opportunities (processions, group practice).
- Healthy: Joins group movement naturally without losing own form
- Pathological: Joins everything (fusion); OR: joins nothing (isolation)
- **Detection:** Quality of spontaneous group-body engagement

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared embodied spaces (open dojos, communal practice areas).
- Healthy: Engages shared body-spaces naturally; moves with others
- Pathological: Avoids shared spaces; prefers solo body-practice
- **Detection:** Engagement rate in shared vs. solo embodied content

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar embodied content (same dojos, same rhythms).
- Healthy: Maintains somatic engagement in familiar territory
- Pathological: Engagement fades with familiarity; body becomes automatic
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in familiar vs. novel embodied content

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel embodied content (new movement challenges, unfamiliar rhythms).
- Healthy: Engages novel body-content with appropriate curiosity
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by novel body-challenges; OR: treats them as routine
- **Detection:** Whether novel embodied content evokes appropriate response

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining embodied engagement over time.
- Healthy: Consistent somatic engagement across sessions
- Pathological: Embodied engagement degrades; world becomes just terrain
- **Detection:** Somatic engagement consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether disciplined body-skills appear in free-navigation.
- Healthy: Posture, breath, rhythm appear naturally during exploration
- Pathological: Body-skills exist only during explicit challenges
- **Detection:** Transfer of somatic skills to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Driller"
**How it surfaces:** OVER-ENGAGES all embodied content. Cannot pass a dojo without entering. Joins every procession. Holds every stance encountered. Compulsive body-discipline — the world is only practice.
- **Detection signals:** Enters every dojo; joins every procession; holds every stance; cannot explore freely; world is only body-practice; compulsive engagement with all movement content

### Dark-Allergy: "The Undisciplined Body"
**How it surfaces:** IGNORES all embodied content. Walks past dojos. Doesn't join processions. Cannot pass breath-gates. The body-honouring world is invisible — just terrain to traverse.
- **Detection signals:** Zero embodied engagement; walks past dojos; ignores processions; fails breath-gates; world has no body-content; purely cognitive navigation

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Virtuoso"
**How it surfaces:** Engages embodied content ONLY at advanced level. Ignores basic practice. Seeks the hardest breath-gates. Attempts master-level stances without foundation.
- **Detection signals:** Skips basic content; seeks advanced only; ignores foundation; attempts master-gates while failing basic; "too easy" for simple body-content; advanced-seeking without mastery

### Golden-Allergy: "The Form-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** APPROACHES embodied content then RETREATS from novelty. Enters familiar dojos but avoids new ones. Joins known processions but not unfamiliar ones.
- **Detection signals:** Engages familiar body-content; avoids novel; brief visits to new dojos; abandons unfamiliar rhythms; stays in known embodied territory; "I know this practice"

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces BODY-REST zones (areas where stillness is rewarded)
- Embodied content that responds to PROPORTIONATE engagement (not compulsive)
- Natural consequence: proportionate engagement = deeper access; compulsive = flat response

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World makes embodied content NECESSARY for navigation (breath-gated paths, posture-locked doors)
- Body-challenges that INVITE engagement (simple, brief, immediately rewarding)
- Natural consequence: body-engagement = access; ignoring = stuck

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces content that requires FOUNDATIONAL mastery (advanced gates locked until basic mastered)
- Simple body-content that responds to quality (not just completion)
- Natural consequence: foundation mastery = advanced access; skipping = locked

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces GRADUAL novelty (familiar dojos with one new element)
- Novel body-content that INVITES without DEMANDING immediate mastery
- Natural consequence: gradual engagement = growing access; no punishment for partial engagement

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Amber-stage world saturated with embodied-discipline content. Dojos with open practice. Processions through streets. Breath-gated passages. Rhythmic environments. Posture-locked doors. Every zone has body-honouring content — opportunities to hold, synchronise, breathe, and move with discipline.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through an embodied world
- **Spontaneous body-engagement:** Entering dojos, joining processions, holding stances
- **Body-gated content:** Passages requiring somatic capacity (breath-gates, posture-locks)
- **Group movement:** Processions and communal practice to join
- **Rhythmic environments:** Zones with pulse that the body can match

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: body-engagement → gated access, procession rewards, rhythmic bonuses
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, stuck paths, incomplete world
- No explicit "somatic score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Embodied content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Body-gate complexity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Procession complexity scales with demonstrated synchrony
- Rhythmic environment tempo scales with demonstrated timing
- Novel body-content appears as capacity grows

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Body-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice embodied content? |
| Body-Joining | 5-15 | Do they engage body-challenges they encounter? |
| Body-Sustaining | 15-30 | Can they sustain embodied engagement over time? |
| Body-Adapting | 30-50 | Can they adapt to novel embodied content? |
| The Living Body | 50+ | Full ecological somatic intelligence |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Dojos:** Open practice spaces (to enter and engage)
- **Processions:** Group movements (to join and synchronise)
- **Breath-gates:** Passages requiring breath-capacity
- **Posture-locks:** Doors requiring sustained holds
- **Rhythmic zones:** Environments with pulse to match

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ dojos, 15+ processions, 15+ breath-gates, 10+ posture-locks, 10+ rhythmic zones

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Over-engages all body-content → dark-addiction; ignores all → dark-allergy
- Advanced-only engagement → golden-addiction; avoids novelty → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Embodied engagement rate:** How often does the player engage body-content spontaneously?
- **Body-gate success:** Can they pass somatic challenges encountered in exploration?
- **Group-body engagement:** Do they join processions and communal practice?
- **Rhythmic attunement:** Do they match environmental rhythms?
- **Transfer evidence:** Do somatic skills appear in free-navigation?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium-High:** World generation, ecological somatic assessment, NPC movement-interactions. Evaluates spontaneous embodied behaviour without explicit tasks.

- Procession system (group movements to join)
- Breath-gate system (passages calibrated to player's breath-rate)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium-High:** World generation, ecological assessment, NPC interactions within embodied spaces
- Detects spontaneous somatic engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates somatic intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive embodied content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; embodied engagement history; navigation patterns; body-gate success records; procession participation; rhythmic attunement metrics; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
