# Somatic / Amber — Module Specification

## 1. Capacity Definition

**Line:** Somatic — the capacity to inhabit and skilfully use the body
**Stage:** Amber (conformist/mythic) — the DISCIPLINED BODY
**Contact boundary:** "Can your body follow the form? Can it hold? Can it move with others? Can it serve the discipline?"

The FORM-BODY: coordinated forms, sustained posture under load, group rhythm, breath-gated movement, kata. The body that serves the code. Not the power-body (Red) but the body that can hold a stance, follow a drill, synchronise with a group, and maintain discipline over time.

- **Key distinction from Red:** Red is the POWER-body (strength, speed, dominance, impulsive force). Amber is the FORM-body (discipline, coordination, sustained posture, group synchrony).
- **Key distinction from Orange:** Orange is the SKILLED-body (multi-domain mastery, rational training, technique trees). Amber cannot optimise technique — only follow the form faithfully.

| Dimension | What it measures | Task vehicle | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sustained posture | Hold a stance under load without breaking | Held-input stability under interference | 8s hold + 2 distractors |
| Form reproduction | Reproduce a demonstrated movement sequence | Sequence-tap matching demonstrated pattern | 4-element form |
| Breath-gated timing | Execute movement in time with breath cadence | Bar-fill matching breath rhythm | 6-breath sustained cadence |
| Group synchrony | Move in time with external rhythm/group | Sensorimotor synchronisation tapping | 120 BPM ± 30ms |
| Coordination under load | Maintain form while performing secondary task | Dual-task posture maintenance | Posture + simple classification |

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rigid Driller"
- **Core pattern:** Body CLINGS to form obsessively. Cannot stop drilling. Cannot adapt when form changes. Form becomes compulsion.
- **Drive pathology:** Agency dominates body (no listening); Communion cannot synchronise (too locked); Eros cannot grow (locked in current form); Agape cannot rest (compulsive return)
- **Behavioural signatures:** Drills same form endlessly; cannot rest between forms; body locked in familiar patterns; pushes through fatigue; high accuracy but RIGID; repetition without variation
- **Protects against:** The anxiety of formlessness; fear of what happens when the drill stops
- **Atman defense:** Fixation — body fixates on known form to avoid formlessness

### Dark-Allergy: "The Undisciplined Body"
- **Core pattern:** Body REFUSES form. Cannot sustain posture. Movement is chaotic, impulsive, undisciplined. The body rejects the code.
- **Drive pathology:** Agency acts impulsively (no sovereignty); Communion cannot join rhythm (too chaotic); Eros cannot sustain effort (gives up); Agape cannot return to practice (avoids)
- **Behavioural signatures:** Cannot hold stance >3s; breaks form under minimal load; random movement; cannot follow patterns; breath-movement decoupled; abandons mid-execution
- **Protects against:** The fear of commitment; the illusion of freedom through formlessness
- **Atman defense:** Avoidance — body avoids disciplined form to preserve illusion of freedom

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Virtuoso"
- **Core pattern:** Body BYPASSES form toward technique. Attempts skilled-body (Orange) without disciplined foundation (Amber). Reaches beyond capacity.
- **Drive pathology:** Agency asserts beyond capacity; Communion cannot join at current level; Eros compulsive upward reach; Agape cannot embody current capacity
- **Behavioural signatures:** Attempts complex sequences when simple forms fail; dismisses basics as "too easy"; overreaches coordination capacity; wants technique without discipline
- **Protects against:** The fear of being ordinary; the tedium of mastery at current level
- **Atman defense:** Bypass — body bypasses disciplined form to avoid tedium of mastery

### Golden-Allergy: "The Form-Refuser"
- **Core pattern:** Body comfortable in KNOWN forms but REFUSES new ones. Cannot tolerate novel movement. Fears growth.
- **Drive pathology:** Agency cannot assert into novelty (freezes); Communion cannot join new group forms; Eros refuses upward call; Agape over-embodies current level
- **Behavioural signatures:** High accuracy on familiar (>90%); sharp drop on novel (<50%); body tenses/freezes at novel demonstration; returns to known forms unprompted; avoids new challenges
- **Protects against:** The fear of incompetence; the vulnerability of being a beginner again
- **Atman defense:** Contraction — body contracts against the emergent to preserve safety of the known

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Dark-domain healthy | Dark-domain pathological | Golden-domain healthy | Golden-domain pathological |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Body moves with sovereign discipline; form is CHOSEN not compelled | Body dominates (forces form) OR is dominated (cannot discipline) | Body asserts into novel forms with confidence | Cannot assert into novelty; freezes at new forms |
| **Communion** | Body synchronises with group without losing own form | Cannot synchronise (too rigid) OR loses own form in group | Body joins new group forms; adapts to collective rhythm | Cannot join new collective forms; stays in known patterns |
| **Eros** | Body rests in current form with satisfaction; no compulsive growth | Cannot rest (compulsive drilling) OR cannot sustain (gives up) | Body reaches for new forms with appropriate effort | Bypasses form toward technique; OR refuses growth entirely |
| **Agape** | Body returns to practice with maintained capacity | Cannot return (avoids practice) OR returns compulsively | Body embodies new forms in daily life; transfer to lived movement | Cannot embody new capacity; stays abstract/theoretical |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

**Dark-Addiction (Rigid Driller):**
- Agape: Return to form with COMPASSION not compulsion. "Your body has earned rest."
- Agency: CHOOSE to modify form. "You can change the form. Sovereignty over your own movement."
- Integration: Form becomes flexible discipline (not rigid compulsion)

**Dark-Allergy (Undisciplined Body):**
- Agape: Return to form with GENTLENESS. "Just two seconds. Hold. Good. That's enough."
- Agency: OWN the discipline. "This is YOUR form. Not imposed — chosen."
- Integration: Discipline becomes empowering (not imprisoning)

### Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

**Golden-Addiction (Premature Virtuoso):**
- Eros: Reach for mastery AT CURRENT LEVEL. "Master THIS form. Beauty in perfection of the simple."
- Communion: Join others at current level. "Move WITH the group. Their rhythm. Your form."
- Integration: Current-level mastery becomes satisfying (not boring)

**Golden-Allergy (Form-Refuser):**
- Eros: Reach for new form GENTLY. "One new element. Just one. Your body can learn this."
- Communion: See others doing new forms. "They learned it. You can too."
- Integration: Novel forms become exciting (not threatening)

### Integration Criteria
1. Sustained posture >90% at ceiling (8s + 2 distractors) without rigidity or collapse
2. Form reproduction >90% at 4-element AND adapts when form changes
3. Breath-movement coupling maintained (6-breath cadence)
4. Group synchrony <30ms deviation at 120 BPM without losing own form
5. Coordination under load without breaking form OR overriding fatigue
6. All 4 drives express healthily in both domains
7. No shadow quadrant dominates (shadow-drag < 0.10)
8. Compound shadows resolved (all < 0.10)

|---|---|---|

- Active maintenance: Playing any Somatic/Amber game resets theta
- Passive decay: 14-day half-life; capacity degrades without practice
- Shadow-mode trigger: theta < 0.6 activates shadow-mode encounters
- Cross-line support: Willpower/Amber health slows decay by up to 15%

**Stage-transition thresholds:**

*Readiness for Orange (upward):*
- Sustained posture >90% at ceiling; Form reproduction >90% at 4-element
- Breath-gated timing >85% at 6-breath; Group synchrony <30ms at 120 BPM
- Drive-health >0.7 all drives; Shadow-drag <0.10

*Regression toward Red (downward):*
- Sustained posture <60%; Form reproduction <60%
- Breath-gated timing <50%; Group synchrony >80ms
- Dark-addiction or dark-allergy shadow-drag >0.15

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### Progressive Revelation
1. **Sustained posture** → Dark-allergy surfaces first (cannot hold = immediately visible)
2. **Form reproduction** → Dark-addiction surfaces (rigid repetition vs. flexible reproduction)
3. **Breath-gated timing** → Golden-allergy surfaces (familiar breath-patterns vs. novel cadences)
4. **Group synchrony** → Compound shadows surface (Lonely Driller, Frozen Soldier)
5. **Coordination under load** → Golden-addiction surfaces (attempts beyond capacity under dual-task)

### Per-Modality Surfacing

| Modality | Primary shadow surfaced | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | All 4 (ground-truth) | Objective measurement reveals capacity gaps directly |
| Language-reflective | Dark-addiction (mechanical language) | Verbal articulation exposes body-as-machine vs. body-as-home |
| Scenario-choice | All 4 (decision patterns) | Choice patterns reveal rigid/impulsive/overreach/avoidant |
| Embodied-somatic | Dark-addiction (no interoception) | Inhabitation quality exposes felt-sense absence |
| Strategic-planning | Dark-allergy (no planning) | Planning capacity exposes impulsive vs. strategic body |
| Social-cooperative | Dark-addiction (cannot synchronise) | Group context exposes isolation within discipline |
| Immersive-rpg | All 4 (ecological) | Spontaneous behaviour reveals genuine somatic structure |

### Cross-Validation
- Deterministic (objective) validates Language-reflective (verbal)
- Embodied-somatic (body) validates Strategic-planning (mental)
- Social-cooperative (group) validates Immersive-rpg (ecological)

### Anti-Gaming
- Form reproduction cannot be faked (timing + sequence are objective)
- Sustained posture is physiological (held-input duration measurable)
- Group synchrony is millisecond-precise (cannot be approximated)
- Breath-gating requires actual breath-rhythm (cannot be random-tapped)
