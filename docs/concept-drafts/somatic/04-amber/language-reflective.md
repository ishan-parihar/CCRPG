# Somatic / Amber — Language-Reflective

> **Axis:** The language-reflective axis probes somatic intelligence through VERBAL ARTICULATION of body-experience — can the player NAME what their body is doing? Describe the form? Articulate the felt-sense of discipline?
>
> **Why this axis:** The disciplined body at Amber is often PRE-VERBAL — the body knows the form but the person cannot articulate it. This modality tests whether somatic intelligence has reached verbal-metacognitive awareness: "I am holding because... The form requires... My body feels..."

---

## §1 Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body's Testimony"
- **Core mechanic:** The player must ARTICULATE their somatic experience — describe what their body is doing, name the form, explain the discipline, report felt-sense during movement. Not just DO the form but SPEAK the form.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Named Hold → The Described Form → The Spoken Breath → The Witnessed Rhythm → The Living Voice

---

## §2 Catalyst Delivery

**What it uniquely presents:** The demand to put WORDS to body-experience. The verbal-metacognitive dimension of embodied discipline.

**How it differs from other 6:** Only modality that requires LANGUAGE about the body (not body-action itself). Tests whether somatic intelligence has reached conscious articulation — the bridge between body-knowledge and verbal-knowledge.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Dark-addiction appears as mechanical language (body-as-manual, no felt-sense). Dark-allergy as empty language (no body-awareness to articulate). Golden-addiction as technical language without experiential grounding. Golden-allergy as frozen vocabulary at novel forms.

**Successful integration:** The player can articulate their somatic experience genuinely — with both accuracy and felt-sense.

---

## §3 Game Design

### Setup
A reflective space where the player articulates somatic experience. After performing a form (or during a hold), the player describes what their body is experiencing. Aesthetic: Amber-stage confessional/testimony space — quiet, intimate, the body's voice honoured.

### Interaction Phases
- **The Named Hold (1-5):** Hold a stance → "Where do you feel it? Name the place."
- **The Described Form (5-15):** Reproduce a form → "Describe what your body just did. How did it feel?"
- **The Spoken Breath (15-30):** Breath-coupled movement → "What happens in your body when breath and movement join?"
- **The Witnessed Rhythm (30-50):** Synchrony → "What does it feel like to move with the beat? Where does the rhythm live in your body?"
- **The Living Voice (50+):** Full integration: articulate complex somatic experience with genuine felt-sense vocabulary.

### Feedback
- Genuine felt-sense → "Your body speaks. 'Heavy in the legs, warm in the chest.' That's real."
- Mechanical only → "You described the position. Now deeper: what does it FEEL like?"
- Cannot describe → "Just one word. Where? What? Even 'tight' or 'warm' is enough."
- Technical without experience → "Simpler. Forget the textbook. What do YOU feel?"
- Frozen at novel → "New feeling. New word. Even 'strange' or 'different' counts."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Articulation demand: single word → phrase → sentence → paragraph
- Experience complexity: simple hold → form → breath-coupling → synchrony → dual-task
- Scaffolding: multiple-choice → fill-in → open response
- Novelty: familiar forms → slightly novel → entirely new
- Depth: location → sensation → quality → meaning

### Progression Table
| Phase | Checkpoints | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Named Hold | 1-5 | Name where body feels effort |
| The Described Form | 5-15 | Describe form-experience in sentences |
| The Spoken Breath | 15-30 | Articulate breath-body coupling |
| The Witnessed Rhythm | 30-50 | Describe synchrony experience |
| The Living Voice | 50+ | Full somatic articulation integration |

| Type | Pool size |
|---|---|
| Location prompts ("Where do you feel it?") | 20+ |

| Process prompts ("What happened in your body?") | 15+ |

| Integration prompts ("What does your body want to say?") | 10+ |

## §5 Technical Requirements

- **Inputs:** Voice/text (somatic descriptions); tap (body-map location); selection (scaffolded vocabulary)
- **Timing:** Response latency tracked (time to articulate); no time pressure but spontaneity noted
- **NPC/AI:** Somatic vocabulary assessment (range, depth, genuineness); body-map system; scaffolding system (vocabulary options when needed); mechanical-vs-experiential classifier; technical-without-grounding detector
- **LLM level:** Very High — evaluates natural language somatic descriptions; assesses genuineness (felt-sense vs. mechanical vs. technical-bypass); tracks vocabulary growth; generates appropriate prompts; detects shadow patterns in language quality
- **State persistence:** Somatic vocabulary history; felt-sense depth scores; accuracy metrics; novelty articulation quality; spontaneity indicators; mechanical-language frequency; empty-response frequency; technical-bypass frequency; frozen-vocabulary indicators; drive/shadow signals; checkpoint position
