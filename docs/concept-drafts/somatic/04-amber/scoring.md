# Somatic / Amber — Scoring Skeleton

## 1. What This Module Measures

**Somatic intelligence at the Amber (conformist/mythic) stage:**

The DISCIPLINED BODY — coordinated forms, sustained posture under load, group rhythm, breath-gated movement, kata. The body that serves the code. Not the power-body (Red) but the FORM-body — the body that can hold a stance, follow a drill, synchronise with a group, and maintain discipline over time.

Key distinction from Red: Red is the POWER-body (strength, speed, dominance, impulsive force). Amber is the FORM-body (discipline, coordination, sustained posture, group synchrony).

Key distinction from Orange: Orange is the SKILLED-body (multi-domain mastery, rational training, technique trees). Amber cannot optimise technique — only follow the form faithfully.

---

## 2. Capacity Dimensions

| Dimension | What it measures | Task vehicle | Ceiling at this stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sustained posture** | Hold a stance under load without breaking | Held-input stability under interference | 8-second hold with 2 distractors |
| **Form reproduction** | Reproduce a demonstrated movement sequence | Sequence-tap matching demonstrated pattern | 4-element form |
| **Breath-gated timing** | Execute movement in time with breath cadence | Bar-fill matching breath rhythm | 6-breath sustained cadence |
| **Group synchrony** | Move in time with external rhythm/group | Sensorimotor synchronisation tapping | 120 BPM ± 30ms |
| **Coordination under load** | Maintain form while performing secondary task | Dual-task posture maintenance | Posture + simple classification |

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|

| **θ max-decay** | 20% | Embodied skills have strong retention floor |

| **Shadow-drag coefficient** | 0.20 | Somatic shadows strongly impair form-capacity |
| **Cross-line bonus cap** | +0.15 | Willpower (sustained effort) and Cognitive (rule-following) support somatic form |

## 6. Theta-Decay and Maintenance

- **Active maintenance:** Playing any Somatic/Amber game resets theta
- **Passive decay:** 14-day half-life; capacity score degrades without practice
- **Shadow-mode trigger:** When theta drops below 0.6, shadow-mode encounters activate
- **Cross-line support:** Willpower/Amber health slows somatic theta-decay by up to 15%

---

## 7. Stage-Transition Indicators

### Readiness for Orange (upward)
- Sustained posture >90% at ceiling (8s + 2 distractors)
- Form reproduction >90% at 4-element
- Breath-gated timing >85% at 6-breath cadence
- Group synchrony <30ms deviation at 120 BPM
- Drive-health >0.7 across all 4 drives
- Shadow-drag <0.10

### Regression toward Red (downward)
- Sustained posture <60% (breaks form under load)
- Form reproduction <60% (cannot follow demonstrated sequence)
- Breath-gated timing <50% (breath and movement decouple)
- Group synchrony >80ms deviation (cannot maintain group rhythm)
- Dark-addiction or dark-allergy shadow-drag >0.15
