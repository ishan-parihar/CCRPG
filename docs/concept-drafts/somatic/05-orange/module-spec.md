# Somatic / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Somatic  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The body trained scientifically — multi-domain technique mastery through rational optimization, biomechanical precision, and performance metrics.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The skilled body operating as a precision instrument across multiple domains. The player demonstrates formal-operational physical intelligence: hypothetico-deductive training (if I adjust X, performance Y improves), technique trees with branching specializations, cross-domain transfer of motor schemas, and self-correcting feedback loops. Movement is no longer duty (Amber) but *craft* — optimized, measured, and rationally refined.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Machine

Body reduced to performance metrics. Over-training past pain signals, injury-through-optimization, dissociation from felt-sense. The body is an instrument to be pushed harder — fatigue is weakness, pain is noise to override. Compulsive metric-tracking replaces embodied awareness.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Sedentary Intellectual

Refuses physical mastery entirely. Remains in Amber routine (mechanical repetition without skill growth) or abandons the body as irrelevant. "The body is just a vehicle for the mind." Physical incompetence rationalized as intellectual superiority.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Somatic Empath

Performs Green-level somatic sensitivity — talks about subtle energy, body wisdom, somatic empathy — without genuine physical mastery underneath. Cannot execute skilled movement but claims to have transcended the need for technique. Spiritual bypassing of physical competence.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Technique Fortress

Physical mastery is present and genuine, but somatic sensitivity is refused. Terrified of "soft" body-awareness. Feelings in the body are distractions from performance. The body listens to the mind, never the reverse. Blocks the developmental call toward Green embodiment.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaching toward new technique domains, cross-training curiosity | Compulsive novelty-seeking, never deepening (GA fuel) |
| Agape | Returning to fundamentals, honoring recovery, embodying basics | Regression to Amber routine, refusing challenge (DAll fuel) |
| Agency | Sovereign training design, self-authored programs | Over-isolation, refusing feedback, The Machine (DA fuel) |
| Communion | Training with others, teaching, synchronized practice | Losing self in group performance standards (GAll fuel) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

For DA: Agency reclaims sovereignty FROM the metrics — "I choose when to rest." Agape returns attention to the body's actual signals. For DAll: Agency authors a physical practice (not imposed by duty). Agape embodies rather than intellectualizes movement.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

For GA: Eros reaches toward genuine skill (not bypassed sensitivity). Communion grounds in shared physical practice that demands real competence. For GAll: Eros reaches toward somatic listening. Communion opens the body to relational attunement.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integrated Somatic/Orange: executes multi-domain technique with precision AND maintains felt-sense contact; trains scientifically AND honors recovery signals; achieves physical excellence AND remains open to somatic sensitivity emerging.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Burnout Athlete (Somatic DA × Willpower DA)

Over-training meets compulsive achievement. The body breaks down through optimization while willpower refuses to acknowledge limits. Physical breakdown rationalized as insufficient effort.

### 6.2 The Disembodied Thinker (Somatic DAll × Cognitive DA)

Refuses body entirely while hyper-investing in rationalism. Complete mind-body split. Physical atrophy paired with cognitive over-identification. The body becomes invisible.

### 6.3 The Performance Addict (Somatic DA × Intrapersonal DA)

Body-as-metrics meets identity-as-achievement. Self-worth derived exclusively from physical performance numbers. Identity collapses when performance declines.

### 6.4 The Technique Collector (Somatic GA × Cognitive GA)

Premature somatic sensitivity meets premature systems-thinking. Elaborate theoretical frameworks about embodiment without genuine physical skill. Talks about movement without moving.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: baseline technique capacity, no shadow provocation. Sessions 4–7: introduce fatigue/pressure to surface DA and DAll. Sessions 8–12: introduce sensitivity/relational cues to surface GA and GAll. Session 13+: compound shadow provocations.

### 7.2 Per-Modality Surfacing Table

| Modality | Primary Shadow Surfaced | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | DA | Metric obsession under increasing load |
| Language-Reflective | GA / DAll | Verbal reveals embodied vs. intellectual relationship |
| Scenario-Choice | DA / GAll | Technique selection under pressure |
| Embodied-Somatic | DA / DAll | Progressive skill acquisition reveals avoidance |
| Strategic-Planning | DA / GA | Training design reveals optimization compulsion or bypass |
| Social-Cooperative | GAll / GA | Relational physical practice reveals fortress or bypass |
| Immersive-RPG | All four | Ecological behavior reveals dominant shadow |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

No shadow is diagnosed from a single modality. DA requires convergence across deterministic + embodied + strategic. GA requires convergence across language-reflective + social + immersive. Compound shadows require cross-line signal correlation.
