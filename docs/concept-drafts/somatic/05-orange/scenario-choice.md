# Somatic / Orange — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Physical dilemmas requiring technique selection under pressure — choosing between competing physical strategies.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange somatic intelligence is revealed in HOW the player decides between physical approaches when stakes are real and time is limited.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** The Crucible of Technique
- **Core Mechanic:** Player faces physical scenarios with multiple valid technique approaches. Each choice reveals drive-orientation and shadow patterns through which strategy is selected and why.
- **Duration:** 5–8 minutes per session, infinite checkpoint.
- **Internal Progression:** Binary technique choices → multi-option strategy selection → time-pressured decisions → fatigue-state decisions → recovery-vs-push dilemmas → integration choices honoring both performance and body.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

- **Unique Presentation:** An arena where physical challenges appear and the player must choose their approach BEFORE executing. The choice itself is the diagnostic — not the execution quality.
- **Differs From Others:** Not about physical precision (deterministic) or verbal knowledge (language-reflective) — about decision-making under physical pressure.
- **Uniquely Surfaces:** DA (always chooses maximum intensity), DAll (always chooses avoidance/minimal effort), GAll (always chooses technique over sensitivity), GA (always chooses "intuitive" over technical).
- **Successful Integration:** Player selects strategies that balance performance with sustainability, technique with felt-sense, challenge with recovery — context-appropriate rather than shadow-driven.

## 3. Game Design

- **Setup:** Physical scenario presented with 2–4 technique options. Context includes: current fatigue level, stakes, time pressure, body-state signals.
- **Interaction Phases:**
  1. Assessment — player reads the physical scenario and body-state indicators
  2. Options — 2–4 technique strategies presented with tradeoffs
  3. Decision — player selects approach under time pressure
  4. Consequence — outcome reveals what the choice cost and gained
  5. Reflection — brief prompt: "Would you choose differently knowing this?"
- **Feedback Examples:**
  1. "You chose maximum intensity despite fatigue signals — the technique succeeded but your body paid" (DA)
  2. "You chose the safest option despite having capacity for more — what were you protecting?" (DAll)
  3. "You chose the technically optimal path but ignored the body's preference for flow" (GAll)
  4. "You chose the intuitive path but lacked the technique to execute it" (GA)
  5. "You matched strategy to context — high effort when fresh, recovery when fatigued" (integration)
- **Difficulty Adaptation:** Scenarios increase in ambiguity — early choices have clear optimal answers, later choices have genuine tradeoffs. Shadow-probing scenarios introduced once baseline is established.
- **Internal Progression Table:**

| Level | Scenario Type | Shadow Probe |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Clear optimal choice | Baseline decision style |
| 4–6 | Performance vs. recovery tradeoffs | DA: always pushes; DAll: always rests |
| 7–9 | Technique vs. intuition tradeoffs | GAll: always technique; GA: always intuition |
| 10–12 | Compound dilemmas (all drives in tension) | Dominant shadow pattern emerges |
| 13+ | Integration scenarios requiring all drives | Shadow-free decision-making |

## 4. Item Pool

- **Performance-Recovery Dilemmas (20):** Push through mild fatigue for a PR, rest before a critical sequence, train injured or modify, sleep vs. early training, deload week vs. momentum, compete fatigued or withdraw, add volume or add recovery, ignore soreness or investigate, push past plateau or consolidate, sacrifice form for intensity, maintain technique under exhaustion, choose between competing recovery needs, train through illness or rest, peak now or build base, sacrifice short-term for long-term, override pain signal or honor it, extend session or end strong, add complexity or master basics, compete under-recovered or skip, train angry or wait for calm
- **Technique-Intuition Dilemmas (18):** Follow the program or follow the body, textbook form or personal adaptation, coach's instruction or felt-sense, systematic progression or organic exploration, measured approach or creative experimentation, planned sequence or spontaneous flow, analytical correction or embodied adjustment, structured warm-up or intuitive preparation, prescribed rest intervals or felt-readiness, technical drill or free practice, biomechanical ideal or comfortable variation, progressive overload or autoregulation, periodized plan or daily readiness, video analysis or internal feedback, metric-driven adjustment or sensation-driven, external cue or internal focus, standardized technique or personal style, systematic troubleshooting or holistic sensing
- **Intensity-Selection Scenarios (15):** Maximum effort available but not required, moderate challenge with guaranteed success vs. hard challenge with possible failure, familiar excellence vs. unfamiliar growth, safe technique vs. risky advancement, conservative pacing vs. aggressive start, controlled environment vs. chaotic challenge, isolated skill vs. integrated performance, slow mastery vs. fast approximation, depth in one domain vs. breadth across many, perfect repetition vs. challenging variation, maintenance vs. development, refinement vs. exploration, specialization vs. generalization, competition vs. practice, demonstration vs. experimentation
- **Social-Physical Dilemmas (15):** Train alone optimally or with partner sub-optimally, match partner's pace or maintain own, teach (slower) or train (faster), compete against weaker or stronger opponent, accept feedback or trust self, follow group energy or self-regulate, perform for audience or practice privately, share technique knowledge or protect advantage, collaborate on shared goal or pursue individual excellence, synchronize with group or optimize individually, accept physical help or maintain independence, train in community or solitude, visible practice or private mastery, mentor others or focus on self, celebrate with others or analyze alone
- **Integration Probes (12):** Scenarios where all four drives are in tension simultaneously, where no single shadow-driven choice is clearly wrong, where context genuinely determines the right answer, where the player must weigh multiple body-signals against multiple performance goals, where recovery IS the high-performance choice, where intuition and technique agree, where pushing IS self-care, where rest IS avoidance, where technique IS sensitivity, where metrics serve embodiment, where the body and mind want the same thing, where integration is the only path forward

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input Types:** Selection from presented options (tap/click). Optional free-text justification for deeper shadow assessment.
- **Timing:** Time pressure on decisions (3–8 seconds) to prevent over-intellectualization. Pressure increases with level.
- **NPC/AI:** Scenario generation engine with fatigue-state modeling. Consequence calculator based on choice + context.
- **LLM:** Generates scenario narratives, evaluates free-text justifications, adapts scenario framing to player's demonstrated patterns.
- **State Persistence:** Choice pattern history, shadow-indicator ratios per decision type, fatigue-state decision correlations, drive-balance trajectories, integration progression markers.
