# Somatic / Infrared — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Co-regulation — basic nervous-system synchrony with another presence.  **Why this axis for this module:** At Infrared, "social" is purely somatic — one nervous system regulating in the presence of another. No communication, no cooperation in the cognitive sense. Just two bodies finding rhythm together. This is the biological foundation of all future social capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Sync  
**Core loop:** Another presence appears (AI-driven rhythm) → player's body attunes to its rhythm → synchrony builds → presence responds to player → mutual regulation emerges or fails.  
**Feel:** Two pulses in a dark space. One is the player's touch-rhythm; the other is the game's heartbeat. When they align, the space warms and expands. When they diverge, the space contracts. No faces, no characters — just two rhythmic presences.  
**Session length:** 90–240 seconds per checkpoint. Infinite checkpoints.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Red-ray co-presence — the most primitive social catalyst: another nervous system exists and your body responds to it.  
**Catalyst → Experience:** The game creates genuine nervous-system attunement. The AI presence's rhythm is designed to entrain the player's touch-rhythm. This is real co-regulation — the body synchronises before the mind decides to.  
**Experience → Integration:** Learning that another presence can be regulating rather than threatening. The body discovers that synchrony is safe, that co-presence can reduce arousal rather than increase it. This is the somatic foundation of all attachment.

**Drive probing:**
- **Agency:** Maintaining own rhythm while in co-presence (not losing self to other)
- **Communion:** Willingness to adjust rhythm toward the other (attunement)
- **Eros:** Reaching toward closer synchrony (approaching the other)
- **Agape:** Allowing the other's rhythm to influence without collapsing (receptive grounding)

**Shadow surfacing:**
- DA: Player's rhythm accelerates in co-presence (hyperarousal triggered by other)
- DAll: Player's rhythm drops out or freezes in co-presence (collapse triggered by other)
- GA: Attempts complex rhythmic interplay before basic synchrony is established
- GAll: Maintains rigid own-rhythm, refuses any attunement (isolated in reflex)

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**
- AI presence: a pulsing visual/haptic rhythm (heartbeat-like, 40–80 BPM range)
- Player input: tap rhythm on screen — their own "heartbeat"
- Synchrony measure: phase-alignment between player-tap and AI-pulse
- Mutual responsiveness: AI rhythm subtly adjusts toward player (bidirectional)
- Proximity: visual distance between the two presences reflects synchrony level

**Adaptive staircase:**
- AI rhythm starts at player's baseline tap-rate (minimal adjustment needed)
- Gradually diverges — player must actively attune to maintain synchrony
- If DA detected: AI rhythm slows deliberately, rewarding player deceleration
- If DAll detected: AI rhythm matches player exactly first, then gently leads
- If GAll detected: AI presence starts distant, approaches only as synchrony builds
- Synchrony threshold widens for early sessions, narrows as capacity builds

**Progression (within-session):**
1. Presence: AI rhythm appears, player taps freely — baseline established
2. Attunement: AI rhythm diverges slightly, player adjusts to maintain synchrony
3. Mutual: both rhythms influence each other — true co-regulation
4. Integration: AI presence withdraws gradually, measuring whether player's rhythm remains regulated or destabilises

## 4. Item Pool

| Item Category | Examples | Shadow Targeted |
|---|---|---|
| AI rhythm rates | 40, 50, 60, 70, 80 BPM | Baseline matching |
| Divergence amount | ±5%, ±10%, ±20% from player | Communion (attunement range) |
| Presence proximity | Far, medium, near, touching | GAll (tolerance of closeness) |
| Responsiveness ratio | AI adjusts 20%, 40%, 60% toward player | Agency (self vs other balance) |
| Co-presence duration | 30s, 60s, 90s, 120s | DAll (endurance in presence) |
| Withdrawal speed | Gradual (20s), moderate (10s), sudden (2s) | DA (separation response) |

The "other" is never a character or face — it is a pure rhythmic presence. This keeps the task at Infrared level: body-to-body, not mind-to-mind.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Touch (tap timing and rhythm), optional: device microphone for breath-rhythm  
**Output:** Visual dual-pulse display, haptic rhythm from AI presence, low-frequency audio heartbeat  
**Synchrony calculation:** Phase-coupling index (PCI) computed in real-time from tap-timing vs AI-pulse timing  
**Data captured:** Tap-rate time-series, phase-alignment score per beat, synchrony duration, rhythm stability in co-presence vs alone, withdrawal response pattern  
**Adaptive engine:** AI rhythm controller adjusts based on real-time PCI; divergence schedule personalised to player's attunement capacity; shadow-profile updated per-session  
**Accessibility:** Visual-only mode (synchrony shown as colour-match); haptic-only mode (feel the other's rhythm); adjustable AI responsiveness for motor impairment
