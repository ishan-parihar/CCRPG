# Somatic / Infrared — Module Specification

> **Line:** Somatic  **Stage:** Infrared / Archaic  **Vibration:** The body responds before the mind notices — pure reflex is the first intelligence.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

The somatic line at Infrared is the organism's raw motor-reflex substrate: startle response, fight-or-flight activation/deactivation, grip, reach, basic locomotion, and tension-release cycling. No self-other differentiation exists; the body IS the intelligence. This is the only line at full expression at Infrared — all other lines are dormant or nascent. Healthy capacity means reflexes fire appropriately, habituate when threat passes, and the body can transition fluidly between mobilisation and rest.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction (Hyperarousal)

The body cannot leave fight-or-flight. Startle response never habituates. Chronic muscular tension, elevated baseline arousal, reflexes firing continuously without resolution. The organism is locked in survival mode even when no threat is present.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy (Freeze/Collapse)

The body shuts down entirely. Dissociation from physical sensation, inability to mobilise, dorsal-vagal collapse. "Playing dead" as a permanent state. Reflexes are suppressed rather than integrated — the organism cannot respond even when response is needed.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction (Premature Rhythm)

Attempts rhythmic, dance-like, or "graceful" movement without basic reflex integration. Reaches toward Magenta-stage somatic expression (ritual movement, body-as-vessel) while the foundational reflexes remain ungrounded. Movement looks evolved but lacks survival substrate.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy (Reflex Fortress)

Reflexes are present and functional but the organism refuses any non-survival movement. Terrified of rhythmic emergence. "Movement is only for escape." The body works but will not play, explore, or express beyond threat-response.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Pole |
|---|---|---|
| Eros (vertical-up) | Reflex reaches toward rhythm, body wants to move beyond survival | Premature rhythm without grounding (GA) |
| Agape (vertical-down) | Returns to embodiment, grounds in sensation, inhabits the body | Collapses into freeze, over-grounds into immobility (DAll) |
| Agency (horizontal-self) | Sovereign mobilisation, "I move," fight response when needed | Locked in fight, cannot stop mobilising (DA) |
| Communion (horizontal-other) | Body attunes to co-presence, nervous-system synchrony | Refuses attunement, isolated in reflex fortress (GAll) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

Agape returns awareness to the body (counters dissociation/DAll). Agency mobilises sovereign movement (counters hyperarousal/DA by completing the fight-or-flight cycle). Together they integrate dark shadows by completing interrupted survival responses and re-inhabiting the body.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

Eros invites upward reach toward rhythm (counters Reflex Fortress/GAll). Communion dissolves isolation through co-regulated movement (counters Premature Rhythm/GA by grounding the reach in relational attunement). Together they dissolve golden shadows by making emergence safe and relational.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integration is demonstrated when: (a) reflexes fire and resolve within appropriate time-windows, (b) startle habituates within 3–5 repetitions, (c) the body can transition between mobilisation and rest without getting stuck, (d) non-survival movement emerges spontaneously without losing reflex grounding.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Frozen Scanner (Somatic DA × Cognitive DA)

Hyperarousal locks the body while hypervigilant cognitive tracking scans endlessly. Result: paralysed alertness — the organism is maximally aroused but cannot act. Intervention requires somatic discharge before cognitive de-escalation.

### 6.2 The Collapsed Body (Somatic DAll × Emotional DAll)

Freeze/collapse meets emotional numbness. Complete shutdown — no movement, no feeling, no response. The deepest withdrawal state. Intervention requires titrated sensory input to re-establish body-awareness before emotional contact.

### 6.3 The Premature Dancer (Somatic GA × Willpower GA)

Premature rhythm meets premature goal-seeking. Ungrounded movement directed at ungrounded goals — "spiritual bypass through the body." Intervention requires returning to basic reflex completion before allowing directed movement.

### 6.4 The Survival Fortress (Somatic GAll × Interpersonal GAll)

Refuses non-survival movement AND refuses co-presence. Isolated in reflex — the body works only for escape and only alone. Intervention requires safe co-regulation (another nervous system present without demand) before movement exploration.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Shadows surface in order of accessibility: DA first (hyperarousal is visible in reaction-time variance), DAll second (freeze appears when arousal tasks escalate), GAll third (refusal of non-survival movement emerges in free-play), GA last (premature rhythm only visible when rhythmic tasks are introduced).

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

A shadow is confirmed when it appears across ≥2 modalities with convergent scoring. Compound shadows require convergent evidence from both constituent lines. No shadow diagnosis is made from a single modality session.
