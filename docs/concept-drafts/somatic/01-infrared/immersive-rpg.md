# Somatic / Infrared — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological body — whether reflex-appropriate responses appear spontaneously in free-play.  **Why this axis for this module:** The RPG modality tests whether integrated reflexes emerge naturally in an unstructured environment. No prompts, no cues — just a world that demands somatic intelligence. This is the ecological validity check for all other modalities.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Primal-World  
**Core loop:** Player inhabits a body in a primordial environment → threats, resources, and rest-zones appear organically → the body must respond with appropriate reflexes → survival emerges from integrated somatic capacity.  
**Feel:** A dark, textured landscape — cave walls, water, heat sources, approaching presences. No HUD, no instructions, no score visible. The player IS a body in a world. Everything is felt through haptic, audio, and minimal visual cues. Deeply immersive, pre-verbal, animal.  
**Session length:** 180–600 seconds per checkpoint. Infinite checkpoints.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Red-ray ecological survival — the full spectrum of somatic capacity tested in naturalistic context.  
**Catalyst → Experience:** The world presents situations requiring all previously trained reflexes: startle-and-recover, fight/flight/freeze choice, tension-release, motor sequencing, breath regulation, co-presence attunement. But nothing is cued — the body must recognise and respond spontaneously.  
**Experience → Integration:** Spontaneous appropriate response in free-play demonstrates true integration. The body doesn't need prompts — it knows what to do. This is the highest expression of Infrared somatic capacity: reflexes that serve life without instruction.

**Drive probing:**
- **Agency:** Spontaneous mobilisation when threats appear (unprompted fight/flight)
- **Communion:** Attunement to environmental rhythms and co-presences (unprompted synchrony)
- **Eros:** Exploration of the world beyond survival needs (curiosity-movement)
- **Agape:** Spontaneous rest when safe (finding and using rest-zones without cue)

**Shadow surfacing:**
- DA: Never rests even in safe zones; constant movement; hypervigilant exploration
- DAll: Remains motionless regardless of context; does not explore or respond
- GA: Movement becomes "graceful" or rhythmic prematurely — dancing through danger
- GAll: Responds only to threats; ignores rest-zones, co-presences, and exploration opportunities

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**
- Open environment with zones: threat-zones (approaching presences), resource-zones (warmth, water), rest-zones (stillness, safety), social-zones (other rhythmic presences)
- No explicit instructions — the world communicates through haptic/audio/visual intensity
- Player moves via touch-drag; interacts via tap, hold, swipe (same vocabulary as other modalities)
- Threats require appropriate survival response (fight/flight/freeze based on intensity)
- Rest-zones reward stillness (body settles, world warms)
- Social-zones reward synchrony (co-presence attunement from Social-Cooperative)

**Adaptive staircase:**
- World complexity scales with demonstrated integration across modalities
- Threat frequency adapts: if DA detected, more rest-zones appear; if DAll, gentler threats
- Zone transitions become subtler as capacity grows (less obvious safety/danger coding)
- If GAll detected: exploration rewards increase (non-survival movement rewarded by world response)
- If GA detected: world demands grounded reflexes before allowing fluid movement

**Progression (within-session):**
1. Orientation: safe environment, player discovers movement vocabulary
2. First threats: simple, obvious threats requiring basic reflex response
3. Ecology: mixed zones — threats, rest, social, resources — requiring flexible response
4. Integration: subtle environment where appropriate response requires genuine somatic intelligence

## 4. Item Pool

| Item Category | Examples | Shadow Targeted |
|---|---|---|
| Threat types | Approaching mass, sudden vibration, heat-surge | DA/DAll (response appropriateness) |
| Rest-zone cues | Warmth, stillness, dim glow, soft haptic | DA (can you rest?), GAll (will you?) |
| Social presences | Rhythmic pulse nearby, approaching heartbeat | GAll (co-presence tolerance) |
| Exploration rewards | New textures, warmth-sources, opening paths | GAll (non-survival movement) |
| Ambiguous zones | Neither clearly safe nor threatening | Integration (flexible response) |
| Transition gradients | Slow safe→threat, sudden threat→safe | All (transition flexibility) |

The world is abstract-organic — no recognisable objects, no language, no symbols. Everything is texture, temperature, rhythm, and presence.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Touch (drag for movement, tap/hold/swipe for interaction), accelerometer (startle detection)  
**Output:** Full-screen environment with zone-based rendering, continuous haptic landscape, spatial audio (proximity-based), minimal visual HUD  
**World engine:** Procedural zone generation based on player's shadow-profile; zone transitions smooth and organic  
**Data captured:** Movement patterns (exploration vs avoidance), response appropriateness per zone-type, rest-zone utilisation, social-zone engagement, spontaneous vs cued responses, time-in-zone distributions  
**Adaptive engine:** Zone generation weighted by shadow-profile; threat/rest ratio personalised; world complexity scaled to cross-modality integration scores  
**Accessibility:** High-contrast zone coding available; audio-described mode; simplified movement (tap-to-move); adjustable world pace
