# Somatic / Green — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological somatic sensitivity — whether relational body-awareness appears spontaneously in free-play.  **Why this axis for this module:** The ultimate test of Green somatic integration is whether the listening body activates WITHOUT prompting — in open-world play, does the player spontaneously attune, set boundaries, and co-regulate?

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Listening World
**Core loop:** Player navigates an open RPG environment populated with NPCs who have body-states. No explicit somatic instructions — the game observes whether the player spontaneously engages relational body-awareness or defaults to instrumental/avoidant patterns.
**Feel:** A living village where bodies speak. NPCs breathe, tense, relax, reach. The world rewards attunement without demanding it.
**Session length:** 8–15 minutes per free-play session. Infinite checkpoints.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Ecological observation — the game environment contains somatic-relational opportunities that are never forced. Catalyst is ambient, not directed.
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as compulsive attunement to every NPC (cannot pass a body in distress without merging). DAll surfaces as treating all NPCs instrumentally (interacts for quest objectives only, ignores body-states). GA surfaces as performing "energy healing" on NPCs without genuine attunement (acts on bodies without reading them). GAll surfaces as accurate reading of nearby NPCs but avoidance of unfamiliar/intense body-states in the world.
**Drive probing:** Agency = selective attunement (choosing WHEN to engage somatically). Communion = quality of spontaneous co-regulation. Eros = exploration of unfamiliar somatic territories in the world. Agape = grounding actions (helping NPCs regulate without losing self).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — NPC Body-States:** Every NPC has a visible/audible body-state (breath rate, tension indicators, energy level). These are ambient — never highlighted or quest-marked. Player may notice or ignore.
**Mechanic 2 — Attunement Opportunities:** Certain NPCs are in somatic distress or joy. Player can approach and engage co-regulation (rhythm-matching mechanic activates on proximity + intent). Outcomes depend on attunement quality.
**Mechanic 3 — Boundary Ecology:** Some NPCs are "loud" somatically — their states push into the player's space. Does the player set boundaries, absorb, or avoid? Tracked passively.
**Mechanic 4 — Emergent Narrative:** Player's somatic engagement pattern shapes the village ecology. Attuned players create calmer environments; overwhelmed players create chaotic ones; avoidant players create disconnected ones.
**Progression:** Village familiarity → stranger encounters → group dynamics → crisis events → transpersonal openings (all optional, all observable).

## 4. Item Pool

| Ecological element | What it tests | Shadow observable |
|---|---|---|
| Distressed NPC (familiar) | Spontaneous attunement to known other | DAll (ignores) vs. DA (compulsive merge) |
| Distressed NPC (stranger) | Attunement beyond comfort zone | GAll (avoids stranger) vs. Communion (engages) |
| Loud NPC (intrusive state) | Boundary-setting in open world | DA (absorbs) vs. Agency (sets boundary) |
| Group gathering | Spontaneous group co-regulation | DA (dissolves) vs. DAll (observes instrumentally) |
| Transpersonal event | World-level somatic phenomenon | GAll (flees) vs. GA (performs response) |
| Quiet NPC (subtle state) | Sensitivity to non-obvious signals | DAll (misses) vs. healthy (notices gently) |

Minimum pool: 40 ecological elements across 6 types, distributed throughout the game world. Elements cycle and refresh across sessions.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Proximity-based engagement (approach + dwell), rhythm-matching when co-regulation activates, movement patterns (toward/away/around NPCs).
**Passive measurement:** Approach frequency, dwell time near distressed NPCs, co-regulation initiation rate, boundary-setting frequency, avoidance patterns, engagement selectivity.
**Ecological scoring:** No per-trial feedback. Scoring aggregates across full sessions — pattern recognition over time.
**Telemetry:** Heatmap of somatic engagement, NPC-interaction quality scores, boundary events, avoidance events, compulsive-engagement events.
**Scoring output:** Agency ← selective engagement + boundary events, Communion ← co-regulation quality + spontaneous attunement, Eros ← unfamiliar-NPC engagement + transpersonal event response, Agape ← grounding actions + village ecology health.
**No LLM dependency.** NPC states are parameterised; ecological dynamics are rule-based. Narrative emerges from player behaviour patterns.
