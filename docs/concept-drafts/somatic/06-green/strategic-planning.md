# Somatic / Green — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** Planning somatic life — structuring sensitivity practice and managing empathic load across time.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green somatic capacity requires STEWARDSHIP — the sensitive body needs strategic care, boundary-scheduling, and load-management to remain healthy rather than overwhelmed or shut down.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Somatic Steward
**Core loop:** Player plans and allocates somatic resources across relational scenarios — scheduling attunement, rest, boundary-setting, and depth-exploration to maintain a healthy sensitive body over time.
**Feel:** A gentle resource-management game — tending a garden of sensitivity. Strategic but warm, never cold optimisation.
**Session length:** 5–10 minutes per planning cycle. Infinite checkpoints.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Resource-allocation dilemmas that reveal how the player relates to their own somatic sensitivity as a resource to be stewarded.
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as over-allocating to attunement with no rest/boundary scheduled (gives body away). DAll surfaces as over-allocating to performance/rest with no attunement scheduled (hoards body). GA surfaces as planning "energy work" and "field practice" without relational attunement blocks. GAll surfaces as careful relational planning that never includes depth/edge exploration.
**Drive probing:** Agency = boundary-scheduling quality. Communion = attunement-allocation richness. Eros = inclusion of growth-edge activities. Agape = rest and grounding allocation.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Week Planning:** Player receives a "somatic week" with relational demands (partner needs, group events, solo time, edge opportunities). Must allocate limited somatic energy across: Attunement, Boundary, Rest, Exploration.
**Mechanic 2 — Consequence Simulation:** Plan plays out — over-attunement leads to depletion, over-boundary leads to disconnection, over-exploration leads to destabilisation, over-rest leads to stagnation. Player sees outcomes and adjusts.
**Mechanic 3 — Adaptive Demand:** Each cycle introduces new relational demands that stress the plan. Can the player adapt without collapsing into shadow-patterns?
**Mechanic 4 — Long-Arc Strategy:** Across multiple cycles, player builds a sustainable somatic lifestyle. Scoring rewards balance and adaptability over any single allocation pattern.
**Progression:** Simple dyadic demands → complex relational ecology → crisis management → long-term sustainability → edge-integration planning.

## 4. Item Pool

| Demand type | Example | Shadow revealed by response |
|---|---|---|
| Partner-need | "Partner is grieving; needs somatic co-presence for 3 days" | DA (gives all) vs. DAll (gives none) |
| Group-event | "Workshop with 20 people; high empathic demand" | DA (no boundary) vs. DAll (skips) |
| Edge-opportunity | "Transpersonal somatic retreat available" | GAll (avoids) vs. GA (over-commits) |
| Self-care | "Body is depleted; needs 2 days minimum rest" | DA (ignores) vs. DAll (over-extends) |
| Boundary-test | "Someone asks for more than you planned to give" | DA (gives anyway) vs. Agency (holds) |

Minimum pool: 50 demand cards across 5 types, 3 intensity tiers. Demands combine into compound scenarios across planning cycles.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Drag-and-drop allocation (energy units to activity slots), slider-based proportion setting, binary accept/decline for demands.
**Simulation engine:** Simple resource model — somatic energy (0–100), depletion/recovery rates per activity type, threshold effects (burnout below 20, stagnation above 80 with no attunement).
**Scoring logic:** Balance score (deviation from healthy distribution), adaptability score (response to unexpected demands), growth score (edge-inclusion over time), sustainability score (long-arc energy maintenance).
**Telemetry:** Allocation patterns per category, response to depletion signals, boundary-holding frequency, edge-avoidance frequency.
**Scoring output:** Agency ← boundary-scheduling + holding under pressure, Communion ← attunement-allocation richness, Eros ← edge-inclusion frequency, Agape ← rest-allocation + depletion-response.
**No LLM dependency.** Deterministic simulation with parameterised demand generation.
