# Somatic / Green — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body attuning to others — progressive somatic empathy development through co-presence practice.  **Why this axis for this module:** This IS the line×stage capacity in its purest form — the body learning to feel others while remaining itself.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Attunement
**Core loop:** Player engages in progressive body-based practices that develop somatic empathy — sensing, synchronising, differentiating, and co-regulating with NPC body-states through their own embodied input.
**Feel:** Warm, intimate, rhythmic. Like sitting with someone in silence and feeling what they feel. The game breathes with you.
**Session length:** 3–7 minutes per practice. Infinite checkpoints.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Embodied co-presence practices that progressively develop the relational body — each practice IS the capacity being built.
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as immediate over-synchronisation (player matches NPC state instantly, loses own rhythm). DAll surfaces as persistent desynchronisation (player maintains own rhythm rigidly, never attunes). GA surfaces as claimed attunement without actual phase-lock (reports feeling but input doesn't match). GAll surfaces as accurate attunement that stops at a threshold (synchronises with familiar states, avoids unfamiliar ones).
**Drive probing:** Agency = maintained self-rhythm during attunement. Communion = accuracy of co-regulation. Eros = willingness to attune to unfamiliar/intense states. Agape = quality of return-to-self after deep attunement.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Breath Attunement:** NPC breathes at a rhythm. Player synchronises their tap-rhythm to the NPC's breath. Then: can they return to their OWN rhythm? Measures co-regulation AND boundary.
**Mechanic 2 — State Mirroring:** NPC displays a body-state (tension pattern, energy level, rhythm quality). Player adjusts their input to mirror it — then must identify: "What am I feeling that is MINE vs. THEIRS?"
**Mechanic 3 — Co-Presence Hold:** Player and NPC maintain synchronised rhythm for extended duration. Difficulty: NPC state shifts subtly — can player track the shift while staying connected? Measures sustained relational attention.
**Mechanic 4 — Return Practice:** After deep attunement, player must re-establish their baseline rhythm. Speed and accuracy of return measures Agape/grounding capacity.
**Progression:** Single-NPC simple rhythm → complex states → multiple NPCs → conflicting states → transpersonal attunement → return mastery.

## 4. Item Pool

| Practice type | Somatic task | Shadow surfaced |
|---|---|---|
| Simple sync | Match NPC breath rhythm | DAll (refuses to sync) |
| State mirror | Mirror NPC tension/ease pattern | DA (over-mirrors, adds own) |
| Sustained hold | Maintain co-presence through shifts | GAll (drops at edge) |
| Multi-body | Attune to 2+ NPCs simultaneously | DA (overwhelm) vs. DAll (picks one instrumentally) |
| Return | Come back to own baseline after deep sync | DA (cannot return) |
| Edge attunement | Sync with unfamiliar/intense NPC state | GAll (avoids) vs. GA (performs without accuracy) |

Minimum pool: 30 practice sequences across 6 types, 4 difficulty tiers. Each sequence is 60–120 seconds of embodied input.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Rhythm-tapping (primary), hold-duration, tempo-matching. Touch-based for mobile; click-based for desktop.
**Measurement:** Phase-lock accuracy (cross-correlation of player rhythm vs. NPC rhythm), synchronisation latency, return-to-baseline speed, sustained coherence duration.
**Adaptive engine:** NPC rhythm complexity scales with player accuracy. Threshold for "attunement achieved" adapts per player.
**Telemetry:** Sync accuracy, sync latency, return speed, hold duration, over-sync frequency (DA indicator), under-sync frequency (DAll indicator).
**Scoring output:** Agency ← return-to-self speed + maintained baseline, Communion ← sync accuracy + hold duration, Eros ← edge-attunement engagement, Agape ← return quality + grounding stability.
**No LLM dependency.** All practices are rhythm-based with parameterised NPC states.
