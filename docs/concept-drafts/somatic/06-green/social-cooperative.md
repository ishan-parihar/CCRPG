# Somatic / Green — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Shared somatic field — group co-regulation, synchronized movement, and somatic attunement with NPC partners.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green somatic capacity is inherently relational — the social-cooperative modality tests whether the body can join a shared field without losing itself or instrumentalising others.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Shared Body
**Core loop:** Player co-regulates with NPC partners in progressively complex group somatic tasks — synchronised movement, shared rhythm, collective attunement — while maintaining individual somatic identity.
**Feel:** A dance with others — not choreographed performance but emergent co-movement. The group breathes together.
**Session length:** 4–8 minutes per cooperative sequence. Infinite checkpoints.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Group somatic tasks that require genuine co-regulation — the shared field either emerges or it doesn't, and the player's contribution is measurable.
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as losing individual rhythm in group (dissolves into collective). DAll surfaces as maintaining rigid individual rhythm regardless of group (performs alongside, never with). GA surfaces as claiming group-field awareness without contributing to actual synchrony. GAll surfaces as accurate dyadic co-regulation that collapses in larger groups (won't extend beyond safe pairs).
**Drive probing:** Agency = maintained individual rhythm within group field. Communion = contribution to group synchrony. Eros = willingness to join unfamiliar group configurations. Agape = grounding the group (offering stability rather than only receiving).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Pair Sync:** Player and one NPC establish co-regulated rhythm. Baseline for relational somatic capacity. Measures: phase-lock accuracy, mutual adaptation rate.
**Mechanic 2 — Group Rhythm:** 3–5 NPCs each with distinct rhythms. Player must find the emergent group rhythm without forcing their own or losing themselves. Measures: contribution to coherence vs. conformity.
**Mechanic 3 — Role Rotation:** Player alternates between leading (offering rhythm for others to join), following (joining another's rhythm), and witnessing (holding space without active participation). Each role tests different drive-balance.
**Mechanic 4 — Disruption Recovery:** Group rhythm is disrupted (one NPC desynchronises). Can the player help re-establish coherence without over-functioning? Measures Agape (grounding) vs. DA (absorbing the disruption).
**Progression:** Pair → triad → group → role-rotation → disruption-recovery → multi-group navigation.

## 4. Item Pool

| Task type | Group configuration | Shadow surfaced |
|---|---|---|
| Pair sync | 1 NPC, simple rhythm | DAll (won't join) |
| Triad emergence | 2 NPCs, find shared rhythm | DA (over-adapts) vs. DAll (dominates) |
| Group hold | 4 NPCs, sustain coherence | DA (dissolves) vs. GAll (withdraws at scale) |
| Lead/follow/witness | Role-switching within group | Agency/Communion balance |
| Disruption repair | 1 NPC breaks sync | DA (absorbs break) vs. Agape (grounds) |
| Edge-group | Unfamiliar NPC rhythms | GAll (avoids) vs. GA (performs joining) |

Minimum pool: 36 cooperative sequences across 6 types, 3 group-size tiers. Each sequence is 90–180 seconds.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Rhythm-tapping synchronised with multiple NPC rhythms, role-selection (lead/follow/witness), timing-based contribution.
**Measurement:** Individual-group coherence ratio, contribution index (how much player adds to vs. takes from group sync), role-flexibility score, disruption-response pattern.
**Adaptive engine:** Group complexity scales with player's pair-sync mastery. NPC rhythms adapt to create appropriate challenge.
**Telemetry:** Coherence ratio, contribution index, role-distribution, dissolution frequency (DA), rigidity frequency (DAll), scale-avoidance (GAll).
**Scoring output:** Agency ← maintained individual rhythm + lead-role quality, Communion ← group coherence contribution + follow-role quality, Eros ← engagement with unfamiliar groups, Agape ← witness-role quality + disruption-grounding.
**No LLM dependency.** All group dynamics are rhythm-based with parameterised NPC behaviours.
