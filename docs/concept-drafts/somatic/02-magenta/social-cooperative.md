# Somatic / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes somatic intelligence through SHARED BODY-EXPERIENCE — can the player engage body-awareness WITH others? At Magenta, shared somatic experience is RHYTHMIC and PROXIMAL: moving together, breathing together, sharing body-space. The first communal body.
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Magenta:** The body at Magenta is not yet isolated (Red) or disciplined (Amber) — it exists in a field of shared feeling-tone. This modality tests whether somatic intelligence extends to relational space: shared rhythm, coordinated movement, body-proximity awareness.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-Circle"
- **Core mechanic:** The player engages in shared somatic activity with a companion — moving in rhythm together, holding postures together, sensing each other's body-states, and coordinating body-actions. Somatic intelligence in relational space.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Shared Beat → The Matched Hold → The Body-Sense-Other → The Coordinated Move → The Living Circle

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Your companion moves. Can you move with them? Can you feel their rhythm? Can you hold together?" The contact boundary is: "Can your body be WITH another body?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player's body relate to another? The Body-Magician demands the other match their rhythm. The Disembodied can't sense the other's body. The Premature Athlete competes. The Sensation-Refuser withdraws from body-proximity.
- *Emergent:* Can they synchronise? Can they sense the other? Can they coordinate without losing themselves?

**Integration path:** Rewards SHARED SOMATIC ENGAGEMENT — synchronising with another while maintaining own body-sense. Not domination, not absence, not competition, not withdrawal.

**Successful integration:** The player moves with a companion — synchronised, aware of both bodies, coordinated without fusion or isolation.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Leading shared movement (setting the rhythm for both).
- Healthy: Leads with awareness of companion's capacity; adjusts
- Pathological: Dominates rhythm; forces companion to match; no adjustment
- **Detection:** Whether leading serves coordination or domination

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Initiating shared body-activity.
- Healthy: Invites companion into shared movement; starts the rhythm
- Pathological: Cannot initiate shared body-activity; waits passively
- **Detection:** Initiation rate of shared somatic activity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Following companion's rhythm (matching their movement).
- Healthy: Follows while maintaining own body-sense; synchronises
- Pathological: Loses own body-sense when following; total fusion
- **Detection:** Whether following enhances or dissolves own body-awareness

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Mutual body-sensing (feeling companion's state).
- Healthy: Senses companion's body-state while maintaining own
- Pathological: Cannot sense companion; body-awareness is private only
- **Detection:** Quality of other-body-sensing

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar shared rhythms (well-practiced together).
- Healthy: Maintains engagement in familiar shared movement
- Pathological: Disengages from familiar shared activity
- **Detection:** Engagement quality on familiar shared somatic tasks

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel shared body-experiences (new companion, new rhythm).
- Healthy: Engages with curiosity; explores new shared movement
- Pathological: Retreats to solo activity in novel shared contexts
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in novel shared somatic contexts

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining shared somatic engagement over time.
- Healthy: Shared body-activity remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Shared engagement degrades; companion forgotten
- **Detection:** Consistency of shared somatic engagement

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether shared body-awareness appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Coordinates with companion naturally in free-play
- Pathological: Shared somatic activity exists only in structured tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous shared body-engagement

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Body-Magician"
**How it surfaces:** DEMANDS companion match their body-magic. "Move like ME. Feel MY power." Shared body-space becomes domination — the companion must submit to the player's somatic ritual.
- **Detection signals:** Forces own rhythm on companion; distress when companion moves differently; shared space = player's space; "Do it MY way"; cannot adapt to companion's rhythm

### Dark-Allergy: "The Disembodied"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot sense companion's body at all. Moves independently regardless of companion. No synchronisation, no shared rhythm, no body-proximity awareness.
- **Detection signals:** Zero synchronisation with companion; independent movement regardless of partner; no response to companion's rhythm; treats shared space as solo space

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Athlete"
**How it surfaces:** COMPETES with companion rather than coordinating. "I can move faster/harder/longer." Shared body-space becomes arena rather than circle.
- **Detection signals:** Competitive behaviour in shared movement; tries to outperform companion; escalates intensity; treats coordination as competition; "I'm better at this"

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sensation-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** WITHDRAWS from body-proximity. Companion's physical presence is threatening. Cannot share body-space. The other body is too close, too real, too felt.
- **Detection signals:** Physical withdrawal from companion; increased tension in proximity; cannot maintain posture near another; shared body-space produces anxiety; approach-withdrawal with companion's body

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Companion sets rhythm; player must FOLLOW (not lead)
- Shared activity requires MATCHING not DOMINATING
- Frame: "Their rhythm. Match it. Not yours this time. Theirs."
- Reward synchronisation accuracy (matching companion, not forcing)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Companion provides OBVIOUS rhythm cues (strong beat, visible movement)
- Make synchronisation produce TANGIBLE shared benefit
- Frame: "They're moving. Can you feel their beat? Just match. One beat."
- Reward ANY synchronisation; celebrate first matched movement

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Remove competition framing; require IDENTICAL movement (not better, same)
- Reward MATCHING over EXCEEDING; make competition produce worse scores
- Frame: "Not faster. Not harder. THE SAME. Match them exactly."
- Build: coordination-as-skill (not competition-as-default)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Increase distance; reduce proximity demand: "Far apart. Same rhythm. No touching."
- Make shared body-space SAFE through distance
- Frame: "You don't need to be close. Just move the same way. From over there."
- Build: shared somatic engagement at comfortable distance, gradually closer

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Body-Circle: a shared somatic space where player and companion move together. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage ritual-circle — two body-outlines moving in rhythm, glowing when synchronised, dimming when disconnected. The first communal body-experience.

### Interaction
- **The Shared Beat (1-5):** Tap in rhythm with companion. Simple synchronisation.
- **The Matched Hold (5-15):** Hold the same posture simultaneously. Shared stability.
- **The Body-Sense-Other (15-30):** Sense companion's body-state. "Are they tense or relaxed?"
- **The Coordinated Move (30-50):** Move together — transitions synchronised.
- **The Living Circle (50+):** Full shared somatic engagement — rhythm, hold, sense, coordinate.

### Feedback
- Synchronised → "Together. Your bodies speak the same language. The circle glows."
- Dominating → "You're leading too hard. Let them move. Match, don't force."
- Disconnected → "They're moving. Can you feel it? Just one beat together."
- Competing → "Not faster. Together. The same speed. The same force."
- Withdrawing → "They're gentle. Safe. Just one movement together. From wherever you are."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Synchronisation precision: generous (±500ms) → moderate (±200ms) → tight (±100ms)
- Shared task complexity: single beat → hold → sense → coordinate
- Proximity demand: distant → moderate → close
- Companion variability: predictable → varied → responsive

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Shared Beat | 1-5 | Simple rhythm synchronisation |
| The Matched Hold | 5-15 | Simultaneous posture holding |
| The Body-Sense-Other | 15-30 | Sensing companion's body-state |
| The Coordinated Move | 30-50 | Synchronised transitions |
| The Living Circle | 50+ | Full shared somatic engagement |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Rhythm synchronisation items:** Shared beats at various tempos
- **Matched hold items:** Simultaneous postures at various durations
- **Body-sensing items:** Companion state-identification tasks
- **Coordinated movement items:** Synchronised transitions
- **Full circle items:** Combined rhythm + hold + sense + coordinate

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ rhythm, 20+ hold, 15+ sensing, 15+ coordinated, 10+ full

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Forces own rhythm → dark-addiction; zero synchronisation → dark-allergy
- Competes → golden-addiction; withdraws from proximity → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Synchronisation accuracy:** Timing match with companion (ms asynchrony)
- **Matched hold quality:** Simultaneous stability
- **Other-body-sensing accuracy:** Correct identification of companion's state
- **Coordination smoothness:** Quality of synchronised transitions
- **Shared engagement rate:** How naturally does shared body-activity occur?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Companion behaviour generation, social scenario construction. Core synchronisation/timing scoring algorithmic.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Timed tap (shared rhythm); sustained touch (matched hold); tap-to-select (body-sensing reports); coordinated input (synchronised transitions)

### Timing requirements
- Synchronisation at ±16ms measurement precision; hold matching continuous; coordination timing tracked

### NPC/AI requirements
- Companion NPC with body-behaviour (moves, holds, breathes, has states)
- Companion adapts to player's pace
- Companion has own body-states that player can sense
- Companion responds to player's synchronisation/domination/withdrawal

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Companion behaviour, social scenario generation. Core synchronisation scoring algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Synchronisation accuracy history; matched hold quality; other-body-sensing accuracy; coordination smoothness; engagement rates; domination indicators; disconnection indicators; competition indicators; withdrawal indicators; drive/shadow signals; companion relationship state; fatigue state; checkpoint position
