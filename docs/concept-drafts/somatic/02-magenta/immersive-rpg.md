# Somatic / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes somatic intelligence through SPONTANEOUS BODY-ENGAGEMENT in an open magical world — not structured posture-tasks but free somatic engagement in an environment that responds to the body. How does the player's body LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe body-awareness through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world is alive and the body is magical — the player navigates a world that RESPONDS to their body-state. Their spontaneous somatic engagement reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world that responds to the player's body-state — posture affects the environment, rhythm opens paths, body-awareness reveals hidden content. The player's somatic intelligence is assessed through the QUALITY of their spontaneous body-engagement with the world.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Body-Noticing → Body-Using → Body-Navigating → Body-Shaping → The Living Body-World

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world responds to the body. Nobody says "hold this posture" — but when the player holds stably, doors open. When they move rhythmically, paths appear. When they're body-aware, hidden content reveals itself. The world IS the somatic feedback.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL somatic style surfaces. Do they engage the body? Perform rituals? Ignore body-content? Push intensity? Their ecological body-behaviour reveals genuine somatic structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage more of the body-world? Hold longer? Move more rhythmically? Sense more deeply?

**Integration path:** The world rewards somatic intelligence NATURALLY. Players who inhabit their bodies find hidden paths. Players who move rhythmically unlock content. Players who sense their body-state gain power. The reward is WONDER and ACCESS (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates somatic intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — inhabiting, moving, sensing, and engaging the body-world without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world where the body is ALIVE and the world RESPONDS. Posture-doors that open to stable holds. Rhythm-paths that appear when the player moves in time. Body-awareness zones that reveal hidden content when the player is present. The world IS somatic feedback.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a world that responds to body-state
- **Spontaneous body-engagement:** Holding, moving, sensing without prompting
- **Natural rhythm:** Moving in time with the world's pulse
- **Body-awareness discovery:** Finding hidden content through presence
- **Shared body-activity:** Engaging companion in somatic exploration

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: body-intelligence → hidden paths, unlocked doors, wonder
- Natural consequences: body-absence → missed content, stuck paths, flat world
- No explicit "somatic score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Body-responsive content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Response sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated awareness
- Companion body-activity scales with demonstrated sharing

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Body-Noticing | 1-5 | Basic: does the player notice body-responsive content? |
| Body-Using | 5-15 | Can they use body-engagement to navigate? |
| Body-Navigating | 15-30 | Do they navigate the world somatically? |
| Body-Shaping | 30-50 | Can they shape the world through body-presence? |
| The Living Body-World | 50+ | Full ecological somatic intelligence |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Posture-doors:** Objects requiring stable holds to activate
- **Rhythm-paths:** Routes requiring rhythmic movement to reveal
- **Awareness-zones:** Areas requiring body-presence to access hidden content
- **Body-state-responsive objects:** Things that respond to player's somatic state
- **Shared body-spaces:** Areas requiring coordinated body-engagement

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ posture-doors, 15+ rhythm-paths, 15+ awareness-zones, 15+ responsive objects, 10+ shared spaces

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Ritual at every object → dark-addiction; zero body-engagement → dark-allergy
- Maximum intensity → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Sustained touch (posture-engagement); rhythmic input (rhythm-paths); presence (awareness-zones); navigation (exploration)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, body-interaction rates, presence quality)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of somatic patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Body-responsive environment (objects that respond to posture, rhythm, presence)
- Companion with own body-behaviour (models engagement)
- Body-gating system (paths requiring somatic engagement)
- Awareness-revelation system (hidden content appearing with body-presence)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, body-responsive dynamics
- Detects spontaneous body-engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates somatic intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive body-content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; body-engagement history; exploration patterns; awareness-depth records; engagement quality; rhythm-path completion; posture-door history; drive/shadow signals; companion relationship; fatigue state (CRITICAL); checkpoint position
