# Somatic / Magenta — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes somatic intelligence through BODY-INFORMED DECISIONS — can the player use body-signals to guide choices? At Magenta, the body "knows" before the mind: gut-feelings, body-pulls, somatic preferences. The question is whether the player can USE body-information to choose.
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Magenta:** At Magenta, the body is the primary decision-maker — "I feel pulled toward X." This modality tests whether body-signals inform choice, which is the somatic-marker hypothesis at its most primitive: does the body guide action?

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-Chooser"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces choices where body-signals provide information. "Which path feels right in your body?" The game measures whether the player can detect and follow somatic markers — body-pulls, gut-feelings, tension/ease signals.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Body-Pull → The Gut-Signal → The Tension-Choice → The Ease-Path → The Wise Body

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Two paths. Your body knows which one. Can you feel the pull? The tension? The ease? Let your body choose." The contact boundary is: "Can your body guide your choices?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player choose? The Body-Magician chooses by "magical body-power" (not actual somatic signal). The Disembodied chooses randomly (no body-input). The Premature Athlete chooses by intensity (not signal). The Sensation-Refuser won't let the body choose (body-signals are threatening).
- *Emergent:* Can they detect genuine somatic markers? Can they follow body-guidance?

**Integration path:** Rewards BODY-INFORMED choice — choosing based on actual somatic signals (tension = warning, ease = alignment, pull = attraction). Not magical body-power, not random, not intensity-based.

**Successful integration:** The player detects somatic markers and uses them to guide choices — body-informed decision-making.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Body-Chooser: a choice-space where the body provides guidance. Two or more paths; the player's body-signals indicate which is aligned. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage crossroads where the body KNOWS — paths that feel warm or cool, pulls toward or away, ease or tension.

### Interaction
- **The Body-Pull (1-5):** Two paths; one produces body-pull (vibration/warmth). Follow the pull.
- **The Gut-Signal (5-15):** Subtler signals; must detect before choosing. Gut-feeling as guide.
- **The Tension-Choice (15-30):** One path produces tension (warning); avoid it. Tension as negative signal.
- **The Ease-Path (30-50):** Multiple paths; find the one that produces ease. Ease as positive signal.
- **The Wise Body (50+):** Full somatic-marker integration — pull, gut, tension, ease, all informing choice.

### Feedback
- Body-informed correct choice → "Your body knew. You listened. The path opens."
- Wish-disguised-as-signal → "That wasn't your body talking. That was your wanting. Listen deeper."
- No signal detected → "Your body spoke. You didn't hear. Slower. Quieter. Listen."
- Intensity-following → "Not the loud signal. The quiet one. What's underneath the excitement?"
- Signal-detected-but-overridden → "You felt it. You ignored it. Next time, trust."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Signal strength: obvious (vibration) → moderate (warmth) → subtle (slight pull)
- Signal type: single → multiple → conflicting
- Choice complexity: binary → ternary → multiple
- Desire-signal conflict: aligned → neutral → conflicting

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Body-Pull | 1-5 | Obvious body-signal; follow the pull |
| The Gut-Signal | 5-15 | Subtler signals; detection required |
| The Tension-Choice | 15-30 | Negative signals; avoid tension |
| The Ease-Path | 30-50 | Multiple paths; find ease |
| The Wise Body | 50+ | Full somatic-marker integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Pull items:** Choices with obvious body-pull toward correct option
- **Gut items:** Choices requiring subtle signal detection
- **Tension items:** Choices where tension signals danger/wrong path
- **Ease items:** Choices where ease signals alignment/right path
- **Conflict items:** Choices where desire and body-signal disagree

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ pull, 20+ gut, 15+ tension, 15+ ease, 10+ conflict

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Wish-as-signal → dark-addiction; no signal detection → dark-allergy
- Intensity-following → golden-addiction; signal-override → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (choose path); body-signal report (verbal or tap-to-select sensation); hold (sense before choosing)

### Timing requirements
- Pre-choice sensing period tracked; decision latency measured; no punishing time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Path environments that produce body-signals (through game mechanics: vibration, colour-warmth, audio cues)
- Optional: companion who shares body-signal reports

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, signal-report evaluation, desire-vs-signal analysis. Core accuracy algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Signal detection rates; signal-following rates; accuracy scores; subtlety thresholds; desire-signal discrimination; wish-as-signal indicators; no-signal indicators; intensity-following indicators; signal-override indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
