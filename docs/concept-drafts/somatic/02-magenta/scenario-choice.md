# Somatic / Magenta — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes somatic intelligence through BODY-INFORMED DECISIONS — can the player use body-signals to guide choices? At Magenta, the body "knows" before the mind: gut-feelings, body-pulls, somatic preferences. The question is whether the player can USE body-information to choose.
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Magenta:** At Magenta, the body is the primary decision-maker — "I feel pulled toward X." This modality tests whether body-signals inform choice, which is the somatic-marker hypothesis at its most primitive: does the body guide action?

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-Chooser"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces choices where body-signals provide information. "Which path feels right in your body?" The game measures whether the player can detect and follow somatic markers — body-pulls, gut-feelings, tension/ease signals.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Body-Pull → The Gut-Signal → The Tension-Choice → The Ease-Path → The Wise Body

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Two paths. Your body knows which one. Can you feel the pull? The tension? The ease? Let your body choose." The contact boundary is: "Can your body guide your choices?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player choose? The Body-Magician chooses by "magical body-power" (not actual somatic signal). The Disembodied chooses randomly (no body-input). The Premature Athlete chooses by intensity (not signal). The Sensation-Refuser won't let the body choose (body-signals are threatening).
- *Emergent:* Can they detect genuine somatic markers? Can they follow body-guidance?

**Integration path:** Rewards BODY-INFORMED choice — choosing based on actual somatic signals (tension = warning, ease = alignment, pull = attraction). Not magical body-power, not random, not intensity-based.

**Successful integration:** The player detects somatic markers and uses them to guide choices — body-informed decision-making.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choices where body-signal conflicts with obvious/easy option.
- Healthy: Follows body-signal even when it's the harder path
- Pathological: Overrides body-signal with force; "I don't care what my body says"
- **Detection:** Whether body-signals are respected or overridden

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Self-directed body-informed choice (no prompt about which signal to follow).
- Healthy: Identifies body-signal independently and follows it
- Pathological: Cannot detect body-signal without being told what to feel
- **Detection:** Self-directed somatic-marker detection rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choices where body-signal conflicts with companion's preference.
- Healthy: Holds own body-signal while acknowledging companion's choice
- Pathological: Abandons body-signal to match companion; "My body must be wrong"
- **Detection:** Whether own somatic markers survive social pressure

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared body-informed choice (both player and companion sensing together).
- Healthy: Coordinates body-signals with companion; shared somatic sensing
- Pathological: Cannot sense with others; body-signals are private only
- **Detection:** Quality of shared somatic-marker engagement

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar body-choice situations.
- Healthy: Maintains body-informed choosing on familiar scenarios
- Pathological: Stops sensing; chooses by habit instead of body-signal
- **Detection:** Whether body-signals are still consulted on familiar choices

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel body-choice situations.
- Healthy: Senses body-signals in novel contexts with curiosity
- Pathological: Claims body-certainty in novel situations (premature confidence)
- **Detection:** Appropriate uncertainty vs. false body-confidence

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining body-informed choosing over time.
- Healthy: Body-informed choice remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Body-signal detection degrades; choices become random
- **Detection:** Somatic-marker consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether body-informed choosing appears in other contexts.
- Healthy: Uses body-signals to guide choices in non-somatic tasks
- Pathological: Body-informed choosing exists only in explicit somatic tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of somatic-marker use to other contexts

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Body-Magician"
**How it surfaces:** Claims body-signals that are actually WISHES. "My body says I should have the treasure." Body-magic disguised as somatic sensing. The "signal" always points toward what they want.
- **Detection signals:** Body-signals always align with desire; no unpleasant body-signals reported; "My body says" = "I want"; somatic markers never produce unwanted guidance

### Dark-Allergy: "The Disembodied"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot detect body-signals. Chooses randomly or cognitively. "I don't feel anything in my body about this." No somatic-marker engagement.
- **Detection signals:** Random choice patterns; no body-signal reports; cognitive-only decision-making; "I just picked one"; no somatic engagement in choice

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Athlete"
**How it surfaces:** Chooses by INTENSITY not SIGNAL. "I feel the ENERGY pulling me!" Confuses arousal/excitement with somatic guidance. Always chooses the more intense option.
- **Detection signals:** Always chooses high-intensity option; confuses excitement with guidance; "I feel the energy!" without actual signal-detection; intensity-bias in all choices

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sensation-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** DETECTS body-signals but REFUSES to follow them. "I felt something but I'll just think about it instead." Body-signals are threatening; following them means trusting the body.
- **Detection signals:** Reports body-signals but doesn't follow them; overrides somatic markers with cognitive reasoning; "I felt it but..."; body-signal detection without body-signal trust

---

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present choices where body-signal points AWAY from desire: "Your body says 'not this one.' Even though you want it."
- Introduce UNPLEASANT body-signals as valid guidance (tension = warning)
- Frame: "Your body doesn't always say what you want to hear. Listen anyway."
- Reward following body-signals that conflict with desire

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present OBVIOUS body-signals: "One path is warm. One is cold. Which feels warmer?"
- Make body-signals TANGIBLE through game mechanics (vibration, temperature cues)
- Frame: "Your body IS feeling something. Maybe small. Just notice. Warm? Cool?"
- Reward ANY body-signal detection; celebrate first somatic-marker report

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Present choices where the QUIET signal is correct (not the intense one)
- Reward SUBTLE body-signal detection over intensity-following
- Frame: "Not the loud one. The quiet one. What's the GENTLE signal saying?"
- Build: subtle somatic-marker detection (not just arousal-following)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make body-signal-following LOW-STAKES: "Follow the feeling. Nothing bad happens either way."
- Present body-trust as SAFE: "Your body won't lead you wrong here. Try trusting it."
- Frame: "Just this once. Follow what your body says. See what happens."
- Reward body-signal-following; build somatic trust gradually

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Body-Chooser: a choice-space where the body provides guidance. Two or more paths; the player's body-signals indicate which is aligned. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage crossroads where the body KNOWS — paths that feel warm or cool, pulls toward or away, ease or tension.

### Interaction
- **The Body-Pull (1-5):** Two paths; one produces body-pull (vibration/warmth). Follow the pull.
- **The Gut-Signal (5-15):** Subtler signals; must detect before choosing. Gut-feeling as guide.
- **The Tension-Choice (15-30):** One path produces tension (warning); avoid it. Tension as negative signal.
- **The Ease-Path (30-50):** Multiple paths; find the one that produces ease. Ease as positive signal.
- **The Wise Body (50+):** Full somatic-marker integration — pull, gut, tension, ease, all informing choice.

### Feedback
- Body-informed correct choice → "Your body knew. You listened. The path opens."
- Wish-disguised-as-signal → "That wasn't your body talking. That was your wanting. Listen deeper."
- No signal detected → "Your body spoke. You didn't hear. Slower. Quieter. Listen."
- Intensity-following → "Not the loud signal. The quiet one. What's underneath the excitement?"
- Signal-detected-but-overridden → "You felt it. You ignored it. Next time, trust."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Signal strength: obvious (vibration) → moderate (warmth) → subtle (slight pull)
- Signal type: single → multiple → conflicting
- Choice complexity: binary → ternary → multiple
- Desire-signal conflict: aligned → neutral → conflicting

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Body-Pull | 1-5 | Obvious body-signal; follow the pull |
| The Gut-Signal | 5-15 | Subtler signals; detection required |
| The Tension-Choice | 15-30 | Negative signals; avoid tension |
| The Ease-Path | 30-50 | Multiple paths; find ease |
| The Wise Body | 50+ | Full somatic-marker integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Pull items:** Choices with obvious body-pull toward correct option
- **Gut items:** Choices requiring subtle signal detection
- **Tension items:** Choices where tension signals danger/wrong path
- **Ease items:** Choices where ease signals alignment/right path
- **Conflict items:** Choices where desire and body-signal disagree

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ pull, 20+ gut, 15+ tension, 15+ ease, 10+ conflict

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Wish-as-signal → dark-addiction; no signal detection → dark-allergy
- Intensity-following → golden-addiction; signal-override → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Signal detection rate:** Can the player report body-signals before choosing?
- **Signal-following rate:** Does the player follow detected signals?
- **Signal accuracy:** Do reported signals correlate with correct choices?
- **Subtlety threshold:** How subtle a signal can the player detect?
- **Desire-signal discrimination:** Can they distinguish wanting from sensing?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, signal-report evaluation, desire-vs-signal discrimination. Core choice-accuracy scoring algorithmic.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (choose path); body-signal report (verbal or tap-to-select sensation); hold (sense before choosing)

### Timing requirements
- Pre-choice sensing period tracked; decision latency measured; no punishing time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Path environments that produce body-signals (through game mechanics: vibration, colour-warmth, audio cues)
- Optional: companion who shares body-signal reports

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, signal-report evaluation, desire-vs-signal analysis. Core accuracy algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Signal detection rates; signal-following rates; accuracy scores; subtlety thresholds; desire-signal discrimination; wish-as-signal indicators; no-signal indicators; intensity-following indicators; signal-override indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
