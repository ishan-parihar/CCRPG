# Somatic / Magenta — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The embodied-somatic axis probes somatic intelligence THROUGH THE BODY ITSELF — the most direct modality for this line. At Magenta, this is body-as-magical-vessel: posture holds, breath awareness, felt-sense exploration, body-boundary discovery. The body experiencing itself.
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Magenta:** This is the HOME modality for the somatic line — maximum affinity. The body probing itself. At Magenta, the body is magical and alive; this game lets the player INHABIT that magic directly through physical engagement.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-Vessel"
- **Core mechanic:** Direct somatic engagement — the player holds postures, breathes with awareness, explores body-boundaries, and discovers felt-sense. The body as the instrument AND the subject. Pure inhabitation.
- **Duration:** 2-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; body-fatigue especially monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The First Hold → The Breath → The Boundary-Feel → The Inner Landscape → The Living Vessel

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Be in your body. Hold this shape. Breathe. Feel where you end and the world begins." The contact boundary is: "Can you INHABIT your body?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player inhabit? The Body-Magician performs elaborate body-rituals. The Disembodied can't stay present in the body. The Premature Athlete pushes intensity. The Sensation-Refuser's body freezes or withdraws.
- *Emergent:* Can they hold gently? Breathe with awareness? Feel boundaries? Discover inner landscape?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE INHABITATION — stable, gentle, aware presence in the body. Not ritual performance, not avoidance, not intensity, not fear. Just being HERE in this body.

**Successful integration:** The player inhabits their body with gentle awareness — holds stably, breathes consciously, senses boundaries, and discovers inner felt-sense.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Holding posture independently for increasing duration.
- Healthy: Holds by own will; stable, gentle, present
- Pathological: Holds as power-display; excessive tension; body as weapon
- **Detection:** Whether holding serves inhabitation or domination

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Self-initiated body-exploration (choosing what to sense).
- Healthy: Explores body independently; discovers new sensations
- Pathological: Cannot explore without instruction; waits to be told what to feel
- **Detection:** Self-initiated somatic exploration rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Shared body-space (holding posture near another; breathing together).
- Healthy: Maintains own body-presence while sharing space
- Pathological: Loses body-boundary in shared space; merges
- **Detection:** Whether shared space enhances or dissolves own body-sense

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Receiving body-guidance (being shown a posture; breathing with a guide).
- Healthy: Integrates guidance while maintaining own body-sense
- Pathological: Cannot receive guidance; body must be entirely self-directed
- **Detection:** Quality of guided body-engagement

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar body-states (well-known postures, familiar breath patterns).
- Healthy: Maintains presence in familiar body-territory
- Pathological: Disengages from familiar body-states; seeks novelty
- **Detection:** Presence quality in familiar somatic territory

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel body-experiences (new postures, unfamiliar sensations).
- Healthy: Approaches with curiosity; explores gently
- Pathological: Pushes into intense novel experiences prematurely
- **Detection:** Whether novel body-experiences produce gentle exploration or intensity-seeking

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining body-inhabitation over time.
- Healthy: Body-presence remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Body-inhabitation degrades; presence fades
- **Detection:** Inhabitation consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether body-inhabitation appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Body-presence transfers to non-somatic tasks
- Pathological: Body-inhabitation exists only during explicit somatic tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous body-presence in ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Body-Magician"
**How it surfaces:** Body-engagement becomes RITUAL PERFORMANCE. Elaborate postures, dramatic breathing, ceremonial movements. The body is a magical instrument being PERFORMED rather than INHABITED. Lots of body-activity, little body-awareness.
- **Detection signals:** Ritual additions to simple holds; dramatic breathing patterns; ceremonial movements; high body-activity + low body-awareness accuracy; body as performance not presence

### Dark-Allergy: "The Disembodied"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot sustain body-presence. Attention drifts away from body within seconds. Postures collapse not from fatigue but from ABSENCE. The player leaves the body mentally.
- **Detection signals:** Rapid attention-drift from body; posture collapse without fatigue; inability to sustain body-focus; "I forgot I was holding"; mental absence during body-tasks

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Athlete"
**How it surfaces:** Pushes body-engagement to MAXIMUM INTENSITY. Cannot hold gently — only forcefully. Cannot breathe slowly — only deeply. Seeks the most intense body-experience rather than the most AWARE one.
- **Detection signals:** Maximum force on all holds; deep/fast breathing instead of gentle; seeks intensity; cannot do "less"; treats gentle engagement as weakness; force ≠ awareness

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sensation-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Body FREEZES or WITHDRAWS when awareness is directed inward. The player can hold postures mechanically but when asked to FEEL the hold, the body tenses or releases. Inner awareness is threatening.
- **Detection signals:** Mechanical holding without felt-sense; tension increase when awareness directed inward; release/withdrawal when asked to feel; can perform but cannot inhabit; inner-directed awareness produces anxiety

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Strip all ritual; present BARE MINIMUM body-engagement: "Just hold. Nothing else. No ceremony."
- Reward STILLNESS over activity; SIMPLICITY over elaboration
- Frame: "Your body doesn't need to perform. It just needs to BE. Hold still. That's enough."
- Build: body-as-presence (not body-as-performance)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present ULTRA-BRIEF body-presence: "3 seconds. Just notice your body. Then you're done."
- Make body-presence produce IMMEDIATE tangible result
- Frame: "Just 3 seconds. Your body is here. Notice it. Done."
- Reward ANY sustained body-presence; celebrate first 3-second hold with awareness

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- REQUIRE gentleness; block intensity: "Softer. Lighter. Half the force. Quarter."
- Reward AWARENESS over INTENSITY; make gentle engagement score higher
- Frame: "Less force. More feeling. Can you hold with almost no effort? THAT'S mastery."
- Build: gentle inhabitation as the real skill (not intensity)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Direct awareness to SAFE body-zones first (hands, feet — not core/chest)
- Make inner awareness BRIEF and GENTLE: "Just your fingertips. 2 seconds. What do they feel?"
- Frame: "Start at the edges. Your fingertips. Safe. Small. Just notice."
- Build: gradual inner-awareness tolerance from periphery to core

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Body-Vessel: a direct somatic engagement space where the player inhabits their body. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage body-temple — a glowing body-outline that responds to the player's presence. When they're truly present, it glows brighter. When they drift, it dims.

### Interaction
- **The First Hold (1-5):** Hold a simple posture. Stability measured. Just be in the body.
- **The Breath (5-15):** Breathe with awareness. Breath-pace measured. Gentle, conscious breathing.
- **The Boundary-Feel (15-30):** Sense where the body ends. Proprioceptive boundary exploration.
- **The Inner Landscape (30-50):** Explore internal sensations. What's happening inside?
- **The Living Vessel (50+):** Full inhabitation — hold, breathe, sense boundaries, explore inner landscape, all with gentle awareness.

### Feedback
- Genuine presence → Body-outline glows warmly. "You're here. Your body feels you."
- Ritual performance → "Too much. Simpler. Just be here. No ceremony needed."
- Absence/drift → "You left. Come back. Your body is still here. Return to it."
- Intensity → "Softer. You're pushing. Let go of the force. Just... be."
- Freeze/withdrawal → "It's safe. Just your hands. Can you feel your hands? Start there."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Hold duration: 3s → 5s → 8s → 12s → 20s
- Breath awareness: notice breath → pace breath → breath-body connection
- Boundary precision: gross (whole body) → moderate (limbs) → fine (digits)
- Inner landscape depth: surface (skin) → moderate (muscle) → deep (organs/core)

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Hold | 1-5 | Simple stable hold with presence |
| The Breath | 5-15 | Conscious breathing; gentle awareness |
| The Boundary-Feel | 15-30 | Proprioceptive boundary sensing |
| The Inner Landscape | 30-50 | Internal sensation exploration |
| The Living Vessel | 50+ | Full gentle inhabitation |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Hold postures:** Various positions with increasing duration/awareness demands
- **Breath patterns:** Various paces and awareness levels
- **Boundary explorations:** Body-zone sensing at various precisions
- **Inner landscape items:** Internal sensation discovery at various depths
- **Integration items:** Combined hold + breath + boundary + inner tasks

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ holds, 20+ breath, 15+ boundary, 15+ inner, 10+ integration

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Ritual performance → dark-addiction; attention-drift → dark-allergy
- Excessive force → golden-addiction; freeze/withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Hold stability with awareness:** Stable hold + evidence of presence (not mechanical)
- **Breath awareness quality:** Conscious breathing (not automatic, not forced)
- **Boundary accuracy:** Correct proprioceptive sensing
- **Inner landscape access:** Can they report internal sensations accurately?
- **Gentleness:** Force level appropriate (not excessive, not absent)

### LLM rubric
- **Low-Medium:** Core hold/breath scoring algorithmic. Inner landscape and awareness-quality evaluation requires some LLM interpretation of verbal reports.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Sustained touch (hold); rhythmic input (breath-pacing); tap-to-select (boundary/inner-landscape reports)

### Timing requirements
- Hold stability at ≥30Hz; breath-pace at ≥10Hz; no time pressure on awareness reports

### NPC/AI requirements
- Body-outline visualization that responds to presence/absence
- Breath-guide (visual/audio pacing)
- Optional: body-companion who models gentle inhabitation

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Awareness-quality evaluation from verbal reports; inner-landscape interpretation. Core hold/breath metrics algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Hold stability + awareness scores; breath quality; boundary accuracy; inner-landscape access depth; gentleness metrics; ritual indicators; drift indicators; force indicators; freeze indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state (CRITICAL for somatic); checkpoint position
