# Somatic / Red — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes somatic capacity through SPONTANEOUS PHYSICAL BEHAVIOUR in an open game world — not structured body-tasks but free physical interaction with an environment that demands embodiment. This modality accesses the ECOLOGICAL dimension of body intelligence: not "can you pass a body-test" but "how does your body LIVE when nobody's testing it?"
>
> **Why this axis for Somatic/Red:** The other 6 modalities probe body intelligence through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Red, the player navigates a physical world: climbing, fighting, dodging, resting, moving through space. Their SPONTANEOUS physical behaviour — how they move, how they rest, how they engage threats, how they inhabit their body moment-to-moment — reveals their genuine somatic structure without the "test" framing.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Flesh Dominion"
- **Core mechanic:** An open-world Red-stage environment where the player freely moves, fights, climbs, rests, and navigates — with somatic capacity assessed through the QUALITY of their spontaneous physical behaviour rather than through explicit body-tasks.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Territory Movement → Physical Challenges → Body-State Management → Sustained Embodiment → The Body's World

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The game world itself IS the catalyst. It presents physical demands implicitly: terrain requires movement quality, threats require reaction, distances require stamina management, rest-points require stillness capacity. The player is never told "this is a body test" — they're just MOVING through the world.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL physical style surfaces without test-anxiety. How do they actually move when they're just playing? Do they rush (dark-addiction)? Float disconnected (dark-allergy)? Move with rigid precision (golden-addiction)? Refuse physical challenges (golden-allergy)?
- *Emergent:* The pull toward deeper embodiment — can they be more present in their movement? More powerful? More alive?

**Integration path:** The world rewards embodied engagement NATURALLY. Players who move with presence navigate better. Players who rest wisely fight stronger. Players who attune to the world's rhythms find hidden paths. The reward is POWER (Red-resonant) — not "good body score."

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates somatic capacity SPONTANEOUSLY — moving with presence without being asked, resting without being told, attuning to physical rhythms without being prompted. The body is inhabited as a way-of-being-in-the-world.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** The world offers rest-points, healing stations, and recovery opportunities. The player can use them or ignore them.
- Healthy: Uses rest strategically; maintains body-sovereignty; knows when to push and when to recover
- Pathological: Ignores all rest; pushes through every obstacle; "My body doesn't need rest"; physical self-abuse as pride
- **Detection:** Rest-point usage patterns; whether the player ever voluntarily stops; body-state at engagement points

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Uncharted physical terrain — areas requiring novel movement (climbing, swimming, balancing) with no instruction.
- Healthy: Explores novel physical terrain with confidence; body adapts to new demands
- Pathological: Cannot enter novel physical terrain; stays on known paths; needs instruction before new movement
- **Detection:** Exploration of physically novel areas; willingness to attempt unfamiliar movement; quality of physical adaptation

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** NPC allies who move through the world alongside the player. Physical proximity and shared movement.
- Healthy: Moves comfortably alongside others; physical presence in shared space; natural attunement
- Pathological: Physical withdrawal from shared space; moves away from allies; cannot be physically present with others
- **Detection:** Physical proximity patterns with NPCs; movement quality in shared space; spontaneous attunement

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Cooperative physical challenges in the world (lifting together, coordinated climbing, shared rhythm-gates).
- Healthy: Engages cooperative physical challenges naturally; body coordinates with others spontaneously
- Pathological: Avoids cooperative physical challenges; attempts solo solutions to group-body problems
- **Detection:** Engagement with cooperative physical opportunities; quality of spontaneous coordination

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** The world has areas of varying physical difficulty. The player can stay in easy terrain or seek challenging terrain.
- Healthy: Engages with physical difficulty appropriate to capacity; enjoys current terrain
- Pathological: Compulsively seeks the most physically demanding areas; cannot rest in any terrain; always pushing harder
- **Detection:** Terrain-seeking patterns; satisfaction in current areas; compulsive difficulty-seeking vs. purposeful

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** World areas that require physical capacity BEYOND current demonstration (locked by body-gates).
- Healthy: Approaches body-gates with curiosity; attempts and learns from failure
- Pathological: Claims to have passed body-gates without demonstration; performs mastery of areas they haven't genuinely engaged
- **Detection:** Engagement quality with beyond-level physical content; honesty about physical limits

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Previously traversed terrain that the player returns through (familiar physical space).
- Healthy: Moves through familiar terrain with full presence; doesn't dismiss known paths
- Pathological: Rushes through familiar terrain; "I already did this"; careless movement in known space
- **Detection:** Movement quality in familiar terrain; whether known paths receive full physical presence

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether physical presence demonstrated in challenges appears in free-movement (walking, standing, waiting).
- Healthy: The body is inhabited even in non-challenge moments; presence persists in stillness and simple movement
- Pathological: Physical presence exists only during challenges; between challenges, the body is abandoned
- **Detection:** Movement quality in non-challenge moments; presence during transitions; body-inhabitation in "boring" moments

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Compulsive Mover"
**How it surfaces:** Observe the player's behaviour at a REST POINT (safe area, no threats, healing available). The compulsive mover cannot stop — they pace, tap, move through the rest area without resting, or leave immediately.
- **Detection signals:** Rest-point avoidance; movement during safe periods; inability to use healing stations; rushing through recovery areas; continuous input even when no challenge is present

### Dark-Allergy: "The Disembodied"
**How it surfaces:** Observe the player's movement QUALITY in free navigation (no threats, just moving through space). The disembodied player moves with flat, mechanical, disconnected quality — the body is a cursor, not a presence.
- **Detection signals:** Flat movement quality (no force variation, no rhythm); mechanical navigation; no physical engagement with the world beyond minimum required; body as cursor rather than presence

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Disciplinarian"
**How it surfaces:** Observe the player's response to a POWER challenge (break through a barrier, overpower an obstacle). The premature disciplinarian approaches with technique rather than force — precise but weak.
- **Detection signals:** Technique-first approach to power challenges; precise but weak engagement; inability to "just hit it hard"; controlled movement when raw force is clearly optimal

### Golden-Allergy: "The Structure-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Observe the player's response to RHYTHM-GATED areas (doors that open to a beat, paths that require timed movement). The structure-refuser fights the rhythm rather than joining it.
- **Detection signals:** Rhythm-gate failure from resistance (not incapacity); fighting environmental rhythms; choosing harder non-rhythmic paths over easier rhythmic ones; rebellion against world-structure

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces "power-rest zones" — areas where STILLNESS accumulates power (visible buff building)
- Rest-points become NECESSARY (stamina doesn't regenerate while moving)
- Frames rest as physical mastery: "The predator who rests between hunts is the one who eats"
- Creates situations where rested-then-moved outperforms constant-movement

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces "presence-responsive" terrain — areas that RESPOND to embodied movement (paths that open to powerful steps, walls that yield to present touch)
- Makes physical presence NECESSARY for navigation (flat/mechanical movement doesn't trigger world-responses)
- Starts with minimal presence-demand (a single powerful tap opens a door)
- Gradually increases: more of the world responds to embodiment quality

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces "force-gated" obstacles — barriers that yield ONLY to raw power (not technique)
- Makes power the currency: the strongest strike opens the best paths
- NPCs model effective raw physicality: "Don't finesse it. HIT it."
- Technique-approaches to force-gates fail visibly

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces rhythm as REWARD (rhythmic paths lead to better loot, not just different paths)
- Environmental rhythms become increasingly attractive (beautiful, powerful, rewarding)
- Frames rhythm as natural: "The world has a heartbeat. Move with it and it carries you."
- Never punishes anti-rhythm — only rewards with-rhythm

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Red-stage physical world: fortress walls to climb, chasms to cross, enemies to fight, terrain to navigate, rest-points to use (or ignore). The player moves freely. No explicit objectives — the world presents physical opportunities and challenges. The player's BODY-BEHAVIOUR is the assessment.

### Interaction
- **Navigation:** Move through varied terrain. Climbing, jumping, balancing, swimming. Movement quality assessed.
- **Combat:** Encounter physical threats. Reaction, rhythm, power, endurance. All somatic capacities tested in context.
- **Rest management:** Use (or ignore) rest-points. Stamina management. Recovery behaviour.
- **Exploration:** Discover new physical terrain. Novel movement challenges. Body-adaptation.
- **Environmental interaction:** Rhythm-gates, force-barriers, presence-responsive elements. The world demands embodiment.

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: embodied movement → world responds (opens, yields, resonates)
- Natural consequences: disconnected movement → world is inert (doors stay closed, paths stay hidden)
- No explicit "body score" — the world IS the feedback
- Power-resonant rewards for physical engagement (territory gained, enemies defeated, paths opened)

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Terrain complexity scales with demonstrated movement quality
- Combat difficulty scales with demonstrated reaction/rhythm capacity
- Environmental demands scale with demonstrated embodiment quality
- Rest-point spacing scales with demonstrated stamina management
- World responsiveness scales with demonstrated presence

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Territory Movement | 1-5 | Simple terrain, few threats, generous rest-points, basic navigation |
| Physical Challenges | 5-15 | Combat encounters, climbing challenges, stamina demands |
| Body-State Management | 15-30 | Rest-point strategy, fatigue management, recovery timing |
| Sustained Embodiment | 30-50 | Presence-responsive world, embodiment quality matters for navigation |
| The Body's World | 50+ | Full physical world, all somatic capacities tested ecologically |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Terrain configurations:** Physical environments with varied movement demands
- **Combat encounters:** Enemies with varied physical challenge profiles
- **Environmental elements:** Rhythm-gates, force-barriers, presence-responsive objects
- **Rest-point configurations:** Recovery opportunities with varied accessibility
- **Exploration rewards:** Hidden areas accessible only through specific physical qualities

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ terrain configurations (climbing, balancing, jumping, swimming variants)
- 30+ combat encounter types (reaction, rhythm, power, endurance variants)
- 15+ environmental element types (rhythm-gates, force-barriers, presence-doors)
- 10+ rest-point configurations (varied spacing, accessibility, recovery rate)
- 15+ exploration rewards (hidden paths, secret areas, body-gate treasures)

### Generation rules
- Terrain generated from modular physical components (walls, gaps, slopes, water)
- Combat encounters scaled to demonstrated somatic capacity
- Environmental elements matched to player's current edge (presence-responsive elements at their threshold)
- Rest-points spaced based on demonstrated stamina management
- Exploration rewards placed to encourage physical growth (just beyond current capacity)

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Rest-point usage patterns → Agency probing / dark-addiction detection
- Novel terrain engagement → Agency golden probing
- Physical proximity to NPCs → Communion probing
- Terrain difficulty seeking → Eros probing
- Familiar terrain movement quality → Agape probing
- Movement during safe periods → dark-addiction signal
- Flat/mechanical navigation → dark-allergy signal
- Technique-first on power challenges → golden-addiction signal
- Rhythm-gate resistance → golden-allergy signal

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL — from spontaneous behaviour)
- **Movement quality:** Force variation, rhythm, presence in free navigation
- **Reaction quality:** Response to threats in context (not isolated RT tasks)
- **Stamina management:** Rest/push ratio relative to actual body-state
- **Physical adaptation:** Quality of movement in novel terrain
- **Embodiment persistence:** Presence quality in non-challenge moments

### Drive-health scoring
Per `scoring.md` §3: Derived from SPONTANEOUS physical behaviour — rest usage, terrain engagement, proximity patterns, familiar-terrain quality.

### Shadow-state scoring
Per `shadow-diagnostics.md` §3: Behavioural patterns in free-world context — rest avoidance, flat movement, technique-over-power, rhythm-resistance.

### Integration scoring
- Movement quality improving over sessions (more present, more varied, more alive)
- Rest management improving (wiser push/rest decisions)
- Physical adaptation improving (faster adjustment to novel terrain)
- Transfer FROM other modalities visible in world behaviour
- Embodiment deepening (presence persisting in more contexts)

### LLM rubric
- **High:** World generation, combat narration, environmental response, ecological assessment
- Evaluates spontaneous physical behaviour quality (not structured task performance)
- Generates world content adapted to player's demonstrated somatic capacity
- Maintains world consistency across sessions
- Narrates physical experiences in Red-stage body-language

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (interact with world objects, combat, environmental elements)
- Swipe (navigate, climb, dodge)
- Hold (sustain actions — climbing holds, posture, rest)
- Pressure-sensitive (force expression in combat and environmental interaction)
- Rhythm tap (rhythm-gates, combat timing)

### Timing requirements
- Combat timing (RT measurement in context)
- Rhythm-gate timing (synchronisation with environmental beats)
- No explicit timing for exploration/rest (player-paced)
- Fatigue tracking (session duration, input intensity, recovery patterns)

### NPC/AI requirements
- Ally NPCs with physical presence (move through world alongside player)
- Enemy NPCs with varied physical challenge profiles
- Environmental AI (world responds to embodiment quality)
- World must be CONSISTENT — same areas behave the same way

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, combat narration, environmental response
- Generates terrain and challenges adapted to demonstrated capacity
- Narrates physical experiences in embodied language
- Maintains world consistency across sessions
- Evaluates ecological somatic behaviour (interpreting spontaneous physical patterns)

### State persistence
- Full world state (terrain, enemies, environmental elements, rest-points)
- Player physical behaviour history (movement patterns, rest patterns, combat patterns)
- Ecological somatic assessment (running estimate from spontaneous behaviour)
- Drive-health signals from world engagement patterns
- Shadow signals from physical behaviour patterns
- Fatigue state (accumulated somatic load, recovery history)
- Checkpoint position (world state at each save point)
- Territory explored and physical challenges completed
