# Somatic / Red — Shadow Diagnostics

> **The Shadow Landscape:** The body at Red can be over-inhabited (compulsive physicality), rejected (dissociation), prematurely disciplined (form without fire), or refused growth (terror of structure). These four quadrants define the pathological relationship to the power-body — and each involves ALL four drives in their dark and golden domains.

---

## 1. The Four Shadow Archetypes

### 1.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Compulsive Mover"

**Core pattern:** The body cannot stop. Movement is used to avoid inner states — feelings, thoughts, stillness. The player reacts to EVERYTHING physically because not-reacting feels like death. The body is not a chosen instrument; it is a compulsion.

**Developmental origin:** Early environment where stillness was dangerous (hypervigilant household, physical threat requiring constant readiness) OR where physical activity was the ONLY source of positive feedback (athletic identity as sole self-worth).

**Drive dynamics:**
- **Agency dark:** Physical dominance as compulsion — must be fastest, strongest, most reactive. Cannot tolerate being physically outperformed.
- **Communion dark:** Cannot synchronise because cannot slow down. Over-powers any coordination attempt. Dominates physical space.
- **Eros dark:** Cannot rest in current capacity — always pushing for more speed, more power, more reaction. The body is never enough.
- **Agape dark:** Cannot return to simple physicality — basic movement is "boring." Dismisses foundational practices.

**Manifestation in gameplay:**
- Reacts to ALL stimuli (including irrelevant ones) — commission errors on Go/No-Go
- Cannot hold posture — fidgets, breaks hold early, adds unnecessary movement
- Rhythm is rushed — consistently ahead of the beat (anticipatory errors)
- Motor sequences are fast but sloppy — speed prioritised over accuracy
- Cannot tolerate rest phases between challenges

**Resolution criteria:** See `scoring.md` §4.

---

### 1.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Disembodied"

**Core pattern:** The body is rejected, numbed, or abandoned. The player lives "above the neck" — thinking, planning, analysing — while the body below is a forgotten vehicle. Physical engagement produces discomfort, disgust, or blankness.

**Developmental origin:** Early environment where the body was shamed (weight, appearance, clumsiness), traumatised (physical/sexual abuse creating dissociation), or irrelevant (intellectual household where only cognitive achievement mattered).

**Drive dynamics:**
- **Agency dark:** Cannot assert physically. The body that would set boundaries is absent. Boundaries exist only verbally/cognitively.
- **Communion dark:** Cannot be physically present with others. The body withdraws from shared space. Physical intimacy (even non-sexual) is threatening.
- **Eros dark:** Cannot reach toward physical development because the body is not acknowledged as self. Growth requires a body to grow.
- **Agape dark:** Cannot return to the body because it was never truly inhabited. There is no "foundation" to return to — only numbness.

**Manifestation in gameplay:**
- Slow reaction times (not from incapacity but from disengagement — the body doesn't "hear" the stimulus)
- Cannot hold posture — not from fidgeting but from forgetting the body exists (drift, collapse)
- Rhythm is absent or mechanical — no felt-sense of the beat, only cognitive counting
- Motor sequences are cognitively correct but physically flat — no force, no presence
- Avoids physical challenges; prefers cognitive/verbal modalities

**Resolution criteria:** See `scoring.md` §4.

---

### 1.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Disciplinarian"

**Core pattern:** The player imposes Amber-level form (technique, precision, "correct" movement) before Red-level power is established. They bypass raw physicality — the messy, forceful, alive body — for controlled, refined, "proper" movement. All form, no fire.

**Developmental origin:** Early environment where raw physicality was punished ("don't be rough," "be gentle," "control yourself") OR where premature exposure to disciplined movement (early ballet, strict martial arts) created form without the power-substrate.

**Drive dynamics:**
- **Agency golden:** Performs physical sovereignty through technique rather than power. Looks competent but lacks force.
- **Communion golden:** Performs physical attunement through mimicry of form rather than genuine felt-sense matching.
- **Eros golden:** Reaches toward refinement without having established raw capacity. Skips the "ugly" learning phase.
- **Agape golden:** Cannot embody power because power was never allowed. The foundation is form, not force.

**Manifestation in gameplay:**
- Reaction times are precise but WEAK — correct timing, minimal force expression
- Posture hold is rigid rather than alive — holds perfectly but without dynamic tension
- Rhythm is metronomic — technically accurate but without groove, swing, or body-feel
- Motor sequences are correct but lack power — the movement is "right" but doesn't LAND
- Prefers structured/formal challenges over free-form physical expression

**Resolution criteria:** See `scoring.md` §4.

---

### 1.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Structure-Refuser"

**Core pattern:** The player refuses any physical discipline, structure, or externally-imposed form. "Don't tell me how to move." They have Red-level power but actively resist the developmental pull toward Amber-level coordination. Freedom is confused with chaos.

**Developmental origin:** Early environment where physical discipline was abusive (punitive exercise, forced postures, militaristic physical control) OR where the player's natural movement was repeatedly "corrected" until structure became associated with oppression.

**Drive dynamics:**
- **Agency golden:** Refuses to develop physical capacity beyond current level because development requires accepting instruction.
- **Communion golden:** Cannot join structured group movement (dance, martial arts, coordinated exercise) because structure feels like submission.
- **Eros golden:** The pull toward discipline is experienced as threat. Growth = loss of freedom.
- **Agape golden:** Cannot embody structured movement because structure was never safe. The body rebels against form.

**Manifestation in gameplay:**
- Good raw capacity (fast RT, strong force) but REFUSES structured challenges
- Cannot follow external rhythm — generates own timing, ignores the beat
- Motor sequences are resisted — does them "wrong" deliberately or disengages
- Posture hold is abandoned early (not from inability but from refusal to be constrained)
- Performance collapses specifically when instruction/structure is introduced

**Resolution criteria:** See `scoring.md` §4.

## 2. Full Drive-Health Landscape

### 2.1 The 4×2 Matrix at Somatic/Red

| Drive | Dark-Domain (submergent) | Golden-Domain (emergent) |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Physical sovereignty without domination — can be powerful without needing to overpower | Physical initiative without dependence — can begin new movement without needing permission |
| **Communion** | Physical joining without self-loss — can synchronise without losing own centre | Physical empathy without isolation — can mirror and match without mechanical performance |
| **Eros** | Physical satisfaction without stagnation — can enjoy current capacity without compulsive advancement | Physical aspiration without bypass — can reach toward new capacity without skipping foundations |
| **Agape** | Physical honouring without regression — can return to basics without dismissing them | Physical embodiment without compartmentalisation — can transfer somatic awareness to all life contexts |

### 2.2 Healthy expression signatures

- **Agency healthy:** Chooses when to be fast and when to be still. Physical power is AVAILABLE but not compulsive.
- **Communion healthy:** Can move with others AND alone. Synchronisation is a choice, not a loss.
- **Eros healthy:** Enjoys the body's current capacity AND reaches toward more. Neither stuck nor rushing.
- **Agape healthy:** Honours basic physicality AND engages complexity. The foundation is loved, not dismissed.

## 3. Detection Signals

### 3.1 Behavioural signals (per-session)

| Signal | What it indicates | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Commission errors on Go/No-Go > 35% | Dark-addiction (over-reactivity) | 3+ sessions |
| Posture-hold fidget rate > 2/second | Dark-addiction (cannot be still) | 3+ sessions |
| RT > 1.5× population median consistently | Dark-allergy (disengagement) | 3+ sessions |
| Rhythm absent (no synchronisation attempt) | Dark-allergy (body-rejection) | 2+ sessions |
| High accuracy + low force expression | Golden-addiction (form without fire) | 5+ sessions |
| Metronomic rhythm (zero variance) | Golden-addiction (mechanical precision) | 3+ sessions |
| Performance collapse on structured tasks only | Golden-allergy (structure-refusal) | 3+ sessions |
| External rhythm rejection (own timing only) | Golden-allergy (instruction-refusal) | 3+ sessions |

### 3.2 Pattern signals (cross-session)

| Pattern | What it indicates | Detection window |
|---|---|---|
| Increasing RT on rest-phase tasks | Dark-addiction worsening (cannot tolerate stillness) | 5+ sessions |
| Decreasing engagement with physical modalities | Dark-allergy deepening | 5+ sessions |
| Increasing precision with decreasing force | Golden-addiction solidifying | 7+ sessions |
| Increasing avoidance of structured challenges | Golden-allergy solidifying | 5+ sessions |
| Physical modality preference > 80% of play time | Dark-addiction (body as escape) | 10+ sessions |
| Physical modality avoidance > 80% of play time | Dark-allergy (body as threat) | 10+ sessions |

### 3.3 Compound signals (cross-module)

| Compound pattern | Modules involved | What it indicates |
|---|---|---|
| High somatic + low cognitive engagement | Somatic/Red + Cognitive/Red | "Thinks with fists" — body used to avoid thinking |
| Low somatic + high cognitive engagement | Somatic/Red + Cognitive/Red | "Lives in head" — thinking used to avoid body |

## 4. Heal/Evolve Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency) — for dark shadows

**Dark-Addiction healing path:**
1. **Agape:** Return to the body's NEED for rest. Honour stillness as a physical state (not absence of movement).
2. **Agency:** Choose stillness sovereignly. "I CHOOSE to be still" — not forced, not punished, but chosen.
3. **Mechanism:** Games introduce rest-phases that are REWARDED. Stillness produces power (stamina recovery, buff accumulation). The body learns: rest IS power.
4. **Resolution:** The player can be still without distress AND move with purpose. Movement becomes chosen, not compulsive.

**Dark-Allergy healing path:**
1. **Agape:** Return to the body's basic aliveness. The simplest physical sensation — a tap, a hold, a breath.
2. **Agency:** Choose to inhabit the body. "I CHOOSE to feel this." Not forced into the body, but invited.
3. **Mechanism:** Games introduce minimal physical engagement with maximum reward. A single tap, perfectly timed, produces dramatic game-world effect. The body learns: I matter.
4. **Resolution:** The player engages physical tasks without avoidance AND reports body-state with basic accuracy.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion) — for golden shadows

**Golden-Addiction healing path:**
1. **Eros:** Reach toward POWER before form. The developmental sequence is: force → form → finesse. You cannot refine what doesn't exist.
2. **Communion:** Join with the raw, messy, alive body. Let the body be imprecise and POWERFUL rather than precise and weak.
3. **Mechanism:** Games reward FORCE over precision at early stages. A powerful-but-imprecise strike scores higher than a precise-but-weak one. The body learns: power first.
4. **Resolution:** The player can move with raw force AND accept imprecision. Form emerges FROM power, not instead of it.

**Golden-Allergy healing path:**
1. **Eros:** Reach toward structure as LIBERATION (not imprisonment). Discipline enables freedom — the martial artist is MORE free than the flailing novice.
2. **Communion:** Join with the rhythm, the form, the pattern. Let the external structure carry you rather than fighting it.
3. **Mechanism:** Games introduce structure as OPTIONAL and REWARDING. Following the beat produces power. Matching the form unlocks new abilities. Structure = more power, not less freedom.
4. **Resolution:** The player can follow external structure without rebellion AND maintain own physical identity within it.

## 5. Shadow Surfacing Mechanics

### 5.1 What conditions each game must create

Every Somatic/Red game must create conditions where:

1. **Stillness is possible** (to surface dark-addiction — can they stop?)
2. **Physical engagement is required** (to surface dark-allergy — will they inhabit the body?)
3. **Raw power is valued over precision** (to surface golden-addiction — can they be messy?)
4. **Structure is introduced** (to surface golden-allergy — can they accept form?)

### 5.2 The surfacing sequence within a session

| Phase | What surfaces | How |
|---|---|---|
| Opening (warm-up) | Dark-allergy | Does the player engage physically or resist? |
| Intensity phase | Dark-addiction | Does the player escalate compulsively or modulate? |
| Precision phase | Golden-addiction | Does the player impose form prematurely? |
| Structure phase | Golden-allergy | Does the player accept external rhythm/form? |
| Rest phase | Dark-addiction (again) | Can the player stop? |
