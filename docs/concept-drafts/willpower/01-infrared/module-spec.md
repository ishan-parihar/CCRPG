# Willpower × Infrared — Module Specification

## 1. Capacity Definition

Reflex-will: the pre-intentional capacity to mobilise the body toward a survival need. No goals, no plans, no deliberation. The organism detects a need (hunger, thirst, warmth, sleep) and the body moves toward satisfaction. Will at this stage IS mobilisation — the raw biological imperative translated into motor output. Healthy reflex-will mobilises when needed, rests when sated, and transitions fluidly between drive-states.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: Drive-Fixation

Cannot stop pursuing survival needs even when met. Compulsive seeking, hoarding, restless scanning for the next need. The body mobilises endlessly — satiation never registers. Safe environments feel dangerous. Rest is impossible.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: Drive-Collapse

Cannot mobilise toward needs. Apathy, learned helplessness, frozen inertia. The body won't move even when hungry, thirsty, cold. Needs are felt but no motor response follows. The will-to-move has been extinguished.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: Premature Wishing

Attempts magical-will (Magenta capacity) without genuine drive-mobilisation. "Wants" without moving toward. The organism skips the body's work — expecting satisfaction without effort. Desire without locomotion.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: Drive Fortress

Mobilisation is present and functional but refuses intentional emergence. Terrified of wanting beyond survival. The organism can move toward food but cannot allow the seed of a wish. Locks itself in pure reflex to avoid the vulnerability of desire.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

- **Eros (vertical-up):** Pull toward Magenta — the first glimmer of wanting-beyond-need. Healthy Eros allows reflex-will to occasionally reach toward novelty.
- **Agape (vertical-down):** Return to embodiment — honouring the body's actual state. Healthy Agape allows rest when sated.
- **Agency (horizontal-self):** Sovereign mobilisation — MY body moves toward MY need. Not dependent on external prompt.
- **Communion (horizontal-other):** Shared mobilisation — moving-with another presence. Co-regulation of drive-states.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Agape + Agency → Dark Shadow Integration

Sovereign return to the body dissolves Drive-Fixation (DA) by restoring satiation-detection. Dissolves Drive-Collapse (DAll) by re-establishing the body's authority to move.

### 4.2 Eros + Communion → Golden Shadow Dissolution

Relational reaching-beyond dissolves Premature Wishing (GA) by grounding desire in shared movement. Dissolves Drive Fortress (GAll) by making emergence safe through co-presence.

### 4.3 Integration Sequence

Dark shadows heal first (body must mobilise before it can wish). Golden shadows dissolve only after reflex-will is stable. The sequence: collapse → fixation → fortress → wishing.

## 6. Compound Shadows

### 6.1 The Compulsive Seeker (Willpower DA × Cognitive DA)

Drive-fixation fused with hypervigilant scanning. Cannot stop searching. The body mobilises AND the mind scans ceaselessly. No rest, no satiation, no stillness.

### 6.2 The Collapsed Will (Willpower DAll × Somatic DAll)

Drive-collapse fused with somatic freeze. Total immobility. Neither will nor body can initiate movement. The deepest form of learned helplessness.

### 6.3 The Wishing Body (Willpower GA × Emotional GA)

Premature wishing fused with premature affect. Wants without grounding. Feelings arise about desires that have no motor basis. Fantasy-longing without locomotion.

### 6.4 The Survival Machine (Willpower GAll × Somatic GAll)

Drive Fortress fused with somatic refusal of non-survival movement. Locked in drive-loop. The organism functions but only within the narrowest survival band. All emergence is blocked.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Detection Phase

Games present need-cues and measure: latency to mobilise, persistence after satiation, mobilisation-without-movement (wishing), and refusal-to-reach-beyond (fortress). Each game surfaces shadows through its unique modality.

### 7.2 Amplification Phase

Once detected, shadow patterns are gently amplified — Drive-Fixation encounters satiation-moments; Drive-Collapse encounters minimal-effort opportunities; Premature Wishing encounters movement-requirements; Drive Fortress encounters safe novelty.

### 7.3 Integration Phase

Catalyst becomes experience: the player's response to amplification IS the integration. No explicit feedback. The game simply offers the next appropriate catalyst based on the shadow profile.
