# Willpower / Green — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** Planning within plurality — structuring multiple commitments across time.  **Why this axis for this module:** Pluralistic will requires temporal architecture — the capacity to sequence, interleave, and sustain multiple goals across time without collapse or scatter.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Weave  
**Core loop:** Player constructs and maintains a commitment-schedule across a simulated multi-week timeline, balancing 4–7 ongoing projects with variable demands, deadlines, and interdependencies. Must plan, execute, revise, and sustain without over-committing or abandoning.  
**Session length:** 5–10 minutes per planning cycle.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each week-boundary is a natural save point.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst → Experience → Integration:**  
- *Catalyst:* New commitments arrive mid-plan; existing ones shift in urgency; collaborative projects require synchronisation with NPC schedules. The plan must be a living document.  
- *Experience:* Player feels the tension between structure and flexibility — the plan that is too rigid breaks, the plan that is too loose dissipates.  
- *Integration:* Successful planning demonstrates that plurality CAN be structured — that multiple commitments are sustainable when temporally architected with revision-capacity built in.

**Shadow surfacing:**  
- DA: Over-planning without execution (endless revision cycles), scheduling everything with no slack (burnout trajectory), inability to finalise a plan.  
- DAll: Planning only personal projects, refusing to integrate collaborative timelines, optimising for individual output.  
- GA: Leaving the plan "open to the universe" — minimal structure, claiming flexibility as virtue while achieving nothing.  
- GAll: Allocating exactly equal time to all projects regardless of urgency or stage, refusing to weight priorities.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**  
- Timeline grid: weeks × commitment-slots. Player drags commitment-blocks into slots, managing energy-budget per week.  
- Energy is finite per week; over-allocation triggers burnout (reduced capacity next week).  
- Collaborative blocks: require alignment with NPC availability windows (communion constraint).  
- Disruption events: mid-plan shifts that require revision — new urgent need, project delay, opportunity emergence.  
- Revision rounds: explicit moments to restructure the plan (revision-willingness is measured, not penalised).

**Difficulty staircase:**  
- Floor: 4 commitments, generous energy, few disruptions, long timelines.  
- Ceiling: 7 commitments, tight energy, frequent disruptions, collaborative dependencies, cascading deadlines.

**Drive probing:**  
- Agency: Quality of personal project planning (sovereign structure).  
- Communion: Integration of collaborative timelines (shared scheduling).  
- Eros: Forward-planning capacity (how far ahead the player structures).  
- Agape: Maintenance of existing commitments when new ones arrive (grounding).

## 4. Item Pool

| Planning Challenge | Mechanic | Shadow Targeted |
|---|---|---|
| Simultaneous deadlines | 3+ projects due same week | DA (paralysis in prioritisation) |
| Solo fast-track | Personal project completable in half-time if done alone | DAll (collaboration avoidance) |
| "Leave space" option | Empty slots framed as spiritual openness | GA (structurelessness as bypass) |
| Equal-urgency week | All projects at identical priority level | GAll (refusal to sequence) |
| Disruption cascade | Plan broken by external event; revision required | Revision-capacity (health) |
| Collaborative sync | Project requiring NPC schedule alignment | Communion (shared planning) |

**Adaptive generation:** Disruption frequency and type calibrated to detected shadow. If over-planning dominates, execution-pressure increases. If under-structuring dominates, consequence-feedback tightens.

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Metrics captured:** Plan-revision frequency, execution-vs-plan fidelity, energy-budget utilisation, collaborative block inclusion rate, slack-ratio (unallocated time), burnout-trigger frequency, prioritisation variance.  
- **Scoring:** Composite of plan-execution fidelity × sustainable energy use × collaborative integration × revision-efficiency.  
- **UI:** Drag-and-drop timeline grid; energy-budget visualisation; NPC availability overlay.  
- **State persistence:** Full plan history; revision diffs; multi-session continuity.  
- **Cross-line hooks:** Cognitive line (planning complexity), Willpower/Orange (strategic capacity baseline), Interpersonal line (collaborative scheduling quality).
