# Willpower / Green — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Objective commitment measurement under pluralistic pressure.  **Why this axis for this module:** Pluralistic will must be measurable — goal-holding latency, revision accuracy, and multi-goal management provide hard metrics that bypass self-report bias.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Confluence  
**Core loop:** Player manages 3–6 simultaneous commitment-streams (visualised as flowing channels), each with legitimate demands. Must allocate finite will-tokens across channels, revise allocations as contexts shift, and maintain minimum viability in each without over-committing to any.  
**Session length:** 3–8 minutes per checkpoint.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each allocation-round is a natural save point.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst → Experience → Integration:**  
- *Catalyst:* Competing demands arrive simultaneously; no single "correct" allocation exists. Pressure increases as channels multiply.  
- *Experience:* Player feels the tension of pluralistic commitment — the pull to scatter, the pull to collapse into one, the pull to abandon collaboration.  
- *Integration:* Successful rounds demonstrate that commitment CAN be sustained across plurality without paralysis or collapse.

**Shadow surfacing:**  
- DA: Measured by allocation-latency spikes (paralysis) and over-spreading (burnout pattern).  
- DAll: Measured by single-channel dominance (>70% tokens to one stream consistently).  
- GA: Measured by premature "release all" gestures (surrender without engagement).  
- GAll: Measured by refusal to prioritise when context demands it (equal allocation despite asymmetric urgency).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**  
- Will-tokens regenerate at fixed rate; total pool is always insufficient for maximum allocation to all channels.  
- Channels have variable urgency (pulsing intensity) — some demand immediate attention, others reward sustained low investment.  
- Revision windows: player can reallocate, but revision has a small cost (commitment-switching tax).  
- Collaborative channels appear mid-game — shared pools where allocation benefits multiple streams simultaneously.

**Difficulty staircase:**  
- Floor: 3 channels, generous tokens, slow urgency shifts.  
- Ceiling: 6 channels, scarce tokens, rapid context-switching, collaborative channels requiring trust.

**Drive probing:**  
- Agency: Does the player maintain sovereign allocation strategy or defer to channel-pressure?  
- Communion: Does the player engage collaborative channels or hoard tokens individually?  
- Eros: Does the player reach toward efficient prioritisation (integral hint)?  
- Agape: Does the player ground scattered attention into embodied commitment?

## 4. Item Pool

| Item Category | Examples | Shadow Targeted |
|---|---|---|
| Urgency spikes | Simultaneous high-demand across 3+ channels | DA (paralysis trigger) |
| Solo-reward channels | High-yield streams that penalise sharing | DAll (lone-achiever bait) |
| Surrender prompts | "Release all tokens" option with vague reward | GA (bypass trigger) |
| Flat-demand rounds | All channels equally urgent, prioritisation required | GAll (anti-hierarchy trigger) |
| Collaborative pools | Shared allocation that multiplies returns | Communion health |
| Revision windows | Opportunities to reallocate with minimal cost | Agape grounding |

**Adaptive generation:** Item frequency shifts based on detected shadow-signature. If DA is dominant, urgency spikes decrease and grounding items increase (healing). If DAll is dominant, collaborative pools become more rewarding.

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Metrics captured:** Allocation latency (ms), revision frequency, token distribution entropy, single-channel dominance ratio, collaborative engagement rate, surrender-gesture frequency.  
- **Scoring:** Composite of goal-maintenance (channels above minimum viability) × revision-efficiency (cost-adjusted) × collaborative engagement.  
- **Adaptive engine:** Difficulty and item-pool shift based on rolling 5-round performance window.  
- **State persistence:** Per-round snapshots; infinite checkpoint at each allocation cycle.  
- **Cross-line hooks:** Emotional line (empathic overwhelm amplifies DA), Interpersonal line (collaborative channel performance feeds social-cooperative data).
