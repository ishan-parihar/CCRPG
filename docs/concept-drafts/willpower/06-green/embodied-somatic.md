# Willpower / Green — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Body as site of pluralistic will — physical commitment, embodied goal-holding, somatic markers of over-commitment.  **Why this axis for this module:** Will lives in the body; pluralistic tension manifests as somatic overwhelm or dissociation — the body reveals what language conceals.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Held Ground  
**Core loop:** Player performs sustained physical gestures (touch-holds, pressure patterns, breath-gated commitments) representing multiple simultaneous goals. The body must literally hold multiple commitments — releasing one to attend another, maintaining baseline presence across all.  
**Session length:** 2–5 minutes per embodiment cycle.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each cycle completes independently.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst → Experience → Integration:**  
- *Catalyst:* Multiple hold-zones activate simultaneously; the body cannot maximally attend all. Physical tension mirrors pluralistic will-tension.  
- *Experience:* Player feels commitment-as-embodiment — the literal sensation of holding multiple things, the relief of release, the cost of over-holding.  
- *Integration:* The body learns that sustainable commitment has a rhythm — hold, release, return — not constant maximal grip.

**Shadow surfacing:**  
- DA: Rapid switching between zones without sustained hold (scattered attention), or freezing entirely (somatic paralysis).  
- DAll: Holding one zone with maximum intensity while ignoring others (somatic lone-achiever).  
- GA: Releasing all holds prematurely with a "relaxation" gesture (somatic bypass — surrender without engagement).  
- GAll: Maintaining identical low-intensity across all zones, refusing to increase pressure on any (somatic anti-prioritisation).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**  
- Touch-screen zones (3–5) represent active commitments; each requires sustained contact above a pressure threshold.  
- Zones pulse with variable demand — some need momentary full attention, others reward steady low contact.  
- Breath-gating: certain commitment-actions only register during specific breath phases (inhale = Eros/reach; exhale = Agape/ground).  
- Collaborative zones: two-finger holds that represent shared commitment (communion gesture).  
- Release-and-return: player must release zones to regenerate capacity, then return — modelling sustainable commitment rhythm.

**Difficulty staircase:**  
- Floor: 3 zones, generous thresholds, slow demand-shifts.  
- Ceiling: 5 zones, tight thresholds, rapid demand-pulses, breath-gated collaborative holds.

**Drive probing:**  
- Agency: Strength and consistency of individual zone-holds (sovereign commitment).  
- Communion: Engagement with collaborative two-finger zones (shared will).  
- Eros: Willingness to increase intensity when zones demand it (reaching toward challenge).  
- Agape: Quality of return-after-release (grounding back into commitment).

## 4. Item Pool

| Somatic Pattern | Mechanic | Shadow Targeted |
|---|---|---|
| Simultaneous demand spike | All zones pulse high simultaneously | DA (freeze/scatter) |
| Single high-reward zone | One zone offers 3× reward for exclusive hold | DAll (over-grip) |
| "Release all" invitation | Gentle vibration suggesting total release | GA (premature surrender) |
| Uniform demand | All zones at identical medium intensity | GAll (flat-hold refusal to prioritise) |
| Breath-synced collaborative | Two-zone hold gated to exhale phase | Communion + Agape |
| Intensity ramp | One zone slowly increases demand | Eros (reaching capacity) |

**Adaptive generation:** Zone-demand patterns shift based on somatic shadow-signature. If scatter-pattern dominates, grounding rhythms are introduced. If over-grip dominates, collaborative zones become more rewarding.

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Metrics captured:** Hold-duration per zone, pressure consistency, switch-frequency, simultaneous-hold count, breath-phase alignment, release-return latency, collaborative zone engagement.  
- **Scoring:** Composite of sustained-hold quality × distribution equity × collaborative engagement × breath-alignment.  
- **Input:** Multi-touch with pressure sensitivity (or duration-as-proxy on non-pressure screens).  
- **State persistence:** Per-cycle somatic profile; rolling pattern across sessions.  
- **Cross-line hooks:** Somatic line (direct overlap — shared metrics), Emotional line (somatic affect markers), Intrapersonal line (body-awareness indicators).
