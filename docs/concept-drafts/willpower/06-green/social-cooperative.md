# Willpower / Green — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Shared will — collaborative goal-setting, group commitment, maintaining individual will within collective.  **Why this axis for this module:** Pluralistic will is inherently relational — it only exists in the tension between sovereign agency and communal joining. This modality tests will-in-relationship directly.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Common Ground  
**Core loop:** Player participates in group commitment-building with 2–4 NPC collaborators. Must negotiate shared goals, maintain personal commitments within group process, and sustain collective will through disagreement, revision, and competing priorities.  
**Session length:** 5–8 minutes per collaborative cycle.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each negotiation round is a natural save point.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst → Experience → Integration:**  
- *Catalyst:* NPCs have genuine (simulated) goals that partially conflict with the player's. Collaboration requires real negotiation — not just agreement.  
- *Experience:* Player feels the tension of maintaining individual will within group commitment — the pull to dominate, the pull to dissolve, the pull to withdraw.  
- *Integration:* Successful collaboration demonstrates that individual will and collective will are not zero-sum — that genuine communion strengthens rather than weakens agency.

**Shadow surfacing:**  
- DA: Agreeing to everything NPCs propose (over-inclusion), unable to state own priorities within group.  
- DAll: Overriding NPC input, making unilateral decisions, treating collaboration as inefficiency to be minimised.  
- GA: Deferring all decisions to "the group wisdom" without contributing personal will ("whatever you all think is best").  
- GAll: Blocking any prioritisation within the group ("we can't say one person's goal matters more"), preventing collective action.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**  
- Negotiation rounds: player and NPCs each propose goals; must find shared commitment that honours all voices.  
- Commitment tokens: each participant has limited tokens to invest in shared projects. Distribution reveals will-structure.  
- Disagreement events: NPCs challenge player's proposals or each other's — player must navigate without dominating or dissolving.  
- Individual-within-collective: player maintains personal goals alongside group goals; both must progress.  
- Trust-building: collaborative success increases NPC trust, unlocking deeper shared commitments.

**Difficulty staircase:**  
- Floor: 2 NPCs with compatible goals, generous token pools, low conflict.  
- Ceiling: 4 NPCs with partially conflicting goals, scarce tokens, high disagreement, time pressure.

**Drive probing:**  
- Agency: Does the player state and maintain personal goals within group process?  
- Communion: Does the player genuinely engage with NPC goals or perform engagement?  
- Eros: Does the player reach toward synthesis that transcends individual positions?  
- Agape: Does the player honour prior group commitments when new proposals arrive?

## 4. Item Pool

| Social Challenge | Mechanic | Shadow Targeted |
|---|---|---|
| NPC conflict | Two NPCs disagree; player must facilitate without dominating | DA (paralysis) / DAll (override) |
| Solo shortcut | Player can achieve personal goal faster by withdrawing from group | DAll (collaboration exit) |
| Group deference trap | NPCs invite player to "just decide for us" | DAll (dominance bait) / GA (deference) |
| Equal-voice deadlock | All proposals equally valid; group needs a tiebreaker | GAll (anti-prioritisation) |
| Trust test | NPC asks player to sacrifice personal token for group benefit | Communion (genuine joining) |
| Revision negotiation | Group commitment needs updating; player must advocate AND listen | Agency + Communion balance |

**Adaptive generation:** NPC behaviour calibrated to detected shadow. If dominance pattern emerges, NPCs become more assertive. If dissolution pattern emerges, NPCs invite player's voice more explicitly.

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Metrics captured:** Proposal-to-acceptance ratio, token distribution (self vs. group), disagreement-navigation style, unilateral-decision rate, deference rate, trust-building trajectory, individual-goal maintenance within group.  
- **Scoring:** Composite of collaborative quality × individual-goal maintenance × trust-trajectory × negotiation-balance.  
- **NPC engine:** Simulated agents with distinct goal-profiles, trust-states, and negotiation styles.  
- **State persistence:** NPC relationship states; group commitment history; trust levels across sessions.  
- **Cross-line hooks:** Interpersonal line (direct overlap — relational quality), Emotional line (affect during disagreement), Moral line (fairness in negotiation).
