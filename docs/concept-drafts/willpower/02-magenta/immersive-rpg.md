# Willpower / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes willpower through SPONTANEOUS WISHING in an open magical world — not structured wish-tasks but free volitional engagement in an environment that responds to wishes. How does the player's will LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Willpower/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe wish-holding through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world is alive and wishes have power — the player navigates a world that RESPONDS to their wishes. Their spontaneous volitional engagement reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Wishing-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world where wishes have visible effects — declared wishes move objects, open paths, attract creatures. The player's spontaneous wish-behaviour is assessed through the QUALITY of their ecological volitional engagement. Do they wish? Do wishes persist? Do wishes guide action?
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Wish-Noticing → Wish-Using → Wish-Holding → Wish-Guiding → The Living Wish-World

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world responds to wishes. Nobody says "hold your wish" — but when the player wishes stably, things move. When they wish and step, paths open. When they wish and wait, treasures appear. The world IS the volitional feedback.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL wish-style surfaces. Do they wish compulsively? Not at all? Force without wishing? Wish without stepping? Their ecological volitional behaviour reveals genuine willpower structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they wish more stably? Hold longer? Step more? Guide more?

**Integration path:** The world rewards volitional intelligence NATURALLY. Players who wish stably find hidden content. Players who wish and step unlock paths. Players who wish and wait discover treasures. The reward is WONDER and MAGIC (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates volitional intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — wishing, holding, stepping, and guiding in the wish-world without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world where wishes have VISIBLE POWER. Wishing-wells that grant small magics. Intention-trees that grow when wished upon. Desire-paths that appear when the player wishes stably. The world IS volitional feedback — wishes make things happen.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a world that responds to wishes
- **Spontaneous wishing:** Declaring wishes at responsive objects
- **Wish-holding in context:** Maintaining wishes while exploring
- **Wish-guided navigation:** Using wishes to find paths
- **Shared wishing:** Engaging companion in wish-activity

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: stable wishing → hidden paths, magical effects, wonder
- Natural consequences: no wishing → missed content, flat world, stuck paths
- No explicit "willpower score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Wish-responsive content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Response sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity (stable wishes needed for deeper content)
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated wish-holding
- Companion wish-activity scales with demonstrated sharing

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Wish-Noticing | 1-5 | Basic: does the player notice wish-responsive content? |
| Wish-Using | 5-15 | Can they use wishes to navigate? |
| Wish-Holding | 15-30 | Do they hold wishes stably in free-play? |
| Wish-Guiding | 30-50 | Can wishes guide their exploration? |
| The Living Wish-World | 50+ | Full ecological volitional intelligence |

---

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Wishing-wells:** Objects requiring stable wishes to activate
- **Intention-trees:** Growing objects that respond to wish-persistence
- **Desire-paths:** Routes requiring wish-holding to reveal
- **Wish-creatures:** Beings attracted by stable wishes
- **Shared wish-spaces:** Areas requiring coordinated wishing

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ wells, 15+ trees, 15+ paths, 15+ creatures, 10+ shared spaces

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Compulsive scattering/demand → dark-addiction; zero wishing → dark-allergy
- Rigid/dutiful engagement → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (wish-declaration at objects); sustained touch (wish-holding); navigation (exploration); verbal (wish-naming)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (wish patterns, engagement rates, persistence quality)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of volitional patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Wish-responsive environment (objects that respond to wishes)
- Companion with own wish-behaviour (models engagement)
- Wish-gating system (paths requiring stable wishes)
- Wish-effect system (visible consequences of wishing)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, wish-responsive dynamics
- Detects spontaneous wish-engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates volitional intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive wish-content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; wish-engagement history; exploration patterns; wish-stability records; engagement quality; wish-path completion; wishing-well history; drive/shadow signals; companion relationship; fatigue state; checkpoint position
