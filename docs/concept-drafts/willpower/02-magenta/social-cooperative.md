# Willpower / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes willpower through SHARED WISHING — can the player wish WITH others? At Magenta, shared will is: wishing together, waiting together, taking a step together. The first communal intention.
>
> **Why this axis for Willpower/Magenta:** Before willpower becomes individual discipline (Red) or group duty (Amber), it exists as SHARED MAGICAL WISHING — "We wish together and the wish is stronger." This modality tests whether the player's wish-function extends to relational space.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Wish-Together"
- **Core mechanic:** The player and companion wish together — forming shared wishes, waiting together, taking steps together. Shared wishing as the relational seed of collective will. Can two wishes become one? Can waiting be shared?
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Shared Wish → The Waiting-Together → The Step-Together → The Wish-Gift → The Wishing Circle

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Your companion has a wish too. Can you wish together? Can you wait together? Can you step together?" The contact boundary is: "Can your will join with another's?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player's wish relate to another's? The Magical Demander demands the companion serve their wish. The Wish-Abandoner adopts the companion's wish (has none of their own). The Premature Disciplinarian imposes discipline on the companion. The Effort-Refuser won't join in shared effort.
- *Emergent:* Can they form a shared wish? Wait together? Step together? Maintain own wish while joining?

**Integration path:** Rewards SHARED WISHING — forming collective intention while maintaining individual desire. Not demanding, not abandoning self, not imposing, not withdrawing.

**Successful integration:** The player wishes with a companion — shared intention that enhances both individual wishes.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Wish-Together: a shared wishing space where player and companion form collective intention. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage wishing-circle — two wish-lights that can merge, dance together, or remain separate. When wishes align, they glow brighter together.

### Interaction
- **The Shared Wish (1-5):** Player and companion each name a wish. Find overlap. Form shared wish.
- **The Waiting-Together (5-15):** Hold the shared wish together across delay. Shared patience.
- **The Step-Together (15-30):** Take one step toward the shared wish together. Coordinated action.
- **The Wish-Gift (30-50):** Wish FOR the companion. "I wish for YOU to have..." Altruistic wishing.
- **The Wishing Circle (50+):** Full shared wishing: form → share → wait → step → gift.

### Feedback
- Shared wish formed → "Your wishes found each other! Together they glow brighter."
- Wish-imposition → "That's YOUR wish. What's THEIRS? Can both wishes live together?"
- No own wish → "They shared theirs. Now you. What do YOU wish? Even something small."
- Duty-imposition → "Not a plan. A wish. Together. What do you both DREAM?"
- Withdrawal → "You can wish nearby. Same direction. You don't have to merge. Just... together."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Wish-overlap: high (easy to find shared wish) → moderate → low (requires negotiation)
- Shared patience: brief → moderate → extended
- Coordinated action: simple → moderate → requires timing
- Altruistic wishing: prompted → self-initiated
- Companion variability: predictable → varied → challenging

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Shared Wish | 1-5 | Form shared wish from two individual wishes |
| The Waiting-Together | 5-15 | Hold shared wish across delay together |
| The Step-Together | 15-30 | Coordinated action toward shared wish |
| The Wish-Gift | 30-50 | Wishing FOR the companion |
| The Wishing Circle | 50+ | Full shared wishing integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Shared wish prompts:** Contexts for finding wish-overlap
- **Shared patience items:** Delays requiring mutual holding
- **Coordinated action items:** Steps requiring shared timing
- **Altruistic wish items:** Opportunities to wish for companion
- **Negotiation items:** Wishes that require finding common ground

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ shared prompts, 20+ patience, 15+ coordinated, 15+ altruistic, 10+ negotiation

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Wish-imposition → dark-addiction; no own wish/fusion → dark-allergy
- Duty-imposition → golden-addiction; withdrawal from shared → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (wish choice); verbal (wish-naming); timed tap (coordinated action); hold (shared patience)

### Timing requirements
- Coordination timing at ±200ms; shared patience duration tracked; no time pressure on wish-formation

### NPC/AI requirements
- Companion NPC with own wishes (that can overlap or differ)
- Companion adapts to player's sharing style
- Companion responds to imposition/fusion/withdrawal
- Shared wish visualization (two lights merging/dancing)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Companion wish-generation, shared wish evaluation, social scenario construction. Core coordination algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Shared wish-formation quality; own-wish maintenance; shared patience duration; coordination accuracy; altruistic wishing frequency; imposition indicators; fusion indicators; duty-imposition indicators; withdrawal indicators; drive/shadow signals; companion relationship state; fatigue state; checkpoint position
