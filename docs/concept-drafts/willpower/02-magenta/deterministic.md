# Willpower / Magenta — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis probes willpower through OBJECTIVE MEASUREMENT — can the player hold a wish, tolerate delay, exert minimal effort? At Magenta, this is the proto-marshmallow test: "Can you wait? Can you hold your wish? Can you do one small thing toward it?"
>
> **Why this axis for Willpower/Magenta:** Pure measurement of wish-holding capacity. No narrative, no language, no social context — just: how long can you hold a wish? How much delay can you tolerate? Can you take one step? The ground-truth for all other modalities.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Wish-Holder"
- **Core mechanic:** The player declares a wish (chooses a desired outcome) and must HOLD it — maintain intention across delay, distraction, and minimal effort-demands. Objective measurement of pre-volitional capacity.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; very short due to Magenta willpower fragility)
- **Internal progression:** The First Wish → The Waiting → The First Step → The Obstacle → The Held Wish

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Choose what you want. Now hold it. Wait. Take one step toward it." The contact boundary is: "Can your wish survive?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* What happens to the wish? The Magical Demander generates wishes compulsively but can't hold any. The Wish-Abandoner can't form one. The Premature Disciplinarian skips wishing and forces action. The Effort-Refuser wishes but won't step.
- *Emergent:* Can they hold the wish? Wait? Step?

**Integration path:** Rewards STABLE WISH-HOLDING — the wish persists through delay and minimal effort. Not compulsive wishing, not wish-absence, not forced effort, not effort-refusal.

**Successful integration:** The player holds a wish stably, tolerates delay, and takes one minimal step toward it.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Holding a wish independently across delay.
- Healthy: Holds wish by own will; patient, stable
- Pathological: Demands immediate fulfilment; tantrum at delay
- **Detection:** Response to delay (patience vs. demand)

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Forming a wish independently (choosing what to want).
- Healthy: Forms clear wish without prompting
- Pathological: Cannot form wish without being told what to want
- **Detection:** Self-initiated wish-formation rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Holding wish when companion has a different wish.
- Healthy: Holds own wish while acknowledging companion's
- Pathological: Abandons own wish to match companion; "I want what they want"
- **Detection:** Whether own wish survives social context

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared wishing (wishing together for a shared outcome).
- Healthy: Contributes to shared wish while maintaining own desire
- Pathological: Cannot participate in shared wishing; wishes are private only
- **Detection:** Quality of shared wish-engagement

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar wish-holding (same wish, repeated sessions).
- Healthy: Maintains wish-holding on familiar wishes
- Pathological: Loses interest; wish-holding degrades with familiarity
- **Detection:** Wish-holding consistency on repeated wishes

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel wishes (new desired outcomes).
- Healthy: Forms and holds novel wishes with curiosity
- Pathological: Generates novel wishes compulsively without holding any
- **Detection:** Whether novel wishes are held or just generated

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining wish-holding capacity over time.
- Healthy: Wish-holding remains stable across sessions
- Pathological: Wish-holding degrades; wishes become shorter-lived
- **Detection:** Wish-duration trend across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether wish-holding appears in other contexts.
- Healthy: Holds intentions in non-willpower tasks
- Pathological: Wish-holding exists only in explicit willpower tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of wish-holding to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Magical Demander"
**How it surfaces:** Generates wishes RAPIDLY but holds NONE. Each wish is immediately replaced by the next. Or: holds one wish with ESCALATING DEMAND — the wish becomes a tantrum.
- **Detection signals:** Rapid wish-switching (>3 wishes per minute); escalating demand signals; zero delay-tolerance; wish-duration <3 seconds; tantrum-pattern at delay

### Dark-Allergy: "The Wish-Abandoner"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot form a wish. When prompted to choose, responds with "I don't know" or chooses randomly without investment. Wish-duration = 0.
- **Detection signals:** No wish-formation; random/uninvested choice; immediate abandonment; "I don't care which one"; zero goal-memory after distraction

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Disciplinarian"
**How it surfaces:** Skips the wishing phase entirely. Immediately begins effortful action without declaring what they want. "Just tell me what to DO." Action without desire-foundation.
- **Detection signals:** Skips wish-declaration; immediate action; "What do I do?"; effort without stated goal; cannot articulate what they want; action-first, wish-never

### Golden-Allergy: "The Effort-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Forms a beautiful wish, holds it stably, but REFUSES the first effort-step. "I want it but I won't DO anything." The wish is precious; effort would contaminate it.
- **Detection signals:** Strong wish-formation; good wish-holding; zero effort-steps; approach-withdrawal at effort-point; "I want it but..."; wish without action

---

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Introduce SINGLE wish constraint: "One wish. Just one. Hold it."
- Reward wish-DURATION not wish-quantity
- Frame: "Your wish is precious. Hold it gently. Don't let it go."
- Build: stable wish-holding (one wish, held well)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present ULTRA-ATTRACTIVE options: "Look at these. Which one calls to you?"
- Make wishing SAFE: "You can change your mind. Just pick one for now."
- Frame: "It's safe to want something. Even something small."
- Reward ANY wish-formation; celebrate first "I want X"

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- REQUIRE wish-declaration before action: "What do you WANT? Say it first."
- Block action until wish is stated; make wish-first produce better results
- Frame: "Before you do, WISH. What do you want? Name it."
- Build: wish-before-effort sequence (desire grounds action)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make first effort-step TINY: "Just touch it. That's the whole step."
- Show that effort doesn't destroy the wish: "You stepped AND the wish is still there."
- Frame: "One tiny step. The wish stays. The magic stays. Just one step."
- Build: effort-tolerance (effort doesn't kill magic)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Wish-Holder: a wish-holding space where the player chooses a desired outcome and holds it across time. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage wishing-well — a glowing wish that the player holds in their hands. It pulses when held stably, dims when attention drifts, shatters when abandoned.

### Interaction
- **The First Wish (1-5):** Choose a wish. Hold it for 3 seconds. That's all.
- **The Waiting (5-15):** Hold the wish across a delay (5-10 seconds). Distractors appear.
- **The First Step (15-30):** Hold the wish AND take one minimal action toward it.
- **The Obstacle (30-50):** Hold the wish when the first obstacle appears. Does it survive?
- **The Held Wish (50+):** Full wish-holding: form → hold → wait → step → persist.

### Feedback
- Wish held stably → "Your wish glows. You're holding it. Beautiful."
- Wish abandoned → "The wish dimmed. It's still there. Can you find it again?"
- Compulsive switching → "Too many wishes. Pick ONE. Hold it. Just one."
- No wish formed → "Something here calls to you. What is it? Even something tiny."
- Effort refused → "Your wish is strong. Now just touch it. One tiny step."
- Effort forced → "Wait. What do you WANT? Wish first. Then step."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Wish-hold duration: 3s → 5s → 8s → 12s → 20s
- Delay before reward: 2s → 5s → 8s → 12s
- Distractor intensity: none → mild → moderate
- Effort-step size: touch → tap → hold → sequence
- Obstacle severity: none → mild → moderate

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Wish | 1-5 | Form and hold a wish (3s) |
| The Waiting | 5-15 | Hold across delay with distractors |
| The First Step | 15-30 | Hold + one minimal effort-step |
| The Obstacle | 30-50 | Hold through first frustration |
| The Held Wish | 50+ | Full wish-holding integration |

---

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Wish options:** Attractive outcomes to choose between (visual, tangible)
- **Delay intervals:** Various wait-durations with different distractors
- **Effort-steps:** Minimal actions of varying size
- **Obstacles:** Frustrations of varying severity
- **Distractor items:** Competing stimuli during wish-holding

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ wish options, 15+ delay intervals, 15+ effort-steps, 10+ obstacles, 15+ distractors

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Rapid switching/tantrum → dark-addiction; no wish/random choice → dark-allergy
- Action-without-wish → golden-addiction; wish-without-action → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Wish-hold duration:** Seconds the wish persists before abandonment/switching
- **Delay-tolerance:** Maximum delay tolerated without demand-escalation
- **Effort-step completion:** Whether minimal effort-steps are taken
- **Frustration-tolerance:** Whether wish survives first obstacle
- **Goal-memory:** Whether wish is recalled after distraction

### LLM rubric
- **Low:** Core scoring entirely algorithmic (duration, completion, recall). LLM generates contextual framing and wish-options only.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (wish choice); sustained touch (wish-holding); tap (effort-step); hold-through-distraction

### Timing requirements
- Wish-hold duration at ≥10Hz; delay measurement at ±100ms; effort-step timing tracked

### NPC/AI requirements
- Wish visualization (glowing object that responds to holding)
- Distractor system (competing stimuli during delay)
- Obstacle system (frustrations that test wish-persistence)
- Optional: companion who models wish-holding

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Wish-option generation; contextual feedback text. Core measurement entirely algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Wish-hold durations; delay-tolerance scores; effort-step completion rates; frustration-tolerance; goal-memory accuracy; wish-switching rate; demand-escalation indicators; wish-absence indicators; action-without-wish indicators; wish-without-action indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
