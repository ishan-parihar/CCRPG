# Willpower / Orange — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Goal dilemmas where achievement conflicts with rest, relationships, or meaning.  **Why this axis for this module:** Achievement-will is tested at its edges — where pushing forward costs something real, and where stopping requires genuine courage.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Crossroads  
**Core loop:** Player navigates branching scenarios where a long-arc goal conflicts with competing goods — rest, relationship, health, meaning-revision. Each choice reveals shadow orientation. No "correct" answer — the pattern across choices is diagnostic.  
**Session length:** 5–10 minutes (2–4 dilemmas per session).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each dilemma is self-contained.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Orange willpower — the tension between sustained pursuit and legitimate competing demands.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces when player ALWAYS chooses goal over rest/relationship (even when scenario makes rest clearly optimal). DAll surfaces when player ALWAYS abandons goal at first friction. GA surfaces when player frames abandonment as wisdom ("this goal no longer serves me") without genuine engagement. GAll surfaces when player cannot revise even when scenario presents clear evidence the goal is wrong.  
**Drive probing:** Eros via choices that reach toward harder goals. Agape via choices that honor embodied limits. Agency via choices that maintain self-authored direction. Communion via choices that hold goals in relational context.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Achievement vs. Rest Dilemmas:** "Your project deadline is tomorrow. You haven't slept in 36 hours. Push through or sleep?" Calibrated so optimal answer varies — sometimes push, sometimes rest. DA always pushes; healthy players read context.  
**Mechanic 2 — Achievement vs. Relationship Dilemmas:** "Your partner needs you tonight. Your goal requires tonight's work." No universally correct answer — pattern across many such dilemmas reveals shadow.  
**Mechanic 3 — Goal-Revision Dilemmas:** "New evidence suggests your 6-month goal is based on a false assumption. Revise, abandon, or persist?" GAll cannot revise. DAll abandons instantly. Healthy: evaluates evidence, decides proportionally.  
**Mechanic 4 — Surrender vs. Bypass Dilemmas:** "You've pursued this for 3 months with no progress. Let go or persist?" Distinguishes genuine discernment (healthy) from premature surrender (GA) and stubborn persistence (GAll).

## 4. Item Pool

| Item Type | Count | Adaptive Range | Shadow Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Achievement-vs-rest scenarios | 60+ | Mild tension → extreme | DA: always push; DAll: always rest |
| Achievement-vs-relationship | 50+ | Minor → life-defining | DA: always goal; healthy: contextual |
| Goal-revision triggers | 40+ | Weak → overwhelming evidence | GAll: never revise; DAll: instant abandon |
| Surrender-or-persist | 40+ | Early → late in pursuit | GA: premature quit; GAll: never quit |

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Scenario engine:** Branching narrative with consequence tracking. Choices affect subsequent scenario framing (not just scoring).  
**Adaptive difficulty:** Scenario ambiguity increases as player demonstrates capacity. Early scenarios have clearer "healthy" answers; later ones are genuinely ambiguous.  
**Scoring outputs:** Push/rest ratio, goal-revision willingness, relationship-integration index, surrender-timing distribution. All feed module-spec §5.  
**No LLM required:** Scenarios pre-authored with adaptive selection. LLM optional for consequence narration only.
