# Willpower / Orange — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Shared goals — collaborative achievement, supporting others' goals while maintaining own.  **Why this axis for this module:** Achievement-will at Orange must be tested in relational context — where others' goals intersect, compete with, or depend on yours.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Expedition  
**Core loop:** Players pursue individual long-arc goals within a shared environment where cooperation accelerates progress but requires goal-negotiation. Each player maintains their own goal while contributing to others'. The tension between self-pursuit and mutual support is the catalyst.  
**Session length:** 5–12 minutes (cooperative phases have natural endpoints).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — each cooperative exchange is complete in itself.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Orange willpower — achievement-will held in relational context without collapse or isolation.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as sacrificing all cooperative opportunities to maximize personal goal-progress (the Driven Isolate pattern). DAll surfaces as abandoning personal goals to serve others' goals exclusively (regression to Amber duty-will). GA surfaces as "we don't need individual goals" — dissolving personal pursuit into group flow. GAll surfaces as rigid personal goal-pursuit that cannot accommodate others even when mutual benefit is clear.  
**Drive probing:** Eros via ambitious shared-goal scoping. Agape via supporting others' progress. Agency via maintaining personal direction within group. Communion via genuine collaborative investment.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Parallel Goal Pursuit:** Each player has a personal multi-session goal. Progress is visible to partners. Cooperation opportunities arise naturally — helping another's goal costs time but yields relationship-capital and future reciprocity.  
**Mechanic 2 — Goal-Negotiation Events:** Moments where two players' goals conflict over shared resources. Must negotiate — pure competition (DA/GAll), pure capitulation (DAll), or creative integration (healthy). Negotiation style is diagnostic.  
**Mechanic 3 — Mutual Accountability:** Players can offer/request check-ins on each other's goals. Accepting accountability = communion. Refusing all accountability = isolation shadow. Over-relying on external accountability = DAll (cannot self-sustain).  
**Mechanic 4 — Shared Milestone Celebrations:** When either player hits a milestone, both can celebrate. DA players skip celebrations (waste of time). Healthy players integrate relational joy into achievement.

## 4. Item Pool

| Item Type | Count | Adaptive Range | Shadow Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperative opportunity events | 80+ | Low-cost → high-sacrifice | DA: refuses; DAll: over-gives |
| Goal-conflict scenarios | 50+ | Minor → fundamental tension | Negotiation style reveals shadow |
| Accountability structures | 30+ | Light check-in → deep partnership | Isolation vs. dependency |
| Celebration prompts | 40+ | Quick acknowledgment → full pause | DA: skips; healthy: integrates |

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Multiplayer architecture:** Asynchronous cooperative play. Partners matched by goal-type and developmental proximity. AI partners available when human partners unavailable.  
**Goal-visibility system:** Shared dashboard showing both players' progress. Privacy controls for sensitive goals.  
**Negotiation interface:** Structured negotiation for resource conflicts — offer/counter/accept/reject with reasoning. Patterns tracked across sessions.  
**Scoring outputs:** Cooperation ratio, goal-maintenance-under-social-pressure, negotiation style distribution, accountability utilization. Feed module-spec §5.  
**AI partner fallback:** When no human partner available, AI simulates cooperative dynamics with calibrated shadow-probing behavior.
