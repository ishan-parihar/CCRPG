# Willpower / Orange — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological achievement — whether rational goal-pursuit appears spontaneously in free-play.  **Why this axis for this module:** The truest measure of achievement-will is whether it emerges naturally when no external structure demands it — will as lived capacity, not performed behavior.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Frontier  
**Core loop:** An open-world sandbox where the player is given no explicit goals. Resources, opportunities, and long-arc projects exist in the environment. Whether the player self-authors goals, sustains them, rests appropriately, and revises adaptively — all emerge from free behavior. The world is the petri dish; the player's will-ecology is what grows.  
**Session length:** 8–20 minutes (open-ended, natural exit points via camp/rest mechanics).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Yes — world state persists continuously.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Orange willpower — self-authored achievement in an unstructured environment.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces as compulsive project-accumulation (starts everything, rests never, world becomes a productivity machine). DAll surfaces as aimless wandering with no self-initiated projects (waits for world to assign goals). GA surfaces as starting projects then abandoning them with "I'm just exploring" framing. GAll surfaces as single-project fixation — one goal pursued to exclusion of all else, world narrowed to a corridor.  
**Drive probing:** Eros via spontaneous ambition (what does the player reach toward?). Agape via voluntary rest/return (does the player ever stop?). Agency via goal-origination (self-authored vs. world-prompted). Communion via relational projects (builds for/with NPCs vs. solo accumulation).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Open World with Latent Projects:** The environment contains materials, NPCs with needs, mysteries, and buildable structures. None are assigned as quests. Player must notice, choose, and commit. What they choose (and don't) is diagnostic.  
**Mechanic 2 — Temporal Persistence:** Projects take multiple sessions to complete. Abandoned projects decay visibly. Completed projects yield lasting world-changes. The world reflects the player's will-history — a mirror of their achievement ecology.  
**Mechanic 3 — Rest Ecology:** The player character has energy/fatigue. Optimal play requires rest cycles. DA players push through fatigue (declining performance). The world rewards strategic rest (dawn bonuses, dream-insights, NPC interactions only available at rest).  
**Mechanic 4 — Revision Invitations:** Mid-project, the world offers new information that might warrant goal-revision (a better material discovered, an NPC reveals the project's unintended harm). GAll players ignore all signals. Healthy players evaluate and adapt.

## 4. Item Pool

| Item Type | Count | Adaptive Range | Shadow Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latent project seeds | 100+ | Simple → multi-session epic | What player chooses reveals ambition level |
| Rest opportunities | Continuous | Micro-rest → full sleep cycle | DA: ignores; healthy: integrates |
| Revision-trigger events | 60+ | Subtle hint → undeniable evidence | GAll: ignores; healthy: evaluates |
| NPC relational hooks | 50+ | Casual → deep partnership | DA: skips; communion-healthy: engages |

## 5. Technical Requirements

**World persistence:** Full state saved continuously. Project progress, decay, completion all tracked across sessions. World visually reflects player's will-history.  
**Behavioral telemetry:** Goal-initiation rate, goal-completion rate, rest-frequency, project-switching patterns, revision-response. All implicit — no explicit measurement visible to player.  
**NPC AI:** NPCs respond to player's will-ecology. Offer rest to DA players. Offer challenge to DAll players. Mirror the player's shadow back through narrative (never explicitly).  
**Scoring outputs:** Goal-origination rate, completion ratio, rest-integration index, revision-responsiveness, project-diversity. Feed module-spec §5.  
**Ecological validity:** This modality provides the highest ecological validity — behavior here is closest to real-world will-expression. Weighted accordingly in cross-validation (§7.3).
