# Willpower / Red — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes willpower through SPONTANEOUS VOLITIONAL BEHAVIOUR in an open game world — not structured will-tasks but free goal-setting and goal-pursuit in an environment that demands commitment. How does the player's will LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Willpower/Red:** The other 6 modalities probe will through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Red, the player navigates a world of opportunities and threats: do they set goals? Do they follow through? Do they rest? Do they persist? Their SPONTANEOUS volitional behaviour reveals their genuine will-structure.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Dominion of Vows"
- **Core mechanic:** An open-world Red-stage environment where the player freely sets goals, pursues them, rests, and navigates — with volitional capacity assessed through the QUALITY of their spontaneous goal-setting and goal-keeping rather than through explicit will-tasks.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Territory Claiming → Goal Pursuit → Vow Management → Sustained Campaigns → The Will's World

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world itself IS the catalyst. It presents opportunities that require commitment: territories to claim (requires holding), enemies to defeat (requires sustained effort), resources to gather (requires delayed gratification). The player is never told "this is a will test" — they're just LIVING in the world.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL volitional style surfaces without test-anxiety. Do they set goals spontaneously? Do they follow through? Do they rest wisely? Do they over-commit? Under-commit?
- *Emergent:* The pull toward deeper volitional engagement — can they commit to harder goals? Hold longer campaigns?

**Integration path:** The world rewards volitional engagement NATURALLY. Players who set and keep goals gain territory. Players who rest wisely fight stronger. Players who persist through difficulty unlock deeper content. The reward is POWER (Red-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates volitional capacity SPONTANEOUSLY — setting goals without being asked, persisting without being told, resting without being prompted. Will is a way-of-being-in-the-world.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** The world offers rest-points and recovery. The player can use them or ignore them.
- Healthy: Uses rest strategically; knows when to push and when to recover
- Pathological: Ignores all rest; pushes through everything; "My will doesn't need rest"
- **Detection:** Rest-point usage; whether the player ever voluntarily stops pursuing

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Uncharted territory — areas requiring novel commitment with no instruction.
- Healthy: Explores with confidence; sets own goals in novel territory
- Pathological: Cannot enter novel territory without a goal being assigned; stays on known paths
- **Detection:** Self-generated goal-setting in novel areas; willingness to commit without instruction

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** NPC allies pursuing their own goals alongside the player.
- Healthy: Coordinates naturally; supports allies' goals when appropriate
- Pathological: Ignores allies entirely; pursues only own goals; cannot join shared pursuits
- **Detection:** Whether player ever spontaneously supports ally goals

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Cooperative challenges in the world (gates requiring shared commitment).
- Healthy: Engages cooperative challenges naturally
- Pathological: Avoids cooperative challenges; attempts solo solutions to group problems
- **Detection:** Engagement with cooperative opportunities

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Areas of varying difficulty. Player can stay in easy territory or seek harder challenges.
- Healthy: Engages appropriate difficulty; enjoys current territory
- Pathological: Compulsively seeks hardest areas; cannot rest in any territory
- **Detection:** Difficulty-seeking patterns; satisfaction in current areas

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** World areas requiring commitment BEYOND current demonstration (locked by vow-gates).
- Healthy: Approaches vow-gates with curiosity; attempts and learns
- Pathological: Claims to have passed gates without demonstration
- **Detection:** Engagement quality with beyond-level content

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Previously claimed territory that the player returns through.
- Healthy: Maintains claimed territory with presence; doesn't dismiss known areas
- Pathological: Rushes through claimed territory; "I already did this"
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in familiar territory

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether volitional behaviour demonstrated in challenges appears in free-movement.
- Healthy: Goal-setting persists even in non-challenge moments
- Pathological: Will exists only during explicit challenges; between them, no goals
- **Detection:** Spontaneous goal-setting in non-challenge moments

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Compulsive Willer"
**How it surfaces:** Observe behaviour at a REST POINT (safe, no threats, recovery available). The compulsive willer cannot stop — they leave immediately, pursue the next goal, or pace restlessly.
- **Detection signals:** Rest-point avoidance; immediate re-engagement after any completion; no pause between goals; session length far exceeding optimal

### Dark-Allergy: "The Will-less"
**How it surfaces:** Observe whether the player sets ANY goals spontaneously. The will-less player wanders without direction — no territory claimed, no enemies engaged, no resources pursued.
- **Detection signals:** No spontaneous goal-setting; aimless wandering; reactive-only engagement (responds to threats but initiates nothing); "waiting for something to happen"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Disciplinarian"
**How it surfaces:** Observe goal-setting STYLE. The premature disciplinarian creates elaborate plans for the world (mental maps, systematic territory-clearing) but executes with low intensity — form without fire.
- **Detection signals:** Systematic but low-intensity engagement; planning visible but execution weak; "clearing the map" without genuine volitional force; organised but passionless

### Golden-Allergy: "The Discipline-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Observe response to LONG-TERM world goals (territory that requires sustained commitment over multiple visits). The refuser engages only burst-goals and avoids anything requiring return.
- **Detection signals:** Only burst-engagement; avoids multi-visit territories; "I'll do it all now or not at all"; cannot commit to goals requiring sustained campaign

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces "power-rest zones" — areas where resting accumulates visible power
- Rest-points become NECESSARY (willpower doesn't regenerate while pursuing)
- Frame: "The warlord who rests between conquests rules longer"

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces "micro-opportunities" — tiny goals that appear and reward immediately
- Makes goal-setting VISIBLE and EASY (glowing targets, obvious rewards)
- Frame: "See that? Take it. That's YOUR territory now."

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces "force-gated" territories — areas that yield ONLY to raw volitional intensity
- Makes intensity the currency; systematic approaches fail at force-gates
- Frame: "Don't plan it. TAKE it."

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces "campaign territories" — areas that reward EXPONENTIALLY for sustained commitment
- Multi-visit territories produce 10× the reward of single-visit ones
- Frame: "Return. Hold. Build. Your kingdom grows with every visit."

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Red-stage world: territories to claim, enemies to defeat, resources to gather, allies to coordinate with. The player moves freely. No explicit objectives — the world presents volitional opportunities. The player's GOAL-SETTING AND GOAL-KEEPING BEHAVIOUR is the assessment.

### Interaction
- **Territory claiming:** Commit to holding an area (sustained presence = ownership)
- **Enemy engagement:** Choose to fight (requires commitment to finish)
- **Resource gathering:** Delayed-gratification opportunities (wait for better, or take now?)
- **Rest management:** Use (or ignore) recovery opportunities
- **Campaign building:** Multi-session territory expansion (optional, rewarded)

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: commitment → territory gained, power accumulated
- Natural consequences: abandonment → territory lost, power stagnant
- No explicit "will score" — the world IS the feedback
- Power-resonant rewards for volitional engagement

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Opportunity complexity scales with demonstrated goal-completion rate
- Enemy difficulty scales with demonstrated persistence
- Territory requirements scale with demonstrated commitment duration
- Campaign length scales with demonstrated sustained engagement

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Territory Claiming | 1-5 | Simple claims, brief holds, obvious rewards |
| Goal Pursuit | 5-15 | Enemies, resources, delayed gratification |
| Vow Management | 15-30 | Multiple active goals, rest strategy, prioritisation |
| Sustained Campaigns | 30-50 | Multi-visit territories, long-term commitment |
| The Will's World | 50+ | Full volitional world, all capacities tested ecologically |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Territory configurations:** Areas requiring varied commitment levels
- **Enemy encounters:** Challenges requiring varied persistence
- **Resource opportunities:** Delayed-gratification scenarios
- **Rest-point configurations:** Recovery opportunities with varied accessibility
- **Campaign territories:** Multi-visit areas with escalating rewards

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ territory configs, 30+ enemy encounters, 15+ resource opportunities, 10+ rest configs, 15+ campaign territories

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Rest-point usage → Agency/dark-addiction; spontaneous goal-setting → Agency golden/dark-allergy
- Ally coordination → Communion; difficulty-seeking → Eros; familiar territory quality → Agape
- Rest avoidance → dark-addiction; aimless wandering → dark-allergy; systematic-but-weak → golden-addiction; burst-only engagement → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Goal-setting rate:** Frequency of spontaneous goal-declaration
- **Goal-completion rate:** Proportion of self-set goals completed
- **Persistence quality:** Effort sustained on challenging goals
- **Rest wisdom:** Appropriateness of rest/push decisions
- **Campaign engagement:** Sustained multi-session commitment

### LLM rubric
- **High:** World generation, goal-detection, ecological assessment, narrative. Evaluates spontaneous volitional behaviour.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (interact, claim, engage); Hold (sustain commitment, hold territory); Swipe (navigate, explore)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (goal-setting patterns, rest patterns, engagement duration)
- No millisecond precision needed; seconds/minutes-level measurement

### NPC/AI requirements
- Ally NPCs with own goals; Enemy NPCs with varied challenge profiles
- World must be CONSISTENT across sessions

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological will-assessment, narrative adaptation
- Detects spontaneous goal-setting from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates volitional quality without explicit tasks

### State persistence
- Full world state; goal-setting history; goal-completion history; rest patterns; campaign progress; territory ownership; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
