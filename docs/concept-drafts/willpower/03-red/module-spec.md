# Willpower / Red — Module Specification

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

**Power-will:** The capacity to set a short-horizon goal and pursue it with full intensity until fatigue ends the burst. At Red, willpower is NOT sustained discipline (Amber) or rational long-arc pursuit (Orange) — it is the raw "I WILL take this hill" energy that burns hot and brief. The will as weapon: a single-pointed burst of volitional force directed at a concrete, immediate goal.

**Healthy expression:** The player can declare a goal, sustain effort toward it for 15-60 seconds under competing demands, resist immediate temptations that conflict with the declared goal, and DELIVER the action that realises it. They can also LET GO when the burst is spent — rest, reset, and burst again. The will is powerful AND finite; health is knowing both.

**Pathological expression:** Either the will cannot ignite (cannot commit, cannot sustain even brief effort) OR the will cannot extinguish (cannot stop, cannot rest, burns past fatigue into self-destruction). Or: the will is performative (declares goals but doesn't follow through) or rigid (cannot revise a goal even when conditions change).

**Red-stage ceiling:**
- Maximum burst: ~60 seconds of sustained volitional effort
- Maximum goal horizon: single-session, concrete, immediate
- Maximum temptation resistance: 3-4 competing demands per burst
- NO multi-session goals (Amber), NO abstract goals (Orange), NO goal-portfolio management (Green)

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### The Compulsive Willer (Dark-Addiction)

Cannot stop willing. Uses volitional force compulsively — sets goal after goal, pursues relentlessly, treats rest as failure. The will is always ON. Willing has become a defence against vulnerability, emptiness, or feeling.

**Drive dynamics:** Agency HYPER — will dominates everything. Communion ABSENT — cannot join others' goals. Eros COMPULSIVE — always reaching for the next goal before integration. Agape REJECTED — cannot return to rest.

**Developmental origin:** The child whose worth was tied to DOING. Rest was punished or ignored. The will became the only safe place.

**Red-stage flavour:** The warlord who cannot stop conquering. Victory brings no satisfaction — only the next target. "If I stop, I am nothing."

**Atman Project defence:** Substitute gratification through volitional intensity.

### The Will-less (Dark-Allergy)

Cannot ignite the will. Goals dissolve before they form. Effort is aversive. Commitment feels like imprisonment. The will is OFF — genuinely absent or inaccessible.

**Drive dynamics:** Agency ABSENT — cannot initiate. Communion OVER-DEPENDENT — borrows others' will. Eros COLLAPSED — no reaching. Agape STUCK — trapped in pre-volitional state.

**Developmental origin:** The child whose will was crushed. Every assertion met with punishment. "Don't want. Don't try. Don't reach."

**Red-stage flavour:** The warrior who cannot fight. Stands in the arena but cannot strike. "I would, but..."

**Atman Project defence:** Avoidance of the terror of failure. If you never will, you never fail.

### The Premature Disciplinarian (Golden-Addiction)

Performs sustained, structured willpower (Amber+) without having mastered burst-will. Creates elaborate goal-systems — all the FORM of higher willpower without the FIRE of Red-stage volitional force. Discipline without power.

**Drive dynamics:** Agency PERFORMATIVE — appears sovereign but will is borrowed from structure. Communion OVER-STRUCTURED. Eros BYPASSING — reaches for sustained discipline without integrating burst-power. Agape DISSOCIATED — cannot return to raw will.

**Developmental origin:** The child rewarded for APPEARING disciplined. Learned the form of willpower without developing the substance.

**Red-stage flavour:** The warrior who trains by the book but cannot fight in the moment. Perfect form, no fire. "According to my system, I should..."

**Atman Project defence:** Premature transcendence — using higher-stage structure to avoid Red-stage rawness.

### The Discipline-Refuser (Golden-Allergy)

Actively refuses any extension of volitional capacity. Treats the call to longer commitment as imprisonment. Identifies with short-burst will and refuses growth beyond it.

**Drive dynamics:** Agency DEFENSIVE — protects current style against expansion. Communion REJECTING — refuses sustained commitment. Eros TERRIFIED — growth triggers existential fear. Agape CLINGING — clings to burst-will as identity.

**Developmental origin:** The child who experienced sustained commitment as LOSS. Sustained will = death of aliveness.

**Red-stage flavour:** The warrior who refuses to become a general. "I fight. I don't plan campaigns. Discipline is for the weak."

**Atman Project defence:** Desacralisation of the emergent — treating sustained will as inferior to burst will.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

### Healthy Expression (4×2)

| Drive | Dark-domain (healthy) | Golden-domain (healthy) |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Sets own goals; pursues with own force; accepts limits without losing sovereignty | Initiates without external prompting; self-starts; will is genuinely self-generated |
| **Communion** | Can align will with shared goals without losing volitional identity | Can commit volitional force to goals that serve others (not just self) |
| **Eros** | Can rest in current volitional capacity; enjoys completed goals | Can attempt stretch-goals with curiosity; reaches without grandiosity |
| **Agape** | Can return to simple goals with full commitment; doesn't dismiss easy willing | Volitional capacity transfers to unstructured life; will persists outside challenges |

### Pathological Expression (4×2)

| Drive | Dark-domain (pathological) | Golden-domain (pathological) |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Will as domination; "My will is law"; cannot accept limits | Cannot initiate; borrows will; waits for permission |
| **Communion** | Abandons own goals in presence of others' will; volitional dissolution | Cannot commit to any goal that serves others; purely egocentric will |
| **Eros** | Compulsive goal-escalation; cannot enjoy completion; will as addiction | Grandiose goal-declaration; claims capacity far beyond demonstration |
| **Agape** | Refuses simple goals; "beneath my will"; only engages impressive targets | Will exists only in structured challenges; no transfer to life |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### Dark-Addiction → Agape + Agency (return with sovereignty)

The game must make REST more powerful than continuous willing. Not by punishing willing but by making rest PRODUCE power.

1. **Power-rest:** Visible power accumulation during voluntary rest. "The warrior who rests strikes harder."
2. **Completion celebration:** Extended positive feedback that rewards STAYING in completion.
3. **Fatigue-as-signal:** Make fatigue visible and frame as wisdom.
4. **Goal-spacing:** Mandatory cool-down that produces better outcomes than immediate re-engagement.

**Resolution:** Can complete a goal, rest voluntarily for ≥10 seconds, and re-engage with HIGHER performance.

### Dark-Allergy → Agape + Agency (return with sovereignty)

The game must make willing SAFE. Not by forcing effort but by making the smallest volitional act produce visible, immediate power.

1. **Micro-goals:** Goals so small they're almost impossible to fail.
2. **Immediate power:** Every completed goal produces visible, immediate power-gain.
3. **No punishment for failure:** Failed goals produce neutral feedback.
4. **Gradual extension:** 3s → 5s → 10s → 15s. Each extension tiny and celebrated.

**Resolution:** Can declare a goal, sustain effort for ≥15 seconds, and complete it with visible satisfaction.

### Golden-Addiction → Eros + Communion (reach with joining)

The game must make RAW BURST-WILL more powerful than structured discipline. Not by punishing structure but by making fire outperform form.

1. **Power-mode:** Challenges where raw volitional force outscores careful planning.
2. **Structure-breaking:** Situations where the plan MUST break and only raw will carries through.
3. **Fire-modelling:** NPC demonstrating powerful burst-will.
4. **Form-failure:** Elaborate plans that fail, followed by simple burst-will that succeeds.

**Resolution:** Can engage a challenge with raw volitional force (no pre-planning) and succeed through intensity alone.

### Golden-Allergy → Eros + Communion (reach with joining)

The game must make LONGER WILL feel like MORE POWER, not less freedom. Not by forcing sustained effort but by making extension feel like expansion.

1. **Power-accumulation:** Longer bursts produce exponentially more power.
2. **Freedom-framing:** "You CHOOSE to hold longer. Nobody makes you."
3. **Gradual extension:** 15s → 20s → 25s → 30s. Optional and rewarded, never required.
4. **Identity-expansion:** "You're not becoming disciplined. You're becoming MORE powerful."

**Resolution:** Can sustain volitional effort for ≥30 seconds without resistance, experienced as power-gain.

## 5. Scoring Parameters

### Capacity Dimensions

| Dimension | Task vehicle | Pass threshold | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goal declaration** | Declare a specific goal before challenge | Concrete goal (not vague) | Specific, achievable, self-chosen |
| **Burst persistence** | Sustain effort under distraction | ≥15s without abandoning | ≥60s under increasing distraction |
| **Delay of gratification** | Resist smaller-sooner reward | 1 temptation per burst | 3+ temptations without wavering |
| **Goal delivery** | Complete the declared goal | ≥50% completion rate | ≥80% on appropriately-sized goals |
| **Fatigue recognition** | Know when burst is spent; rest voluntarily | Stop before total collapse | Stop at optimal fatigue point |

### Checkpoint Progression

- **CP 1-5:** Declare concrete goal; sustain ≥10s; resist 1 temptation; ≥40% completion; recognise fatigue.
- **CP 5-20:** Self-chosen goals; ≥20s under distraction; resist 2 temptations; ≥60% completion; voluntary rest; drives stabilising.
- **CP 20-50:** Appropriately-sized goals; ≥45s under significant distraction; resist 3+; ≥75% completion; optimal fatigue recognition; shadows diminishing.
- **CP 50+:** Effortless declaration; full 60s burst; automatic resistance; ≥80% completion; perfect fatigue calibration; all drives healthy; shadow integration complete; transfer to life.

### Shadow Integration States

| Shadow | Active | Resolving | Resolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compulsive Willer** | Cannot stop; will as escape | Notices compulsion; can pause | Can will AND rest; will is tool not compulsion |
| **The Will-less** | Cannot commit; avoids effort | Sustains brief efforts; starting to feel satisfaction | Commits to short-horizon goals; effort not aversive |
| **Premature Disciplinarian** | Form without fire; multi-session goals at Red | Recognises burst-mastery precedes discipline | Engages burst-will with full power; honest about horizon |
| **Discipline-Refuser** | Refuses extension; treats longer will as prison | Tolerates slightly longer commitments | Engages longer demands without terror; sees extension as power |

### Theta-Decay

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Half-life | 21 days | Slower than somatic (14d), faster than cognitive (28d) |
| Maximum decay | 30% | Will doesn't fully disappear but weakens significantly |
| Recovery rate | 3-5 sessions | Faster than cognitive, slower than somatic |
| Decay trigger | No goal-setting/completion for >7 days | Will decays when not USED |

### Cross-Module Dependencies

**Supports Willpower/Red:** Cognitive/Red (planning → goal-formation), Somatic/Red (body-energy → fuel), Emotional/Red (emotional fuel → power), Intrapersonal/Red (identity → the one who wills).

**Willpower/Red supports:** Cognitive/Red (sustained attention), Somatic/Red (posture/endurance), Moral/Red (override impulse), Interpersonal/Red (honour commitments).

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### Compound Patterns

| Name | Modules | Pattern | Break point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Impotent Thinker** | Will-allergy + Cognitive-addiction | Plans endlessly, never acts; cognition substitutes for will | Micro-goals requiring ACTION not thought |
| **The Blind Charger** | Will-addiction + Cognitive-allergy | Acts compulsively without planning; will without direction | Forced pause before action (planning window) |
| **The Burning Body** | Will-addiction + Somatic-addiction | Cannot stop moving OR willing; exhaustion spiral | Power-rest rewarding BOTH stopping |
| **The Ghost** | Will-allergy + Somatic-allergy | Neither body nor will present; total withdrawal | Micro-action (single tap = goal complete) |

### Feeding Relationships

**External shadows feeding Willpower/Red:**
- Cognitive/Red dark-addiction → Will-allergy ("I'll think instead of doing")
- Cognitive/Red dark-allergy → Will-addiction (no planning → blind willing)
- Somatic/Red dark-addiction → Will-addiction (compulsive body + compulsive will)
- Somatic/Red dark-allergy → Will-allergy (no body-energy → will cannot ignite)
- Emotional/Red dark-addiction → Will-addiction (emotional intensity fuels compulsive willing)
- Emotional/Red dark-allergy → Will-allergy (no emotional fuel → will has no energy)

**Willpower/Red feeding others:**
- Will-addiction → Somatic/Red dark-addiction, Cognitive/Red dark-allergy
- Will-allergy → Cognitive/Red dark-addiction, Interpersonal/Red dark-allergy
- Golden-addiction → Moral/Red golden-addiction (premature moral structure)
- Golden-allergy → Moral/Red dark-allergy (cannot maintain moral commitments)

### Bidirectional Loops

| Loop | Pattern | Break |
|---|---|---|
| **Think-Don't-Do** | Will-allergy ↔ Cognitive-addiction | Micro-goals requiring action |
| **Burn-Without-Direction** | Will-addiction ↔ Cognitive-allergy | Forced pause before action |
| **Exhaustion Spiral** | Will-addiction ↔ Somatic-addiction | Power-rest rewarding both stopping |

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### The Sequence

```
1. GOAL-DECLARATION → observe: Can they commit? (allergy surfaces)
2. SUSTAINED EFFORT → observe: Can they maintain? (addiction/allergy surfaces)
3. TEMPTATION → observe: Can they resist? (addiction surfaces)
4. COMPLETION → observe: Can they rest? (addiction surfaces)
5. EXTENSION → observe: Can they grow? (golden shadows surface)
```

### Surfacing Conditions per Quadrant

| Quadrant | Condition | Observe |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark-Addiction** | Offer rest after goal-completion | Do they rest? Or immediately re-engage? |
| **Dark-Allergy** | Present goal requiring ≥15s effort | Do they commit? Or avoid/abandon? |
| **Golden-Addiction** | Present challenge requiring RAW FORCE (no planning) | Do they engage with fire? Or systematise? |
| **Golden-Allergy** | Offer OPTIONAL longer commitment with bonus power | Do they engage? Or refuse/resist? |

### Behavioural Detection Signals

| Shadow | Primary signal | Secondary signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark-Addiction** | Zero rest between goals; immediate re-engagement | Goal-escalation without satisfaction; fatigue denial; inability to use rest-points |
| **Dark-Allergy** | Goal-abandonment within first 5s; effort-avoidance | Passive waiting; "I don't care" responses; minimal input |
| **Golden-Addiction** | Elaborate systems with poor execution; form without fire | Multi-step plans at Red stage; collapses when structure breaks |
| **Golden-Allergy** | Performance cliff at sustained-effort threshold | Verbal resistance to longer commitments; "Just let me fight" |

### Pattern Signals (Across Sessions)

| Shadow | Pattern |
|---|---|
| **Dark-Addiction** | Session length increasing without performance gain; rest-point usage declining |
| **Dark-Allergy** | Session length decreasing; goal-completion rate declining; avoidance of willpower challenges |
| **Golden-Addiction** | Goal-complexity increasing while completion drops; more planning, less doing |
| **Golden-Allergy** | Engagement drops when duration extends; fine at 15s, collapses at 30s |
