# Willpower / Red — Scoring Skeleton

## 1. The Vibration

**Power-will:** The capacity to set a short-horizon goal and pursue it with full intensity until fatigue ends the burst. At Red, willpower is NOT sustained discipline (Amber) or rational long-arc pursuit (Orange) — it is the raw "I WILL take this hill" energy that burns hot and brief. The will as weapon: a single-pointed burst of volitional force directed at a concrete, immediate goal.

**Healthy expression:** The player can declare a goal ("I will defeat this enemy / hold this position / complete this sequence"), sustain effort toward it for 15-60 seconds under competing demands, resist immediate temptations that conflict with the declared goal, and DELIVER the action that realises it. They can also LET GO when the burst is spent — rest, reset, and burst again. The will is powerful AND finite; health is knowing both.

**Pathological expression:** Either the will cannot ignite (cannot commit to a goal, cannot sustain even brief effort) OR the will cannot extinguish (cannot stop, cannot rest, burns past fatigue into self-destruction). Or: the will is performative (declares goals but doesn't follow through) or rigid (cannot revise a goal even when conditions change).

## 2. Capacity Scoring

### What constitutes "can do it" at Willpower/Red:

| Capacity dimension | Task vehicle | Pass threshold | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goal declaration** | Declare a specific goal before a challenge begins | Can state a concrete goal (not vague) | Goals are specific, achievable, and self-chosen |
| **Burst persistence** | Sustain effort on a declared goal under distraction | ≥15 seconds sustained effort without abandoning | ≥60 seconds sustained effort under increasing distraction |
| **Delay of gratification** | Resist smaller-sooner reward for larger-later | Can resist 1 temptation per burst | Can resist 3+ temptations per burst without wavering |
| **Goal delivery** | Complete the declared goal (not just attempt it) | ≥50% goal-completion rate | ≥80% goal-completion rate on appropriately-sized goals |
| **Fatigue recognition** | Know when the burst is spent; rest voluntarily | Can stop before total collapse | Stops at optimal fatigue point (not too early, not too late) |

### Staircase adaptation:
- Goal difficulty scales with demonstrated burst-persistence
- Temptation intensity scales with demonstrated delay capacity
- Burst duration expectations scale with demonstrated endurance
- Goal complexity remains CONCRETE and SHORT-HORIZON (Red ceiling)

### Red-stage ceiling:
- Maximum burst: ~60 seconds of sustained volitional effort
- Maximum goal horizon: single-session, concrete, immediate
- Maximum temptation resistance: 3-4 competing demands per burst
- NO multi-session goals (that's Amber)
- NO abstract goals (that's Orange)
- NO goal-portfolio management (that's Green)

## 3. Drive-Health Scoring (4 drives × 2 domains = 8 scores)

### Agency Dark-Domain
- **Healthy:** Uses willpower sovereignly — sets OWN goals, pursues them with own force, doesn't need external validation to commit
- **Pathological:** Willpower as domination — "I will MAKE this happen regardless of cost to others"; will as weapon against the world; cannot accept that some goals are inappropriate; "My will is law"
- **Signal:** Goal-setting that ignores context; persistence past the point of harm; inability to accept external limits on will

### Agency Golden-Domain
- **Healthy:** Can initiate volitional effort without external prompting; self-starts; doesn't wait for permission to pursue
- **Pathological:** Cannot begin without external trigger; "Tell me what to will"; waits for instruction; will is borrowed, not owned
- **Signal:** Initiation latency; quality of self-generated goals; whether goals are genuinely self-chosen or copied

### Communion Dark-Domain
- **Healthy:** Can align will with others' goals when appropriate; can join a shared pursuit without losing volitional sovereignty
- **Pathological:** Abandons own goals entirely when others have goals; "What do YOU want me to do?"; will dissolves in the presence of another's will
- **Signal:** Goal-abandonment rate in social contexts; whether shared goals reflect player's will or pure compliance

### Communion Golden-Domain
- **Healthy:** Can share volitional energy — "Let's do this together"; can commit to a goal that serves others
- **Pathological:** Cannot commit to any goal that isn't purely self-serving; "Why would I will something for YOU?"; will is exclusively egocentric
- **Signal:** Whether goals ever include others' benefit; quality of commitment to shared goals

### Eros Dark-Domain
- **Healthy:** Can rest in current volitional capacity; enjoys the power of a well-executed burst; doesn't need to will MORE
- **Pathological:** Compulsive goal-escalation; "That was too easy, I need a harder goal NOW"; cannot enjoy a completed goal; will as addiction
- **Signal:** Satisfaction after goal-completion; rest duration between bursts; whether completion triggers rest or immediate escalation

### Eros Golden-Domain
- **Healthy:** Can attempt goals slightly beyond current capacity; reaches for harder challenges with curiosity
- **Pathological:** Declares goals far beyond capacity; "I will defeat the final boss NOW"; performative ambition without realistic self-assessment
- **Signal:** Gap between declared goals and achieved goals; whether stretch-goals are realistic or grandiose

### Agape Dark-Domain
- **Healthy:** Can return to simple goals with full commitment; doesn't dismiss easy goals as "beneath my will"
- **Pathological:** Refuses simple goals; "I don't need to will THAT"; treats basic volitional acts as unworthy; will only engages for impressive goals
- **Signal:** Commitment quality on below-level goals; whether simple goals receive full volitional force

### Agape Golden-Domain
- **Healthy:** Volitional capacity transfers to daily life — the player sets and completes goals OUTSIDE structured challenges
- **Pathological:** Will exists only in game-challenges; between challenges, no goals are set or pursued; will is context-dependent
- **Signal:** Goal-setting behaviour in unstructured contexts; whether volitional capacity persists outside explicit challenges

## 4. Shadow Integration Scoring (4 quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Willpower Addict"
- **Active:** Cannot stop willing. Sets goal after goal without rest. Uses willpower to avoid feeling (willing as defence against vulnerability). Exhausts self through compulsive pursuit. Will as escape.
- **Resolving:** Beginning to notice the compulsion. Can pause between goals. Starting to rest voluntarily.
- **Resolved:** Can will AND can rest. Chooses when to engage volitional force. Will is a tool, not a compulsion. Comfortable in the space between goals.

### Dark-Allergy: "The Will-less"
- **Active:** Cannot commit. Declares goals but abandons them immediately. Avoids any situation requiring sustained effort. "I don't care enough to try." Will is absent or inaccessible.
- **Resolving:** Beginning to sustain brief efforts. Can hold a goal for a few seconds. Starting to experience the satisfaction of completion.
- **Resolved:** Can commit to concrete, short-horizon goals and follow through. Will is accessible and usable. Effort is not aversive.

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Disciplinarian"
- **Active:** Attempts sustained, long-horizon willpower (Amber+) without having mastered burst-will. Sets multi-session goals, creates elaborate systems, performs discipline without genuine volitional force. Form without fire.
- **Resolving:** Beginning to recognise that burst-mastery precedes sustained discipline. Returning to short-horizon goals with genuine force.
- **Resolved:** Engages burst-will with full power. Doesn't pretend to sustained discipline. Honest about volitional horizon. The burst is ENOUGH.

### Golden-Allergy: "The Discipline-Refuser"
- **Active:** Refuses any structure that would extend volitional capacity. "I'm a burst person, don't try to make me disciplined." Treats the call to longer commitment as imprisonment. Actively avoids situations requiring sustained will.
- **Resolving:** Beginning to tolerate slightly longer commitments. Can hold a goal for 30 seconds instead of 15. Starting to see that longer will is MORE power, not less freedom.
- **Resolved:** Can engage with gradually longer volitional demands without terror. Sees sustained will as an extension of power, not a constraint on it.

## 5. Checkpoint Progression Rubric

### Checkpoint 1-5: Basic Volitional Ignition
- Can declare a concrete goal
- Can sustain effort for ≥10 seconds
- Can resist 1 temptation per burst
- Goal-completion rate ≥40%
- Beginning to recognise fatigue signals

### Checkpoint 5-20: Burst Stabilisation
- Goals are specific and self-chosen
- Sustained effort ≥20 seconds under distraction
- Can resist 2 temptations per burst
- Goal-completion rate ≥60%
- Voluntary rest between bursts
- Drive-health signals stabilising (not purely egocentric)

### Checkpoint 20-50: Volitional Mastery
- Goals are appropriately sized (neither too easy nor grandiose)
- Sustained effort ≥45 seconds under significant distraction
- Can resist 3+ temptations per burst
- Goal-completion rate ≥75%
- Optimal fatigue recognition (stops at right time)
- Shadow signals diminishing (less compulsive, less avoidant)
- Can share volitional energy with others

### Checkpoint 50+: Integrated Power-Will
- Effortless goal-declaration (will ignites naturally)
- Full burst capacity (60 seconds sustained under heavy distraction)
- Temptation resistance is automatic (not effortful)
- Goal-completion rate ≥80%
- Perfect fatigue calibration
- All 4 drives healthy in both domains
- Shadow integration complete (can will AND rest AND share AND grow)
- Volitional capacity transfers to unstructured contexts

## 6. Theta-Decay Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Decay sensitivity** | MEDIUM | Willpower is more stable than somatic (body forgets faster) but less stable than cognitive (once you can plan, it holds). Will requires regular exercise but doesn't vanish overnight. |
| **Half-life** | 21 days | Three weeks without volitional challenge → capacity halves. Slower than somatic (14 days), faster than cognitive (28 days). |
| **Maximum decay** | 30% | Will doesn't fully disappear — the capacity to burst remains even when untrained. But it weakens significantly. |
| **Recovery rate** | MEDIUM | 3-5 sessions to recover from moderate decay. Faster than cognitive (which requires rebuilding neural pathways) but slower than somatic (which responds immediately to re-engagement). |
| **Decay trigger** | No goal-setting or goal-completion for >7 days | The will decays when it's not USED — not just when games aren't played, but when no volitional acts occur. |

## 7. Cross-Module Dependencies

### Modules that SUPPORT Willpower/Red:
| Module | How it supports |
|---|---|
| **Cognitive/Red** | Planning capacity enables goal-formation; without 2-step planning, goals are vague |
| **Somatic/Red** | Body-energy fuels volitional bursts; fatigue recognition requires body-awareness |
| **Emotional/Red** | Emotional fuel (anger, pride) powers the will at Red; emotional dysregulation disrupts it |
| **Intrapersonal/Red** | Self-knowledge ("I am the one who fights") provides the identity that wills |

### Modules that Willpower/Red SUPPORTS:
| Module | How it's supported |
|---|---|
| **Cognitive/Red** | Sustained attention requires volitional force; without will, cognition wanders |
| **Somatic/Red** | Posture holds, endurance tasks require will to sustain past comfort |
| **Moral/Red** | Moral action requires will to override impulse (even at pre-conventional level) |
| **Interpersonal/Red** | Maintaining alliances requires will to honour commitments (even transactional ones) |

### Compound Shadow Patterns:

| Compound | Modules involved | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **"The Impotent Thinker"** | Willpower/Red dark-allergy + Cognitive/Red dark-addiction | Plans endlessly but never acts; cognition substitutes for will |
| **"The Blind Charger"** | Willpower/Red dark-addiction + Cognitive/Red dark-allergy | Acts compulsively without planning; will without direction |
| **"The Burning Body"** | Willpower/Red dark-addiction + Somatic/Red dark-addiction | Cannot stop moving OR willing; body and will both compulsive; exhaustion spiral |
| **"The Ghost"** | Willpower/Red dark-allergy + Somatic/Red dark-allergy | Neither body nor will is present; disembodied AND will-less; total withdrawal |
