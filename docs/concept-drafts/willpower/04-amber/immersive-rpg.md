# Willpower / Amber — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological duty-will — how the player's commitment manifests spontaneously in free-play, without structured prompts or explicit vow-tasks.
> **Why this axis for this module:** The deepest test of duty-will is whether it appears SPONTANEOUSLY in unstructured contexts. A player who holds vows in structured tasks (deterministic) but abandons them in free-play has compartmentalised capacity. The immersive-RPG reveals whether duty-will has genuinely transferred to lived behaviour.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Knight's Errand"
- **Core mechanic:** The player takes on a role within an Amber-stage world (knight, guild member, temple guardian) and navigates a free-play environment where obligations arise naturally. No explicit vow-tasks — the player's duty-will is revealed through spontaneous choices, role-consistent behaviour, and how they handle the inevitable conflicts between duty and desire.
- **Duration:** 8-20 minutes per session (open-ended; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Taking the Role → The World's Demands → The Temptation Arises → The Code Is Tested → The Knight's Honour

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The ECOLOGICAL dimension of duty-will — whether commitment appears spontaneously in unstructured contexts. Unlike all other modalities (which present explicit commitment challenges), this modality observes the player's natural behaviour in a world that has obligations embedded in it.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike deterministic (explicit vow-tasks), language-reflective (explicit reflection prompts), scenario-choice (explicit dilemmas), embodied-somatic (explicit physical challenges), strategic-planning (explicit planning tasks), and social-cooperative (explicit group obligations) — this modality presents NO explicit commitment challenges. The player simply inhabits the world. Their duty-will (or lack of it) emerges naturally.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** Whether duty-will has genuinely transferred to lived behaviour. The vow-slave will find obligations everywhere and cannot rest. The oath-breaker will ignore role-consistent obligations spontaneously. The premature strategist will optimise the world rather than inhabit it. The code-clinger will stay in known areas and refuse novel obligations.

**Successful integration:** The player inhabits their role naturally — holding obligations when they arise, resting when appropriate, adapting when the world demands it — without either compulsive duty-fulfilment or spontaneous abandonment.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Knight's Errand: an open-world RPG environment set in an Amber-stage world. The player has a role (knight, guild member, temple guardian) with associated obligations. The world presents situations that naturally invoke duty-will — without labelling them as "commitment challenges." Aesthetic: Amber-stage world — feudal order, guild structures, temple hierarchies, the lived texture of obligation.

### Interaction
- **Taking the Role (1-5):** Player selects a role and receives its associated obligations. "As a knight of the order, you are sworn to: protect the weak, honour your lord, keep your word." No explicit tasks — just the role.
- **The World's Demands (5-15):** The world presents situations that invoke the role's obligations. A traveller needs help. A lord gives an order. A companion asks for support. The player responds (or doesn't) spontaneously.
- **The Temptation Arises (15-30):** The world presents temptations that conflict with the role's obligations. A shortcut that violates the code. A reward that requires oath-breaking. The player's spontaneous response is observed.
- **The Code Is Tested (30-50):** The world presents situations where the code itself is questioned. An unjust order. A vow that has become destructive. The player's spontaneous response to code-examination is observed.
- **The Knight's Honour (50+):** Full integration: spontaneous obligation-fulfilment + temptation-resistance + code-examination — all in free-play.

### Feedback
- Role-consistent behaviour → "The knight acts as a knight. The world notices. The order's reputation grows."
- Role-inconsistent behaviour → "The knight acted outside the code. The world noticed. What does this mean for the oath?"
- Compulsive obligation-seeking → "The knight finds duty everywhere. Even in rest. The order asks: can you serve AND rest?"
- Obligation-avoidance → "The knight passed the traveller. The code was not invoked. The world asks: was this a choice or an absence?"
- Code-examination → "The knight questioned the order. This is not betrayal — this is wisdom. The code grows stronger when examined."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- World complexity: simple obligations → competing obligations → multi-layered duty-conflicts
- Temptation frequency: rare → occasional → frequent
- Code-examination depth: gentle → direct → fundamental
- Role complexity: single obligation → multiple obligations → obligation-hierarchy

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Taking the Role | 1-5 | Role selection and obligation-awareness |
| The World's Demands | 5-15 | Spontaneous obligation-response |
| The Temptation Arises | 15-30 | Spontaneous temptation-resistance |
| The Code Is Tested | 30-50 | Spontaneous code-examination response |
| The Knight's Honour | 50+ | Full ecological duty-will integration |

## 4. Item Pool

- **World-demand scenarios:** Situations invoking role obligations spontaneously — 30+
- **Temptation scenarios:** Situations conflicting with role obligations — 25+
- **Code-examination scenarios:** Situations questioning the role's obligations — 20+
- **Rest scenarios:** Situations where rest is appropriate — 15+
- **Novel obligation scenarios:** Situations presenting new role-consistent obligations — 15+

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input types:** Tap (action selection); hold (deliberation); swipe (world navigation); free movement (open-world navigation)
- **Timing:** Response latency to obligation-invoking situations tracked; temptation-response time measured; code-examination engagement duration recorded
- **NPC/AI:** World-event generation based on role and shadow-signal accumulation; obligation-invocation calibration; temptation-magnitude adaptation; code-examination depth scaling; ecological behaviour analysis
- **LLM:** World narration; NPC dialogue; consequence delivery; obligation-invocation naturalness; code-examination depth
- **State persistence:** Full world-interaction history; spontaneous obligation-response patterns; temptation-response quality; code-examination engagement; role-consistency tracking; cross-session world-state continuity; drive/shadow signal accumulation; checkpoint position
