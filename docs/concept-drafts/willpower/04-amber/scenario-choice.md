# Willpower / Amber — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Decision-making under obligation — how the player chooses when duty conflicts with desire, when vows conflict with each other, and when the code demands sacrifice.
> **Why this axis for this module:** Duty-will is revealed most clearly in CHOICE under pressure. The scenario-choice modality presents the exact situations where each shadow quadrant manifests: the vow-slave cannot choose against the code, the oath-breaker chooses impulse over commitment, the premature strategist rationalises away the obligation, and the code-clinger refuses to examine the choice.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Crossroads of Oaths"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces branching scenarios where their declared commitments conflict with immediate desires, competing obligations, or the call to examine the code. Choices reveal the structure of their duty-will. No choice is "wrong" — but each choice is diagnostic.
- **Duration:** 4-10 minutes per session (scenario depth scales with demonstrated capacity)
- **Internal progression:** The Simple Duty → The Competing Vow → The Costly Obligation → The Code Questioned → The Oath-Keeper's Choice

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The DECISION dimension of duty-will — how the player chooses when obligation and desire conflict. Unlike deterministic (which measures raw holding), this modality measures the QUALITY of volitional choice under moral pressure.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike language-reflective (which measures verbal articulation), this modality measures actual choice behaviour. A player who articulates duty beautifully but chooses impulse here has a language-behaviour gap (golden-addiction). A player who cannot articulate but chooses consistently has implicit duty-will.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** All 4 shadow quadrants through choice patterns. The vow-slave always chooses the code. The oath-breaker always chooses impulse. The premature strategist always chooses the "rational" option. The code-clinger always chooses the familiar obligation over the examined one.

**Successful integration:** The player makes contextually appropriate choices — holding vows when appropriate, releasing them when appropriate, examining them when appropriate — with healthy drive expression across all four drives.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Crossroads: a narrative interface where the player's character faces obligation-dilemmas. Each scenario presents a concrete situation with 3-4 choices. The player chooses; the consequences unfold. Aesthetic: Amber-stage world — guild halls, sacred oaths, honour codes, the weight of sworn word.

### Interaction
- **The Simple Duty (1-5):** Clear obligation, clear temptation. "You swore to guard the gate. A friend asks you to leave for an hour. What do you do?" Tests basic vow-holding vs. impulse.
- **The Competing Vow (5-15):** Two obligations conflict. "You swore to your guild AND to your family. Both need you now. What do you do?" Tests vow-hierarchy and sovereign choice.
- **The Costly Obligation (15-30):** Holding the vow costs significantly. "Keeping your oath will cost you the reward you've been working toward. What do you do?" Tests duty-persistence under sacrifice.
- **The Code Questioned (30-50):** The obligation itself is challenged. "Your elder says the vow you swore was unjust. Do you examine it? Release it? Hold it anyway?" Tests code-examination capacity.
- **The Oath-Keeper's Choice (50+):** Full integration: competing vows + cost + examination + sovereign choice simultaneously.

### Feedback
- Vow held under pressure → "The gate holds. Your word holds. The guild remembers."
- Vow broken for impulse → "The gate was left. Your friend was helped. The guild asks: was this the right choice? Not wrong — but examine it."
- Rational bypass → "You calculated the optimal outcome. But the vow was not a calculation. It was a word. The crossroads asks: what is your word worth?"
- Code-examination refused → "The elder asked. You held the vow. Good. But the question remains: do you know why? The crossroads will ask again."
- Sovereign release → "You examined the vow. You found it unjust. You released it with honour. This is not weakness — this is wisdom."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Scenario complexity: single obligation → competing obligations → multi-layered duty-conflicts
- Cost magnitude: minor inconvenience → significant sacrifice → identity-level cost
- Code-examination depth: gentle questioning → direct challenge → fundamental examination
- Choice consequences: immediate → delayed → cross-session

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Simple Duty | 1-5 | Single obligation vs. single temptation |
| The Competing Vow | 5-15 | Two obligations in conflict |
| The Costly Obligation | 15-30 | Duty under significant sacrifice |
| The Code Questioned | 30-50 | Obligation examined and potentially revised |
| The Oath-Keeper's Choice | 50+ | Full duty-will integration under maximum pressure |

---

## 4. Item Pool

- **Simple duty scenarios:** Single obligation vs. single temptation — 25+
- **Competing vow scenarios:** Two obligations in conflict — 20+
- **Costly obligation scenarios:** Duty requiring significant sacrifice — 20+
- **Code-examination scenarios:** Obligation challenged or questioned — 15+
- **Integration scenarios:** Multi-dimensional duty-conflicts — 10+

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input types:** Tap (choice selection); hold (deliberation time); swipe (scenario navigation)
- **Timing:** Choice latency tracked; deliberation time measured; avoidance patterns detected
- **NPC/AI:** Adaptive scenario selection based on shadow-signal accumulation; consequence-delivery system; cross-session narrative continuity; drive-health signal extraction from choice patterns
- **LLM:** Consequence narration; scenario adaptation; choice-quality assessment for ambiguous cases
- **State persistence:** Full choice history; shadow-pattern tracking; drive-health signal accumulation; cross-session narrative coherence; vow-history integration; checkpoint position
