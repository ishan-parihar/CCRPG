# Willpower / Amber — Module Specification

> **Line:** Willpower (volitional force, goal-pursuit, self-regulation)
> **Stage:** Amber / Mythic-Membership (conformist, rule-following, duty-bound)
> **Vibration:** The vow that holds — will as sustained commitment to a code, role, or duty. Not the burst-fire of Red but the long-burning flame of obligation. "I will because I must. I must because I swore."

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Willpower at Amber is **duty-will** — the capacity to sustain volitional effort across extended time horizons by binding the will to a code, role, or vow. The Red warrior who could hold a goal for 60 seconds now holds a commitment for days, weeks, or a lifetime — not through rational planning (Orange) but through OBLIGATION to something larger than the self.

**Key distinction from Red:** Red will is burst-fire — intense, short, self-generated. Amber will is sustained-flame — moderate, long, code-generated. Red says "I WILL take this hill." Amber says "I will hold this post because I swore to."

**Key distinction from Orange:** Orange will is rational-strategic — goals are chosen by cost-benefit analysis and pursued through planned effort. Amber will is duty-bound — goals are assigned by role and pursued through obligation. Amber cannot yet question whether the vow is worth keeping; Orange can.

### Core capacity dimensions

| Dimension | Definition | Assessment vehicle | Ceiling at this stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vow-holding** | Sustain a declared commitment across multiple sessions | Multi-session goal-tracking; vow-renewal tasks | Holds vow across 7+ days without abandonment |
| **Duty-persistence** | Continue effort when motivation is absent but obligation remains | Low-motivation sustained tasks; "boring but required" challenges | 10+ minutes of low-reward sustained effort |
| **Role-consistency** | Maintain volitional behaviour appropriate to declared role | Role-congruent choice tasks; identity-consistency probes | Consistent across 5+ role-relevant decisions |
| **Temptation-resistance** | Resist short-term rewards that violate the code | Delay-of-gratification under role-pressure | Resists 4+ competing temptations per session |
| **Fatigue-through** | Continue past fatigue because the duty demands it | Effort-continuation under fatigue signals | Continues for 2+ minutes past first fatigue signal |

### What this is NOT

- **NOT Red:** Red will ignites and burns out. Amber will is a slow, steady flame. Red cannot sustain across sessions; Amber can.
- **NOT Orange:** Orange will is self-chosen and rationally justified. Amber will is role-assigned and obligation-justified. Amber cannot yet ask "is this vow worth keeping?" — that requires Orange reflective capacity.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Vow-Slave"

**Core pattern:** Cannot stop fulfilling obligations. Every duty is absolute. Every vow is eternal. The will is OWNED by the code — not serving it but enslaved to it. Cannot rest, cannot revise, cannot release even when the vow has been fulfilled or has become destructive.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Will is surrendered to the code; "I have no choice — I swore"
- Communion: Imposes duty on others; "You also must honour the code"
- Eros: Cannot grow beyond current obligations; new vows pile on old ones
- Agape: Cannot return to rest; every moment of non-duty is a violation

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Continues obligations past the point of health or sense
- Cannot distinguish between binding vows and expired ones
- Treats all duties as equally absolute (no hierarchy of obligation)
- Guilt and shame when any duty is not fulfilled, regardless of context
- Cannot accept release from a vow even when the vow-giver releases them
- Adds new obligations compulsively; the duty-load only grows

**Atman Project defenses:** Duty-fixation, obligation-inflation, will-surrender to external code.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can hold vows AND release them when fulfilled or when context changes. Duty serves the self and the group; the self is not consumed by duty.

---

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Oath-Breaker"

**Core pattern:** Cannot sustain any commitment. Every vow dissolves at the first inconvenience. Stays in Red burst-will — intense but brief. The call to sustained obligation is experienced as imprisonment. "I said I would but I changed my mind."

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Acts impulsively; cannot bind will to future self
- Communion: Cannot coordinate through shared obligations; "I'll do what I want"
- Eros: Reaches for new goals before completing current ones; serial abandonment
- Agape: Cannot return to abandoned commitments; "That was then"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Declares commitments with full sincerity then abandons them within hours
- Treats vow-breaking as freedom rather than failure
- Cannot maintain role-consistent behaviour across sessions
- Duty-persistence is near zero; stops when motivation drops
- Rationalises abandonment: "The situation changed" / "I didn't really mean it"
- Interpersonal relationships suffer from repeated commitment-failure

**Atman Project defenses:** Commitment-avoidance, freedom-identification, Red-stage fixation.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can hold a commitment across multiple sessions. Can distinguish between genuine change-of-context (legitimate revision) and impulse-driven abandonment.

---

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Strategist"

**Core pattern:** Bypasses duty-will entirely and performs rational-strategic willpower (Orange) without Amber integration. Creates elaborate goal-systems, cost-benefit analyses, and self-improvement plans — all the FORM of Orange will without the FOUNDATION of Amber sustained commitment. Plans without the capacity to hold a simple vow.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Performs rational self-determination without genuine sustained will
- Communion: Dismisses duty as "irrational"; "I follow my own reasoning"
- Eros: Compulsive upward reach toward rational self-mastery
- Agape: Cannot return to simple obligation; "Duty is for people who can't think"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Elaborate planning systems that collapse because the underlying will cannot sustain
- Dismisses vows and duties as "primitive" or "irrational"
- Can articulate sophisticated goal-frameworks but cannot hold a simple 7-day commitment
- Cross-validation: strategic-planning scores HIGH, vow-holding scores LOW
- Treats sustained commitment as weakness; "I adapt my goals to new information"

**Atman Project defenses:** Premature rationalism, Orange-bypass, intellectualisation of will.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can hold a simple vow for 7+ days before engaging strategic planning. Duty-will is the foundation; rational-will builds on top.

---

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Code-Clinger"

**Core pattern:** Duty-will is PRESENT and strong, but the call to rational self-examination (Orange) is refused. Cannot question whether the vow is worth keeping. Cannot revise obligations based on new understanding. The code is SACRED and unquestionable. "I don't need to think about my duties — I just fulfil them."

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Cannot assert independent reasoning about obligations
- Communion: Clings to group's code; fears individual moral reasoning
- Eros: Refuses the call to examine and potentially revise the code
- Agape: Over-embodies current obligations; makes them permanent and sacred

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Excellent vow-holding and duty-persistence
- But: freezes when asked "why is this vow worth keeping?"
- Treats questioning of obligations as betrayal or weakness
- Cannot distinguish between healthy duty and destructive obligation
- Anxiety or hostility when the code is examined rather than just followed
- Refuses to revise vows even when context has clearly changed

**Atman Project defenses:** Code-sacralisation, anti-intellectualism, Orange-refusal.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can hold vows AND examine them. Can ask "is this still the right commitment?" without experiencing the question as betrayal.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Dark-domain healthy | Dark-domain pathological | Golden-domain healthy | Golden-domain pathological |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Holds vows as sovereign choice; "I swore and I choose to honour it" | Vow-Slave (will surrendered to code; "I have no choice") OR Oath-Breaker (cannot bind will to future self) | Asserts into new commitments with confidence; self-chosen duty | Cannot commit without external authority; waits for role-assignment |
| **Communion** | Coordinates through shared obligations; honours group code without losing self | Vow-Slave (imposes duty on others) OR Oath-Breaker (cannot coordinate through shared obligations) | Joins others in new forms of commitment; grows relationally | Cannot join new commitment-structures; clings to known code |
| **Eros** | Rests in current commitments with satisfaction; duty is fulfilling | Vow-Slave (cannot rest; compulsive duty-addition) OR Oath-Breaker (serial abandonment; always new goals) | Reaches toward rational self-examination of obligations | Premature Strategist (bypasses duty for rational planning) OR Code-Clinger (refuses examination) |
| **Agape** | Returns to obligations with renewed commitment; maintenance is honourable | Vow-Slave (compulsive return; cannot rest) OR Oath-Breaker (cannot return to abandoned commitments) | Embodies duty in lived life; commitment transfers outside challenges | Cannot embody; duty exists only in structured contexts |

**Drive-health modifier formula:**
```
drive_health_modifier = agency × 0.30 + communion × 0.25 + eros × 0.25 + agape × 0.20
```

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → dark shadows)

**For Vow-Slave:**
- Agape: Return to the SELF beneath the duty. "You are more than your obligations. The code serves you; you don't serve the code."
- Agency: Sovereign examination of each vow. "Is this still mine? Do I still choose this?"
- Mechanism: Present fulfilled vows that can be released. Reward the act of conscious release. "The vow is complete. You are free. That is not failure — that is mastery."

**For Oath-Breaker:**
- Agape: Return to the abandoned commitment with compassion. "You said you would. You can still choose to."
- Agency: Own the commitment as a sovereign choice. "Not because you must — because you CHOOSE to hold it."
- Mechanism: Ultra-short vow-windows (24 hours). Celebrate every completion. Extend gradually. Make commitment feel like power, not prison.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → golden shadows)

**For Premature Strategist:**
- Eros: Reach toward GENUINE sustained commitment (not planned commitment). "Hold this one vow. No system. No plan. Just hold it."
- Communion: Join with others in shared obligation. "The code is not irrational — it is the group's wisdom. Can you honour it?"
- Mechanism: Lock strategic-planning tasks behind demonstrated vow-holding. Simple sustained commitment must precede complex planning.

**For Code-Clinger:**
- Eros: Reach toward the QUESTION (not the answer). "Why is this vow worth keeping? Not to abandon it — to understand it."
- Communion: Join with others who examine AND honour their code. "They question AND they hold. Both are possible."
- Mechanism: Introduce gentle meta-cognitive tasks about obligations. "Notice the vow. Don't change it. Just notice why you hold it." Reward noticing without requiring revision.

### 4.3 Integration criteria

The player demonstrates: sustained vow-holding (7+ days) without rigidity; duty-persistence through low-motivation periods; role-consistency without identity-fusion; temptation-resistance without compulsion; capacity to release fulfilled obligations; openness to examining (not necessarily revising) the code.

## 5. Scoring Parameters

### Theta model
- **Half-life:** 18 days (duty-will is more durable than burst-will; slower decay)
- **Max decay:** 22% of peak theta
- **Composite weights:** vow-holding 0.30, duty-persistence 0.25, role-consistency 0.20, temptation-resistance 0.15, fatigue-through 0.10

### Stage-transition thresholds
- **Amber → Orange:** Theta ≥ 0.7 on vow-holding AND duty-persistence ≥ 0.65; confirmed across ≥ 5 sessions; ≥ 2 other lines at Amber; first signs of code-examination without anxiety
- **Regression toward Red:** vow-holding < 0.4 OR duty-persistence < 0.35 across 3+ sessions

### Drive-health weight: 0.30
### Shadow-drag coefficient: 0.22
### Cross-line bonus cap: +0.15 (from Cognitive/Amber + Somatic/Amber)

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Rigid Soldier (Willpower × Somatic, both dark-addiction)

**Loop:** Duty-will FORCES the body through form regardless of pain or fatigue. The body is a tool of obligation. Somatic dark-addiction (rigid drilling) + Willpower dark-addiction (vow-slavery) = self-punishment through discipline. The body breaks but the will continues.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Vow-slavery → "My body must serve the code" → forced somatic form
- Rigid drilling → "Stopping is failure" → more vow-slavery

**Break point:** Present situations where REST is the duty. "The code requires you to recover. Stopping IS the obligation."

---

### 6.2 The Hollow Performer (Willpower × Emotional, both dark-allergy)

**Loop:** Cannot sustain commitment (Willpower) because cannot feel the emotional weight of obligation (Emotional). And cannot feel obligation because there is nothing to commit to. Motivational void + emotional flatness = total volitional collapse.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Emotional allergy → "I don't feel why this matters" → cannot sustain commitment
- Commitment-failure → "Nothing I do matters" → deeper emotional flatness

**Break point:** Introduce TINY obligations with immediate emotional resonance. "Hold this for one hour. Notice how it feels to keep your word."

---

### 6.3 The Dogmatic Enforcer (Willpower × Moral, both dark-addiction)

**Loop:** Rigid duty-will + rigid conventional morality = fundamentalist enforcement. "The code is absolute AND I will enforce it absolutely." Will serves moral rigidity; moral rigidity justifies will-slavery.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Vow-slavery → "I must enforce the moral code" → moral dark-addiction
- Moral rigidity → "The code demands absolute will" → more vow-slavery

**Break point:** Present situations where the code requires MERCY, not enforcement. "The code says: know when to hold and when to release."

---

### 6.4 The Loyal Isolate (Willpower × Interpersonal, dark-addiction × dark-allergy)

**Loop:** Absolute commitment to the code (Willpower) + inability to coordinate outside the code (Interpersonal) = isolated duty-fulfilment. Holds obligations perfectly but cannot form new relational bonds outside the existing code-structure.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Vow-slavery → "My only relationships are defined by the code" → interpersonal rigidity
- Interpersonal dark-allergy → "I cannot form bonds outside the code" → more code-dependence

**Break point:** Present relational opportunities that are CONSISTENT with the code but not defined by it. "The code permits friendship. Can you form one?"

### Dependencies
- **Supports:** Moral/Amber (sustained commitment enables vow-keeping); Cognitive/Amber (duty-will enables rule-mastery)
- **Depends on:** Somatic/Amber (embodied discipline grounds duty-will); Emotional/Amber (felt obligation fuels sustained commitment)

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive revelation order

1. **Vow-declaration** → Dark-allergy surfaces first (cannot commit = immediately visible)
2. **Vow-holding across time** → Dark-addiction surfaces (compulsive continuation vs. healthy maintenance)
3. **Low-motivation persistence** → Dark-allergy deepens (abandonment when motivation drops)
4. **Temptation-resistance** → Dark-addiction deepens (cannot accept release even when appropriate)
5. **Code-examination** → Golden-allergy surfaces (refusal to question the code)
6. **Strategic-planning tasks** → Golden-addiction surfaces (bypasses duty for rational planning)

### 7.2 Per-modality surfacing

| Modality | Dark-Addiction signal | Dark-Allergy signal | Golden-Addiction signal | Golden-Allergy signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | Continues past fatigue signals; cannot stop | Abandons at first difficulty drop | Attempts strategic optimisation of simple vow | Avoids meta-cognitive vow-examination |
| Language-reflective | Absolute duty-language; "I have no choice" | "I changed my mind" / "It didn't feel right" | Rational planning language for simple commitments | Refuses to discuss WHY the vow matters |
| Scenario-choice | Chooses duty regardless of context | Chooses immediate reward over commitment | Chooses rational analysis over simple obligation | Chooses duty but refuses to examine it |
| Embodied-somatic | Body forced through form past pain | Body abandons form at first discomfort | Attempts complex technique without basic form | Body holds form but freezes at novel demands |
| Strategic-planning | Over-plans obligations; cannot adapt | Cannot plan; impulsive execution | Plans at Orange complexity without Amber foundation | Refuses to plan beyond current code |
| Social-cooperative | Enforces code on group | Cannot coordinate through shared obligations | Dismisses group code as "irrational" | Defers all reasoning to group code |
| Immersive-rpg | Follows world-obligations obsessively | Ignores obligations; chaotic | Attempts rational meta-game without basic commitment | Stays in known obligation-structures; refuses new ones |

### 7.3 Cross-validation rules
- Deterministic high + Language-reflective low = implicit duty without metacognition (normal early)
- Language-reflective high + Deterministic low = verbal performance without sustained capacity (golden-addiction)
- Social-cooperative high + Deterministic low = group-dependent commitment (communion-addiction)
- Strategic-planning high + vow-holding low = rational bypass of duty-will (golden-addiction)
