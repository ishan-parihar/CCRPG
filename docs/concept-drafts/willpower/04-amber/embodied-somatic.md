# Willpower / Amber — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as the site of duty — how the player's physical engagement reveals the embodied quality of their commitment. Duty-will that is genuinely integrated lives in the body; duty-will that is performed or bypassed does not.
> **Why this axis for this module:** The body cannot lie about commitment. Sustained physical effort, posture-holding under obligation, and breath-regulated persistence reveal whether duty-will is genuinely embodied or merely conceptual.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sentinel's Post"
- **Core mechanic:** The player holds a physical post — sustained input (held button/touch), breath-regulated effort, and posture-maintenance — representing the embodied experience of duty. The body must hold what the will has sworn. Fatigue is real; the choice to continue is real.
- **Duration:** 3-8 minutes per session (post-duration scales with demonstrated capacity)
- **Internal progression:** Taking the Post → The Long Watch → The Body's Protest → The Breath of Duty → The Post Held

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The SOMATIC dimension of duty-will — whether commitment is genuinely embodied or merely conceptual. A player who holds vows verbally (language-reflective) but cannot sustain physical effort has a mind-body split in their duty-will.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike deterministic (which measures cognitive vow-holding), this modality measures whether the will is GROUNDED IN THE BODY. Unlike scenario-choice (which measures decision quality), this modality measures the physical texture of sustained commitment.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** The vow-slave's body is rigid and cannot rest. The oath-breaker's body abandons the post at first discomfort. The premature strategist's body is disconnected from the effort. The code-clinger's body holds the known post but freezes at novel physical demands.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates embodied duty-will — sustained physical commitment that is neither compulsive nor abandoned, grounded in breath and body-awareness.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Sentinel's Post: a physical-interface game where the player holds a post (sustained touch/button) while managing breath rhythm and responding to disturbances. The post represents the duty; the body represents the will. Aesthetic: Amber-stage guard duty — stone walls, night watch, the weight of responsibility in the body.

### Interaction
- **Taking the Post (1-5):** Simple sustained hold. "Place your hand on the post. Hold it." Tests basic physical commitment.
- **The Long Watch (5-15):** Extended hold with breath-regulation. "Hold the post. Breathe with the rhythm. The watch is long." Tests duty-persistence through low-motivation periods.
- **The Body's Protest (15-30):** Fatigue signals appear. "Your body wants to rest. The post must be held. What do you choose?" Tests fatigue-through capacity.
- **The Breath of Duty (30-50):** Breath-gated effort. "The post holds when you breathe with it. Breathe out — the post is yours. Breathe in — the post is the duty's." Tests breath-will integration.
- **The Post Held (50+):** Full integration: sustained hold + breath-regulation + fatigue-through + disturbance-response simultaneously.

### Feedback
- Post held through fatigue → "The body protests. The will holds. The post stands. This is duty in the flesh."
- Post abandoned → "The body rested. The post was left. Notice: was this sovereign rest or impulse-abandonment? The sentinel asks."
- Rigid holding (no breath) → "The post is held but the body is stone. Duty breathes. Can you hold AND breathe?"
- Compulsive continuation → "The watch is complete. The post can be set down. Rest is not abandonment — it is the completion of duty."
- Novel post refused → "A new post is offered. Your body knows the old one. Can it learn the new?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Hold duration: 30 seconds → 2 minutes → 5 minutes → 10 minutes
- Breath-regulation complexity: simple rhythm → variable rhythm → breath-gated actions
- Fatigue-signal intensity: mild → moderate → strong → overwhelming
- Disturbance frequency: rare → occasional → frequent → continuous
- Post novelty: familiar → slightly novel → significantly novel

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Taking the Post | 1-5 | Simple sustained hold |
| The Long Watch | 5-15 | Extended hold with breath-regulation |
| The Body's Protest | 15-30 | Fatigue-through capacity |

## 4. Item Pool

- **Post-holding tasks:** Sustained input challenges of varying duration — 25+
- **Breath-regulation tasks:** Breath-gated effort challenges — 20+
- **Fatigue-through tasks:** Effort-continuation past fatigue signals — 20+
- **Disturbance-response tasks:** Maintaining hold while responding to interruptions — 15+
- **Novel-post tasks:** Unfamiliar physical commitment challenges — 15+

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input types:** Hold (sustained touch/button); breath-sensor or rhythm-tap (breath-regulation); tap (disturbance-response)
- **Timing:** Hold duration tracked (ms precision); breath-rhythm measured; fatigue-signal timing recorded; abandonment latency measured
- **NPC/AI:** Adaptive hold-duration system; fatigue-signal calibration; breath-rhythm detection; disturbance-frequency adaptation; shadow-signal accumulator
- **LLM:** None — fully somatic scoring
- **State persistence:** Full hold history; abandonment patterns; fatigue-through rates; breath-regulation accuracy; disturbance-response quality; cross-session embodied commitment tracking; drive/shadow signal accumulation; checkpoint position
