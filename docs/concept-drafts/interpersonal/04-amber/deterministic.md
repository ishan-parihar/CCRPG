# Interpersonal / Amber — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Objective measurement of role-based coordination — role-reading accuracy, code-coordination quality, loyalty-maintenance, and conflict-resolution through the code.
> **Why this axis for this module:** Role-based coordination is measurable. Whether a player accurately reads others' roles, coordinates through shared obligations, and maintains loyalty across time are all distinguishable through objective tasks — the ground truth for all other modalities.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Order's Ledger"
- **Core mechanic:** The player encounters role-based coordination tasks: identify others' roles from behavioural cues, execute multi-step code-based coordination, maintain loyalty across adversity, and resolve conflicts by appealing to the code. Binary, objective, no social mediation.
- **Duration:** 3-6 minutes per session
- **Internal progression:** Role Recognition → Code-Coordination → Loyalty Under Pressure → Conflict-Through-Code → Full Role-Coordination

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** Pure role-coordination challenge stripped of language, narrative, and social context. The role is presented; the player reads it or doesn't. The code is given; the player coordinates through it or doesn't. Ground truth.

**How this differs from other modalities:** No language (unlike language-reflective). No narrative ambiguity (unlike scenario-choice). No body (unlike embodied-somatic). No planning (unlike strategic-planning). No live group (unlike social-cooperative). No free-play (unlike immersive-rpg). JUST: here is the role. Here is the code. Coordinate.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** The RAW capacity. A player who talks well about group-belonging but fails role-reading here has golden-addiction. A player who performs coordination in social contexts but fails here has group-dependent capacity.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates role-based coordination — accurate, flexible, code-grounded — in pure psychophysical terms.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Order's Ledger: a codex-style interface presenting role-coordination tasks. Each task is binary and objective. Aesthetic: Amber-stage order — duty-rosters, role-assignments, the systematic structure of group obligation.

### Interaction
- **Role Recognition (1-5):** "This person bows to the elder and defers to the healer. What is their role?" Multiple choice. Tests role-reading from behavioural cues.
- **Code-Coordination (5-15):** "Your role is guardian. The healer's role is care. The messenger's role is speed. Coordinate a 3-step response to the threat." Tests code-based multi-step coordination.
- **Loyalty Under Pressure (15-30):** Loyalty-maintenance tasks across adversity. "Your companion failed. Do you maintain the alliance?" Tracks loyalty-holding across multiple interactions.
- **Conflict-Through-Code (30-50):** "Two members dispute. The code says X. Apply the code." Tests conflict-resolution through code-appeal rather than power or transaction.
- **Full Role-Coordination (50+):** All dimensions simultaneously: role-reading + coordination + loyalty + conflict-resolution.

### Feedback
- Correct role-reading → "The role is seen. The code is understood. The order functions."
- Coordination failure → "The steps were out of order. The code has a sequence. Look again."
- Loyalty broken → "The alliance was abandoned. The code asks: was this proportional? Or was this impulse?"
- Code-bypass in conflict → "The conflict was resolved by force. The code was available. Why was it not used?"
- Enforcement over coordination → "The code was enforced, not applied. The difference matters. Enforcement controls; coordination serves."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Role complexity: 2 roles → 4 roles → 6 roles across contexts
- Coordination steps: 2-step → 3-step → 4-step
- Loyalty adversity: minor → moderate → severe
- Conflict complexity: 2-party → 3-party → multi-party

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Role Recognition | 1-5 | Basic role-reading from behavioural cues |
| Code-Coordination | 5-15 | Multi-step code-based coordination |
| Loyalty Under Pressure | 15-30 | Loyalty-maintenance across adversity |
| Conflict-Through-Code | 30-50 | Code-based conflict resolution |
| Full Role-Coordination | 50+ | All dimensions simultaneously |

---

## 4. Item Pool

- **Role-recognition items:** Behavioural cue sets requiring role identification — 30+
- **Code-coordination tasks:** Multi-step role-based coordination sequences — 25+
- **Loyalty-maintenance tasks:** Alliance-holding across adversity scenarios — 20+
- **Conflict-resolution tasks:** Code-based dispute resolution scenarios — 20+
- **Role-boundary tasks:** Role-appropriate limit assertion and respect — 15+

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Input types:** Tap (role selection, coordination step); drag (sequence ordering); hold (loyalty confirmation)
- **Timing:** Role-reading latency tracked; coordination accuracy measured; loyalty-holding duration tracked; conflict-resolution quality scored
- **NPC/AI:** Adaptive role complexity; coordination sequence calibration; loyalty-adversity scaling; conflict complexity adaptation; shadow-signal accumulator
- **LLM:** None — fully deterministic scoring
- **State persistence:** Full task history; role-reading accuracy; coordination quality; loyalty-maintenance rates; conflict-resolution patterns; cross-session stability; drive/shadow signal accumulation; checkpoint position
