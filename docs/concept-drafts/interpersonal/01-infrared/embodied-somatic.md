# Interpersonal × Infrared — Embodied-Somatic Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** Nearness Field
- **Modality:** Embodied-Somatic (body in proximity)
- **Unique Axis:** Physical response to another's nearness — co-regulation through body, not mind
- **Core Loop:** A presence approaches at variable speed → player's avatar breathes/pulses → player regulates their avatar's state to match the proximity → co-regulation emerges

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Proximity-Body Response
The other-presence moves closer/farther. Player's avatar has a visible breath-pulse that accelerates with proximity. Player must regulate (tap-rhythm to slow breath) to maintain calm-in-nearness. Measures: breath-regulation accuracy, proximity-tolerance threshold.

### 2.2 Co-Regulation Sync
When player's breath-pulse is regulated, the other-presence's pulse begins to sync. Player must maintain regulation long enough for sync to establish. Measures: sync-onset latency, sync-duration, regulation-under-proximity.

### 2.3 Proximity Gradient
- Zone 1 (far): No regulation needed — baseline
- Zone 2 (mid): Mild acceleration — easy regulation
- Zone 3 (near): Strong acceleration — effortful regulation
- Zone 4 (intimate): Maximum acceleration — full capacity required

### 2.4 Adaptive Difficulty
- Staircase on approach speed (faster = less preparation time)
- Staircase on zone-4 duration (longer intimate proximity)
- Staircase on other-presence's pulse irregularity (harder to sync with)

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Pulls avatar toward presence, cannot maintain zone boundaries, seeks zone-4 immediately |
| DAll (Avoidance) | Cannot regulate in zone 3-4, retreats, breath-pulse spikes uncontrollably at nearness |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Attempts sync before own regulation is established — forces co-regulation prematurely |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Regulates perfectly but blocks sync — maintains own rhythm, refuses shared pulse |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Tolerates increasing proximity without retreat (vertical openness) |
| Agape | Accepts the other's approach-pace without rushing or resisting |
| Agency | Maintains own breath-regulation regardless of other's state |
| Communion | Allows sync to emerge naturally when both are regulated |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Proximity-tolerance balanced against pace-acceptance
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Self-regulation-stability balanced against sync-allowance

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
Another body approaches yours. Your body responds. Can you stay regulated? Can you allow co-regulation?

### 5.2 Experience
The player discovers their somatic-relational pattern: does the body fuse? flee? force? fortify? The felt-sense of regulated-nearness develops through embodied repetition.

### 5.3 Integration
The player can be physically near another presence while maintaining self-regulation AND allowing shared rhythm. The somatic foundation of all intimacy.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each proximity-cycle (approach → regulate → sync → release) is a checkpoint. Theta-decay on 10-day half-life.
