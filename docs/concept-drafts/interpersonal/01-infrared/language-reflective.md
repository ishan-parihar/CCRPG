# Interpersonal × Infrared — Language-Reflective Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** Echo Warmth
- **Modality:** Language-Reflective (pre-verbal at this stage)
- **Unique Axis:** Pre-verbal co-vocalisation — sound-matching with another presence, proto-turn-taking
- **Core Loop:** A presence emits a simple tone/hum → player echoes it → presence responds to the echo → a proto-dialogue of sound emerges

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Sound-Matching
The other-presence emits a tone (pitch + duration). Player reproduces it via tap-hold (duration) and vertical position (pitch). No words — pure sonic co-presence. Measures: pitch accuracy, duration accuracy, onset latency.

### 2.2 Proto-Turn-Taking
After matching, the presence waits. Player must wait too — then the presence emits again. The rhythm of emit-wait-emit-wait establishes turn structure. Measures: premature-response rate, wait tolerance, turn-rhythm stability.

### 2.3 Co-Vocalisation Drift
The presence gradually shifts its tone across trials. Player must track the drift while maintaining turn structure. Measures: drift-tracking accuracy, turn-structure maintenance under change.

### 2.4 Adaptive Difficulty
- Staircase on pitch-discrimination threshold
- Staircase on wait-duration (longer pauses between turns)
- Staircase on drift-rate of tone changes

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Cannot tolerate the wait — fills silence, overlaps turns, panic-vocalises during pauses |
| DAll (Avoidance) | Refuses to echo, long onset latency, minimal sound production, lets turns expire |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Adds complexity to echoes (embellishes), initiates rather than responds, imposes melody |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Echoes perfectly but refuses drift-tracking — locks into first tone, won't follow the other's change |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Willingness to echo (reach toward the other's sound) |
| Agape | Accepts the other's tone without correcting it |
| Agency | Produces own echo clearly, doesn't dissolve into murmur |
| Communion | Maintains turn-structure, co-creates the rhythm |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Echo-willingness balanced against acceptance-of-other's-tone
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Clarity-of-own-production balanced against turn-rhythm fidelity

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
A sound appears from another. It asks nothing except to be heard and returned. The simplest possible interpersonal act: I hear you, I echo you.

### 5.2 Experience
The player discovers their sonic-relational pattern: do they flood the space? go silent? embellish? rigidify? The felt-sense of vocal co-presence emerges.

### 5.3 Integration
Healthy proto-turn-taking becomes natural — hear, wait, echo, wait. The foundation of all future dialogue is laid in pure sound.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each turn-sequence (6-10 exchanges) is a checkpoint. Session length is player-determined. Theta-decay on 10-day half-life.
