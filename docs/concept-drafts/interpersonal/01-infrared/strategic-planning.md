# Interpersonal × Infrared — Strategic-Planning Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** Approach-and-Settle
- **Modality:** Strategic-Planning (basic proximity-sequencing)
- **Unique Axis:** 2-step co-presence sequences — approach THEN settle, move THEN wait — the simplest possible planning in relational space
- **Core Loop:** Player must execute a 2-step sequence (move toward presence → hold position) to achieve co-presence. Premature or misordered steps fail.

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Sequence Presentation
A presence appears at a location. A path-indicator shows: step 1 (approach to mid-zone), step 2 (settle/hold). Player must execute in order. Measures: sequence-accuracy, step-timing, premature-action rate.

### 2.2 Sequence Complexity
- Level 1: Approach → Settle (2 steps)
- Level 2: Wait → Approach → Settle (3 steps, patience first)
- Level 3: Approach → Settle → Adjust (3 steps, presence moves slightly)
- Level 4: Wait → Approach → Settle → Adjust (4 steps maximum at Infrared)

### 2.3 Timing Windows
Each step has a timing window. Too early = premature (GA signal). Too late = avoidant (DAll signal). Correct timing = within window. Measures: timing-error direction and magnitude.

### 2.4 Adaptive Difficulty
- Staircase on sequence length (2→4 steps)
- Staircase on timing-window width (narrower = harder)
- Staircase on presence-movement unpredictability (adjust steps harder)

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Skips wait-steps, rushes approach, cannot hold settle-position (moves closer) |
| DAll (Avoidance) | Delays approach-step beyond window, over-holds wait-steps, never reaches settle |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Executes steps out of order, adds unasked steps (tries to "do more"), skips settle |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Executes approach perfectly but refuses adjust-steps — won't adapt to presence's movement |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Initiates approach-step willingly within window |
| Agape | Holds settle-step patiently, accepts the wait |
| Agency | Executes each step distinctly, doesn't blur steps together |
| Communion | Adjusts when presence moves — follows the shared sequence |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Approach-willingness balanced against settle-patience
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Step-distinctness balanced against adjust-responsiveness

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
Co-presence requires a sequence: you cannot just BE there — you must approach, then settle. The simplest plan in relational space.

### 5.2 Experience
The player discovers their sequencing pattern: do they rush? delay? skip steps? rigidify? The felt-sense of ordered-approach-to-another develops.

### 5.3 Integration
The player can plan a simple relational approach — move toward, then be still. The foundation of all future relational strategy: approach is not instant, it has steps.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each sequence-block (4-6 sequences) is a checkpoint. Theta-decay on 10-day half-life.
