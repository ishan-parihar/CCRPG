# Interpersonal × Infrared — Deterministic Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** Presence Pulse
- **Modality:** Deterministic (objective measurement, fixed mechanics)
- **Unique Axis:** Other-detection latency, proximity response accuracy, attunement timing
- **Core Loop:** A presence appears → player detects it → player responds with correct proximity behaviour → presence modulates → player tracks modulation

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Detection Task
A warm glow (other-presence) fades into the field at variable distances. Player taps/responds when they detect it. Measures: detection latency (ms), false-alarm rate, miss rate.

### 2.2 Proximity Response
Once detected, the presence pulses at a rhythm. Player must match proximity — move closer when pulse strengthens, hold distance when pulse steadies, withdraw when pulse dims. Measures: proximity-accuracy (distance error), response lag.

### 2.3 Attunement Tracking
The presence shifts its pulse-rate gradually. Player must track the shift without overshooting. Measures: tracking smoothness, overshoot frequency, attunement duration before drift.

### 2.4 Adaptive Difficulty
- Staircase on detection threshold (glow opacity)
- Staircase on proximity-response window (timing tolerance)
- Staircase on pulse-rate change speed

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Moves too close, cannot maintain distance when pulse dims, proximity error biased toward fusion |
| DAll (Avoidance) | High detection latency, maintains excessive distance, withdraws before pulse dims |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Responds before detection is confirmed (anticipatory), imposes rhythm rather than tracking |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Detects accurately but refuses proximity modulation, stays at fixed distance regardless of pulse |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Willingness to approach when pulse invites |
| Agape | Accepts the presence's rhythm without forcing own |
| Agency | Maintains self-position, doesn't collapse into other |
| Communion | Tracks shared pulse-field, modulates with it |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Ratio of approach-willingness to acceptance-of-rhythm across trials
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Ratio of boundary-maintenance to shared-tracking across trials

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
The presence appears and demands nothing — only exists. The player's body-response IS the data.

### 5.2 Experience
Through repeated trials, the player discovers their default: do they cling? avoid? impose? freeze? The felt-sense of proximity-to-another emerges as a skill, not a concept.

### 5.3 Integration
Healthy co-presence becomes automatic — detect, approach-appropriately, track, maintain boundary. The player can BE WITH another presence without fusion or flight.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each trial block (8-12 trials) is a checkpoint. Player can leave after any block. Theta-decay ensures return within 10-day half-life.
