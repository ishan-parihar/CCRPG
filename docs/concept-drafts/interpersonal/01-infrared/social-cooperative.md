# Interpersonal × Infrared — Social-Cooperative Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** Shared Stillness
- **Modality:** Social-Cooperative (shared presence)
- **Unique Axis:** Basic synchrony with another — co-existing in shared space, mutual pulse-matching without communication
- **Core Loop:** Player and other-presence each pulse independently → player adjusts toward shared rhythm → when rhythms align, the shared field glows → maintaining alignment = co-presence achieved

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Dual-Pulse Display
Screen shows two pulse-circles: player's (controlled by tap-rhythm) and other's (autonomous). Both pulse at slightly different rates. Player must adjust their tap-rhythm toward the other's without the other adjusting toward them.

### 2.2 Synchrony Threshold
When pulses align within tolerance window, a shared-field glow appears between them. Player must maintain alignment for increasing durations. Measures: sync-onset time, sync-duration, drift-correction frequency.

### 2.3 Mutual Adjustment (Advanced)
At higher levels, the other-presence ALSO adjusts slightly toward the player — creating genuine mutual synchrony. Player must find the meeting-point rather than simply matching. Measures: meeting-point accuracy, over-adjustment rate.

### 2.4 Adaptive Difficulty
- Staircase on other's pulse variability (more erratic = harder to match)
- Staircase on sync-duration requirement (longer holds)
- Staircase on mutual-adjustment ratio (more mutual = more complex)

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Over-adjusts — abandons own rhythm entirely, copies other exactly, loses self-pulse |
| DAll (Avoidance) | Refuses adjustment — maintains own rhythm rigidly, never achieves sync |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Forces sync by rapid oscillation — "finds" alignment artificially without genuine rhythm-shift |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Achieves sync briefly but breaks it deliberately — cannot sustain shared field |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Adjusts toward other's rhythm (willingness to move toward) |
| Agape | Accepts the other's rhythm as valid (doesn't try to make other match self) |
| Agency | Maintains own pulse-identity while adjusting (doesn't dissolve) |
| Communion | Sustains shared-field once achieved (holds the co-presence) |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Adjustment-willingness balanced against rhythm-acceptance
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Pulse-identity-maintenance balanced against shared-field-duration

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
Two rhythms exist. Neither is wrong. Can they find a shared beat? No words, no negotiation — only pulse.

### 5.2 Experience
The player discovers their synchrony pattern: do they dissolve? resist? force? flee? The felt-sense of genuine shared rhythm — neither domination nor submission — emerges.

### 5.3 Integration
The player can co-exist in shared rhythmic space — adjusting without losing self, holding without clinging. The somatic foundation of all cooperation.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each sync-cycle (achieve → hold → release → re-achieve) is a checkpoint. Theta-decay on 10-day half-life.
