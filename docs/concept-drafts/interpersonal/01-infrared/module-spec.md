# Interpersonal × Infrared — Module Specification

## 1. Module Identity

- **Line:** Interpersonal (LL quadrant home)
- **Stage:** Infrared / Archaic (Stage 1)
- **Energy Ray:** Red
- **Core Capacity:** Pre-social co-presence — detecting and responding to another presence
- **Developmental Essence:** The other is not a person but a PRESENCE. No theory-of-mind, no communication, no negotiation. Only: can the organism register that something-else-alive is here, and modulate its state accordingly?
- **Prototype:** Mother-infant bond — the first interpersonal experience is shared warmth, not shared meaning

## 2. Shadow Diagnostics

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction (DA): Clinging
Cannot separate from the other presence. Fusion, panic when alone, symbiotic attachment. The boundary between self-presence and other-presence has never formed.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy (DAll): Avoidance
Refuses co-presence entirely. Withdraws from all other-contact. Isolation as default state. The organism treats other-presence as threat.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction (GA): Premature Relating
Attempts relational engagement (Magenta-level) without basic co-presence capacity. "Connects" without genuine attunement — skips presence for performance.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy (GAll): Presence Fortress
Co-presence capacity is present but refuses relational emergence. Terrified of the other becoming a person. Locks into Infrared to avoid Magenta.

## 3. Drive Dynamics

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaches toward other-presence (vertical pull upward) | GA — leaps past presence into pseudo-relating |
| Agape | Accepts current co-presence capacity without shame | DA — dissolves self into other to avoid separation |
| Agency | Maintains self-boundary while in proximity | GAll — fortifies boundary into wall |
| Communion | Opens to shared field with other-presence | DAll — seals off shared field entirely |

## 4. Compound Shadows

1. **The Fused Body** (Interpersonal DA × Somatic DA): Clinging + hyperarousal = cannot separate body from other's body
2. **The Isolated Numb** (Interpersonal DAll × Emotional DAll): Avoidance + affect-numbness = complete relational shutdown
3. **The Premature Connector** (Interpersonal GA × Cognitive GA): Premature relating + premature symbolism = "reads" others without basic presence
4. **The Lone Survivor** (Interpersonal GAll × Willpower GAll): Refuses relational emergence + refuses intentional emergence = survives alone
