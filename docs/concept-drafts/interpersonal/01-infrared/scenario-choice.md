# Interpersonal × Infrared — Scenario-Choice Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** Toward or Away
- **Modality:** Scenario-Choice (presence-based decisions)
- **Unique Axis:** Approach/avoid another presence based on safety cues — no language, no reasoning, only felt-sense
- **Core Loop:** A presence appears with ambient cues (warmth/cold, light/dark, steady/erratic) → player chooses approach or avoid → outcome reveals whether cue-reading was accurate

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Safety-Cue Presentation
Each scenario presents a presence with 2-3 ambient signals: temperature glow (warm=safe, cold=threat), movement pattern (steady=safe, erratic=threat), luminosity (soft=safe, harsh=threat). Player has binary choice: approach or avoid.

### 2.2 Cue Complexity Progression
- Level 1: Single cue (warm glow = approach)
- Level 2: Two cues, congruent (warm + steady = approach)
- Level 3: Two cues, incongruent (warm + erratic = ambiguous — must weigh)
- Level 4: Three cues with one misleading

### 2.3 Outcome Feedback
Correct approach → presence settles, shared warmth. Correct avoid → presence passes, player safe. Incorrect approach → startle (mild). Incorrect avoid → missed warmth (subtle loss signal).

### 2.4 Adaptive Difficulty
- Staircase on cue-ambiguity (congruence ratio)
- Staircase on presentation duration (less time to read cues)
- Staircase on number of simultaneous presences

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Approaches regardless of cues — cannot avoid even when signals are threatening |
| DAll (Avoidance) | Avoids regardless of cues — cannot approach even when signals are safe |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Approaches quickly without reading cues — fast but inaccurate, impulsive toward connection |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Reads cues accurately but defaults to avoid — correct detection, refused approach |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Approaches when cues are genuinely safe (vertical reach) |
| Agape | Accepts ambiguous cues without forcing a decision prematurely |
| Agency | Avoids when cues are genuinely threatening (self-preservation) |
| Communion | Approaches with appropriate timing (not too fast, not too slow) |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Approach-rate-when-safe balanced against tolerance-of-ambiguity
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Avoid-rate-when-threatened balanced against approach-timing

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
Presences appear. Some are safe, some are not. The player must feel the difference — not think it.

### 5.2 Experience
Patterns emerge: does the player approach everything? avoid everything? rush? freeze? The felt-sense of safety-discrimination develops through repetition.

### 5.3 Integration
Accurate presence-reading becomes embodied — the player can feel safe/unsafe in the presence of another without needing to reason about it. Foundation for all future social discernment.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each scenario block (5-8 choices) is a checkpoint. Theta-decay ensures return within half-life.
