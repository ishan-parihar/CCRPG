# Interpersonal × Infrared — Immersive-RPG Modality

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** The Other in the Dark
- **Modality:** Immersive-RPG (ecological co-presence)
- **Unique Axis:** Whether other-awareness appears spontaneously in free-play — unstructured environment where presences exist and the player's natural relational behaviour is observed
- **Core Loop:** Player explores a dim, warm environment → other presences exist in the space → no instructions given → player's spontaneous approach/avoid/ignore behaviour IS the assessment

## 2. Mechanic Specification

### 2.1 Ecological Environment
A soft-lit space with ambient warmth. Player avatar moves freely. Other presences (warm glows, gentle pulses) inhabit the space. No objectives stated. No prompts. The environment simply IS.

### 2.2 Behavioural Observation
System tracks without prompting: proximity-to-others over time, approach frequency, avoidance frequency, time-in-shared-space, time-in-isolation, movement patterns relative to presences.

### 2.3 Presence Variety
- Stationary presences (always there, passive)
- Wandering presences (move through space, may approach player)
- Responsive presences (glow brighter when player is near)
- Retreating presences (dim when player approaches too fast)

### 2.4 Adaptive Environment
- Density of presences adjusts based on player's comfort-zone
- Responsive presences become more/less reactive based on player engagement
- New presences introduced gradually, never overwhelming

## 3. Shadow Probes

| Shadow | Probe Signature |
|---|---|
| DA (Clinging) | Follows one presence constantly, panics (erratic movement) when it wanders away |
| DAll (Avoidance) | Stays in empty zones, moves away when presences approach, minimises shared-space time |
| GA (Premature Relating) | Rapidly moves between presences, "collects" proximity without settling, high approach-count but low dwell-time |
| GAll (Presence Fortress) | Occupies space near presences but never closes final distance, maintains fixed perimeter |

## 4. Drive Scoring

| Drive | Healthy Signal |
|---|---|
| Eros | Spontaneously approaches presences (natural curiosity toward other) |
| Agape | Remains comfortable when presences approach uninvited |
| Agency | Moves away from presences when needed without distress |
| Communion | Dwells in shared space — co-exists without agenda |

### 4.1 Dual-Domain Scoring
- **Vertical (Eros/Agape):** Spontaneous-approach-rate balanced against comfort-with-uninvited-approach
- **Horizontal (Agency/Communion):** Healthy-withdrawal-capacity balanced against shared-space-dwell-time

## 5. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

### 5.1 Catalyst
No instructions. No goals. Just a space with others in it. What does the player do when no one is watching and nothing is required?

### 5.2 Experience
The player's ecological relational pattern reveals itself: seeker? avoider? collector? fortress? The truest data comes from unstructured behaviour.

### 5.3 Integration
Over sessions, the player's spontaneous behaviour shifts — more balanced approach/dwell/withdraw patterns emerge naturally. The environment rewards nothing — growth is intrinsic.

### 5.4 Infinite Checkpoint
Each session (any duration) is a checkpoint. The environment persists. Theta-decay on 10-day half-life ensures return.
