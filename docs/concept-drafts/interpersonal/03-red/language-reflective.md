# Interpersonal / Red — Language-Reflective Game Concept

> **Axis:** The language-reflective axis probes interpersonal intelligence through VERBAL and METACOGNITIVE channels — can the player articulate what others want? Can they explain WHY someone behaves as they do? Can they predict others' actions through verbal reasoning? Can they reflect on their OWN interpersonal patterns?
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Red:** At Red, verbal interpersonal reasoning is CONCRETE and EGOCENTRIC: "He wants my stuff because he's greedy." "She helps me because I'm strong." The language-reflective axis tests whether the player can THINK ABOUT others' minds verbally — and cross-validates against deterministic (if verbal sophistication >> reading accuracy, golden-addiction is active).

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The War-Tongue"
- **Core mechanic:** The player verbally models others' minds — naming intentions, explaining behaviours, predicting actions, and reflecting on their own interpersonal patterns. LLM evaluates the quality of verbal interpersonal reasoning.
- **Duration:** 3-6 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** Naming Intent → Explaining Behaviour → Predicting Action → Teaching Others → Reflecting on Self

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Tell me what they want. Tell me WHY. Tell me what they'll do NEXT." The game asks the player to VERBALISE their interpersonal intelligence — making implicit social cognition explicit.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's verbal model of others reveals their interpersonal structure. Do they see others as threats (dark-allergy)? As objects to control (dark-addiction)? As complex beings they can't simply read (golden-addiction)? As irrelevant (golden-allergy)?
- *Emergent:* Can they articulate more nuanced models? Predict more accurately? Reflect on their own patterns?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE verbal modelling that matches behavioural reality. Sophistication without accuracy = golden-addiction. Accuracy without reflection = incomplete integration.

**Successful integration:** The player verbally models others' intentions accurately, explains behaviour in concrete causal terms, predicts actions correctly, and can reflect on their own interpersonal patterns.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** "Describe what this NPC wants." Observe whether the description is ABOUT the NPC or about the player's power over them.
- Healthy: "They want to trade — they have food, they need weapons"
- Pathological: "They want what I'll let them have"; all descriptions centre the player's dominance
- **Detection:** Whether verbal models of others centre the OTHER or the SELF's power

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** "What would YOU do in this situation?" — self-directed interpersonal reasoning.
- Healthy: Generates own interpersonal strategy with confidence
- Pathological: Cannot articulate own interpersonal approach; "I don't know, whatever works"
- **Detection:** Quality of self-directed interpersonal verbal reasoning

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** "Describe what your ally wants." Observe whether the description maintains self-other distinction.
- Healthy: "They want protection; I want resources; we can trade"
- Pathological: "They want what I want" (fusion); "We're the same" (loss of self-other boundary)
- **Detection:** Whether verbal models maintain self-other distinction

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** "Explain your alliance to a third party." — verbalising relational structure.
- Healthy: Can articulate mutual benefit clearly
- Pathological: Cannot explain why they're allied; "I don't know, they're just there"
- **Detection:** Quality of relational-structure articulation

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Simple verbal modelling vs. complex.
- Healthy: Comfortable with current verbal complexity; advances naturally
- Pathological: Demands complex interpersonal scenarios immediately; "This is too simple"
- **Detection:** Satisfaction with current verbal-modelling complexity

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Verbal predictions about NPCs with ambiguous intent.
- Healthy: Attempts prediction with appropriate uncertainty; "I think they want X because Y"
- Pathological: Claims certainty about ambiguous situations; "I KNOW exactly what they want"
- **Detection:** Confidence-accuracy calibration in verbal predictions

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Simple verbal intent-naming (obvious NPC intent).
- Healthy: Engages with full attention; names accurately
- Pathological: Dismisses; "Obviously they want X"; accuracy drops from disengagement
- **Detection:** Engagement quality on simple verbal tasks

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Spontaneous verbal modelling in non-task contexts.
- Healthy: Talks about NPCs' intentions naturally in free-play
- Pathological: Verbal interpersonal reasoning exists only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous verbal social cognition

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Dominator"
**How it surfaces:** All verbal models of others are ABOUT CONTROL. "They want to serve me." "They're afraid of me." "They'll do what I say." Every description centres the player's dominance.
- **Detection signals:** All intent-descriptions reference player's power; cannot describe others' autonomous motivations; verbal models = "what they'll do FOR me or TO me"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Isolate"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot or won't verbalise others' intentions. Responses are minimal, dismissive, or threat-projecting. "I don't know what they want." "They probably want to hurt me." "Why does it matter?"
- **Detection signals:** Minimal verbal output about others; all-threat framing; refusal to engage verbal modelling; "Others are unpredictable/dangerous"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Diplomat"
**How it surfaces:** ELABORATE verbal models that don't match reality. "I sense a complex interplay of wounded pride and suppressed need for belonging, manifesting as..." when the NPC simply wants to trade.
- **Detection signals:** Verbal sophistication >> deterministic accuracy; over-complex models of simple situations; therapy-language applied to concrete Red interactions; cannot give a ONE-WORD answer

### Golden-Allergy: "The Loyalty-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Accurate verbal models but REFUSES to verbalise relational commitment. Can say "They want to help" but cannot say "They're my ally" or "I trust them."
- **Detection signals:** Accurate intent-naming but no relational language; avoids words like "trust," "loyalty," "bond"; describes all relationships as temporary/transactional even when they're stable

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Prompt: "Describe what THEY want — not what you want FROM them. Their goal. Their need."
- Frame: "The warrior who knows what his ally truly wants can offer it. That's how you build a war-band that CHOOSES to follow."
- Reward other-centred descriptions

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Prompt with maximum scaffolding: "This NPC is smiling and offering food. What might they want? Just one guess."
- Frame: "You don't have to trust them. Just NAME what they want. Naming isn't trusting."
- Celebrate any verbal engagement with others' minds

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require ONE-WORD intent naming. "Help, harm, or trade? One word."
- Penalise elaboration; reward accuracy
- Frame: "The warrior's tongue is sharp and short. One word. True."

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Prompt relational language: "You've worked with them 5 times. What are they to you?"
- Frame: "Naming the bond doesn't create weakness. It creates clarity. A named ally is a KNOWN ally."
- Reward relational vocabulary without requiring vulnerability

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The War-Tongue: a council fire where the player speaks about others. NPCs arrive, behave, and the player must VERBALISE their reading. The aesthetic: Red-stage war-council, the warlord's advisors speaking of allies and enemies, firelit deliberation.

### Interaction
- **Naming Intent:** NPC behaves; player types/selects what they want. (1-5)
- **Explaining Behaviour:** "WHY do they want that?" Player provides causal reasoning. (5-15)
- **Predicting Action:** "What will they do NEXT?" Player predicts from verbal model. (15-30)
- **Teaching Others:** Player explains an NPC's intent to a third NPC (scaffolding another's reading). (30-50)
- **Reflecting on Self:** "What do YOU do when someone wants to help you?" Player reflects on own patterns. (50+)

### Feedback
- Accurate naming → "Your tongue speaks true. You see their heart."
- Inaccurate → "Your words miss the mark. Watch them again — what do their actions say?"
- Over-elaborate → "Too many words. The warrior's tongue is sharp. One truth. Speak it."
- Self-reflective → "You see yourself among others. That is the war-chief's wisdom."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Intent complexity: obvious → moderate → ambiguous → shifting
- Verbal demand: naming → explaining → predicting → teaching → reflecting
- NPC count: 1 → 2 → 3 (simultaneous verbal modelling)

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Naming Intent | 1-5 | Single NPC, obvious intent, one-word response |
| Explaining Behaviour | 5-15 | Causal reasoning required; "why?" |
| Predicting Action | 15-30 | Future-oriented verbal modelling |
| Teaching Others | 30-50 | Scaffolding another's interpersonal reading |
| Reflecting on Self | 50+ | Metacognitive awareness of own patterns |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **NPC scenarios for naming:** Behavioural vignettes requiring intent identification
- **Causal prompts:** "Why does X want Y?" requiring concrete reasoning
- **Prediction scenarios:** "What will X do next?" requiring verbal forecasting
- **Teaching scenarios:** Explain an NPC's intent to a naive third party
- **Self-reflection prompts:** "What do you do when...?" metacognitive questions

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ naming scenarios; 25+ causal prompts; 20+ prediction scenarios; 15+ teaching scenarios; 15+ self-reflection prompts

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All-about-control language → dark-addiction; minimal/threat language → dark-allergy
- Over-elaborate language → golden-addiction; no relational vocabulary → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Naming accuracy:** Does the verbal label match the NPC's actual intent?
- **Causal quality:** Is the explanation concrete, causal, and accurate?
- **Prediction accuracy:** Does the verbal prediction match what the NPC actually does?
- **Teaching effectiveness:** Does the third-party NPC "learn" from the player's explanation?
- **Self-reflection depth:** Quality of metacognitive awareness about own interpersonal patterns

### LLM rubric
- **Very High:** All responses are free-text evaluated by LLM against rubrics. LLM assesses accuracy, sophistication-appropriateness, drive-health signals, and shadow indicators.

### Cross-validation with deterministic
- If language-reflective score >> deterministic score: golden-addiction (talks better than reads)
- If deterministic score >> language-reflective score: capacity present but not metacognitively accessible
- Ideal: both scores within 15% of each other (integrated capacity)

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Free-text input (primary); multiple-choice for scaffolded phases; voice input (optional)

### Timing requirements
- No time pressure on verbal responses (quality over speed)
- Response latency tracked as secondary signal (very fast = possibly scripted; very slow = possibly struggling)

### NPC/AI requirements
- NPCs with defined intents that the LLM can verify against
- Ground-truth intent must be algorithmically defined (not LLM-generated) for scoring accuracy

### LLM requirements
- **Very High:** Evaluates free-text responses against rubrics; assesses sophistication level; detects shadow signals in language patterns; generates contextual feedback

### State persistence
- Verbal accuracy history; sophistication level; causal reasoning quality; prediction accuracy; self-reflection depth; language patterns (control-language, threat-language, over-elaborate, relational-avoidant); drive/shadow signals; cross-validation ratio vs. deterministic; fatigue state; checkpoint position
