# Interpersonal / Red — Module Specification Skeleton

> **Module vibration:** Egocentric-others — others as instruments of the self's will. At Red, the interpersonal field is TRANSACTIONAL: "What can you do for me? What will you do TO me?" Allies are tools. Enemies are obstacles. Betrayal logic operates: "They will betray me if it serves them, so I will betray first if it serves me." The healthy Red interpersonal capacity is: accurate modelling of others' intentions + transactional coordination + boundary assertion + first loyalty bonds (war-band).

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

### What constitutes stage-pass at Interpersonal/Red

The player demonstrates:

1. **Intent-reading accuracy:** Can identify what another agent WANTS (not what they say, what they WANT) from behavioural cues — at least 3 basic intentions (help, harm, trade)
2. **Transactional coordination:** Can execute a 2-step coordinated action with an ally ("I attack, you cover" or "I distract, you steal")
3. **Betrayal detection:** Can identify when an ally's behaviour shifts toward betrayal (inconsistency between stated and actual intent)
4. **Boundary assertion:** Can say NO to an alliance that costs more than it gives; can exit a bad deal
5. **First loyalty:** Can maintain a transactional alliance across 3+ interactions without unnecessary betrayal

### Checkpoint progression

| Checkpoint | Capacity demonstrated |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | Reads 1 NPC's basic intent (help/harm/trade); responds appropriately |
| 5-15 | Coordinates 2-step actions with 1 ally; detects obvious betrayal signals |
| 15-30 | Reads 2-3 NPCs simultaneously; maintains alliance across multiple interactions |
| 30-50 | Detects subtle betrayal; manages competing alliances; boundary assertion under pressure |
| 50+ | Full Red interpersonal mastery: reads intent, coordinates, detects betrayal, asserts boundaries, maintains loyalty — all simultaneously |

### Red ceiling (what this module does NOT measure)

- NO perspective-taking beyond "what do they want?" (that's Amber+)
- NO empathic attunement (that's Emotional line, not Interpersonal)
- NO contractual reasoning ("we agreed to X") — that's Orange
- NO multi-perspective coordination (holding 3+ viewpoints) — that's Green
- NO genuine altruism (helping without transactional expectation) — that's Amber+
- Maximum: 2-3 agents modelled simultaneously, 2-step coordination, concrete behavioural cues only

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Dominator"

**Core distortion:** Cannot interact without controlling. Every relationship is a dominance hierarchy. Others exist ONLY as objects to be manipulated, commanded, or broken. Intent-reading is weaponised exclusively for control.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: HYPER — all interactions are power contests; must win every exchange
- Communion: ABSENT — cannot join; only dominate or be dominated
- Eros: Fixated on dominance-intensity; seeks ever-more-complete control
- Agape: Cannot return to simple coordination; "Why coordinate when I can command?"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Reads others' intentions accurately but ONLY uses readings for manipulation
- Cannot allow an ally to lead, even when the ally is better suited
- Treats every NPC interaction as a dominance contest
- Alliance = "they obey me"; coordination = "they do what I say"
- Betrays allies pre-emptively ("before they betray me")
- Cannot tolerate being in a non-dominant position for any duration

**Developmental origin:** The self differentiated at Red but OVER-differentiated — others became objects, not subjects. The power-self consumed the relational-self entirely. Often: early environment where being dominated was traumatic; dominance became the only safe position.

**What it protects against:** Vulnerability. Being controlled. The terror of depending on another who might betray.

---

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Isolate"

**Core distortion:** Cannot engage others at all. Treats ALL other agents as threats. Refuses all alliance, all coordination, all reading. "I need no one. Others are dangerous. Alone is safe."

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Intact but SOLO — can act on the world but not WITH others
- Communion: BLOCKED — the relational channel is closed; others are opaque walls
- Eros: Cannot grow relationally; stuck at pre-interpersonal
- Agape: Cannot return to basic social engagement; "I was never social"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Refuses to read NPC intentions (doesn't engage the task)
- Refuses alliance even when solo is clearly disadvantageous
- Treats all coordination requests as threats or traps
- Cannot detect betrayal because they never trust enough to be betrayed
- Boundary assertion is TOTAL — no one gets in, ever
- Performs worse on multi-agent tasks than capacity would predict

**Developmental origin:** The self differentiated at Red but the interpersonal field was SO dangerous that the self withdrew entirely. Others = pain. Alone = safe. Often: early betrayal so severe that all others became threats.

**What it protects against:** Betrayal. Being used. The pain of relational engagement in a world perceived as universally hostile.

---

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Diplomat"

**Core distortion:** Performs sophisticated social behaviour (negotiation, empathy-language, conflict resolution, "I hear you" statements) WITHOUT genuine Red-level intent-reading. The FORM of advanced interpersonal capacity without the SUBSTANCE of basic Red mastery.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Performed — appears to initiate but actually follows social scripts
- Communion: Over-developed in FORM — joins everything, agrees with everyone, "validates" constantly
- Eros: Fixated on appearing advanced; cannot rest in basic transactional honesty
- Agape: Cannot return to simple "what do you want?" directness

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Uses sophisticated social language ("I understand your perspective," "Let's find a win-win") but cannot accurately identify what the NPC actually WANTS
- High verbal sophistication + low intent-reading accuracy = golden-addiction signal
- Cannot do simple transactional coordination ("I attack, you cover") because it feels "beneath them"
- Performs empathy without genuine reading; "validates" without understanding
- Conflict resolution attempts that don't address the actual conflict
- Cross-validates against deterministic: if language-reflective score >> deterministic score, golden-addiction is active

**Developmental origin:** Exposed to sophisticated social models (therapy-speak, conflict-resolution training, "emotional intelligence" culture) before Red interpersonal capacity was established. Learned the WORDS without the CAPACITY. Often: children of therapists, or adults who consumed self-help before doing the developmental work.

**What it protects against:** The rawness of Red interpersonal reality — that others are self-interested, that betrayal is real, that power dynamics exist. The premature diplomat cannot face the JUNGLE of Red interpersonal life.

---

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Loyalty-Refuser"

**Core distortion:** Can read others accurately and coordinate effectively but REFUSES loyalty, bonding, or any form of relational commitment. Treats all bonds as weakness. "Caring about allies is a vulnerability I won't accept."

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Intact — can act interpersonally with skill
- Communion: REFUSED at the golden edge — can join transactionally but refuses the BOND
- Eros: Refuses the call toward deeper relational capacity; "I don't need to grow here"
- Agape: Can do basic interpersonal but won't let it MATTER

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Accurate intent-reading; effective coordination; good betrayal detection
- But: rotates allies constantly; never maintains a bond across sessions
- Treats loyalty as "getting attached" and attachment as weakness
- Can form alliances but cannot form WAR-BANDS (the Red loyalty structure)
- Performs well on individual interpersonal tasks but fails on loyalty-maintenance tasks
- Exits alliances at the first sign of cost, even when the alliance is net-positive

**Developmental origin:** Red interpersonal capacity is PRESENT but the emergent call toward Amber (belonging, loyalty, group-identity) is REFUSED. The player can DO interpersonal but won't let it TRANSFORM them. Often: betrayal that was survived through competence, creating the belief that competence without attachment is the safest position.

**What it protects against:** The vulnerability of caring. The pain of betrayal when you've invested. The terror of belonging (which requires surrendering some sovereignty).

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

### Agency (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Uses interpersonal intelligence to pursue own goals; reads others to gain advantage; asserts boundaries
- **Pathological:** Dominates all interactions; cannot allow others to have agency; every relationship is a power contest; "I must control them"

### Agency (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Initiates alliances; proposes coordinated action; leads when appropriate
- **Pathological:** Cannot initiate interpersonal engagement; waits for others to approach; "They should come to me"

### Communion (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Joins alliances that serve mutual interest; coordinates without losing sovereignty
- **Pathological:** Merges with stronger ally; loses own goals in service of another's; "I'll do whatever they want so they don't leave"

### Communion (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Shares resources/information with allies when it serves the alliance
- **Pathological:** Cannot share; hoards all advantage; "Why would I help them?"

### Eros (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Satisfied with current alliance quality; deepens existing relationships
- **Pathological:** Constantly seeks new/better allies; discards current allies for upgrades; "There must be someone more useful"

### Eros (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Curious about more complex coordination; attempts 3-step sequences
- **Pathological:** Claims sophisticated interpersonal capacity without demonstration; "I already understand people"

### Agape (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Returns to basic interpersonal tasks with full engagement; maintains simple alliances
- **Pathological:** Dismisses basic coordination; "I'm beyond needing allies for this"

### Agape (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Interpersonal intelligence appears spontaneously in non-task contexts
- **Pathological:** Reads others only when explicitly prompted; no spontaneous social intelligence

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → dark shadows)

**For The Dominator:**
- Agape: Return to BASIC coordination (not dominance). "Can you simply coordinate? Not command — coordinate."
- Agency: Use sovereign will to CHOOSE non-dominance. "You are strong enough to let another lead."
- Mechanism: Present situations where non-dominant positions produce MORE power than dominant ones
- Integration marker: Can occupy follower position without distress; can coordinate without commanding

**For The Isolate:**
- Agape: Return to the SIMPLEST interpersonal act (reading one obvious intention). "Just look. What do they want?"
- Agency: Use sovereign will to APPROACH despite fear. "You choose to engage. No one forces you."
- Mechanism: Present ultra-safe interpersonal contexts (NPC who CANNOT betray; stakes are zero)
- Integration marker: Can read one NPC's intent; can accept one alliance; can tolerate another's presence

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → golden shadows)

**For The Premature Diplomat:**
- Eros: Reach toward GENUINE reading (not performed). "What do they ACTUALLY want? Not what you think they should want."
- Communion: Join at the REAL level (transactional honesty, not performed empathy). "Be direct. What's the deal?"
- Mechanism: Lock advancement behind ACCURACY, not sophistication. Simple correct > elaborate wrong.
- Integration marker: Intent-reading accuracy matches verbal sophistication; can do simple transactions

**For The Loyalty-Refuser:**
- Eros: Reach toward the BOND (not just the coordination). "What if you stayed? What if this alliance MATTERED?"
- Communion: Join at the COMMITMENT level (not just the transactional). "They're your war-band. Not just your tools."
- Mechanism: Make loyalty produce UNIQUE rewards unavailable to solo players or alliance-rotators
- Integration marker: Maintains alliance across 5+ interactions; experiences loss when ally is threatened

### Integration Criteria
### What "healed Interpersonal/Red" looks like

The player demonstrates ALL of the following:

1. **Accurate intent-reading:** Identifies what 2-3 agents want from behavioural cues (≥70% accuracy)
2. **Flexible positioning:** Can lead, follow, or coordinate based on context (not fixed in one position)
3. **Transactional honesty:** Engages alliances with clear mutual benefit; doesn't manipulate OR avoid
4. **Betrayal detection without paranoia:** Detects actual betrayal signals; doesn't project betrayal onto neutral behaviour
5. **Boundary assertion without isolation:** Says no when appropriate; says yes when appropriate
6. **First loyalty:** Maintains at least one alliance across 5+ interactions; experiences the alliance as meaningful (not just useful)
7. **Sovereignty within connection:** Engages the interpersonal field without losing self OR dominating others

### The integration test

Present a scenario requiring: read an ally's intent + coordinate a 2-step action + detect a third party's betrayal attempt + assert a boundary + maintain the alliance.

If the player can do ALL FIVE in a single scenario with healthy drive expression across all four drives, Interpersonal/Red is integrated.

## 5. Scoring Parameters

### Decay rate: MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Half-life:** 18 days without interpersonal engagement
- **Max decay:** 25%
- **Rationale:** Interpersonal skills atrophy faster than cognitive skills but slower than somatic. The "reading others" capacity requires ongoing practice — social intelligence that isn't exercised becomes stale. But the STRUCTURES (intent-reading, coordination patterns) persist longer than body-skills.

### Decay triggers
- No interpersonal game engagement for >7 days → decay begins
- No coordinated action with allies for >14 days → accelerated decay
- No intent-reading tasks for >21 days → maximum decay rate

### Recovery
- Single interpersonal session recovers ~40% of decayed capacity
- Full recovery requires 3-5 sessions across 7 days
- Recovery is FASTER than initial acquisition (the structures remain; only sharpness decays)

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Puppet-Master (Interpersonal dark-addiction × Cognitive healthy)

```
High cognitive capacity → reads others perfectly
  → uses readings ONLY for manipulation (interpersonal dark-addiction)
  → manipulation success reinforces cognitive-as-weapon
  → cognitive development serves domination exclusively
  → LOOP: smarter = more manipulative = more isolated from genuine connection
```

**Breaking the loop:** Cognitive capacity must be redirected toward MUTUAL benefit. Present problems that require genuine coordination (not manipulation) to solve. Make manipulation produce diminishing returns.

### 6.2 The Feral (Interpersonal dark-allergy × Emotional dark-allergy)

```
Cannot read others (interpersonal dark-allergy)
  → cannot feel others' emotions (emotional dark-allergy)
  → total social isolation reinforces both allergies
  → isolation confirms "others are dangerous/irrelevant"
  → LOOP: more isolated = less practice = less capacity = more isolation
```

**Breaking the loop:** Start with NON-HUMAN interpersonal (animal companions, simple NPCs with unmistakable signals). Build the reading muscle in safe contexts before introducing human-complexity.

### 6.3 The Charmer (Interpersonal golden-addiction × Emotional golden-addiction)

```
Performs sophisticated social behaviour (interpersonal golden-addiction)
  → performs sophisticated emotional attunement (emotional golden-addiction)
  → both performances reinforce each other
  → "I'm so emotionally intelligent and socially skilled" (without genuine capacity in either)
  → LOOP: more performance = less genuine development = more performance needed
```

**Breaking the loop:** Require MEASURABLE accuracy (deterministic tasks) not just performed sophistication. Ground both lines in objective measurement. Simple + accurate > elaborate + inaccurate.

### 6.4 The Lone Wolf (Interpersonal golden-allergy × Willpower dark-addiction)

```
Refuses all bonds (interpersonal golden-allergy)
  → channels all energy into solo power (willpower dark-addiction)
  → solo success reinforces "I don't need anyone"
  → willpower becomes the substitute for connection
  → LOOP: more solo power = less relational need = more isolation = more solo power
```

**Breaking the loop:** Present challenges that CANNOT be solved solo regardless of willpower. Make the Lone Wolf's solo power INSUFFICIENT. The only path forward requires an ally. Not as punishment — as reality.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 The diagnostic progression

Each game surfaces shadows through a specific sequence:

```
PHASE 1: INTENT-READING
  → Can they read what others want? (capacity baseline)
  → Do they read ONLY to manipulate? (dark-addiction)
  → Do they refuse to read at all? (dark-allergy)
  → Do they over-elaborate readings? (golden-addiction)
  → Do they read accurately but refuse to care? (golden-allergy)

PHASE 2: COORDINATION
  → Can they coordinate with an ally? (capacity)
  → Do they dominate the coordination? (dark-addiction)
  → Do they refuse to coordinate? (dark-allergy)
  → Do they over-complicate coordination? (golden-addiction)
  → Do they coordinate but refuse commitment? (golden-allergy)

PHASE 3: BETRAYAL DETECTION
  → Can they detect betrayal? (capacity)
  → Do they see betrayal everywhere? (dark-addiction projection)
  → Do they never detect it because they never trust? (dark-allergy)
  → Do they "understand" betrayal without detecting it? (golden-addiction)
  → Do they detect it and immediately exit without repair? (golden-allergy)

PHASE 4: BOUNDARY ASSERTION
  → Can they say no? (capacity)
  → Is every interaction a boundary contest? (dark-addiction)
  → Is the boundary total and impermeable? (dark-allergy)
  → Do they "set boundaries" using therapy-language without actual assertion? (golden-addiction)
  → Do they assert boundaries to prevent all bonding? (golden-allergy)

PHASE 5: LOYALTY
  → Can they maintain an alliance across time? (capacity)
  → Is "loyalty" actually "obedience to me"? (dark-addiction)
  → Can they not form loyalty at all? (dark-allergy)
  → Do they perform loyalty without genuine commitment? (golden-addiction)
  → Do they refuse loyalty as weakness? (golden-allergy)
```

### 7.2 Cross-modality validation

| Signal | Modality A | Modality B | If A >> B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intent-reading accuracy | Deterministic | Language-reflective | Language > accuracy = golden-addiction |
| Coordination quality | Social-cooperative | Strategic-planning | Planning > execution = golden-addiction |
| Betrayal detection | Deterministic | Immersive-RPG | Structured > spontaneous = not integrated |
| Boundary assertion | Scenario-choice | Embodied-somatic | Verbal > physical = incomplete |
| Loyalty maintenance | Social-cooperative | Immersive-RPG | Structured > ecological = not integrated |
