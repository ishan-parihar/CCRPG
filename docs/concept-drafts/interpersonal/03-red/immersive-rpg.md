# Interpersonal / Red — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes interpersonal intelligence through SPONTANEOUS RELATIONAL BEHAVIOUR in an open game world — not structured interpersonal tasks but free relational engagement in an environment populated with agents who have their own intentions, alliances, and conflicts. How does the player's interpersonal life LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Red:** The other 6 modalities probe interpersonal intelligence through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Red, the player navigates a world of agents: do they read others spontaneously? Do they form alliances naturally? Do they detect betrayal without being prompted? Their SPONTANEOUS relational behaviour reveals their genuine interpersonal structure.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The War-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open-world Red-stage environment where interpersonal intelligence is assessed through the QUALITY of spontaneous relational engagement — reading NPCs without being asked, forming alliances naturally, coordinating without prompts, and navigating the social landscape of a living world.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Social Awareness → Alliance Formation → Relational Navigation → Social Leadership → The Living World

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world is populated with AGENTS. They have intentions. They form alliances. They betray. They trade. The player is never told "read this NPC" — they must NOTICE, ENGAGE, and NAVIGATE spontaneously.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL interpersonal style surfaces. Do they dominate every interaction? Avoid all NPCs? Perform sophistication? Refuse commitment? Their ecological behaviour reveals their genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage more of the social world? Read subtler agents? Navigate more complex relational terrain?

**Integration path:** The world rewards interpersonal intelligence NATURALLY. Players who read NPCs gain allies. Players who coordinate gain power. Players who maintain alliances gain compounding advantages. The reward is POWER (Red-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates interpersonal intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — reading, coordinating, maintaining, and navigating without being prompted.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** NPCs with their own agendas populate the world.
- Healthy: Engages NPCs as agents with their own goals; coordinates when mutual benefit exists
- Pathological: Dominates every NPC interaction; treats all agents as subjects to command
- **Detection:** Whether NPC interactions are mutual or unilateral

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Situations requiring the player to INITIATE relational engagement.
- Healthy: Approaches NPCs, proposes alliances, initiates coordination
- Pathological: Waits for NPCs to approach; never initiates relational engagement
- **Detection:** Spontaneous relational initiation rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** NPCs who request help, alliance, or resources.
- Healthy: Evaluates requests; joins when mutual benefit is clear
- Pathological: Joins every request; cannot refuse; loses resources to every NPC who asks
- **Detection:** Refusal rate on NPC requests (0% = communion dark)

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Opportunities for shared action (group hunts, coordinated raids, joint defence).
- Healthy: Joins shared actions naturally; contributes proportionally
- Pathological: Never joins group actions; remains solo even when group is clearly better
- **Detection:** Group-action participation rate

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar NPCs vs. new NPCs in the world.
- Healthy: Maintains existing relationships while exploring new ones appropriately
- Pathological: Constantly seeks new relational contacts; discards familiar NPCs
- **Detection:** NPC-rotation rate; time spent with familiar vs. novel agents

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Complex social situations (multi-NPC dynamics, competing factions).
- Healthy: Engages complexity with curiosity; navigates multi-party dynamics
- Pathological: Avoids complex social situations; stays in simple dyadic interactions
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in multi-agent social contexts

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Established alliances that need maintenance (checking in, reciprocating).
- Healthy: Maintains alliances through regular engagement
- Pathological: Neglects established relationships; "They'll be fine"
- **Detection:** Alliance maintenance behaviour over time

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether interpersonal skills demonstrated in tasks appear in free-navigation.
- Healthy: Intent-reading, coordination, loyalty appear naturally
- Pathological: Interpersonal intelligence exists only during explicit challenges
- **Detection:** Transfer of interpersonal skills to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Dominator"
**How it surfaces:** Observe relational patterns across the session. The Dominator's interactions are ALL dominance-based — commands NPCs, takes from weaker agents, builds a hierarchy with themselves at top.
- **Detection signals:** All interactions are dominance-based; NPC satisfaction declining across session; player accumulates "servants" not "allies"; no mutual interactions; takes without giving

### Dark-Allergy: "The Isolate"
**How it surfaces:** Observe whether the player engages the social world AT ALL. The Isolate navigates as if NPCs don't exist — avoids all agents, refuses all interaction, treats the world as empty of social content.
- **Detection signals:** Zero NPC interactions; avoids populated areas; treats agents as obstacles; no alliance formation; purely solo navigation despite available allies

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Diplomat"
**How it surfaces:** Observe the QUALITY of relational engagement. The Premature Diplomat PERFORMS social behaviour (approaches NPCs, uses alliance language, makes "diplomatic" gestures) but actual coordination quality and intent-reading accuracy are low.
- **Detection signals:** High social activity + low coordination quality; many "alliances" that produce no actual coordination; diplomatic language without genuine reading; style over substance in relational navigation

### Golden-Allergy: "The Loyalty-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Observe relational DURATION. The Loyalty-Refuser engages NPCs effectively but NEVER maintains relationships. Every interaction is one-shot. No NPC is visited twice. No alliance persists.
- **Detection signals:** High interaction quality + zero relationship persistence; never returns to an NPC; all interactions are transactional one-shots; no alliance lasts beyond a single encounter

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces NPCs who REFUSE to be dominated (they leave if commanded; stay if coordinated)
- Valuable resources available ONLY through genuine coordination (not command)
- Frame: Natural consequence — domination empties the world; coordination fills it

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World makes social engagement NECESSARY for navigation (socially-gated paths, NPC-only resources)
- NPCs who approach the player (reducing initiation burden)
- Frame: Natural consequence — isolation = missing 80% of the world's content

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces accuracy-gated content — performed diplomacy doesn't open doors; only genuine coordination does
- NPCs who respond to ACTUAL coordination quality, not social performance
- Frame: Natural consequence — style without substance = locked out

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces RETURNING NPCs who offer more each time (compounding loyalty rewards)
- Unique content available ONLY to sustained alliances (3+ interactions with same NPC)
- Frame: Natural consequence — one-shots get scraps; loyalty gets fortresses

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Red-stage world populated with agents. NPCs have intentions, form their own alliances, trade, fight, and betray. The player navigates freely. Social intelligence produces tactical advantage. Social blindness produces disadvantage. The aesthetic: Red-stage territory — war-camps, trading posts, contested borders, roaming war-bands.

### Interaction
- **Social navigation:** Moving through a world where relational awareness produces advantage
- **Spontaneous intent-reading:** Noticing NPC intentions without being prompted
- **Natural alliance formation:** Forming alliances through organic interaction
- **Coordination in context:** Coordinating with allies during world-events (raids, defences, hunts)
- **Alliance maintenance:** Returning to allies, reciprocating, maintaining bonds

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: interpersonal intelligence → allies, resources, territory, power
- Natural consequences: interpersonal blindness → isolation, missed opportunities, vulnerability
- No explicit "social score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- NPC intent clarity scales with demonstrated reading accuracy
- Alliance opportunities scale with demonstrated coordination quality
- Social complexity scales with demonstrated navigation capacity
- Betrayal subtlety scales with demonstrated detection ability

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Social Awareness | 1-5 | Obvious NPCs; clear intentions; simple world |
| Alliance Formation | 5-15 | Alliance opportunities; coordination contexts |
| Relational Navigation | 15-30 | Multi-NPC dynamics; competing factions |
| Social Leadership | 30-50 | Leading alliances; managing multiple relationships |
| The Living World | 50+ | Full social ecology; all capacities tested spontaneously |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **NPC agent configurations:** Characters with varied intentions, alliance preferences, and betrayal thresholds
- **Social zones:** Areas with distinct relational dynamics (trading posts, war-camps, neutral ground)
- **World events:** Situations that create relational opportunities or pressures
- **Faction dynamics:** Competing groups the player can navigate between
- **Returning NPCs:** Characters who remember past interactions and compound rewards

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ NPC configs, 15+ social zones, 20+ world events, 10+ faction dynamics, 15+ returning NPCs

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All-dominance interactions → dark-addiction; zero NPC engagement → dark-allergy
- High activity + low quality → golden-addiction; high quality + zero persistence → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Spontaneous reading rate:** How often the player reads NPC intent without prompting
- **Alliance formation rate:** How naturally alliances form through organic interaction
- **Coordination quality:** Effectiveness of spontaneous coordination with allies
- **Alliance persistence:** Duration of maintained relationships
- **Navigation wisdom:** Quality of social zone-selection and faction-navigation

### LLM rubric
- **High:** World generation, ecological interpersonal assessment, NPC agent behaviour, narrative adaptation. Evaluates spontaneous relational behaviour patterns.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (interact, approach, coordinate); hold (maintain, assert); swipe (navigate, avoid)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (relational engagement patterns, alliance health over time)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of relational patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- NPCs with persistent intentions and alliance preferences
- NPCs must RESPOND to player's relational behaviour (reward coordination, punish domination/neglect)
- NPCs must REMEMBER past interactions (returning NPC system)
- World must have consistent social dynamics across sessions

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, NPC agent behaviour, narrative adaptation
- Detects spontaneous relational engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates interpersonal intelligence quality without explicit tasks

### State persistence
- Full world state; NPC relationship states; alliance health per NPC; reading accuracy history; coordination patterns; alliance persistence; faction standings; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
