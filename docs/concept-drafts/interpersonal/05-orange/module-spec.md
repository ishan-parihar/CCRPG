# Interpersonal / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Interpersonal  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The capacity to choose relationships deliberately, coordinate through explicit contracts, and build mutual-benefit networks from sovereign autonomy.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Orange interpersonal capacity is **contractual coordination** — the ability to form, maintain, and renegotiate explicit relational agreements between autonomous parties. Unlike Amber's role-based belonging ("we follow the code together"), Orange relationships are *chosen*, defined by clear expectations, specialised role allocation, and mutual benefit. This is the first stage where the individual selects their relational field rather than inheriting it. Core sub-capacities: expectation-setting, boundary articulation, strategic networking, role negotiation, and social-capital management within ethical limits.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Networker

Instrumentalises all relationships. Every person is a resource; cannot relate without agenda. "What can you do for me?" Social capital hoarding — accumulates connections without genuine reciprocity. Relationships are investments, not encounters.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Lone Wolf

Refuses relational engagement at the contractual level. Remains in Amber code-coordination or Red transactional exchange. "I don't need anyone." Cannot form mutual contracts because forming them would require acknowledging interdependence.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Authentic

Performs Green deep-listening and vulnerability without genuine Orange contractual capacity. Claims "authentic relating" while unable to set clear expectations or boundaries. Bypasses the hard work of explicit coordination by leaping to pseudo-intimacy.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Contract Fortress

Contractual relating is present and functional but refuses authentic opening. Terrified of vulnerability. "Keep it professional." The contract becomes a wall rather than a bridge — technically competent relating that never deepens.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaches toward deeper relating (Green authenticity) | Bypasses contracts into premature vulnerability (GA) |
| Agape | Returns to embody clear boundaries and expectations | Collapses into rigid professionalism (GAll) |
| Agency | Sovereign self chooses relationships deliberately | Instrumentalises others as resources (DA) |
| Communion | Joins others through mutual contracts | Refuses joining entirely (DAll) |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

The Networker (DA) heals when Agency is directed inward — "I am enough without extracting value from others." Agape grounds the relational field in genuine care. The Lone Wolf (DAll) heals when Agency forms its first voluntary contract — choosing relationship from strength, not need.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

The Premature Authentic (GA) dissolves when Communion is grounded in actual contractual skill — real vulnerability requires real boundaries first. The Contract Fortress (GAll) dissolves when Eros draws the player toward authentic connection and Communion softens the professional wall.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Full integration: player can form explicit contracts, renegotiate them under pressure, maintain boundaries without rigidity, and allow authentic moments without losing contractual clarity. All four drives score ≥ 0.6 in both domains simultaneously.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Driven Isolate (Interpersonal DAll × Willpower DA)

Refuses relationship + compulsive achievement = lonely success. The player excels at goals but has no relational field to share them with. Willpower's dark-addiction fuels isolation.

### 6.2 The Charming Manipulator (Interpersonal DA × Emotional DA)

Instrumentalises relationships + weaponises emotional intelligence = sophisticated exploitation. Reads others perfectly but only to extract value. The most socially dangerous compound shadow.

### 6.3 The Authentic Performer (Interpersonal GA × Emotional GA)

Premature authentic relating + premature empathy = performed vulnerability. Looks deeply connected but neither party has genuine contractual ground. A shared bypass.

### 6.4 The Professional Fortress (Interpersonal GAll × Emotional GAll)

Contract fortress + regulation fortress = competent but unreachable. Technically excellent at coordination, emotionally impenetrable. High-functioning isolation disguised as professionalism.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: baseline contractual capacity (no shadow provocation). Sessions 4–8: dark shadows surfaced via relational pressure (betrayal, exploitation opportunities, isolation temptations). Sessions 9–14: golden shadows surfaced via authenticity invitations and vulnerability openings. Session 15+: full-spectrum probing with adaptive emphasis on detected patterns.
