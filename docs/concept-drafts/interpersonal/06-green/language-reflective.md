# Interpersonal / Green — Language-Reflective Game Concept

> **Axis:** Verbal articulation of relational life — how the player talks about relationships reveals depth vs. performance.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green interpersonal capacity lives in the quality of relational discourse; language reveals whether vulnerability is genuine or performed, whether mutuality is embodied or conceptual.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Relational Mirror  
**Core loop:** Player responds to open-ended prompts about relational experiences, dilemmas, and aspirations. LLM analysis scores depth of mutual perspective-taking, authenticity of vulnerability language, and presence of shadow patterns in relational discourse.  
**Session length:** 3–6 minutes per checkpoint (2–3 prompts).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Each prompt is self-contained; player exits after any response.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Shadow surfacing:** GA surfaces when player uses transpersonal/spiritual language without concrete mutual vulnerability ("I honour the divine in all beings" but cannot describe a specific moment of being seen). DAll surfaces through contractual/transactional framing ("I give X, they give Y, it works").  
**Drive probing:** Agency visible in self-referential language — does the player maintain "I" within relational descriptions? Communion visible in genuine other-inclusion — does the player represent the other's perspective with specificity? Eros visible in growth-language — does the player articulate relational edges? Agape visible in embodied presence-language — does the player describe felt relational moments?  
**Catalyst→Experience→Integration:** Prompts begin at player's demonstrated level, surface shadow through increasingly specific relational questions, then offer reflective reframes modelling authentic Green relational language.

## 3. Game Design

**Prompt categories:**  
- *Relational narrative:* "Describe a moment when someone truly saw you." / "Tell about a conflict that deepened a relationship."  
- *Perspective-taking:* "How might your closest friend describe what you bring to the relationship?"  
- *Edge exploration:* "What kind of closeness frightens you?" / "When does authenticity become too much?"  
- *Shadow probes:* "What do you think people who 'keep it professional' are protecting?"  
**LLM scoring rubric:** Concreteness (specific vs. abstract), mutuality (self+other vs. self-only), vulnerability depth (felt-sense vs. concept), boundary presence (self-coherence within relating), growth-edge awareness (honest about limits vs. performed mastery).  
**Adaptive difficulty:** Early prompts accept broad responses. Later prompts require increasing specificity, concrete examples, and genuine edge-exploration. Shadow-specific follow-ups generated when patterns emerge.

## 4. Item Pool

**Structure:** 120+ prompts across 4 tiers of depth. Tier 1: general relational reflection. Tier 2: specific relational moments. Tier 3: shadow-adjacent territory. Tier 4: direct growth-edge confrontation.  
**Shadow-specific prompts:** DA-surfacing prompts ask about boundaries within closeness. DAll-surfacing prompts ask about moments of genuine vulnerability. GA-surfacing prompts ask for concrete (not spiritual) relational depth. GAll-surfacing prompts ask about relational territory beyond current comfort.  
**Refresh:** Prompts rotate on 14-day cycles within tiers. LLM generates personalised follow-ups. No exact repetition within 60 days.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**LLM integration:** Rubric-bound scoring (not free interpretation). Scores: concreteness (0–1), mutuality (0–1), vulnerability-depth (0–1), boundary-presence (0–1), growth-edge (0–1). Shadow-pattern flags emitted when scores cluster characteristically.  
**Privacy:** Responses stored encrypted. No raw text leaves device without explicit consent. On-device scoring where possible; cloud fallback with anonymisation.  
**Timing:** No time pressure on responses. Session duration tracked for engagement only.  
**Adaptive engine:** Prompt selection based on prior response scores. Shadow-confidence updated per response. Cross-modality validation flags at 0.7 confidence.  
**Accessibility:** Voice-to-text input supported. Prompts available in audio. Response length unconstrained.
