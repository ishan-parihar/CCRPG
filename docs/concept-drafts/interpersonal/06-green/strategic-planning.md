# Interpersonal / Green — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** Planning relational life — structuring authentic connections, managing relational demands, and allocating relational energy.  **Why this axis for this module:** How a player organises their relational world reveals whether authenticity is a practice or a performance, and whether mutuality is sustainable or episodic.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Relational Architect  
**Core loop:** Player manages a network of NPC relationships across time — allocating attention, planning authentic encounters, navigating competing relational demands, and structuring a relational life that sustains genuine mutuality without burnout or enmeshment.  
**Session length:** 5–9 minutes per checkpoint (one planning cycle).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Each planning cycle resolves independently; player exits between cycles.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Shadow surfacing:** DAll surfaces when player structures relational life around efficiency and obligation rather than authenticity — scheduling "vulnerability time" as a task, optimising relationships for output. GAll surfaces when player maintains current relational depth but refuses to plan for deepening — comfortable relationships preserved, growth-edges avoided.  
**Drive probing:** Agency measured by capacity to say no to relational demands without guilt-collapse. Communion measured by genuine prioritisation of mutual encounters over solo productivity. Eros measured by planning for relational growth (not just maintenance). Agape measured by planning for embodied presence (not just scheduled contact).  
**Catalyst→Experience→Integration:** Planning cycles begin with player's natural relational-management style, surface shadow through consequences over simulated time, then offer planning frameworks modelling integrated Green relational architecture.

## 3. Game Design

**Planning mechanics:**  
- *Relational energy budget:* Finite attention/energy units per cycle. Over-allocation to one = enmeshment signal. Under-allocation to all = avoidance signal.  
- *Depth vs. breadth:* Player chooses between deepening existing relationships and maintaining wider network. Shadow patterns emerge in consistent bias.  
- *Authenticity scheduling:* Player plans encounters — choosing between "safe" interactions and "edge" interactions (difficult conversations, vulnerability invitations). Avoidance of edge = GAll/DAll signal.  
- *Conflict anticipation:* Player must plan for upcoming relational tensions. Avoidance = DAll. Over-preparation = DA (controlling relational outcomes).  
**Consequence simulation:** Planning choices produce relational outcomes 2–3 cycles later. Neglected relationships degrade. Over-invested relationships show enmeshment symptoms. Avoided growth-edges create stagnation.  
**Adaptive difficulty:** Early cycles have clear optimal allocations. Later cycles present genuine trade-offs requiring the player to hold relational paradox in planning.

## 4. Item Pool

**Structure:** 60+ planning scenarios across 4 relational-life contexts (intimate/family, friendship network, community/group, professional-becoming-authentic). Each tagged with: shadow probed, drive measured, complexity tier (1–5).  
**Shadow-specific scenarios:** DA scenarios present a partner demanding all available energy. DAll scenarios present opportunities for deepening disguised as "unnecessary." GA scenarios present "spiritual community" planning that bypasses one-to-one depth. GAll scenarios present growth-invitations framed as risks.  
**Temporal depth:** Each scenario spans 3–5 planning cycles with cumulative consequences. Player sees long-term relational patterns emerge.  
**Refresh:** Scenario sets rotate on 28-day cycles. Network configurations vary to prevent memorisation.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Simulation engine:** Relational-network model with per-NPC state (trust, depth, neglect-timer, growth-edge-proximity). State updates based on allocation choices. Consequence generation follows relational-psychology models.  
**Scoring:** Planning patterns analysed across cycles (not individual allocations). Shadow-pattern confidence requires 3+ convergent choices. Drive-health derived from allocation-cluster analysis over time.  
**Adaptive engine:** Scenario selection based on under-probed shadow quadrants. Complexity escalation based on integrated-planning frequency. Cross-modality validation flags at 0.7 confidence.  
**Rendering:** Network visualisation showing relationship states. Timeline view of planned vs. actual outcomes. Colour-coded health indicators per relationship.  
**Accessibility:** All planning interfaces keyboard-navigable. Screen-reader descriptions for network state. No time pressure on decisions.
