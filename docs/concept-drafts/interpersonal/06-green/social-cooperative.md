# Interpersonal / Green — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Live authentic relating — mutual vulnerability with NPC partners, deep listening, conflict resolution in real-time.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green interpersonal capacity is ultimately a relational practice; this modality measures whether the player can DO authentic relating, not just recognise or describe it.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Circle  
**Core loop:** Player engages in real-time relational exchanges with AI-driven NPC partners — practising deep listening, offering vulnerability, receiving vulnerability, navigating conflict, and co-creating mutual understanding. The NPCs are sophisticated relational agents with their own boundaries, needs, and growth-edges.  
**Session length:** 5–10 minutes per checkpoint (one complete relational exchange).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Each exchange is self-contained; player exits between exchanges.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Shadow surfacing:** GA surfaces when player performs depth without genuine risk — using "relational language" without actual vulnerability, offering spiritual reframes instead of personal truth. DA surfaces when player loses self in the NPC's emotional field — agreeing, merging, abandoning own perspective to maintain connection.  
**Drive probing:** Agency measured by maintenance of own perspective and boundaries during relational pressure. Communion measured by genuine attunement and responsiveness to NPC's emotional state. Eros measured by willingness to deepen the exchange beyond comfortable territory. Agape measured by capacity to stay present with relational difficulty without escape.  
**Catalyst→Experience→Integration:** Exchanges begin at player's relational comfort zone, NPCs gradually introduce relational challenges (vulnerability invitations, boundary tests, conflict), and resolution requires integrated Green relating.

## 3. Game Design

**Exchange types:**  
- *Deep listening:* NPC shares something vulnerable. Player must respond in ways that demonstrate genuine hearing (not fixing, advising, or spiritualising). Quality of response scored by LLM rubric.  
- *Mutual vulnerability:* NPC invites player to share. Player's response scored for authenticity, specificity, and appropriate depth (not too guarded, not too exposed for context).  
- *Conflict navigation:* NPC disagrees or expresses hurt. Player must navigate toward mutual understanding without capitulation (DA), withdrawal (DAll), or spiritual bypass (GA).  
- *Boundary negotiation:* NPC requests something that crosses player's boundary. Player must maintain self while preserving relationship.  
**NPC sophistication:** NPCs have persistent relational states, remember prior exchanges, and respond dynamically to player's relational style. They have their own shadows and growth-edges.  
**Adaptive difficulty:** NPC relational challenges calibrated to player's demonstrated edge. Early exchanges are supportive. Later exchanges introduce genuine relational complexity.

## 4. Item Pool

**Structure:** 40+ NPC relational partners, each with distinct personality, relational style, shadow pattern, and growth-edge. Partners rotate to prevent over-familiarity.  
**Shadow-specific NPCs:** DA-triggering NPCs are emotionally intense and boundary-testing. DAll-triggering NPCs are warm and inviting (testing whether player can receive). GA-triggering NPCs use spiritual language (testing whether player matches or grounds). GAll-triggering NPCs invite deepening beyond comfort.  
**Exchange variety:** Each NPC has 10+ exchange scripts that adapt based on player's prior responses. No exact exchange repetition within 60 days.  
**Refresh:** NPC roster rotates on 21-day cycles. New relational challenges introduced based on player's shadow profile.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**LLM integration:** NPC responses generated by rubric-bound LLM with character-specific personality constraints. Player responses scored by separate rubric-LLM for: listening quality, vulnerability authenticity, boundary clarity, growth-edge engagement.  
**Scoring:** Per-exchange scores across 4 dimensions. Shadow-pattern detection via exchange-pattern analysis over sessions. Drive-health updated per exchange block.  
**Adaptive engine:** NPC selection and challenge calibration based on under-probed shadows. Difficulty escalation based on integrated-response frequency. Cross-modality validation flags at 0.7 confidence.  
**Privacy:** Exchange content stored encrypted. No raw dialogue leaves device without consent.  
**Accessibility:** Full voice interaction supported. Text-based fallback. No time pressure (turn-based exchange, not real-time typing race).
