# Interpersonal / Green — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Relational dilemmas where authenticity conflicts with harmony, or vulnerability conflicts with safety.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green interpersonal capacity is forged in the crucible of genuine relational tension — moments where being authentic risks the relationship, and protecting the relationship risks the self.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Authentic Edge  
**Core loop:** Player navigates branching relational dilemmas where each choice reveals drive-orientation and shadow-pattern. No choice is "wrong" — but choices cluster into shadow signatures when patterns emerge across scenarios.  
**Session length:** 5–8 minutes per checkpoint (1–2 complete scenarios).  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Each scenario is self-contained; player exits between scenarios.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Shadow surfacing:** DA surfaces when player consistently chooses fusion/merger over boundary-maintenance ("I'll just go along with what they need"). DAll surfaces when player consistently chooses self-protection over genuine vulnerability ("I need to be clear about my boundaries" used as relational avoidance). GAll surfaces when player refuses choices that would deepen beyond current relational comfort.  
**Drive probing:** Agency measured by willingness to maintain self in relational pressure. Communion measured by willingness to genuinely join despite risk. Eros measured by willingness to reach toward deeper relational territory. Agape measured by willingness to stay present with relational pain rather than escape upward.  
**Catalyst→Experience→Integration:** Scenarios begin at player's edge, surface shadow through escalating relational pressure, then offer resolution paths modelling integrated Green relating — authentic AND connected, vulnerable AND boundaried.

## 3. Game Design

**Scenario structure:** Each scenario presents a relational situation with 2–4 characters. Tension escalates across 3–5 decision points. Each decision point offers 4 choices mapped to shadow quadrants plus an integrated response.  
**Dilemma types:**  
- *Authenticity vs. harmony:* Being honest will hurt someone; silence preserves peace but erodes self.  
- *Vulnerability vs. safety:* Opening up risks rejection; staying guarded prevents deepening.  
- *Boundary vs. connection:* Saying no protects self but distances other; saying yes joins but may enmesh.  
- *Growth vs. stability:* Deepening risks what currently works; staying comfortable prevents evolution.  
**Consequence modelling:** Choices produce visible relational consequences 2–3 beats later. Player sees how shadow-driven choices create relational patterns. No moralising — consequences speak.  
**Adaptive difficulty:** Early scenarios have clear shadow-choices. Later scenarios blur the lines — the integrated response requires holding genuine paradox.

## 4. Item Pool

**Structure:** 80+ scenarios across 4 relational contexts (intimate partnership, close friendship, family, community). Each tagged with: primary dilemma type, shadow quadrants probed, difficulty tier (1–5).  
**Shadow-specific scenarios:** DA scenarios present enmeshment as love. DAll scenarios present vulnerability as manipulation. GA scenarios present spiritual bypass as depth. GAll scenarios present comfort as wisdom.  
**Branching depth:** Each scenario has 3–5 decision points × 4 choices = 12–20 unique paths. Paths converge at consequence-revelation moments.  
**Refresh:** Scenarios rotate on 21-day cycles. No exact repetition within 90 days.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Narrative engine:** Branching dialogue with state tracking across decision points. Character emotional states update based on player choices. Consequence propagation across 2–3 beats.  
**Scoring:** Choice patterns analysed across scenarios (not individual choices). Shadow-pattern confidence requires 3+ convergent choices. Drive-health derived from choice-cluster analysis.  
**Adaptive engine:** Scenario selection based on under-probed shadow quadrants. Difficulty escalation based on integrated-response frequency. Cross-modality validation flags at 0.7 confidence.  
**Rendering:** Character portraits with emotional state indicators. Dialogue with tonal markers. Consequence scenes as brief narrative vignettes.  
**Accessibility:** Full text-to-speech. Choice descriptions include emotional subtext for screen readers. No time pressure on decisions.
