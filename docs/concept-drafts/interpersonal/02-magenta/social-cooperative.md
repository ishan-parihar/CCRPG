# Interpersonal / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes interpersonal intelligence through DOING TOGETHER — can the player act in coordination with another? At Magenta, this is proto-coordination: moving together, acting in sync, shared rhythm without explicit communication. Not strategic cooperation (Red+) — FELT synchrony.
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Magenta:** Before explicit cooperation (Red+), there is SYNCHRONY — moving together through felt-sense. At Magenta, coordination is magical: "We move as one because we ARE one." This modality tests whether the player can synchronise with another through felt-sense rather than language.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Moving-Together"
- **Core mechanic:** The player and a phantom-ally must move in synchrony. Not through instructions — through FELT-SENSE. Can they match the other's rhythm? Move when it moves? Stop when it stops? The pre-verbal coordination that is the foundation of all cooperation.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Shared Step → The Rhythm-Match → The Moving-Pair → The Felt-Sync → The Dancing Bond

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Your companion moves. Can you move with it? Not because it told you — because you FEEL it. Match its rhythm. Move when it moves. Stop when it stops." The contact boundary is: "Can you synchronise with another through felt-sense?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player coordinate? The Fusion-Addict merges completely (loses own movement in the other's). The Phantom-Denier moves independently (doesn't register the other's rhythm). The Premature Coordinator executes perfect timing without felt connection. The Bond-Refuser begins to sync then breaks away.
- *Emergent:* Can they match rhythm while maintaining some own-movement? Genuine felt synchrony?

**Integration path:** Rewards FELT SYNCHRONY — matching the other's rhythm through genuine sensing while maintaining some own-movement. Not total merger, not independence, not mechanical timing, not approach-withdrawal.

**Successful integration:** The player moves WITH the companion through felt-sense — genuine proto-coordination.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Moving-Together: a synchrony space where player and phantom-ally move in shared rhythm. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage dance — two glowing figures moving together. When in sync, a shared glow connects them. When out of sync, they dim separately.

### Interaction
- **The Shared Step (1-5):** Companion makes simple movements → player matches. Basic rhythm-following.
- **The Rhythm-Match (5-15):** More complex rhythms → match timing AND quality.
- **The Moving-Pair (15-30):** Mutual movement → neither leads exclusively; co-created rhythm.
- **The Felt-Sync (30-50):** Sync without visual cues → feel the rhythm; move from felt-sense alone.
- **The Dancing Bond (50+):** Full synchrony: follow → match → co-create → feel → dance together.

### Feedback
- Felt synchrony → "Together! You moved as one. The bond glows. You feel each other."
- Total merger → "Your own movement too. You can be WITH and still be YOU. Add something yours."
- Independent → "They're moving. Can you feel their rhythm? Just one beat. Match one."
- Mechanical → "Warmer. Not just timing — FEELING. Let their rhythm touch you. Move from that."
- Break-away → "You were together! Stay. Just a moment more. It's safe. Keep moving with."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Rhythm complexity: simple (one beat) → moderate → complex
- Cue availability: visual + audio → audio only → felt-sense only
- Sync precision: loose matching → moderate → precise
- Duration: brief sync → sustained sync
- Mutuality: follow only → lead + follow → co-create

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Shared Step | 1-5 | Basic rhythm-following (match simple movements) |
| The Rhythm-Match | 5-15 | Match timing and quality |
| The Moving-Pair | 15-30 | Mutual/co-created movement |
| The Felt-Sync | 30-50 | Sync from felt-sense (reduced visual cues) |
| The Dancing Bond | 50+ | Full synchrony integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Simple rhythms:** Easy to match (slow, clear, repetitive)
- **Complex rhythms:** Harder to match (faster, varied, syncopated)
- **Mutual items:** Requiring co-creation (not just following)
- **Felt-sense items:** Reduced cues; sync from feeling alone
- **Duration items:** Requiring sustained synchrony

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ simple, 20+ complex, 15+ mutual, 15+ felt-sense, 10+ duration

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Total merger/no individuality → dark-addiction; zero sync → dark-allergy
- Mechanical precision without felt-quality → golden-addiction; sync-break cycling → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (rhythm-matching); hold (sustained movement); swipe (directional movement); multi-touch (complex rhythms)

### Timing requirements
- Millisecond-level sync measurement; rhythm-matching precision; duration tracking

### NPC/AI requirements
- Phantom-ally with dynamic movement patterns
- Rhythm generation system (simple → complex)
- Sync-quality measurement (timing + felt-quality indicators)
- Shared-glow visualization (connection quality indicator)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic sync measurement. LLM for felt-quality assessment and ecological context only.

### State persistence
- Sync accuracy scores; felt-quality indicators; individuality scores; mutuality capacity; sustainability; merger indicators; independence indicators; mechanical indicators; break-away indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
