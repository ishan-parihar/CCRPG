# Interpersonal / Magenta — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The embodied-somatic axis probes interpersonal intelligence through THE BODY — can the player feel another's presence IN their body? At Magenta, this is: "When they're near, what happens in your body? Warm? Tingly? Heavy?" The body as relational sensor.
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Magenta:** Before cognitive theory-of-mind (Red+), the body senses others. At Magenta, another's presence is felt somatically — warmth, tingling, heaviness, lightness. This modality tests whether the player's body registers other-presence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body-Between"
- **Core mechanic:** Presences appear at varying distances and the player reports what happens in their body. Not what they THINK about the other — what their BODY does. "When it comes closer, what happens in your chest? Your stomach? Your hands?" The body as interpersonal instrument.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; body-fatigue monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The Body-Near → The Presence-Feeling → The Distance-Body → The Shared Body → The Relational Soma

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Something is near you. What does your body do? Does it warm? Tighten? Open? What happens in your body when another is close?" The contact boundary is: "Does your body register other-presence?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player's body respond to other-presence? The Fusion-Addict's body dissolves (loses boundary). The Phantom-Denier's body registers nothing. The Premature Coordinator describes body-responses that don't match proximity. The Bond-Refuser's body responds then shuts down.
- *Emergent:* Can their body register other-presence at calibrated intensity?

**Integration path:** Rewards SOMATIC OTHER-SENSING — the body registering another's presence with appropriate intensity. Not dissolved, not numb, not performed, not blocked.

**Successful integration:** The player's body reliably registers other-presence — warmth, tingling, openness — at calibrated intensity.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Body-Between: a somatic-relational space where presences approach and the player maps body-responses. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage body-glow — a body-outline that lights up where other-presence is felt. Presences approach and the body-map responds.

### Interaction
- **The Body-Near (1-5):** Presence approaches → "What does your body do?" Tap body-area.
- **The Presence-Feeling (5-15):** Identify quality: warm/cold, tight/open when presence is near.
- **The Distance-Body (15-30):** Body-response changes with distance → track how body changes as presence moves.
- **The Shared Body (30-50):** Compare body-responses with companion → shared somatic exploration.
- **The Relational Soma (50+):** Full somatic-relational mapping: presence → body-response → distance-calibration → sharing.

### Feedback
- Calibrated body-response → "Your body felt them! Right there. That's how your body knows another is near."
- Dissolution → "Your body is still YOURS. Feel your feet. Now — what does YOUR body do? Not theirs. Yours."
- Nothing → "Warmer or cooler? Just in your chest. When they come closer. Just temperature."
- Over-elaborate → "Simpler. Warm or cold? When they're near. Just that."
- Shutdown → "You felt it! For a moment. That's real. Can you let it stay? Just one more second?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Presence intensity: strong (easy to feel in body) → moderate → subtle
- Distance variation: close (strong body-response) → moderate → far
- Body-area precision: large area → specific location
- Quality discrimination: binary (warm/cold) → multiple qualities
- Duration: brief presence → sustained proximity

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Body-Near | 1-5 | Basic body-response to presence (tap area) |
| The Presence-Feeling | 5-15 | Quality of body-response (warm/cold/tight/open) |
| The Distance-Body | 15-30 | Body-response changes with distance |
| The Shared Body | 30-50 | Shared somatic exploration with companion |
| The Relational Soma | 50+ | Full somatic-relational integration |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Close presences:** Strong proximity, easy body-response
- **Distance presences:** Varying distances testing body-sensitivity
- **Quality presences:** Different emotional qualities producing different body-responses
- **Moving presences:** Approach/recede testing body-response tracking
- **Shared items:** Comparing body-responses with companion

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ close, 20+ distance, 15+ quality, 15+ moving, 10+ shared

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Body-dissolution → dark-addiction; zero body-response → dark-allergy
- Elaborate/inconsistent body-reports → golden-addiction; response-then-shutdown → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-on-body-map (where response is felt); select quality (warm/cold/tight/open); hold (sustain sensing); drag (track changes)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked; no time pressure; body-fatigue especially monitored

### NPC/AI requirements
- Presence system with proximity control (approach/recede)
- Body-map visualization (interactive body-outline)
- Distance-response correlation system (expected body-responses at distances)
- Optional: companion for shared somatic exploration

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Open-ended somatic description evaluation; consistency checking; dissolution detection. Core tap-location and quality-selection algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Body-response patterns; quality reports; distance-response correlations; self-stability scores; tracking ability; dissolution indicators; numbness indicators; elaboration indicators; shutdown indicators; drive/shadow signals; body-fatigue state; checkpoint position
