# Interpersonal / Magenta — Language-Reflective Game Concept

> **Axis:** The language-reflective axis probes interpersonal intelligence through WORDS ABOUT OTHERS — can the player name what they sense about another? At Magenta, this is: "Something is there. What is it? Can you say what it's like?" The first words for other-beings.
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Magenta:** Before sophisticated relational language (Red+), there must be NAMING the other. At Magenta: "It's a friend." "It's scary." "It's warm." The simplest verbal labels for sensed presences. This modality tests whether the player can bridge other-sensing and language.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Other-Word"
- **Core mechanic:** The player senses a presence and must find words for it. Not describing relationship (Red+) — describing the OTHER. "What is it like? Warm? Cold? Big? Small? Friend? Stranger?" The first vocabulary of other-beings.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Name → The Other-Feeling → The Presence-Story → The Shared Name → The Speaking Bond

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "You sense something there. What would you call it? What is it like? Can you find a word for this other?" The contact boundary is: "Can you name what you sense about another being?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player verbalise other-presence? The Fusion-Addict cannot separate self-words from other-words ("it's me" / "we are..."). The Phantom-Denier has no words because nothing is sensed. The Premature Coordinator produces elaborate descriptions without genuine sensing. The Bond-Refuser names then stops ("it's there. That's all.").
- *Emergent:* Can they find simple, genuine words for what they sense about the other?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE OTHER-NAMING — simple, accurate words that match what is actually sensed. Not self-other confusion, not nothing, not over-elaborate, not truncated.

**Successful integration:** The player finds words for other-presences that are simple, genuine, and accurate — the seed of relational language.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Finding own words for the other (not given options).
- Healthy: Generates own other-words; trusts own naming
- Pathological: Cannot find words (fusion — no separation to name); OR: no words (nothing sensed)
- **Detection:** Whether other-naming is self-generated and genuine

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Naming ambiguous presences (unclear others).
- Healthy: Offers tentative names under ambiguity
- Pathological: Cannot name without certainty; freezes on ambiguous presences
- **Detection:** Naming quality under other-ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Naming when companion names differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own naming while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's names; loses own other-words
- **Detection:** Whether own naming survives social influence

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared naming (building vocabulary together).
- Healthy: Contributes to shared other-vocabulary; integrates perspectives
- Pathological: Cannot share naming; other-words are private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared other-naming

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Naming familiar presences (same others, same words).
- Healthy: Maintains genuine naming for familiar presences
- Pathological: Naming becomes rote; loses genuine contact with the other
- **Detection:** Whether familiar naming retains genuine quality

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Naming novel presences (unfamiliar others).
- Healthy: Finds new words for new presences with curiosity
- Pathological: Cannot name unfamiliar others; OR: forces familiar names onto novel presences
- **Detection:** Novel other-naming quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining naming accuracy over time.
- Healthy: Other-naming remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Naming degrades; others become unnamed again
- **Detection:** Naming accuracy across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether other-naming appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Names others naturally in free-play
- Pathological: Other-naming exists only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous other-naming in ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Fusion-Addict"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot name the OTHER because self-other boundary doesn't exist. Names are self-referential: "It's me." "We are the same." "It feels like I feel." Cannot produce words that distinguish self from other.
- **Detection signals:** Self-referential naming; "it's me"; "we are"; cannot distinguish self-words from other-words; all descriptions are self-descriptions projected; no genuine other-naming

### Dark-Allergy: "The Phantom-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** NO WORDS because nothing is sensed. "I don't know." "There's nothing to name." Cannot produce other-words because the other is not perceived. Empty naming.
- **Detection signals:** "I don't know"; "nothing"; empty responses; cannot generate any other-words; no naming because no sensing; blank

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Coordinator"
**How it surfaces:** ELABORATE naming without genuine sensing. "I perceive a benevolent entity with nurturing qualities and a warm energetic signature." Sophisticated vocabulary disconnected from actual other-sensing.
- **Detection signals:** Over-elaborate descriptions; sophisticated vocabulary without felt-sense; inconsistent naming across similar presences; naming "too good" for Magenta capacity; learned not felt

### Golden-Allergy: "The Bond-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Names BRIEFLY then stops. "It's there. Warm. That's all I want to say." Accurate initial naming followed by refusal to continue. The naming works but is truncated.
- **Detection signals:** Brief accurate naming followed by "that's enough"; truncated responses; approach-withdrawal in naming; "I don't want to say more"; naming capacity demonstrated then refused

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Require OTHER-words (not self-words): "Not you. IT. What is IT like? Different from you."
- Reward any word that distinguishes self from other
- Frame: "You are warm. Is IT warm? Or is it something else? What is IT — not you?"
- Build: self-other distinction in language (the first "it" vs. "I")

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Provide WORD OPTIONS: "Is it warm or cold? Big or small? Just pick one."
- Make naming ultra-simple; binary choices; accept minimal language
- Frame: "Something IS there. Just one word. Warm? Cold? Friend? Stranger? Just one."
- Reward ANY other-word; celebrate first naming

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require SIMPLE words: "One word. Not a description. Just: warm? cold? big? small?"
- Block elaborate descriptions; reward simple genuine naming
- Frame: "Not a story. One word. What do you FEEL about it? Just that."
- Build: honest other-naming (simple and genuine)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make continued naming SAFE: "Just one more word. Then done. What else do you notice?"
- Reduce naming-pressure; make each word low-stakes
- Frame: "One more. Just one. What else? Then you can stop. Just one more word."
- Build: sustained naming (continuing past the first word)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Other-Word: a naming space where presences appear and the player finds words for them. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage spirit-naming — presences shimmer and the player's words give them form. When named accurately, the presence solidifies. When named poorly, it remains vague.

### Interaction
- **The First Name (1-5):** Clear presence → one word. "What is it?" Simple naming.
- **The Other-Feeling (5-15):** Presence has emotional quality → "What does it feel like?"
- **The Presence-Story (15-30):** Multiple words → build a brief description of the other.
- **The Shared Name (30-50):** Name WITH companion → shared other-vocabulary.
- **The Speaking Bond (50+):** Full other-naming: sense → name → describe → share → bond through words.

### Feedback
- Genuine simple naming → "Yes! That's what it's like. Your word found it."
- Self-referential → "Not you. IT. What is IT like? Something different from you."
- Nothing → "Something IS there. Just one word. Any word. What comes to mind?"
- Over-elaborate → "Simpler. One word. What do you FEEL about it?"
- Truncated → "Good! One more? Just one more word about it?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Presence clarity: obvious → moderate → subtle
- Naming format: binary choice → select from 4 → generate own word
- Description length: one word → two words → brief phrase
- Presence complexity: simple → mixed qualities → ambiguous
- Sharing requirement: solo naming → shared naming → co-created vocabulary

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Name | 1-5 | One word for a clear presence |
| The Other-Feeling | 5-15 | Emotional quality naming |
| The Presence-Story | 15-30 | Multi-word descriptions |
| The Shared Name | 30-50 | Shared naming with companion |
| The Speaking Bond | 50+ | Full other-naming integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Clear presences:** Obvious qualities, easy to name
- **Feeling presences:** Emotional qualities requiring affect-words
- **Complex presences:** Multiple qualities requiring multi-word descriptions
- **Ambiguous presences:** Unclear qualities testing naming under uncertainty
- **Shared presences:** Requiring co-naming with companion

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ clear, 20+ feeling, 15+ complex, 15+ ambiguous, 10+ shared

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Self-referential naming → dark-addiction; empty naming → dark-allergy
- Over-elaborate naming → golden-addiction; truncated naming → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Naming accuracy:** Do the words match the presence's actual qualities?
- **Self-other distinction:** Are the words about the OTHER (not self-projection)?
- **Vocabulary range:** Can they use different words for different presences?
- **Naming consistency:** Same presence → same words across trials?
- **Sharing quality:** Can they co-name with companion?

### LLM rubric
- **Very High:** Open-ended naming requires LLM evaluation. Self-other distinction, vocabulary quality, genuine vs. performed naming all require language understanding.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Text input (generated words); select from options (binary/multiple choice); voice (optional); collaborative input (shared naming)

### Timing requirements
- Naming latency tracked; no time pressure; response length monitored

### NPC/AI requirements
- Presence system with defined qualities (for validation)
- Naming evaluation (does the word match the presence?)
- Self-other distinction detection (is the naming self-referential?)
- Companion for shared naming phases

### LLM requirements
- **Very High:** Evaluates open-ended naming; detects self-referential language; assesses vocabulary quality; distinguishes genuine from performed naming; facilitates shared naming.

### State persistence
- Naming accuracy rates; self-other distinction scores; vocabulary range; naming consistency; sharing quality; self-referential indicators; empty-naming indicators; over-elaboration indicators; truncation indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
