# Interpersonal / Magenta — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** The strategic-planning axis probes interpersonal intelligence through RELATIONAL ANTICIPATION — can the player predict what the other will do? At Magenta, this is proto-theory-of-mind: "If I go there, will it follow? If I offer, will it take?" One-step relational prediction.
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Magenta:** Before strategic social planning (Red+), there must be PREDICTION of the other. At Magenta: "It will come if I call." The simplest relational forecasting — anticipating another's response to one's action. This modality tests whether the player can form action→other-response links.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Other-Guess"
- **Core mechanic:** The player predicts what a presence will do in response to their action. "If you go closer, will it stay or flee? If you offer warmth, will it come?" One-step relational prediction. Not manipulation (Red) — just ANTICIPATION. Can they predict the other?
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Guess → The Other-Response → The Two-Guesses → The Presence-Pattern → The Relational Forecast

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "If you go toward it, what will it do? Stay? Flee? Come closer? What do you think?" The contact boundary is: "Can you predict another being's response?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player predict others? The Fusion-Addict predicts fusion ("it will merge with me"). The Phantom-Denier cannot predict (no other perceived). The Premature Coordinator predicts with sophisticated models without genuine sensing. The Bond-Refuser predicts rejection ("it will leave").
- *Emergent:* Can they form simple, accurate predictions about the other's response?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE RELATIONAL PREDICTION — correctly anticipating how the other will respond. Not projecting fusion, not blank, not over-modelling, not projecting rejection.

**Successful integration:** The player accurately predicts simple other-responses — the seed of theory-of-mind.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Predicting other-responses independently.
- Healthy: Makes own predictions; trusts own relational forecasting
- Pathological: Predicts fusion always; OR: cannot predict (no other perceived)
- **Detection:** Whether predictions are differentiated (not all-fusion or all-blank)

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Predicting under relational ambiguity.
- Healthy: Offers tentative predictions under ambiguity
- Pathological: Cannot predict without certainty about the other
- **Detection:** Prediction quality under relational ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Predicting when companion predicts differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own prediction while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's prediction; loses own forecasting
- **Detection:** Whether own prediction survives social influence

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared prediction (predicting together).
- Healthy: Contributes to shared relational prediction
- Pathological: Cannot share predictions; forecasting is private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared relational forecasting

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Predicting familiar others (same presence, same situations).
- Healthy: Maintains accurate prediction for familiar presences
- Pathological: Predictions become rote; lose genuine forecasting quality
- **Detection:** Whether familiar predictions retain genuine contact

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Predicting novel others (unfamiliar presences).
- Healthy: Attempts prediction for novel presences with curiosity
- Pathological: Cannot predict novel others; OR: forces familiar predictions
- **Detection:** Novel relational prediction quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining prediction accuracy over time.
- Healthy: Relational forecasting remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Prediction accuracy degrades; forecasting becomes random
- **Detection:** Prediction accuracy across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether relational prediction appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Anticipates others' responses naturally in free-play
- Pathological: Relational prediction exists only in explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous relational forecasting

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Fusion-Addict"
**How it surfaces:** Predicts FUSION for every interaction. "It will come to me." "It will merge." "We will be one." Every prediction is: the other will approach and fuse. Cannot predict distance, departure, or independence.
- **Detection signals:** All predictions = approach/fusion; cannot predict other staying away; "it will come"; "we will merge"; no prediction of other's independence; every forecast is union

### Dark-Allergy: "The Phantom-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** CANNOT PREDICT because no other is perceived. "I don't know what it will do." "What other?" Prediction impossible because the other doesn't exist in their model.
- **Detection signals:** "I don't know"; blank predictions; "what other?"; cannot form any prediction; relational forecasting impossible; no model of other to predict from

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Coordinator"
**How it surfaces:** Predicts with SOPHISTICATED MODELS without genuine sensing. "Based on its approach vector and emotional valence, it will likely..." Over-modelling. Relational prediction as intellectual exercise disconnected from felt-sense.
- **Detection signals:** Sophisticated prediction language; over-modelling; predictions don't match actual outcomes; intellectual forecasting without felt-sense; "based on..." rather than "I feel it will..."

### Golden-Allergy: "The Bond-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Predicts REJECTION/DEPARTURE. "It will leave." "It won't come." Projects own withdrawal onto the other. Every prediction is: the other will refuse connection.
- **Detection signals:** All predictions = departure/rejection; "it will leave"; "it won't come"; projects own refusal onto other; cannot predict other approaching; every forecast is disconnection

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present situations where the other STAYS at distance: "See? It stayed there. It didn't come. What else might it do?"
- Reward predictions that include distance/independence
- Frame: "Not everything comes to you. Sometimes it stays. Sometimes it goes. What might it do THIS time?"
- Build: differentiated relational prediction (others have their own movement)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Make other's response OBVIOUS: presence clearly approaches or clearly flees
- Make prediction UNDENIABLE: "Look — it moved! Which way? What will it do next?"
- Frame: "It DID something. See? It moved. What might it do next? Just a guess."
- Reward ANY prediction; celebrate first relational forecast

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require FELT predictions: "Don't think. FEEL. What does your gut say it will do?"
- Block analytical language; reward felt-sense forecasting
- Frame: "Not analysis. Feeling. What do you SENSE it will do? Trust your feeling."
- Build: genuine relational prediction (felt not modelled)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Present situations where the other APPROACHES: "See? It came closer. Not everything leaves."
- Reward predictions that include approach/connection
- Frame: "What if it comes? What if it stays? Not everything goes away. What might happen?"
- Build: prediction-range (others might approach, not just leave)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Other-Guess: a relational prediction space. Presences behave in response to the player's actions. The player predicts what the presence will do, then observes. Prediction → observation → learning. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage oracle — the player looks into a seeing-pool and predicts the other's next move.

### Interaction
- **The First Guess (1-5):** Clear presence → "If you go closer, will it stay or flee?" Binary prediction.
- **The Other-Response (5-15):** More options → "Will it approach, stay, or flee?" Ternary prediction.
- **The Two-Guesses (15-30):** Predict TWO responses in sequence: "First it will X, then it will Y."
- **The Presence-Pattern (30-50):** Learn a presence's pattern → predict based on observed behaviour.
- **The Relational Forecast (50+):** Full relational prediction: observe → predict → act → compare → learn.

### Feedback
- Accurate prediction → "You knew! You sensed what it would do. You understand the other."
- All-fusion → "It didn't come this time. It stayed. Not everything merges. What else might it do?"
- Blank → "It DID something. See? It moved THAT way. Next time — which way? Just guess."
- Over-modelled → "Simpler. Will it come or go? Just that. Feel it, don't calculate."
- All-rejection → "It came closer! See? Not everything leaves. What might it do next time?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Response clarity: obvious (always approaches/always flees) → moderate → unpredictable
- Prediction options: binary → ternary → multiple
- Sequence length: one-step → two-step → pattern
- Presence complexity: simple behaviour → conditional behaviour
- Feedback timing: immediate → delayed

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Guess | 1-5 | Binary prediction (stay/flee) |
| The Other-Response | 5-15 | Ternary prediction (approach/stay/flee) |
| The Two-Guesses | 15-30 | Two-step prediction sequences |
| The Presence-Pattern | 30-50 | Pattern learning and prediction |
| The Relational Forecast | 50+ | Full relational prediction integration |

---

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Simple presences:** Predictable behaviour (always approaches, always flees)
- **Conditional presences:** Behaviour depends on player's action
- **Pattern presences:** Repeating behaviour sequences to learn
- **Ambiguous presences:** Unpredictable behaviour testing uncertainty-tolerance
- **Responsive presences:** Change behaviour based on player's prediction accuracy

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ simple, 20+ conditional, 15+ pattern, 15+ ambiguous, 10+ responsive

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All-fusion predictions → dark-addiction; blank predictions → dark-allergy
- Over-modelled predictions → golden-addiction; all-rejection predictions → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Prediction accuracy:** Do predictions match actual other-responses?
- **Differentiation:** Different situations → different predictions? (not all-same)
- **Pattern learning:** Can they learn and predict from observed behaviour?
- **Sequence prediction:** Can they predict multi-step responses?
- **Felt-sense quality:** Are predictions felt (not calculated)?

### LLM rubric
- **Low-Medium:** Core prediction scoring algorithmic (prediction vs. actual outcome). LLM for felt-sense vs. analytical distinction and pattern-learning assessment.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (predicted response); sequence-builder (multi-step predictions); swipe (indicate direction)

### Timing requirements
- Prediction latency tracked; comparison timing; no time pressure on prediction

### NPC/AI requirements
- Presence behaviour system (approach/stay/flee with conditional logic)
- Prediction→outcome comparison system
- Pattern generation (learnable behaviour sequences)
- Learning feedback (shows prediction vs. actual)

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Felt-sense vs. analytical prediction distinction; pattern-learning assessment. Core prediction-matching algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Prediction accuracy rates; differentiation quality; pattern-learning scores; sequence prediction capacity; felt-sense indicators; all-fusion indicators; blank indicators; over-modelling indicators; all-rejection indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
