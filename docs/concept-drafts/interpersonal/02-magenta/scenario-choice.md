# Interpersonal / Magenta — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes interpersonal intelligence through RELATIONAL DECISIONS — can the player make choices that involve another being? At Magenta, this is: "The presence is there. Do you go toward it or away? Do you stay or leave?" The simplest relational choices — approach or avoid.
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Magenta:** Before complex relational decisions (Red+), there must be APPROACH/AVOID choices about others. At Magenta: "Do I go to the other?" The most basic interpersonal decision. This modality tests whether the player can make presence-guided relational choices.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Presence-Path"
- **Core mechanic:** The player encounters presences and must choose: approach or avoid? Stay or leave? Go closer or keep distance? Each choice reveals their relational orientation — toward connection or away from it. Not complex dilemmas — simple relational approach/avoid decisions.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Warm Path → The Presence-Choice → The Two-Others → The Staying → The Relational Heart

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "A presence is there. Do you go toward it? Do you stay near it? Do you leave?" The contact boundary is: "Can you make relational choices guided by other-sensing?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player choose regarding others? The Fusion-Addict always approaches (compulsive). The Phantom-Denier doesn't register the choice (no other perceived). The Premature Coordinator makes "strategic" relational choices without genuine sensing. The Bond-Refuser approaches then retreats.
- *Emergent:* Can they make calibrated relational choices — approaching when appropriate, maintaining distance when appropriate?

**Integration path:** Rewards CALIBRATED RELATIONAL CHOOSING — approaching when the presence is safe/warm, maintaining distance when uncertain, staying when connection is genuine. Not compulsive approach, not blind avoidance, not strategic performance, not approach-withdrawal.

**Successful integration:** The player makes relational choices guided by genuine other-sensing — the seed of relational wisdom.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Presence-Path: a relational choice space where presences appear at various locations and the player chooses their path relative to them. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage spirit-paths — glowing trails leading toward or away from presences. The player's choices light up their relational path.

### Interaction
- **The Warm Path (1-5):** One clear presence → approach or avoid? Simple binary.
- **The Presence-Choice (5-15):** Presence with emotional quality → approach/avoid based on quality.
- **The Two-Others (15-30):** Two presences → which to approach? Relational prioritising.
- **The Staying (30-50):** Approach AND stay → can they maintain proximity? Duration of connection.
- **The Relational Heart (50+):** Full relational choosing: sense → choose → approach → stay → calibrate distance.

### Feedback
- Calibrated choice → "You chose well. The right distance. You can feel them from here."
- Compulsive approach → "You don't have to go ALL the way. This distance is good too. Feel from here."
- No choice → "Something IS there. It matters which way you go. Can you feel which way is warm?"
- Strategic → "Not thinking. FEELING. Which way pulls? Trust the pull, not the plan."
- Approach-retreat → "You went toward! Good. Stay. Just a moment. It's safe here. Stay."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Presence clarity: obvious quality → moderate → ambiguous
- Choice complexity: binary (approach/avoid) → ternary (approach/stay/avoid) → distance-calibration
- Multiple presences: one → two → three (prioritising)
- Duration: momentary choice → sustained proximity
- Reversibility: always reversible → some commitment required

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Warm Path | 1-5 | Binary approach/avoid for clear presences |
| The Presence-Choice | 5-15 | Quality-guided choosing |
| The Two-Others | 15-30 | Choosing between multiple presences |
| The Staying | 30-50 | Sustained proximity (staying near) |
| The Relational Heart | 50+ | Full relational choice integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Clear presences:** Obvious warm/cold quality (easy approach/avoid)
- **Quality presences:** Emotional qualities requiring sensing-guided choice
- **Multiple presences:** Two or more requiring prioritisation
- **Duration items:** Requiring sustained proximity (staying)
- **Ambiguous presences:** Unclear quality testing choice under uncertainty

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ clear, 20+ quality, 15+ multiple, 15+ duration, 10+ ambiguous

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All-approach/compulsive → dark-addiction; no relational choice → dark-allergy
- Strategic/calculated choosing → golden-addiction; approach-retreat → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Directional (move toward/away); tap (choose presence); hold (sustain proximity); drag (calibrate distance)

### Timing requirements
- Choice latency tracked; proximity duration; approach-retreat cycling detection

### NPC/AI requirements
- Presence system with qualities (warm/cold/ambiguous)
- Distance-tracking (player position relative to presences)
- Multiple presence management
- Optional: companion who makes own relational choices

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Felt-sense vs. strategic choice distinction; approach-retreat pattern detection; relational quality assessment. Core approach/avoid scoring partially algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Choice calibration scores; discrimination quality; duration capacity; prioritisation quality; felt-sense indicators; compulsive-approach indicators; no-choice indicators; strategic indicators; approach-retreat indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
