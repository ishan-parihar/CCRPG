# Interpersonal / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes interpersonal intelligence through SPONTANEOUS RELATIONAL ENGAGEMENT in an open world populated with others — not structured detection or coordination tasks but free relational navigation. How does the player's interpersonal intelligence LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Interpersonal/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe other-sensing through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world is populated with phantom-allies, spirit-presences, and magical companions. The player's spontaneous relational engagement reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Companion-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world populated with phantom-allies, spirit-presences, and potential companions. The player's spontaneous relational behaviour is assessed. Do they sense others? Approach? Bond? Coordinate? Their ecological interpersonal behaviour reveals genuine capacity.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Presence-Noticing → Presence-Approaching → Presence-Bonding → Presence-Coordinating → The Living We-Space

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world is populated. Nobody says "sense the other" — but presences are there, companions are available, coordination opportunities exist. The player's NATURAL relational engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL interpersonal style surfaces. Do they merge with every presence? Ignore all others? Perform relating without genuine sensing? Approach then retreat? Their ecological relational behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they sense more accurately? Bond more genuinely? Coordinate more fluidly?

**Integration path:** The world rewards interpersonal intelligence NATURALLY. Players who sense presences find companions. Players who bond genuinely unlock shared content. Players who coordinate access cooperative paths. The reward is COMPANIONSHIP and BELONGING (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates interpersonal intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — sensing, approaching, bonding, and coordinating in the companion-world without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world populated with phantom-allies, spirit-presences, and potential companions. Every zone has relational content — presences to sense, companions to bond with, coordination opportunities. The world IS relational.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a populated world
- **Spontaneous sensing:** Noticing and responding to presences
- **Companion-gated content:** Paths/items requiring relational engagement
- **Bonding opportunities:** Presences that respond to genuine connection
- **Coordination opportunities:** Shared tasks requiring synchrony

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: relational engagement → companions, shared paths, bonding magic
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, solo limitations, stuck paths
- No explicit "interpersonal score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Relational content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Companion-gate sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated sensing quality
- Companion complexity scales with demonstrated bonding quality

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Presence-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice others? |
| Presence-Approaching | 5-15 | Do they approach presences? |
| Presence-Bonding | 15-30 | Can they form genuine bonds? |
| Presence-Coordinating | 30-50 | Can they coordinate with companions? |
| The Living We-Space | 50+ | Full ecological interpersonal intelligence |

---

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Presences:** Beings to sense and approach
- **Companions:** Potential bonding partners
- **Companion-gates:** Paths requiring relational engagement
- **Coordination tasks:** Shared activities requiring synchrony
- **Bonding spaces:** Areas for deepening connection

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ presences, 15+ companions, 15+ gates, 15+ coordination tasks, 10+ bonding spaces

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Merges with everything/cannot be alone → dark-addiction; ignores all presences → dark-allergy
- Mechanical relating → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage presence); hold (sustain connection); gesture (coordinate with companion)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, sensing quality, bonding duration)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of relational patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Populated environment (presences with varied qualities)
- Companion NPCs with dynamic relational states
- Companion-gating system (paths requiring relational engagement)
- Bonding-response system (companions respond to genuine connection)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, companion dynamics
- Detects spontaneous relational engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates interpersonal intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive relational content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; relational engagement history; navigation patterns; sensing accuracy records; bonding quality; coordination quality; companion relationships; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
