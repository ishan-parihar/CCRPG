# Emotional × Infrared — Module Specification

## 1. Capacity Definition

**Line:** Emotional Intelligence
**Stage:** Infrared (Archaic / Sensorimotor)
**Ray:** Red
**Core capacity:** Raw primary affect — the body's ability to activate and deactivate feeling-states (fear, rage, primary pleasure) without reflective awareness. Emotion IS the organism at this stage; there is no observer separate from the affect. No labelling, no regulation, no self-awareness — only: can the body feel? Can affect rise and fall?

**Developmental marker:** Presence or absence of primary affective responsiveness. The organism either feels or it doesn't. Activation either resolves or it doesn't.

---

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: Affect-Storm

Cannot leave emotional activation. Rage/fear loops endlessly without resolution. No return to baseline. Emotional flooding without discharge — the organism is trapped inside activation with no off-switch. Affect becomes a closed loop that feeds itself.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: Affect-Numbness

Cannot access primary affect. Emotional flatline. Dissociation from feeling — "nothing touches me." The organism has severed contact with its own affective ground. Stimuli arrive but produce no felt response. The body is present but unfelt.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: Premature Emotional Contagion

Attempts to feel others' emotions without genuine primary affect grounding. "Empathy" without self-feeling. Reaches toward relational affect (Magenta capacity) while bypassing the prerequisite: one's own primary feeling. Feels others instead of feeling self.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: Affect Fortress

Primary affect is present but refuses emotional expansion. Terrified of feeling more than survival-affect. The organism CAN feel but will not allow feeling to grow, deepen, or connect. Walls around the affective core prevent any movement toward the next stage.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

For DA (Affect-Storm): Agency provides the boundary that activation lacks — "I end here." Agape provides the return — settling back into the body after activation. Together they teach: affect activates, then resolves.

For DAll (Affect-Numbness): Agency provides the sovereign claim — "I am allowed to feel." Agape provides the embodiment — returning attention to the body where feeling lives. Together they teach: the body is safe to feel in.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

For GA (Premature Contagion): Communion grounds the reach — feeling WITH requires feeling FIRST. Eros redirects upward through one's own affect rather than borrowing others'. Together they teach: grow from your own root.

For GAll (Affect Fortress): Eros provides the upward pull — there is more to feel. Communion provides the relational invitation — feeling can expand safely toward another. Together they teach: expansion is not annihilation.

### 4.3 Dual-Vector Integration

Full health = affect activates cleanly (Agency), resolves fully (Agape), reaches toward growth (Eros), and remains available to contact (Communion). All four drives in balance produce an organism that feels freely, settles naturally, grows willingly, and connects openly.

## 6. Compound Shadows

### 6.1 The Frozen Storm (Emotional DA × Somatic DA)

Affect-storm meets hyperarousal. The body is locked in emotional activation with simultaneous somatic overwhelm. Feeling and flesh fuse into a single undischargeable state. Neither emotional nor physical resolution is possible — the organism is frozen mid-scream.

### 6.2 The Numb Body (Emotional DAll × Somatic DAll)

Affect-numbness meets freeze/collapse. Complete disconnection from both feeling and flesh. The organism exists but is not present to itself in any felt dimension. A ghost inhabiting meat.

### 6.3 The Premature Feeler (Emotional GA × Cognitive GA)

Premature emotional contagion meets premature symbolism. "Feeling meaning" without grounding in either genuine affect or genuine cognition. The organism constructs elaborate emotional narratives about others while having no contact with its own primary feeling.

### 6.4 The Affect Fortress (Emotional GAll × Willpower GAll)

Refuses emotional expansion AND refuses intentional emergence. Survival-only feeling locked behind survival-only willing. The organism can feel and can act but will not allow either to grow beyond the minimum required to stay alive.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Detection Phase

Games measure: activation latency (how quickly affect rises), deactivation speed (how quickly affect resolves), activation range (how many primary affects are accessible), and expansion tolerance (whether affect can grow without triggering fortress/numbness). Anomalies in any metric flag shadow presence.

### 7.2 Surfacing Phase

Once detected, shadows are surfaced through increased catalyst intensity in the relevant dimension. DA: stimuli that require resolution. DAll: stimuli that demand feeling. GA: stimuli that require self-feeling before other-feeling. GAll: stimuli that invite expansion without threat.

### 7.3 Integration Phase

Integration is confirmed when: activation rises AND resolves (DA healed), stimuli produce felt response (DAll healed), self-feeling precedes other-feeling (GA healed), and affect expands beyond survival-minimum (GAll healed). All metrics must stabilise across sessions.
