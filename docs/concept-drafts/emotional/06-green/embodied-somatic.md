# Emotional / Green — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Body as site of empathy — somatic empathy, where others' feelings live in own body, embodied co-regulation.  **Why this axis for this module:** Pluralistic empathy is not merely cognitive perspective-taking; it registers somatically. The body that can hold multiple affective signatures without bracing or flooding is the embodied ground of Green emotional capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Resonant Body  
**Core loop:** Player engages in rhythm-based and breath-gated interactions that simulate somatic co-regulation — synchronising with another's emotional rhythm, maintaining bodily coherence under empathic load, and distinguishing somatic self from somatic other.  
**Session length:** 4–7 minutes (infinite checkpoint).  
**Progression:** Co-regulation partners increase from 1 → 4; rhythm complexity increases; empathic load escalates; boundary challenges intensify.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Somatic-rhythmic under empathic resonance load.  
**Shadow surfacing:**
- DA surfaces as somatic flooding — player's rhythm destabilises when exposed to others' emotional rhythms, loses own baseline, body becomes undifferentiated resonance chamber.
- DAll surfaces as somatic rigidity — player maintains own rhythm perfectly but refuses synchronisation, body stays "sealed" against others' affect.
- GA surfaces as premature somatic transcendence — attempts to "hold all rhythms at once" without genuine attunement to any single one.
- GAll surfaces as accurate somatic empathy that braces against deepening — synchronises well but tenses/guards when transpersonal prompts appear.

**Drive probing:** Agency via somatic boundary maintenance (own rhythm stability); Communion via synchronisation quality; Eros via willingness to enter more complex somatic fields; Agape via return-to-baseline after co-regulation.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**
- **Baseline phase:** Player establishes own somatic rhythm via tapping/breathing pattern. This becomes their "home frequency."
- **Attunement phase:** An NPC partner expresses an emotional rhythm (visual + audio pulse). Player must synchronise — partial synchronisation, not total merger.
- **Multi-attunement phase:** 2–4 partners express different emotional rhythms simultaneously. Player must track all, selectively synchronise with one while holding awareness of others.
- **Boundary phase:** Partners' rhythms become more intense/distressed. Player must maintain co-regulation without losing own baseline.
- **Return phase:** After load, player must return to own baseline rhythm. Speed and accuracy of return measures boundary integrity.

**Scoring:** Synchronisation accuracy (timing precision), baseline stability (own rhythm coherence), multi-tracking (awareness of non-synchronised partners), boundary integrity (baseline deviation under load), return speed (recovery after engagement).

**Difficulty staircase:** Partner count, rhythm complexity, emotional intensity of partner rhythms, duration of load phases, introduction of out-group rhythmic patterns.

## 4. Item Pool

- 30+ emotional rhythm patterns (joy-pulse, grief-wave, anger-spike, anxiety-flutter, calm-tide)
- 15 multi-partner configurations (2–4 simultaneous rhythms)
- 10 boundary-escalation sequences (progressive intensity increase)
- Cultural rhythm variants (different somatic-emotional expression patterns)
- Transpersonal ceiling prompts (rhythms that invite dissolution — for GA/GAll detection)
- Return-to-baseline calibration sequences

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Input capture: tap timing at 16ms precision, or breath-detection via microphone (optional, fallback to tap)
- Rhythm-matching algorithm: phase-alignment scoring with tolerance windows
- Multi-rhythm display: visual + audio representation of 1–4 simultaneous emotional rhythms
- Baseline drift detection: continuous monitoring of player's home frequency stability
- Somatic load model: cumulative stress on baseline as function of partner intensity × duration
- Shadow detection: baseline-deviation > threshold flags DA; synchronisation-refusal flags DAll; all-rhythm-merge flags GA; tension-spike-at-transpersonal flags GAll
- Session persistence at phase boundaries
- Accessibility: visual-only mode for hearing-impaired; audio-only mode for vision-impaired
