# Emotional / Green — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Shared emotional field — group co-regulation, emotional attunement with multiple NPC partners simultaneously.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green empathy's social dimension is the shared field — not just feeling WITH one person but co-regulating across a group, maintaining attunement with multiple others whose emotional states differ and shift in real-time.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Empathic Circle  
**Core loop:** Player operates within a group of 3–6 NPC partners, each with distinct and shifting emotional states. Player must maintain attunement with all simultaneously, facilitate co-regulation between group members, and sustain the group's emotional coherence without sacrificing their own centre.  
**Session length:** 5–9 minutes (infinite checkpoint).  
**Progression:** Group size increases; emotional volatility escalates; inter-member conflicts introduced; out-group members added.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Multi-relational co-regulation under group complexity.  
**Shadow surfacing:**
- DA surfaces as group enmeshment — player loses own emotional state, becomes a mirror of whoever is most distressed, cannot maintain centre while attuning.
- DAll surfaces as facilitation without participation — player manages the group's emotions from outside, never entering the shared field themselves.
- GA surfaces as performed group attunement — player claims to "hold the space" but responses don't track individual members' actual states.
- GAll surfaces as genuine multi-attunement that refuses to let the group field deepen beyond comfortable pluralism.

**Drive probing:** Agency via centre-maintenance while attuning to group; Communion via quality of multi-relational co-regulation; Eros via willingness to let group field deepen; Agape via attention to each individual within the group (not just "the group").

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**
- **Attunement phase:** Group members express emotional states through dialogue, tone, and behaviour cues. Player must track each member's state and respond with appropriate attunement signals (selection from context-sensitive options).
- **Co-regulation phase:** One member becomes dysregulated. Player must co-regulate with them while maintaining awareness of how this affects other members.
- **Conflict phase:** Two members' emotional needs conflict. Player must facilitate without taking sides, holding both perspectives in the group field.
- **Field-deepening phase:** The group's shared emotional state intensifies. Player must allow deepening without losing individual tracking or own centre.
- **Centre-check phase:** Periodic prompts ask player to identify their OWN emotional state distinct from the group's. Accuracy measures boundary integrity.

**Scoring:** Multi-attunement accuracy (tracking all members), co-regulation effectiveness (dysregulated member stabilises), conflict facilitation (both parties feel held), field-coherence (group emotional state remains integrated), centre-integrity (player's own state remains distinct and stable).

**Difficulty staircase:** Group size, emotional volatility, conflict frequency, out-group member introduction, field-deepening intensity.

## 4. Item Pool

- 40+ NPC partner profiles with distinct emotional signatures and triggers
- 25 group configurations (varying emotional dynamics)
- 20 dysregulation scenarios (different types of emotional crisis)
- 15 inter-member conflict patterns
- 10 field-deepening sequences (progressive intensity)
- Out-group member profiles (cultural, neurotype, age diversity)
- Centre-check prompts calibrated to current group emotional state

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Multi-agent emotional state engine: each NPC has independent affect model that responds to player input and other NPCs
- Group-field model: emergent property of individual states + player's facilitation quality
- Co-regulation mechanics: player input → NPC state change with realistic latency and resistance
- Centre-tracking system: periodic self-report prompts with accuracy scoring against player's behavioural indicators
- Shadow detection: centre-loss-under-load flags DA; facilitation-without-entry flags DAll; generic-responses-not-tracking-individuals flags GA; field-deepening-refusal flags GAll
- Session persistence at phase boundaries
- NPC dialogue generation: context-sensitive emotional expression (template-based with variation)
