# Emotional / Magenta — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** The scenario-choice axis probes emotional intelligence through FEELING-INFORMED DECISIONS — can the player use emotional sensing to guide choices? At Magenta, this is: "This path feels scary. That path feels warm. Which do you choose?" Feelings as navigation data.
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Magenta:** Before emotions become decision-tools (Orange), they must first INFORM choices at the most basic level. At Magenta: "I go toward what feels good and away from what feels bad." This modality tests whether affect-sensing connects to action.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Feeling-Path"
- **Core mechanic:** The player faces choices where emotional atmosphere is the ONLY information. No logic, no rules — just: "This feels X, that feels Y. Which do you choose?" Can feelings guide navigation? Can the player use mood as a compass?
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Warm Path → The Mood-Compass → The Feeling-Dilemma → The Mood-Trust → The Guided Heart

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Two paths. One feels warm. One feels cold. Which do you take? Why?" The contact boundary is: "Can your feelings guide your choices?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player use feelings in decisions? The Mood-Flooder is overwhelmed by both paths. The Affect-Denier can't sense either path's mood. The Premature Empath over-analyses both paths. The Feeling-Refuser senses but refuses to let feelings guide.
- *Emergent:* Can they sense the mood of each option AND use that sensing to choose?

**Integration path:** Rewards FEELING-GUIDED CHOOSING — using emotional sensing as navigation data. Not overwhelmed by options, not blind to their moods, not over-analysing, not refusing to let feelings matter.

**Successful integration:** The player uses emotional sensing to guide choices — feelings become functional navigation data.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing independently based on own feeling-sense.
- Healthy: Uses own emotional sensing to choose; trusts own feelings as data
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by feelings of both options; cannot choose; OR: ignores feelings entirely
- **Detection:** Whether own feeling-sense produces a choice

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Choosing when feelings are ambiguous (no clear "warm" vs. "cold").
- Healthy: Makes feeling-guided choice even under ambiguity; tolerates uncertainty
- Pathological: Cannot choose without clear emotional signal; freezes
- **Detection:** Choice-making quality under emotional ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Choosing when companion's feelings point differently.
- Healthy: Holds own feeling-guided choice while acknowledging companion's
- Pathological: Abandons own feeling-sense for companion's; OR: dismisses companion's feelings
- **Detection:** Whether own feeling-guided choice survives social context

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared feeling-guided choosing (deciding together based on shared sensing).
- Healthy: Integrates own and companion's feeling-sense into shared choice
- Pathological: Cannot share feeling-guided choosing; decisions are private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared feeling-guided decision-making

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar feeling-choices (same paths, same moods).
- Healthy: Maintains genuine feeling-contact on familiar choices
- Pathological: Choosing becomes habitual; feelings no longer consulted
- **Detection:** Whether familiar choices still involve genuine sensing

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel feeling-choices (unfamiliar emotional atmospheres).
- Healthy: Engages novel feeling-choices with curiosity
- Pathological: Cannot use feelings to navigate novel situations; overwhelmed or frozen
- **Detection:** Feeling-guided choosing quality in novel contexts

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining feeling-guided choosing over time.
- Healthy: Feeling-guided choosing remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Feeling-choice connection degrades; choices become random
- **Detection:** Feeling-choice alignment across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether feeling-guided choosing appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Uses feelings to guide choices in non-emotional tasks
- Pathological: Feeling-guided choosing exists only in explicit emotional tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of feeling-guided choosing to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Mood-Flooder"
**How it surfaces:** OVERWHELMED by both options. Cannot choose because BOTH paths flood them. "They both feel SO MUCH." Or: always chooses the most intense option regardless of valence. Intensity-seeking in choices.
- **Detection signals:** Cannot choose (overwhelmed by both); always chooses highest-intensity option; distress at choice-points; "both feel too much"; intensity-seeking regardless of valence

### Dark-Allergy: "The Affect-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** Cannot sense the mood of either option. "They both feel the same." "I don't feel anything from either." Choices are random because there's no emotional data to guide them.
- **Detection signals:** Reports both options feel identical; random choice patterns; "I don't feel anything"; choice-mood alignment at chance level; no emotional data in decision-making

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"
**How it surfaces:** OVER-ANALYSES both options. "The first path has undertones of melancholic hope while the second suggests a bittersweet..." Sophisticated analysis that doesn't produce a choice. Analysis paralysis through emotional over-reading.
- **Detection signals:** Elaborate analysis without choosing; sophisticated emotional vocabulary at choice-points; analysis paralysis; cannot simplify to "this feels better"; over-reads both options

### Golden-Allergy: "The Feeling-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** SENSES both moods but refuses to let feelings guide the choice. "I can feel them but I'll choose based on something else." Actively rejects emotional data as valid decision-input.
- **Detection signals:** Accurate sensing + non-feeling-based choosing; "feelings shouldn't decide"; chooses against own feeling-sense; rejects emotional data; prefers "logic" (which doesn't exist at Magenta)

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present options at LOWER emotional intensity: "Gentle feelings. One is a little warm. One is a little cool. Which?"
- Reward choosing at low intensity; make overwhelm unnecessary
- Frame: "Not intense. Quiet feelings. One is slightly warmer. Can you feel the difference? Choose the warmer one."
- Build: calibrated feeling-guided choosing (not intensity-driven)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Make emotional difference OBVIOUS: one path clearly warm/bright, other clearly cold/dark
- Require feeling-based choice: "Which one feels BETTER? Not thinks. Feels."
- Frame: "One is warm. One is cold. You can feel it. Which feels better? Go there."
- Reward ANY feeling-guided choice; celebrate first feeling-based decision

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require QUICK choosing: "Don't think. Feel. Choose NOW. Which one? Go."
- Time-limit prevents over-analysis; reward gut-feeling choices
- Frame: "No time to analyse. Just feel. Which one pulls you? Go. NOW."
- Build: intuitive feeling-guided choosing (not analytical)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make feeling-guided choices PRODUCE BETTER OUTCOMES than non-feeling choices
- Show that feelings are USEFUL data: "You felt the warm one. Look — it led somewhere good."
- Frame: "Your feelings knew. They're not just feelings — they're a compass. Trust them."
- Build: feeling-trust (emotions as valid decision-data)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Feeling-Path: a choice-space where emotional atmosphere is the only navigation data. Paths with distinct moods — warm/cold, bright/dark, calm/agitated. No signs, no logic — just feeling. The player navigates by mood.

### Interaction
- **The Warm Path (1-5):** Two paths, one clearly warm, one clearly cold. Choose the warm one.
- **The Mood-Compass (5-15):** Multiple paths with different moods. Navigate toward a target feeling.
- **The Feeling-Dilemma (15-30):** Two paths, both attractive but different moods. Which feeling matters more?
- **The Mood-Trust (30-50):** Path that FEELS wrong but leads right (or vice versa). Can feelings be trusted?
- **The Guided Heart (50+):** Full feeling-guided navigation: sense → choose → trust → navigate.

### Feedback
- Feeling-guided choice → "Your feelings led you. See where you are? Your heart knew."
- Overwhelmed → "Quieter. Both are gentle. One is just a LITTLE warmer. Feel for the little difference."
- No sensing → "One is warm. Really. Feel it. The warmth. It's there. Choose the warm one."
- Over-analysis → "Stop thinking. Feel. Quick. Which one? Go. Your gut knows."
- Refuses feelings → "Try it. Just once. Let the feeling choose. See what happens."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Mood difference between options: large → moderate → subtle
- Number of options: 2 → 3 → 4
- Mood valence: clear (warm=good) → ambiguous (both good, different) → paradoxical
- Time pressure: none → moderate (prevents over-analysis)
- Outcome feedback: immediate → delayed

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Warm Path | 1-5 | Clear warm vs. cold; obvious choice |
| The Mood-Compass | 5-15 | Navigate toward target feeling |
| The Feeling-Dilemma | 15-30 | Both attractive; which feeling matters more? |
| The Mood-Trust | 30-50 | Feelings as reliable navigation data |
| The Guided Heart | 50+ | Full feeling-guided navigation |

---

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Clear-contrast items:** Obvious mood differences between paths
- **Navigation items:** Multiple paths toward a target feeling
- **Dilemma items:** Two attractive options with different moods
- **Trust items:** Feeling-guided choices with delayed outcome feedback
- **Ambiguity items:** Subtle mood differences requiring calibrated sensing

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ clear-contrast, 20+ navigation, 15+ dilemma, 15+ trust, 10+ ambiguity

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Overwhelmed/intensity-seeking → dark-addiction; no sensing/random → dark-allergy
- Over-analysis/paralysis → golden-addiction; sensing without using → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Feeling-choice alignment:** Does the choice match the emotional data?
- **Sensing accuracy at choice-point:** Can they identify the mood of each option?
- **Choice latency:** Appropriate speed (not impulsive, not paralysed)
- **Feeling-trust:** Do they use feelings as valid data? (not override with "logic")
- **Dilemma resolution:** Can they choose between two attractive feelings?

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, dilemma construction, feeling-trust assessment. Core choice-mood alignment partially algorithmic.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (path choice); hold-to-sense (linger on option to feel it); swipe (navigate)

### Timing requirements
- Choice latency tracked; sensing-duration per option; no punishing time pressure but latency informative

### NPC/AI requirements
- Path visualization with distinct emotional atmospheres (colour, sound, movement)
- Mood-contrast system (ensures options are distinguishable)
- Optional: companion who models feeling-guided choosing

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, dilemma construction, outcome narratives. Core choice-mood alignment partially algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Feeling-choice alignment rates; sensing accuracy at choice-points; choice latencies; feeling-trust indicators; dilemma resolution quality; overwhelm indicators; no-sensing indicators; over-analysis indicators; feeling-rejection indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
