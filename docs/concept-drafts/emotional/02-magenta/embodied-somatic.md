# Emotional / Magenta — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The embodied-somatic axis probes emotional intelligence through THE BODY — can the player feel emotions IN the body? At Magenta, this is: "Where does the feeling live? Is it warm or cold? Heavy or light?" The body as the first emotional instrument.
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Magenta:** Emotions are felt IN the body before they are named or understood. At Magenta, the body IS the emotional organ — feelings are sensations. This modality tests whether the player can locate and describe emotional sensations somatically.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Feeling-Body"
- **Core mechanic:** Emotional atmospheres are presented and the player identifies WHERE in the body they feel it and WHAT it feels like somatically (warm/cold, heavy/light, tight/open, buzzing/still). The body maps the emotion.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; body-fatigue monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The Body-Feeling → The Feeling-Place → The Feeling-Shape → The Moving Feeling → The Emotional Body

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "You feel something. Where in your body? What does it feel like there? Warm? Cold? Heavy? Light?" The contact boundary is: "Can you feel emotions in your body?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player's body relate to emotion? The Mood-Flooder's body is overwhelmed everywhere. The Affect-Denier's body feels nothing. The Premature Empath describes body-feelings that don't match the stimulus. The Feeling-Refuser senses body-feeling then blocks it.
- *Emergent:* Can they locate a feeling in the body and describe its somatic quality?

**Integration path:** Rewards SOMATIC AFFECT-AWARENESS — locating emotions in the body with accurate somatic description. Not overwhelmed everywhere, not numb, not performing, not blocking.

**Successful integration:** The player locates emotions in specific body areas and describes their somatic quality accurately.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Locating body-feelings independently.
- Healthy: Identifies body-location and quality by own sensing
- Pathological: Body overwhelmed everywhere (no location); OR: body reports nothing
- **Detection:** Whether body-feeling has specific location vs. diffuse/absent

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Sensing body-feelings in ambiguous emotional contexts.
- Healthy: Trusts body-sensing even when emotion is unclear
- Pathological: Cannot sense body-feeling without clear emotional stimulus
- **Detection:** Body-sensing quality under emotional ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Body-feeling when companion reports different location.
- Healthy: Maintains own body-sensing while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's body-location; loses own sensing
- **Detection:** Whether own body-sensing survives social comparison

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared body-feeling exploration (comparing somatic experiences).
- Healthy: Shares own body-sensing AND receives companion's with interest
- Pathological: Cannot share body-feelings; somatic experience is private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared somatic-emotional exploration

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar body-feelings (same emotions, same body-locations).
- Healthy: Maintains genuine body-contact on familiar emotions
- Pathological: Body-sensing becomes rote; loses genuine somatic contact
- **Detection:** Whether familiar body-feelings retain genuine quality

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel body-feelings (unfamiliar somatic-emotional experiences).
- Healthy: Explores novel body-feelings with curiosity
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by unfamiliar body-sensations; OR: forces familiar patterns
- **Detection:** Quality of novel somatic-emotional exploration

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining body-emotion connection over time.
- Healthy: Body-emotion link remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Body-emotion connection degrades; body becomes numb to feelings
- **Detection:** Body-emotion connection quality across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether body-emotion sensing appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Notices body-feelings naturally in non-emotional tasks
- Pathological: Body-emotion sensing exists only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous somatic-emotional awareness

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Mood-Flooder"
**How it surfaces:** Body is overwhelmed EVERYWHERE. "I feel it in my chest AND stomach AND head AND arms AND legs AND..." No specific location — the entire body is flooded. Somatic overwhelm mirrors emotional overwhelm.
- **Detection signals:** Reports feeling in ALL body areas simultaneously; maximum intensity everywhere; no specific location; body-flood; "it's everywhere"; cannot isolate a single body-area

### Dark-Allergy: "The Affect-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** Body reports NOTHING. "I don't feel anything in my body." No somatic sensation associated with emotion. The body-emotion link is absent. Somatic numbness.
- **Detection signals:** Reports no body-sensation; "nothing" for all emotions; no location identified; flat somatic response; body-emotion link absent; cannot locate any feeling in body

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"
**How it surfaces:** Reports SOPHISTICATED body-feelings that don't match the stimulus. "I feel a spiralling warmth in my solar plexus with radiating tendrils of..." Elaborate somatic description disconnected from actual sensing.
- **Detection signals:** Elaborate descriptions that don't match stimulus; sophisticated somatic vocabulary without genuine sensing; body-descriptions that sound learned not felt; inconsistent across similar stimuli

### Golden-Allergy: "The Feeling-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** BEGINS to sense body-feeling then BLOCKS it. "I started to feel something in my chest but..." Approach-withdrawal at the somatic level. The body senses then shuts down.
- **Detection signals:** Brief body-sensation followed by "nothing"; approach-withdrawal pattern; "I felt something but it went away"; initial sensing then shutdown; body-blocking

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Direct attention to ONE body area: "Just your chest. Only there. What does JUST your chest feel?"
- Reward specific location over diffuse flooding
- Frame: "Not everywhere. One place. Put your attention in ONE spot. What's there?"
- Build: focused somatic sensing (one area at a time)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Start with TEMPERATURE: "Is your chest warm or cool? Just temperature. Nothing else."
- Make the question ultra-simple; accept minimal somatic data
- Frame: "Not a feeling yet. Just: warm or cool? Right there. That's enough."
- Reward ANY somatic report; celebrate first body-sensation

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require SIMPLE somatic language: "Warm or cold? Heavy or light? That's all."
- Penalise elaborate descriptions; reward basic accurate sensing
- Frame: "Not a story. One word. Warm? Cold? Heavy? Light? Tight? Open? Just that."
- Build: honest somatic sensing (simple and accurate)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make body-sensing BRIEF and SAFE: "Just notice for 1 second. Then you can stop."
- Reduce threat of sustained body-feeling; make it tiny
- Frame: "One second. What's there? Then done. Safe. Just one second of noticing."
- Build: somatic safety (body-feelings aren't dangerous)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Feeling-Body: a somatic-emotional mapping space. Emotional atmospheres are presented and the player maps where they feel them in the body. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage body-map — a glowing body-outline where touched areas light up with the colour/quality of the feeling found there.

### Interaction
- **The Body-Feeling (1-5):** Clear emotion → "Where do you feel it?" Tap body-area.
- **The Feeling-Place (5-15):** Identify quality: warm/cold, heavy/light, tight/open.
- **The Feeling-Shape (15-30):** Describe the shape/size of the body-feeling.
- **The Moving Feeling (30-50):** Track how body-feelings move/change over time.
- **The Emotional Body (50+):** Full somatic-emotional mapping: locate → describe → track.

### Feedback
- Specific location + accurate quality → "There! You found it. That's where the feeling lives."
- Body-flood → "Everywhere? Let's narrow. Just ONE spot. Where is it STRONGEST?"
- Nothing → "Warm or cool? Just in your chest. Not a feeling — just temperature. What's there?"
- Over-elaborate → "Simpler. Warm or cold? That's enough. Just that."
- Approach-withdrawal → "You felt it! For a moment. That's real. Can you find it again? Just briefly."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Emotion intensity: high (easy to feel in body) → moderate → subtle
- Body-area precision: large area → specific location → point
- Quality discrimination: binary (warm/cold) → ternary → multiple qualities
- Duration of sensing: brief → moderate → sustained
- Emotion complexity: single → shifting → mixed

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Body-Feeling | 1-5 | Locate feeling in body (tap area) |
| The Feeling-Place | 5-15 | Describe quality (warm/cold, heavy/light) |
| The Feeling-Shape | 15-30 | Shape and size of body-feeling |
| The Moving Feeling | 30-50 | Track changes in body-feeling over time |
| The Emotional Body | 50+ | Full somatic-emotional integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Location items:** Emotions with clear body-locations (anger→chest, fear→stomach, etc.)
- **Quality items:** Same location, different qualities (warm vs. cold in chest)
- **Shape items:** Body-feelings with distinct shapes/sizes
- **Movement items:** Body-feelings that shift location or quality
- **Subtle items:** Low-intensity body-feelings requiring careful sensing

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ location, 20+ quality, 15+ shape, 15+ movement, 10+ subtle

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All-areas/flood → dark-addiction; no-area/nothing → dark-allergy
- Elaborate/inconsistent → golden-addiction; brief-then-blocked → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Location specificity:** Can they identify a specific body-area? (not "everywhere" or "nowhere")
- **Quality accuracy:** Does the reported quality match expected somatic correlates?
- **Consistency:** Same emotion → same body-area across trials?
- **Discrimination:** Different emotions → different body-areas/qualities?
- **Tracking ability:** Can they follow body-feeling changes over time?

### LLM rubric
- **Low-Medium:** Core location/quality scoring partially algorithmic (tap-area + quality-selection). LLM needed for open-ended somatic descriptions and consistency evaluation.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-on-body-map (location); select quality (warm/cold/heavy/light/tight/open); hold (sustained sensing); drag (track movement)

### Timing requirements
- Sensing duration tracked; no time pressure; body-fatigue especially monitored (shorter sessions)

### NPC/AI requirements
- Body-map visualization (glowing outline with interactive areas)
- Emotion-to-body mapping system (expected correlates for validation)
- Quality visualization (colours/textures for somatic qualities)
- Optional: companion who shares their body-sensing

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Open-ended somatic description evaluation; consistency checking across trials. Core tap-location and quality-selection algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Body-location patterns; quality reports; consistency scores; discrimination scores; tracking ability; flood indicators (all-areas); numbness indicators (no-area); elaboration indicators; blocking indicators; drive/shadow signals; body-fatigue state (CRITICAL); checkpoint position
