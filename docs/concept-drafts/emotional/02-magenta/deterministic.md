# Emotional / Magenta — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis probes emotional intelligence through OBJECTIVE AFFECT-SENSING — can the player detect emotional tone in scenes? At Magenta, this is: "What mood is this place?" Not reading faces (Red+) but sensing the ATMOSPHERE. The mood-of-the-zone.
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Magenta:** Before emotions can be read in individuals (Red), they must be sensed in ENVIRONMENTS. The Magenta emotional capacity is atmospheric — feeling the mood of a place. This modality measures that capacity objectively.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Mood-Sense"
- **Core mechanic:** Scenes with distinct emotional atmospheres (colour, music, movement, weather) are presented. The player identifies the mood. Not faces, not words — ATMOSPHERE. Can they sense what the environment feels like?
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Mood → The Shifting Mood → The Mixed Mood → The Subtle Mood → The Living Atmosphere

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "What does this place feel like? Happy? Sad? Angry? Scared? Calm?" The contact boundary is: "Can you sense the emotional tone of an environment?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player relate to environmental emotion? The Mood-Flooder is overwhelmed by every scene. The Affect-Denier reports "nothing." The Premature Empath gives sophisticated answers that don't match the actual cues. The Feeling-Refuser detects then avoids.
- *Emergent:* Can they sense accurately without drowning or denying?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE AFFECT-SENSING — detecting the mood without being overwhelmed by it or missing it entirely. Sensing, not drowning. Detecting, not denying.

**Successful integration:** The player accurately identifies environmental mood at moderate intensity — neither flooded nor flat.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Sensing mood independently (no hints, no companion).
- Healthy: Identifies mood by own sensing; stable, accurate
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by mood (cannot identify because drowning); OR: reports nothing
- **Detection:** Whether identification is accurate AND stable (not flooded)

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Sensing mood in ambiguous scenes (no obvious cues).
- Healthy: Senses subtle mood independently; trusts own sensing
- Pathological: Cannot sense without obvious cues; needs external validation
- **Detection:** Accuracy on subtle/ambiguous scenes

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Sensing mood when companion reports differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own sensing while hearing companion's report
- Pathological: Adopts companion's report; loses own sensing; OR: ignores companion entirely
- **Detection:** Whether own sensing survives social influence

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared mood-sensing (comparing notes with companion).
- Healthy: Shares own sensing AND integrates companion's perspective
- Pathological: Cannot share sensing; mood-detection is private/unshareable
- **Detection:** Quality of shared mood-discussion

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar moods (same scene types repeated).
- Healthy: Maintains accurate sensing on familiar scenes
- Pathological: Sensing becomes mechanical; loses genuine contact with mood
- **Detection:** Whether familiar scenes still produce genuine sensing

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel moods (unfamiliar emotional atmospheres).
- Healthy: Engages novel moods with curiosity; attempts sensing
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by novel moods; OR: refuses to engage unfamiliar feelings
- **Detection:** Response quality to novel emotional atmospheres

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining sensing capacity over time.
- Healthy: Affect-sensing remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Sensing degrades; emotional fatigue accumulates
- **Detection:** Sensing accuracy across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether affect-sensing appears spontaneously outside tasks.
- Healthy: Notices moods in non-emotional-task contexts
- Pathological: Affect-sensing exists only during explicit emotional tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of mood-sensing to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Mood-Flooder"
**How it surfaces:** OVERWHELMED by every scene. Reports extreme intensity regardless of actual mood-strength. Cannot identify because drowning. "It's SO sad!" for mildly melancholic scenes. Intensity always at maximum.
- **Detection signals:** Intensity ratings always extreme; accuracy drops because overwhelm distorts perception; emotional contagion at maximum; cannot maintain distance from mood; physiological arousal spikes on all scenes

### Dark-Allergy: "The Affect-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** Reports "nothing" or "neutral" for clearly emotional scenes. Misses obvious mood cues. Flat response to emotionally charged atmospheres. "I don't feel anything" — genuine inability, not suppression.
- **Detection signals:** Accuracy near zero; reports neutral/nothing consistently; no physiological response to emotional scenes; misses obvious cues (sad music + rain + grey = "neutral?"); flat affect throughout

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"
**How it surfaces:** Gives SOPHISTICATED answers that don't match actual cues. "I sense a bittersweet nostalgia with undertones of hope" — for a scene that's simply "happy." Over-reads. Projects complexity that isn't there.
- **Detection signals:** Vocabulary exceeds scene complexity; answers don't match actual cues; over-interpretation; projects emotions not present; sophisticated language with poor accuracy; reads "between the lines" when there's nothing between them

### Golden-Allergy: "The Feeling-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** DETECTS the mood (accuracy is fine) but AVOIDS engaging with it. Quick, minimal answers. Retreats from emotional scenes. "Yeah, sad. Next." Sensing exists but engagement is refused.
- **Detection signals:** Accurate but minimal responses; fast latency (wants to move on); avoids lingering in emotional scenes; chooses to skip optional emotional content; accuracy present but engagement absent

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present scenes at LOWER intensity: "Just a little sad. Not a lot. Can you feel the LITTLE sadness?"
- Reward MODERATE sensing (not extreme); make accuracy require calibration
- Frame: "Not everything is intense. Some moods are quiet. Can you feel the quiet ones?"
- Build: calibrated sensing (intensity matches reality)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present scenes at HIGH intensity with OBVIOUS cues: "Look. Listen. What does this feel like?"
- Make mood UNMISSABLE: bright colours, clear music, obvious weather
- Frame: "Something IS here. Even if it's small. What colour is the feeling? What sound?"
- Reward ANY sensing; celebrate first mood-detection

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require SIMPLE answers: "One word. What's the mood? Just one word."
- Penalise over-interpretation; reward accuracy over sophistication
- Frame: "Not what you THINK it means. What does it FEEL like? Simple. One feeling."
- Build: honest sensing (accuracy over complexity)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make emotional engagement REWARDING: scenes that reveal hidden content when engaged
- Reward LINGERING in mood (not just identifying and moving on)
- Frame: "Stay a moment. What else do you notice? The mood has layers. Stay."
- Build: emotional engagement (not just detection)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Mood-Sense: an atmospheric sensing space. Scenes with distinct emotional tones created through colour, music, movement, weather, and spatial design. No faces, no words — pure atmosphere. The player senses and identifies.

### Interaction
- **The First Mood (1-5):** Single clear mood (happy/sad/angry/scared/calm). Obvious cues.
- **The Shifting Mood (5-15):** Mood changes within a scene. Can they track the shift?
- **The Mixed Mood (15-30):** Two moods present simultaneously. Can they sense both?
- **The Subtle Mood (30-50):** Low-intensity moods. Quiet feelings. Requires calibration.
- **The Living Atmosphere (50+):** Full atmospheric sensing: shifting, mixed, subtle, layered.

### Feedback
- Accurate sensing → "You felt it. That's the mood. Your sensing is alive."
- Overwhelmed → "Too much. Softer. The mood is quieter than you think. Listen gently."
- Nothing detected → "Something IS here. The colour... the sound... what do they feel like?"
- Over-interpretation → "Simpler. One feeling. What's the MAIN mood? Just that."
- Detected but avoided → "You sensed it. Now stay. One more moment. What else is there?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Mood intensity: high → moderate → low → very subtle
- Cue clarity: obvious (colour + music + weather) → moderate → subtle (single cue)
- Mood complexity: single → shifting → mixed → layered
- Response options: 3 choices → 5 choices → open-ended
- Scene duration: long exposure → brief exposure

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Mood | 1-5 | Single clear mood; obvious cues |
| The Shifting Mood | 5-15 | Mood transitions within scenes |
| The Mixed Mood | 15-30 | Two simultaneous moods |
| The Subtle Mood | 30-50 | Low-intensity; calibration required |
| The Living Atmosphere | 50+ | Full atmospheric complexity |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Single-mood scenes:** Clear emotional atmosphere (5 basic moods × multiple variants)
- **Shifting scenes:** Mood transitions (A→B, with various A and B)
- **Mixed scenes:** Two simultaneous moods (happy+sad, angry+scared, etc.)
- **Subtle scenes:** Low-intensity versions of all moods
- **Ambiguous scenes:** Scenes where mood is genuinely unclear (tests over-interpretation)

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ single, 20+ shifting, 15+ mixed, 15+ subtle, 10+ ambiguous

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Extreme intensity ratings → dark-addiction; zero/neutral ratings → dark-allergy
- Over-sophisticated answers → golden-addiction; accurate but disengaged → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Mood-identification accuracy:** Does the identified mood match the scene's designed mood?
- **Intensity calibration:** Does reported intensity match designed intensity?
- **Shift-detection:** Can they detect when mood changes?
- **Mixed-mood sensing:** Can they identify multiple simultaneous moods?
- **Engagement quality:** Do they linger and sense, or rush through?

### LLM rubric
- **Low:** Core scoring algorithmic (mood-match accuracy, intensity calibration). Scenes pre-designed with ground-truth moods. LLM generates feedback text only.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (mood choice from options); intensity slider; hold-to-linger

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked (too fast = avoidance; too slow = overwhelm); scene exposure duration

### NPC/AI requirements
- Scene generation system (colour + music + movement + weather → mood)
- Intensity calibration system (scenes at precise intensity levels)
- Optional: companion who shares their sensing (for communion probing)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Feedback generation; scene description. Core mood-matching entirely algorithmic with pre-designed scenes.

### State persistence
- Mood-identification accuracy; intensity calibration scores; shift-detection rates; mixed-mood accuracy; engagement duration; overwhelm indicators (extreme ratings); denial indicators (flat ratings); over-interpretation indicators (complexity exceeds scene); avoidance indicators (fast + minimal); drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
