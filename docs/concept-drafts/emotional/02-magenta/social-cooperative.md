# Emotional / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes emotional intelligence through SHARED FEELING — can the player feel WITH another? At Magenta, this is emotional contagion and co-feeling: "They feel sad. Do you feel it too?" Not empathy (that requires differentiation) — CONTAGION. Feeling together without boundary.
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Magenta:** Before empathy (Red+), there is CONTAGION — catching feelings from others. At Magenta, self-other emotional boundaries don't exist. This modality tests whether the player can feel WITH a companion — the relational seed of emotional intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Feeling-Together"
- **Core mechanic:** The player and companion share emotional space. The companion feels things; the player catches (or doesn't catch) those feelings. Can they feel WITH? Can they share emotional space? The proto-empathic bond through contagion.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Shared Mood → The Feeling-Catch → The Mood-Match → The Feeling-Gift → The Emotional Bond

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Your companion feels sad. Can you feel it too? Not think about it — FEEL it. What happens in you when they feel?" The contact boundary is: "Can you feel with another?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player relate to companion's emotions? The Mood-Flooder drowns in companion's feeling (total fusion). The Affect-Denier doesn't register companion's emotion at all. The Premature Empath performs understanding without genuine co-feeling. The Feeling-Refuser senses companion's emotion then blocks it.
- *Emergent:* Can they catch the feeling at moderate intensity? Feel WITH without drowning or denying?

**Integration path:** Rewards MODERATE EMOTIONAL CONTAGION — catching companion's feeling at appropriate intensity. Not total fusion, not total isolation, not performed empathy, not blocked feeling.

**Successful integration:** The player feels WITH the companion — genuine emotional contagion at moderate intensity, the foundation of empathy.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Feeling-Together: a shared emotional space where player and companion feel together. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage feeling-bridge — two figures connected by a glowing emotional bridge. When co-feeling happens, the bridge brightens. When blocked, it dims. When flooded, it overwhelms both figures.

### Interaction
- **The Shared Mood (1-5):** Companion feels clearly → player identifies what they catch. Simple contagion.
- **The Feeling-Catch (5-15):** Companion's feeling is subtler → can they still catch it?
- **The Mood-Match (15-30):** Match intensity — catch the feeling at the RIGHT level (not too much, not too little).
- **The Feeling-Gift (30-50):** Share OWN feeling with companion. Initiate emotional exchange.
- **The Emotional Bond (50+):** Full mutual co-feeling: catch → match → share → exchange.

### Feedback
- Moderate co-feeling → "You felt it with them. Just right. The bridge glows warm."
- Total fusion → "Too much. Their feeling is theirs. You can feel WITH, not AS. Gentler."
- Nothing → "They're feeling something. Can you let it touch you? Even a tiny bit? It's safe."
- Performed → "Not what you THINK. What do you FEEL? Let their feeling actually reach you."
- Blocked → "You felt it starting. Let it stay. Just a moment. It won't hurt. Let it in."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Companion emotion intensity: high (easy to catch) → moderate → subtle
- Emotion valence: positive (safe) → neutral → negative (more threatening)
- Contagion calibration: any catching → moderate catching → precise matching
- Sharing requirement: receive only → receive + share → mutual exchange
- Duration: brief co-feeling → sustained co-feeling

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Shared Mood | 1-5 | Simple contagion; catch companion's clear feeling |
| The Feeling-Catch | 5-15 | Catch subtler feelings |
| The Mood-Match | 15-30 | Match intensity appropriately |
| The Feeling-Gift | 30-50 | Share own feeling with companion |
| The Emotional Bond | 50+ | Full mutual emotional exchange |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Clear contagion items:** Companion with obvious emotions to catch
- **Subtle contagion items:** Companion with quiet emotions
- **Calibration items:** Requiring intensity-matching (not just detection)
- **Sharing items:** Opportunities to initiate emotional exchange
- **Mutual items:** Bidirectional emotional exchange scenarios

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ clear, 20+ subtle, 15+ calibration, 15+ sharing, 10+ mutual

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Total fusion/overwhelm → dark-addiction; zero contagion → dark-allergy
- Performed empathy without feeling → golden-addiction; blocked contagion → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-identify (what feeling caught); intensity slider (how much); hold (sustain co-feeling); offer gesture (share own feeling)

### Timing requirements
- Contagion latency tracked; co-feeling duration; sharing timing; no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Companion NPC with dynamic emotional states
- Companion responds to player's co-feeling (brightens when felt-with)
- Companion shares own feelings (for mutual exchange)
- Feeling-bridge visualization (connection quality indicator)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Companion emotional dynamics, co-feeling quality assessment, mutual exchange evaluation. Core contagion-detection partially algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Contagion accuracy; intensity calibration; sharing frequency; mutuality scores; boundary maintenance; fusion indicators; isolation indicators; performance indicators; blocking indicators; drive/shadow signals; companion relationship state; fatigue state; checkpoint position
