# Emotional / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes emotional intelligence through SPONTANEOUS AFFECT-ENGAGEMENT in an open emotional world — not structured sensing tasks but free emotional navigation in an environment alive with feeling. How does the player's emotional intelligence LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe affect-sensing through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world IS emotional — every zone has a mood, every creature radiates feeling. The player's spontaneous emotional engagement reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Mood-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world where every zone has an emotional atmosphere, every creature radiates feeling, and the player's spontaneous emotional engagement is assessed. Do they sense moods? Do they respond? Do they navigate by feeling? Their ecological emotional behaviour reveals genuine capacity.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Mood-Noticing → Mood-Responding → Mood-Navigating → Mood-Sharing → The Living Mood-World

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world IS emotional. Nobody says "sense the mood" — but zones have feelings, creatures radiate emotion, paths have emotional textures. The player's NATURAL emotional engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL emotional style surfaces. Do they drown in every zone's mood? Miss all emotional content? Over-analyse? Avoid emotional zones? Their ecological emotional behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they sense more accurately? Navigate more fluidly? Share more genuinely?

**Integration path:** The world rewards emotional intelligence NATURALLY. Players who sense moods find hidden content. Players who navigate by feeling discover secret paths. Players who share feelings with companions unlock emotional bonds. The reward is WONDER and BEAUTY (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates emotional intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — sensing, responding, navigating, and sharing in the mood-world without being prompted.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Opportunities to sense and respond to moods independently.
- Healthy: Senses moods spontaneously; navigates by feeling; responds to emotional content
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by every zone's mood; OR: misses all emotional content
- **Detection:** Quality of spontaneous emotional engagement (sensing vs. flooding vs. missing)

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Situations requiring emotional initiative (mood-gated paths, feeling-responsive objects).
- Healthy: Engages emotional content without prompting
- Pathological: Cannot engage emotional content without being directed
- **Detection:** Spontaneous emotional initiative rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Companion's emotional engagement in the open world.
- Healthy: Engages with companion's emotional responses; shares mood-sensing
- Pathological: Ignores companion's emotional engagement; solo emotional navigation
- **Detection:** Response to companion's emotional activity

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared emotional spaces (mood-pools, feeling-gardens).
- Healthy: Engages shared emotional spaces naturally with companion
- Pathological: Avoids shared emotional spaces; prefers solo sensing
- **Detection:** Engagement rate in shared vs. solo emotional content

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar emotional terrain (same zones, same moods).
- Healthy: Maintains emotional engagement in familiar territory
- Pathological: Emotional engagement fades with familiarity; zones become "just scenery"
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in familiar vs. novel emotional zones

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel emotional content (new zones, new moods, new creatures).
- Healthy: Engages novel emotional content with curiosity
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by novel moods; OR: avoids unfamiliar emotional zones
- **Detection:** Whether novel emotional content is engaged or avoided

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining emotional engagement over time.
- Healthy: Consistent emotional engagement across sessions
- Pathological: Emotional engagement degrades; world becomes emotionally flat
- **Detection:** Emotional engagement consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether structured emotional skills appear in free-navigation.
- Healthy: Affect-sensing, mood-holding, co-feeling appear naturally
- Pathological: Emotional skills exist only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of structured emotional skills to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Mood-Flooder"
**How it surfaces:** OVERWHELMED by the mood-world. Cannot navigate because every zone's feeling floods them. Stuck in emotional zones. Seeks the most intense emotional content compulsively. Cannot leave a mood-zone once entered.
- **Detection signals:** Gets stuck in emotional zones; seeks maximum-intensity content; overwhelmed navigation; cannot leave mood-zones; emotional paralysis in rich environments

### Dark-Allergy: "The Affect-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** Navigates the mood-world WITHOUT SENSING. Misses emotional content entirely. Walks through mood-zones without registering them. The emotional world is invisible — just scenery.
- **Detection signals:** Zero engagement with emotional content; misses mood-gated paths; navigates without sensing; emotional content invisible; world is "just scenery"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"
**How it surfaces:** Engages emotional content with ANALYTICAL DISTANCE. "This zone represents melancholy." Talks ABOUT the moods rather than feeling them. Intellectual engagement without genuine emotional contact.
- **Detection signals:** Analytical engagement; talks about moods; intellectual distance; no genuine feeling-contact; "interesting" rather than "I feel"; observer not participant

### Golden-Allergy: "The Feeling-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** APPROACHES emotional content then RETREATS. Enters mood-zones then leaves quickly. Begins to engage then withdraws. The mood-world attracts and repels simultaneously.
- **Detection signals:** Approach-withdrawal at emotional content; enters then leaves mood-zones; brief engagement followed by retreat; emotional content both attractive and threatening

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces GENTLE mood-zones (low intensity, safe, brief)
- Intense zones become accessible only after demonstrating calibrated sensing in gentle zones
- Natural consequence: calibrated sensing = access to richer content

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World makes emotional content NECESSARY for navigation (mood-gated paths)
- Emotional zones produce TANGIBLE results (hidden items, secret paths)
- Natural consequence: sensing = access; not-sensing = stuck

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces content that requires FEELING not ANALYSING (feeling-responsive objects that don't respond to intellectual engagement)
- Genuine emotional contact produces results; analysis produces nothing
- Natural consequence: feeling = magic; analysis = flat

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces LOW-COMMITMENT emotional engagement (brief, safe, rewarding mood-touches)
- Emotional content that INVITES without DEMANDING sustained engagement
- Natural consequence: brief feeling = small magic; no punishment for withdrawal

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world where EVERYTHING has emotional texture. Zones with distinct moods (the Warm Meadow, the Sad Rain-Forest, the Angry Volcano, the Calm Lake). Creatures that radiate feeling. Paths that respond to emotional engagement. The world IS feeling.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through emotionally textured zones
- **Spontaneous sensing:** Noticing and responding to zone moods
- **Mood-gated content:** Paths/items that require emotional engagement to access
- **Creature interaction:** Beings that respond to player's emotional state
- **Companion co-feeling:** Sharing emotional experiences with companion

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: emotional engagement → hidden paths, magical effects, creature bonds
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, flat world, stuck paths
- No explicit "emotional score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Emotional content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Mood-gate sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated sensing quality
- Companion emotional activity scales with demonstrated sharing

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mood-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice emotional content? |
| Mood-Responding | 5-15 | Do they respond to moods? |
| Mood-Navigating | 15-30 | Can they navigate by feeling? |
| Mood-Sharing | 30-50 | Can they share emotional experiences? |
| The Living Mood-World | 50+ | Full ecological emotional intelligence |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Mood-zones:** Areas with distinct emotional atmospheres
- **Feeling-creatures:** Beings that radiate specific emotions
- **Mood-gates:** Paths requiring emotional engagement to open
- **Feeling-objects:** Items that respond to emotional contact
- **Shared mood-spaces:** Areas for co-feeling with companion

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ zones, 15+ creatures, 15+ gates, 15+ objects, 10+ shared spaces

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Stuck in zones/intensity-seeking → dark-addiction; misses all content → dark-allergy
- Analytical distance → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Mood-engagement rate:** How often does the player engage emotional content spontaneously?
- **Sensing accuracy:** When they engage, do they sense correctly?
- **Navigation quality:** Do they use feelings to navigate?
- **Sharing quality:** Do they share emotional experiences with companion?
- **Transfer evidence:** Do structured emotional skills appear in free-play?

### LLM rubric
- **High:** World generation, ecological emotional assessment, mood-responsive dynamics. Evaluates spontaneous emotional behaviour without explicit tasks.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage emotional content); hold (sustain emotional contact); gesture (share with companion)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, sensing quality, navigation choices)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of emotional patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Emotionally textured environment (zones with distinct moods)
- Feeling-creatures with dynamic emotional states
- Mood-gating system (paths requiring emotional engagement)
- Companion with own emotional engagement patterns

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, mood-responsive dynamics
- Detects spontaneous emotional engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates emotional intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive emotional content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; emotional engagement history; navigation patterns; sensing accuracy records; engagement quality; mood-gate completion; creature bond states; companion relationship; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
