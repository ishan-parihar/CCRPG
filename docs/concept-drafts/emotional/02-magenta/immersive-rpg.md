# Emotional / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes emotional intelligence through SPONTANEOUS AFFECT-ENGAGEMENT in an open emotional world — not structured sensing tasks but free emotional navigation in an environment alive with feeling. How does the player's emotional intelligence LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe affect-sensing through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world IS emotional — every zone has a mood, every creature radiates feeling. The player's spontaneous emotional engagement reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Mood-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world where every zone has an emotional atmosphere, every creature radiates feeling, and the player's spontaneous emotional engagement is assessed. Do they sense moods? Do they respond? Do they navigate by feeling? Their ecological emotional behaviour reveals genuine capacity.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Mood-Noticing → Mood-Responding → Mood-Navigating → Mood-Sharing → The Living Mood-World

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world IS emotional. Nobody says "sense the mood" — but zones have feelings, creatures radiate emotion, paths have emotional textures. The player's NATURAL emotional engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL emotional style surfaces. Do they drown in every zone's mood? Miss all emotional content? Over-analyse? Avoid emotional zones? Their ecological emotional behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they sense more accurately? Navigate more fluidly? Share more genuinely?

**Integration path:** The world rewards emotional intelligence NATURALLY. Players who sense moods find hidden content. Players who navigate by feeling discover secret paths. Players who share feelings with companions unlock emotional bonds. The reward is WONDER and BEAUTY (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates emotional intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — sensing, responding, navigating, and sharing in the mood-world without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world where EVERYTHING has emotional texture. Zones with distinct moods (the Warm Meadow, the Sad Rain-Forest, the Angry Volcano, the Calm Lake). Creatures that radiate feeling. Paths that respond to emotional engagement. The world IS feeling.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through emotionally textured zones
- **Spontaneous sensing:** Noticing and responding to zone moods
- **Mood-gated content:** Paths/items that require emotional engagement to access
- **Creature interaction:** Beings that respond to player's emotional state
- **Companion co-feeling:** Sharing emotional experiences with companion

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: emotional engagement → hidden paths, magical effects, creature bonds
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, flat world, stuck paths
- No explicit "emotional score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Emotional content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Mood-gate sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated sensing quality
- Companion emotional activity scales with demonstrated sharing

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mood-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice emotional content? |
| Mood-Responding | 5-15 | Do they respond to moods? |
| Mood-Navigating | 15-30 | Can they navigate by feeling? |
| Mood-Sharing | 30-50 | Can they share emotional experiences? |
| The Living Mood-World | 50+ | Full ecological emotional intelligence |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Mood-zones:** Areas with distinct emotional atmospheres
- **Feeling-creatures:** Beings that radiate specific emotions
- **Mood-gates:** Paths requiring emotional engagement to open
- **Feeling-objects:** Items that respond to emotional contact
- **Shared mood-spaces:** Areas for co-feeling with companion

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ zones, 15+ creatures, 15+ gates, 15+ objects, 10+ shared spaces

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Stuck in zones/intensity-seeking → dark-addiction; misses all content → dark-allergy
- Analytical distance → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage emotional content); hold (sustain emotional contact); gesture (share with companion)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, sensing quality, navigation choices)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of emotional patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Emotionally textured environment (zones with distinct moods)
- Feeling-creatures with dynamic emotional states
- Mood-gating system (paths requiring emotional engagement)
- Companion with own emotional engagement patterns

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, mood-responsive dynamics
- Detects spontaneous emotional engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates emotional intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive emotional content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; emotional engagement history; navigation patterns; sensing accuracy records; engagement quality; mood-gate completion; creature bond states; companion relationship; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
