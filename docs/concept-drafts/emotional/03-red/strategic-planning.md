# Emotional / Red — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** The strategic-planning axis probes emotional intelligence through MULTI-STEP EMOTIONAL SEQUENCING — planning how to manage emotions across a sequence of events, anticipating emotional reactions, and preparing emotional responses in advance.
>
> **Why this axis for Emotional/Red:** At Red, emotional planning is 2-step: "First I'll use my rage to attack, THEN I'll contain it to negotiate." The player can sequence 2 emotional states but cannot yet manage complex emotional portfolios. This axis isolates the PLANNING component of emotional intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Wroth-Strategist"
- **Core mechanic:** The player plans emotional sequences — which emotions to deploy when, how to transition between states, and how to manage emotional resources across a multi-step challenge.
- **Duration:** 4-7 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** 2-Emotion Sequences → 3-Emotion Sequences → Emotional Budgeting → Adaptive Sequences → Contested Emotions

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** Challenges that require SEQUENTIAL emotional management — "First feel rage (to break through), THEN feel calm (to negotiate), THEN feel pride (to hold the gain)." The contact boundary is: "Can you PLAN your emotional states?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* Do they plan at all (or just react)? Do they over-plan (golden-addiction)? Refuse to sequence (golden-allergy)? Get stuck in one emotion (dark-addiction)? Cannot access any to plan with (dark-allergy)?
- *Emergent:* Can they hold one more emotional transition? Can they anticipate emotional needs?

**Integration path:** Rewards EXECUTED emotional sequences (not just conceived). A simple 2-emotion sequence kept cleanly scores higher than a complex sequence that collapses.

**Successful integration:** The player conceives and executes 2-3 step emotional sequences appropriate to Red capacity.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Emotional plan suggested by NPC vs. player's own plan.
- Healthy: Evaluates suggestions; uses good ones
- Pathological: Rejects all emotional guidance; "I'll feel what I want when I want"
- **Detection:** Rejection rate independent of suggestion quality

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Open emotional planning — "Design your own emotional sequence for this challenge."
- Healthy: Generates emotional plans with confidence
- Pathological: Cannot plan emotions without template; "Tell me what to feel"
- **Detection:** Self-generated emotional plan quality

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Joint emotional sequences — player and ally must coordinate emotional states.
- Healthy: Integrates ally's emotional needs into own plan
- Pathological: Abandons own emotional plan to match ally's
- **Detection:** Whether joint plans reflect player's emotional sovereignty

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Teaching an NPC to plan their emotional sequence.
- Healthy: Scaffolds the NPC's emotional planning
- Pathological: Does it for them; cannot let others develop emotional plans
- **Detection:** Genuine scaffolding vs. imposition

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Mastered 2-emotion sequences vs. advancing to 3-emotion.
- Healthy: Enjoys clean execution; advances when ready
- Pathological: Demands longer sequences immediately
- **Detection:** Satisfaction at current sequence-length

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Sequences requiring emotions beyond current demonstrated range.
- Healthy: Attempts with curiosity; learns from failure
- Pathological: Claims complex sequences without demonstration
- **Detection:** Gap between planned and executed sequence complexity

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Simple 1-emotion "plans" (barely planning).
- Healthy: Executes with full commitment
- Pathological: Dismisses; "That's not real planning"
- **Detection:** Engagement quality on below-level sequences

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Emotional sequencing appearing in free-play.
- Healthy: Plans emotional states spontaneously in other contexts
- Pathological: Emotional planning exists only in structured challenges
- **Detection:** Spontaneous emotional sequencing outside task contexts

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rage-Addict"
**How it surfaces:** Present a 2-emotion sequence (rage → calm). The rage-addict cannot execute the transition — they get STUCK in rage. The plan exists but execution fails at the transition point.
- **Detection signals:** Stuck in first emotion; cannot transition; plan-execution gap at emotional shift points; all plans default to "rage → more rage"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Frozen"
**How it surfaces:** Present a sequence requiring emotional ACCESS. The frozen player cannot initiate the first emotion in the sequence — the plan exists cognitively but the body/feeling won't engage.
- **Detection signals:** Correct verbal plan but no emotional execution; planning without feeling; "I know I should feel angry here but I can't"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"
**How it surfaces:** Present a 2-emotion problem; observe whether they attempt a 5-emotion "elegant" sequence instead. Over-complex emotional planning for simple situations.
- **Detection signals:** Over-complex sequences for simple problems; elaborate emotional plans that collapse; inability to choose the simple emotional path

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sensitivity-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Increase sequence length. Resistance manifests when sequences require vulnerability-emotions (fear → courage, sadness → resolve).
- **Detection signals:** Performance cliff when vulnerability-emotions are required in the sequence; avoids planning sequences that include "soft" emotions

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Introduce "transition power" — the SHIFT between emotions produces more power than staying in one
- Frame: "Rage breaks the gate. Calm claims the throne. The SHIFT is where power lives."
- Reward clean transitions over sustained single-emotion

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Introduce "micro-sequences" — 2 emotions, each held for 3 seconds only
- Frame: "Feel anger for 3 seconds. Then feel calm. That's a PLAN. You executed it."
- Celebrate any emotional sequence completion

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Reward MINIMUM-EMOTION solutions (shortest sequence that works)
- Frame: "Two emotions. Clean. Done. That's how a warrior plans."
- Penalise unnecessary emotional complexity

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Introduce vulnerability-emotions in sequences as POWER MULTIPLIERS
- Frame: "Fear → courage = 3× the power of courage alone. The fear FUELS it."
- Make vulnerability-inclusion produce superior outcomes

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
A sequence of challenges requiring different emotional states. The player must PLAN which emotions to deploy in which order, then EXECUTE the plan. The aesthetic: Red-stage war-planning, emotional preparation before battle, the warrior's inner strategy.

### Interaction
- **2-Emotion Sequences:** "Rage to break, calm to hold." Plan two emotional states in order, execute.
- **3-Emotion Sequences:** "Fear to scout, rage to strike, pride to claim." Three sequential emotions.
- **Emotional Budgeting:** "You have energy for 2 intense emotions. Allocate wisely across 3 challenges."
- **Adaptive Sequences:** Mid-challenge, conditions change. Emotional plan must adapt.
- **Contested Emotions:** Enemy has their own emotional strategy. Anticipate and counter.

### Feedback
- Sequence completed → "Your emotional plan held. Each feeling served its moment."
- Stuck in one emotion → "You planned the shift but couldn't make it. What held you?"
- Over-complex plan → "Three emotions would have done it. You planned five. Simpler is stronger."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Sequence length: 2 → 3 → 4; transition speed: slow → moderate → rapid
- Budget constraints: unlimited → limited → scarce
- Adaptivity: static → changing → adversarial

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 2-Emotion Sequences | 1-5 | Simple two-emotion plans; generous timing |
| 3-Emotion Sequences | 5-15 | Longer sequences; timing constraints |
| Emotional Budgeting | 15-30 | Limited emotional energy; must allocate |
| Adaptive Sequences | 30-50 | Conditions change; must revise emotional plan |
| Contested Emotions | 50+ | Adversarial emotional strategy; anticipation |

---

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Sequence challenges:** Multi-step situations requiring planned emotional responses
- **Budget scenarios:** Limited-resource emotional allocation problems
- **Adaptation triggers:** Mid-sequence changes requiring emotional revision
- **Enemy emotional strategies:** Adversarial emotional patterns to counter

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ 2-emotion, 25+ 3-emotion, 20+ budget, 15+ adaptive, 10+ contested

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Stuck-in-one-emotion → dark-addiction; cannot-initiate → dark-allergy
- Over-complex plans → golden-addiction; vulnerability-avoidance in sequences → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Sequence completion:** % of planned sequences successfully executed
- **Transition quality:** Cleanness of emotional shifts
- **Budget efficiency:** How well emotional resources are allocated
- **Adaptation speed:** How quickly emotional plans revise

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, sequence evaluation, adaptive difficulty. Core transition-scoring algorithmic.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Sequence input (order emotions in a queue); sustained expression (execute each emotion with intensity); revision input (modify plan mid-execution)

### Timing requirements
- Transition timing measured; execution quality per emotion in sequence; no millisecond precision needed for planning phase

### NPC/AI requirements
- Strategist NPC: suggests plans, provides feedback; Ally NPC: coordinates joint sequences; Enemy AI: executes counter-strategies

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Scenario generation, outcome narration, adaptive difficulty. Core scoring algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Maximum sequence-length; transition quality history; budget management patterns; adaptation speed; stuck-patterns; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
