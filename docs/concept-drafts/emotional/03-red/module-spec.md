# Emotional / Red — Module Specification

> **Module vibration:** Emotional intelligence at Red — the self-other emotion split, egocentric affect (rage/pride/fear), emotion-as-power, first self-management. "I know I am angry." Rage as weapon, pride as armour, fear as enemy. The player can RECOGNISE their own emotion and USE it (rage-buff), but cannot yet regulate across contexts (Amber) or reflect on emotion-as-data (Orange).

---

## §1 Capacity Definition

**What this module measures:** Can the player (a) accurately recognise basic emotions in self and other, (b) USE emotion as power (channel rage, deploy pride), and (c) perform first-pass self-management (override fear, contain rage when it would destroy)?

**Stage-pass criteria:**
1. **Affect recognition (self):** Identifies own emotional state from basic palette (anger, fear, pride, disgust, excitement) — ≥70% accuracy
2. **Affect recognition (other):** Reads basic emotion in NPC faces/voices — ≥60% accuracy on 5 basic emotions
3. **Emotion-as-power:** Channels a named emotion into action (rage → attack buff, pride → defence buff) — ≥50% success rate
4. **First self-management:** Overrides ONE emotion to serve a goal (suppress fear to attack, contain rage to not destroy ally) — ≥1 successful override per session
5. **Self-other distinction:** Does not confuse own emotion with other's — ≤20% emotional contagion errors

**Integration criteria — what "emotionally healthy at Red" looks like:**
- Accurate recognition of 5 basic emotions in self and other
- Successful channelling of emotion into action
- At least one reliable self-management override
- Self-other distinction maintained
- Engagement with emotional content without avoidance OR compulsion
- Curiosity toward emotional nuance (even if not yet mastered)
- All 4 drives expressing healthily in both domains

## §2 Shadow Archetypes

### Dark-Addiction: "The Rage-Addict"
Cannot stop feeling. Emotion IS identity. Intensity is the only proof of aliveness. Clings to affect — especially rage and pride — because without intense feeling, they experience existential emptiness. Atman Project defence: fixation at the emotional-body level; letting go of emotion = letting go of self.

**In-game signals:** Channelling scores HIGH but self-management ZERO. Recognition accurate for intense emotions, low for subtle. Session engagement increases with emotional intensity. Post-channelling escalation; cannot return to baseline.

### Dark-Allergy: "The Frozen"
Cannot access emotion. Affect is threatening. Dissociated from feeling through FEAR, not sophistication. Numbness is safety. Atman Project defence: premature transcendence of the emotional body to avoid pain. This is NOT equanimity — this is absence.

**In-game signals:** Recognition accuracy uniformly low. Channelling near zero. Self-management appears "perfect" (nothing to manage) — FALSE SIGNAL. Low engagement with emotional content. May score HIGH on cognitive tasks (compensatory).

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"
Performs emotional sophistication without Red-level mastery. Claims to "understand" emotions, "hold space," "feel deeply" — but cannot RECOGNISE basic emotions accurately or USE them as power. Has bypassed Red's raw emotional force for Amber/Orange vocabulary. Atman Project defence: spiritual bypass; identified with HIGHER capacity without integrating LOWER.

**In-game signals:** Language-reflective scores HIGH (can TALK about emotions). Deterministic scores LOW (can't IDENTIFY them). Channelling fails. Cross-modality discrepancy is the KEY diagnostic.

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sensitivity-Refuser"
Treats emotional subtlety as weakness. Has mastered Red-level emotional power but REFUSES the call toward nuance. "Real warriors don't do feelings." Emotional sophistication threatens because it implies vulnerability. Atman Project defence: refusal of the emergent; fears growth will dissolve power.

**In-game signals:** High accuracy for anger/pride/excitement; LOW for fear/sadness/vulnerability. Excellent channelling of power-emotions; refuses soft emotions. Dismisses nuance tasks; disengages from subtlety-requiring content.

## §3 Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Dark-domain (healthy) | Dark-domain (pathological) | Golden-domain (healthy) | Golden-domain (pathological) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Uses own emotion sovereignly — rage serves MY goal | Emotion dominates — "I AM my anger"; rage controls player | Stands in own emotional truth without needing validation | Cannot feel without external trigger; depends on others provoking |
| **Communion** | Reads others' emotions without losing own centre | Emotional contagion — absorbs others', loses own | Shares emotional experience without premature fusion | Cannot share emotional state; emotional isolation |
| **Eros** | Enjoys current emotional power without compulsive escalation | Cannot rest; always escalating intensity; "More rage!" | Reaches toward subtler awareness with genuine curiosity | Performs subtlety without genuine access; claims unearned nuance |
| **Agape** | Returns to basic emotions with full presence | Dismisses basic emotions; "Anger is beneath me"; bypasses Red | Embodies emotional power in lived action | Power exists only in recognition tasks; cannot deploy in life |

**Scoring:** Each drive-domain pair scored 0–1. 0.0–0.3 clearly pathological; 0.3–0.6 mixed; 0.6–0.8 predominantly healthy; 0.8–1.0 consistently healthy. Drive-health score = mean of all 8 pairs.

## §4 Healing Vectors

### Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency) — for dark shadows

**Rage-Addict:** Return to emotion with compassion — "Your rage is valid. Now you can CHOOSE it." Sovereign relationship: "You HAVE anger. You are not anger." Game rewards CHOSEN intensity over compulsive intensity.

**The Frozen:** Return to the body where emotion lives — "Your numbness protected you. Now it's safe to feel." Sovereign access: "You can feel a LITTLE. You control the volume." Micro-emotions; celebrate any affect-access.

### Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion) — for golden shadows

**Premature Empath:** Genuine aspiration toward depth, not performance — "Real empathy requires FEELING, not just knowing." Join with others at ACTUAL level. Game requires DEMONSTRATED capacity before advancing. No verbal bypass.

**Sensitivity-Refuser:** Pull toward growth framed as POWER — "The warrior who reads fear in the enemy strikes first." Emotional attunement as tactical advantage. Nuance produces COMBAT ADVANTAGE. Subtlety = power, not weakness.

## §5 Scoring Parameters

### Staircase parameters

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Starting difficulty | 3 emotions, clear expressions, unlimited time | Minimal palette, obvious signals |
| Ceiling | 5 emotions, subtle/mixed expressions, 3s window | Full Red palette, nuanced, time pressure |

| Checkpoint | Capacity demonstrated |

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |

## §6 Compound Shadows & Cross-Module

### Cross-module dependencies

**Supports Emotional/Red:** Cognitive/Red (pattern recognition → emotion identification), Somatic/Red (interoception → affect recognition), Intrapersonal/Red (self-awareness → self-other distinction), Willpower/Red (volitional override → self-management).

**Emotional/Red supports:** Interpersonal/Red (reading others), Moral/Red (emotional data → moral intuition), Cognitive/Red (regulation prevents affect-hijack), Willpower/Red (channelling fuels volitional force).

### Compound shadow loops

| Compound | Modules | Pattern | Breaking mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| "The Raging Machine" | Emotional/dark-addiction + Cognitive/dark-allergy | Emotion overwhelms cognition → failure → more emotion → spiral | Cognitive tasks DURING emotional activation; thinking and feeling coexist |
| "The Cold Calculator" | Emotional/dark-allergy + Cognitive/dark-addiction | Cognitive over-development compensates for emotional absence → reinforces avoidance | Emotional content embedded IN cognitive tasks (emotional Stroop) |
| "The Burning Warrior" | Emotional/dark-addiction + Willpower/dark-addiction | Rage fuels compulsive willing → more rage → exhaustion → rage at exhaustion | Rest produces MORE power than continued effort |
| "The Frozen Ghost" | Emotional/dark-allergy + Willpower/dark-allergy | Cannot feel → nothing motivates → cannot will → total withdrawal | Micro-provocations; any response celebrated |

## §7 Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### Diagnostic progression

```
Step 1: RECOGNITION (can they identify emotions at all?)
  → Dark-allergy surfaces (cannot access)
  → Uniform failure → Frozen pattern

Step 2: CHANNELLING (can they USE emotion as power?)
  → Dark-addiction surfaces (cannot stop using)
  → Golden-addiction surfaces (claims but can't demonstrate)
  → Compulsive channelling → Rage-Addict
  → Fails despite verbal claims → Premature Empath

Step 3: SELF-MANAGEMENT (can they override emotion for a goal?)
  → Dark-addiction confirmed (cannot override)
  → Golden-allergy surfaces (refuses to manage power-emotions)
  → Override fails for rage → Rage-Addict confirmed
  → Succeeds for fear, fails for rage → Sensitivity-Refuser

Step 4: NUANCE (can they detect subtle/mixed emotions?)
  → Golden-allergy confirmed (refuses nuance)
  → Golden-addiction confirmed (claims without accuracy)
  → Actively rejected → Sensitivity-Refuser confirmed
  → Claimed but inaccurate → Premature Empath confirmed

Step 5: EXTENSION (can they engage emotional growth?)
  → All 4 quadrants fully differentiated
  → Game deploys appropriate healing vector
```

### Cross-modality validation

| Signal | Detecting modality | Validating modality |
|---|---|---|
| Cannot access emotion | Deterministic (low accuracy) | Embodied-somatic (no body-emotion link) |
| Cannot stop feeling | Deterministic (no override) | Immersive-RPG (escalating intensity) |
| Claims without demonstration | Language-reflective (high verbal) | Deterministic (low accuracy) |
| Refuses nuance | Scenario-choice (avoids subtle options) | Language-reflective (limited vocabulary) |

### Shadow-mode re-engagement triggers
- Theta-decay degrades recognition accuracy below 60%
- Drive-health scores drop below 0.5 on any drive-domain pair
- Shadow severity rises above 0.4 on any quadrant
- Cross-module compound shadow detected
- Player advanced to Amber+ but emotional/Red health declining
