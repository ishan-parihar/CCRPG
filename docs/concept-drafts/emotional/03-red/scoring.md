# Emotional / Red — Scoring Skeleton

> **Module vibration:** Emotional intelligence at the Red (Power) stage. The first self-other emotion split — "I know I am angry." Egocentric affect dominates: rage as weapon, pride as armour, fear as enemy. The player can RECOGNISE their own emotion and USE it (rage-buff), but cannot yet regulate across contexts (Amber) or reflect on emotion-as-data (Orange). First emotional self-management beats: "I will not let my fear stop me."
>
> **What this module measures:** Can the player (a) accurately recognise basic emotions in self and other, (b) USE emotion as power (channel rage, deploy pride), and (c) perform first-pass self-management (override fear, contain rage when it would destroy)?

---

## 1. Capacity Scoring Rubric

### 1.1 What constitutes stage-pass at Emotional/Red

The player demonstrates:
1. **Affect recognition (self):** Accurately identifies own current emotional state from a basic palette (anger, fear, pride, disgust, excitement) — ≥70% accuracy
2. **Affect recognition (other):** Reads basic emotion in NPC faces/voices — ≥60% accuracy on 5 basic emotions
3. **Emotion-as-power:** Can channel a named emotion into action (rage → attack buff, pride → defence buff) — successful channelling ≥50% of opportunities
4. **First self-management:** Can override ONE emotion to serve a goal (suppress fear to attack, contain rage to not destroy an ally) — ≥1 successful override per session
5. **Self-other distinction:** Does not confuse own emotion with other's — ≤20% emotional contagion errors

### 1.2 Staircase parameters

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Starting difficulty | 3 emotions, clear expressions, unlimited time | Minimal palette, obvious signals |
| Ceiling | 5 emotions, subtle/mixed expressions, 3s window | Full Red palette, nuanced signals, time pressure |
| Step size | +1 emotion OR +1 subtlety level OR -0.5s time | Single-dimension advancement |
| Reversal rule | 2 consecutive misidentifications → step down | Prevents frustration |
| Stage-pass threshold | 5 emotions at moderate subtlety within 5s | Functional Red-level emotional intelligence |

### 1.3 Checkpoint progression

| Checkpoint | Capacity demonstrated |
|---|---|
| 1 | Identifies anger vs. not-anger in self |
| 5 | Identifies 3 emotions in self; 2 in other |
| 10 | Identifies 5 emotions in self and other; first channelling |
| 25 | Channels emotion reliably; first self-management |
| 50 | Full Red palette; subtle expressions; self-management under load |
| 100+ | Shadow-mode: drive-health and integration quality |

## 2. Drive-Health Scoring Rubric

### 2.1 Healthy expression of each drive at Emotional/Red

| Drive | Dark-domain (healthy) | Golden-domain (healthy) |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Uses own emotion sovereignly — rage serves MY goal, not the rage itself | Stands in own emotional truth without needing others to validate it |
| **Communion** | Reads others' emotions without losing own emotional centre | Shares emotional experience without premature fusion |
| **Eros** | Enjoys current emotional power without compulsive escalation | Reaches toward subtler emotional awareness with genuine curiosity |
| **Agape** | Returns to basic emotions with full presence (doesn't dismiss "simple" feelings) | Embodies emotional power in lived action (not just recognition) |

### 2.2 Pathological expression of each drive at Emotional/Red

| Drive | Dark-domain (pathological) | Golden-domain (pathological) |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency** | Emotion dominates — rage controls the player, not the reverse; "I AM my anger" | Cannot feel without external trigger; emotional life depends on others provoking it |
| **Communion** | Emotional contagion — absorbs others' emotions, loses own; "I feel what they feel" | Cannot share emotional state; emotional isolation; "My feelings are mine alone" |
| **Eros** | Cannot rest in current emotional state; always escalating intensity; "More rage! More!" | Performs emotional subtlety without genuine access; claims nuance they don't have |
| **Agape** | Dismisses basic emotions; "Anger is beneath me"; bypasses Red-level affect | Emotional power exists only in recognition tasks; cannot deploy in lived situations |

### 2.3 Scoring method

Each drive-domain pair scored 0-1 based on behavioural signals:
- 0.0-0.3: Clearly pathological pattern
- 0.3-0.6: Mixed signals, some healthy some pathological
- 0.6-0.8: Predominantly healthy with occasional pathological spikes
- 0.8-1.0: Consistently healthy expression

Drive-health score = mean of all 8 drive-domain scores.

## 3. Shadow Integration Criteria

### 3.1 The 4 shadow archetypes at Emotional/Red

| Quadrant | Archetype | Core pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark-Addiction** | "The Rage-Addict" | Cannot stop feeling; emotion as identity; clings to intensity |
| **Dark-Allergy** | "The Frozen" | Cannot access emotion; affect is threatening; numbness as safety |
| **Golden-Addiction** | "The Premature Empath" | Performs emotional sophistication without Red-level mastery; "I'm beyond anger" |
| **Golden-Allergy** | "The Sensitivity-Refuser" | Treats emotional subtlety as weakness; "Real warriors don't do nuance" |

### 3.2 What "resolved" means for each quadrant

| Quadrant | Integration criterion |
|---|---|
| Dark-Addiction | Can CHOOSE to feel intensely AND choose to release; emotion serves will, not the reverse |
| Dark-Allergy | Can access basic emotions on demand; affect is available, not threatening |
| Golden-Addiction | Demonstrates genuine Red-level emotional power BEFORE reaching for subtlety |
| Golden-Allergy | Engages emotional nuance with curiosity rather than contempt |

### 3.3 Shadow severity scoring

Each quadrant scored 0-1 (0 = fully resolved, 1 = maximally active):
- Measured through behavioural patterns across all 7 game modalities
- Cross-validated between modalities (language claims vs. behavioural demonstration)
- shadow_integration_score = 1 - max(4 quadrant severities)

## 4. Theta-Decay Sensitivity

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Decay sensitivity | **HIGH** | Emotional capacity degrades quickly without practice — affect recognition accuracy drops, channelling weakens, self-management erodes |
| Half-life | 14 days | Without emotional engagement, recognition accuracy drops 50% in 2 weeks |
| Maximum decay | 30% | Floor prevents total loss; some emotional capacity is trait-stable |
| Recovery rate | **MEDIUM** | Emotional skills recover moderately quickly with re-engagement (faster than cognitive, slower than somatic) |

### 4.1 Decay justification

Emotional intelligence at Red is SKILL-based (recognition accuracy, channelling timing, override speed). Skills decay without practice. The affect-recognition literature shows significant accuracy drops after 2-3 weeks without exposure to emotional stimuli. Self-management is even more fragile — it requires ongoing practice to maintain.
