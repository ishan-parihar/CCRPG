# Emotional / Orange — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Shared emotional awareness — co-regulation with NPC partners and emotional attunement in group contexts.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange emotional intelligence doesn't exist in a vacuum. The social dimension tests whether self-awareness translates into relational competence — can you read your own emotions AND attune to a partner's without losing yourself?

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Resonance Pact
**Fantasy:** The player forms emotional bonds with NPC companions whose affective states are visible as aura-fields. Successful cooperation requires reading the companion's emotional state, managing one's own in response, and co-regulating — neither absorbing the other's affect nor walling it off.

**Core loop:** Engage with NPC partner → read their emotional state → manage own emotional response → co-regulate (attune without merging) → accomplish shared objective that requires emotional synchrony.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**DA surfacing:** The Emotion Engineer reads the partner's state accurately but responds with strategic management rather than genuine attunement. Uses emotional intelligence as a tool ON the partner rather than WITH them.
**DAll surfacing:** The Emotionally Illiterate cannot read the partner's state because they cannot read their own. Misattunes consistently. Responds to the situation's logistics, not its emotional content.
**GA surfacing:** The Premature Empath over-merges with the partner's state. Loses self-boundary. "I feel what you feel" becomes "I AM what you feel." Co-regulation collapses into emotional fusion.
**GAll surfacing:** The Regulation Fortress reads accurately but refuses to be affected. Maintains perfect self-regulation while the partner's emotional needs go unmet. Competent but cold.

**Heal/Evolve path:** Agape tasks require receiving the partner's emotion without fixing it. Agency tasks require maintaining self while being affected.
**Evolve/Heal path:** Eros tasks require reaching toward deeper attunement. Communion tasks require genuine emotional sharing (not performance).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Emotional State Reading:** NPC companion displays affect through behavioural cues (tone, posture, action patterns). Player must identify the companion's emotional state from a granularity wheel. Accuracy scored.
**Mechanic 2 — Self-State Management:** While reading the partner, player must simultaneously track their own emotional response. Dual-tracking scored — can you hold both?
**Mechanic 3 — Co-Regulation Actions:** Player selects responses to the partner's state: validate, mirror, contain, redirect, share own state. Context determines which is adaptive. Over-managing = DA; ignoring = DAll; merging = GA; walling = GAll.
**Mechanic 4 — Synchrony Challenges:** Tasks that require emotional alignment between player and NPC — timing-based mechanics where success depends on affective attunement rather than reflexes.

**Difficulty staircase:** Partner emotions move from obvious to subtle. Player's own emotional response becomes more intense. Dual-tracking demand increases. Partner's needs conflict with player's comfort.

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **NPC affect model:** State machine with emotional inertia, responsiveness to player actions, and authentic-feeling transitions
- **Dual-tracking UI:** Split attention between partner-state indicators and self-state indicators without overwhelming
- **Co-regulation scoring:** Measures attunement (accuracy), boundaries (self-maintenance), and effectiveness (partner state improvement)
- **Session length:** 5–10 minutes per cooperative encounter; natural breakpoints between encounters
- **Relationship persistence:** NPC companions remember past interactions; trust builds or erodes based on co-regulation quality
- **Shadow flags:** Strategic management without warmth → DA; consistent misattunement → DAll; boundary loss + fusion → GA; accurate reading + no response → GAll
