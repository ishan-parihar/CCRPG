# Emotional / Orange — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as site of emotion — interoceptive awareness, somatic markers, and the felt-sense of affect.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange emotional self-awareness must include the body. Emotion lives in the soma before it reaches cognition. A player who can label "anger" but cannot locate it in their chest has conceptual knowledge, not embodied awareness.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Soma Resonance
**Fantasy:** The player inhabits an energy body — a luminous form whose regions pulse, tighten, warm, or go numb in response to emotional stimuli. The player must detect these somatic signals, map them accurately, and use them as early-warning data for emotional states before cognitive labelling catches up.

**Core loop:** Receive emotional stimulus → detect somatic response location/quality → map to body region → identify emotion from somatic signature → demonstrate interoceptive accuracy.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**DA surfacing:** The Emotion Engineer shows fast cognitive labelling but poor somatic mapping. They know what they "should" feel but cannot locate it in the body. Head-heavy, body-absent. Regulation happens above the neck.
**DAll surfacing:** The Emotionally Illiterate shows somatic numbness — cannot detect body signals at all, or reports uniform "nothing" across regions. The body speaks but no one listens.
**GA surfacing:** The Premature Empath shows high somatic resonance to others' emotions (mirroring) but poor discrimination of own somatic signals. Feels everything; owns nothing.
**GAll surfacing:** The Regulation Fortress shows accurate self-somatic mapping but active dampening — detects the signal, then suppresses it. Body awareness without body permission.

**Heal/Evolve path:** Agape tasks require staying with somatic discomfort without regulating it away. Agency tasks require precise self-mapping under arousal.
**Evolve/Heal path:** Eros tasks introduce somatic resonance with others' states. Communion tasks require sharing somatic experience.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Interoceptive Detection:** Timed windows where the player must indicate "something is happening in my body" after an emotional stimulus. Speed and accuracy of detection scored. Calibrated against baseline.
**Mechanic 2 — Body Mapping:** Player selects body regions (from a silhouette) where they detect sensation. Compared against normative somatic-emotion maps and personal baseline. Accuracy and specificity scored.
**Mechanic 3 — Somatic-to-Emotion Bridge:** Given a body sensation pattern, player identifies the emotion. Reverses the typical label-first approach. Tests whether body knowledge precedes or follows cognitive knowledge.
**Mechanic 4 — Arousal Tolerance:** Somatic intensity increases; player must maintain awareness without suppressing or dissociating. Measures the window between detection and regulation — healthy Orange has a gap; DA has no gap; DAll has no detection.

**Difficulty staircase:** Stimuli move from high-arousal/obvious to subtle/ambiguous. Body regions move from gross (chest, gut) to fine (throat, jaw, fingertips). Mixed emotions require multi-region mapping.

All items tagged by: target emotion, expected somatic region, arousal level, subtlety tier.

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Biometric integration (optional):** Heart rate, skin conductance if available — validates self-report against physiology for DA detection
- **Baseline calibration:** First session establishes personal somatic baseline; all scoring is relative to individual
- **Body map UI:** Touch/click selectable silhouette with region highlighting; must be accessible and non-gendered
- **Session length:** 5–8 minutes per checkpoint; somatic work requires pacing (not rushed)
- **Arousal safety:** If arousal tolerance task triggers distress markers, auto-offer grounding exercise and reduce intensity
- **Shadow flags:** Fast label + no body map → DA; no detection + no label → DAll; other-resonance > self-resonance → GA; detection + immediate suppression → GAll
