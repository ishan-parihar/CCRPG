# Emotional / Orange — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological emotional intelligence — whether reflective emotional awareness appears spontaneously in free-play.  **Why this axis for this module:** The ultimate test of Orange emotional capacity is not whether the player can perform it when prompted, but whether it emerges naturally in an unstructured environment. Does the examined emotional life persist when no one is asking?

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Feeling World
**Fantasy:** An open-world RPG where the environment, NPCs, and narrative arcs are emotionally saturated. Nothing is labelled. No prompts ask "how do you feel?" The world simply IS emotionally rich, and the player's spontaneous engagement with that richness — or avoidance of it — reveals their ecological emotional intelligence.

**Core loop:** Explore emotionally textured world → encounter affect-laden situations → respond spontaneously (no forced choice) → system observes behavioural patterns → ecological shadow profile emerges over time.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**DA surfacing:** The Emotion Engineer navigates emotional content efficiently. Completes affect-laden quests without being affected. Treats emotional NPCs as puzzle-inputs. The world's feelings are data to be processed, not experiences to be had.
**DAll surfacing:** The Emotionally Illiterate avoids emotional content entirely. Gravitates toward action/combat/logistics. When emotional content is unavoidable, responds with confusion or irritation. "Why is this NPC crying at me?"
**GA surfacing:** The Premature Empath over-engages with others' emotional content while showing no self-referential emotional behaviour. Helps every crying NPC; never pauses to notice their own state.
**GAll surfacing:** The Regulation Fortress engages with emotional content but maintains observable distance. Completes emotional quests competently but never lingers, never revisits, never lets the world's affect change their behaviour.

**Heal/Evolve path:** World events that reward staying with discomfort (Agape) and authentic self-expression (Agency) — not through explicit rewards but through narrative depth that only opens to genuine engagement.
**Evolve/Heal path:** World events that reward reaching toward emotional growth (Eros) through vulnerability with NPCs (Communion) — relationships that deepen only when the player risks being affected.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Emotional Ecology:** The world contains emotionally rich zones — grief gardens, rage forges, joy springs, fear caverns. Player's navigation patterns (approach/avoid/linger/rush) are tracked without prompting.
**Mechanic 2 — NPC Emotional Depth:** NPCs have layered emotional lives. Surface engagement yields surface responses. Deeper engagement (time, attention, vulnerability) reveals deeper NPC affect. The player's depth of engagement is diagnostic.
**Mechanic 3 — Spontaneous Reflection Points:** Optional journal/campfire moments where the player CAN reflect but is never forced to. Engagement frequency and depth are tracked. Avoidance is data.
**Mechanic 4 — Emotional Consequence Threads:** Player choices in emotional moments create long-term narrative consequences. The pattern of consequences reveals whether the player's emotional engagement is consistent or situational.

**Difficulty staircase:** Early world is emotionally obvious (clear grief, clear joy). Later zones present ambiguous, mixed, or subtle affect. Highest difficulty: zones where the player's own emotional state is the primary content (mirror zones).

## 5. Technical Requirements

- **Behavioural telemetry:** Track navigation patterns, time-in-zone, NPC interaction depth, reflection engagement — all without prompting or revealing measurement
- **Pattern recognition:** Rolling behavioural profile built over multiple sessions; no single-session judgements
- **Ecological validity:** Nothing in the world should feel like a "test." All emotional content must be narratively justified and immersively delivered
- **Session length:** Player-determined; infinite checkpoint; minimum meaningful session ~10 minutes
- **Cross-modality validation:** Ecological patterns compared against structured modality results; convergence confirms shadow, divergence flags context-dependent behaviour
- **Shadow flags:** Efficient emotional navigation without affect → DA; emotional content avoidance → DAll; other-focused engagement + self-absence → GA; competent engagement + observable distance → GAll
