# Emotional / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Emotional  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The examined emotional life — feeling becomes data, regulation becomes skill, and the self authors its own affective landscape.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Reflective self-awareness of emotion. The player can name what they feel with granular precision, select regulation strategies deliberately, use emotion as informational input to decisions, and distinguish between experiencing an emotion and being determined by it. "I notice that I am angry" rather than "I am angry." Emotion-as-skill: learnable, trainable, deployable.

**Below (Amber):** Emotion serves role — "I feel what my role requires." Affect is group-congruent, not self-authored.
**Above (Green):** Pluralistic empathy — holding contradictory affects, co-regulating, extending empathy to out-groups.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: The Emotion Engineer

Over-manages emotions. Treats feelings as problems to optimise away. Emotional suppression disguised as regulation. "I've processed that" (hasn't felt it). Weaponises emotional intelligence as social leverage. Confuses control with health.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: The Emotionally Illiterate

Refuses emotional self-awareness entirely. Remains in Amber role-affect or Red raw reactivity. "Emotions are weakness." Cannot name what they feel. Alexithymia as identity. Treats the examined life as navel-gazing.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: The Premature Empath

Performs Green pluralistic empathy without genuine Orange self-awareness. Claims to "hold space for all feelings" while unable to identify their own. Emotional bypassing — leaps to compassion without passing through honest self-recognition.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: The Regulation Fortress

Emotional self-awareness is present but refuses empathic opening. Terrified of others' emotions contaminating their regulated interior. "I can manage MY feelings; don't bring yours." Competence without connection.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency)

Dark shadows dissolve when the player embodies emotion (Agape) with sovereign honesty (Agency). The Emotion Engineer must actually feel. The Emotionally Illiterate must name what is already present. Both require the courage to be with affect rather than above or below it.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion)

Golden shadows dissolve when the player reaches toward growth (Eros) through genuine connection (Communion). The Premature Empath must build real self-awareness before extending outward. The Regulation Fortress must risk letting another's emotion in.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Integration = the player can: (a) label emotions at ≥80% granularity accuracy, (b) select contextually appropriate regulation strategies, (c) use emotion as decision-data without being hijacked, (d) tolerate emotional discomfort without suppressing or bypassing.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Cold Architect (Emotional DAll × Cognitive DA)

Emotional suppression fused with hyper-rationalism. Brilliant analysis, zero felt connection. The mind works perfectly; the heart is offline. Dissolves when cognitive capacity is turned toward emotional data.

### 6.2 The Productive Machine (Emotional DAll × Willpower DA)

Emotional suppression fused with compulsive achievement. Functional but dead inside. Output without meaning. Dissolves when willpower is directed toward feeling rather than doing.

### 6.3 The Emotion Performer (Emotional GA × Interpersonal GA)

Premature empathy fused with premature authentic relating. Performed sensitivity — looks like connection, lacks substance. Dissolves when both lines ground in genuine Orange self-awareness before reaching toward Green.

### 6.4 The Regulated Isolate (Emotional GAll × Interpersonal DAll)

Regulation fortress fused with refusal to relate. Emotionally competent but utterly alone. Self-sufficient to the point of starvation. Dissolves when regulation skill is risked in relational context.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Shadows surface in order: DAll first (most visible — player simply cannot label), then DA (subtler — player labels but doesn't feel), then GAll (player regulates but won't share), then GA (player performs empathy without foundation).

| Language-Reflective | DA (performed processing) | GA (performed empathy) |
| Scenario-Choice | DA (regulation over authenticity) | GAll (refuses vulnerability) |
| Embodied-Somatic | DAll (body disconnection) | DA (somatic suppression) |

### 7.3 Cross-Validation

A shadow is confirmed only when ≥2 modalities independently surface it. Single-modality signals flag for observation but do not trigger shadow-mode encounters. The immersive-rpg modality serves as ecological validator for patterns detected in structured modalities.
