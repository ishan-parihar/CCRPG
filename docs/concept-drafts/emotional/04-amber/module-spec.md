# Emotional / Amber — Module Specification

> **Line:** Emotional (affect recognition, regulation, channelling, empathy)
> **Stage:** Amber / Mythic-Membership (conformist, rule-following, role-bound)
> **Vibration:** Emotion-as-role — "I feel what my role requires." The warrior feels pride and duty. The guardian feels vigilance and care. The penitent feels guilt and contrition. Emotion is no longer raw ego-fuel (Red) but the felt texture of belonging to a code.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Emotional intelligence at Amber is **role-congruent affect** — the capacity to feel, regulate, and express emotions appropriate to one's role and the group's code. The Red player who could recognise and channel raw emotions (rage, pride, fear) now feels the RELATIONAL emotions of belonging: loyalty, guilt, shame, duty-feeling, group-pride, and the warmth of shared code.

**Key distinction from Red:** Red emotion is egocentric — "I am angry." Amber emotion is role-bound — "As a guardian, I feel vigilance." Red channels rage as power. Amber channels duty-feeling as sustained motivation. Red has no guilt (only shame/fear of punishment); Amber has genuine guilt (violation of internalised code).

**Key distinction from Orange:** Orange emotion is reflective — "I notice I feel X; I wonder why." Amber emotion is role-embedded — "I feel X because my role requires it." Amber cannot yet examine whether the role's emotional demands are appropriate; Orange can.

### Core capacity dimensions

| Dimension | Definition | Assessment vehicle | Ceiling at this stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Role-affect recognition** | Identifies emotions appropriate to a given role/context | Role-congruent emotion-matching tasks | Accurate for 6 role-emotions across 3 roles |
| **Guilt-capacity** | Experiences genuine guilt (not just shame) when violating the code | Guilt-vs-shame discrimination tasks | Distinguishes guilt from shame reliably |
| **Loyalty-feeling** | Feels the warmth of group-belonging and shared code | Group-affect tasks; loyalty-bond scenarios | Sustained loyalty-feeling across 5+ interactions |
| **Duty-emotion** | Channels duty-feeling as sustained motivational affect | Duty-motivation tasks; obligation-affect probes | Duty-feeling sustains effort for 5+ minutes |
| **Emotional regulation (role-based)** | Regulates affect to match role requirements | Role-congruent regulation tasks | Regulates 3+ emotions to role-appropriate levels |

### What this is NOT

- **NOT Red:** Red emotion is raw and egocentric. Amber emotion is role-bound and relational. Red has no guilt; Amber has genuine guilt. Red channels rage; Amber channels duty-feeling.
- **NOT Orange:** Orange emotion is reflective and self-examined. Amber emotion is role-embedded and code-justified. Amber cannot yet ask "is this emotional demand appropriate?" — that requires Orange reflective capacity.

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Duty-Martyr"

**Core pattern:** Clings to role-emotions as ABSOLUTE IDENTITY. Cannot feel anything outside the role's emotional script. Guilt is constant and crushing. Duty-feeling has become self-punishment. The emotional life is entirely consumed by obligation-affect.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Emotional life is surrendered to the role; "I feel what I must feel"
- Communion: Imposes role-emotions on others; "You should feel guilty too"
- Eros: Cannot grow emotionally beyond current role-script; new emotions are "inappropriate"
- Agape: Cannot return to rest; every moment of non-duty-feeling is emotional failure

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Constant guilt even when no code has been violated
- Cannot feel joy, pleasure, or rest without guilt
- Emotional vocabulary is entirely role-bound (no personal emotions)
- Imposes emotional standards on others; "A true guardian doesn't feel fear"
- Cannot distinguish between healthy guilt (code violated) and pathological guilt (code not violated)
- Emotional exhaustion from constant duty-feeling

**Atman Project defenses:** Emotional role-fusion, guilt-inflation, affect-surrender to code.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can feel role-emotions AND personal emotions. Guilt is proportional to actual code-violation. Duty-feeling is motivating, not punishing.

---

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Emotional Deserter"

**Core pattern:** Refuses all role-congruent affect. Stays in Red egocentric emotion — raw, self-serving, code-indifferent. Cannot feel loyalty, guilt, or duty-feeling. The call to role-bound emotion is experienced as emotional imprisonment.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Feels only self-serving emotions; "I feel what I want to feel"
- Communion: Cannot feel group-emotions; "Their feelings are their problem"
- Eros: Reaches for new emotional experiences without role-grounding
- Agape: Cannot return to role-congruent affect; "That's not how I feel"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- No guilt when code is violated; only shame/fear of punishment (Red-stage)
- Cannot feel loyalty-warmth; relationships are transactional
- Duty-feeling is absent; motivation is purely self-interest
- Emotional responses are role-incongruent (laughs at solemn moments, feels nothing at group loss)
- Cannot sustain role-appropriate emotional regulation
- Interpersonal relationships suffer from emotional code-indifference

**Atman Project defenses:** Emotional commitment-avoidance, Red-stage fixation, affect-freedom identification.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can feel loyalty, guilt, and duty-feeling. Role-congruent affect is accessible and genuine.

---

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Empath"

**Core pattern:** Performs emotional sophistication (empathy, emotional attunement, "holding space") without genuine Amber role-affect underneath. Claims to "feel deeply" and "understand others' emotions" but cannot feel simple loyalty or genuine guilt. Has bypassed role-bound emotion for Orange/Green emotional vocabulary.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Performed emotional sovereignty without genuine role-affect
- Communion: Over-developed in FORM — "validates" constantly without genuine feeling
- Eros: Compulsive reaching toward emotional sophistication; cannot rest in simple role-emotion
- Agape: Cannot return to simple loyalty-feeling; "That's too basic for me"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Uses sophisticated emotional language ("I hold space for your experience") but cannot feel simple loyalty
- High language-reflective scores + low deterministic scores = golden-addiction signal
- Cannot feel genuine guilt (only performs guilt-language)
- Emotional attunement is performed, not felt
- Cross-validation: articulates emotional complexity but fails role-affect recognition tasks

**Atman Project defenses:** Emotional bypass, Orange-stage performance without Amber foundation.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can feel simple role-emotions genuinely before engaging emotional sophistication. Loyalty is felt, not performed. Guilt is genuine, not performed.

---

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Feeling-Refuser"

**Core pattern:** Role-affect is PRESENT but the call to emotional reflection (Orange) is refused. Cannot examine whether the role's emotional demands are appropriate. "I feel what I feel because the code says so — I don't need to examine it." Emotional life is code-justified and unexaminable.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: Cannot assert independent emotional reasoning
- Communion: Clings to group's emotional code; fears individual emotional examination
- Eros: Refuses the call to examine and potentially revise emotional demands
- Agape: Over-embodies current role-emotions; makes them permanent and sacred

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Excellent role-affect recognition and expression
- But: freezes when asked "why does your role require this emotion?"
- Treats emotional examination as betrayal or weakness
- Cannot distinguish between healthy role-emotion and destructive role-emotion
- Anxiety or hostility when role-emotions are examined rather than just felt

**Atman Project defenses:** Emotional code-sacralisation, Orange-refusal, affect-examination avoidance.

**What "healed" looks like:** Can feel role-emotions AND examine them. Can ask "is this emotional demand appropriate?" without experiencing the question as betrayal.

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → dark shadows)

**For Duty-Martyr:**
- Agape: Return to the SELF beneath the role-emotion. "You are more than your duty-feeling. The code serves you; you don't serve the code."
- Agency: Sovereign examination of each emotional demand. "Is this guilt proportional? Do I choose to feel this?"
- Mechanism: Present situations where rest and joy are role-appropriate. "The code permits happiness. The guardian can feel warmth. This is not a violation."

**For Emotional Deserter:**
- Agape: Return to the group with compassion. "Their loss is real. Can you feel even a small part of it?"
- Agency: Own the role-emotion as a sovereign choice. "Not because you must — because you CHOOSE to feel with them."
- Mechanism: Ultra-small loyalty-moments. "Hold this feeling for one second. Notice it. That's enough."

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → golden shadows)

**For Premature Empath:**
- Eros: Reach toward GENUINE role-affect (not performed). "Feel the loyalty first. Then the sophistication."
- Communion: Join with others at the ACTUAL emotional level. "Not 'I hold space' — 'I feel with you.'"
- Mechanism: Lock emotional sophistication tasks behind demonstrated role-affect. Simple genuine feeling must precede complex emotional performance.

**For Feeling-Refuser:**
- Eros: Reach toward the QUESTION (not the answer). "Why does your role require this feeling? Not to change it — to understand it."
- Communion: Join with others who examine AND feel their role-emotions. "They question AND they feel. Both are possible."
- Mechanism: Introduce gentle meta-cognitive tasks about role-emotions. "Notice the feeling. Don't change it. Just notice why the role requires it."

### 4.3 Integration criteria

The player demonstrates: genuine role-affect (loyalty, guilt, duty-feeling) without role-fusion; proportional guilt (code-violation only); emotional regulation to role-appropriate levels; capacity to feel personal emotions alongside role-emotions; openness to examining (not necessarily revising) emotional demands.

### Stage-transition thresholds
- **Amber → Orange:** Theta ≥ 0.7 on role-affect-recognition AND guilt-capacity ≥ 0.65; confirmed across ≥ 4 sessions; ≥ 2 other lines at Amber; first signs of emotional examination without anxiety

### Drive-health weight: 0.30

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Hollow Performer (Emotional × Willpower, both dark-allergy)

**Loop:** Cannot sustain commitment (Willpower) because cannot feel the emotional weight of obligation (Emotional). And cannot feel obligation because there is nothing to commit to. Motivational void + emotional flatness = total volitional collapse.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Emotional allergy → "I don't feel why this matters" → cannot sustain commitment
- Commitment-failure → "Nothing I do matters" → deeper emotional flatness

**Break point:** Introduce TINY obligations with immediate emotional resonance. "Hold this for one hour. Notice how it feels to keep your word."

---

### 6.2 The Guilt-Machine (Emotional × Moral, both dark-addiction)

**Loop:** Constant guilt (Emotional) + rigid conventional morality (Moral) = crushing self-punishment. Every action is a potential code-violation; every code-violation produces overwhelming guilt. The emotional and moral systems reinforce each other's rigidity.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Moral dark-addiction → "Every action might be wrong" → constant guilt
- Emotional dark-addiction → "I must feel guilty" → more moral rigidity

**Break point:** Present situations where the code explicitly permits rest, joy, and imperfection. "The code says: you are allowed to be human."

---

### 6.3 The Cold Enforcer (Emotional × Interpersonal, dark-allergy × dark-addiction)

**Loop:** Cannot feel group-emotions (Emotional allergy) + enforces group code rigidly (Interpersonal addiction) = enforces rules without feeling their human weight. The code is enforced but the people inside it are invisible.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Emotional allergy → "I don't feel their pain" → rigid enforcement
- Interpersonal rigidity → "The code is all that matters" → deeper emotional allergy

**Break point:** Present situations where enforcement requires FEELING the human cost. "The code says enforce. But can you feel what enforcement costs them?"

---

### 6.4 The Spiritual Penitent (Emotional × Spiritual, both dark-addiction)

**Loop:** Constant guilt (Emotional) + mythic-membership faith (Spiritual) = endless penitence. The sacred demands perfection; the emotional system produces guilt for every imperfection; the sacred demands more penitence; the cycle continues.

**Bidirectional mechanism:**
- Spiritual dark-addiction → "The sacred demands perfection" → constant guilt
- Emotional dark-addiction → "I must feel guilty" → more spiritual penitence

**Break point:** Present a sacred tradition that explicitly includes forgiveness and rest. "The tradition says: you are forgiven. Can you feel that?"

### Dependencies
- **Supports:** Interpersonal/Amber (role-affect enables group-coordination); Moral/Amber (guilt-capacity enables genuine moral reasoning)
- **Depends on:** Willpower/Amber (duty-will sustains emotional commitment); Intrapersonal/Amber (role-identity grounds role-affect)

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive revelation order

1. **Role-affect recognition** → Dark-allergy surfaces first (cannot recognise role-emotions = immediately visible)
2. **Guilt-capacity** → Dark-addiction surfaces (constant guilt vs. proportional guilt)
3. **Loyalty-feeling** → Dark-allergy deepens (cannot feel group-warmth)
4. **Duty-emotion** → Dark-addiction deepens (duty-feeling as punishment vs. motivation)
5. **Emotional regulation** → Golden-allergy surfaces (refusal to examine emotional demands)
6. **Emotional sophistication tasks** → Golden-addiction surfaces (performance without genuine feeling)

### 7.2 Per-modality surfacing

| Modality | Dark-Addiction signal | Dark-Allergy signal | Golden-Addiction signal | Golden-Allergy signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deterministic | Constant guilt signals; cannot rest | No role-affect recognition; flat | High verbal sophistication + low recognition accuracy | Avoids emotional examination tasks |
| Language-reflective | Absolute duty-emotion language; "I must feel this" | "I feel what I feel" (Red-stage language) | Sophisticated emotional vocabulary without genuine feeling | Refuses to discuss WHY the role requires this emotion |
| Scenario-choice | Chooses role-emotion regardless of context | Chooses self-serving emotion over role-appropriate | Chooses emotional sophistication over simple role-affect | Chooses role-emotion but refuses to examine it |
| Embodied-somatic | Body rigid with duty-feeling; cannot rest | Body disconnected from role-affect | Body performs emotional attunement without genuine feeling | Body holds role-emotion but freezes at novel emotional demands |
| Strategic-planning | Plans emotional obligations obsessively | Cannot plan emotional engagement; impulsive | Plans emotional sophistication without role-affect foundation | Refuses to plan beyond current emotional code |
| Social-cooperative | Enforces emotional standards on group | Cannot feel group-emotions; isolated | Performs group emotional attunement without genuine feeling | Defers all emotional reasoning to group code |
| Immersive-rpg | Finds emotional obligations everywhere; cannot rest | Ignores role-emotional cues; emotionally flat | Performs emotional sophistication spontaneously without genuine feeling | Stays in known emotional territory; refuses novel role-affect |

### 7.3 Cross-validation rules
- Deterministic high + Language-reflective low = implicit role-affect without metacognition (normal early)
- Language-reflective high + Deterministic low = verbal performance without genuine feeling (golden-addiction)
- Social-cooperative high + Deterministic low = group-dependent affect (communion-addiction)
- Guilt-capacity high + loyalty-feeling low = punitive emotion without warmth (dark-addiction variant)
