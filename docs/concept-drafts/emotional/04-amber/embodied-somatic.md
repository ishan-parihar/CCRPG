# Emotional / Amber — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as the site of role-affect — interoceptive accuracy for role-emotions, whether loyalty, guilt, and duty-feeling are genuinely felt in the body or merely conceptual.
> **Why this axis for this module:** Role-emotions that are genuinely integrated live in the body. Loyalty has a felt warmth in the chest. Guilt has a felt weight in the stomach. Duty-feeling has a felt steadiness in the spine. This modality reveals whether role-affect is embodied or performed.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Body Knows"
- **Core mechanic:** The player is guided through interoceptive tasks that locate role-emotions in the body. Where is the loyalty felt? Where is the guilt? Where is the duty-feeling? The game measures interoceptive accuracy and the quality of body-emotion connection for role-congruent affect.
- **Duration:** 4-8 minutes per session
- **Internal progression:** Body Scan → Locating Loyalty → Feeling Guilt in the Body → Duty-Feeling as Posture → The Embodied Role

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**What this modality uniquely presents:** The SOMATIC dimension of role-affect — whether role-emotions are genuinely embodied or merely conceptual. A player who identifies role-emotions accurately (deterministic) but cannot locate them in the body has a mind-body split in their emotional life.

**How this differs from other modalities:** Unlike deterministic (which measures cognitive affect accuracy), this modality measures whether role-affect is GROUNDED IN THE BODY. Unlike scenario-choice (which measures decision quality), this modality measures the physical texture of role-emotion.

**What it uniquely surfaces:** The duty-martyr's body is rigid with constant guilt-weight. The emotional deserter's body is disconnected from role-affect. The premature empath's body performs emotional attunement without genuine felt-sense. The feeling-refuser's body holds role-emotions but freezes at novel emotional demands.

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates embodied role-affect — loyalty felt as warmth, guilt felt as weight, duty-feeling felt as steadiness — neither compulsive nor absent, genuinely grounded in body-awareness.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Body Knows: a somatic-interface game where the player is guided through body-awareness tasks. The interface shows a body-map; the player locates emotions in the body and rates their intensity. Aesthetic: Amber-stage contemplative space — quiet, candlelit, the body as sacred vessel.

### Interaction
- **Body Scan (1-5):** "Bring your attention to your body. What do you notice?" Baseline interoceptive awareness. Tests whether the player can sense their body at all.
- **Locating Loyalty (5-15):** "Think of your order. Where in your body do you feel the belonging?" Player locates and rates loyalty-feeling. Tests genuine vs. absent loyalty-affect.
- **Feeling Guilt in the Body (15-30):** "You broke a small vow. Where in your body do you feel it?" Player locates guilt. Tests genuine guilt (weight/constriction) vs. shame (heat/exposure) vs. absence.
- **Duty-Feeling as Posture (30-50):** "Your duty calls. How does your body respond? What posture does duty require?" Player enacts duty-posture. Tests embodied duty-feeling.
- **The Embodied Role (50+):** Full integration: body-scan + loyalty-location + guilt-feeling + duty-posture simultaneously.

### Feedback
- Genuine body-emotion connection → "The loyalty lives in your chest. The guilt in your stomach. The duty in your spine. The body knows the code."
- Body-emotion disconnection → "You named the feeling but the body was silent. The feeling lives somewhere. Can you find it?"
- Rigid body-guilt → "The guilt is everywhere in your body. It has taken over. The body asks: is this proportional? Can the guilt be located, not spread?"
- Performed body-emotion → "The body moved as if feeling. But was the feeling there? The body cannot lie. Try again — not the gesture, the feeling."
- Novel emotion refused → "A new role-feeling is offered. Your body knows the old ones. Can it learn this new one?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Interoceptive precision: general area → specific location → intensity rating
- Emotion complexity: single role-emotion → multiple → mixed
- Guilt-shame discrimination: obvious → subtle → ambiguous
- Duty-posture complexity: simple → sustained → under load
- Novel emotion introduction: familiar → slightly novel → significantly novel

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Body Scan | 1-5 | Baseline interoceptive awareness |
| Locating Loyalty | 5-15 | Loyalty-feeling body-location |
