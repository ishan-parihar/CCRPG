# Spiritual / Amber — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Objective faith measurement — tradition-knowledge accuracy, ritual-consistency, and value-coherence within the tradition.  **Why this axis for this module:** Mythic-membership faith has a *knowledge* dimension — how accurately and coherently the player holds their tradition's content reveals whether faith is rigid, absent, bypassed, or genuinely integrated.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Keeper's Codex

**Core Mechanic:** Pattern-matching and sequence-completion tasks using tradition-specific symbols, ritual orderings, and value-hierarchies. The player demonstrates knowledge of sacred sequences, identifies inconsistencies, and maintains coherence across increasingly complex tradition-structures.

**Duration:** 4–8 minutes per session.

**Internal Progression:** (1) Simple symbol recognition → (2) Ritual sequence completion → (3) Value-hierarchy ordering → (4) Cross-tradition pattern discrimination → (5) Paradox-holding under time pressure.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Unique Presentation:** Abstract sacred geometries and symbol-sequences presented as puzzles. No narrative framing, no language production, no social context — pure pattern and structure.

**Differs From Others:** No verbal articulation (language-reflective), no dilemmas (scenario-choice), no body (embodied-somatic), no planning (strategic), no others (social), no free-play (immersive). Pure cognitive-spiritual measurement.

**Uniquely Surfaces:** DA through hyper-accuracy on own tradition + inability to process other-tradition patterns. DAll through low accuracy across all traditions. GA through high accuracy on post-conventional frameworks but gaps in any single tradition's specifics. GAll through high own-tradition accuracy + anxiety/errors when cross-tradition items appear.

**Successful Integration:** Player demonstrates deep tradition-knowledge AND can process other-tradition patterns without threat or premature synthesis.

## 3. Game Design

**Setup:** Player selects or is assigned a tradition-stream (based on prior sessions). Sacred symbols, ritual sequences, and value-orderings from that tradition populate the item pool.

**Interaction Phases:**
1. **Recognition** — identify which symbols belong to the player's tradition vs. others
2. **Sequencing** — complete ritual orderings (liturgical calendar, prayer sequence, ceremonial steps)
3. **Coherence** — given a set of values, order them as the tradition would
4. **Discrimination** — distinguish subtle differences between similar traditions' symbols
5. **Paradox** — hold two apparently contradictory tradition-truths simultaneously (dual-select)

**Feedback Examples:**
1. Correct sequence → symbol illuminates, tradition-resonance score rises
2. Rigid exclusion of other-tradition symbols → subtle dimming (faith narrowing)
3. Random/careless responses → codex pages scatter (no tradition-ground)
4. Premature synthesis of traditions → symbols blur and lose distinctness
5. Anxiety-driven avoidance of cross-tradition items → codex locks certain pages

**Difficulty Adaptation:** Sequence length increases, cross-tradition similarity increases, paradox complexity deepens, time pressure modulates based on demonstrated capacity.

**Internal Progression Table:**

| Level | Task | Shadow Probed | Drive Engaged |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Symbol recognition (own tradition) | Baseline | Communion (belonging) |
| 2 | Ritual sequence completion | DAll (gaps in knowledge) | Agape (embodying tradition) |
| 3 | Value-hierarchy ordering | DA (rigid ordering) | Agency (personal relationship) |
| 4 | Cross-tradition discrimination | DA/GAll (threat/avoidance) | Eros (reaching toward complexity) |
| 5 | Paradox-holding | All four | All four (integration) |

## 4. Item Pool

**Category 1 — Sacred Symbols (30+):** Geometric mandalas, cross-variants, crescents, wheels, trees, flames, waters, mountains, eyes, hands, spirals, stars, lotuses, serpents, gates, pillars, vessels, crowns, keys, books, bells, drums, feathers, stones, circles, triangles, squares, rays, voids, seeds.

**Category 2 — Ritual Sequences (20+):** Prayer orderings, ceremonial steps, liturgical calendars, initiation stages, purification sequences, offering orders, meditation progressions, chant structures, pilgrimage routes, feast-fast cycles, blessing formulas, invocation chains, seasonal rites, life-passage ceremonies, daily devotion structures, sabbath preparations, vigil sequences, communion steps, confession orders, dedication rites.

**Category 3 — Value-Hierarchies (15+):** Virtue orderings, sin-severity rankings, duty-priority lists, sacred-profane boundaries, purity codes, charity hierarchies, obedience structures, devotion levels, sacrifice orderings, honour codes, taboo gradients, blessing conditions, grace requirements, merit systems, covenant obligations.

**Category 4 — Cross-Tradition Pairs (20+):** Baptism/mikveh, rosary/mala, sabbath/shabbat/jumu'ah, confession/vidui, saints/bodhisattvas, heaven/jannah/moksha, sin/karma, grace/baraka, scripture pairs, ritual-meal variants, pilgrimage sites, sacred-calendar overlaps, priesthood structures, mystical-branch pairs, creation narratives, flood narratives, prophet-figures, sacred-law codes, afterlife models, sacred-architecture forms.

**Category 5 — Paradox Items (15+):** Mercy vs. justice, free will vs. divine plan, suffering as punishment vs. gift, exclusivity vs. universal love, literal vs. metaphorical scripture, law vs. spirit, individual vs. community salvation, sacred violence vs. peace, doubt as sin vs. faith-deepener, tradition-change vs. eternal truth, mystic vs. orthodox, body as sacred vs. fallen, world-affirming vs. world-denying, immanence vs. transcendence, silence vs. proclamation.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input Types:** Tap/click selection, drag-and-drop sequencing, swipe for categorisation, hold for paradox-acceptance (dual-select).

**Timing:** 2–6 second response windows (adaptive). Paradox items allow 8–12 seconds. Session hard-cap at 10 minutes.

**NPC/AI:** None. Pure player-vs-pattern.

**LLM:** None in-session. LLM generates tradition-specific item pools during content-authoring phase only.

**State Persistence:** Tradition-stream assignment, accuracy per category, cross-tradition threat-response patterns, paradox-tolerance scores, session-over-session trajectory per shadow quadrant.
