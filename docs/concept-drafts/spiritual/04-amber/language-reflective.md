# Spiritual / Amber — Language-Reflective Game Concept

> **Axis:** Verbal articulation of faith — how the player talks about tradition, the sacred, and meaning reveals their shadow structure.  **Why this axis for this module:** Mythic-membership faith lives in *language* — the stories we tell, the words we use for the sacred, the way we speak about belonging and otherness expose whether faith is rigid, absent, bypassed, or genuinely held.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Sacred Tongue

**Core Mechanic:** LLM-prompted reflective writing/speaking about tradition, faith, belonging, and the sacred. The player responds to evocative prompts; the LLM scores linguistic markers of shadow-structure without the player knowing what is being measured.

**Duration:** 5–10 minutes per session.

**Internal Progression:** (1) Simple tradition-naming → (2) Describing sacred experiences → (3) Articulating why *this* tradition → (4) Responding to challenge-prompts → (5) Holding complexity in language.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Unique Presentation:** A contemplative journal interface — the player writes to prompts that feel like spiritual reflection, not assessment. Prompts are framed as questions from a wise elder within the tradition.

**Differs From Others:** No objective measurement (deterministic), no dilemmas (scenario-choice), no body (embodied-somatic), no planning (strategic), no others (social), no free-play (immersive). Pure language-as-window-to-faith.

**Uniquely Surfaces:** DA through absolutist language ("the ONLY truth," "they are wrong," "never question"). DAll through dismissive/cynical language ("it's all made up," "traditions are control"). GA through premature-complexity language ("all paths lead to the same place," "I'm beyond that"). GAll through anxiety-language when doubt-prompts appear ("I don't want to think about that," deflection, topic-change).

**Successful Integration:** Player uses language that holds tradition as genuinely sacred AND acknowledges complexity without collapsing into rigidity or premature transcendence.

## 3. Game Design

**Setup:** The player enters a "sacred study" space. A tradition-elder NPC (text-based) poses reflective questions. The player types or speaks responses.

**Interaction Phases:**
1. **Grounding** — "Tell me about a tradition that feels like home to you"
2. **Deepening** — "What makes this tradition sacred, not merely useful?"
3. **Boundary** — "What would someone outside this tradition not understand?"
4. **Challenge** — "A wise person from another tradition says [X]. How do you respond?"
5. **Integration** — "Can your tradition hold this doubt and still be sacred?"

**Feedback Examples:**
1. Rich, embodied tradition-language → elder nods, study brightens
2. Absolutist/exclusionary language → elder's expression becomes concerned (subtle)
3. Dismissive/cynical language → study grows cold, elder becomes distant
4. Over-abstract/bypassing language → elder asks "but what do YOU feel in your bones?"
5. Anxious deflection → elder gently returns to the question with compassion

**Difficulty Adaptation:** Challenge-prompts become more pointed. Other-tradition perspectives become more compelling. Doubt-prompts become more existentially threatening. Complexity of integration required increases.

**Internal Progression Table:**

| Level | Prompt Type | Shadow Probed | Drive Engaged |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tradition-naming | Baseline | Communion (belonging-language) |
| 2 | Sacred-experience description | DAll (inability to articulate) | Agape (returning to felt-faith) |
| 3 | Why-this-tradition | DA (exclusionary reasoning) | Agency (personal ownership) |
| 4 | Other-tradition challenge | DA/GAll (threat/avoidance) | Eros (reaching toward other) |
| 5 | Doubt-integration | All four | All four (linguistic integration) |

## 4. Item Pool

**Category 1 — Grounding Prompts (20+):** "Name a tradition that shaped you," "Describe a ritual that feels sacred," "What story from your tradition moves you most?", "Who taught you about the sacred?", "What does 'belonging' feel like in faith?", "Describe a moment you felt held by something larger," "What prayer/practice do you return to?", "What symbol carries meaning for you?", "When did tradition first feel real?", "What would you miss if your tradition vanished?", "Describe the sacred in one image," "What does your tradition say about suffering?", "How does your tradition mark time?", "What is the oldest thing in your tradition?", "Describe the feeling of communal worship/practice," "What does your tradition ask of you daily?", "Name a saint/sage/ancestor who matters," "What does your tradition say about death?", "How does your tradition welcome newcomers?", "What is the heart of your tradition in one sentence?"

**Category 2 — Deepening Prompts (15+):** "What makes this sacred rather than merely comforting?", "How is your tradition different from a philosophy?", "What does your tradition know that reason alone cannot?", "Where does your tradition locate the divine?", "What is the relationship between faith and certainty?", "How does your tradition handle mystery?", "What is the role of story in your faith?", "How does ritual differ from habit?", "What does 'revelation' mean in your tradition?", "What is the difference between belief and faith?", "How does your tradition understand the body?", "What does your tradition say about the natural world?", "How does your tradition relate to time?", "What is sacred about community in your tradition?", "Where does your tradition locate authority?"

**Category 3 — Boundary Prompts (15+):** "What would an outsider misunderstand?", "What criticism of your tradition has some truth?", "What do you find difficult about your tradition?", "Where does your tradition draw lines?", "How does your tradition relate to traditions that contradict it?", "What has your tradition gotten wrong historically?", "Is there a teaching you struggle with?", "How do you relate to members who interpret differently?", "What would make you leave?", "What is non-negotiable?", "How does your tradition handle heresy?", "What is the shadow of your tradition?", "How does your tradition relate to power?", "What does your tradition suppress?", "Where is your tradition growing?"

**Category 4 — Challenge Prompts (20+):** Statements from other traditions that directly challenge the player's tradition — presented respectfully by named wise figures. Designed to evoke DA (defensive rigidity), DAll (cynical dismissal), GA (premature "they're all the same"), or GAll (anxious avoidance). LLM-generated based on player's tradition-stream.

**Category 5 — Integration Prompts (15+):** "Can your tradition hold doubt and remain sacred?", "Can two contradictory traditions both be true?", "What would it mean to deepen your faith rather than defend it?", "How might questioning strengthen rather than destroy?", "Can you belong fully AND see clearly?", "Can the sacred survive scrutiny?", "How does your tradition grow without betraying itself?", "What is the relationship between your tradition and truth?", "Can you love your tradition without needing it to be the only way?", "What would mature faith look like?", "How do you hold the tension between loyalty and honesty?", "Can tradition be both human and sacred?", "What does your tradition look like from the inside vs. outside?", "Where is the growing edge of your faith?", "What does your tradition need from you now?"

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input Types:** Free-text typing (primary), voice-to-text (optional). Minimum 20 words per response for scoring validity.

**Timing:** No time pressure. Prompts remain until answered. Session pacing is player-determined. Gentle nudge after 3 minutes of inactivity.

**NPC/AI:** Tradition-elder NPC delivers prompts and provides subtle emotional feedback. Not conversational — structured prompt-response with adaptive follow-ups.

**LLM:** Core engine. Scores responses on: absolutism markers, dismissal markers, bypassing markers, avoidance markers, integration markers, emotional authenticity, linguistic complexity, tradition-specificity, self-awareness. Generates adaptive follow-up prompts based on detected shadow-pattern.

**State Persistence:** Response transcripts (encrypted), shadow-marker scores per session, linguistic-complexity trajectory, tradition-stream identification, prompt-difficulty level, cross-session shadow-pattern convergence data.
