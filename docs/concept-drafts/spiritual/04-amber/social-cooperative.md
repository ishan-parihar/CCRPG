# Spiritual / Amber — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** Shared faith — group ritual, responding to others' faith-violations, maintaining tradition together.  **Why this axis for this module:** Mythic-membership faith is inherently *communal* — it exists in the space between believers. How the player relates to others' faith (supports, polices, dismisses, or transcends it) reveals shadow-structure in the social dimension.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Congregation

**Core Mechanic:** Cooperative ritual-maintenance with AI-driven community members who exhibit various faith-behaviours (devout, questioning, lapsing, converting, zealous). The player must respond to others' faith-states while maintaining communal sacred practice together.

**Duration:** 5–8 minutes per session.

**Internal Progression:** (1) Simple communal ritual (sync with others) → (2) Supporting a questioner → (3) Responding to a faith-violation → (4) Holding community through disagreement → (5) Welcoming the outsider without losing tradition.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Unique Presentation:** A communal sacred space with 3–5 AI community members. The player participates in group ritual while social dynamics unfold — someone questions, someone transgresses, someone from outside approaches.

**Differs From Others:** No solo measurement (deterministic), no private language (language-reflective), no abstract dilemmas (scenario-choice), no solo body (embodied-somatic), no solo planning (strategic), no free-play (immersive). Pure faith-in-relationship.

**Uniquely Surfaces:** DA through policing others' faith — correcting, excluding, demanding conformity. DAll through disengagement from communal practice — present but not participating. GA through performing spiritual superiority — "I'm beyond this group practice." GAll through anxious conformity — matching the group exactly, terrified of standing out or questioning.

**Successful Integration:** Player participates authentically in communal practice AND holds space for others' different faith-expressions without policing or withdrawing.

## 3. Game Design

**Setup:** Player joins a communal ritual space with AI community members. A shared practice begins (chanting, prayer, ceremony). Social dynamics emerge during the practice.

**Interaction Phases:**
1. **Joining** — synchronise with the group's sacred practice (rhythm, gesture, response)
2. **Witnessing** — a community member expresses doubt or struggle during practice
3. **Responding** — choose how to relate to the struggling member (support, correct, ignore, transcend)
4. **Boundary** — someone violates a tradition-norm (arrives late, uses wrong words, brings outsider)
5. **Integration** — hold the community together through the disruption while maintaining sacred quality

**Feedback Examples:**
1. Supportive presence to questioner → community cohesion rises, questioner stays
2. Harsh correction of questioner → community tightens, questioner withdraws
3. Ignoring the community entirely → player becomes isolated, practice loses communal quality
4. Performing superiority → others feel diminished, community fragments
5. Anxious over-conformity → community becomes rigid, spontaneity dies

**Difficulty Adaptation:** Community members' challenges become more severe. Violations become more ambiguous. The tension between inclusion and tradition-integrity increases. Multiple simultaneous social demands emerge.

**Internal Progression Table:**

| Level | Social Task | Shadow Probed | Drive Engaged |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Synchronise with group practice | Baseline | Communion (joining) |
| 2 | Support a questioning member | DAll (disengagement) | Agape (returning to care) |
| 3 | Respond to faith-violation | DA (policing) | Agency (sovereign response) |
| 4 | Hold community through disagreement | DA/GAll (rigidity/anxiety) | Eros (reaching toward complexity) |
| 5 | Welcome outsider without losing tradition | All four | All four (social integration) |

## 4. Item Pool

**Category 1 — Communal Practices (20+):** Group chanting, responsive prayer, shared silence, ritual meal, circle ceremony, processional walking, communal singing, call-and-response, group meditation, shared scripture reading, collective offering, hand-holding prayer, swaying together, group prostration, candle-lighting chain, water-sharing ritual, bread-breaking, incense-passing, bell-ringing round, seasonal group celebration.

**Category 2 — Community Member Archetypes (15+):** The devoted elder, the questioning youth, the lapsed returner, the zealous newcomer, the grieving parent, the intellectual doubter, the tradition-rebel, the quiet faithful, the performative believer, the outsider-curious, the convert-in-process, the tradition-scholar, the mystic-within, the social-joiner, the wounded-by-tradition.

**Category 3 — Faith-Violations (15+):** Arriving late to sacred practice, using wrong liturgical words, bringing an uninitiated outsider, questioning during sacred silence, refusing a required gesture, wearing inappropriate attire, eating during fast, speaking heresy casually, mixing traditions openly, skipping required preparation, challenging the elder publicly, bringing secular concerns into sacred space, photographing the ritual, laughing during solemnity, leaving before completion.

**Category 4 — Social Responses Available (20+):** Gentle correction, harsh rebuke, silent acceptance, warm welcome, cold shoulder, private conversation later, public address, modelling correct behaviour, asking the group, deferring to elder, protecting the violator, explaining tradition kindly, setting boundary with love, ignoring entirely, over-explaining defensively, spiritual one-upmanship, anxious smoothing-over, authentic vulnerability, holding silence, inviting dialogue.

**Category 5 — Community Crises (15+):** A schism over interpretation, an elder exposed as hypocrite, a youth exodus, a tradition-change proposed, an outsider group moves nearby, a member converts away publicly, a sacred space is threatened, financial crisis in community, a scandal, a miracle-claim divides opinion, generational conflict over practice-form, a charismatic newcomer challenges leadership, interfaith event proposed, a member's suffering challenges theodicy, the tradition's relevance questioned publicly.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input Types:** Choice-selection for social responses, rhythm-sync for communal practice (tap/swipe), proximity-gestures (move toward/away from community members).

**Timing:** Communal practice runs in real-time (adaptive tempo). Social responses have 5–10 second windows (urgency varies). Crisis responses allow 15–20 seconds.

**NPC/AI:** 3–5 AI community members with distinct faith-personalities. They react dynamically to player's choices. Emotional states visible through subtle cues.

**LLM:** Generates community-member dialogue, adapts crisis scenarios to tradition-stream, scores player's social-response patterns for shadow-mapping.

**State Persistence:** Social-response pattern history (policing vs. supporting vs. withdrawing vs. transcending), communal-sync accuracy, community-cohesion trajectory, tradition-stream social norms, cross-session relationship continuity with recurring AI members.
