# Spiritual × Infrared — Scenario-Choice Game

## 1. Core Concept

**Title:** Toward the Edge

The organism is safe by the fire. Beyond the firelight, something vast exists — not dangerous, but enormous. The night sky. A canyon. An ocean sound. The game presents binary choices at the most primitive level: approach the vast, or retreat to the known. Not moral choices. Not symbolic choices. Pure orientation-toward-wonder vs. orientation-toward-safety.

The question is never "what does it mean?" — only "do you move toward it?"

## 2. Mechanical Specification

**Input:** Binary directional choice — toward (swipe/tap outward) or away (swipe/tap inward). Timing and hesitation are measured but the choice itself is the primary data.

**Scenario structure:** Each scenario is a 3-beat micro-narrative:
1. Safety established (fire, warmth, shelter — 3 seconds)
2. Vast-reveal at periphery (something enormous becomes perceptible — 5 seconds)
3. Choice moment (approach or retreat — untimed but latency measured)

**Metrics captured:**
- Approach/retreat ratio across scenarios of varying magnitude
- Hesitation latency: time between vast-reveal and choice (longer = more conflict)
- Magnitude sensitivity: does approach-rate change with how vast the stimulus is?
- Sequential pattern: approach-approach-approach (fixation?) vs. retreat-retreat-retreat (numbness?) vs. alternating (healthy oscillation)
- Post-choice dwell: after approaching, does the player linger (integration) or immediately return (fortress)?

**Progression:** Early scenarios have dramatic, unmissable vastness. Later scenarios present subtle enormity — a slightly brighter star, a distant sound that might be wind or might be ocean. The choice becomes harder as the vast becomes quieter.

## 3. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Behavioural Signal |
|---|---|
| DA (Awe-Fixation) | Always approaches; never retreats; post-choice dwell extends indefinitely; cannot choose safety |
| DAll (Awe-Numbness) | Always retreats; no magnitude sensitivity; treats vast and ordinary identically |
| GA (Premature Animism) | Approaches even when nothing vast is present (false-vast scenarios); indiscriminate approach |
| GAll (Awe Fortress) | Approaches but with minimal dwell; immediately retreats after contact; approach-then-flee pattern |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The vast-reveal at the edge of the known — the invitation to move toward enormity.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of choosing. The game never judges approach or retreat — both are valid survival responses. But the PATTERN across many choices reveals the organism's relationship to wonder.

**Integration:** After each choice, a brief settling phase. If approached: a moment WITH the vast (stars overhead, ocean sound surrounding). If retreated: a moment of deeper fire-warmth. Both are honoured. Integration = the capacity to choose EITHER freely, without compulsion in either direction.

**Healing vector (bottom-up):** For awe-numbness — vast-reveals gradually increase in salience until approach becomes possible; for awe-fixation — retreat is presented as warmth-return rather than loss.

**Evolution vector (top-down):** For premature animism — false-vast scenarios teach discrimination; for awe-fortress — post-approach dwell phases are extended with no pressure to return, allowing wonder to simply exist.

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session = 8–15 micro-scenarios (4–7 minutes). Every choice is a checkpoint. The player can leave after any settling phase. The cumulative choice-profile evolves over sessions — a map of the organism's wonder-orientation. Theta-decay ensures the profile must be periodically refreshed; old approach-patterns don't count forever.
