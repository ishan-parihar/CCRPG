# Spiritual × Infrared — Social-Cooperative Game

## 1. Core Concept

**Title:** Shared Fire, Shared Sky

Two organisms sit at the same fire. The vast appears. The game measures whether wonder can be CO-EXPERIENCED — not communicated (no language at Infrared), not interpreted, but simply shared. The presence of another who is also struck amplifies the numinous. The absence of shared wonder isolates.

This is the seed of all later communal spirituality: two beings, struck by the same enormity, aware of each other's awe. No ritual, no symbol, no word — only shared presence before the vast.

## 2. Mechanical Specification

**Input:** Same as deterministic (wonder-tap) but with a visible co-player (AI companion or async ghost of another player's recorded session). Both players' responses are visible to each other as subtle avatar-animations.

**Stimuli:** Vast-reveals identical to deterministic game, but with the addition of the co-player's visible response (their avatar's stillness, orientation-shift, breath-catch animation).

**Metrics captured:**
- Synchrony index: how closely the player's wonder-response aligns temporally with the co-player's
- Social amplification: does the player's response magnitude increase when the co-player also responds? (shared wonder)
- Social suppression: does the player's response decrease when the co-player doesn't respond? (social conformity overriding genuine awe)
- Independent wonder: can the player respond when the co-player doesn't? (agency in awe)
- Co-regulation: does the player's return-to-ordinary synchronise with the co-player's? (communion in integration)
- False social mirroring: responding only because the co-player did, without genuine stimulus (premature communion)

**Progression:** Early trials have a co-player who responds genuinely and obviously. Later trials introduce ambiguity — co-player sometimes responds to non-vast stimuli, sometimes doesn't respond to vast ones. Can the player maintain genuine awe-response independent of social pressure while still being open to shared wonder?

## 3. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Behavioural Signal |
|---|---|
| DA (Awe-Fixation) | Synchrony becomes fusion; cannot return to ordinary unless co-player does; shared wonder becomes shared paralysis |
| DAll (Awe-Numbness) | No social amplification; co-player's wonder doesn't affect response; isolated even in shared presence |
| GA (Premature Animism) | Mirrors co-player's response regardless of stimulus; social mirroring replaces genuine awe |
| GAll (Awe Fortress) | Responds genuinely but shows no synchrony; wonder is real but cannot be shared; isolated awe |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The vast-reveal in the presence of another — the numinous becomes a shared field rather than a private event.

**Experience:** The felt-sense of "we are both struck." Not communicated, not discussed — simply felt through temporal synchrony and visible co-response. This is communion at its most primitive: shared orientation toward the same enormity.

**Integration:** The shared return-to-ordinary — both organisms settling back to firelight together. Integration = wonder can be shared without fusion (maintaining agency) and without isolation (maintaining communion).

**Healing vector (bottom-up):** For awe-numbness — the co-player's visible response serves as social scaffolding for the player's own; for awe-fixation — the co-player models healthy return-to-ordinary.

**Evolution vector (top-down):** For premature animism — trials where co-player responds to non-vast stimuli test whether the player follows social cues or genuine awe; for awe-fortress — the co-player's warmth during shared wonder demonstrates that communion doesn't threaten autonomy.

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session = one shared fire-sitting (4–8 minutes). Checkpoints at each shared return-to-ordinary. The player's "shared wonder profile" tracks synchrony, independence, and amplification patterns over time. Theta-decay: the capacity for shared wonder must be re-demonstrated; social-spiritual capacity decays without practice just as individual awe-response does.
