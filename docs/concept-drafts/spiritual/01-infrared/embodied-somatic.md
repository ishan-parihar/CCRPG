# Spiritual × Infrared — Embodied-Somatic Game

## 1. Core Concept

**Title:** The Body Opens

When genuine awe strikes, the body responds before the mind: shoulders drop, chest opens, breath catches, movement stills. This game measures the somatic signature of wonder — can the organism's body enter the awe-posture? Not performed or mimicked, but genuinely evoked by encounter with the vast.

The player's avatar mirrors their physical state. When the vast appears, the game measures whether the body opens or contracts, stills or flees, breathes or braces.

## 2. Mechanical Specification

**Input:** Gyroscope/accelerometer data (phone held or placed on chest) measuring stillness, orientation-shift, and micro-movement. Fallback: touch-pressure patterns on screen (light sustained touch = open/still; grip = contracted; release = collapse).

**Stimuli:** Immersive vast-reveals with strong somatic pull — deep bass rumbles (felt in chest), slow visual expansions, warmth-pulses (screen colour temperature shifts). Designed to evoke involuntary body-response.

**Metrics captured:**
- Stillness onset: how quickly movement ceases when the vast appears (awe-freeze, distinct from fear-freeze by subsequent opening)
- Opening signature: does stillness transition to openness (reduced grip, orientation-shift upward) or remain contracted?
- Breath-catch duration: measured via micro-movement periodicity change
- Recovery time: how long before ordinary movement resumes
- Premature movement: moving/tapping during vast-reveal before it completes (impatience / fortress)
- Somatic non-response: no measurable change in movement/pressure profile (numbness)

**Progression:** Early stimuli are physically overwhelming (deep bass, full-screen bloom). Later stimuli are subtle — a slight warmth, a distant low tone. Can the body still open for quiet enormity?

## 3. Shadow Diagnostics

| Shadow | Behavioural Signal |
|---|---|
| DA (Awe-Fixation) | Stillness never resolves; body remains frozen in open-posture indefinitely; cannot resume movement |
| DAll (Awe-Numbness) | No somatic change; movement profile identical during vast-reveal and ordinary moments |
| GA (Premature Animism) | Opening-posture adopted before stimulus arrives; body performs awe without being struck |
| GAll (Awe Fortress) | Brief stillness immediately followed by deliberate contraction; body opens then slams shut |

## 4. Catalyst → Experience → Integration

**Catalyst:** The somatic vast-reveal — stimuli designed to bypass cognition and strike the body directly. Bass frequencies, warmth-shifts, spatial audio creating sense of enormity.

**Experience:** The body's own wisdom responding to the numinous. Not thought, not choice — the flesh recognising something vast. The game honours this as the most primitive and most genuine spiritual response.

**Integration:** After each vast-reveal, a "body-return" phase — gentle rhythmic stimuli (heartbeat-paced audio, warm colour pulse) guiding the body back to ordinary aliveness. Integration = the body can open AND close, be struck AND return.

**Healing vector (bottom-up):** For awe-numbness — somatic stimuli gradually intensify until body-response is evoked; for awe-fixation — body-return phases provide rhythmic scaffolding back to movement.

**Evolution vector (top-down):** For premature animism — the game only registers genuine somatic response (involuntary timing signatures, not performed ones); for awe-fortress — body-return phases are slowed, allowing openness to persist without threat.

## 5. Infinite Checkpoint Structure

Each session = one somatic sequence (3–8 minutes). Checkpoints at each body-return phase. The player can leave whenever the body has returned to ordinary. The somatic profile evolves across sessions — tracking whether the body's capacity for awe-openness is growing, stable, or decaying. Theta-decay: the body must periodically re-demonstrate its capacity to be struck.
