# Spiritual × Infrared — Module Specification

## 1. Module Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Line | Spiritual |
| Stage | Infrared (Archaic / Sensorimotor) |
| Energy Ray | Red |
| Core Capacity | Awe-Response — the organism's capacity to be struck by the numinous |
| Proto-Skill | Startle-into-wonder; the seed of all later spirituality |
| Below | Nothing (first stage) |
| Above | Magenta — animistic numinosity, participatory sacred |

At Infrared, spirituality is pre-religious, pre-symbolic. No gods, no spirits, no meaning-making. The sacred IS the warmth of fire, the safety of shelter. The only question: can the organism be struck by wonder?

## 2. Shadow Taxonomy

| Quadrant | Name | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Dark-Addiction | Awe-Fixation | Cannot leave the numinous moment; frozen in wonder; paralysed by vastness; cannot return to ordinary |
| Dark-Allergy | Awe-Numbness | Cannot be struck by anything; nothing is wondrous; flat affect toward the vast; 'it's just fire' |
| Golden-Addiction | Premature Animism | Attempts to see spirits/meaning without genuine awe-response; 'everything is sacred' without being struck |
| Golden-Allergy | Awe Fortress | Awe-response present but refuses spiritual emergence; terrified of meaning beyond survival; 'beautiful but means nothing' |

## 3. Compound Shadows

| # | Name | Cross-Line | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Frozen Wonder | Spiritual DA × Cognitive DA | Awe-fixation + hypervigilant scanning = paralysed by the vast |
| 2 | The Flat World | Spiritual DAll × Emotional DAll | Awe-numbness + affect-numbness = nothing moves, nothing matters |
| 3 | The Premature Mystic | Spiritual GA × Intrapersonal GA | Premature animism + premature self-image = 'chosen one' without ground |
| 4 | The Wonder Fortress | Spiritual GAll × Somatic GAll | Refuses spiritual emergence + refuses non-survival movement = beauty without meaning |

## 4. Drive Dynamics

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros (vertical-up) | Drawn toward the vast; approach-wonder | Fixated on the numinous, cannot return (DA) |
| Agape (vertical-down) | Returns from wonder to ordinary with warmth | Refuses to be moved at all (DAll) |
| Agency (horizontal-out) | Reaches toward the unknown alone | Fabricates meaning without genuine awe (GA) |
| Communion (horizontal-in) | Shares the wonder-moment with another | Walls off wonder from any larger significance (GAll) |

## 6. Developmental Vectors

**Heal/Evolve (bottom-up):** Agape + Agency integrate dark shadows — restore capacity to be struck (DAll) or to return from wonder (DA). Ground the organism in its own awe-response before any emergence.

**Evolve/Heal (top-down):** Eros + Communion dissolve golden shadows — genuine awe dissolves premature animism (GA); shared wonder dissolves the fortress of isolation (GAll).

## 7. Game Modalities

| # | Modality | Unique Probe Axis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deterministic | Objective awe-response measurement — startle-to-wonder latency, novelty magnitude, habituation resistance |
| 2 | Language-Reflective | Pre-verbal wonder-expression — breath-catch, gasp, silence-as-response, proto-reverence sounds |
| 3 | Scenario-Choice | Wonder-based choices — approach the vast / retreat to the known |
| 4 | Embodied-Somatic | Body in awe — stillness response, breath-catch, physical openness to the numinous |
| 5 | Strategic-Planning | Basic wonder-sequencing — approach-then-pause, 2-step engagement with the vast |
| 6 | Social-Cooperative | Shared wonder — co-experiencing the numinous with another presence |
| 7 | Immersive-RPG | Ecological wonder — whether awe-response appears spontaneously in free-play |
