# Spiritual / Orange — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Objective meaning-reasoning measurement — logical consistency of value-hierarchies, rational examination accuracy, belief-revision under evidence.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange spiritual capacity IS rational examination; deterministic mechanics measure whether that examination is logically sound or merely performed.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Examined Life  
**Core loop:** Player receives propositions about meaning/purpose/ultimate-concern and must evaluate logical consistency, identify hidden assumptions, and revise stated hierarchies when new evidence is introduced.  
**Feel:** A philosophical laboratory — clean, precise, intellectually demanding. The player is a meaning-scientist testing hypotheses about what matters.  
**Session length:** 4–8 minutes. Infinite checkpoint at each proposition-set.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Logical-consistency challenges that reveal whether the player's meaning-framework is genuinely examined or contains unacknowledged contradictions.  
**Shadow activation:** DA surfaces as rigid dismissal of non-measurable propositions. DAll surfaces as refusal to apply logic to sacred claims. GA surfaces as accepting contradictory meaning-claims without noticing inconsistency. GAll surfaces as insisting only one logical resolution exists when multiple are valid.  
**Integration path:** Catalyst → player notices their own logical pattern → experience of the pattern's cost → integration through revised reasoning that holds complexity without contradiction.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Value-Hierarchy Sorting:** Player arranges meaning-propositions into consistent hierarchies. System detects circular preferences, hidden axioms, and unexamined assumptions. Scored on logical consistency, not content.  
**Mechanic 2 — Belief-Revision Trials:** New evidence is introduced that challenges a previously stated position. Scored on appropriate revision magnitude — neither rigid (DA/GAll) nor over-accommodating (GA).  
**Mechanic 3 — Assumption Extraction:** Player identifies the unstated premises in meaning-claims. Surfaces DAll (cannot see own tradition's assumptions) and DA (cannot see scientism's assumptions).  
**Difficulty staircase:** Early items use abstract logical structures. Later items use emotionally charged meaning-content that tests whether rationality persists under affective load.  
**Drive probing:** Agency = sovereign logical conclusions. Communion = acknowledging valid alternative conclusions. Eros = reaching toward more comprehensive frameworks. Agape = grounding conclusions in lived implication.

## 4. Item Pool

**Category A — Logical Consistency (40%):** Sets of 4–6 meaning-propositions that may or may not form a consistent hierarchy. Player identifies contradictions or confirms consistency.  
**Category B — Evidence Integration (30%):** Scenarios where new information challenges a meaning-claim. Player demonstrates appropriate belief-revision.  
**Category C — Assumption Surfacing (20%):** Meaning-claims with hidden premises from rationalist, religious, pluralist, and nihilist traditions equally. Player extracts the unstated axiom.  
**Category D — Synthesis (10%):** Complex items requiring integration of multiple meaning-sources into a non-contradictory framework. Tests GAll and GA simultaneously.  
**Adaptive selection:** IRT-calibrated. Items selected to maximize information at current theta while rotating across shadow quadrants.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Scoring engine:** 4PL-IRT with discrimination, difficulty, guessing, and ceiling parameters. Separate theta tracks for each shadow quadrant plus overall capacity.  
**Response format:** Forced-choice (hierarchy sorting), magnitude estimation (belief-revision), free-identification (assumption extraction — scored against rubric).  
**State persistence:** Full item-response history, current theta vector, shadow-activation profile, drive-balance indicators.  
**Cross-modality hooks:** Exports logical-consistency theta to language-reflective (does verbal articulation match logical structure?) and scenario-choice (do choices align with stated hierarchy?).  
**Performance:** All scoring deterministic and local. No LLM dependency. Item pool pre-calibrated. Target 60fps with <100ms response-scoring latency.
