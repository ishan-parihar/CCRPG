# Spiritual / Orange — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Body as site of meaning — where purpose lives in the body, embodied sense of significance.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange rationalism often disembodies meaning entirely; this modality tests whether examined meaning has somatic grounding or floats as pure abstraction.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Living Inquiry  
**Core loop:** Player engages body-awareness exercises paired with meaning-prompts — noticing where purpose, doubt, conviction, and emptiness register somatically. Rhythm and breath mechanics gate access to deeper inquiry.  
**Feel:** A contemplative movement practice — slow, precise, attentive. The body becomes a philosophical instrument.  
**Session length:** 3–6 minutes. Infinite checkpoint after each somatic-inquiry cycle.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Somatic prompts that reveal the body's relationship to meaning — whether examined beliefs are embodied or merely intellectual, whether doubt lives in the chest or is suppressed below awareness.  
**Shadow activation:** DA surfaces as complete disconnection between body and meaning ("meaning is cognitive, the body is irrelevant"). DAll surfaces as body-as-sin or body-as-threat-to-faith (somatic signals suppressed to maintain belief). GA surfaces as performed embodiment without genuine somatic awareness. GAll surfaces as rigid body-patterns that enforce singular meaning.  
**Integration path:** Catalyst (somatic prompt) → experience (felt-sense of meaning's bodily location) → integration (meaning becomes both rational AND embodied).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — Somatic Mapping:** Player identifies where specific meaning-states register in the body. System tracks consistency and specificity of body-awareness over sessions. Scored on differentiation (can player distinguish purpose from conviction from doubt somatically?).  
**Mechanic 2 — Breath-Gated Inquiry:** Meaning-prompts delivered in sync with breath rhythm. Player must maintain somatic awareness while engaging rational examination. Tests whether rationality requires dissociation from body.  
**Mechanic 3 — Tension-Release Cycles:** Player identifies held tension patterns and explores what meaning-commitment the tension is protecting. Release of tension = willingness to examine what was held rigidly.  
**Difficulty staircase:** Level 1: basic body-awareness during neutral prompts. Level 2: maintaining somatic awareness during emotionally charged meaning-content. Level 3: identifying shadow-specific body patterns. Level 4: voluntary release of protective tension while holding rational inquiry.  
**Drive probing:** Agency = sovereign body-awareness without external instruction. Communion = body-resonance with others' meaning-claims. Eros = somatic reaching toward larger significance. Agape = grounding abstract meaning in bodily felt-sense.

## 4. Item Pool

**Category A — Location Prompts (35%):** "Where in your body do you feel [purpose/doubt/conviction/emptiness]?" Scored on specificity, consistency, and differentiation.  
**Category B — Breath-Inquiry Pairs (25%):** Meaning-questions delivered at specific breath-phases. Scored on maintained somatic awareness (via rhythm consistency) during cognitive engagement.  
**Category C — Tension Exploration (25%):** Guided attention to held tension + inquiry into what the tension protects. Scored on willingness to explore (not forced release).  
**Category D — Integration Sequences (15%):** Combined somatic-awareness + rational-examination tasks. Player must hold both simultaneously. Tests the DA split between body and mind.  
**Adaptive selection:** Items selected based on detected body-meaning dissociation patterns. High DA activation → more integration sequences. High DAll → more permission-to-feel items.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Scoring engine:** Rhythm analysis (tap/breath timing), response-consistency tracking, somatic-map evolution scoring. No biometric hardware required — all input via touch/tap timing and self-report.  
**Response format:** Timed taps (rhythm), touch-location on body-map (somatic mapping), brief free-text (tension exploration — LLM-scored).  
**State persistence:** Somatic map history, rhythm-consistency scores, tension-pattern evolution, shadow-activation profile, breath-baseline calibration.  
**Cross-modality hooks:** Exports embodiment score to language-reflective (is articulated meaning also embodied?). Imports logical-consistency from deterministic (does body-awareness match rational conclusions?).  
**Performance:** Rhythm scoring real-time (<50ms latency). Body-map rendering 60fps. LLM scoring for free-text async (3s).
