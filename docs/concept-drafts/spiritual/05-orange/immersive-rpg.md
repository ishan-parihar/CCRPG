# Spiritual / Orange — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological meaning-making — whether rational spiritual examination appears spontaneously in free-play.  **Why this axis for this module:** The ultimate test of Orange spiritual capacity is whether examined meaning-making emerges naturally in unstructured situations, not just when prompted.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Seeker's World  
**Core loop:** Player navigates an open world populated with meaning-laden environments, NPCs holding diverse worldviews, and narrative situations that invite (but never force) rational spiritual examination. System observes whether and how the player engages.  
**Feel:** A contemplative open-world RPG — rich, atmospheric, philosophically alive. Meaning is everywhere but never imposed. The world asks questions by existing.  
**Session length:** 5–15 minutes. Infinite checkpoint via persistent world-state.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Environmental and narrative catalysts that create opportunities for spontaneous meaning-examination. Temples, libraries, philosophical NPCs, moments of beauty, encounters with death, achievement and its aftermath.  
**Shadow activation:** DA surfaces as avoiding all meaning-laden environments or engaging only to debunk. DAll surfaces as gravitating exclusively toward authority-figures and tradition-spaces without questioning. GA surfaces as collecting spiritual experiences without integrating any. GAll surfaces as engaging only with environments that confirm existing conclusions.  
**Integration path:** Catalyst (world presents meaning-opportunity) → experience (player's spontaneous response reveals their relationship to meaning) → integration (player's behavior evolves toward genuine examined engagement over sessions).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic 1 — World as Mirror:** Environment contains meaning-rich zones: temples of different traditions, philosophical academies, nature-contemplation spaces, achievement-halls, community gatherings. System tracks which zones the player gravitates toward, avoids, or engages with.  
**Mechanic 2 — NPC Worldview Encounters:** NPCs hold coherent meaning-frameworks (rationalist, mystic, traditionalist, pluralist, nihilist). Player can engage in dialogue, observe, or ignore. Engagement quality is tracked but never forced.  
**Mechanic 3 — Narrative Meaning-Moments:** Story events that naturally raise meaning-questions: loss, success, beauty, suffering, connection. System observes whether player pauses, reflects, or rushes past. No explicit prompts — all observation is behavioral.  
**Difficulty staircase:** World complexity increases over sessions. Early: clear meaning-zones with obvious invitations. Later: subtle meaning-opportunities embedded in apparently mundane situations. Advanced: meaning-crises that arise from the player's own narrative choices.  
**Drive probing:** Agency = self-directed exploration of meaning-spaces. Communion = spontaneous engagement with NPCs' worldviews. Eros = seeking out increasingly challenging meaning-content. Agape = returning to embody insights in world-action.

## 4. Item Pool

**Category A — Environmental Catalysts (30%):** Meaning-rich spaces the player can discover. Scored on engagement depth (time spent, interaction quality, return visits) not mere visitation.  
**Category B — NPC Encounters (30%):** Philosophical characters with coherent worldviews. Scored on dialogue quality, willingness to engage across difference, and genuine curiosity vs. performance.  
**Category C — Narrative Moments (25%):** Story events that raise meaning-questions organically. Scored on behavioral response — does player pause, reflect, discuss with NPCs, or rush past?  
**Category D — Spontaneous Integration (15%):** Moments where the player's behavior demonstrates examined meaning without any prompt — choosing based on articulated values, questioning their own assumptions unprompted, seeking out challenge.  
**Adaptive selection:** World-state adapts to player's engagement patterns. Avoidant players encounter more gentle invitations. Over-engaged players encounter challenges to their engagement pattern.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Scoring engine:** Behavioral observation system — zone-tracking, dialogue-engagement metrics, pause-detection, choice-pattern analysis. No explicit scoring visible to player. All assessment is ecological.  
**Response format:** Free navigation, dialogue choices, action selections. No forced responses — all data from voluntary behavior.  
**State persistence:** Full behavioral history, zone-engagement maps, NPC-relationship states, narrative-state, shadow-activation profile derived from behavioral patterns.  
**Cross-modality hooks:** Imports all other modality scores to validate ecological observations. Exports ecological-capacity score as the highest-confidence indicator of genuine Orange spiritual development.  
**Performance:** World rendering 60fps. NPC dialogue LLM-generated (<2s). Behavioral scoring continuous and invisible. World-state persistence across sessions.
