# Spiritual / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes spiritual intelligence through SPONTANEOUS SACRED ENGAGEMENT in an open world — not structured spiritual tasks but free numinous behaviour. How does the player relate to the sacred when nobody's asking "do you feel the spirits?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe spiritual capacity through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world contains shrines, spirit-presences, sacred places, and numinous content. The player's spontaneous sacred behaviour reveals genuine spiritual intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Spirit-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world where sacred content is woven throughout. Shrines glow faintly. Spirit-presences drift. Sacred places hum. The player's spontaneous engagement with numinous content — how they navigate a spirit-filled world without being prompted — reveals genuine spiritual intelligence.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Sacred-Noticing → Sacred-Relating → Sacred-Sharing → Sacred-Growing → The Living Spirit-Walker

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world contains sacred content. Nobody says "feel the spirits" — but shrines are there, spirit-presences drift past, sacred places hum, offerings can be made. The player's NATURAL sacred engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL spiritual style surfaces. Do they stop at every shrine? Ignore all sacred content? Exploit spirits for power? Approach then retreat? Their ecological sacred behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage sacred content more healthily? Relate to the numinous more genuinely?

**Integration path:** The world rewards spiritual intelligence NATURALLY. Players who sense the sacred access spirit-gated content. Players who relate to spirits gain spirit-companions. Players who participate in ritual unlock sacred territories. The reward is NUMINOUS BELONGING (being at home in a sacred world).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates spiritual intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — sensing, relating to, participating in, and being oriented by the sacred without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world saturated with sacred content. Shrines at crossroads. Spirit-presences drifting. Sacred groves humming. Offering-places waiting. Every zone has numinous content — opportunities to sense, relate, participate, and be oriented by the sacred.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a spirit-filled world
- **Spontaneous sacred engagement:** Noticing and responding to numinous content
- **Spirit-gated content:** Paths/items requiring sacred relationship
- **Spirit-companion encounters:** Meeting and relating to spirit-presences
- **Ritual opportunities:** Places where offerings/rituals can be performed

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: sacred engagement → spirit-gated access, spirit-companions, sacred territories
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, stuck paths, flat world
- No explicit "spiritual score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Sacred content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Spirit-gate sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Spirit-companion depth scales with demonstrated relationship quality
- Sacred territory access scales with demonstrated participation
- Novel sacred content appears as capacity grows

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Sacred-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice sacred content? |
| Sacred-Relating | 5-15 | Do they relate to spirits/shrines? |
| Sacred-Sharing | 15-30 | Can they share sacred with companions? |
| Sacred-Growing | 30-50 | Do they engage deeper sacred content? |
| The Living Spirit-Walker | 50+ | Full ecological spiritual intelligence |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Shrines:** Sacred places for engagement/offering
- **Spirit-presences:** Beings to relate to
- **Sacred groves:** Territories requiring spiritual capacity
- **Ritual opportunities:** Places for sacred action
- **Spirit-gates:** Paths requiring sacred relationship

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ shrines, 15+ spirit-presences, 15+ sacred groves, 10+ ritual opportunities, 10+ spirit-gates

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Over-engages all → dark-addiction; ignores all → dark-allergy
- Strategic-only engagement → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage sacred content); hold (sustain sacred contact); gesture (ritual actions/offerings)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, relationship quality)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of sacred patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Spirit-filled environment (sacred content throughout)
- Spirit-companion system (beings that respond to relationship quality)
- Spirit-gating system (paths requiring sacred capacity)
- Ritual system (offering/invocation mechanics)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, spirit-companion dynamics
- Detects spontaneous sacred engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates spiritual intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive sacred content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; sacred engagement history; navigation patterns; spirit-relationship records; ritual participation quality; growth engagement; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
