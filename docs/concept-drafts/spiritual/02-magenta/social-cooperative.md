# Spiritual / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes spiritual intelligence through SHARED SACRED EXPERIENCE — can the player participate in the numinous WITH others? At Magenta, this is: "The spirits are here. You're not alone. Others feel them too. Can you share the sacred? Can you be in the numinous together?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Magenta:** Before institutional religion (Amber+), shared sacred experience is COMMUNAL RITUAL — dancing together, chanting together, feeling the spirits together. This modality tests whether the player can share numinous experience in relationship — the social dimension of spiritual intelligence.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred Circle"
- **Core mechanic:** The player participates in shared sacred experience with companions. Not solo numinous sensing but COMMUNAL sacred participation. "The spirits are here. Others feel them too. Can you feel the sacred TOGETHER? Can you share the glow? Can you be in the circle?"
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Sharing → The Sacred Together → The Different Sacred → The Changing Circle → The Living Community

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "You are not alone with the sacred. Others are here. They feel it too. Can you share this? Can you be in the numinous together — not losing your own sacred-sense, not ignoring theirs?" The contact boundary is: "Can you share sacred experience?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player share the sacred? The Spirit-Addict DOMINATES the sacred space ("I feel it MOST! The spirits speak to ME!"). The Spirit-Denier cannot participate ("I don't feel anything. What are they doing?"). The Premature Priest leads for power ("I'll lead the ritual. Follow me"). The Sacred-Refuser approaches the circle then withdraws ("I was going to join but… I'll watch").
- *Emergent:* Can they share sacred experience genuinely — neither dominating nor absent nor controlling nor withdrawing?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE SHARED SACRED EXPERIENCE — participating in communal numinosity while maintaining own sacred-sense. Not dominating, not absent, not controlling, not withdrawing.

**Successful integration:** The player can share sacred experience with others — the relational foundation of spiritual intelligence.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred Circle: a communal sacred space. Player and companions participate in shared numinous experience. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage ritual circle — figures gathered around a sacred center, each with their own glow, the shared glow between them growing as they participate together.

### Interaction
- **The First Sharing (1-5):** Join the circle. "Be with them. Feel the sacred together." Basic presence.
- **The Sacred Together (5-15):** Contribute to shared sacred. "Add your glow to theirs." Active participation.
- **The Different Sacred (15-30):** Companions feel sacred DIFFERENTLY. "They feel it their way. You feel it yours. Both real."
- **The Changing Circle (30-50):** Circle changes (new members, challenges). Maintain participation.
- **The Living Community (50+):** Full communal sacred: equal + contributing + receiving + adapting + spontaneous.

### Feedback
- Equal participation → "Beautiful. The circle glows together. Your light and theirs. Shared. Equal."
- Dominating → "Quieter. Not louder than them. Equal. Your glow beside theirs. Not over."
- Absent → "Step closer. You don't have to feel everything. Just be near. In the circle. Present."
- Controlling → "Not leading. Just being. With them. Equal. No one needs to be in charge of the sacred."
- Withdrawing → "One step in. Just one moment. With them. You can always step back. But try. One moment."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Circle size: one companion → small group → large circle
- Difference: similar sacred-sense → different → conflicting
- Duration: brief shared moment → sustained communal participation
- Challenge: harmonious circle → disrupted circle → circle under threat
- Depth: surface sharing → deep communal sacred → vulnerable sacred sharing

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Sharing | 1-5 | Basic presence in sacred circle |
| The Sacred Together | 5-15 | Active contribution to shared sacred |
| The Different Sacred | 15-30 | Maintaining self with different others |
| The Changing Circle | 30-50 | Adapting to changing communal sacred |
| The Living Community | 50+ | Full communal sacred integration |

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Harmonious circles:** Easy shared sacred (similar companions)
- **Diverse circles:** Different sacred-senses (tolerance test)
- **Challenging circles:** Companions who challenge or disrupt
- **Growing circles:** New members joining (adaptation test)
- **Deep circles:** Vulnerable/intimate shared sacred

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ harmonious, 15+ diverse, 15+ challenging, 10+ growing, 10+ deep

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Dominates circle → dark-addiction; cannot participate → dark-allergy
- Controls/leads → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (join circle); gesture (contribute to shared sacred); hold (sustain participation); rhythm (synchronise with group)

### Timing requirements
- Participation duration tracked; contribution frequency noted; no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Companion system (various sacred-styles for circle members)
- Shared-sacred system (group numinous experience that responds to participation)
- Equality tracking (dominance detection, absence detection)
- Difficulty adaptation (circle size, difference, challenge)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic participation tracking. LLM for companion behaviour generation and nuanced equality assessment.

### State persistence
- Participation history; equality scores; contribution quality; reception capacity; adaptation metrics; dominance indicators; absence indicators; control indicators; withdrawal indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
