# Spiritual / Magenta — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis probes spiritual intelligence through OBJECTIVE NUMINOUS RECOGNITION — can the player detect the sacred? Not interpret it, not relate to it, not participate in it — just DETECT it. "Is this sacred or ordinary? Can you tell?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Magenta:** Before verbal theology (Amber+), spiritual capacity begins with raw DETECTION — the felt-sense that "something here is different, special, numinous." This modality tests the most basic spiritual capacity: can you sense the sacred at all?

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred Sense"
- **Core mechanic:** The player is presented with scenes/objects/sounds. Some carry numinous quality; some are ordinary. The player must DETECT which is sacred. Not explain why — just sense it. "Which one feels sacred? Which one feels ordinary? Can you tell the difference?"
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Glow → The Many Glows → The Hidden Sacred → The False Sacred → The Living Sense

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Here are two things. One is sacred. One is ordinary. Which is which? You don't need to explain — just feel. Which one glows?" The contact boundary is: "Can you sense the numinous?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player detect? The Spirit-Addict says EVERYTHING is sacred ("They all glow!"). The Spirit-Denier says NOTHING is sacred ("None of them glow"). The Premature Priest detects only POWERFUL ones ("That one — it's strong"). The Sacred-Refuser detects then doubts ("I thought that one but… no, I'm not sure").
- *Emergent:* Can they detect accurately — neither over-attributing nor blind?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE NUMINOUS DETECTION — sensing the sacred where it is, not sensing it everywhere or nowhere. Calibrated spiritual perception.

**Successful integration:** The player can reliably detect numinous quality — the perceptual foundation of spiritual intelligence.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred Sense: a numinous detection space. Pairs or groups of stimuli are presented. Some carry sacred quality (glow, warmth, depth); some are ordinary. The player detects which is which. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage spirit-sight — a dim space where sacred things glow faintly. The player develops "spirit-eyes."

### Interaction
- **The First Glow (1-5):** Binary detection. "Which one glows?" Two items, one sacred.
- **The Many Glows (5-15):** Multiple items; identify all sacred ones. Build detection range.
- **The Hidden Sacred (15-30):** Sacred quality is SUBTLE. Requires refined detection.
- **The False Sacred (30-50):** Some items APPEAR sacred but aren't (impressive but ordinary). Discrimination.
- **The Living Sense (50+):** Full detection: subtle + discriminating + consistent + spontaneous.

### Feedback
- Accurate detection → "Yes! You saw it. Your spirit-eyes are open. That one glows."
- Everything sacred → "Not all of them. Look again. Which one REALLY glows? Just one. The real one."
- Nothing sacred → "Look closer. Softer. Not with your eyes — with your feeling. One of them is different."
- Power-only → "Not the big one. The quiet one. Sacred isn't always loud. Feel the gentle glow."
- Detect-then-doubt → "Your first feeling was right! Trust it. You saw it. Don't take it back."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Contrast: high (obvious sacred vs. obvious ordinary) → low (subtle differences)
- Number: 2 items → 4 → 8 → field of items
- Distractors: none → impressive-but-ordinary → sacred-mimics
- Subtlety: strong glow → faint glow → barely perceptible
- Speed: unlimited time → gentle time-awareness (not pressure)

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Glow | 1-5 | Binary detection (obvious sacred vs. ordinary) |
| The Many Glows | 5-15 | Multiple sacred items; range of numinous quality |
| The Hidden Sacred | 15-30 | Subtle sacred detection |
| The False Sacred | 30-50 | Discrimination (impressive ≠ sacred) |
| The Living Sense | 50+ | Full integrated numinous perception |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Visual stimuli:** Objects, scenes, creatures with/without numinous quality
- **Auditory stimuli:** Sounds, tones, melodies with/without sacred quality
- **Tactile stimuli:** Textures, temperatures, pressures (haptic) with/without numinous quality
- **Composite stimuli:** Multi-sensory items requiring integrated detection
- **Distractors:** Impressive-but-ordinary items; sacred-mimics

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ visual, 20+ auditory, 15+ tactile, 15+ composite, 20+ distractors

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All marked sacred → dark-addiction; none marked → dark-allergy
- Only impressive marked → golden-addiction; detect-then-retract → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (identify sacred item); swipe (sort sacred from ordinary); hold (confirm detection)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked (hesitation = uncertainty); no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Stimulus generation system (sacred/ordinary items with controlled numinous quality)
- Difficulty adaptation (contrast, number, subtlety)
- Distractor system (impressive-but-ordinary items)
- Detection tracking (accuracy, consistency, sensitivity)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic. LLM for generating novel sacred/ordinary stimuli and assessing edge-case detections.

### State persistence
- Detection accuracy history; sensitivity scores; discrimination quality; consistency metrics; spontaneity indicators; inflation indicators; blindness indicators; power-bias indicators; doubt indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
