# Spiritual / Magenta — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis probes spiritual intelligence through OBJECTIVE NUMINOUS RECOGNITION — can the player detect the sacred? Not interpret it, not relate to it, not participate in it — just DETECT it. "Is this sacred or ordinary? Can you tell?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Magenta:** Before verbal theology (Amber+), spiritual capacity begins with raw DETECTION — the felt-sense that "something here is different, special, numinous." This modality tests the most basic spiritual capacity: can you sense the sacred at all?

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred Sense"
- **Core mechanic:** The player is presented with scenes/objects/sounds. Some carry numinous quality; some are ordinary. The player must DETECT which is sacred. Not explain why — just sense it. "Which one feels sacred? Which one feels ordinary? Can you tell the difference?"
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Glow → The Many Glows → The Hidden Sacred → The False Sacred → The Living Sense

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Here are two things. One is sacred. One is ordinary. Which is which? You don't need to explain — just feel. Which one glows?" The contact boundary is: "Can you sense the numinous?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player detect? The Spirit-Addict says EVERYTHING is sacred ("They all glow!"). The Spirit-Denier says NOTHING is sacred ("None of them glow"). The Premature Priest detects only POWERFUL ones ("That one — it's strong"). The Sacred-Refuser detects then doubts ("I thought that one but… no, I'm not sure").
- *Emergent:* Can they detect accurately — neither over-attributing nor blind?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE NUMINOUS DETECTION — sensing the sacred where it is, not sensing it everywhere or nowhere. Calibrated spiritual perception.

**Successful integration:** The player can reliably detect numinous quality — the perceptual foundation of spiritual intelligence.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Independent numinous detection (no hints, no guidance).
- Healthy: Detects sacred independently with confidence
- Pathological: Detects everything (inflation); OR: detects nothing (blindness)
- **Detection:** Whether numinous detection is self-generated and accurate

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Detection under ambiguity (stimuli that are borderline sacred/ordinary).
- Healthy: Offers tentative detection under ambiguity; "I think this one, but I'm not sure"
- Pathological: Cannot detect under ambiguity; freezes or guesses randomly
- **Detection:** Quality of detection under perceptual ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Detection when companion reports differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own detection while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's detection; loses own numinous sense
- **Detection:** Whether own sacred-sense survives social disagreement

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared detection (comparing sacred-senses).
- Healthy: Shares own detection AND receives companion's; mutual calibration
- Pathological: Cannot share sacred-sense; numinous detection is private/shameful
- **Detection:** Quality of shared numinous calibration

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Detection consistency over time.
- Healthy: Same stimuli detected as sacred consistently across sessions
- Pathological: Detection is random; no stable sacred-sense
- **Detection:** Numinous detection consistency

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Detecting SUBTLER sacred qualities (growth in sensitivity).
- Healthy: Can detect increasingly subtle numinous qualities
- Pathological: Cannot grow in sensitivity; OR: sensitivity grows without discrimination
- **Detection:** Growth in detection sensitivity with maintained accuracy

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining detection capacity over time.
- Healthy: Numinous detection remains reliable across sessions
- Pathological: Detection capacity degrades; sacred-sense fades
- **Detection:** Detection reliability across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether numinous detection appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Notices sacred quality naturally in non-detection tasks
- Pathological: Sacred-sense exists only during explicit detection tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous numinous recognition

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Spirit-Addict"
**How it surfaces:** Detects EVERYTHING as sacred. "They all glow! Everything is sacred!" Over-attribution. Cannot distinguish sacred from ordinary because the numinous sense is inflated to cover everything.
- **Detection signals:** All items marked sacred; "everything glows"; cannot identify ordinary; over-attribution; no discrimination; sacred = everything

### Dark-Allergy: "The Spirit-Denier"
**How it surfaces:** Detects NOTHING as sacred. "None of them glow. I don't see anything special." Numinous blindness. The sacred-sense is absent or repressed.
- **Detection signals:** No items marked sacred; "nothing glows"; cannot identify sacred; flat perception; "they're all the same"; no numinous response

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Priest"
**How it surfaces:** Detects only POWERFUL/IMPRESSIVE stimuli as sacred. "That big one — it's sacred. The strong one." Confuses sacred with powerful. Numinous detection is filtered through power-lens.
- **Detection signals:** Only large/impressive items marked sacred; subtle sacred missed; power = sacred; "the strong one"; ignores gentle/quiet sacred; sacred = impressive

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sacred-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** DETECTS then DOUBTS. "I thought that one glowed but… no, probably not." Approach-withdrawal in detection. The sacred-sense activates then is suppressed.
- **Detection signals:** Initial correct detection followed by retraction; "I thought so but…"; self-doubt; approach-withdrawal; detection then denial; "never mind"

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present CLEAR ordinary items: "This one doesn't glow. It's just a rock. Not everything is sacred. Can you see the difference?"
- Reward accurate ORDINARY detection (identifying non-sacred)
- Frame: "Some things are sacred. Some are ordinary. Both are real. Can you tell which is which?"
- Build: discriminating sacred-sense (not everything, not nothing — SOME things)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present STRONGLY numinous stimuli: "Look at this one. Really look. Does anything feel different? Even a little?"
- Start with most obvious sacred quality; build from there
- Frame: "Just notice. Not believe. Not explain. Just… does this one feel different? Even slightly?"
- Reward ANY detection; celebrate first "I think that one glows"

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Present SUBTLE sacred stimuli: "Not the big one. The quiet one. The gentle glow. Can you see it?"
- Reward detection of subtle/gentle sacred; block power-only detection
- Frame: "Sacred isn't always loud. Sometimes it whispers. The small one. The quiet one. Feel it?"
- Build: subtle sacred-sense (sacred ≠ powerful)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make detection LOW-STAKES: "Just point. Quick. Which one? Don't think. Just feel. Trust it."
- Reduce self-doubt; make detection playful and brief
- Frame: "Your first feeling was right. Trust it. You CAN sense this. Don't second-guess. Just point."
- Build: detection confidence (trusting own sacred-sense)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred Sense: a numinous detection space. Pairs or groups of stimuli are presented. Some carry sacred quality (glow, warmth, depth); some are ordinary. The player detects which is which. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage spirit-sight — a dim space where sacred things glow faintly. The player develops "spirit-eyes."

### Interaction
- **The First Glow (1-5):** Binary detection. "Which one glows?" Two items, one sacred.
- **The Many Glows (5-15):** Multiple items; identify all sacred ones. Build detection range.
- **The Hidden Sacred (15-30):** Sacred quality is SUBTLE. Requires refined detection.
- **The False Sacred (30-50):** Some items APPEAR sacred but aren't (impressive but ordinary). Discrimination.
- **The Living Sense (50+):** Full detection: subtle + discriminating + consistent + spontaneous.

### Feedback
- Accurate detection → "Yes! You saw it. Your spirit-eyes are open. That one glows."
- Everything sacred → "Not all of them. Look again. Which one REALLY glows? Just one. The real one."
- Nothing sacred → "Look closer. Softer. Not with your eyes — with your feeling. One of them is different."
- Power-only → "Not the big one. The quiet one. Sacred isn't always loud. Feel the gentle glow."
- Detect-then-doubt → "Your first feeling was right! Trust it. You saw it. Don't take it back."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Contrast: high (obvious sacred vs. obvious ordinary) → low (subtle differences)
- Number: 2 items → 4 → 8 → field of items
- Distractors: none → impressive-but-ordinary → sacred-mimics
- Subtlety: strong glow → faint glow → barely perceptible
- Speed: unlimited time → gentle time-awareness (not pressure)

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Glow | 1-5 | Binary detection (obvious sacred vs. ordinary) |
| The Many Glows | 5-15 | Multiple sacred items; range of numinous quality |
| The Hidden Sacred | 15-30 | Subtle sacred detection |
| The False Sacred | 30-50 | Discrimination (impressive ≠ sacred) |
| The Living Sense | 50+ | Full integrated numinous perception |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Visual stimuli:** Objects, scenes, creatures with/without numinous quality
- **Auditory stimuli:** Sounds, tones, melodies with/without sacred quality
- **Tactile stimuli:** Textures, temperatures, pressures (haptic) with/without numinous quality
- **Composite stimuli:** Multi-sensory items requiring integrated detection
- **Distractors:** Impressive-but-ordinary items; sacred-mimics

### Minimum pool size
- 30+ visual, 20+ auditory, 15+ tactile, 15+ composite, 20+ distractors

### Drive/shadow mapping
- All marked sacred → dark-addiction; none marked → dark-allergy
- Only impressive marked → golden-addiction; detect-then-retract → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Detection accuracy:** Correct identification of sacred vs. ordinary
- **Discrimination:** Can distinguish sacred from merely impressive
- **Sensitivity:** Can detect subtle numinous quality
- **Consistency:** Same items detected consistently across sessions
- **Spontaneity:** Detection appears in non-task contexts

### LLM rubric
- **Low:** Core detection scoring is algorithmic (correct/incorrect identification). LLM for subtle discrimination assessment and spontaneity detection.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (identify sacred item); swipe (sort sacred from ordinary); hold (confirm detection)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked (hesitation = uncertainty); no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Stimulus generation system (sacred/ordinary items with controlled numinous quality)
- Difficulty adaptation (contrast, number, subtlety)
- Distractor system (impressive-but-ordinary items)
- Detection tracking (accuracy, consistency, sensitivity)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic. LLM for generating novel sacred/ordinary stimuli and assessing edge-case detections.

### State persistence
- Detection accuracy history; sensitivity scores; discrimination quality; consistency metrics; spontaneity indicators; inflation indicators; blindness indicators; power-bias indicators; doubt indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
