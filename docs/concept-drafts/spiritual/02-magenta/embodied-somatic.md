# Spiritual / Magenta — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The embodied-somatic axis probes spiritual intelligence through FEELING THE SACRED IN THE BODY — where does the numinous live somatically? At Magenta, this is: "Where in your body do you feel the sacred? When the spirits are near, what does your body do? Where does the glow live in you?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Magenta:** Before conceptual theology (Amber+), the sacred is FELT IN THE BODY — goosebumps, warmth in the chest, tingling, expansion. This modality tests whether the player can locate numinous experience somatically — the body as sacred antenna.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred Body"
- **Core mechanic:** The player identifies WHERE in their body they feel the sacred. Not thinking about the sacred — FEELING it somatically. "When the spirits are near, where does your body feel it? Your chest? Your skin? Your belly? Show me where the sacred lives in you."
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; body-fatigue monitored)
- **Internal progression:** The First Sacred-Feeling → The Body-Shrine → The Many Sacred-Places → The Changing Sacred-Body → The Living Temple

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "The sacred is here. Can you feel it in your body? Not think it — FEEL it. Where? Your chest gets warm? Your skin tingles? Your belly opens? Show me where the sacred touches your body." The contact boundary is: "Can you feel the numinous somatically?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player's body respond to the sacred? The Spirit-Addict feels it EVERYWHERE ("My whole body is on fire! The spirits fill me completely!"). The Spirit-Denier feels NOTHING ("I don't feel anything different in my body"). The Premature Priest feels it only as ENERGY/POWER ("Strength in my arms! Power in my fists!"). The Sacred-Refuser begins feeling then shuts down ("I felt warmth but then it stopped").
- *Emergent:* Can they feel the sacred somatically with appropriate specificity — neither everywhere nor nowhere?

**Integration path:** Rewards SPECIFIC SOMATIC SACRED EXPERIENCE — feeling the numinous in particular body-areas with genuine felt-sense. Not everywhere, not nowhere, not only as power, not flickering.

**Successful integration:** The player can feel the sacred in their body — the somatic foundation of spiritual intelligence.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred Body: a somatic sacred-exploration space. Sacred content is presented; the player locates where they feel it in their body. A body-outline lights up where the player indicates sacred-feeling. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage body-temple — a glowing body-outline that illuminates where the sacred is felt. Golden/warm colours for sacred-body.

### Interaction
- **The First Sacred-Feeling (1-5):** "Where do you feel the sacred?" Tap one body-area during numinous stimulus.
- **The Body-Shrine (5-15):** Identify quality: warm/tingling/expanding/light at sacred-location.
- **The Many Sacred-Places (15-30):** Multiple sacred stimuli → multiple body-locations. Build sacred body-map.
- **The Changing Sacred-Body (30-50):** Sacred-feeling in different contexts (alone/together/scared/joyful).
- **The Living Temple (50+):** Full somatic sacred integration: locate + qualify + expand + sustain.

### Feedback
- Specific location → "There! The sacred lives there in you. Your body is a temple. That's your shrine."
- Everywhere → "Smaller. Not all of you. Your CENTER. Where is the heart of the glow? Just there."
- Nothing → "Breathe. Feel the glow come. Anything? Even tiny? Your chest? Your belly? … There?"
- Power-only → "Softer. Not strength. WARMTH. Where do you feel warm? Gentle? That's sacred too."
- Flickering → "You felt it! For a moment. That's real. Can you stay? Just one more breath with it?"

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Sacred intensity: strong numinous stimulus → subtle → barely perceptible
- Location precision: large area → specific spot
- Quality discrimination: binary (warm/cool) → multiple qualities
- Context variation: neutral → emotional → challenging
- Duration: brief → sustained somatic sacred contact

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Sacred-Feeling | 1-5 | Basic sacred body-location |
| The Body-Shrine | 5-15 | Quality of sacred body-feeling |
| The Many Sacred-Places | 15-30 | Multiple sacred body-locations |
| The Changing Sacred-Body | 30-50 | Sacred-feeling across contexts |
| The Living Temple | 50+ | Full somatic sacred integration |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Strong sacred stimuli:** Obviously numinous content (easy to feel in body)
- **Subtle sacred stimuli:** Gently numinous (requires sensitivity)
- **Context stimuli:** Sacred in different emotional contexts
- **Social stimuli:** Sacred during relationship (feeling sacred with others)
- **Growth stimuli:** New kinds of sacred (expanding somatic range)

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ strong, 20+ subtle, 15+ context, 10+ social, 10+ growth

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Everywhere → dark-addiction; nothing → dark-allergy
- Power-only → golden-addiction; flickering → golden-allergy
