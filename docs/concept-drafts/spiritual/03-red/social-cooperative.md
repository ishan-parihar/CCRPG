# Spiritual / Red — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes spiritual intelligence through RELATIONAL VALUE-DYNAMICS — how do values function when others are involved? At Red, the key questions are: "Does your value survive social pressure? Can you share the sacred? Does your transactional faith extend to others?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Red:** At Red, spirituality is primarily PRIVATE — "MY power-deity, MY sacred." But the social dimension reveals whether values are genuine or performed. Do they hold their value when others disagree? Can they engage in shared sacred action? The social context tests value-robustness.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred Circle"
- **Core mechanic:** The player engages spiritual intelligence in SOCIAL contexts — holding values when others challenge them, sharing sacred action with allies, engaging transactional faith in community, and building value-reputation through consistent behaviour.
- **Duration:** 4-7 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** Value Under Challenge → Shared Sacred → Value-Reputation → The Sacred Ally → The Circle's Faith

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Your ally values something different. They challenge your value. Do you hold? Do you listen? Can you share the sacred without losing yours?" The contact boundary is: "Is your value robust enough to survive social contact?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does social context affect value-holding? The Zealot attacks challengers. The Nihilist doesn't care. The Premature Mystic claims "all values are one." The Sacred-Refuser abandons value under social pressure.
- *Emergent:* Can they hold value AND engage others? Can they share sacred action without losing sovereignty?

**Integration path:** Rewards value-robustness in social context — holding values under challenge, sharing sacred action genuinely, and building value-reputation through consistency. Not isolation (holding by avoiding others) but ENGAGED value-holding.

**Successful integration:** The player holds their value in social contexts, engages shared sacred action, and builds value-reputation — all while remaining open to others' values without being threatened.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred Circle: a social spiritual space where values are shared, challenged, and held in community. The player engages with allies who have different values, participates in shared sacred action, and builds value-reputation. The aesthetic: Red-stage war-band around the fire — warriors declaring what they fight for, challenging each other, and finding shared cause.

### Interaction
- **Value Under Challenge (1-5):** Ally challenges player's value. Does the player hold? Simple social value-robustness.
- **Shared Sacred (5-15):** Joint value-coherent action with ally. Can they share the sacred?
- **Value-Reputation (15-30):** Past value-behaviour affects how allies respond. Consistency builds trust.
- **The Sacred Ally (30-50):** Deep shared sacred engagement — mutual value-support, joint sacred-boundary defence.
- **The Circle's Faith (50+):** Full social spiritual ecology — challenge, sharing, reputation, alliance, all integrated.

### Feedback
- Value held under challenge → "They questioned. You held. Your value is stronger for the test."
- Attacked challenger → "They questioned. You attacked. A value that needs violence to survive is fragile."
- Abandoned under pressure → "They questioned. You folded. Is it your value or theirs?"
- Shared sacred success → "You fought together for the same cause. The circle strengthens."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Social complexity: 1 ally → 2 allies → group with mixed values
- Challenge intensity: gentle → moderate → aggressive
- Shared sacred complexity: simple joint action → coordinated campaign
- Reputation stakes: low → moderate → high

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Value Under Challenge | 1-5 | Hold value when ally questions it |
| Shared Sacred | 5-15 | Joint value-coherent action |
| Value-Reputation | 15-30 | Consistency builds social trust |
| The Sacred Ally | 30-50 | Deep mutual value-support |
| The Circle's Faith | 50+ | Full social spiritual ecology |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Value-challenge scenarios:** Allies question player's value with varying intensity
- **Shared sacred tasks:** Joint value-coherent actions requiring coordination
- **Reputation events:** Past behaviour affecting present social dynamics
- **Value-coexistence scenarios:** Holding own value while acknowledging different values
- **Sacred alliance tasks:** Deep mutual value-support and joint boundary-defence

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ challenge, 20+ shared sacred, 15+ reputation, 15+ coexistence, 10+ alliance

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Attacks challengers → dark-addiction; zero social spiritual engagement → dark-allergy
- False synthesis → golden-addiction; abandons under pressure → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-select (responses to challenges); coordination input (shared sacred action); timing (response latency to challenges)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency to challenges tracked (diagnostic); no punishing time pressure
- Cross-session tracking for reputation

### NPC/AI requirements
- Ally NPCs with distinct values who challenge and share
- Challenge system with calibrated intensity
- Reputation system tracking value-consistency in social contexts
- Shared sacred action system requiring coordination

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Social scenario generation, ally dialogue, challenge construction. Core scoring algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Value-robustness scores; shared sacred quality; reputation history; coexistence capacity; alliance depth; aggression/abandonment patterns; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
