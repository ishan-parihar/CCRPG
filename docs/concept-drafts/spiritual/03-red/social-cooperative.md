# Spiritual / Red — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes spiritual intelligence through RELATIONAL VALUE-DYNAMICS — how do values function when others are involved? At Red, the key questions are: "Does your value survive social pressure? Can you share the sacred? Does your transactional faith extend to others?"
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Red:** At Red, spirituality is primarily PRIVATE — "MY power-deity, MY sacred." But the social dimension reveals whether values are genuine or performed. Do they hold their value when others disagree? Can they engage in shared sacred action? The social context tests value-robustness.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred Circle"
- **Core mechanic:** The player engages spiritual intelligence in SOCIAL contexts — holding values when others challenge them, sharing sacred action with allies, engaging transactional faith in community, and building value-reputation through consistent behaviour.
- **Duration:** 4-7 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** Value Under Challenge → Shared Sacred → Value-Reputation → The Sacred Ally → The Circle's Faith

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Your ally values something different. They challenge your value. Do you hold? Do you listen? Can you share the sacred without losing yours?" The contact boundary is: "Is your value robust enough to survive social contact?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does social context affect value-holding? The Zealot attacks challengers. The Nihilist doesn't care. The Premature Mystic claims "all values are one." The Sacred-Refuser abandons value under social pressure.
- *Emergent:* Can they hold value AND engage others? Can they share sacred action without losing sovereignty?

**Integration path:** Rewards value-robustness in social context — holding values under challenge, sharing sacred action genuinely, and building value-reputation through consistency. Not isolation (holding by avoiding others) but ENGAGED value-holding.

**Successful integration:** The player holds their value in social contexts, engages shared sacred action, and builds value-reputation — all while remaining open to others' values without being threatened.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Others challenge the player's stated value.
- Healthy: Holds value under challenge; explains without attacking
- Pathological: Attacks challengers; "How DARE you question my sacred"
- **Detection:** Response to value-challenge (defence vs. aggression)

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Initiating shared sacred action (inviting ally into value-coherent activity).
- Healthy: Invites others into sacred action; leads value-coherent group activity
- Pathological: Cannot initiate shared sacred; values are private only
- **Detection:** Spontaneous sacred-sharing initiation rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Ally's value conflicts with player's value.
- Healthy: Acknowledges ally's value; maintains own; "Both are real"
- Pathological: Abandons own value to match ally's; "Your value is better"
- **Detection:** Whether own value survives contact with different values

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared sacred action with ally who holds same value.
- Healthy: Strengthened by shared value; deeper engagement together
- Pathological: Cannot engage shared sacred; values must be private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared sacred engagement

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Simple social value-holding (state value to one ally).
- Healthy: Engages fully; shares value clearly
- Pathological: Dismisses social value-sharing; "They don't need to know"
- **Detection:** Engagement quality on simple social spiritual tasks

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel social spiritual situations (unfamiliar ally, unfamiliar value-context).
- Healthy: Engages with curiosity; holds value while exploring
- Pathological: Retreats to private spirituality in novel social contexts
- **Detection:** Value-holding quality in novel social spiritual contexts

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining value-reputation over time (consistent social spiritual behaviour).
- Healthy: Value-reputation builds naturally through consistent behaviour
- Pathological: Value-reputation degrades; inconsistent social spiritual behaviour
- **Detection:** Value-reputation consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether social spiritual engagement appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Shares values, engages sacred action with others naturally
- Pathological: Social spiritual engagement exists only in structured tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous social spiritual behaviour in free-play

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Zealot"
**How it surfaces:** Social spiritual engagement is AGGRESSIVE. Challenges others' values. Demands conversion. Cannot tolerate different values in proximity. Social context triggers crusade-mode.
- **Detection signals:** Attacks others' values; demands agreement; cannot coexist with different values; social spiritual engagement = proselytising; "My value is the ONLY value"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Nihilist"
**How it surfaces:** Social spiritual engagement is ABSENT. Cannot share values because claims to have none. Others' values are dismissed. "Why are we talking about this?"
- **Detection signals:** Zero social spiritual engagement; dismisses others' value-sharing; cannot participate in shared sacred action; "This is meaningless"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Mystic"
**How it surfaces:** Claims "all values are one" in social contexts — uses false synthesis to avoid genuine engagement with value-difference. Cannot hold OWN value while acknowledging OTHER's value.
- **Detection signals:** "All paths are one" in response to value-difference; cannot hold own value AND acknowledge other's; false synthesis replacing genuine engagement; sounds pluralistic but actually avoids commitment

### Golden-Allergy: "The Sacred-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Abandons value under social pressure. Others' challenge causes immediate value-abandonment. "Maybe you're right. Maybe my value doesn't matter." Social context triggers commitment-collapse.
- **Detection signals:** Value-abandonment under social pressure; "You're probably right" responses; cannot hold value when challenged; social context = commitment-collapse trigger

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present social contexts where COEXISTENCE produces more power than conversion
- Frame: "Your ally values something different. Together you're stronger than alone. Can you fight beside someone with a different sacred?"
- Make value-coexistence produce tangible advantage

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present social contexts where SHARING a value produces group power
- Frame: "Your ally asked what you fight for. Tell them. One word. The war-band that shares a cause fights harder."
- Reward ANY social value-sharing; celebrate the first declaration to another

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- FORCE value-differentiation in social contexts ("Your value is X. Their value is Y. They're DIFFERENT. Hold yours.")
- Frame: "They're not the same. Yours is yours. Theirs is theirs. Both real. Both different. Hold yours."
- Reward holding own value while acknowledging difference

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Present social challenges with LOW STAKES (ally challenges gently; no real consequence)
- Frame: "They questioned your value. Nothing bad happened. Your value survived. It's stronger now."
- Build social value-robustness through low-stakes challenges

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred Circle: a social spiritual space where values are shared, challenged, and held in community. The player engages with allies who have different values, participates in shared sacred action, and builds value-reputation. The aesthetic: Red-stage war-band around the fire — warriors declaring what they fight for, challenging each other, and finding shared cause.

### Interaction
- **Value Under Challenge (1-5):** Ally challenges player's value. Does the player hold? Simple social value-robustness.
- **Shared Sacred (5-15):** Joint value-coherent action with ally. Can they share the sacred?
- **Value-Reputation (15-30):** Past value-behaviour affects how allies respond. Consistency builds trust.
- **The Sacred Ally (30-50):** Deep shared sacred engagement — mutual value-support, joint sacred-boundary defence.
- **The Circle's Faith (50+):** Full social spiritual ecology — challenge, sharing, reputation, alliance, all integrated.

### Feedback
- Value held under challenge → "They questioned. You held. Your value is stronger for the test."
- Attacked challenger → "They questioned. You attacked. A value that needs violence to survive is fragile."
- Abandoned under pressure → "They questioned. You folded. Is it your value or theirs?"
- Shared sacred success → "You fought together for the same cause. The circle strengthens."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Social complexity: 1 ally → 2 allies → group with mixed values
- Challenge intensity: gentle → moderate → aggressive
- Shared sacred complexity: simple joint action → coordinated campaign
- Reputation stakes: low → moderate → high

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Value Under Challenge | 1-5 | Hold value when ally questions it |
| Shared Sacred | 5-15 | Joint value-coherent action |
| Value-Reputation | 15-30 | Consistency builds social trust |
| The Sacred Ally | 30-50 | Deep mutual value-support |
| The Circle's Faith | 50+ | Full social spiritual ecology |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Value-challenge scenarios:** Allies question player's value with varying intensity
- **Shared sacred tasks:** Joint value-coherent actions requiring coordination
- **Reputation events:** Past behaviour affecting present social dynamics
- **Value-coexistence scenarios:** Holding own value while acknowledging different values
- **Sacred alliance tasks:** Deep mutual value-support and joint boundary-defence

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ challenge, 20+ shared sacred, 15+ reputation, 15+ coexistence, 10+ alliance

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Attacks challengers → dark-addiction; zero social spiritual engagement → dark-allergy
- False synthesis → golden-addiction; abandons under pressure → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Value-robustness:** Does value survive social challenge without aggression?
- **Shared sacred quality:** Quality of joint value-coherent action
- **Value-reputation coherence:** Consistency of social spiritual behaviour over time
- **Coexistence capacity:** Can they hold own value while acknowledging others'?
- **Sacred alliance depth:** Quality of mutual value-support relationships

### LLM rubric
- **Medium:** Social scenario generation, ally dialogue, challenge narration. Core scoring (value-robustness, consistency) algorithmic.
