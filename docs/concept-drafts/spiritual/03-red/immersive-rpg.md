# Spiritual / Red — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes spiritual intelligence through SPONTANEOUS VALUE-COHERENT BEHAVIOUR in an open game world — not structured value-tests but free spiritual engagement in an environment where values can be lived, violated, or ignored. How does the player's spiritual life LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Red:** The other 6 modalities probe spiritual intelligence through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Red, the player navigates a world where values can guide or be ignored: do they act on their values spontaneously? Do they protect the sacred without being asked? Do they engage transactional faith naturally? Their SPONTANEOUS spiritual behaviour reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open-world Red-stage environment where spiritual intelligence is assessed through the QUALITY of spontaneous value-coherent behaviour — acting on values without prompting, protecting the sacred naturally, engaging transactional faith spontaneously, and building value-reputation through consistent free-play behaviour.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Spontaneous Value-Action → Sacred Navigation → Transactional Ecology → Value-Reputation → The Living Sacred

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** The world has sacred spaces, profane spaces, value-testing situations, and transactional faith opportunities. Nobody asks "what do you value?" — the player simply ACTS, and the world reveals whether their actions are value-coherent.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL spiritual style surfaces. Do they act on values spontaneously? Weaponise them? Ignore them? Perform them? Their ecological behaviour reveals genuine spiritual structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they engage more of the spiritual world? Act on values more consistently? Navigate sacred terrain more skillfully?

**Integration path:** The world rewards spiritual intelligence NATURALLY. Players who act on values gain power (transactional faith works). Players who protect the sacred gain allies. Players who build value-reputation gain trust. The reward is POWER (Red-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates spiritual intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — acting on values, protecting the sacred, engaging transactional faith, and building value-reputation without being prompted.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
An open Red-stage world where the sacred is REAL and POWERFUL. Sacred spaces grant buffs. Transactional faith produces resources. Value-coherent behaviour builds reputation. The world responds to the player's spiritual pattern. The aesthetic: Red-stage territory with temples, altars, power-shrines, sacred boundaries — the spiritual ecology of power.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a world where spiritual content is present and responsive
- **Spontaneous value-action:** Acting on values without being asked
- **Natural transactional faith:** Serving the sacred and receiving power
- **Sacred-boundary navigation:** Respecting and defending sacred spaces
- **Value-reputation building:** Consistent spiritual behaviour creating identity

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: spiritual intelligence → power, allies, access, reputation
- Natural consequences: spiritual blindness → locked paths, missed resources, isolation
- No explicit "spiritual score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Sacred content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Transactional faith complexity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Value-challenge intensity scales with demonstrated robustness
- Spiritual ecology complexity scales with demonstrated navigation

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Spontaneous Value-Action | 1-5 | Basic value-coherent behaviour in free-play |
| Sacred Navigation | 5-15 | Engaging sacred spaces; transactional faith |
| Transactional Ecology | 15-30 | Sustained faith-engagement; reciprocal sacred |
| Value-Reputation | 30-50 | Consistent behaviour building spiritual identity |
| The Living Sacred | 50+ | Full spiritual ecology; all capacities spontaneous |

---

## 4. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Sacred space configurations:** Altars, shrines, boundaries with varied spiritual dynamics
- **Transactional faith opportunities:** Serve-and-receive exchanges with the sacred
- **Value-challenge encounters:** NPCs and situations that test value-robustness
- **Reputation events:** Past spiritual behaviour affecting present options
- **Spiritual ecology dynamics:** Sacred spaces that respond to player's spiritual pattern

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ sacred spaces, 15+ faith opportunities, 15+ challenge encounters, 15+ reputation events, 10+ ecology dynamics

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Value-weaponisation → dark-addiction; zero spiritual engagement → dark-allergy
- Spiritual tourism → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap (interact, serve, receive, protect); hold (engage sacred space); swipe (navigate)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (spiritual engagement patterns, value-coherence rates, faith quality)
- No millisecond precision; minutes-level measurement of spiritual patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Sacred spaces that respond to player's spiritual engagement
- Transactional faith system (serve → receive; consistent → more powerful)
- NPCs with spiritual memory (remember player's value-behaviour)
- Reputation system affecting NPC spiritual responses

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, sacred space dynamics, NPC spiritual memory
- Detects spontaneous spiritual engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates spiritual intelligence quality without explicit tasks

### State persistence
- Full world state; sacred space engagement history; transactional faith records; value-coherence patterns; reputation scores; spiritual engagement rates; approach-withdrawal patterns; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
