# Spiritual / Red — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The embodied-somatic axis probes spiritual intelligence THROUGH THE BODY — does the body participate in value-holding? At Red, the sacred is felt PHYSICALLY: the surge of power when invoking the power-deity, the tension when violating a sacred boundary, the relaxation when acting in alignment with values.
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Red:** Values that live only in the mind are fragile. Values that live in the BODY are robust. At Red, where the body IS the self, spiritual intelligence has a somatic dimension: does the body RESPOND to value-coherence and value-violation? Does it signal when the sacred is honoured or betrayed?

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred-Body"
- **Core mechanic:** The player's physical responses (force, timing, rhythm, tension) during value-charged actions reveal whether spiritual intelligence has somatic grounding. Does the body respond differently when acting in alignment vs. violation of stated values?
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The Surge → The Tension → The Alignment-Feel → The Sacred Rhythm → The Value-Body

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "Your body knows what matters. When you act on your value, something shifts. When you betray it, something tightens. Can you feel it?" The contact boundary is: "Does your body participate in your spiritual life?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* Does the body respond to value-content at all? Does it respond only with aggression (Zealot)? Not at all (Nihilist)? With performed reverence (Premature Mystic)? With approach-then-withdrawal (Sacred-Refuser)?
- *Emergent:* Can the body signal more subtle value-alignment? Can somatic markers guide value-coherent action?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE somatic spiritual engagement — physical responses that correlate with value-alignment. Not performed reverence but REAL body-response to the sacred.

**Successful integration:** The player's physical responses correlate with value-content — energised during value-coherent action, tense during value-violation, relaxed during sacred-boundary defence.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred-Body: a physical space where value-charged actions are measured somatically. The player performs actions in sacred and profane contexts; the game measures whether the body DIFFERENTIATES. The aesthetic: Red-stage temple-ground where the warrior's body responds to the sacred — the surge of power at the altar, the tension at the boundary.

### Interaction
- **The Surge (1-5):** Does the body energise during value-coherent action? Measure force/speed on value-aligned vs. neutral tasks.
- **The Tension (5-15):** Does the body tense during value-violation? Measure tension/hesitation on value-violating tasks.
- **The Alignment-Feel (15-30):** Does the body signal BEFORE the mind? Somatic value-markers preceding conscious choice.
- **The Sacred Rhythm (30-50):** Can the body sustain value-coherent action rhythmically? Sustained sacred-physical engagement.
- **The Value-Body (50+):** Full somatic spiritual integration — surge, tension, alignment-feel, rhythm, all genuine.

### Feedback
- Genuine somatic engagement → "Your body knows the sacred. The surge was real. Trust it."
- Aggressive-only → "Your body only knows the sacred as a weapon. Can it also hold gently?"
- No response → "Your body didn't respond. The sacred spoke. Listen with your body."
- Performed → "You looked reverent. But your body didn't feel it. Slower. Simpler. Feel it."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Value-weight: trivial → moderate → significant → sacred
- Action complexity: single tap → sequence → sustained hold → rhythm
- Somatic demand: gross motor → fine motor → timing-sensitive
- Differentiation demand: binary (sacred/profane) → gradient (degrees of value-weight)

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The Surge | 1-5 | Binary: does the body respond to value-content? |
| The Tension | 5-15 | Does body signal value-violation? |
| The Alignment-Feel | 15-30 | Somatic markers preceding conscious choice |
| The Sacred Rhythm | 30-50 | Sustained somatic spiritual engagement |
| The Value-Body | 50+ | Full somatic spiritual integration |

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Value-aligned physical actions:** Actions in service of stated value
- **Value-violating physical actions:** Actions that betray stated value
- **Sacred-boundary physical tasks:** Defending/protecting the sacred physically
- **Transactional-faith physical tasks:** Serving and receiving physically
- **Sustained sacred-physical tasks:** Holding value-coherent action over time

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ aligned, 20+ violating, 15+ boundary, 15+ transactional, 10+ sustained

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Aggression-only → dark-addiction; no somatic response → dark-allergy
- Performed without genuine engagement → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Force-sensitive tap/hold; timing-sensitive; rhythm; sustained hold duration

### Timing requirements
- Pre-action latency at ≥60Hz (hesitation measurement); force measurement continuous; rhythm at 30Hz+

### NPC/AI requirements
- Minimal. Sacred-physical scenarios are environmental.
- Altar/boundary objects that respond to player's physical engagement.

### LLM requirements
- **Low-Medium:** Pattern interpretation; contextual framing. Core somatic measurement algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Somatic differentiation coefficients (value-aligned vs. neutral); value-violation tension patterns; pre-decision signal data; sustained engagement duration; gentle-force range; aggression-only markers; approach-withdrawal patterns; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
