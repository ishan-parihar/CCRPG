# Spiritual / Red — Strategic-Planning Game Concept

> **Axis:** The strategic-planning axis probes spiritual intelligence through MULTI-STEP VALUE-COHERENT PLANNING — can the player plan sequences of actions that SERVE their stated value across time? At Red, this means: "I value victory. My plan serves victory across 3 steps."
>
> **Why this axis for Spiritual/Red:** Values that don't inform planning are decorative. Spiritual intelligence becomes STRATEGIC when the value-hierarchy guides multi-step action. The warrior who values honour plans honourably. The warrior who values power plans for power. This modality tests whether values ORGANISE planning.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Value-Strategist"
- **Core mechanic:** The player plans multi-step sequences where each step must serve their stated primary value. Plans are evaluated for value-coherence across steps — does the value GUIDE the plan, or is the plan value-free?
- **Duration:** 4-7 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** Value-Aligned Steps → Value-Coherent Sequences → Value Under Pressure → Adaptive Value-Plans → The Sacred Campaign

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "You said X matters most. Plan 3 steps that serve X. Every step must honour your value." The contact boundary is: "Can your value ORGANISE your planning?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* Does the value actually inform planning? The Zealot plans only crusades. The Nihilist plans without values. The Premature Mystic over-plans with abstract value-language. The Sacred-Refuser starts value-coherent plans then abandons them.
- *Emergent:* Can they plan longer value-coherent sequences? Can they maintain value-coherence when plans face obstacles?

**Integration path:** Rewards plans that are BOTH effective AND value-coherent. A simple 2-step value-coherent plan that works scores higher than a complex value-free plan or an elaborate value-plan that collapses.

**Successful integration:** The player creates and executes plans where every step serves their stated value — value-coherence is maintained across the entire sequence.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Value-Strategist: a campaign-planning game where every step must serve the player's stated value. The player declares a value, then plans a sequence of actions where each step honours that value. The aesthetic: Red-stage war-council where the warrior plans a campaign in service of their sacred cause.

### Interaction
- **Value-Aligned Steps (1-5):** Plan 2 steps that serve your value. Simple value-coherent sequencing.
- **Value-Coherent Sequences (5-15):** Plan 3 steps; each must serve the value; steps must also be effective.
- **Value Under Pressure (15-30):** Mid-plan, an obstacle appears. Maintain value-coherence while adapting.
- **Adaptive Value-Plans (30-50):** Complex situations where value-coherent planning requires creativity.
- **The Sacred Campaign (50+):** Full multi-step value-coherent campaign with obstacles, adaptations, and sustained coherence.

### Feedback
- Value-coherent success → "Every step served your cause. The campaign honours your value."
- Crusade-only → "Every step was an attack. Can your value be served without violence?"
- Value-free → "Your plan worked. But it didn't serve your value. Why have a value if it doesn't guide you?"
- Abandoned → "You started with honour. You finished with expedience. The value broke mid-plan."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Plan length: 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 steps
- Value-coherence demand: any alignment → clear alignment → creative alignment
- Obstacles: none → predictable → surprising
- Efficiency-vs-value tension: low → moderate → high

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Value-Aligned Steps | 1-5 | 2-step value-coherent plans |
| Value-Coherent Sequences | 5-15 | 3-step; effectiveness + coherence |
| Value Under Pressure | 15-30 | Mid-plan obstacles; maintain coherence |
| Adaptive Value-Plans | 30-50 | Creative value-coherent adaptation |
| The Sacred Campaign | 50+ | Full value-coherent campaign |

---

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Simple value-alignment tasks:** 2-step plans serving stated value
- **Effectiveness+coherence tasks:** Plans must work AND serve value
- **Obstacle scenarios:** Mid-plan disruptions requiring value-coherent adaptation
- **Efficiency-vs-value dilemmas:** Faster path violates value; slower path honours it
- **Campaign sequences:** Multi-step sustained value-coherent planning

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ simple, 20+ effectiveness, 20+ obstacle, 15+ efficiency-vs-value, 10+ campaign

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Crusade-only plans → dark-addiction; value-free plans → dark-allergy
- Over-elaborate plans → golden-addiction; mid-plan abandonment → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Drag-and-drop (plan construction); tap-to-select (step selection); sequence input (plan ordering)

### Timing requirements
- Planning phase untimed; execution phase at standard precision
- Cross-session tracking for sustained coherence patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Campaign environment with consistent rules
- Obstacle system that disrupts plans mid-execution
- Efficiency-vs-value tension system (faster paths that violate values)

### LLM requirements
- **Medium:** Campaign generation, value-coherence evaluation, obstacle narration. Core scoring algorithmic.

### State persistence
- Plan history + value-coherence scores; effectiveness rates; coherence-under-pressure patterns; adaptation quality; sustained coherence duration; crusade/value-free/elaborate/abandonment patterns; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
