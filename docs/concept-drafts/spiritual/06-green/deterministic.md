# Spiritual / Green — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** Objective interfaith measurement — tradition-recognition accuracy, cross-tradition pattern detection, sacred-diversity appreciation metrics.  **Why this axis for this module:** Pluralistic faith requires genuine KNOWLEDGE of multiple traditions, not just warm feelings about diversity — deterministic measurement separates real interfaith literacy from spiritual tourism.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** Sacred Cartography  
**Core loop:** Player identifies, classifies, and connects elements across spiritual traditions through timed pattern-recognition tasks. Symbols, practices, texts, and concepts from world traditions are presented; the player must demonstrate both breadth (how many traditions recognized) and depth (how accurately elements are placed within their tradition's internal logic).  
**Session length:** 3–7 minutes per checkpoint.  
**Infinite checkpoint:** Each session is a self-contained cartography challenge; progress accumulates as the player's "sacred map" grows more detailed and interconnected.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst type:** Cross-tradition pattern recognition under accuracy constraints.  
**Shadow surfacing:** DAll surfaces as low recognition accuracy combined with dismissive classification errors ("that's just mythology"). DA surfaces as high breadth but low depth — recognizes many traditions but misattributes internal elements. GA surfaces as premature pattern-collapse — connects everything to "universal truth" before demonstrating particular knowledge. GAll surfaces as refusal to rank difficulty or acknowledge depth-differences between traditions.  
**Drive probes:** Eros measured by willingness to engage harder cross-tradition connections. Agape measured by care taken with each tradition's particularity. Agency measured by confident classification under uncertainty. Communion measured by pattern-connections that honor relationship between traditions.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:** (a) Symbol-sort: sacred symbols appear; player assigns to tradition + context (e.g., "this mandala is Tibetan Buddhist, used in Vajrayana visualization practice"). (b) Practice-match: descriptions of spiritual practices presented; player identifies tradition, developmental function, and analogues in other traditions. (c) Text-attribution: passages from sacred texts; player identifies source, tradition, and thematic resonance with other texts. (d) Pattern-web: player draws connections between elements across traditions, scored on accuracy and insight.  
**Difficulty staircase:** Begins with obvious distinctions (Christian cross vs. Hindu om), progresses to subtle internal distinctions (Theravada vs. Mahayana meditation instructions), then to cross-tradition structural parallels (apophatic theology across traditions).  
**Shadow-mode:** When DA detected, tasks demand depth within single traditions. When DAll detected, tasks present undeniably beautiful elements that resist rational dismissal. When GA detected, tasks require PARTICULAR knowledge before allowing synthesis. When GAll detected, tasks present clear depth-hierarchies within traditions that must be acknowledged.

## 4. Item Pool

**Tradition coverage:** Minimum 12 major traditions (Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism, Taoism, Shinto, Indigenous traditions, Sufism, Kabbalah, Earth-spirituality, Baha'i) with 50+ elements per tradition.  
**Element types:** Symbols (visual), practices (procedural), texts (verbal), concepts (abstract), figures (biographical), cosmologies (structural).  
**Difficulty tags:** Each item tagged with recognition-difficulty (1–5), depth-required (surface/intermediate/deep), and cross-tradition-connection potential (isolated/moderate/rich).  
**Adaptive selection:** Item selection weighted by player's demonstrated gaps — if breadth exceeds depth, deeper items from known traditions are prioritized; if depth exceeds breadth, new traditions are introduced.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Response format:** Multiple-choice for symbol-sort (4 options), drag-and-drop for practice-match, free-classification for pattern-web.  
**Scoring:** Accuracy × depth-weight × speed-bonus. Depth-weight multiplier: surface-correct = 1.0×, intermediate-correct = 1.5×, deep-correct = 2.5×. Speed bonus capped at 1.2× to prevent rushing.  
**Data requirements:** Curated database of sacred elements with expert-validated classifications. Cross-tradition connection graph with weighted edges.  
**Telemetry:** Per-tradition accuracy, depth-profile, breadth-profile, pattern-connection quality, shadow-indicator scores (DA = high-breadth/low-depth ratio; DAll = low-engagement/dismissive-errors; GA = premature-synthesis attempts; GAll = hierarchy-refusal patterns).  
**Adaptive engine:** Staircase adjusts within-session based on rolling accuracy. Between-session, item-pool shifts based on shadow-profile and drive-health scores.
