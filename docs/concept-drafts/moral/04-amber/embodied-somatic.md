# Moral / Amber — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as site of moral feeling — where guilt lives somatically, moral posture, embodied vow-making.  **Why this axis for this module:** Genuine conventional morality is FELT in the body — guilt has a somatic signature, duty has a posture, vow-keeping has a physical weight. The body reveals what cognition can mask.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Weight of the Oath
**Core Mechanic:** Player engages with moral situations through somatic channels — posture, breath, tension patterns, gesture. The body's response to moral content (guilt, duty, commitment) is the primary data stream.
**Duration:** 4-7 minutes per session, infinite checkpoint.
**Internal Progression:** Moral-body awareness → guilt-location → duty-posture → embodied vow → somatic flexibility under moral tension.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Unique Presentation:** Body-first moral engagement — player is guided to notice WHERE moral feelings live in the body, WHAT posture duty creates, HOW guilt manifests somatically. Not cognitive rule-application (deterministic), not verbal (language-reflective), not choice-based (scenario), not planning (strategic), not social (cooperative), not free-play (immersive).
**Differs From Others:** The body IS the instrument of measurement. Moral content is delivered through somatic prompts, not cognitive or narrative ones.
**Uniquely Surfaces:** DA through rigid/locked moral posture, chronic tension, inability to soften; DAll through absent somatic moral response, no body-guilt, dissociation from moral feeling; GA through performed moral embodiment (looks right but lacks depth); GAll through frozen embodiment (present but immobile, cannot shift).
**Successful Integration:** Player can feel guilt in the body without being crushed by it, hold duty-posture without rigidity, make embodied vows with genuine weight, and soften under moral tension without collapse.

## 3. Game Design

**Setup:** Player enters "The Body Temple" — a somatic space where moral feeling is explored through the body. Guided awareness prompts direct attention to physical moral experience.

**Interaction Phases:**
1. **Moral Body Scan** — "Where does right/wrong live in your body?" (baseline somatic moral awareness)
2. **Guilt Location** — "Feel a broken promise — where does it land?" (guilt-soma mapping)
3. **Duty Posture** — "Stand as one who keeps their word" (commitment embodiment)
4. **Vow Weight** — "Make a promise with your whole body" (embodied commitment)
5. **Somatic Flexibility** — "Hold duty AND soften" (integration)

**Feedback Examples:**
1. Genuine somatic moral awareness → body-map illuminates, warmth spreads
2. Rigid/locked posture → body-map shows red tension zones (too tight)
3. Absent somatic response → body-map remains dark (not embodied)
4. Performed posture without depth → body-map flickers (unstable)
5. Frozen but present → body-map shows blue stillness (calcified)

**Difficulty Adaptation:** Somatic prompts deepen based on demonstrated body-awareness. Flexibility challenges only appear after genuine embodied morality is established. Tension-tolerance scales gradually.

**Internal Progression Table:**

| Level | Somatic Task | Body Awareness Required | Shadow Probed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Locate moral feeling | Basic interoception | Baseline |
| 2 | Feel guilt somatically | Guilt-body connection | DAll |
| 3 | Embody duty/commitment | Postural moral expression | DA/GAll |
| 4 | Make embodied vow | Full-body commitment | All |
| 5 | Hold duty + soften | Somatic flexibility | DA/GA |

**Flexibility Prompts (15+):** Hold duty and breathe. Hold commitment and soften your jaw. Hold loyalty and release your shoulders. Hold the code and let your belly be soft. Hold your vow and let your hands open. Hold obedience and breathe into your back. Hold tradition and let your chest expand. Hold the rule and soften your eyes. Hold guilt and let it move. Hold duty and let it breathe. Hold the code and find ease within it. Hold your word and release what is not needed. Hold belonging and keep your own shape. Hold the oath and stay alive within it. Hold the weight and find lightness.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input Types:** Accelerometer/gyroscope (posture), touch-pressure, breath rate (microphone), self-report body-maps (tap-to-locate), gesture recognition.
**Timing:** Slow, somatic pace. Hold-duration measured. Tension-release timing tracked. No rush — the body moves at its own speed.
**NPC/AI:** The Body Temple — ambient somatic guide. Breath-pacing cues. Postural suggestions.
**LLM:** Minimal — generates adaptive somatic prompts based on player's body-map history. Primary scoring is sensor-based, not language-based.
**State Persistence:** Body-map history, tension-pattern evolution, guilt-location consistency, posture-flexibility trajectory, vow-embodiment depth, somatic shadow-signal accumulation.
