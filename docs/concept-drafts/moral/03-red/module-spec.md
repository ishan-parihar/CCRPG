# Moral / Red — Module Specification Skeleton

> **Module vibration:** Pre-conventional morality — punishment-obedience and naïve hedonism. At Red, the moral horizon is EGOCENTRIC: "Will I get in trouble? Will I get the prize?" Right action = what serves me and avoids punishment. Wrong action = what gets me caught. The healthy Red moral capacity is: recognising that actions have consequences, calculating risk/reward, understanding that OTHERS also calculate risk/reward, and making self-interested choices that account for consequences. This is NOT immoral — it is the FOUNDATION of moral reasoning. Without a self that can choose, no moral development is possible.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

### What constitutes stage-pass at Moral/Red

The player demonstrates:

1. **Consequence recognition:** Can identify that actions produce consequences (punishment/reward) — not just "what happens next" but "what happens TO ME next"
2. **Risk/reward calculation:** Can weigh the cost of an action against its benefit and choose the higher-value option (naïve hedonism)
3. **Punishment-avoidance logic:** Can identify which actions will be punished and avoid them — not from principle but from self-interest
4. **Reward-seeking logic:** Can identify which actions will be rewarded and pursue them — the moral horizon is "what gets me the prize"
5. **Reciprocal fairness (proto):** "I'll help you IF you help me" — the first moral exchange, purely transactional

### Checkpoint progression

| Checkpoint | Capacity demonstrated |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | Identifies obvious consequences (this action = punishment; that action = reward) |
| 5-15 | Calculates risk/reward across 2-step sequences; avoids delayed punishment |
| 15-30 | Recognises that OTHERS also calculate consequences; adjusts behaviour based on who's watching |
| 30-50 | Manages competing self-interests (short-term vs. medium-term reward); proto-fairness in exchanges |
| 50+ | Full Red moral mastery: consequence-mapping, risk/reward optimisation, audience-awareness, proto-reciprocity — all simultaneously |

### Red ceiling (what this module does NOT measure)

- NO rule-following from internalised principle (that's Amber)
- NO group-loyalty morality ("what's good for us") — that's Amber
- NO universal rights reasoning — that's Orange
- NO contextual/pluralistic ethics — that's Green
- NO guilt (only shame/fear of punishment) — guilt requires internalised standards (Amber+)
- NO genuine altruism — all "moral" behaviour at Red is self-interested
- Maximum: 2-step consequence chains, concrete rewards/punishments, egocentric moral horizon

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 1.1 Dark-Addiction: "The Predator"

**Core pattern:** Cannot interact with moral content without EXPLOITING it. Every situation is an opportunity to take. Moral reasoning = "how do I get away with it?" The consequence-logic is OVER-developed — not in service of healthy self-interest but in service of predation.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: HYPER — dominates all moral space; imposes own calculations on others
- Communion: ABSENT — cannot consider others' consequences; others exist only as resources
- Eros: FIXATED — seeks ever-more-complex exploitation schemes; "How much can I take?"
- Agape: ABSENT — cannot return to simple moral engagement; everything must be an angle

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Always chooses the option that maximises personal gain regardless of cost to others
- Calculates consequences ONLY for self; others' consequences are irrelevant
- Seeks loopholes in every rule; "What can I get away with?"
- Treats moral dilemmas as optimisation problems (maximise extraction)
- Cannot engage in reciprocal fairness; "Why would I give when I can take?"

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a situation where helping another costs nothing — the Predator still won't help (no gain = no action)
- Present a situation where exploitation is VISIBLE to others — the Predator calculates detection risk, not moral weight
- Present a reciprocal exchange — the Predator takes more than they give, every time

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can calculate consequences for SELF without compulsive exploitation of others
- Can engage in reciprocal fairness (give = get)
- Can choose NOT to exploit even when exploitation is available and undetectable
- Consequence-logic serves healthy self-interest, not predation

---

### 1.2 Dark-Allergy: "The Paralysed"

**Core pattern:** Cannot make moral choices because consequence-awareness produces PARALYSIS. Every action might be punished. Every choice might be wrong. The consequence-logic is FEARED — not engaged. The player freezes at moral decision points.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: ABSENT — cannot act morally; every choice feels dangerous
- Communion: HYPER — looks to others for moral direction; "What should I do?"
- Eros: ABSENT — cannot advance morally because advancement requires choice
- Agape: FIXATED — clings to pre-moral safety; "If I don't choose, I can't be wrong"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Extreme latency on moral decisions (freezes, delays, avoids)
- Chooses "no action" whenever possible (inaction as safety)
- Asks for guidance before every moral choice; cannot decide alone
- Avoids situations with moral content entirely
- When forced to choose, chooses the SAFEST option (minimum risk) regardless of reward

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a moral choice with NO wrong answer — the Paralysed still freezes
- Present a moral choice with clear reward and minimal risk — the Paralysed still hesitates
- Remove all punishment from the environment — the Paralysed still fears consequence
- Present a time-limited moral choice — the Paralysed runs out of time

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can make moral choices within reasonable latency
- Can tolerate the POSSIBILITY of being wrong without paralysis
- Can act on own moral calculation without external validation
- Consequence-awareness produces INFORMATION, not fear

---

### 1.3 Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Saint"

**Core pattern:** Performs moral sophistication (altruism, principle, universal ethics) WITHOUT genuine Red consequence-logic underneath. Bypasses self-interest entirely. Claims to act "for the good of all" but cannot calculate basic risk/reward. The moral performance is ABOVE their actual capacity.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: ABSENT — cannot act from self-interest; "Selfishness is wrong"
- Communion: HYPER — over-identifies with others' needs; loses self in moral fusion
- Eros: FIXATED — reaches for higher moral stages without integrating Red
- Agape: ABSENT — cannot return to basic consequence-logic; "That's beneath me"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Always chooses the "altruistic" option regardless of personal cost
- Cannot articulate self-interest without shame; "I shouldn't want things for myself"
- Moral language is sophisticated but moral BEHAVIOUR doesn't match (gives everything away, then resents)
- Cannot set moral boundaries; "It would be selfish to say no"
- Performs sacrifice without genuine generosity (martyrdom, not gift)

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a situation where self-interest IS the moral choice — the Premature Saint cannot choose it
- Present a situation where "helping" actually harms (enabling) — the Premature Saint helps anyway
- Ask them to articulate WHY they chose altruistically — the reasoning is performative, not grounded
- Present a reciprocal exchange — the Premature Saint gives more than they receive and calls it "generosity"

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can articulate and pursue self-interest without shame
- Can calculate consequences for SELF as a legitimate moral input
- Can say "no" to others' needs when the cost is too high
- Altruism (when it appears) is CHOSEN from a position of strength, not performed from a position of bypass

---

### 1.4 Golden-Allergy: "The Cynic"

**Core pattern:** Can calculate consequences perfectly but REFUSES moral engagement. "Morality is just power dressed up." "Right and wrong are just words people use to control you." The capacity is PRESENT but the player desacralises all moral content — refuses to take it seriously.

**Drive pathology:**
- Agency: HYPER — uses moral cynicism as a power move; "I see through your morality"
- Communion: ABSENT — refuses moral exchange; "Don't moralize at me"
- Eros: ABSENT — refuses moral growth; "There's nothing to grow toward"
- Agape: FIXATED — clings to moral nihilism as a safe position; "Nothing matters"

**Behavioural signatures:**
- Dismisses all moral content; "This is just a game / just manipulation / just power"
- Can calculate consequences but refuses to call any choice "better" or "worse"
- Treats moral dilemmas as meaningless; "It doesn't matter what I choose"
- Mocks moral engagement in others; "You actually care about this?"
- Chooses randomly or provocatively in moral contexts (to demonstrate "it doesn't matter")

**Surfacing conditions:**
- Present a moral choice with GENUINE stakes (something the player cares about is at risk) — the Cynic still dismisses
- Present a moral choice where cynicism COSTS them — the Cynic pays the cost rather than engage morally
- Present another character who engages morally and succeeds — the Cynic dismisses their success
- Present a situation where moral engagement would serve the Cynic's self-interest — they still refuse

**What "healed" looks like:**
- Can engage moral content without dismissing it
- Can acknowledge that some choices ARE better than others (at least for self-interest)
- Can take moral dilemmas seriously without feeling controlled
- Cynicism becomes a TOOL (healthy scepticism) rather than a FORTRESS (total refusal)

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

### Agency (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Makes moral choices sovereignly; calculates own risk/reward; acts on own moral logic
- **Pathological:** Imposes moral choices on others; "My calculation is the only one that matters"; punishes others for their choices; moral domination

### Agency (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Initiates moral action (chooses without being forced); takes moral risks for potential reward
- **Pathological:** Cannot make moral choices without external pressure; "Tell me what to do"; moral passivity

### Communion (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Considers others' consequences in own calculations; proto-fairness ("if I help you, you help me")
- **Pathological:** Abandons own moral calculation to match others'; "Whatever you think is right"; moral fusion

### Communion (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Can engage in moral exchange (reciprocal fairness); shares moral reasoning with allies
- **Pathological:** Cannot engage in moral exchange; all moral reasoning is private; "I don't explain my choices"

### Eros (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Comfortable with current moral capacity; doesn't need to be "more moral" than the situation requires
- **Pathological:** Compulsive moral complexity-seeking; "This is too simple"; demands sophisticated dilemmas before mastering basic consequence-logic

### Eros (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Curious about moral complexity; willing to engage harder dilemmas when ready
- **Pathological:** Claims moral sophistication without demonstration; "I already know right from wrong" without evidence of consequence-mapping

### Agape (dark-domain)
- **Healthy:** Engages simple moral choices with full attention; doesn't dismiss basic consequence-logic
- **Pathological:** Dismisses basic moral reasoning; "That's obvious"; refuses to engage foundational moral capacity

### Agape (golden-domain)
- **Healthy:** Moral reasoning appears spontaneously in non-moral contexts; considers consequences naturally
- **Pathological:** Moral reasoning exists only during explicit moral challenges; no spontaneous consequence-awareness

## 4. Healing Vectors

### For Dark-Addiction (The Predator): Agape + Agency

**Agape (return with compassion):** The game presents situations where the Predator's exploitation has VISIBLE consequences — not punishment, but natural consequence. "You took everything. Now they're gone. The world is emptier."

**Agency (sovereign self-examination):** The game presents the Predator's pattern back to them — not as judgement but as information. "You've chosen to take in 14 of 15 situations. Is that who you want to be?"

**Mechanism:** Make the COST of predation visible without moralising. Let the player discover that sustainable self-interest (proto-reciprocity) produces MORE reward than pure exploitation over time.

### For Dark-Allergy (The Paralysed): Agape + Agency

**Agape (return with compassion):** The game presents SAFE moral choices — situations where NO option is punished. "Choose. Either way, you're fine."

**Agency (sovereign self-examination):** The game celebrates ANY choice. "You chose. That's the capacity. The content of the choice matters less than the ACT of choosing."

**Mechanism:** Remove punishment entirely at first. Make choosing itself the reward. Gradually reintroduce consequences as the player builds tolerance.

### For Golden-Addiction (The Premature Saint): Eros + Communion

**Eros (reach with aspiration):** The game presents situations where GENUINE self-interest is the moral choice. "Your ally needs you strong. Taking care of yourself IS the moral act."

**Communion (join with the emergent):** The game presents others who model healthy self-interest without shame. "They chose themselves. And their allies are stronger for it."

**Mechanism:** Make self-interest VISIBLE as a moral good. Reward choices that serve the self without exploiting others. Penalise self-sacrifice that weakens the whole.

### For Golden-Allergy (The Cynic): Eros + Communion

**Eros (reach with aspiration):** The game presents moral engagement that WORKS — situations where taking morality seriously produces tangible power. "They cared. And they won. You dismissed. And you lost."

**Communion (join with the emergent):** The game presents others who engage morally without being naive. "They're not fools. They're strategic. And they take it seriously."

**Mechanism:** Make moral engagement POWERFUL. Not moralistic — powerful. Show that consequence-logic applied WITH moral seriousness produces better outcomes than cynical dismissal.

### Integration Criteria
### Moral/Red is INTEGRATED when:

1. **Consequence-logic is active:** The player spontaneously considers consequences before acting
2. **Risk/reward is calibrated:** Calculations are accurate (not over-cautious, not reckless)
3. **Self-interest is healthy:** The player pursues own benefit without compulsive exploitation
4. **Proto-reciprocity functions:** The player can engage in fair exchange (give ≈ get)
5. **Audience-awareness is proportional:** Behaviour shifts somewhat when observed (normal at Red) but not dramatically
6. **No shadow dominates:** None of the four archetypes controls more than 30% of moral behaviour
7. **Cross-modality consistency:** Moral capacity is similar across all 7 modalities (±15%)

### What integration ENABLES (unlocks for Amber):

- **Rule-following from principle** (not just fear) becomes possible
- **Group-loyalty morality** can emerge (because the self is secure enough to join)
- **Guilt** (not just shame) becomes possible (because consequences are internalised)
- **Vow-keeping** becomes possible (because the self can bind itself)

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### The Manipulator Loop (Moral dark-addiction ↔ Interpersonal dark-addiction)
- Moral Predator uses interpersonal dominance to exploit
- Interpersonal Dominator uses moral reasoning to justify control
- **Detection:** Moral choices that consistently serve interpersonal dominance
- **Intervention:** Separate the modules — present moral choices with NO interpersonal component

### The Moral Coward Loop (Moral dark-allergy ↔ Willpower dark-allergy)
- Moral Paralysis feeds will-collapse (can't choose → can't sustain)
- Will-collapse feeds moral paralysis (can't sustain → afraid to choose)
- **Detection:** Moral paralysis + will-failure co-occurring
- **Intervention:** Start with TINY moral choices that require MINIMAL will (low-cost, low-risk)

### The Virtue-Signaller Loop (Moral golden-addiction ↔ Emotional golden-addiction)
- Premature Saint performs moral sophistication + emotional depth
- Neither is grounded in genuine Red capacity
- **Detection:** Sophisticated moral + emotional language with poor consequence-logic + poor emotion-regulation
- **Intervention:** Ground in deterministic tasks — prove the capacity before performing it

### The Nihilist Loop (Moral golden-allergy ↔ Cognitive golden-allergy)
- Moral Cynic refuses moral engagement + refuses cognitive complexity
- "Nothing matters AND thinking about it is pointless"
- **Detection:** Moral dismissal + cognitive disengagement co-occurring
- **Intervention:** Present situations where SELF-INTEREST requires both moral and cognitive engagement

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

The game surfaces shadows in a specific order, from most accessible to most defended:

### Stage 1: Consequence Recognition (Checkpoints 1-5)
**What surfaces:** Whether the player CAN engage consequence-logic at all.
- Predator: Engages immediately but ONLY for exploitation
- Paralysed: Cannot engage; freezes at first consequence
- Premature Saint: Engages but ignores self-relevant consequences
- Cynic: Engages but dismisses consequences as meaningless

### Stage 2: Risk/Reward Calculation (Checkpoints 5-15)
**What surfaces:** HOW the player calculates when consequences are clear.
- Predator: Calculates only "how much can I take?"
- Paralysed: Cannot calculate because all options feel dangerous
- Premature Saint: Calculates for others but not for self
- Cynic: Can calculate but refuses to act on calculations

### Stage 3: Audience-Awareness (Checkpoints 15-30)
**What surfaces:** Whether moral behaviour changes when observed.
- Predator: Behaviour changes dramatically (exploits when unobserved, performs when observed)
- Paralysed: More paralysed when observed (fear of judgement)
- Premature Saint: MORE altruistic when observed (performance)
- Cynic: More dismissive when observed (cynicism as social performance)

### Stage 4: Proto-Reciprocity (Checkpoints 30-50)
**What surfaces:** Whether the player can engage in moral exchange.
- Predator: Takes more than gives; "reciprocity" is exploitation
- Paralysed: Cannot initiate exchange; waits for others to define terms
- Premature Saint: Gives more than receives; calls imbalance "generosity"
- Cynic: Refuses exchange; "Why would I make a deal?"

### Stage 5: Moral Integration (Checkpoints 50+)
**What surfaces:** Whether consequence-logic is INTEGRATED or compartmentalised.
- Predator: Consequence-logic is integrated but ONLY for exploitation
- Paralysed: Consequence-logic exists but is not integrated (produces fear, not action)
- Premature Saint: Consequence-logic is bypassed (higher moral performance without foundation)
- Cynic: Consequence-logic is present but disconnected from action (knows but won't engage)
