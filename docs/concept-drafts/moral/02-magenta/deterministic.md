# Moral / Magenta — Deterministic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The deterministic axis probes moral intelligence through OBJECTIVE MEASUREMENT — can the player detect sacred weight? At Magenta, this is: "Does this object/action carry taboo? Is this sacred or ordinary?" Binary moral detection without reasoning — pure sensing.
>
> **Why this axis for Moral/Magenta:** Before moral reasoning (Red+), there must be moral SENSING — the felt detection that something carries sacred weight. This modality tests whether the player can detect taboo/sacred markers objectively, like a moral Geiger counter.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred-Sense"
- **Core mechanic:** Objects and actions are presented. The player must detect which carry sacred weight and which are ordinary. Not WHY they're sacred — just WHETHER they are. Pure taboo-detection. The moral Geiger counter.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Taboo → The Sacred Object → The Weighted Action → The Hidden Sacred → The Living Taboo

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "Some things are sacred. Some are ordinary. Can you tell which is which? Not why — just which. Feel it. Which one carries weight?" The contact boundary is: "Can you detect moral weight?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player detect sacred weight? The Taboo-Slave marks EVERYTHING sacred. The Profane-Breaker marks NOTHING sacred. The Premature Rule-Maker applies rules ("it LOOKS sacred because X"). The Ritual-Refuser detects correctly but doesn't engage.
- *Emergent:* Can they accurately detect sacred weight — neither over-sensing nor under-sensing?

**Integration path:** Rewards ACCURATE SACRED DETECTION — correctly identifying which items/actions carry moral weight. Not everything-sacred, not nothing-sacred, not rule-based, not disengaged.

**Successful integration:** The player reliably detects sacred weight — the foundation of all moral sensing.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Detecting sacred weight independently.
- Healthy: Makes own sacred/profane judgments; trusts own moral sensing
- Pathological: Cannot judge (everything sacred = paralysis); OR: nothing registers
- **Detection:** Whether judgments are differentiated and self-generated

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Detecting sacred weight under ambiguity.
- Healthy: Offers tentative judgment under ambiguity; tolerates uncertainty
- Pathological: Cannot judge without certainty; freezes on ambiguous items
- **Detection:** Judgment quality under moral ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Detecting when companion judges differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own judgment while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's judgment; loses own moral sensing
- **Detection:** Whether own judgment survives social influence

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared sacred detection (comparing moral sensing).
- Healthy: Shares own sensing AND receives companion's
- Pathological: Cannot share moral sensing; sacred detection is private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared moral exploration

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Detecting familiar sacred items (same objects, same contexts).
- Healthy: Maintains genuine sensing for familiar sacred content
- Pathological: Sensing becomes rote; loses genuine moral contact
- **Detection:** Whether familiar sacred detection retains genuine quality

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Detecting novel sacred items (unfamiliar objects/actions).
- Healthy: Attempts sacred detection for novel content with curiosity
- Pathological: Cannot detect sacred weight in unfamiliar content; OR: forces familiar categories
- **Detection:** Novel sacred detection quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining detection accuracy over time.
- Healthy: Sacred detection remains consistent across sessions
- Pathological: Detection accuracy degrades; moral sensing becomes random
- **Detection:** Detection accuracy across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether sacred detection appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Notices sacred weight naturally in non-moral tasks
- Pathological: Sacred detection exists only during explicit moral tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous moral sensing

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Taboo-Slave"
**How it surfaces:** Marks EVERYTHING as sacred. Cannot identify anything as ordinary. "They're ALL sacred." "I can't tell — what if I'm wrong?" Over-detection. Moral hypersensitivity.
- **Detection signals:** All items marked sacred; no "ordinary" judgments; excessive hesitation; "what if it's sacred?"; cannot commit to "ordinary"; over-detection rate > 80%

### Dark-Allergy: "The Profane-Breaker"
**How it surfaces:** Marks NOTHING as sacred. Cannot detect moral weight in anything. "They're all just things." "None of them matter." Under-detection. Moral blindness.
- **Detection signals:** All items marked ordinary; no "sacred" judgments; zero hesitation; "it's just a thing"; cannot detect weight; under-detection rate > 80%

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Rule-Maker"
**How it surfaces:** Applies RULES to determine sacred status. "It's sacred because it's old/shiny/special-looking." Rule-based detection without felt-sense. Correct answers for wrong reasons.
- **Detection signals:** Rule-based language; "because it looks like..."; correct detection but no felt-sense indicators; mechanical categorisation; cannot explain felt-weight

### Golden-Allergy: "The Ritual-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** DETECTS correctly but DISENGAGES. Accurate sacred sensing followed by "so what?" Identifies sacred weight then dismisses it. Detection without engagement.
- **Detection signals:** Accurate detection + dismissive language; "yeah it's sacred, whatever"; correct sensing + no engagement; detection without care; "I can tell but I don't care"

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Present CLEARLY ORDINARY items: "This one. Just a rock. Nothing sacred. Feel it — just ordinary."
- Build confidence in "ordinary" judgments; reward accurate non-sacred detection
- Frame: "Not everything is sacred. Some things are just things. Can you feel the difference? THIS one — just ordinary."
- Build: discrimination (sacred AND ordinary both exist)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Present UNDENIABLY SACRED items: items with maximum sacred markers
- Make sacred weight OBVIOUS: "This one. Feel it. Something different. Something that matters."
- Frame: "Not everything is ordinary. Some things carry weight. THIS one — feel it. Different."
- Reward ANY sacred detection; celebrate first moral sensing

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require FELT detection: "Don't look at it. FEEL it. Close your eyes. Which one has weight?"
- Block visual/rule-based cues; require felt-sense detection
- Frame: "Not because it looks sacred. Because it FEELS sacred. In you. Which one has weight?"
- Build: genuine moral sensing (felt not categorised)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make detection MATTER: sacred items unlock content; detection has consequence
- Connect detection to engagement: "You felt it. Now — what does that mean? It matters."
- Frame: "You can tell. You DO sense it. What if that sensing meant something? What if you honoured it?"
- Build: moral engagement (detection → action)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred-Sense: a moral detection space. Objects and actions are presented. Some carry sacred weight; some are ordinary. The player's task: detect which is which. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage altar — objects glow faintly when sacred; the player must sense the glow.

### Interaction
- **The First Taboo (1-5):** Two objects → "Which is sacred?" Binary choice, high contrast.
- **The Sacred Object (5-15):** Multiple objects → identify all sacred ones. Moderate contrast.
- **The Weighted Action (15-30):** Actions (not just objects) → "Which action carries weight?"
- **The Hidden Sacred (30-50):** Reduced visual cues → detect from felt-sense alone.
- **The Living Taboo (50+):** Full sacred detection: objects + actions + hidden + novel.

### Feedback
- Accurate detection → "You felt it! That one carries weight. Your sensing is true."
- Over-detection → "Not that one. Just ordinary. Feel the difference. Not everything is sacred."
- Under-detection → "That one! Feel it again. Something there. Weight. It matters."
- Rule-based → "Not because it looks that way. FEEL it. Close your eyes. Which has weight?"
- Disengaged → "You felt it. You knew. That matters. Your sensing means something."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Sacred/profane contrast: high (obvious) → moderate → subtle
- Item complexity: objects → actions → situations
- Cue availability: visual + felt → felt only
- Ambiguity: clear sacred/profane → ambiguous items
- Novel content: familiar sacred markers → unfamiliar sacred weight

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Taboo | 1-5 | Binary sacred/profane (high contrast) |
| The Sacred Object | 5-15 | Multiple items, moderate contrast |
| The Weighted Action | 15-30 | Actions carry moral weight |
| The Hidden Sacred | 30-50 | Reduced cues; felt-sense detection |
| The Living Taboo | 50+ | Full sacred detection integration |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **High-contrast sacred:** Obviously sacred objects (altar items, ritual objects)
- **High-contrast profane:** Obviously ordinary objects (rocks, sticks)
- **Moderate sacred:** Sacred but less obvious (worn ritual items, quiet sacred spaces)
- **Ambiguous items:** Could be sacred or profane depending on context
- **Sacred actions:** Actions that carry moral weight (giving, taking, breaking, honouring)

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ high-contrast sacred, 25+ high-contrast profane, 20+ moderate, 15+ ambiguous, 15+ actions

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Over-detection (all sacred) → dark-addiction; under-detection (all profane) → dark-allergy
- Rule-based detection → golden-addiction; accurate but disengaged → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Detection accuracy:** Correct sacred/profane classification rate
- **Discrimination:** Can they tell sacred from profane (not all-same)?
- **Felt-sense quality:** Detection based on feeling (not rules)?
- **Ambiguity tolerance:** Can they handle uncertain items?
- **Engagement:** Do they care about what they detect?

### LLM rubric
- **Low:** Core detection scoring algorithmic (correct/incorrect classification). LLM for felt-sense vs. rule-based distinction and engagement quality assessment.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-classify (sacred/profane); drag-to-sort (sacred pile / profane pile); hold (sustain sensing)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked (hesitation = data); no time pressure; detection confidence measured

### NPC/AI requirements
- Item generation system (sacred/profane with varying contrast)
- Sacred-weight system (items carry measurable sacred markers)
- Difficulty adaptation (contrast, cue availability, novelty)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic classification scoring. LLM for felt-sense language evaluation and novel item generation.

### State persistence
- Detection accuracy rates; discrimination quality; felt-sense indicators; ambiguity tolerance; engagement quality; over-detection rates; under-detection rates; rule-based indicators; disengagement indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
