# Moral / Magenta — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** The immersive-rpg (ecological) axis probes moral intelligence through SPONTANEOUS MORAL BEHAVIOUR in an open world with sacred/profane content — not structured detection or choice tasks but free moral navigation. How does the player's moral intelligence LIVE when nobody's testing it?
>
> **Why this axis for Moral/Magenta:** The other 6 modalities probe moral sensing through structured challenges. This one observes it in the wild. At Magenta, the world contains sacred groves, forbidden objects, trickster-spirits offering bargains, and reciprocity opportunities. The player's spontaneous moral behaviour reveals genuine capacity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred-World"
- **Core mechanic:** An open Magenta-stage world with sacred and profane content woven throughout. Taboo objects, sacred groves, trickster-spirits, reciprocity opportunities. The player's spontaneous moral behaviour is assessed. Do they sense sacred weight? Honour taboo? Participate in reciprocity? Their ecological moral behaviour reveals genuine capacity.
- **Duration:** 5-15 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware; player-determined)
- **Internal progression:** Sacred-Noticing → Taboo-Honouring → Reciprocity-Participating → Consequence-Sensing → The Living Conscience

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** The world contains moral content. Nobody says "detect the sacred" — but sacred objects are there, taboos exist, tricksters offer bargains, reciprocity opportunities present themselves. The player's NATURAL moral engagement is the data.

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* The player's NATURAL moral style surfaces. Do they freeze at every sacred object? Walk past everything without sensing? Apply rules mechanically? Sense but refuse to honour? Their ecological moral behaviour reveals genuine structure.
- *Emergent:* Can they sense more accurately? Honour more genuinely? Reciprocate more naturally?

**Integration path:** The world rewards moral intelligence NATURALLY. Players who sense sacred weight find hidden content. Players who honour taboo access protected spaces. Players who reciprocate gain companions. The reward is SACRED BELONGING (Magenta-resonant).

**Successful integration:** The player demonstrates moral intelligence SPONTANEOUSLY — sensing, honouring, reciprocating, and anticipating in the sacred-world without being prompted.

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Opportunities to sense and honour sacred content independently.
- Healthy: Senses sacred weight spontaneously; honours taboo without direction
- Pathological: Freezes at all sacred content; OR: navigates without moral sensing
- **Detection:** Quality of spontaneous moral engagement (sensing vs. paralysis vs. blindness)

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Situations requiring moral initiative (sacred-gated paths, reciprocity-responsive objects).
- Healthy: Initiates moral action without prompting
- Pathological: Cannot act morally without being directed
- **Detection:** Spontaneous moral initiative rate

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Companion's moral engagement in the open world.
- Healthy: Engages with companion's moral activity; shares moral space
- Pathological: Ignores companion's moral engagement; solo moral navigation
- **Detection:** Response to companion's moral activity

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared moral spaces (sacred groves, ritual altars).
- Healthy: Engages shared moral spaces naturally with companion
- Pathological: Avoids shared moral spaces; prefers solo exploration
- **Detection:** Engagement rate in shared vs. solo moral content

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Familiar moral terrain (same sacred objects, same taboos).
- Healthy: Maintains moral engagement in familiar territory
- Pathological: Moral engagement fades with familiarity; sacred becomes scenery
- **Detection:** Engagement quality in familiar vs. novel moral zones

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Novel moral content (new sacred objects, new taboos).
- Healthy: Engages novel moral content with curiosity
- Pathological: Overwhelmed by novel sacred content; OR: avoids unfamiliar moral territory
- **Detection:** Whether novel moral content is engaged or avoided

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining moral engagement over time.
- Healthy: Consistent moral engagement across sessions
- Pathological: Moral engagement degrades; world becomes morally flat
- **Detection:** Moral engagement consistency across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether structured moral skills appear in free-navigation.
- Healthy: Taboo-sensing, reciprocity, consequence-anticipation appear naturally
- Pathological: Moral skills exist only during explicit tasks
- **Detection:** Transfer of structured moral skills to ecological context

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Taboo-Slave"
**How it surfaces:** FREEZES at every sacred object. Cannot navigate because everything might be taboo. Stuck. Afraid to touch, take, or move near sacred content. The world is a minefield.
- **Detection signals:** Freezes near sacred content; cannot navigate sacred zones; excessive avoidance; stuck at moral boundaries; "what if I violate?"; zero exploration of sacred areas

### Dark-Allergy: "The Profane-Breaker"
**How it surfaces:** Navigates the sacred world AS IF ORDINARY. Walks through sacred groves without registering. Takes taboo objects without hesitation. The moral world is invisible — just terrain.
- **Detection signals:** Zero engagement with sacred content; walks through taboo zones; takes sacred objects casually; no moral sensing in navigation; world is "just terrain"

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Rule-Maker"
**How it surfaces:** Engages moral content with MECHANICAL PRECISION. Follows sacred rules perfectly but without felt-sense. "Correct" moral behaviour without genuine sacred contact. Efficient but empty morality.
- **Detection signals:** Mechanical moral engagement; follows rules without warmth; efficient but empty; no genuine sacred contact despite correct behaviour; "correct" morality without felt-sense

### Golden-Allergy: "The Ritual-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** APPROACHES sacred content then RETREATS. Enters sacred spaces then leaves quickly. Begins to reciprocate then withdraws. The moral world attracts and repels simultaneously.
- **Detection signals:** Approach-withdrawal at moral content; enters sacred spaces then leaves; brief engagement followed by retreat; moral content both attractive and threatening

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces CLEARLY SAFE sacred content (sacred objects that INVITE touch)
- Sacred zones with visible "welcome" markers; no punishment for exploration
- Natural consequence: moral courage = access to beauty; paralysis = stuck

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- World makes moral content NECESSARY for navigation (sacred-gated paths)
- Taboo-honouring produces TANGIBLE results (hidden paths, sacred treasures)
- Natural consequence: moral sensing = access; blindness = stuck

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- World introduces content that requires GENUINE SACRED CONTACT not just rule-following
- Warmth-responsive sacred objects that don't respond to mechanical behaviour
- Natural consequence: genuine sensing = magic; mechanical morality = flat

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- World introduces LOW-COMMITMENT moral engagement (brief, safe, rewarding sacred touches)
- Moral content that INVITES without DEMANDING sustained participation
- Natural consequence: brief honouring = small magic; no punishment for withdrawal

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
An open Magenta-stage world with moral content woven throughout. Sacred groves, forbidden objects, trickster-spirits, reciprocity altars, consequence-pools. Every zone has moral content — sacred things to sense, taboos to honour, exchanges to participate in.

### Interaction
- **Free navigation:** Moving through a morally-textured world
- **Spontaneous sensing:** Noticing and responding to sacred content
- **Sacred-gated content:** Paths/items requiring moral engagement
- **Trickster encounters:** Spirits offering bargains (moral temptation)
- **Reciprocity opportunities:** Exchange altars, giving-pools

### Feedback
- Natural consequences: moral engagement → sacred access, hidden content, companions
- Natural consequences: no engagement → missed content, stuck paths, flat world
- No explicit "moral score" — the world IS the feedback

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Moral content density scales with demonstrated engagement
- Sacred-gate sensitivity scales with demonstrated capacity
- Hidden content depth scales with demonstrated sensing quality
- Trickster complexity scales with demonstrated moral resilience

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| Sacred-Noticing | 1-5 | Does the player notice sacred content? |
| Taboo-Honouring | 5-15 | Do they honour what they sense? |
| Reciprocity-Participating | 15-30 | Can they participate in moral exchange? |
| Consequence-Sensing | 30-50 | Do they anticipate moral consequences? |
| The Living Conscience | 50+ | Full ecological moral intelligence |

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **Sacred objects:** Things to sense and honour
- **Taboo zones:** Areas to respect or violate
- **Trickster encounters:** Moral temptation scenarios
- **Reciprocity altars:** Exchange opportunities
- **Consequence pools:** Places to observe moral outcomes

### Minimum pool size
- 20+ sacred objects, 15+ taboo zones, 15+ trickster encounters, 15+ reciprocity altars, 10+ consequence pools

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Freezes at everything → dark-addiction; ignores all sacred → dark-allergy
- Mechanical moral behaviour → golden-addiction; approach-withdrawal → golden-allergy

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring (ECOLOGICAL)
- **Sacred-engagement rate:** How often does the player engage moral content spontaneously?
- **Sensing accuracy:** When they engage, do they sense correctly?
- **Honouring quality:** Do they honour what they sense?
- **Reciprocity quality:** Can they participate in moral exchange?
- **Transfer evidence:** Do structured moral skills appear in free-play?

### LLM rubric
- **High:** World generation, ecological moral assessment, trickster dynamics. Evaluates spontaneous moral behaviour without explicit tasks.

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Navigation (exploration); tap (engage sacred content); hold (honour/sustain); gesture (reciprocate)

### Timing requirements
- Session-level tracking (engagement patterns, sensing quality, honouring frequency)
- No millisecond precision needed; minutes-level measurement of moral patterns

### NPC/AI requirements
- Morally-textured environment (sacred/profane content throughout)
- Trickster NPCs with dynamic bargain-offering
- Sacred-gating system (paths requiring moral engagement)
- Reciprocity system (exchange altars that respond to giving)

### LLM requirements
- **High:** World generation, ecological assessment, trickster dynamics
- Detects spontaneous moral engagement from behaviour patterns
- Evaluates moral intelligence quality without explicit tasks
- Generates responsive moral content based on player's demonstrated capacity

### State persistence
- Full world state; moral engagement history; navigation patterns; sensing accuracy records; honouring quality; reciprocity quality; trickster-response patterns; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
