# Moral / Magenta — Social-Cooperative Game Concept

> **Axis:** The social-cooperative axis probes moral intelligence through SHARED MORAL ACTION — can the player participate in moral reciprocity with another? At Magenta, this is proto-fairness enacted: "I give, you give. I honour, you honour. We maintain the sacred together." Shared ritual as moral cooperation.
>
> **Why this axis for Moral/Magenta:** Before negotiated fairness (Red+) or rule-based cooperation (Amber), moral cooperation is RITUAL — shared sacred action. "We do this together because the spirits require it." This modality tests whether the player can participate in shared moral action through felt reciprocity.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Shared-Sacred"
- **Core mechanic:** The player and a companion must maintain sacred balance TOGETHER. Taking requires giving. Honouring requires shared action. Neither can maintain the sacred alone — it requires BOTH. Proto-fairness through shared ritual.
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Exchange → The Giving-Back → The Shared Ritual → The Balance-Keepers → The Sacred Circle

---

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst:** "The sacred requires both of you. You take — you must give back. Your companion takes — they must give back. Together you keep the balance. Can you maintain the sacred together?" The contact boundary is: "Can you participate in shared moral action?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player cooperate morally? The Taboo-Slave is paralysed (afraid to take OR give — might violate). The Profane-Breaker takes without giving (no reciprocity sensed). The Premature Rule-Maker follows exchange rules mechanically. The Ritual-Refuser senses the reciprocity but won't participate.
- *Emergent:* Can they participate in genuine moral reciprocity — taking AND giving in felt balance?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE MORAL RECIPROCITY — participating in shared sacred action with felt balance. Not paralysed, not exploitative, not mechanical, not refusing.

**Successful integration:** The player participates in shared moral action — the foundation of all moral cooperation.

---

## 3. Game Design

### Setup
The Shared-Sacred: a moral cooperation space. Player and companion maintain sacred balance through exchange. Taking creates obligation; giving fulfils it. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage altar between two figures — a shared sacred space that glows when balanced and dims when imbalanced.

### Interaction
- **The First Exchange (1-5):** Companion gives → player gives back. Simple reciprocity.
- **The Giving-Back (5-15):** Player takes → must give back. Obligation sensing.
- **The Shared Ritual (15-30):** Coordinated giving → both contribute to shared sacred action.
- **The Balance-Keepers (30-50):** Maintain balance over time → ongoing reciprocity.
- **The Sacred Circle (50+):** Full moral cooperation: initiate + receive + reciprocate + co-create + maintain.

### Feedback
- Genuine reciprocity → "Balance! You gave and received. The sacred is maintained. Together."
- Paralysis → "Safe. Just give this one thing. Nothing bad happens. Any giving is good."
- Exploitation → "They gave. Feel that? Something wants to go back. Give something. Balance."
- Mechanical → "Not counting. Does it FEEL balanced? Trust the feeling, not the number."
- Refusal → "Just once. One exchange. See how it feels. The sacred asks so little."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Exchange complexity: simple (give/receive) → moderate (proportional) → complex (multi-party)
- Obligation clarity: obvious → moderate → subtle
- Balance precision: loose → moderate → precise
- Duration: single exchange → sustained reciprocity
- Novelty: familiar exchanges → novel moral cooperation

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Exchange | 1-5 | Simple give/receive reciprocity |
| The Giving-Back | 5-15 | Obligation sensing (taking → giving) |
| The Shared Ritual | 15-30 | Coordinated shared moral action |
| The Balance-Keepers | 30-50 | Sustained reciprocity over time |
| The Sacred Circle | 50+ | Full moral cooperation integration |

---

## 4. Item Pool

### Item types
- **Simple exchanges:** Clear give/receive pairs
- **Proportional exchanges:** Different values requiring felt-balance
- **Shared rituals:** Coordinated actions requiring both participants
- **Sustained exchanges:** Ongoing reciprocity over multiple rounds
- **Novel exchanges:** Unfamiliar moral cooperation contexts

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ simple, 20+ proportional, 15+ shared rituals, 15+ sustained, 10+ novel

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Paralysis in exchange → dark-addiction; takes without giving → dark-allergy
- Mechanical counting → golden-addiction; sensing + refusing → golden-allergy

---

## 5. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Tap-to-give (offer item); tap-to-receive (accept item); hold (sustain shared action); drag (exchange)

### Timing requirements
- Exchange timing tracked; reciprocity latency; obligation-response time; no time pressure

### NPC/AI requirements
- Companion with exchange behaviour (gives, receives, responds to imbalance)
- Balance system (tracks give/receive ratio)
- Obligation system (taking creates visible obligation)
- Shared-glow visualization (balance indicator)

### LLM requirements
- **Low:** Primarily algorithmic balance tracking. LLM for felt-sense assessment and ecological context only.

### State persistence
- Reciprocity balance scores; felt-sense indicators; initiation rates; sustainability quality; mutuality capacity; paralysis indicators; exploitation indicators; mechanical indicators; refusal indicators; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
