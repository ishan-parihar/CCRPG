# Moral / Magenta — Language-Reflective Game Concept

> **Axis:** The language-reflective axis probes moral intelligence through WORDS — can the player name sacred weight? At Magenta, this is: "What do you call it when something matters? When something is forbidden? When something must be honoured?" Finding words for moral sensing.
>
> **Why this axis for Moral/Magenta:** Before moral reasoning (Red+), there must be moral VOCABULARY — words for sacred, forbidden, heavy, light, right, wrong at the felt-sense level. This modality tests whether the player can articulate moral weight in language.

---

## 1. Game Identity

- **Title:** "The Sacred-Word"
- **Core mechanic:** The player encounters sacred/profane content and must find WORDS for the moral weight they sense. Not arguments or reasoning — just naming. "What do you call this feeling? This weight? This 'must not'?" The birth of moral vocabulary.
- **Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session (infinite checkpoints; fatigue-aware)
- **Internal progression:** The First Forbidden → The Weight-Name → The Sacred-Story → The Shared Sacred-Word → The Speaking Conscience

---

## 2. The Catalyst→Experience→Integration Flow

**Catalyst:** "You feel something. This thing has weight. What do you CALL that? What word is it? Not why — just what do you call the feeling of 'this matters'?" The contact boundary is: "Can you name moral weight?"

**Unconscious response:**
- *Submergent:* How does the player name moral weight? The Taboo-Slave uses FEAR words ("dangerous," "forbidden," "don't touch"). The Profane-Breaker has NO moral vocabulary ("it's just a thing"). The Premature Rule-Maker uses RULE words ("wrong," "not allowed," "against the rules"). The Ritual-Refuser names accurately but flatly ("sacred, I guess").
- *Emergent:* Can they find genuine felt-sense moral vocabulary — words that carry the weight they describe?

**Integration path:** Rewards GENUINE MORAL VOCABULARY — words that carry felt-sense weight. Not fear-words, not blank, not rule-words, not flat naming.

**Successful integration:** The player has living moral vocabulary — words that carry the sacred weight they describe.

---

## 3. Drive Probing (All 4 × Both Domains)

### Agency Dark-Domain
**Context:** Finding moral words independently.
- Healthy: Generates own moral vocabulary; trusts own naming
- Pathological: Only fear-words (Taboo-Slave); OR: no moral words (Profane-Breaker)
- **Detection:** Whether moral vocabulary is self-generated and varied

### Agency Golden-Domain
**Context:** Naming moral weight under ambiguity.
- Healthy: Offers tentative moral words for ambiguous content
- Pathological: Cannot name without certainty; moral vocabulary freezes
- **Detection:** Moral naming quality under ambiguity

### Communion Dark-Domain
**Context:** Naming when companion names differently.
- Healthy: Maintains own moral vocabulary while hearing companion's
- Pathological: Adopts companion's moral words; loses own naming
- **Detection:** Whether own moral vocabulary survives social influence

### Communion Golden-Domain
**Context:** Shared moral naming (building vocabulary together).
- Healthy: Contributes to shared moral vocabulary AND receives companion's
- Pathological: Cannot share moral words; naming is private
- **Detection:** Quality of shared moral vocabulary building

### Eros Dark-Domain
**Context:** Naming familiar moral content (same sacred items).
- Healthy: Maintains genuine moral vocabulary for familiar content
- Pathological: Moral words become rote; lose genuine weight
- **Detection:** Whether familiar moral naming retains genuine quality

### Eros Golden-Domain
**Context:** Naming novel moral content (unfamiliar sacred weight).
- Healthy: Finds new moral words for novel content with curiosity
- Pathological: Cannot name novel moral weight; OR: forces familiar words
- **Detection:** Novel moral naming quality

### Agape Dark-Domain
**Context:** Maintaining moral vocabulary over time.
- Healthy: Moral vocabulary remains consistent and alive across sessions
- Pathological: Moral words degrade; naming becomes random or empty
- **Detection:** Moral vocabulary quality across sessions

### Agape Golden-Domain
**Context:** Whether moral naming appears spontaneously.
- Healthy: Names moral weight naturally in non-moral tasks
- Pathological: Moral vocabulary exists only during explicit moral tasks
- **Detection:** Spontaneous moral naming

## 4. Shadow Surfacing (All 4 Quadrants)

### Dark-Addiction: "The Taboo-Slave"
**How it surfaces:** Uses FEAR-BASED moral vocabulary. "Dangerous." "Forbidden." "Don't touch." "Bad." All moral words are threat-words. Cannot name sacred weight without fear. Moral vocabulary = danger vocabulary.
- **Detection signals:** Fear-words dominate; "dangerous," "forbidden," "scary"; no positive sacred words; moral = threat; cannot name "good sacred" only "bad taboo"

### Dark-Allergy: "The Profane-Breaker"
**How it surfaces:** HAS NO MORAL VOCABULARY. "It's just a thing." "I don't know what to call it." Cannot find words for sacred weight because no weight is felt. Moral vocabulary = empty.
- **Detection signals:** "I don't know"; "it's just..."; no moral words available; blank naming; cannot generate any sacred/profane vocabulary; "nothing" responses

### Golden-Addiction: "The Premature Rule-Maker"
**How it surfaces:** Uses RULE-BASED vocabulary. "Wrong." "Not allowed." "Against the rules." "Should/shouldn't." Moral words are prescriptive, not felt. Vocabulary of obligation without weight.
- **Detection signals:** Rule-words; "wrong," "not allowed," "should"; prescriptive language; no felt-sense words; "the rule says" rather than "I feel"; obligation without weight

### Golden-Allergy: "The Ritual-Refuser"
**How it surfaces:** Names ACCURATELY but FLATLY. "Yeah, sacred." "Taboo, I guess." Correct moral vocabulary without engagement. Words without weight. Naming without caring.
- **Detection signals:** Accurate but flat; "I guess"; no engagement in naming; correct words without felt-weight; naming as performance not sensing; "whatever" quality

---

## 5. Healing/Evolution Mechanics

### Heal/Evolve Vector (Agape + Agency for dark shadows)

**When Dark-Addiction detected:**
- Introduce POSITIVE sacred vocabulary: "Not dangerous. PRECIOUS. SPECIAL. HONOURED. What word feels right?"
- Build non-fear moral words; reward positive sacred naming
- Frame: "Not all sacred is scary. Some sacred is beautiful. Precious. What word fits THIS one?"
- Build: balanced moral vocabulary (sacred includes beauty, not just fear)

**When Dark-Allergy detected:**
- Start with WEIGHT words: "Heavy or light? Just that. Does this one feel heavy or light?"
- Make moral vocabulary SIMPLE; accept minimal weight-words
- Frame: "Not a big word. Just: heavy or light? This one. Does it have weight? Just that."
- Reward ANY weight-word; celebrate first moral naming

### Evolve/Heal Vector (Eros + Communion for golden shadows)

**When Golden-Addiction detected:**
- Require FELT words: "Not 'wrong.' How does it FEEL? What word describes the FEELING of this weight?"
- Block rule-words; reward felt-sense vocabulary
- Frame: "Not what the rule says. What YOU feel. What word lives in your body for this weight?"
- Build: genuine moral vocabulary (felt not prescribed)

**When Golden-Allergy detected:**
- Make naming MATTER: "You said 'sacred.' Say it like you MEAN it. Like it matters. Because it does."
- Connect words to engagement; naming as honouring
- Frame: "Your word has power. When you name it, you honour it. Say it with weight. Mean it."
- Build: engaged moral naming (words that carry the weight they describe)

## 6. Game Design

### Setup
The Sacred-Word: a moral vocabulary space. Sacred/profane content is presented and the player must find words for the moral weight they sense. The aesthetic: Magenta-stage naming-ritual — words glow when they carry genuine weight; flat words fade.

### Interaction
- **The First Forbidden (1-5):** Clearly sacred item → "What do you call this? One word." Simple naming.
- **The Weight-Name (5-15):** Multiple items → find different words for different weights.
- **The Sacred-Story (15-30):** String words together → "Tell the weight. What is this? Why does it matter?"
- **The Shared Sacred-Word (30-50):** Compare moral vocabulary with companion → shared naming.
- **The Speaking Conscience (50+):** Full moral vocabulary: name + differentiate + share + create.

### Feedback
- Genuine moral word → "That word carries weight! You named it. The sacred has a word now."
- Fear-word → "Not dangerous. Something else. What ELSE could you call it? Something precious?"
- Blank → "Heavy or light? Just that. One word. Does it have weight?"
- Rule-word → "Not 'wrong.' How does it FEEL? What word lives in your feeling?"
- Flat → "Say it again. Like you mean it. That word has power. Let it carry weight."

### Difficulty Adaptation
- Sacred clarity: obvious weight → moderate → subtle
- Vocabulary demand: single word → multiple words → sentences
- Novelty: familiar sacred content → novel sacred weight
- Sharing: solo naming → shared vocabulary building
- Engagement depth: naming → meaning → honouring

### Internal Progression
| Phase | Checkpoint range | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| The First Forbidden | 1-5 | Single-word naming (high-contrast sacred) |
| The Weight-Name | 5-15 | Differentiated vocabulary (different weights) |
| The Sacred-Story | 15-30 | Connected moral language (sentences) |
| The Shared Sacred-Word | 30-50 | Shared moral vocabulary with companion |
| The Speaking Conscience | 50+ | Full moral vocabulary integration |

---

## 7. Item Pool Specification

### Item types
- **High-weight items:** Obviously sacred (require naming)
- **Low-weight items:** Obviously ordinary (require "ordinary" naming)
- **Differentiated items:** Different KINDS of sacred weight (precious vs. forbidden vs. honoured)
- **Novel items:** Unfamiliar sacred content requiring new vocabulary
- **Shared items:** Items for comparative naming with companion

### Minimum pool size
- 25+ high-weight, 20+ low-weight, 20+ differentiated, 15+ novel, 10+ shared

### Drive/shadow mapping
- Fear-vocabulary → dark-addiction; no vocabulary → dark-allergy
- Rule-vocabulary → golden-addiction; flat/disengaged naming → golden-allergy

---

## 8. Scoring Rubric

### Capacity scoring
- **Vocabulary range:** How many different moral words can they generate?
- **Felt-sense quality:** Do words carry weight (not just labels)?
- **Differentiation:** Different words for different weights?
- **Engagement:** Do they care about their naming?
- **Novelty:** Can they name unfamiliar moral weight?

### LLM rubric
- **Very High:** Core moral vocabulary assessment requires LLM. Evaluates felt-sense vs. rule-based language, vocabulary richness, engagement quality, fear-word detection, flat-naming detection.

---

## 9. Technical Requirements

### Input types
- Voice/text (moral word generation); select-from-options (for younger players); drag-word-to-item (matching)

### Timing requirements
- Response latency tracked; no time pressure; vocabulary richness measured over time

### NPC/AI requirements
- Sacred content generation (items with varying moral weight)
- Vocabulary evaluation system (felt-sense vs. rule-based vs. fear-based)
- Companion for shared vocabulary building

### LLM requirements
- **Very High:** Evaluates moral vocabulary quality, felt-sense indicators, fear-word patterns, rule-word patterns, flat-naming patterns, engagement quality, vocabulary richness, novel naming capacity.

### State persistence
- Moral vocabulary inventory; felt-sense quality scores; differentiation capacity; engagement indicators; fear-word rates; blank rates; rule-word rates; flat-naming rates; drive/shadow signals; fatigue state; checkpoint position
