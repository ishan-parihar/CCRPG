# Moral / Green — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as site of moral feeling — embodied moral intuition and somatic markers of contextual ethical sensitivity.  **Why this axis for this module:** Green moral capacity is not purely cognitive; genuine care-justice integration registers somatically. The body knows before the mind articulates.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Moral Body
**Core loop:** Player engages with moral scenarios through somatic channels — breath rhythm, body-tension mapping, felt-sense reporting, and movement responses. The game measures whether moral engagement produces embodied resonance or somatic dissociation/rigidity.
**Session length:** 5–8 minutes (3–5 somatic-moral trials per session).
**Infinite checkpoint:** Exit between any trial; somatic state resets naturally.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Green moral — embodied contextual ethics.
**Shadow provocation:** DA surfaces as somatic flatness — no embodied response to moral scenarios (dissociation from ethical feeling). DAll surfaces as somatic rigidity — tension/bracing patterns that don't shift with contextual changes. GA surfaces as performed somatic responses that don't match physiological indicators. GAll surfaces as embodied discomfort specifically when moral ranking is introduced.
**Drive probing:** Eros — somatic opening toward unfamiliar moral territory. Agape — somatic grounding in care-feeling. Agency — somatic commitment (forward lean, breath deepening). Communion — somatic resonance with others' moral experience.
**Integration path:** Diagnosis (baseline somatic-moral responsiveness) → Healing (somatic practices that reconnect moral feeling to body) → Evolution (somatic integration of care-justice tension as felt wholeness).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic:** Each trial: (a) Brief moral vignette presented (audio + visual, 15–30s). (b) Player reports felt-sense via body-map interface (tap areas of activation/tension/openness). (c) Breath-rhythm task during moral reflection (breath pattern recorded). (d) Movement response — player makes a gesture-choice (swipe direction, hold duration) that expresses moral orientation.
**Adaptive difficulty:** Early trials use emotionally clear scenarios; later trials introduce care-justice tension that produces genuine somatic ambivalence. Advanced trials require the player to hold somatic tension without resolving it prematurely.
**Somatic markers tracked:** (a) Body-map activation patterns across moral domains, (b) breath coherence during ethical reflection, (c) gesture-choice latency and commitment, (d) somatic consistency across similar scenarios.
**Shadow-specific probes:** DA — scenarios that should produce strong somatic response (harm to vulnerable); flatness = shadow. DAll — contextual shifts that should change somatic pattern; rigidity = shadow.

## 4. Item Pool

**Pool structure:** 120+ somatic-moral vignettes. Each vignette tagged with: expected somatic activation zone, care-justice orientation, contextual complexity, shadow-provocation target.
**Vignette types:** (a) Care-activating — harm to vulnerable, relational betrayal. (b) Justice-activating — unfairness, rights violation. (c) Tension-holding — care and justice in genuine conflict. (d) Context-shifting — same act, different somatic meaning in different context.
**Body-map regions:** Heart/chest (care), gut (justice/conviction), throat (voice/commitment), shoulders (burden/responsibility), hands (agency/action), whole-body (integration).
**Rotation:** Vignettes cycle across domains; no repeat within 14 days.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Body-map touch interface (anatomical silhouette, tap to mark activation). Breath tracking via microphone (rhythm only, no content). Gesture input via swipe/hold mechanics.
**Timing:** Vignette presentation is timed; response windows are generous (somatic awareness requires space). No rush.
**Telemetry:** Body-map activation vectors, breath coherence scores, gesture latency/direction/duration, cross-trial somatic consistency, shadow-flag activations.
**Adaptive engine:** Somatic-pattern analysis across 5+ trials detects shadow orientation. Vignette selection targets somatic zones showing flatness or rigidity. Breath-coherence thresholds adapt to player's baseline.
**Accessibility:** Body-map interface scalable; alternative input for motor impairment (verbal body-report); no reliance on breath-tracking for core scoring (optional enrichment data).
