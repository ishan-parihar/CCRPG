# Moral / Green — Immersive-RPG Game Concept

> **Axis:** Ecological contextual ethics — whether care-justice integration appears spontaneously in free-play moral behaviour.  **Why this axis for this module:** The ultimate test of Green moral capacity is whether contextual ethics emerge naturally in unstructured situations, not just when explicitly prompted.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Name:** The Living Village
**Core loop:** Player inhabits a persistent village-world populated by NPCs with genuine moral lives — relationships, conflicts, needs, injustices. No explicit moral prompts. The player's spontaneous choices — who they help, how they intervene, what they ignore, how they navigate competing claims — reveal their moral ecology in action.
**Session length:** 8–15 minutes (open-ended exploration per session).
**Infinite checkpoint:** World-state saves continuously; exit anytime.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Green moral — spontaneous contextual ethics.
**Shadow provocation:** DA surfaces as moral passivity — player observes conflicts without engaging, or engages only superficially ("everyone has a point"). DAll surfaces as principled intervention that ignores context — applying the same moral logic regardless of relational dynamics. GA surfaces as performed moral sophistication — player narrates their own moral development rather than acting. GAll surfaces as refusal to intervene in situations where one party is clearly causing harm (refuses moral ranking).
**Drive probing:** Eros — NPCs who invite the player toward moral growth. Agape — NPCs who need the player to return to embodied care. Agency — situations requiring the player to take a moral stand. Communion — situations requiring the player to join another's moral world.
**Integration path:** Diagnosis (baseline spontaneous moral behaviour) → Healing (world-events that make shadow-patterns visible through consequences) → Evolution (situations requiring genuine care-justice synthesis without prompting).

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanic:** Open-world village with 15–20 NPCs in ongoing relational dynamics. NPCs have: needs, relationships, conflicts, moral frameworks, and developmental arcs. Player interacts freely — talk, help, trade, mediate, ignore, challenge. No quest markers for moral content. Moral situations emerge organically from NPC dynamics.
**Adaptive difficulty:** Early sessions: clear moral situations (someone being harmed, obvious injustice). Later sessions: ambiguous situations where care and justice conflict, cultural differences between NPC groups, systemic issues without clear individual solutions.
**Ecological scoring:** System tracks player's moral behaviour across dimensions: (a) engagement breadth (how many NPCs' moral lives does player notice?), (b) contextual sensitivity (does player's approach shift with context?), (c) commitment depth (does player follow through?), (d) care-justice integration (does player honour both in action?).
**Shadow emergence:** World-events are seeded based on detected shadow — DA players encounter situations demanding commitment; DAll players encounter situations demanding contextual flexibility. Events feel organic, never didactic.

## 4. Item Pool

**Pool structure:** 200+ NPC interaction scripts, 50+ emergent moral situations, 30+ systemic dynamics that unfold over multiple sessions.
**NPC archetypes:** Care-oriented elder, justice-focused leader, contextual pragmatist, principled outsider, vulnerable dependent, moral provocateur, shadow-mirror (adapts to player's pattern).
**Situation types:** (a) Interpersonal harm — one NPC hurting another. (b) Systemic injustice — structural unfairness. (c) Cultural tension — different moral frameworks in conflict. (d) Care-overload — too many legitimate needs for one person. (e) Developmental invitation — NPC growing, needing support.
**World dynamics:** NPC relationships evolve based on player's (in)action. Neglected conflicts escalate. Resolved conflicts create new dynamics. The village is alive.

## 5. Technical Requirements

**Input:** Standard RPG interaction — move, talk, select dialogue options, perform actions. Touch/keyboard/controller compatible.
**Timing:** No time pressure. World evolves between sessions (NPC dynamics advance). Player sets their own pace.
**Telemetry:** Interaction logs (who, what, when, how), moral-behaviour dimensional scores (computed from action patterns, not self-report), shadow-flag activations, NPC-relationship trajectories, spontaneous vs. prompted moral engagement ratio.
**Adaptive engine:** Behavioural-pattern analysis across 3+ sessions detects shadow orientation. World-event seeding targets detected shadow. NPC dialogue adapts to player's moral framework. Shadow-mirror NPC calibrates to player's specific pattern.
**Accessibility:** Full keyboard navigation; NPC dialogue as text with optional audio; no reliance on spatial awareness for moral content; adjustable world-pace.
