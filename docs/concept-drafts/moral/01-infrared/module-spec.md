## 2. Developmental Thesis

At Infrared, morality does not exist. There are no rules, principles, or judgements. The only proto-moral capacity is HARM-SENSITIVITY: can the organism detect that something hurts? Can it distinguish painful from non-painful? This is pure sensorimotor discrimination — the biological seed from which all later moral development grows. "Eat or be eaten" is the entire moral universe.

## 3. Shadow Taxonomy

| Quadrant | Name | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Dark-Addiction | Pain-Fixation | Cannot stop attending to harm; hypervigilant to threat; sees danger everywhere; cannot distinguish safe from unsafe |
| Dark-Allergy | Pain-Numbness | Cannot detect harm; dissociated from pain signals; walks into danger without registering it |
| Golden-Addiction | Premature Taboo | Attempts magical-consequence thinking without genuine harm-sensitivity; 'knows' things are wrong without feeling why |
| Golden-Allergy | Harm Fortress | Harm-sensitivity present but refuses moral emergence; terrified of 'should'; 'it hurts but so what?' |
