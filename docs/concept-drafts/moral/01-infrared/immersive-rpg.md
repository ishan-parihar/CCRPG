# Moral × Infrared — Immersive-RPG

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Immersive-RPG |
| Unique lateral | Ecological harm-sensitivity — whether harm-detection appears spontaneously in free-play |
| Core mechanic | Open exploration where harm-sources are embedded naturally; no explicit instructions |
| Session length | 90–180 seconds per checkpoint |
| Infinite checkpoint | Yes — each exploration zone is a checkpoint |

## 2. Catalyst Design

### 2.1 Surface Experience

A small primordial environment — tide pools, volcanic vents, thorned undergrowth. The player's creature explores freely. No instructions say "avoid harm." Harm-sources exist naturally (hot vents, sharp coral, toxic pools). The game observes: does the creature spontaneously detect and avoid harm without being told to? This is ecological validity — harm-sensitivity in context, not in a test.

### 2.2 Environment Parameters

- **Harm-sources:** thermal vents (heat-pulse), sharp surfaces (jagged visual), toxic zones (colour-shift)
- **Safe zones:** cool pools, smooth surfaces, sheltered alcoves
- **Neutral zones:** neither harmful nor explicitly safe
- **Density:** harm-sources occupy 25–40% of explorable space
- **Feedback:** subtle (creature flinches on harm-contact) not punitive

### 2.3 Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Does the creature explore toward increasingly subtle harm-boundaries (curiosity about edges)? |
| Agape | Does the creature return to safe zones after harm-contact (self-soothing)? |
| Agency | Does the creature actively navigate away from harm (not just freeze)? |
| Communion | Does the creature register environmental harm-cues before contact (anticipatory avoidance)? |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Behavioural signature |
|---|---|
| Pain-Fixation (DA) | Creature stays in one safe zone; never explores; treats entire environment as threat |
| Pain-Numbness (DAll) | Creature wanders through harm-sources repeatedly without altering path |
| Premature Taboo (GA) | Creature avoids entire categories (all water, all red things) rather than actual harm-sources |
| Harm Fortress (GAll) | Creature pauses at harm-boundaries (detects them) but enters anyway; no avoidance behaviour |

## 4. Integration Mechanics

### 4.1 Heal Vector (bottom-up: Agape + Agency)

- **Pain-Fixation:** Expand safe zones; place rewards at safe-zone edges; build trust in exploration
- **Pain-Numbness:** Increase harm-feedback intensity; make harm-contact more salient; rebuild ecological awareness

### 4.2 Evolve Vector (top-down: Eros + Communion)

- **Premature Taboo:** Place rewards inside "feared categories" that are actually safe; break category-avoidance; ground in actual sensation
- **Harm Fortress:** Make lingering in harm-zones progressively costly; reward the avoidance-action itself

## 5. Progression & Difficulty

| Level | Parameters |
|---|---|
| Entry | Small environment, obvious harm-sources, high safe-zone density |
| Mid | Larger environment, moderate harm-cues, harm/safe boundaries less distinct |
| Advanced | Complex environment, subtle cues, harm-sources shift location slowly |
| Shadow-mode | Environment seeded to trigger dominant shadow (e.g., all-safe for DA, all-harm for DAll) |

### 5.1 Theta-Decay Triggers

- 3+ days: environment complexity regresses one sub-level
- 7+ days: spontaneous-avoidance baseline resets
- Decay capped at 25%

### 5.2 Mastery Criteria

- Spontaneous harm-avoidance rate ≥ 80% (avoids without instruction)
- Exploration coverage ≥ 70% of safe zones (not frozen in one spot)
- Harm-contact rate declining across sessions
- Category-avoidance absent (explores safe instances of "feared" categories)
- All four shadow signatures below threshold
