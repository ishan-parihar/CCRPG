# Moral × Infrared — Embodied-Somatic

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Embodied-Somatic |
| Unique lateral | Body as harm-detector — where pain registers, withdrawal reflexes, protective postures |
| Core mechanic | Localising harm in the body-map and executing protective withdrawal |
| Session length | 45–90 seconds per checkpoint |
| Infinite checkpoint | Yes — each body-scan cycle is a checkpoint |

## 2. Catalyst Design

### 2.1 Surface Experience

A creature-avatar has a visible body-map. Harm-stimuli strike different body regions (indicated by flash, vibration-pattern, or audio-spatial cue). The player must tap WHERE the harm landed and execute the correct withdrawal direction (pull limb away from source). The body learns: harm has location, and location demands directional response.

### 2.2 Stimulus Parameters

- **Harm locations:** 6 body zones (head, torso, left-arm, right-arm, left-leg, right-leg)
- **Harm types:** sharp (quick tap), burning (sustained pulse), crushing (heavy vibration)
- **Withdrawal directions:** away from harm-source (left, right, up, down, curl inward)
- **Intensity grades:** mild (brief flash), moderate (sustained), severe (repeated pulse)

### 2.3 Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Can the player discriminate harm-type (sharp vs burning vs crushing)? |
| Agape | Does the player attend to all body zones equally (not neglect any)? |
| Agency | Speed and accuracy of withdrawal execution |
| Communion | Does the player register harm-intensity proportionally (graded response)? |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Behavioural signature |
|---|---|
| Pain-Fixation (DA) | Reports harm in zones that weren't struck; phantom-pain responses |
| Pain-Numbness (DAll) | Fails to localise harm; no withdrawal response; flat body-map |
| Premature Taboo (GA) | Withdraws before stimulus arrives (anticipatory flinch based on pattern, not sensation) |
| Harm Fortress (GAll) | Localises accurately but does not withdraw; body registers but won't protect |

## 4. Integration Mechanics

### 4.1 Heal Vector (bottom-up: Agape + Agency)

- **Pain-Fixation:** Reduce stimulus density; confirm "no harm here" for unstruck zones; rebuild body-trust
- **Pain-Numbness:** Increase stimulus salience; pair with multi-modal cues; rebuild zone-awareness

### 4.2 Evolve Vector (top-down: Eros + Communion)

- **Premature Taboo:** Randomise stimulus timing; make anticipation costly (false-withdrawal penalty); ground in actual sensation
- **Harm Fortress:** Make non-withdrawal accumulate damage; reward protective action; dissolve freeze

## 5. Progression & Difficulty

| Level | Parameters |
|---|---|
| Entry | Single zone, obvious harm-type, unlimited response time |
| Mid | 2 zones simultaneously, mixed harm-types, 3s response window |
| Advanced | Rapid multi-zone sequences, subtle intensity grades, 1.5s window |
| Shadow-mode | Stimuli targeting player's neglected or over-attended zones |

### 5.1 Theta-Decay Triggers

- 3+ days: localisation accuracy regresses one sub-level
- 7+ days: withdrawal speed resets to mid-level
- Decay capped at 25%

### 5.2 Mastery Criteria

- Zone localisation accuracy ≥ 90% at advanced level
- Withdrawal direction correct ≥ 85%
- Harm-type discrimination ≥ 80%
- No zone-neglect (all 6 zones within 1 SD of mean accuracy)
- All four shadow signatures below threshold
