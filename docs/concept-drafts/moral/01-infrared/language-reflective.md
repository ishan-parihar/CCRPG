# Moral × Infrared — Language-Reflective

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Language-Reflective |
| Unique lateral | Pre-verbal harm-expression — pain vocalisations, distress sounds, proto-communication of hurt |
| Core mechanic | Matching, producing, and recognising pain-vocalisations and distress signals |
| Session length | 45–90 seconds per checkpoint |
| Infinite checkpoint | Yes — each vocalisation set is a checkpoint |

## 2. Catalyst Design

### 2.1 Surface Experience

The player hears or sees creatures making sounds — some expressing pain/distress, others expressing comfort/safety. At Infrared, "language" is pre-verbal: grunts, cries, whimpers, hisses, sighs. The player must identify which sounds signal harm and which signal safety, then produce (tap-pattern or sound-selection) the appropriate distress or comfort response.

### 2.2 Stimulus Parameters

- **Pain vocalisations:** sharp cries, hissing, whimpering, staccato yelps
- **Safety vocalisations:** purring, humming, slow sighs, rhythmic cooing
- **Ambiguous vocalisations:** mixed-valence sounds (groan that could be pain or effort)
- **Production prompts:** "this creature is hurt — which sound matches?" (selection from 2–4 options)

### 2.3 Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Can the player distinguish increasingly subtle pain-sounds from neutral sounds? |
| Agape | Does the player acknowledge distress sounds as valid (not dismiss them)? |
| Agency | Can the player produce/select the correct distress signal when prompted? |
| Communion | Does the player match the intensity of the pain-sound (not over- or under-match)? |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Behavioural signature |
|---|---|
| Pain-Fixation (DA) | Hears pain in every sound; classifies neutral vocalisations as distress |
| Pain-Numbness (DAll) | Cannot distinguish pain-sounds from safety-sounds; flat response to all |
| Premature Taboo (GA) | Labels sounds as "bad" or "wrong" rather than identifying felt-quality of pain |
| Harm Fortress (GAll) | Correctly identifies pain-sounds but refuses to produce matching distress response |

## 4. Integration Mechanics

### 4.1 Heal Vector (bottom-up: Agape + Agency)

- **Pain-Fixation:** Present clearly safe sounds; reward accurate "safe" identification; build trust in non-pain
- **Pain-Numbness:** Amplify pain-sound features; pair with visual harm-cues; rebuild auditory pain-sensitivity

### 4.2 Evolve Vector (top-down: Eros + Communion)

- **Premature Taboo:** Remove verbal labels; force felt-quality matching (which sound FEELS like this image?); ground in sensation
- **Harm Fortress:** Make production necessary for progression; reward expressive matching; dissolve resistance to voicing pain

## 5. Progression & Difficulty

| Level | Parameters |
|---|---|
| Entry | Binary pain/safe sounds, high contrast, 2 options for production |
| Mid | 3-valence sounds (pain/neutral/safe), moderate contrast, 3 options |
| Advanced | Subtle gradations of distress intensity, 4 options, ambiguous stimuli |
| Shadow-mode | Sounds designed to trigger specific shadow; adaptive selection |

### 5.1 Theta-Decay Triggers

- 3+ days: pain-sound discrimination regresses one sub-level
- 7+ days: production accuracy resets to mid-level
- Decay capped at 25%

### 5.2 Mastery Criteria

- Pain/safe vocalisation discrimination ≥ 85% at advanced level
- Production accuracy (correct match) ≥ 80%
- Intensity-matching within 1 grade of target
- All four shadow signatures below threshold
