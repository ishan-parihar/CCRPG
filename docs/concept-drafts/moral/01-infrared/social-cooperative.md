# Moral × Infrared — Social-Cooperative

## 1. Game Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Modality | Social-Cooperative |
| Unique lateral | Shared harm-detection — responding to another's pain signals, co-avoidance |
| Core mechanic | Detecting harm via another creature's distress and coordinating avoidance |
| Session length | 60–120 seconds per checkpoint |
| Infinite checkpoint | Yes — each co-avoidance trial is a checkpoint |

## 2. Catalyst Design

### 2.1 Surface Experience

Two creatures share an environment. One creature encounters harm first and emits distress signals (visual flinch, pain-sound, colour-change). The player's creature must read these signals and avoid the same harm-source. The proto-social moral seed: another's pain is information about MY danger. Shared harm-detection is the root of empathy-as-survival.

### 2.2 Stimulus Parameters

- **Partner distress cues:** flinch animation, pain vocalisation, colour-shift to red, retreat movement
- **Harm sources:** environmental hazards that partner encounters first
- **Signal clarity:** obvious (full flinch + sound) to subtle (slight colour-shift only)
- **Temporal gap:** partner's distress precedes player's encounter by 3s (entry) to 0.5s (advanced)

### 2.3 Drive Probes

| Drive | Probe |
|---|---|
| Eros | Can the player read increasingly subtle partner-distress signals? |
| Agape | Does the player attend to partner's signals (not ignore them)? |
| Agency | Does the player act on partner's distress (avoid the same source)? |
| Communion | Does the player's avoidance correlate with partner's signal intensity? |

## 3. Shadow Surfacing

| Shadow | Behavioural signature |
|---|---|
| Pain-Fixation (DA) | Reads distress in every partner movement; avoids everything partner does |
| Pain-Numbness (DAll) | Ignores partner's distress entirely; walks into same harm-source |
| Premature Taboo (GA) | Avoids based on partner's position ("where partner was = bad") not partner's pain-signal |
| Harm Fortress (GAll) | Reads partner's distress accurately but does not alter own path |

## 4. Integration Mechanics

### 4.1 Heal Vector (bottom-up: Agape + Agency)

- **Pain-Fixation:** Partner shows clear comfort signals too; reward distinguishing partner-pain from partner-neutral
- **Pain-Numbness:** Amplify partner distress cues; make ignoring costly (player hits same harm); rebuild social-signal sensitivity

### 4.2 Evolve Vector (top-down: Eros + Communion)

- **Premature Taboo:** Partner moves through safe zones too; force reading SIGNAL not POSITION; ground in distress-quality
- **Harm Fortress:** Make solo-avoidance impossible (only partner can detect certain harms); require acting on partner's data

## 5. Progression & Difficulty

| Level | Parameters |
|---|---|
| Entry | Obvious partner distress, 3s warning gap, single harm-source |
| Mid | Moderate distress cues, 1.5s gap, 2 harm-sources |
| Advanced | Subtle cues, 0.5s gap, 3+ harm-sources, partner also shows comfort |
| Shadow-mode | Partner signals tuned to trigger player's dominant shadow |

### 5.1 Theta-Decay Triggers

- 3+ days: signal-reading accuracy regresses one sub-level
- 7+ days: co-avoidance success resets to mid-level
- Decay capped at 25%

### 5.2 Mastery Criteria

- Partner-distress detection accuracy ≥ 85% at advanced level
- Co-avoidance success (avoid harm partner flagged) ≥ 85%
- False co-avoidance rate (avoiding when partner showed comfort) < 15%
- All four shadow signatures below threshold
