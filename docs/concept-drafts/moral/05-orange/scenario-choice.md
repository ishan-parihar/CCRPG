# Moral / Orange — Scenario-Choice Game Concept

> **Axis:** Moral dilemmas where principles conflict or universal rights compete with particular care.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange moral capacity is tested most precisely at the point where two valid principles collide — the player must choose AND justify, revealing reasoning depth and shadow.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Crucible of Principles  
**Genre:** Branching narrative dilemmas with consequence revelation  
**Session length:** 5–9 minutes (infinite checkpoint)  
**Core loop:** Scenario presented → principles identified → choice made → consequences unfold → reflection prompt → next scenario escalates

The player faces scenarios where no option is cleanly "right." Two or more legitimate principles compete. The game measures not WHAT the player chooses but HOW they reason about the choice — and whether they can hold the tension without collapsing into calculation, convention, relativism, or rigidity.

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Orange moral — principled reasoning under genuine conflict  
**Shadow surfacing:** GAll (fortress) surfaces when player refuses to acknowledge the legitimacy of the competing principle. GA (relativist) surfaces when player refuses to commit, claiming both sides are equally valid without reasoning through.  
**Progression:** Diagnosis (sessions 1–3) → Healing (4–8) → Evolution (9+)

Healing catalyst: consequence revelation shows the fortress that rigid application caused harm — inviting flexibility without abandoning principle. Shows the relativist that refusal to commit caused harm — inviting backbone. Evolution catalyst: scenarios where genuine integration of competing principles is possible through creative moral reasoning.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**
- Scenarios present 2–3 stakeholders with legitimate but competing claims
- Player identifies which principles are at stake (from principle-bank)
- Player chooses an action AND selects a justification-type (rights-based, contract-based, consequential, duty-based)
- Consequences unfold over 2–3 beats, revealing downstream effects
- Reflection prompt: "knowing what happened, would you choose differently? Why?"

**Difficulty staircase:**
- Level 1: Clear principle-conflict (rights vs. utility) with one stronger claim
- Level 2: Balanced conflicts where both principles have equal weight
- Level 3: Hidden stakeholders revealed mid-scenario (information asymmetry)
- Level 4: Player's prior choices create the current dilemma (moral responsibility)
- Level 5: Genuine tragic dilemmas — no resolution without loss

**Shadow-specific mechanics:**
- GAll probe: scenarios where the player's preferred principle causes clear harm to a particular person
- GA probe: scenarios where "both sides have a point" is true but action is still required
- DA probe: scenarios where optimal calculation ignores individual dignity
- DAll probe: scenarios where existing rules/laws are clearly unjust

## 4. Item Pool

- 60 base scenarios across 6 conflict-types (rights vs. utility, justice vs. care, liberty vs. equality, individual vs. collective, present vs. future, letter vs. spirit)
- 3 consequence-branches per scenario (best-case, worst-case, unexpected)
- 20 hidden-stakeholder reveals per conflict-type
- 30 reflection prompts keyed to choice-justification combinations
- Moral-responsibility chains: 10 multi-session arcs where prior choices seed future dilemmas
- Adaptive difficulty: conflict-balance, stakeholder complexity, information timing

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Branching narrative engine with consequence-state tracking
- Justification-type classifier: maps player's stated reasoning to formal categories
- Cross-session moral-responsibility tracker: links prior choices to current scenarios
- Reflection-response scorer: evaluates quality of post-consequence reasoning
- Shadow-pattern detector: identifies fortress/relativist/calculator/conformist signatures across sessions
- Drive-health integration: choice patterns + justification quality → four-drive scores
- Consequence-revelation timing engine: controls information flow for maximum catalyst effect
