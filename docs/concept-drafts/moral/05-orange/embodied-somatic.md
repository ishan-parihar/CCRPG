# Moral / Orange — Embodied-Somatic Game Concept

> **Axis:** The body as site of moral feeling — moral intuition, gut-response, and embodied conscience.  **Why this axis for this module:** Orange moral reasoning risks disembodiment; this modality tests whether principled reasoning is grounded in felt moral weight or has become pure calculation divorced from the body.

---

## 1. Game Identity

**Title:** The Moral Gut  
**Genre:** Timed somatic-response tasks with moral stimuli  
**Session length:** 3–6 minutes (infinite checkpoint)  
**Core loop:** Moral stimulus → body-response window → somatic signal registered → reasoning prompt → congruence scored

The player's body responds to moral scenarios before cognition catches up. The game measures the relationship between somatic moral intuition and subsequent principled reasoning — are they aligned, or has one been suppressed?

## 2. Catalyst Delivery

**Catalyst frequency:** Orange moral — embodied grounding of principled reasoning  
**Shadow surfacing:** DA (calculator) surfaces when somatic response is absent or suppressed — the body doesn't register moral weight. DAll (conformist) surfaces when somatic response is present but reasoning cannot articulate why — gut-feeling without principle.  
**Progression:** Diagnosis (sessions 1–3) → Healing (4–8) → Evolution (9+)

Healing catalyst: for the calculator, scenarios designed to evoke unavoidable somatic response (visceral injustice, harm to vulnerable) — reconnecting body to moral reasoning. For the conformist, prompts that ask "your body said no — can you find the principle?" — connecting gut to reason. Evolution catalyst: scenarios where somatic intuition and principled reasoning initially conflict, inviting integration.

## 3. Game Design

**Mechanics:**
- Rapid moral stimuli (micro-scenarios, audio clips) presented in 2–4 second windows
- Player registers somatic response via input (tap intensity, hold duration, swipe direction as valence)
- Response-time and intensity measured as somatic-signal proxies
- Post-stimulus: brief reasoning prompt ("why did your body respond that way?")
- Congruence score: alignment between somatic intensity and reasoning quality

**Difficulty staircase:**
- Level 1: Clear moral violations (harm, injustice) — somatic response expected
- Level 2: Ambiguous scenarios — somatic signal may conflict with initial reasoning
- Level 3: Scenarios where "correct" principled reasoning opposes gut-response
- Level 4: Rapid-fire sequences testing whether somatic moral compass remains calibrated under speed
- Level 5: Scenarios requiring integration — body AND mind must agree for full score

**Shadow-specific mechanics:**
- DA probe: scenarios of individual suffering — is somatic response present or flatlined?
- DAll probe: scenarios where convention is violated but no harm occurs — does body react to rule-breaking or to harm?
- GA probe: scenarios where sophisticated framing masks genuine harm — does body see through performance?
- GAll probe: scenarios where rigid principle causes embodied distress — can player feel the cost of their fortress?

## 4. Item Pool

- 100 rapid moral stimuli (micro-scenarios, 2–4 sentences each)
- 40 ambiguous stimuli designed to split somatic/cognitive response
- 30 congruence-conflict stimuli (gut says X, principle says Y)
- Response-time baselines per player (calibrated in session 1)
- 50 post-stimulus reasoning prompts keyed to response-intensity patterns
- Somatic-signal calibration tasks (non-moral baseline for comparison)

## 5. Technical Requirements

- Sub-200ms input registration for somatic-response timing accuracy
- Response-intensity mapping: tap pressure / hold duration → signal strength
- Baseline calibration engine: establishes player's non-moral somatic response range
- Congruence calculator: somatic-signal × reasoning-quality alignment score
- Longitudinal somatic-pattern tracker: monitors body-mind alignment over sessions
- Drive-health mapping: flatline somatic → DA; reactive-without-reason → DAll
- Privacy: somatic response data stored locally, aggregated only for scoring
