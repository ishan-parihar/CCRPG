# Moral / Orange — Module Specification

> **Line:** Moral  **Stage:** Orange / Rational  **Vibration:** The examined moral life — reasoning from principle rather than convention, asking WHY a rule should hold.

---

## 1. Capacity Definition

Post-conventional principled moral reasoning (Kohlberg Stage 5). The player demonstrates the ability to evaluate moral rules from a social-contract and universal-rights framework rather than accepting them as given. Core capacities: moral justification from principle, utilitarian vs. deontological discrimination, belief revision under moral evidence, consistency of reasoning across novel dilemmas, and the felt-sense that principles serve persons (not the reverse).

## 2. Shadow Archetypes

### 2.1 Dark-Addiction — The Moral Calculator

Reduces all ethics to utility maximisation. Treats persons as variables in equations. Cannot feel moral weight — only compute it. "The greatest good for the greatest number" without felt-sense of individual suffering. Ethics becomes spreadsheet optimisation.

### 2.2 Dark-Allergy — The Moral Conformist

Refuses principled reasoning entirely. Remains in Amber conventional morality: "the rules are the rules." Cannot examine WHY a rule should hold. Experiences principled questioning as dangerous relativism. Clings to authority-given codes.

### 2.3 Golden-Addiction — The Premature Relativist

Performs Green contextual ethics without genuine principled reasoning underneath. Claims "morality is relative" before ever genuinely reasoning from principle. Moral sophistication without moral backbone. Bypasses Orange into pseudo-Green.

### 2.4 Golden-Allergy — The Principled Fortress

Principled reasoning is present and genuine, but refuses all contextual nuance. Terrified that admitting context means admitting relativism. "There ARE universal principles and I will NOT compromise them." Rigidity masquerading as integrity.

## 3. Drive-Health Landscape

| Drive | Healthy Expression | Pathological Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Eros | Reaches toward universal principle, seeks higher moral coherence | Golden-addiction: bypasses to relativism without grounding |
| Agape | Returns to embody principles in concrete situations | Dark-addiction: returns only to calculate, not to feel |
| Agency | Sovereign moral reasoning, self-authored ethics | Golden-allergy: fortress of principle, refuses communion |
| Communion | Principles serve relational flourishing | Dark-allergy: dissolves into group convention, refuses autonomy |

## 4. Healing Vectors

### 4.1 Heal/Evolve (Agape + Agency → Dark Shadows)

DA: Agape re-embodies felt moral weight; Agency refuses to let calculation replace conscience. DAll: Agency authors own principles; Agape grounds them in lived experience rather than abstract authority.

### 4.2 Evolve/Heal (Eros + Communion → Golden Shadows)

GA: Eros demands genuine principled depth before contextual sophistication; Communion tests principles in relationship. GAll: Communion softens the fortress through encounter with the particular; Eros reaches toward integration rather than rigidity.

### 4.3 Integration Criteria

Player demonstrates: (a) consistent principled reasoning across novel dilemmas, (b) felt moral weight accompanying rational analysis, (c) willingness to revise principles under genuine moral evidence, (d) principles applied relationally without collapsing into convention.

## 6. Compound Shadows & Cross-Module Relationships

### 6.1 The Utilitarian Machine (Moral DA × Cognitive DA)

Moral calculation fused with hyper-rationalism. Ethics becomes pure logic without embodied conscience. Intervention: somatic moral-feeling tasks + emotional line engagement.

### 6.2 The Principled Isolate (Moral GAll × Interpersonal DAll)

Principled fortress combined with refusal of relationship. Righteous but alone. Intervention: social-cooperative modality + interpersonal line tasks requiring principle-in-relationship.

### 6.3 The Moral Performer (Moral GA × Emotional GA)

Premature relativism fused with premature empathy. Performed ethics without genuine depth in either line. Intervention: deterministic reasoning tasks demanding backbone before nuance.

### 6.4 The Obedient Achiever (Moral DAll × Willpower DA)

Refuses principled reasoning while compulsively achieving. Succeeds without examining why. Intervention: strategic-planning tasks forcing examination of goals' moral foundations.

## 7. Shadow Surfacing Sequence

### 7.1 Progressive Revelation

Sessions 1–3: baseline principled reasoning capacity. Sessions 4–7: introduce competing principles to surface fortress/calculator. Sessions 8–12: introduce contextual pressure to surface conformist/relativist. Session 13+: compound shadow probes.

Shadow identification requires convergent evidence from ≥2 modalities. No shadow is diagnosed from a single session. Compound shadows require evidence from ≥3 modalities across ≥2 lines.
